# Guardrail: Battle Plausibility

**Source:** Part 44 (`cultivation-battle-plausibility-law`, Canon Amendment 2026-10-17) · cross-referenced into
`cultivation-power-and-lifespan-law` Area 7.2 and `cultivation-combat-and-fight-law` Area 5.1
**Applies to:** the Outline Planner (plans every fight with an Engagement Ledger), the Area Maker (writes to the ledger), the Continuity Checker (**Gate 1 Check #23**), and the Chapter Editor (keeps the fight's levels, offsets and result as drafted).
**Authority Tier:** 1 (Part 44 is a Tier 1 domain authority in the cultivation group). The guardrail states the hard rules; the detail is in the skill.

## Why this exists

Part 3 Area 7.2 judges one pairing (the 3-Level Rule) and Part 5 judges who may start and end a fight.
Neither asks whether a whole engagement (squads, numbers, ground, weapons) makes sense at the levels written.
Chapter 3 failed that way: a patrol of one Warrior at Level 2 Peak and eight Seniors was written holding a road against a raiding band led by a Warrior at Level 9 Peak with line fighters at Warrior Levels 3 to 5. This guardrail makes the question a Gate 1 check, and Part 44 tells a writer how a lower power can lawfully hold, delay or beat a higher one.

## The rule

- **Levels first.** Every fight is read from the ledgers: each side's anchors (its strongest few) and every pairing that meets, with the gap in minor steps (Part 3 Area 1.6). No math for a group: there is no sum, average or score for a side. A group is judged in words by the checklist of Part 44 Area 5.
- **Three bands.** A pairing is Open (gap 0 to 2 minor steps), Shut (3 or more inside one Major Realm) or Barred (across a Major Realm). A lower fighter cannot win a fair contest in a Shut pairing and cannot harm a Barred one except through Part 3 Rule 17.3.3.
- **A lower side needs a named, priced, lawful offset** to hold, delay or beat a higher one: (a) numbers and formation, (b) ground, preparation, ambush, surprise and shielding, (c) technology and weapons, (d) exhaustion and cost, (e) a registered gifted fighter, (f) limit break and striving. An offset changes the engagement, not any fighter's level (the gifted fighter's lift excepted), and none crosses a Major Realm or lets a lower side win a Shut fair contest.
- **Registered gifted fighters.** A rare, named, non-hero person with a recorded reason (talent, rare technique, long hard practice) fights as if up to 3 minor steps above their level, never more, never across a Major Realm, registered in the ledger before the scene and ruled by the author. They may tide a battle and may not win it alone. A hero keeps the Combat Level Advantage of Part 16 Rule 16.16.4.2, unchanged.
- **Limit break and striving.** A fighter with qi who strives or breaks their limit fights above their level for a burst, when the gap is not too big. The reach follows the person's affinity level (Part 4 Area 1.2): an ordinary person 2, 4 or 6 minor steps, a hero 4, 7 or 10. Cost tiers: Tier 1 (ordinary up to 2, hero up to 4) taxing only, cured by rest; Tier 2 (up to 6, up to 10) injury, fainting or similar; Tier 3 (beyond, up to the hard caps of 10 and 20) qi damage or Qi Deviation (Part 3 Area 15.3). Never across a Major Realm; a burst, not a standing level; priced in the spec and on the page afterwards. The 3-Level Rule is not waived (the lift is counted before the comparison and the remaining gap is read). It does not simply add to a gifted lift or a hero's advantage: the standing lift plus the burst never pass the hard cap.
- **Story and spec kept apart.** The gap arithmetic (minor steps, gaps, percentages, the bands, anchors, ledger terms) lives only in the Chapter Spec, the build plan, the ledgers and the gate reports. Narration and dialogue show soldier counts, formations, ground and strategy, and show power as it is sensed and fought with (pressure, weight, slowed qi, aching knees, a blade that will not move). Level strings (Part 29 Rule 29.12.1.3) stay and stay required once for each person in a Chapter, and sensed comparison in words (far above, about equal) is allowed; numbers, steps, percentages and "N levels above" never are (this reads with Part 3 Rule 3.4.1 and weakens neither).
- **The Engagement Ledger.** Every Area of the Chapter Spec with a fight carries one under its Fight line, made at the spec build and fixed before drafting: both sides' anchors, each fighter's realm and Level, role, pairing, gap in steps, offset used and expected outcome (format in Part 44 Area 5.2).
- **Nothing above is weakened.** Part 3 and Part 5 stand as written; where Part 44 and Part 3 differ on one pairing, Part 3 rules. The story decides which fights are shown and who wins; this guardrail only says whether a result is lawful.

## Gate 1 Check #23: Battle Plausibility

Gate 1 runs **23 checks**. Check #23 sits after Check #22. Elements 23-1 to 23-6 run on every Area whose text holds a fight (any exchange of blows or weapon fire between named or counted sides, and any battle, siege, ambush, pursuit or war event shown or reported) and read **N/A** for an Area with no fight; element 23-7 reads every Area. In a whole-built Chapter it runs once for each fight Area block.

| Element | What is measured | Class |
|---|---|---|
| **23-1** | Anchors and levels: each side's anchors and every combatant's level agree with the ledgers and the Chapter Spec | **BLOCKING** |
| **23-2** | Pairings: each pairing that meets is in its band; the lower side wins no Shut pairing in a fair contest and harms no Barred pairing without Rule 17.3.3 | **BLOCKING** |
| **23-3** | Presence and survival: each fighter can be there and survive at the stated levels (aura, lethal threshold, speed, qi for the length of the fight; a non-cultivator on a field of flattening fighters) | **BLOCKING** |
| **23-4** | Offsets: each offset used is lawful, within its bound and priced in the text; every gifted fighter is registered in the ledger before the scene; every limit break is within its reach and caps and priced on the page afterwards | **BLOCKING** |
| **23-5** | Outcome: the result is not one the levels forbid (who is wounded, killed, holds, flees, and for how long) and fits Part 44 Area 7 and the end class of Part 5 Area 12 | **BLOCKING** |
| **23-6** | Strain: a result reached only through offsets, through several at once, through one offset carrying a Shut pairing, or through a Tier 2 or Tier 3 limit break | Reported to the author as a ruling point; never a FAIL |
| **23-7** | No gap arithmetic in prose: narration and dialogue state no minor steps, computed gap, percentage, multiplier or "N levels above", and use no band name or ledger term as a measurement (level strings and sensed comparison in words are lawful) | **BLOCKING** (quoted from the text) |

- **Blocking statement.** A break of 23-1 to 23-5 or 23-7 is a FAIL (a hard-rule break: the 3-Level Rule, a Major Realm crossing with no lawful offset under Part 3 Rule 17.3.3, a side unable to be present or survive at the stated levels, or a result the levels forbid). The finding is quoted from the text with the pairing and its gap. The checker labels it on its usual severity scale, Critical where a Barred crossing is won by the lower side. A FAIL goes back through the normal loop (`[REVISE AREA]` or the Whole-Chapter FAIL loop).
- **Strain is not a FAIL.** A 23-6 item is listed in the audit report with the offset used, why it is strained and the options, and the author rules at `[CONFIRM WRITE]` (accept it, or `[REVISE AREA]`). Naming a lawful offset is never presented as a cost.
- **Builds on Check #8.** Element 8-V already FAILs a fight with an unknown combatant level; Check #23 reads what the known levels allow.
- **Plan and text.** The checker builds an engagement ledger from the draft and the ledgers and compares it with the spec's. A difference on a level, a pairing or an offset is a plan-versus-written difference listed at Check #18; a break of the law is a Check #23 FAIL.
- **Not retroactive.** Chapters 1 and 2 hold no fight and are untouched. A written Area is re-checked only when it is revised.
- **Hand edits only.** The registration of a gifted fighter and every ledger line are written by hand through the normal ledger path; no script writes them.

## Escalation

When a level, a pairing or an offset is unclear, or a registration is wanted, ask the author with `AskUserQuestion` through the orchestrating command rather than guessing. A conflict between this guardrail and Part 3 or Part 5 on one pairing is resolved for Part 3 or Part 5; anything wider goes to `/run-canon-audit`.
