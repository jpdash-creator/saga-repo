# Guardrail: Situational Combat Selection

**Source:** Part 45 (`cultivation-situational-combat-law`, Canon Amendment 2026-10-19) · cross-referenced into `cultivation-move-taxonomy-law` Area 2.1, `manual-and-weapon-style-law` Rule 42.11.6.4 and `cultivation-combat-and-fight-law` Appendix A
**Applies to:** the Outline Planner (writes the Situation line of every fight in the Chapter Spec), the Area Maker (chooses each move by the Move Selection Test), the Continuity Checker (**Gate 1 Check #24**), and the Chapter Editor (keeps the moves, their order and their prices as drafted).
**Authority Tier:** 1 (Part 45 is a Tier 1 domain authority in the cultivation group). The guardrail states the hard rules; the detail is in the skill.

## Why this exists

The canon says which moves exist, which a person may use and what they cost. It did not say how a writer picks among them. A rule that never existed, that an Intermediate or Advanced move must follow a Basic move in a scene, crept into planning and made fights read as ladders. The author removed it on 2026-10-19: anyone can use any move they have access to, at any point; effort and fatigue are the limit, not sequence; the situation chooses the move (a crouching target cannot be kicked in the head). This guardrail makes the fit between move and moment a Gate 1 check.

## The rule

- **No order rule.** No move of a higher type must follow, or be paired with, a move of a lower type in a scene. Any Usable move may open, carry or end a fight. A couple of Advanced moves may finish one. The link codes of Part 42 (LINK-PRIOR, LINK-STEP, LINK-GUARD) are eligibility prerequisites to hold a manual and are never an ordering rule; no scene has to show a lower manual for them to count as met.
- **Usable only.** A fighter uses only moves that are Usable for them (Part 42 Rule 42.3.3.1). A Latent move is never executed (Rule 42.13.1.3). The Seal is unchanged: for Hercules and Bison Signature and Catastrophic stay blocked by the Seal and Elite, Fusion and Mythic stay Latent through the effective realm (Rule 42.3.4.2).
- **The situation chooses.** Before each move the writer reads the Situation Frame (ground and footing, space and cover, the posture of both sides, reach and height, size and anatomy, tempo, numbers, people to protect, light and weather, witnesses, hands and weapon, injury and energy left, intent and style) and runs the Move Selection Test of Part 45 Area 11. A move the moment makes impossible is not used.
- **The body and posture decide what can reach what.** No high kick at a crouching target, no sweep at an airborne one, no standing blow at a part the target has turned away.
- **Ground and space size the move.** No wide or whirling move in a lane or a crowd, no leap or heavy lunge on mud, no long weapon where it has no room.
- **Reach and anatomy.** The range, the height and reach difference and the opponent's body (a beast included: quadruped, winged, swarm, armoured, horned) decide what lands.
- **Hands and weapons.** A bare-handed fighter uses only unarmed geometry (strikes, grips, locks, throws, braces) and takes reach from a thrown thing, a tool or a Step. An armed fighter uses the weapon's geometry. A move is used only with the tool it needs.
- **Cost and fatigue.** A higher-grade move costs more and tires the user more (Part 6 Area 5.3; Depletion States of Part 3 Area 12.4). An Advanced or higher move has a cause in the moment and shows a price in proportion; a run of costly moves shows fatigue. The figures stay in the spec and the gate; the page shows signs.
- **Protect and spare.** A protector keeps their line and protectee; a move fits the stated intent (spare, stop, drive off, kill); an area move is not used with friends in its arc unless the cost is shown.
- **Menu, not ladder.** The spec's Situation line lists the moves a fight may use as a menu with the situation each fits, and never as a sequence. A fight is not built as Basic, Intermediate, Advanced in step. A fight does not name a move every sentence.
- **Named Moves unchanged.** A move used in prose is named bold-italic at every mention, with its picture, from the Manual Ledger by exact name (Part 42 Rule 42.13.1.1); the Move-Naming Law stands; no gap arithmetic, band name or percentage on the page (Part 44 Rule 44.5.3; Check #23 element 23-7); plain words (Check #22).
- **Author's word rules.** Where the author names a move or a type (Elite, Signature), that is used within the Access Table and the Seal; where the author wants options, the planner offers two or three with their reading.
- **Not retroactive.** Applies to future writing only, starting with Chapter 006 (author ruling, 2026-10-19). Chapters 001 to 005 are left as written.

## Gate 1 Check #24: Situational Combat Selection

Gate 1 runs **24 checks**. Check #24 sits after Check #23. It runs on every Area whose text holds a fight (the definition of Check #23) and reads **N/A** for an Area with no fight. In a whole-built Chapter it runs once for each fight Area block.

| Element | What is measured | Class |
|---|---|---|
| **24-1** | Body and posture: each move is possible in the user's posture and reaches a part of the target that is open and in reach at that moment | **BLOCKING** |
| **24-2** | Ground, space, light and weather: each move fits footing, room for its arc, cover, crowding, light and weather the text has set | **BLOCKING** |
| **24-3** | Reach, size and anatomy: each move fits the range, the height and reach difference and the opponent's body (a beast included) | **BLOCKING** |
| **24-4** | Hands and weapons: a bare-handed fighter uses no weapon geometry; an armed fighter uses the weapon's; no Latent or Seal-blocked move is executed | **BLOCKING** |
| **24-5** | No fixed ladder, no move-name spam: no order of moves by type is stated or followed as a rule; no run of moves rises in grade step by step with no cause in the situation; the named moves do not crowd out the action | **BLOCKING** (quoted from the text) |
| **24-6** | Cost, cause and fatigue: an Advanced or higher move has a cause in the moment and a proportionate price; a run of costly moves shows fatigue and respects the Depletion State | **BLOCKING** |
| **24-7** | Faith and intent: a protector keeps their line and protectee; the move fits the stated intent; friends are not in an area move's arc without a cost shown | **BLOCKING** |
| **24-8** | Strain: a lawful move that is a poor fit by the Move Selection Test with no reason in the text, or a result that rests on a perfect window | Reported to the author as information; never a FAIL |

- **Blocking statement.** A break of 24-1 to 24-7 is a FAIL. The finding is quoted from the text with the move, the reading it breaks (posture, ground, hands, order) and the lawful fix. The checker labels it on its usual severity scale. A FAIL goes back through the normal loop (`[REVISE AREA]` or the Whole-Chapter FAIL loop).
- **Information is not a FAIL.** A 24-8 item is listed with the move, why it is a poor fit and the better options; the author rules at `[CONFIRM WRITE]`.
- **Builds on, repeats nothing.** Check #23 reads whether the engagement is plausible by level; Check #17(b) the Move-Naming Law; Check #12 the bold-italic form; Check #8 element 8-V the level made known. Check #24 reads the fit of move to moment.
- **Plan and text.** The checker compares the moves and the Situation of the draft with the Situation line of the Chapter Spec's Fight line. A difference is listed at Check #18; a break of this law is a Check #24 FAIL.
- **Not retroactive.** Chapters 001 to 005 are not checked against #24, are not redrafted or revised because of it, and a written Area is re-checked only if the author orders its revision.
- **Hand edits only.** No script writes this check's findings or any ledger line for it.

## Escalation

When a move, a posture or a Situation reading is unclear, or the author's wish for a move conflicts with the Frame, ask the author with `AskUserQuestion` through the orchestrating command rather than guessing. A conflict between this guardrail and Part 3, 5, 6 or 42 is resolved for the Part; anything wider goes to `/run-canon-audit`.
