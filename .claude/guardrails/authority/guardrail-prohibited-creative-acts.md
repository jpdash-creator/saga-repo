# Guardrail: Prohibited Creative Acts

**Source:** Part 28 (Novel Creative Flexibility Law), Area 12 · full detail: `narration-novel-creative-flexibility-law` §Area 12
**Applies to:** Area Maker (primarily); Continuity Checker (audits it, ALCA dimension AL-6)

## The 7 absolute prohibitions (never permitted, regardless of dramatic convenience)

1. **Causally Unsupported Cultivation Jumps** — no breakthrough that bypasses Part 3's
   requirements without a narratively-prepared, canon-compliant mechanism.
2. **Causally Unsupported Technology Jumps** — no tech exceeding an established TA-axis tier
   without a compliant acquisition mechanism (transfer, inheritance, merger, leading-edge
   breakthrough).
3. **Causally Unsupported Location Changes** — no character appears somewhere without a
   travel-time-consistent explanation per Part 15.
4. **Causally Unsupported Faction/Affiliation Changes** — an affiliation shift must be
   rendered in prose as the consequence of shown events, never assumed between chapters.
5. **Causally Unsupported Character Behavioral Reversals** — a deeply established ideology,
   loyalty, or tactical stance may only change through a rendered, earned arc.
6. **Information Miracles** — a character may not "just know" something without a plausible
   acquisition mechanism consistent with Part 24.
7. **Consequence Evasion** — no escaping a Part 27-mandated consequence without a
   canon-compliant resolution pathway being shown on the page. The protagonist is never
   exempt (Part 27 Standard N27-6 is absolute).

Also prohibited: **Civilization Characteristic Violations** — a civilization acting outside
what its CCT tier and type allow (e.g. a Subsistence-tier civilization deploying
Galactic-scale force) without a rendered, complete restructuring.

## Forbidden realm vocabulary (hard block, all contexts)

These generic cultivation-novel terms are **not part of this canon** and may never appear in
prose, dialogue, thought, narration, or ledger text as a realm name or label: *Nascent Soul, Foundation Establishment,
Qi Condensation (as a realm label), Golden Core, Spirit Severing, Dao Seeking, Mahayana,
Tribulation Transcendence, Eternal Life, Immortal Ascension*. ("Nascent" as a plain adjective
— e.g. Part 13's Nascent Village/Empire tiers, "a nascent rivalry" — remains fine; only the
compound realm name and its use as a Part 3 realm-stage modifier are forbidden.)

A forbidden term used as a realm name or label anywhere — including inside a canon document
or ledger file — is a bug requiring immediate corrective edit, not a stylistic choice. The
terms may be named only inside a prohibition list: Part 28 Area 12.2, this guardrail, and the
required "Forbidden Realm Vocabulary" statement in a ledger.
