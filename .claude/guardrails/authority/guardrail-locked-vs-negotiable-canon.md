# Guardrail: Locked Canon vs. Negotiable Canon

**Source:** Part 28 (Novel Creative Flexibility Law), Area 2 · full detail: `narration-novel-creative-flexibility-law` §Area 2
**Applies to:** Area Maker, Outline Planner (primarily); Continuity Checker (audits it)

## The rule

**Locked Canon** — governed by the numbered canon Parts, never improvised, never
contradicted — includes (non-exhaustive; see the skill for the full list): the cultivation
realm ladder and breakthrough requirements (Part 3), affinity types and the soul rung ladder
(Part 4), the escalation-tier combat framework (Part 5), the Named Move taxonomy (Part 6),
the technology tier scale and domain types (Part 8-10), the cosmological hierarchy and
Cosmic Measurement Scale (Parts 13-14), every Ledger architecture — Hero Ledger, Villain
Ledger, Side Character Profile, Ideology Ledger (Parts 17-20) — and the civilization tier,
faction taxonomy, affiliation spectrum, information classification, cultural paradigm, and
psychological/consequence architectures that govern the same entities (Parts 21-27), the
CDL-2 category definitions (Part 35; its Anchor Floors and targets are information only),
and the Phase 1 End-State Locked Canon (Part 30).

**Negotiable Canon** — free creative territory, governed by Part 28 — includes: the specific
*names* of locations, factions, techniques, characters, vehicles, weapons, and relics not
already named by a governing Part; the specific *history*, *politics*, *tactics*, *emotional
responses*, and *dialogue content* that aren't required by canon law, so long as they stay
consistent with every Locked Canon rule.

## Hard rules

- **Establishment Rule.** Once Negotiable Canon content is written into accepted prose (a
  name given, a custom described, a history told), it is fixed and acquires the same
  inviolability as Locked Canon. It may never be silently contradicted later.
- **Establishment Threshold.** Content counts as established when it is (a) given a proper
  name, (b) described with detail that creates an expectation of consistency, or (c) has a
  character-level consequence (IE/CSS/PIS event) attached. Vague or consequentially
  unattached content is not yet established and may still be refined.
- **Retroactive Enrichment is allowed** — later prose may deepen or expand established
  Negotiable Canon, provided the enrichment never contradicts what was already established.

## Escalation

Whether a specific fact is Locked or Negotiable, or whether something already counts as
"established," is not a judgment call to make silently — when genuinely unclear, escalate
via `AskUserQuestion`, through the orchestrating command, rather than guessing.
