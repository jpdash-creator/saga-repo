## Section 3 — Operation (B/L)

**Canonical Function (hub):** The bridge carries the cart road over the village's stream at the crossing. A toll collector at the bridge records the carts and log-carts that cross (the author's description of 2026-10-06; `greenhollow-cross.md` §5.6). The toll collector is Pasco Cobb (Tier D, `ledger-character-side/_index.md`; the name and the role stood by the author's ruling of 2026-10-07). Shown in Chapter 002 (Ref: 1.1.1.002), on the morning of 1247.1.1 / PD 1: the bridge is grey planks on stone legs over the quick, clear stream, with a bare bank at the far end. At the near end Pasco pulls down the short end of the bar so that the long pole rises clear of the road, and a loaded charcoal cart passes under it. A slate hangs from the post with a pencil on a string; he marks it while he counts, speaks the count to the slate without looking up ("I make it eleven sacks, though the forge asked for twelve."), counts a person as he counts a cart, and passes a villager through by name and errand ("Hercules, that is two buckets for the bakery, so go through."; the lines are those of the text revised in place on 2026-10-17). Nobody asks what he has written. No toll is named on the page: Hercules, carrying two buckets on a yoke, is counted and passed. At the far end a handcart that catches a wheel in the gap between the planks and the bank is set back on the boards by Bison, and a cart drums onto the planks later in the morning. *Chapter 006 (Ref: 1.1.1.006):* on the night of 1247.2.15 / PD 43 the host snaps the long pole of the toll bar across the road, "and the short end hung from its post" (Area block 02); Pasco Cobb runs up from the bridge "with nothing in his hands, counting aloud the people who ran past him" and later sits in the hall with the slate "he had gone back for at the bridge post" (Area blocks 02 and 05). On 1247.2.17 / PD 45 he mends the bar "with a new pole and a bolt and counted each blow of the mallet as it fell", and at the noon meeting he lists "The toll bar is broken" among the damage (Area block 06). The Chapter shows no toll taken, no traveller and no cart on the planks, and the planks and stone legs are not shown damaged.
**Capacity (hub):** Not established. No limit of weight, width or number is given.
**Faction Control / Controller:** Not established. Whom the toll collector answers to, what the toll is, who receives it and what exemptions apply are open (OPEN-AREA). The bridge stands on Azure Sky ground, since the village is at the border of the Azure Sky Kingdom. No access rule for a traveller who carries no goods is on record.

**Narrative summary (as of Chapter 002).** Alder Bridge is where the cart road crosses the village stream at Greenhollow Cross: grey planks on stone legs over a quick, clear stream, with a bare bank at the far end. A toll collector at the bridge records the carts and log-carts that cross (the author's description, 2026-10-06). The toll collector is Pasco Cobb: in the morning he pulls down the short end of the bar to raise the long pole clear of the road, counts each load and each person who goes through, marks a slate that hangs from the post with a pencil on a string, and passes the villagers he knows by name. The planks and stone legs are not shown damaged, and who keeps the bridge, the toll and the night practice stay open.
