The planks bowed under Garrick's boot, and a long crack ran out along the grain. Dread rose in his chest like a cold tide, closed on him like a fist and hung over him like a low sky. The bridge could not carry the cart, and he understood it at once.

*"This bridge will not hold the cart,"* he said.

Lenna nodded slowly. She had seen it too. The bridge was too weak and the cart was too heavy, and there was no sense in pretending otherwise.

*"We cannot cross here,"* she said. *"The planks will not take the weight."*

Garrick turned the facts over once more, as though they might change. The bridge was rotten, the cart was loaded, and the river was wide. There would be no crossing that day.
