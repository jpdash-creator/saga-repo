Brann Tolle set the cracked beam on the clerk's counter and wiped his hands.

*"A beam that has been argued with long enough will agree with anyone,"* he said. *"A sound one never needs to be asked twice."*

Pol Aske did not look up from the roll. *"A ledger that balances is a quiet liar, Master Tolle. One that does not is the only honest book in the city."*

The door opened, and Queen Aveline came in out of the rain. *"Then we are all fortunate in our difficulties,"* she said. *"A kingdom is only what its quarrels leave standing."*

*"Majesty,"* said Brann, *"a crack that is named is a crack half mended."*

*"And a name,"* said Pol, *"is only a crack in the silence."*
