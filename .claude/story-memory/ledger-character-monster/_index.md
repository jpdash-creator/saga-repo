# ledger-character-monster — Ledger Index

**Ledger Domain:** Character — Monster (tracked beasts: Kinds and Individuals; sentient monsters reserved)
**Governed By:** the ledger update law (`guardrails/ledger/guardrail-ledger-update-law.md`), Part 37 (Ledger Schema Law), Part 40 (Monster Law), Part 3 (Cultivation Realm System: the one ladder), Part 6 Area 12 (Move Substrate Taxonomy) and Rule 6.5 (the Named-Move register), Part 11 (Beast Cores and Beast Attraction), Part 16 Areas 1.1, 12.2 and 17–19 (what a character is; the Cultivated Beast; hybrids, species and other sentient beings), Part 19 Area 1.2 (the Depth Spectrum reused as depth of record), Part 39 (behaviour codes reused by the twelve traits)
**Template:** Part 37 Area 3.10 — Schema Template: Monster Ledger a thirteen-section file for a Tier A or Tier B Individual and for a Kind that needs governance detail; this index for the Kind Registry, the Tier C entries and the Tier D rows
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-20
**Index Version:** Canon v10.0
**Total Entries:** 6 established (Kinds: 6 established at the Gate 2 of Chapter 006 (Ref: 1.1.1.006), all Tier C; Individuals — Tier A: 0, Tier B: 0, Tier C: 0, Tier D: 0; sentient monsters in the Reserved table: 0, plus one Reserved fact (the artifact-made king, Canon Amendment 2026-10-19, not a registered monster; its first allusion on the page is the man's single line in Chapter 006, never shown); migrated to the villain ledger: 0)

> **Purpose:** This folder is the one ledger for every monster of the Starward Saga: a beast that holds its own qi (from Senior Level 1 Low upward) or a body-refined beast below Senior that is dangerous from strength alone (Part 40 Rule 40.1.1.1). It holds two kinds of entry. A **Kind** is the record of a kind of creature and carries the Baseline. An **Individual** is a named monster of a Kind: it points at its Kind and records only what is its own. Ordinary animals (oxen, horses, Ridebeasts, Beastdray animals, game, vermin) are not monsters and are not tracked here. Monsters carry tracked behaviour traits.
>
> **Six Kinds are established and no Individual is.** The six Kinds of the host of Chapter 1.1.1.006 were established at its Gate 2 on 2026-10-20 (see the Kind Registry), each from the written text; before that they were PLANNED rows (2026-10-19). No Individual has been entered. The first Kinds come with the Phase 1 plan: the Kind of the beasts that Part 30 plans for Phase 1 is defined and named at the Chapter Spec that plans them ("Define during chapter-spec"), and its Kind row is made by Threshold M-1 (Part 40 Rule 40.7.5.1).

> **One ladder.** Monsters follow the same cultivation hierarchy as all characters, with no ladder of their own (Part 40 Rule 40.3.1.1). A Kind holds a stage band (for example Warrior Level 2 to Level 6); an Individual holds an exact stage (realm, Level, sub-level). Every stage is a Part 3 name.

> **Tier A, B, C and D are depth of record, not power** (Part 40 Rule 40.6.1.1, as Part 19 Area 1.2 for side characters). A Kind and an Individual each carry a tier (Part 40 Rule 40.6.1.4). Separately, whatever its tier, a Kind is promoted to its own full file at its first Named Move, at Section scale, or when it is sentient; that changes the form of the record, not the tier, and needs no ruling.
> - **Tier A and Tier B:** a full file (Part 37 Area 3.10), built by hand with the author before the monster appears.
> - **Tier C:** a short entry in this index (the Tier C table below).
> - **Tier D:** one row in this index (the Tier D table below).
> - Most monsters will be Tier C and D. A Tier C or D monster attacks, injures, flees and is killed freely. It comes to the author only when it kills or maims a named person, destroys a named place or thing, becomes a recurring individual the story must remember, or is tied to a Named Move; the author rules each time, and nothing promotes on its own (Part 40 Rules 40.6.2.1 to 40.6.2.4).

> **Reserved: sentient monsters (Phase 2+).** Sentient monsters (the Awakened Cultivated Beasts of Part 16 Area 12.2) are a reserved class that no Phase 1 Area or spec may touch (Law P30-L9; Part 40 Area 11). Where they may live is not decided: OPEN-AUTHOR, "It will come in future phases." None is placed anywhere until the author opens the class. The Reserved table below holds no awakened monster: no names, no numbers, no entries of that class. It holds one Reserved fact only, from Canon Amendment 2026-10-19 (Law P30-L9): a lion-kind beast of the Skyvein forest, a non-sentient Champion Level 9 Peak monster converted to Ruler Level 2 Mid and mutated into a sentient 'Monster King' ONLY by a named Xiantian artifact (*Grayseed*, the author's choice of 2026-10-19, ledger only) that a veiled party gave to the unnamed man; its sentience is caused solely by the artifact, so no naturally awakened monster is admitted. In Phase 1 only the unnamed man alludes to it, once, in Chapter 006 Scene 3. Ledger-reference facts here are lawful; they are not prose permission.

> **Not monsters, and not entered here:** a person of any species (the Minotaur is a species, Bison a hero), a hybrid sentient, another sentient being of Part 16 Area 19, a World Tree, a Formation Spirit or Relic Intelligence, an AI or construct, an environmental spirit without independent will, a beast people with a culture (Part 40 Area 1.2).

> **A monster is never a villain unless it is sentient and ideology-driven.** A non-sentient monster's hostility is not qualifying behaviour for Threshold C-2 (Part 40 Rule 40.12.2.1). A recurring non-sentient foe stays here with the Threat Role Nemesis. When a sentient monster is classified Villain, its record moves to `ledger-character-villain` and a pointer row marked `migrated` stays in the Migrated table below; its Kind stays here (Part 40 Rule 40.12.3.1).

> **Entry schema (Part 37 Area 3.10).** The six standard columns of Part 37 Area 2.11 come first in every registry table, in their order; the further columns of each table follow. The B and L marks follow Part 37 Area 5.

> **Classification cell:** a Kind reads `Monster Kind — [class] — Tier A | B | C | D` (the class from Part 40 Rule 40.2.2.1); an Individual reads `Monster — Tier A | B | C | D (Part 40)`. **Status cell:** `Active`, `Tamed`, `Driven off`, `Killed`, `Sealed` or `Migrated` (Part 40 Rule 40.7.3.2). **Threat Role:** Hazard, Territorial Guardian, Pursuer, Raider or Swarm, Quarry, Tamed or Companion, Nemesis (Part 40 Rule 40.7.3.1). A Tier C or Tier D Individual takes the usual Threat Role of its Kind unless its entry says otherwise. **Stage:** a Part 3 name with Level and sub-level for an Individual, two such stages for a Kind's band. A cell not yet set reads `OPEN-AREA` or `OPEN-AUTHOR` (Part 37 Area 5).
>
> **Tier C behaviour line:** one paragraph of the six traits temperament, drives, fears and aversions, attack pattern, social structure and response to people, each beside the Part 39 dimension it reuses (Part 40 Rule 40.5.1.2), with the monster's one distinct ability or habit if it has one. **Tier D behaviour note:** one line in plain words drawn from the Kind's Baseline, with no code. A Kind's Behaviour Note cell holds the six-trait line for a Tier C Kind, one line for a Tier D Kind, and `see the Kind file` for a Tier A or B Kind or any Kind that has a file. A Kind carries affinity, soul and every other cultivation variety (Part 40 Rule 40.3.4.2), by reference to Parts 3 and 4. **Current Condition (Tier C and D):** two lines, the first the body (each wound or mark with how long it lasts), the second the monster's temper and state; `none recorded at story start` until an Area changes something.

> **Update Protocol:** Per the ledger update law (`guardrails/ledger/guardrail-ledger-update-law.md`) and Part 37 Thresholds M-1 to M-3:
> - **New Kind row:** a confirmed and written Area first describes a kind of monster by any physical, behavioural or habitat feature, or the author sets the Kind in an interview. Made by the Post-Write Ledger Update at Gate 2. A Kind named only in a spec or in a draft discarded by `[SKIP WRITE]` is not entered (Part 38 Rule 1.3.2). A Kind gets its own full file, whatever its tier, at its first Named Move, at Section scale, or when it is sentient.
> - **New Individual entry:** a monster has a proper name, acts with story consequence, speaks (a sentient monster), or a Chapter Spec names it; entered at the tier the story gives it. Tier A and B and every sentient monster are built by hand with the author (`/new-character <name> monster-kind` for a Kind, `/new-character <name> monster` for an Individual); a Tier C or D monster improvised in an Area is registered at Gate 2 from what the Area established.
> - **Names (Part 40 Rule 40.13.3.1; Part 28 Rule 20.4(g)):** MAJOR, so the author chooses from 2 to 3 crafted candidates: every sentient monster, every Tier A and Tier B Individual, every Tier A and Tier B Kind, and any Kind that recurs at Section scale. A Tier C or Tier D Kind or Individual is named by the assistant, with `[REVISE AREA]` as the author's veto. Names are set in roman.
> - **Pre-Write Ledger Check:** reads the Kind and the Individual entries of every monster the unit will use; refuses a first appearance of a Tier A or B Individual or of a sentient monster with no entry, pointing the author to `/new-character <name> monster-kind` for a Kind and `/new-character <name> monster` for an Individual.
> - **Named Moves:** every distinct, memorable ability the Area names (never a regular punch, kick, bite or charge, which stays plain description) is entered in `ledger-named-moves/_index.md` with the Kind or Individual as user, and in this ledger's entry, each pointing at the other (Part 40 Rule 40.4.1.2).
> - **Location mirror:** each Kind's habitat is mirrored by the `Monster Kinds Present` line of the location entry; the Kind stands on a difference (Part 40 Rule 40.8.1.2).

> **Mandatory Update Triggers:** a breakthrough (the Individual's exact stage changes; Section 10 row; Section 12 entry where a trait moves); a Beast Core formed at Core Realm; a wound or lasting mark (Current Condition); a change of Survival Status (driven off, killed, tamed, sealed, migrated); a change of handler; a Named Move named; a trigger of Part 40 Rule 40.6.2.2 (flag to the author, who rules); a Kind's habitat or Harvest line changed by Canon Amendment; a classification ruling that migrates a sentient monster to the villain ledger.

> **Marker Legend:** These markers appear in the entries of this ledger. Open cells otherwise carry `OPEN-AREA` or `OPEN-AUTHOR` (Part 37 Area 5).
> - `[ASKED]`: the author chose this value.
> - `[DERIVED]`: the drafter inferred this value from cited evidence and the author reviewed it in the digest without overriding it (Part 39 Rule 39.3.3.1); it is pending only when the entry also says flagged for author confirmation.
> - `[AUTHOR-PENDING]`: a question about this value has been raised and is not yet answered.
> - `[MARKED SLOT]`: a slot left empty on purpose until the author rules.
> - "flag, do not invent": when a scene needs the value, the prose may establish one small fact at a time under Part 28 Area 17, logged in this ledger and offered to the author as a draft value at the next Section close. A name or a major entity (Part 28 Area 20.4) goes to the author first, through the Decision Consultation Gate.

## Part 28 Area 17 — Lore Section Schema (Character Domain — Monster)

**Governing Law:** Part 28 Area 17 (Lore Creation Law). **Audit Hook:** ALCA dimension AL-10 (Lore Completeness Alignment; `guardrails/audit/guardrail-alca.md`).

**Depth Test for a Kind:** "Can a scene with this creature be written from the ledger alone: what it is, where it lives, what it does when it meets people, what it fears, what its body yields?" If yes, depth is satisfied. **Depth Test for an Individual:** "Can a reader tell this monster from the others of its Kind, and can its next move be predicted from its history and state?"

**Silent Overwrite Prohibition (Part 28 Area 17.5):** Once a Kind or an Individual is recorded, it is fixed under the Establishment Threshold. Later Areas may enrich it and never contradict it.
