**Shown on the page (Chapter 006, Ref: 1.1.1.006; first met in Area block 01).** *Body:* "lean and dark grey as the hour and pale at the throat, as high as a man's hip"; the narration calls them "Gloam Wolves, the hunters of the deep edge at dusk", and Hercules, after a year of Yestin Hocking's talk at the slide, had "never once heard of them running with anything but their own kind" (Area block 01). *Level:* Hercules's sense reads "the lean ones in front" as running "from the middle of the second Level to the high end of the fourth" and every beast of the host as a Warrior by its pressure (Area block 01); the plan band is confirmed. *Attack pattern:* they cross the strips "in pairs, one running wide and one close" (Area block 01); a pair takes Yestin Hocking in the lane's mouth, "one low for his legs and one wide for his flank", and goes over him, one into his thigh and one into his forearm (Area block 02); a wolf braced on Tomas Truscott's apron "had the smith's knee" and pulled at it; a wolf comes along a wall toward Jenifer Pender "with its belly almost on the ground"; a wolf has its jaws in Gwenna Tangye's shoulder (Area block 02); a wolf takes Lowen Wray's forearm in its jaws (Area block 03). A pair's pressure "arrived before they did" and bent Yestin's knees before a tooth touched him (Area block 02). *Response to a stronger being:* "The wolves' flanks checked at his first step toward them. Their hindquarters sank before he had raised an arm, as a cart checks when its wheel meets a stone" (Area block 03); the wolves behind a pair thrown back "checked at the sight and could not stop" (Area block 04). *Breaking off:* "Wolves that have pulled a man down stay with him. These did not stay; they went on across the crossing with their heads up" (Area block 02). *Ends:* the eight die in Area blocks 03 and 04, to Hercules's hands (among them ***Heavy Palm*** and ***Heavy Shove***) and to Bison's swing of one against the oven shed's wall; none is named, none is an Individual, none has a Named Move.
