# Canon Amendment log: the Move-Naming Law (2026-10-08)

Not canon; an audit trail.

- Authority: the author's word in chat, 2026-10-08. Trigger: author instruction after the Chapter 003 draft review. Applied by the canon-amendment worker under the author's `[CANON AMENDMENT]` authority.
- What the author ordered: a move name is tied to the weapon type and to the level and grade of the move, never to the situation in which it is called; one or two words, at most 5 percent of a manual's moves at three words (Catastrophic and Mythic may use three words but are still mostly two), never routinely three or more; the name carries the impact of the attack; Basic and Intermediate simpler, Advanced and Elite more impactful, Signature and Fusion highly impactful, Catastrophic and Mythic the highest; generic and weapon-based names from Mortal Tier 1 up to Mortal Tier 2 Senior level, generic, weapon-anchored, cultivation-anchored or combined from Mortal Tier 2 Warrior level upward; a name fits the move's function (heal, scout, defend, control, mobility and the rest); a name never names a place, a setting, a tactic or an enemy; the old name is kept in the row's notes when a name is replaced; one grandfathered name, `Open Guard Reading Cut` (Varys's personal move, written in the frozen Chapter 001). The Part 6 Area 6.2 patterns and their three-word-and-longer examples are illustrations, not minimum lengths.
- Evidence read first (read-only survey of 2026-10-08, scratchpad): 39,990 moves (39,866 generic and 124 personal) in 4,358 manuals; names by whitespace word count: 1 word, 6; 2 words, 4,253 (10.7 percent); 3 words, 25,529 (64.0 percent); 4 words, 8,497 (21.3 percent); 5 or more, 1,581 (4.0 percent). The stock therefore predates the law almost wholly.
