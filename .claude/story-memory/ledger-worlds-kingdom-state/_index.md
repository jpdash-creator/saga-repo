# ledger-worlds-kingdom-state — Ledger Index

**Ledger Domain:** World Systems — Kingdom-State (the political, military, economic and institutional state of each kingdom)
**Governed By:** `guardrails/ledger/guardrail-ledger-update-law.md`, Part 37 (Ledger Schema Law), Part 13 (Location Law), Part 22 (Faction Law), Part 31 (Synergy Law)
**Template:** Part 37 Area 2.5 — Schema Template: Kingdom-State Ledger
**Folder:** `ledger-worlds-kingdom-state/`
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-20
**Index Version:** 10.0
**Total Entries:** 6 (plus one placeholder row with no file: the unnamed third kingdom)

> **Purpose:** This folder holds one file per kingdom: its classification, military and political power, treaties, institutions, economy and notable characters, so that a kingdom's state reads the same in every Area. It is the one owner of a ruling state's Technology Tier, Cultivation Ceiling, FPT and CS%/TS% (Part 37 Area 2.6, One owner for each number). It records the state of the polity and does not repeat the place itself (the location ledger) or the faction that rules it (the faction ledger).

> **Update Protocol:** Per `guardrails/ledger/guardrail-ledger-update-law.md`, Part 37 Threshold L-2 and the Area 2.5 template: the Pre-Write Ledger Check reads the kingdom file before any Area where the kingdom's rulers, forces, treaties, institutions or economy are in play. A kingdom file is created when a named kingdom, empire, polity or governing entity is first named in an Area (Threshold L-2). Living fields (B/L and L sections) are rewritten at Gate 2 by the Post-Write Ledger Update when an Area changes them; Baseline fields change only by Canon Amendment.

> **Mandatory Update Triggers:**
> - A new named kingdom, empire or polity appears in an Area
> - A ruler dies, abdicates or is replaced
> - A treaty is signed, broken or renewed
> - A military event, political crisis or change of posture
> - A significant economic event, or a dependency starts or ends
> - An institution (academy, guild, information service) is named or changes
> - A change to Technology Tier, Cultivation Ceiling, FPT or CS%/TS% (joint update with the faction file)

> **Marker Legend:** These markers appear in the entries of this ledger. Open cells otherwise carry `OPEN-AREA` or `OPEN-AUTHOR` (Part 37 Area 5).
> - `[ASKED]`: the author chose this value.
> - `[DERIVED]`: the drafter inferred this value from cited evidence and the author reviewed it in the digest without overriding it (Part 39 Rule 39.3.3.1); it is pending only when the entry also says flagged for author confirmation.
> - `[AUTHOR-PENDING]`: a question about this value has been raised and is not yet answered.
> - `[MARKED SLOT]`: a slot left empty on purpose until the author rules.
> - "flag, do not invent": when a scene needs the value, the prose may establish one small fact at a time under Part 28 Area 17, logged in this ledger and offered to the author as a draft value at the next Section close. A name or a major entity (Part 28 Area 20.4) goes to the author first, through the Decision Consultation Gate.
