## Section 9 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

**Schema:** Part 28 Area 17.2 — Kingdom/State tier
**Depth Standard:** Proportionality Exception (Part 28 Area 17.3): populated to what the page and the author's words establish; every other axis reads not established.
**Audit Hook:** ALCA dimension AL-10 (`guardrails/audit/guardrail-alca.md`)

**Who knows the raiders were Brackenhold's (as of 1247.1.14 / PD 14).** The company itself, which its king's few words told; and the reader. The Azure Sky Court does not (Kade, 1247.1.2 / PD 2: "I do not know who they were, and nothing they left behind says."). The Gale Court does not: its lieutenant reads plain coats, long hafts, about two dozen men and a mule-track lost on the shale, and says the ground "does not give a name or a mark or a kingdom" (Area block 05). Nothing on the page shows any other party knowing. *As of 1247.2.20 / PD 48 (Chapter 006, Ref: 1.1.1.006) the answer is unchanged:* the man's one line names the king of Brackenhold and says he is working on and influencing him (to the reader alone, Area block 07), but it does not say the raiders were Brackenhold's, no character in the village or in either court hears it, and no character places the village with Brackenhold; the file of men at the far ford (Area block 05) is seen by Bison alone and named by no one.

**Who knows, as of 1247.2.21 / PD 49 (Chapter 008, Ref: 1.1.1.008).** The answer on the raiders is unchanged: the company itself and the reader know, and no court has been told or has learned it. What the page adds is a suspicion held in private by two people, King Varys and Queen Valia of Azure Sky. Varys names Brackenhold, with Cindervast and Ironshore, to Valia by where each lies, as a suspicion and not knowledge, on positional grounds alone, and says he has no proof of any of the three; Valia asks for no reason beyond the places and agrees to watch it and not feed it. The Azure Sky Court does not know of it (Varys: "I could say as much to the Court, and I will not, because the Court would want a name"), and the Gale Court is sent nothing. It is not the Azure Sky Court learning whose raiders they were.

**Open (flag, do not invent):** the kingdom's borders beyond its placement (ruled 2026-10-18: north-west of the Azure Sky Kingdom, across the lower ford, bordering Azure Sky and the Ember Reach polities) and how the Gale frontier at the foot of the Windward Range (Section 1, the author's answer of 2026-10-18) joins it; its capital; its tier and class; its forces beyond the Redhaft Vanguard and how the Vanguard sits among them; its relations with any other kingdom; the king's age, tier and arc (`osgar-brack.md`).
