## Section 3 — Military and Political Power (B/L)

**Military Organization:** Not established. One formation of the kingdom is shown, the Redhaft Vanguard (`ledger-people-society-faction/redhaft-vanguard.md`, which holds its code and what the page shows of it); its place among the kingdom's forces is OPEN-AUTHOR. At Chapter 005 (Ref: 1.1.1.005) the same company is shown a second time, in the raid leader's own viewpoint, as twenty-four men with Hadric Stoll as one of its two squad leaders. The kingdom's manual stock is held in the Manual Ledger (`ledger-manuals/kingdoms/brackenhold.csv`), which owns every value of it; nothing of it is copied here.
**Cultivation Force Tier:** Not established. The one company shown is of Warriors, line fighters at Warrior Level 3 to 5 with squad leaders and a raid leader above them (the formation's file holds the levels).
**Technology Force Tier:** Not established
**Active Conflicts:** None declared. Two hostile acts on record. The first, in the Azure Sky Kingdom: on 1246.12.8 / PD -19 a company of about twenty Warriors in plain coats, with halberds and no mark of any kingdom, took a mine's file on the lower road (`lower-road.md`); the Azure Sky Court does not know whose company it was (Ref: 1.1.1.003). The second, on Gale ground by the page's caravan and patrol: on 1247.1.9 / PD 9 a company of twenty-four (the raid leader's count in his own scene; "about two dozen" in the Gale lieutenant's reading of the ground) took the Whistledeep mine's caravan at Stirrup Ford on the Seam Road, killed its escort of six and its five hands, and took the ore and the strongbox's book and papers (`ledger-worlds-location/stirrup-ford.md`, Ref: 1.1.1.005); the Gale Court hears of it on 1247.1.14 / PD 14 and names no band and no kingdom.
**Political Posture:** What the page gives, in the narration of the company's scene: "a king three years crowned wants a first road"; King Osgar Brack "wanted this one, and the mine at the top of it"; the company "had been told so when it was called together, in the king's own few words, and told one thing more: it must shed no soldier's blood, because a dead soldier would turn a raid into a war, and a war would name the company". The company went to its work "with nothing on it that named anything" (Ref: 1.1.1.003). *(Chapter 005, Ref: 1.1.1.005, adds one line of the king's wish and no more: in the raid leader's narration "Brackenhold's king had asked for this road in few words, and Rask carried the words exactly" (Area block 02), the road being the Seam Road of the ford; no word of the king's, no reason and no order beyond that is given.)* *(Author's ruling 2026-10-18: the Vanguard may kill soldiers and its code provision on this order is removed; the words above are the Chapter 003 page as written. See Section 8.)*
**Who Holds Real Power (versus titled power):** Not established. The king's "own few words" reached the company; the page shows nothing between the crown and the company.
