> **Purpose:** This folder tracks named canonical vehicles that appear in the novel — vessels, craft, and vehicle systems of narrative significance that exceed individual character ownership scope, or that are named and recurrent enough to warrant dedicated tracking. Character-owned personal vehicles are also recorded in Character Ledger Section 8; this folder holds the vehicle-level record for named vessels and fleets.

> **Entry Categories:**
> - **Named Vessel Files:** Individual files for named vehicles (typically SC-III+ or narratively significant smaller craft) — tracks vehicle tier, Scale Class (SC), vehicle type, configuration codes, crew, armament (cross-referenced with ledger-technology-weapon), operational status, and history.
> - **Fleet Registry Files:** For faction fleets of narrative significance — tracks fleet composition by SC class, commanding faction, current deployment, and operational status.

> **Update Protocol:** Per `guardrails/ledger/guardrail-ledger-update-law.md`, the Pre-Write Ledger Check reads vehicle state before any prose where a named vehicle's capabilities or availability are plot-relevant. Vehicle acquisition, loss, significant modification, destruction, and change of commanding officer trigger mandatory Post-Write Ledger Update. Character Ledger Section 8 and this folder are both updated for any state change involving a character's named vessel.

> **Command Interface:** [STATUS] (C-13) can report key vehicle asset states from this ledger.

> **CS%/TS% Tracking:** CS% (Cultivation Synergy %) and TS% (Technology Synergy %) for Named Vehicles are Base Information (B) at ledger entry creation per Part 31 Area 21.12, with Living Information (L) updates triggered by CHV class upgrade, soul-interface formation, or major structural modification. CS% is governed by CHV Class (CHV-A through CHV-E) and formation array drive density; TS% is governed by Scale Class (SC-I through SC-VII) and technology tier of manufactured systems. The registry table includes CS%/TS% and CHV Class columns for quick reference. For the Synergy Dawncraft ranges (Model C/D pilot-integrated), see Part 31 Area 21.12.

> **Marker Legend:** These markers appear in the entries of this ledger. Open cells otherwise carry `OPEN-AREA` or `OPEN-AUTHOR` (Part 37 Area 5).
> - `[ASKED]`: the author chose this value.
> - `[DERIVED]`: the drafter inferred this value from cited evidence and the author reviewed it in the digest without overriding it (Part 39 Rule 39.3.3.1); it is pending only when the entry also says flagged for author confirmation.
> - `[AUTHOR-PENDING]`: a question about this value has been raised and is not yet answered.
> - `[MARKED SLOT]`: a slot left empty on purpose until the author rules.
> - "flag, do not invent": when a scene needs the value, the prose may establish one small fact at a time under Part 28 Area 17, logged in this ledger and offered to the author as a draft value at the next Section close. A name or a major entity (Part 28 Area 20.4) goes to the author first, through the Decision Consultation Gate.

## Part 28 Area 17 — Lore Section Schema (Vehicle Domain)

**Governing Law:** Part 28 Area 17 (Lore Creation Law). **Audit Hook:** ALCA dimension AL-10 (`guardrails/audit/guardrail-alca.md`) (Lore Completeness Alignment).

**Mandate:** Every named vessel, fleet, and narratively significant vehicle must carry a dedicated **Lore (Part 28 Area 17)** section, placed after the template's numbered sections, populated to the Breadth-and-Depth Standard.

**Required Axes (Minimum Lore Fields per Part 28 Area 17.2 — Vehicle):**

1. **Designer** — who engineered it; named lead designer or design house; governing Part 32 CT-Band tier.
2. **Commission Date** — when built; commissioning authority; named commissioning event if notable.
3. **Operator Lineage** — who has piloted/owned it; named captains and crew where known.
4. **Distinguishing Features** — physical and functional signature; named hull paint, named call-sign, named quirks of handling, named modifications.
5. **Known Missions** — events it has participated in; named battles, named expeditions, named transport runs.

**Depth Test (Part 28 Area 17.3):** "Can I write a pacing-appropriate immersion scene (Part 28 Area 17.3) aboard or about this vehicle using only what is in the ledger?" If yes, depth is satisfied.

**Silent Overwrite Prohibition (Part 28 Area 17.5):** Once vehicle lore is recorded, it is fixed under the Establishment Threshold.
