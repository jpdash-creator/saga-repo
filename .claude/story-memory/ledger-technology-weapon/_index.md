> **Purpose:** This folder tracks named canonical weapons of narrative significance — Named Weapons (character-bonded or culturally significant weapons), weapon systems of political or military importance, and weapon stockpiles that are plot elements. Character weapon loadouts are also recorded in Character Ledger Section 3 (Power Snapshot) and Section 8 (Resources and Possessions); this folder holds the weapon-level record for Named Weapons and significant weapon systems.

> **Entry Categories:**
> - **Named Weapon Files:** Individual files for Named Weapons — tracks weapon tier (WS class, WU class), weapon type (Part 33 taxonomy), current holder, Soul Bond status if applicable, capabilities, and narrative history.
> - **Weapon System Files:** For significant weapon systems (formation weapons, faction arsenal entries, vehicle-mounted weapon arrays) — tracks WS class, controlling faction, operational status, and deployment history.
> - **Weapon Stockpile Files:** Significant weapon caches or arsenals that are narrative plot elements — tracks WS class distribution, faction control, location, and access status.

> **Update Protocol:** Per `guardrails/ledger/guardrail-ledger-update-law.md`, the Pre-Write Ledger Check reads weapon state from Character Ledger Section 3 (personal loadout) and this folder (Named Weapons, weapon systems) before any prose where weapon deployment is relevant. Named weapon acquisition, transfer, or destruction triggers mandatory Post-Write Ledger Update to both Character Ledger Section 8 and this folder.

> **Rule 33.11.1 Interface:** Weapon discharge events (Rule 33.11.1 — CR-3 hostile act) trigger Post-Write Ledger Update to the relevant character's Canon Events Log (Section 10) and may generate CSS consequence events requiring [CONSEQUENCE AUDIT] (C-18).

> **CS%/TS% Tracking:** CS% (Cultivation Synergy %) and TS% (Technology Synergy %) for Named Weapons are Base Information (B) established at ledger entry creation per Part 31 Area 21.11, with Living Information (L) updates triggered by Soul Bond formation, T-SYN class upgrade, or structural modification. CS% is governed by weapon type (T-SYN class, formation array integration, Soul Bond status); TS% is governed by WS Scale Class and technology tier of manufactured components. The registry table includes CS%/TS% columns for quick reference against the full weapon ledger file.

## Part 28 Area 17 — Lore Section Schema (Weapon Domain)

**Governing Law:** Part 28 Area 17 (Lore Creation Law). **Audit Hook:** ALCA dimension AL-10 (`guardrails/audit/guardrail-alca.md`) (Lore Completeness Alignment).

**Mandate:** Every Named Weapon, weapon system, and significant weapon stockpile must carry a dedicated **Lore (Part 28 Area 17)** section, placed after the template's numbered sections, populated to the Breadth-and-Depth Standard.

**Required Axes (Minimum Lore Fields per Part 28 Area 17.2 — Weapon):**

1. **Smith** — who made it; named forge, named master, apprentice lineage if known; under what conditions.
2. **Commission Story** — why it was made; for whom; what the making cost (material, spiritual, or human cost).
3. **Transfer History** — ownership lineage; named prior wielders; their fates.
4. **Named-Move Affinity** — Part 6 Named Moves the weapon is shaped for, if any; moves that are shaped for this weapon specifically.
5. **Grade** — per Part 33 WS classification.
6. **Distinguishing Marks** — physical features that identify this particular weapon (inscriptions, named materials, named decorations, named damage scars).

**Depth Test (Part 28 Area 17.3):** "Can I write a pacing-appropriate immersion scene (Part 28 Area 17.3) in which this weapon is drawn, named, or decisive using only what is in the ledger?" If yes, depth is satisfied.

**Silent Overwrite Prohibition (Part 28 Area 17.5):** Once weapon lore is recorded, it is fixed under the Establishment Threshold.
