2. **Category density** = Part 35 Area 2.1 Step 1/Step 2 procedure. The Area is divided into
   thematic content blocks by counted word extent; each of the eleven categories is then scored
   as the proportion of the Area's word count **meaningfully engaged** in that category.
   Per **CDL2-P2/P5** the categories are **lenses, not buckets** — a word counted toward one
   category is never withheld from another, and totals routinely exceed 100%.
3. **Section aggregate** = Part 35 Area 2.2 weighted formula,
   `Σ(wordcount_i × score_i) / Σ(wordcount_i)`, over the Areas of the Section. A Chapter
   aggregate is the same formula over the Areas of one Chapter. It runs on the whole-number Area
   scores of the table below and the result is rounded to the nearest whole point. A whole-built
   Chapter holds one row and enters the formula once, weighted by its own word count (Part 35
   Area 4.1a as amended 2026-10-07).
4. **Reading conventions held fixed pass to pass.** These state how the Area 1.2 definitions
   were read when the first Area was scored. They add no rule, category or weight, and a later
   pass reads the same way so that figures stay comparable:
   - A word counts toward every category it meaningfully engages (CDL2-P2). Full engagement
     counts in full; partial engagement (a passage that touches the category only in part)
     counts at partial weight, about half unless stated below; no engagement counts zero.
   - **LORE & WORLD-BUILDING:** places, peoples, infrastructure and daily texture rendered in
     use (setting, weather, the city waking, the old wire as part of the world's fabric). A
     character's own stance or movement does not count.
   - **Solo practice with no foe (forms, breath, one cut):** COMBAT counts the sword-work at
     partial weight (about 60 to 90%, because no foe is present and nothing is struck);
     CULTIVATION GROWTH counts breath work and qi use in full and weapon forms at half, because
     nothing advances; NAMED MOVES & TECHNIQUES counts the named move's three layers (the state
     before, the act, the aftermath) in full and unnamed forms at about 20%.
   - **TRAVEL & DISCOVERY:** movement through the world, including a traveller seen on a road,
     and a character's first encounter with the unknown. A stationary survey of a known home
     reads as LORE & WORLD-BUILDING.
   - **CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT:** change, choice and cost shown on the page. A character
     established at his entry state, with a contradiction planted and no change shown, scores low.
   - **EMOTIONS:** felt state carried by body, sense and hesitation counts in full; the steady
     rhythm of a grounded scene counts at half; description of the world counts zero.
   - **CDL2-P9 floor:** a category counts as meaningful content when a distinct passage of about
     100 words (about 5% of a 2,000-word Area) engages it.
