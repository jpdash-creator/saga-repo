**Sacred Ground Status:** Not yet established — mountain passes are sites of Windward Range pilgrimage tradition (non-sect cultural rite); to be formally established in prose

**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:** Visible from the Azure Sky Palace platform on clear mornings as *"a dark and irregular line above the plain."* The air above the passes moves in patterns that experienced cultivators can read for weather prediction. The range is the source of the Lyraen River's headwaters.
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** A pilgrimage tradition — citizens of many kingdoms undertake a pilgrimage to one or more Windward Range passes as a personal threshold rite; routes protected together under the Northern Accord; the range is the northern boundary of civilization on the continent's central spine

**Schema:** Part 28 Area 17.2 — Location Domain (mountain system tier)

### 5.1 — Geographic Extent (axis 1; as shown so far)

**Shown in Area 01 (Ref: 1.1.1.001.01), from the Azure Sky Palace's west-edge platform.** The Range lies along the north as the far edge of what can be seen: in the last dark hour it is a dark, irregular line, no blacker than the sky above it but surer, with a few stars over it. Between the Range and the city lies the Azure Sky Kingdom's plain (`azorion.md` §5.1), and a pale road crosses the plain and runs out toward the Range. Nothing north of the Range's near edge is shown: no pass, pilgrim, patrol, formation, village or forest is seen or named. The distance from the platform to the Range is not stated.

**Ruling, 2026-10-18 (`[ASKED]` Author ruling, 2026-10-18 (map consultation)), verbatim:** **"One range running west to east along the north of both kingdoms. The Windward Pass cuts its southern flank north-north-east of Azorion; the Aerie Plateau is tucked under its eastern stretch; Gale's 'climb' is through the Stormbreak Foothills on the Range's southern side."** The pass, the plateau and the foothills are placed against the Range as the ruling says (`windward-pass.md` Section 2, `aerie-plateau.md` §5.1, `gale-kingdom.md` §5.1). The ruling sets no length and no distance, and the distance from the platform to the Range is still not stated.

### 5.2 — Distinctive Sensory Signature (axis 12; as shown so far)

As the dark thins, the single line comes apart into ridges, each a different black. Before first light the last stars go out along its crest, and the sky goes from black to slate. At first light its highest edge draws a thin line of the same white that the roofs of Azorion catch, and holds it: the one hard line on the bright side of the view.

### 5.3 — Climate and Seasonal Texture (axis 13; Part 13 Area 27)

**Established:** the Range is seen on the first morning of spring at the last dark hour (Azorion's in-world season names are set in `azorion.md` §5.14), with a few stars over it. **Not established:** the Range's own climate, its weather and winds, and its passes' seasonal access (OPEN-AREA).

**Added at Chapter 005 (Ref: 1.1.1.005), from Gale's side.** In the first days of spring, on 1247.1.9 / PD 9, "the snow" was "only now going off the slopes below Shalegate Pass" (Area block 01). On 1247.1.12 / PD 12 the evening wind presses along the foot of the Range in "the long steady press ... where the mountain narrowed it to one course", and "the weather that sat on the Range" hung over the two northernmost masts of the Kite Line, which stood silent; at dusk the Range was "a darker dark with weather sitting on it" (Area blocks 03 and 04). On the night of 1247.1.11 / PD 11 "the weather came down on the Range" and stopped the Kite Line at the plateau's north edge (the lieutenant's count, Area block 05). The Range's winds and weather are otherwise not established.

**Added at Chapter 008 (Ref: 1.1.1.008), from the Aerie Palace on the evening of 1247.2.21 / PD 49.** A front stands over the Range: the king reads from the wind and the wards that "by morning the plateau would have whatever sat on the Range tonight, a dark mass in the window under a darker edge of cloud", and that "the dark on the Range will be on Varys's roofs tonight and on ours by morning" (Area block 03). "A seam of light opened along the underside of the cloud, far to the north, and closed", and the air that comes in at the curtain "had rain in it, cold and thin"; later the queen sees the Range "as one solid shape, a long dark leaning on a darker dark, its far side hidden", with the seam of light "low in the north" and the edge of the cloud neither moving nor at rest, and says "The same sky will be here by morning" (Area block 04). No rain falls on the plateau on the page.

### 5.5 — Shalegate Pass, and the Range as Seen from Gale (Chapter 005; Ref: 1.1.1.005)

**Shalegate Pass** is named once, in the first scene (Area block 01), in the captain's reading of the morning of 1247.1.9 / PD 9: "with the snow only now going off the slopes below Shalegate Pass." That is the whole of the page: the pass has slopes below it, those slopes held snow in the first days of spring, and the Seam Road and Stirrup Ford lie in the country below (`seam-road.md`, `stirrup-ford.md`). The page does not say that it is a pass of the Windward Range; the Chapter Spec's plan reads it as the high pass of the Range above the ford (Chapter Spec 1.1.1.005, Skipped: a planner's pick, "the author may swap any"), and the registry gives the Range as its parent on that reading only. It is tied to no track, no road and no event, and no one passes through it on the page. Whether the Windward Trail runs through it is OPEN-AUTHOR, the question `aerie-plateau.md` §5.1 holds for the Windward Pass. Its height, length, make and holder are not established.

**The Range from the Aerie Palace.** Seen from the open edge of the palace's terrace on 1247.1.12 / PD 12, the Range lies to the north of the plateau and its palace: the wind presses along its foot (§5.3), a line of masts of the Kite Line stands on the high ground of the plateau, and the weather that sits on the Range silences the last two masts to the north (`aerie-plateau.md` §5.4). Nothing is said of its height, its passes beyond Shalegate Pass, or any patrol of it in Chapter 005; the Northern Accord Expeditionary Wing is not named. No ground north of the Range's near edge is shown, named or hinted at.

**The Range from the family wing (Chapter 008; Ref: 1.1.1.008).** On the evening of 1247.2.21 / PD 49 the Range is the dark in the window of the bedroom of the Aerie Palace's family wing (the Range stands in that window; see `aerie-palace.md` §5.5): a long dark mass under a darker edge of cloud, its far side hidden, with the seam of light low in the north. Nothing is said of its height, its passes or any patrol, and no ground north of the Range's near edge is shown, named or hinted at.

**Narrative summary (as of Area 01).** From the capital the Windward Range is the far northern edge of the world: a dark, ragged line that is nearly one with the sky at the last dark hour, that separates into ridges as the dark thins, and that takes a thin white line along its highest edge when the first light comes, the one hard line on the bright side of the view. A pale road across the plain runs out toward it. What lies in its passes and beyond is held by the sections above and by the ledger, and has not yet been shown in prose. *(Added at Chapter 005, Ref: 1.1.1.005: from Gale the Range is the weather over the plateau's north, the wind pressing along its foot and the cause of the silence of the Kite Line's northern masts; Shalegate Pass is named once, with snow going off the slopes below it (§5.5).)* *(Added at Chapter 008, Ref: 1.1.1.008: at night from the Aerie bedroom window the Range is a dark mass under a front, with a seam of light low in the north, and the front is read as reaching Azure Sky's roofs that night and the plateau by morning (§5.3, §5.5).)*
