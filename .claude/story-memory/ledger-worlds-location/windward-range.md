### 5.1 — Geographic Extent (axis 1; as shown so far)

**Shown in Area 01 (Ref: 1.1.1.001.01), from the Azure Sky Palace's west-edge platform.** The Range lies along the north as the far edge of what can be seen: in the last dark hour it is a dark, irregular line, no blacker than the sky above it but surer, with a few stars over it. Between the Range and the city lies the Azure Sky Kingdom's plain (`azorion.md` §5.1), and a pale road crosses the plain and runs out toward the Range. Nothing north of the Range's near edge is shown: no pass, pilgrim, patrol, formation, village or forest is seen or named. The distance from the platform to the Range is not stated.

**Ruling, 2026-10-18 (`[ASKED]` Author ruling, 2026-10-18 (map consultation)), verbatim:** **"One range running west to east along the north of both kingdoms. The Windward Pass cuts its southern flank north-north-east of Azorion; the Aerie Plateau is tucked under its eastern stretch; Gale's 'climb' is through the Stormbreak Foothills on the Range's southern side."** The pass, the plateau and the foothills are placed against the Range as the ruling says (`windward-pass.md` Section 2, `aerie-plateau.md` §5.1, `gale-kingdom.md` §5.1). The ruling sets no length and no distance, and the distance from the platform to the Range is still not stated.

### 5.2 — Distinctive Sensory Signature (axis 12; as shown so far)

As the dark thins, the single line comes apart into ridges, each a different black. Before first light the last stars go out along its crest, and the sky goes from black to slate. At first light its highest edge draws a thin line of the same white that the roofs of Azorion catch, and holds it: the one hard line on the bright side of the view.

### 5.3 — Climate and Seasonal Texture (axis 13; Part 13 Area 27)

**Established:** the Range is seen on the first morning of spring at the last dark hour (Azorion's in-world season names are set in `azorion.md` §5.14), with a few stars over it. **Not established:** the Range's own climate, its weather and winds, and its passes' seasonal access (OPEN-AREA).

### 5.4 — Hazards and Dangers to a Visitor (axis 14)

Not established (OPEN-AREA): nothing of the Range's terrain, weather, wildlife, ambient energy, patrols or frontier risk is shown in Area 01.

**Narrative summary (as of Area 01).** From the capital the Windward Range is the far northern edge of the world: a dark, ragged line that is nearly one with the sky at the last dark hour, that separates into ridges as the dark thins, and that takes a thin white line along its highest edge when the first light comes, the one hard line on the bright side of the view. A pale road across the plain runs out toward it. What lies in its passes and beyond is held by the sections above and by the ledger, and has not yet been shown in prose.
