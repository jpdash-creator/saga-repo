### 5.1 — Geography

The Aerie Plateau is the high ground of the central Gale Kingdom: a broad, wind-scoured tableland that lifts out of the Stormbreak Foothills on its west and south, runs under the Windward Range along its north edge, and tips away to the east into the Tempest Valleys and so down to the Long Coast. It lies in the Central Highlands at an altitude that thins the air and keeps the nights cold, and its ambient wind-energy is Class 4 to 5. The ground is stone and short grass, worked into stepped terraces on the slopes where the plateau clans grow what will grow, a modest yield. The prevailing wind is easterly, coming up the Tempest Valleys, and every building on the plateau is low and set to ride it. The plateau holds the kingdom's people in their densest numbers: the clan holdings, the wind-academy monasteries, the markets, and the capital city, Highgale (named by the author's ruling of 2026-10-18, `[ASKED]`; cross-ref: highgale.md), beneath the royal seat. The Aerie Plateau Governor, a direct appointee of the Wind-Crown, governs it. The Windward Trail, the seasonal highland trail across the Range, is reached from its north edge; whether the Trail runs through the Windward Pass is OPEN-AUTHOR. **Added at Chapter 005 (Ref: 1.1.1.005):** the Trail "had opened to horses only days before" 1247.1.9 / PD 9 (Area block 01; the page gives no day), so it is open to horses in the first days of spring; the Ridgewatch patrol's ride up the Mule Stair and the Seam Road on 1247.1.11 / PD 11 was its first since the Trail opened (Area block 05); whether the Trail runs through Shalegate Pass is likewise OPEN-AUTHOR (`windward-range.md` §5.5). By the author's ruling of 2026-10-18 (`[ASKED]` Author ruling, 2026-10-18 (map consultation)), the plateau is tucked under the Windward Range's eastern stretch (`windward-range.md` §5.1).

### 5.2 — Three Landmarks

- **Aerion's Landing Stone.** The founding stone of the kingdom, where Aerion gathered the plateau clans and the Gale Oath-Binding was sworn. The palace file holds its physical detail and its Stone Vigil garrison. For the plateau it is the place the people return to: the Festival of the Gale Ascent ends its ceremonial flight here, and the kingdom's sense of itself begins here.
- **The Terraces** (proposed working name). The stepped fields cut into the plateau slopes, worked by clan families with wind-cultivation. Each stair of the slope is a field, a wall and a wind-break at once. The yield is modest, and so is the tithe it pays.
- **The Kite Line** (a working name when proposed; the page of Chapter 005 uses it, Ref: 1.1.1.005, and it is registered as a named item, `ledger-named-items/_index.md`). The chain of storm-kite relay masts that crosses the plateau, each on a high point, carrying signals from mast to mast on inscribed kite-chains. It runs only when the wind allows and is weather-limited; when it fails, the kingdom falls back on couriers and on signaling instruments bought from Azure Sky. What the page of Chapter 005 adds, its northern end and its stop in bad weather, is at §5.4.

**The capital city.** Beneath the royal seat, on the plateau floor, stands the Gale capital city, **Highgale**, about 12 km east-south-east of the Aerie Palace (DIST-011, SKETCH). The name is the author's (`[ASKED]` Author ruling, 2026-10-18 (map consultation)): "The high city of the Gale, in the plain-English register of Stormbreak, Long Coast and Wind Crossing." Its record is `highgale.md`; it is first on the page at Chapter 005 (Ref: 1.1.1.005), where the king reads it from the palace terrace by its eight districts (`highgale.md` §5.3).

### 5.3 — Sensory Signature

Wind first and last, steady from the east, with a dry rattle in the short grass and a crack of banners on every ridge. The air is thin, cold and very clear. The scent of storm-tempered herbs drying on racks in the market squares comes and goes with the wind. Over everything there is the high thin whistle of storm-kite tails, from the Kite Line and, in autumn, from the storm-kites every household sends up. The colors are storm-grey stone, silver-grey grass and dragon-green on household cloth. Buildings are low and pale. A cultivator who is not of the wind finds the plateau subtly inhospitable, an unplaceable unease rather than anything a person could point to.

### 5.4 — As Shown at Chapter 005 (Ref: 1.1.1.005)

**The plateau read by its wind (Area block 03).** On the evening of 1247.1.12 / PD 12 the east wind stands "cold and thin and clear" and comes up out of the Tempest Valleys; read from the open edge of the palace terrace, it gives the plateau in order: the long steady press of wind along the foot of the Windward Range, where the mountain narrows it to one course; a warmer current off the terraced fields, each ledge of them handing back a little of the sun; and the high ground where the masts of the Kite Line stand one after another. Below the masts the road runs down to Highgale, "an hour at a rider's pace to the east-south-east" (DIST-011 in `ledger-worlds-distance/_index.md`), and the city lies at the end of it on the plateau floor "with its smoke leaning west" (`highgale.md`). The morning easterly of 1247.1.14 / PD 14 crosses the Court "clear and cold, with the first warmth of the sun in it" (Area block 05).

**The Kite Line, its northern end and its stop (Area blocks 03 to 06).** The line is a row of masts on the plateau's high ground, "one after another, each whistling on its kite-chains in the same thin note". On 1247.1.12 / PD 12 all but the last two, to the north, whistle; those two "hung silent under the weather that sat on the Range" and are still silent at dusk. The weather that "came down on the Range" on the night of 1247.1.11 / PD 11 "stopped the Kite Line at the plateau's north edge", so that no word could go ahead of the lieutenant riding to the palace; the line stops in bad weather at the plateau's north edge, and it "does not reach the mine road". On 1247.1.14 / PD 14, in the afternoon, "a mast took up its note and let it go" on the high ground (Area block 06). How many masts stand, what the line carries and how it is kept are not established (the Aerie Artificers' College builds its frames, `gale-kingdom.md` §5.6).

**The terraces and a case of water (Area blocks 03 and 05).** The terraced fields are stepped, with walls and drains. Ottar Haug's terrace wall "stood forty years" until it came down across his field after his neighbour Knut Dahl dug a drain above it in the autumn and the thaw followed; Dahl says he dug the drain "where the ground fell away from his own terrace" and that nobody had told him a spring ran under it, and he says the spring comes out below Haug's wall and that Haug's father once complained of a wet corner. The king hears Haug beside the Stone Ring on 1247.1.12 / PD 12 and hears Dahl at the Court on the morning of 1247.1.14 / PD 14, and puts the rest off for a letter; it is not settled on the page.

**Gliders and the north road.** One-seat gliders of ash and kite-cloth come in "low over the terraced fields from the west" and run out their speed along the flags at the palace terrace's edge (Area blocks 03 and 05). The old wire stops at "the corridor"; the rest of the way to the palace is by glider and by hand (Area blocks 03 and 05). The Ridgewatch lieutenant comes to the palace "from the north road at first light" on 1247.1.14 / PD 14, after three days in the saddle (Area block 05; DIST-016). The Wind Crossing brings petitioners up "from the plateau road" (Area block 03; `aerie-palace.md` §5.2).
