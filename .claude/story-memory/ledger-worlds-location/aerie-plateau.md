### 5.1 — Geography

The Aerie Plateau is the high ground of the central Gale Kingdom: a broad, wind-scoured tableland that lifts out of the Stormbreak Foothills on its west and south, runs under the Windward Range along its north edge, and tips away to the east into the Tempest Valleys and so down to the Long Coast. It lies in the Central Highlands at an altitude that thins the air and keeps the nights cold, and its ambient wind-energy is Class 4 to 5. The ground is stone and short grass, worked into stepped terraces on the slopes where the plateau clans grow what will grow, a modest yield. The prevailing wind is easterly, coming up the Tempest Valleys, and every building on the plateau is low and set to ride it. The plateau holds the kingdom's people in their densest numbers: the clan holdings, the wind-academy monasteries, the markets, and the capital city, Highgale (named by the author's ruling of 2026-10-18, `[ASKED]`; cross-ref: highgale.md), beneath the royal seat. The Aerie Plateau Governor, a direct appointee of the Wind-Crown, governs it. The Windward Trail, the seasonal highland trail across the Range, is reached from its north edge; whether the Trail runs through the Windward Pass is OPEN-AUTHOR. By the author's ruling of 2026-10-18 (`[ASKED]` Author ruling, 2026-10-18 (map consultation)), the plateau is tucked under the Windward Range's eastern stretch (`windward-range.md` §5.1).

### 5.2 — Three Landmarks

- **Aerion's Landing Stone.** The founding stone of the kingdom, where Aerion gathered the plateau clans and the Gale Oath-Binding was sworn. The palace file holds its physical detail and its Stone Vigil garrison. For the plateau it is the place the people return to: the Festival of the Gale Ascent ends its ceremonial flight here, and the kingdom's sense of itself begins here.
- **The Terraces** (proposed working name). The stepped fields cut into the plateau slopes, worked by clan families with wind-cultivation. Each stair of the slope is a field, a wall and a wind-break at once. The yield is modest, and so is the tithe it pays.
- **The Kite Line** (proposed working name). The chain of storm-kite relay masts that crosses the plateau, each on a high point, carrying signals from mast to mast on inscribed kite-chains. It runs only when the wind allows and is weather-limited; when it fails, the kingdom falls back on couriers and on signaling instruments bought from Azure Sky.

**The capital city.** Beneath the royal seat, on the plateau floor, stands the Gale capital city, **Highgale**, about 12 km east-south-east of the Aerie Palace (DIST-011, SKETCH). The name is the author's (`[ASKED]` Author ruling, 2026-10-18 (map consultation)): "The high city of the Gale, in the plain-English register of Stormbreak, Long Coast and Wind Crossing." Its record is `highgale.md`; it is not yet on the page.

### 5.3 — Sensory Signature

Wind first and last, steady from the east, with a dry rattle in the short grass and a crack of banners on every ridge. The air is thin, cold and very clear. The scent of storm-tempered herbs drying on racks in the market squares comes and goes with the wind. Over everything there is the high thin whistle of storm-kite tails, from the Kite Line and, in autumn, from the storm-kites every household sends up. The colors are storm-grey stone, silver-grey grass and dragon-green on household cloth. Buildings are low and pale. A cultivator who is not of the wind finds the plateau subtly inhospitable, an unplaceable unease rather than anything a person could point to.
