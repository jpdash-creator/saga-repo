## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** Nivalis
**Common Name:** Nivalis / The Ice Planet / The Frozen World
**Location Tier (Part 13):** Tier 7 — World
**Cosmological Area (Part 13):** Area 15 — World Classification System
**Part 13 World Class:** W-4 Affinity World — Nivalis is defined entirely by an extreme cold/ice affinity that renders the planetary environment lethally hostile to any cultivator below Core Formation realm. The planet's ambient energy is cold-dominant in both character and physical expression. It is not merely cold in climate — the ambient energy of the planet itself is lethally hostile to sub-Core cultivators at the biological level.
**Sacred Ground Status:** Not yet established — to be defined in prose
**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:**
Nivalis is a frigid ice planet. Its surface, atmosphere, and ambient energy environment are all ice/cold dominant. The planetary conditions are so extreme that only Core Formation realm cultivators and above possess the physical fortitude, energy output, and Core stability required to survive entry and prolonged exposure. Lower-realm cultivators — regardless of protection technology or equipment — cannot survive on Nivalis's surface. The mechanisms of this lethality (ambient energy suppression of sub-Core cultivators, extreme physical temperature, dimensional cold, or a combination) are not yet fully established in prose.

**Entry Restriction:**
> Only Core Formation realm cultivators and above may enter Nivalis. Sub-Core cultivators (Mortal Tier 1, Mortal Tier 2, and Xiantian Realm: Demigod, Void God, True God, Absolute God) cannot survive on Nivalis regardless of protective equipment. This is a natural planetary restriction, not a sect or political imposition.

**Phase 1 prose-safe phrasing:** Nivalis kills anyone who has not passed the ceiling of the mortal realms; never name Core Formation on the page without a Rule 1.7.2 exception.

**Cultural Association (Part 25):** Unknown — whether Nivalis has any permanent inhabitants or civilization is not yet established. At story start it is classified as an uninhabited frontier planet with a natural lethal restriction.

**Dominant Affinity Presence (Part 4):** Ice / Cold affinity — extreme, pervasive, and environmentally enforced

**Special Zone — FIRMAMENT LOCK (inherited from Sol Star System):**
The Firmament Lock suppression field applies across Nivalis as part of the Sol Star System. System-wide cultivation ceiling: Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid. Technology ceiling: Tier 6.

**Nivalis Local Cultivation Ceiling (Locked Canon):**
> Nivalis's local cultivation cap is **Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid** — identical to the Sol Star System's Firmament Lock ceiling. Nivalis is the Sol System's ceiling-habitable world: it is the only location within the Sol Star System where the full Firmament Lock cap can be reached and sustained. This is consistent with Nivalis's character as the Sol Star System's apex physical environment; the very cold, cultivation-pure, and entry-restricted nature of the planet corresponds to the highest accessible cultivation tier permitted by the Firmament Lock. Any cultivator who reaches Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid anywhere within the Sol Star System must reside on Nivalis (or off-world, beyond the Lock) to sustain that cultivation — no other location within the system permits habitation at that ceiling.
>
> **Access Floor:** Unchanged from the Section 1 entry restriction — only Core Formation realm cultivators and above can enter Nivalis at all; sub-Core cultivators cannot survive Nivalis's environment regardless of equipment.
> **Growth Ceiling:** Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid — the Firmament Lock's hard cap, reached only on Nivalis within the Sol Star System.
>
> **Phase 1 Narrative Ceiling on Nivalis (Part 30):** Ruler Level 9 early — far below Nivalis's local ceiling and, critically, far below Nivalis's access floor (Core Formation). Phase 1 prose does not feature Nivalis as a setting; Nivalis is wholly off-screen during Phase 1 because no Phase 1 character has the cultivation level required to enter. See `sol-star-system.md` Section 2 for the full two-layer ceiling model.
