**Orbiting World:** Saha (cross-ref: saha.md)
**Physical Description:**
A large habitable moon orbiting Saha, comparable in scale to a small world. The moon's environment has been shaped over an extended period by the presence of the Three Moon Sects — Fallen Moon Sect (Space), Full Moon Sect (Time), Dark Moon Sect (Darkness). The convergence of three advanced cultivation powers of distinct and rare affinities has created an ambient energy environment of exceptional quality and unusual character. The moon is formally restricted: only Demigods (Demigod Level 1 and above) and Void Gods and above may enter. This entry restriction is imposed and enforced by the Three Moon Sects collectively.

**Entry Restriction:**
> Only Demigod cultivators of Level 1 and above and Void God realm and above may enter the Moon of Saha. Lower-realm cultivators cannot enter — whether this is enforced by sect formation arrays, a naturally hostile energy environment, or both is not yet established in prose.
>
> **Unaided travel to the Moon:** needs True God or any higher realm, Core Formation and above included (Part 15 Areas 4.2 and 5.3). A Demigod goes only by vessel or formation gate.

**Cultural Association (Part 25):** Cultivation-dominant; sect-governed; highly restricted access culture; the Three Moon Sects define all cultural norms on the moon — outsiders do not reside here

**Dominant Affinity Presence (Part 4):** Three co-dominant affinities —
- **Space Affinity** — Fallen Moon Sect domain
- **Time Affinity** — Full Moon Sect domain
- **Darkness Affinity** — Dark Moon Sect domain
All three affinities are present and active in the ambient environment simultaneously, creating a rare and complex multi-affinity energy landscape

**Special Zone — FIRMAMENT LOCK (inherited from Sol Star System):**
The Firmament Lock suppression field applies across the Moon of Saha as part of the Sol Star System. System-wide cultivation ceiling: Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid. Technology ceiling: Tier 6.

**Moon of Saha Local Cultivation Ceiling (Locked Canon):**
> The Moon of Saha imposes its own local environmental cap beneath the Sol Star System's Firmament Lock ceiling. The maximum cultivation realm that can be reached and sustained on the Moon of Saha is **Core Formation Level 3 High**. This sits above Saha's planetary ceiling (Absolute God Level 8 High) and below the system ceiling (Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid) — the Moon of Saha is the first location within the Sol Star System where the Core realm is accessible to residents, but only up to Core Formation Level 3 High. Cultivators who advance beyond Core Formation Level 3 High cannot remain on the Moon of Saha; further advancement within the system is possible only on Nivalis (which admits up to the system ceiling of Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid). The Three Moon Sects operate at the apex of what the Moon's local ceiling permits — their most senior masters approach but do not exceed Core Formation Level 3 High.
>
> **Phase 1 Narrative Ceiling on the Moon of Saha (Part 30):** Ruler Level 9 early — far below the Moon's local ceiling. Phase 1 prose set on the Moon of Saha (if any occurs — the Moon is primarily background/aspirational in Phase 1 per Part 30) must not render any character above Ruler Level 9 early; higher in-world realms up to the Core Formation Level 3 High Moon cap exist but are off-screen during Phase 1. See `sol-star-system.md` Section 2 for the full two-layer ceiling model.

## Section 3 — Faction/Civilization Control (B/L)

**Controlling Faction(s) (cross-ref: ledger-people-society-faction):**
The Moon of Saha is controlled collectively by the Three Moon Sects — the three strongest sects in the Sol Star System. No single sect holds absolute dominance; they govern the moon as a collective apex power.
- **Fallen Moon Sect** — primary power: Space affinity (cross-ref: fallen-moon-sect.md)
- **Full Moon Sect** — primary power: Time affinity (cross-ref: full-moon-sect.md)
- **Dark Moon Sect** — primary power: Darkness affinity (cross-ref: dark-moon-sect.md)

**Controlling Civilization (cross-ref: ledger-people-society-civilization):** None — the moon is not governed by a civilian civilization; it is governed exclusively by the Three Moon Sects
**Contested Status:** Not contested — the Three Moon Sects hold unchallenged authority over the Moon of Saha within the Sol Star System's power hierarchy
**Known Characters Present (cross-ref: character ledgers):** None of the primary protagonists are present on the Moon of Saha at story start
