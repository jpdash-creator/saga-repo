The air smells of oil and cold metal. The window shows only sky, which at first light has no colour in it yet. Nothing outside the room is visible from it: no courtyard, platform, stair or bridge.

## Section 5 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

**Schema:** Part 28 Area 17.2 — Location Domain (sub-location tier)

**Audit Hook:** ALCA dimension AL-10 (`guardrails/audit/guardrail-alca.md`)

### 5.1 — Function and Jurisdiction

The Peg Room is a small workroom on the household side of the Azure Sky Palace, set out for someone small and plainly Princess Varya's own. It is where she is found at her work at first light on the first morning (Area 1.1.1.001.03). Who arranged it, how long it has been hers, whether she may be there and who else knows of it or of what she does there are not established (OPEN-AREA). The palace block records court and class decorum as not established, and this file writes none.

### 5.2 — Distinctive Sensory Signature (axis 12)

A visitor at the door first meets the smell of oil and cold metal, then the low pegs, with a cloak too long for the room hanging from the nearest one, and the table under the window with its pieces. The sounds are small ones: the stool creaking and going quiet, and the faint knock of metal on wood and wood on metal as pieces are set against each other. The boards answer under a man's weight. At first light the one high window shows only sky with no colour in it. Nothing is said in the room.

### 5.3 — Climate and Seasonal Texture (axis 13)

Not separately established. The household side was cold at first light on the first morning of spring (cross-ref: azure-sky-palace.md §5.3). While she worked, cold reached the room's occupant all at once and she took the long cloak from its peg. Whether the room is heated, and how cold it runs, is not established (OPEN-AREA).

### 5.4 — Hazards and Dangers to a Visitor (axis 14)

None shown. Tools hang on the pegs and small parts of metal and pale wood lie in pieces on the table; no danger is stated or implied. Not established beyond that (OPEN-AREA).

**Not established:** where the name comes from; what the tools are; what the mechanism on the table is for (the author keeps it unnamed, and no purpose is shown or named); whether the room has another door or another occupant; whether the cloak is hers alone; what lies beyond the household corridor from this room.

**Narrative summary (as of Area 03).** The Peg Room is a closet-sized workroom on the household side of the palace, set out for someone small: pegs at a child's height along the outer wall, a long cloak on the one nearest the door, tools on the rest, and under the high window a table with a stool raised on a box. At first light on the first morning Princess Varya sits there with her back half turned to the door, her small hands working a mechanism of metal and pale wood that lies in pieces across the table. The king crosses the doorway and she does not turn; his sense for qi meets nothing of her, for she is eight and her first awakening is still ahead of her. When the cold reaches her she slides down off the box, wraps herself in the cloak and climbs back to her work (Area 1.1.1.001.03).
