### 5.1 — The post below the Twinpost markers, as shown on the page (Chapter 009; Ref: 1.1.1.009)

*(Written at the Gate 2 of 2026-10-23 from the written Chapter and nothing else; each fact is cited by Area block. This section sets no distance, no travel time beyond the page's and no open item, and it does not describe the Twinpost's markers beyond what the page shows of them. The persons and the fight are in the faction ledgers.)*

**The shelf and its works (Area block 01).** The post lies on a shelf below the lip where "the two grey markers of the Twinpost" stand "against the final green of the sky". At its east end is the wire house, a stone house with one window and a door that bangs on the wind (one lamp lit in the window at dusk); at its west end is the glider shed "where the ledge ran out over nothing", with the post's two gliders of ash frame and kite-cloth under its roof; between them lies "the yard of bare stone with the cookfire to one side and the table under its roof", the long table with its benches under a lean-to that "creaked on its poles as the pass wind leaned on it". The racks of blades stand on the back wall of the lean-to, six paces behind the benches (the post tells how they came to be set there: two commanders, each of whom thought the matter too small to argue and left it to the other, Area block 02). Behind the lean-to the bank lies in the lee of the rock shoulder, "a waist-high wall of piled stone twenty paces long", with the timber frames of the shelters standing behind it (Area block 02); a second shelter is named, with a stream below it (Colm Ardley went through the stream "for the water", Area block 01). The south approach is the south road, held at night by a mixed pair; the company came up out of the dusk at the yard's south edge and withdrew toward the trees, and the rear rank stood across the south road to cover it (Area blocks 01 and 04); "the lashings" are the post's edge in the telling ("nobody goes past the lashings", Area block 04; "the shelf is the fight, from the lashings to the ledge", Area block 02). The post keeps no mounts.

**The roads (Area blocks 01, 04 and 05).** The road down to Azorion is a foot road, "three days of gravel and switchbacks", and "every rotation had walked it home"; the Gale road to Highgale is ridden on the Host's relay horses, four days. The company withdrew toward the trees on the south road; the page's direction for its flight is "West ... Down the Reach and into the trees" (Corin Vale's answer to the king, Area block 06). A runner of each kingdom goes up the Pass to the Rotation Camp from the post that night.

**The post's customs, as shown.** Before dark every lashing is re-turned ("because the wind ate rope"); the night's watch is said standing in the open, the south approach and the wire house door each held by a mixed pair, "each is changed at the hour, in low voices, by another mixed pair" (Area block 01). The post's own tale of its racks (above) is the one custom of its history the page gives.

**What the page does not show.** The markers close at hand, the parting of the roads, the Twinpost's own works if any, the Rotation Camp, the Midpoint Array and the Shoulder Stations of `windward-pass.md` §5.2; no distance is stated between the post and the markers beyond "below"; the stream and the second shelter are seen once.
