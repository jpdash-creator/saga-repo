## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** The Twinpost
**Common Name / Aliases:** Twinpost; "the stone post"
**Location Tier (Part 13 Area 2.1):** Sub-World — waypoint marker at the geographic and jurisdictional boundary where the combined Azure Sky/Gale patrol corridor (Windward Pass, within Windward Range) splits from Azure Sky's own descent route through the Windward Reach toward the Sapphire Basin
**Cosmological Area (Part 13):** Above the treeline, at the upper edge of the Windward Reach; adjacent to/continuous with the Windward Pass corridor within Windward Range (cross-ref: windward-pass.md, windward-range.md, azure-sky-kingdom.md §5.1)
**Physical Description:** Two grey markers stand shoulder to shoulder above the treeline, where the combined corridor splits. The point at which the soldiers of **one combined bilateral formation — the Northern Accord Expeditionary Wing**, Azure Sky and Gale serving together — part ways after a shared rotation and go home by different roads: the Gale road continuing east toward the Lyraen crossings and the Aerie Plateau, the Azure Sky road turning south and beginning the descent through the Windward Reach toward the Sapphire Basin. **The parting is of soldiers of one formation, not of two contingents.**
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** A working boundary-marker for the two kingdoms' shared frontier duty under the Northern Accord — not a ceremonial site, but the road-split where the shared corridor ends. How a rotation ends here: OPEN-AREA.
