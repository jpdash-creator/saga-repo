## Section 5 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

### 5.1 — The Ford, and Why the Town Is There

Ashford exists because of a crossing that **works in any month of the year** — the reliability is the town, and the name records the price of making it: ash cut down to clear the ford and to raise the first watchtower. Both halves of that history are **established and immutable**.

### 5.2 — What the Town Sends the Frontier

*"The men it sent him came knowing things about a border that the capital's own garrison could serve thirty years and never learn."* Established as a property of Ashford's people. **Nothing about the capital's garrison is established by it beyond the comparison as written**, and no garrison of Ashford's own is established.
