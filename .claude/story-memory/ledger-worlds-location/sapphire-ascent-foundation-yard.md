## Collective Behavior Code (Part 39 Layer L19) (B/L)

*(Location form, Part 39 Area 41.4. A **pointer block**: it carries the elements the author gave for the floor, cites the Nine Halls Collective Behavior Code for the elements that code holds, and marks the one custom that is the yard's own. Nothing is added to the author's elements.)*

**Code Status:** **Registered 2026-10-17** *(Author ruling, 2026-10-17; Part 39 Rule 39.23.1.5: registered at the ledger step before Chapter Spec 1.1.1.004 is drafted)*. The loud floor, the quiet kept only in cultivation and meditation areas, the guard line and *"not yet"* said and heard plainly are **cited from the Nine Halls Collective Behavior Code** (`ledger-people-society-faction/nine-halls.md`, as amended 2026-10-17), and the address by name is cited from the same file (§6.1b: a student is addressed by name, and the grades are never vocatives). They are that code's text and are not restated as new text here. The custom marked **the yard's own** is not cited to that code.

**Members currently bound:** None named. The place binds anyone standing in it, member or not. Grandmaster Yun Ashvale is the Academy's Headmaster and is bound to the Nine Halls code as its head (`ledger-character-side/yun-ashvale.md` §11.5); the pupils and the staff show conformance, not membership.

**Shared conduct — what every member displays:** None beyond the place's own conduct below.

**Greeting and deference ritual:** Not established for the yard. *(The halls' greeting, the Head Elder's morning round, is the Nine Halls code's and is not restated here.)*
**In-group markers:** Not established beyond the conduct below.
**Conduct toward outsiders:** Not established.
**Conduct when the group is criticized:** Not established.
**Induction conduct:** Not established.
**Enforcement:** Not established. *(The guard line below is a limit on what an instructor or office may do; it is not an enforcement mechanism.)*

**For locations only — conduct the place imposes:**

- **The floor is lively.** Talk, banter, taunting and excitement are allowed, inside the ring too. *(Cited: the Nine Halls code, the floor amendment.)*
- **"Not yet" is said plainly and heard plainly.** *(Cited: the Nine Halls code, NOBODY IS FORCED ACROSS.)*
- **Correction is a movement done beside you.** *(The yard's own custom, not cited to the Nine Halls code: that code no longer carries a demonstration element, *Author ruling, 2026-10-17*.)*
- **The youngest watch from the bench and do not enter the ring.** *(This year's custom, described, and not a rule or a prohibition on any child.)*
- **Everyone is a student here and is addressed by name, with no title.** The princess and prince titles of the two children stay outside. *(Cited: `nine-halls.md` §6.1b for the address by name; the no-title rule on the floor and in the Academy is the author's ruling of 2026-10-17. Open: how far it reaches the office words of the staff, §5.2 item 2.)*
- **The quiet is kept only in cultivation and meditation areas.** *(Cited: the Nine Halls code, the floor amendment.)*
- **The Headmaster is Grandmaster Yun Ashvale.** She rules the first practice bouts of an intake herself; on other days instructors or elders rule. *(Cited: `nine-halls.md` §6.1a.)*

**What is forbidden here by custom rather than by rule:** Nothing is forbidden by rule. By custom, no instructor or office shames a student for pace, and the taunting of the floor is the students' own and is never the halls' lever *(cited: the Nine Halls code, the guard line)*.

**As shown on the page of Chapter 004 (Ref: 1.1.1.004), Area blocks 02 to 06 (instances, not customs):** the floor is lively (the older pupils "called across the rings to one another, mostly insults, mostly glad ones", and the bouts carry banter inside the ring); "not yet" is said plainly (Kerra Tonkin to Varya, "with no apology in it and no scorn"); the youngest sit on the benches, and the Grandmaster says "This year the youngest will watch from the bench"; every pupil is addressed by name and no title is spoken to the children in the yard; and the Headmaster, Yun Ashvale, rules each of the three bouts by a raised hand. **Not shown on the page:** correction as a movement done beside you (no instructor or elder corrects any pupil in the yard in this Chapter), and the quiet of a cultivation or meditation area (none is shown).

**Registered deviations by member:** None. *(Grandmaster Yun Ashvale's seam is registered against the Nine Halls code in her own file and is not restated here.)*
