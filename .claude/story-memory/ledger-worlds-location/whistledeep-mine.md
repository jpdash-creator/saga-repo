## Section 5 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

**Schema:** Part 28 Area 17.2 — Location Domain (Sub-World site)
**Depth Standard:** Proportionality Exception (Part 28 Area 17.3): populated to what the page shows; every other axis reads not established.
**Audit Hook:** ALCA dimension AL-10 (`guardrails/audit/guardrail-alca.md`)

### 5.1 — What the Mine Sends Out

Ore of low-grade Power Crystal, "the stone that holds qi", in sacks: four wagons of it on 1247.1.9 / PD 9, loaded on mules' backs at "a mule's load apiece" when the company took it, each sack "dull and heavy and giving off no more pressure than gravel" (Area block 02). It is bound for the Aerie Artificers' College (`ledger-worlds-location/gale-kingdom.md` §5.6; the College builds the Flightcraft and the Storm-Kite frames). The page states no grade beyond "low-grade", so none is set here (Part 11 owns the grades; the Chapter Spec's plan reads Grades 1 to 3 as a plan fact only). How much the mine digs, how often a caravan goes down and what the College does with the ore are not established.

### 5.2 — The Caravan

The caravan of 1247.1.9 / PD 9 was four wagons with mules in harness, four carters and a clerk who did not fight, and an escort of a captain of the Windward Host and five of the Host. Its strongbox rode on the second wagon with Jorund Ulm on its bench and held the cargo list and the route papers. The escort's captain halted every file at the shale ledge above Stirrup Ford, spoke her standing orders aloud and sent the file down in one file by daylight. Nothing else of the mine's people or yard is shown.

**Narrative summary (as of Chapter 005).** Whistledeep is the mine up the mountain from the Seam Road that sends low-grade Power Crystal, in sacks, down to the Aerie Artificers' College on the Aerie Plateau. Its caravan of four wagons, four carters and a clerk travels under a captain of the Windward Host and five of the Host. On 1247.1.9 / PD 9 the caravan was taken at Stirrup Ford, its ore and its papers carried off and all eleven of its people killed; the Ridgewatch patrol found the wagons standing in the ford on 1247.1.11 / PD 11, and the Court heard of it on 1247.1.14 / PD 14 (Ref: 1.1.1.005).
