## Section 5 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

### 5.1 — The Smell

The town's one established sensory fact is that it **announces itself a kilometer out**. It is recorded here because it is the whole of what the prose gave, and because it is now **immutable**: Saltmarsh is a place that is smelled before it is seen.

*(Recorded as an observation and not as a rule: the capital is established as doing the same thing with its forge-quarter solder — `azorion.md` §5.6. **No relationship between the two facts is established** and none is asserted here.)*
