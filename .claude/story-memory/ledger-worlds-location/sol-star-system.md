**Physical Description:**
A star system anchored by a single primary star (yellow star), with eight bodies in steady orbit around it. Named bodies of significance:
- **Saha** — primary habitable planet; large-scale world with multiple continents, kingdoms, and empires; orbited by one habitable moon (the Moon of Saha; its canonical name is open)
- **Moon of Saha** — large habitable moon orbiting Saha (not the star directly); hosts the Three Moon Sects

Additional celestial bodies (gas giants, asteroid fields, uninhabitable worlds) are not yet named — to be established in prose.

**Cultural Association (Part 25):** Mixed cultivation-dominant and technology-dominant cultures; overall civilization character is Balanced Hybrid — dual-track (cultivation + technology) at moderate-to-high integration level, constrained by the Firmament Lock

**Special Zone — THE FIRMAMENT LOCK (System-Wide Suppression Field):**
> **Classification:** Special Zone — External Suppression Field (unique; does not map to standard Part 13 Formation Zone categories)
> **Origin:** Imposed by an entity of extreme power — origin, identity, and purpose not yet established in prose; classified as unknown higher-power origin
> **Suppression Effect 1 — Technology:** Maximum technology tier achievable within the Sol Star System is capped at **Tier 6** (Part 8). No technology above Tier 6 can be constructed, maintained, or operated within the system boundary. Current civilizational technology level is **Tier 3** — well below the cap.
> **Suppression Effect 2 — Cultivation:** Maximum cultivation realm achievable within the Sol Star System is capped at **Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid** (Part 3). No cultivator within the system can break through above this ceiling regardless of talent, resources, or method. The late-stage Core Assimilation (Levels above 3 Mid), Core Solidification, and all Divine-realm cultivation are inaccessible to any cultivator born or residing within the system.
> **Firmament Lock Boundary:** The suppression field operates at the full star system spatial boundary — every world, moon, and body within the gravitational influence of the Sol System's star is subject to the lock.
> **External Cultivators within the Lock:** Cultivators who achieved cultivation outside the system (e.g., Hercules — Demigod; Bison — Demigod) retain their existing cultivation level and stand subject to the Lock's system-wide ceiling exactly as every other being inside the boundary does. The Lock is **not** what holds Hercules and Bison at Champion Level 8 High at story start: that far tighter cap is the **Olympus Suppression Seal**, a personal binding carried by those two specifically. See **Layered Suppression Model** immediately below — the authoritative statement of how the two suppressions relate.
> **Lock Origin Status:** Unknown — classified as a mystery of cosmic significance. The identity, purpose, and conditions of release of the Firmament Lock are not yet established in prose.

**LAYERED SUPPRESSION MODEL — Firmament Lock and Olympus Suppression Seal (Locked Canon; AUTHORITATIVE HOME):**

> *This block is the single authoritative statement of how the Sol Star System's two suppressions relate. Every other ledger, skill, and session record cross-references it rather than restating the mechanism (Part 37: the Firmament Lock is a Special Zone property of this location entity, so the model that governs it is owned here). It is **distinct from the Two-Layer Ceiling Model** below, which separates the system's physical ceiling from the Phase 1 narrative ceiling — a different pair of layers entirely. Do not conflate the two blocks.*
>
> Two suppressions operate on Hercules and Bison at the same time. Both are real; neither cancels the other; they are layered.
>
> - **Outer layer — THE FIRMAMENT LOCK (system-wide).** The ceiling on the entire Sol Star System: **Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid** (cultivation) and **Tier 6** (technology). It applies to everyone inside the system boundary, Hercules and Bison included. It is a property of the place.
> - **Inner layer — THE OLYMPUS SUPPRESSION SEAL (personal).** A relic-based binding imposed on **Hercules and Bison specifically** at the betrayal of Mount Olympus. It caps their effective expression at **Champion Level 8 High** at Phase 1 start. It is a property of those two persons, carried into the system with them, and it binds far tighter than the Lock.
>
> **Governing rule — the tighter binding governs.** For these two the operative cap is the Seal's, which is why they never approach the Lock's ceiling. The Lock names the outer, system-wide limit, but it is not the binding constraint on them.
>
> **What neither layer touches.** Actual internal cultivation. Hercules and Bison each remain **Demigod Level 1 Mid** by true cultivation under both layers; suppression governs expression and output, never the Core, the meridians, or the qi density (cross-ref `hercules.md` §2, `bison.md` §2).
>
> **Phase 1 breaks the Seal, not the Lock.** The Phase 1 end-states of **Ruler Level 7 Low (Hercules) and Ruler Level 6 High (Bison), effective,** are a partial breakthrough of the **Seal's** binding — Part 30 §1.1, §1.3.1, and Law P30-L1 all state this. Nothing in Phase 1 weakens, cracks, or reveals the Firmament Lock; the Lock's ceiling, origin, and conditions of release are untouched by the Phase 1 arc, and no Phase 1 character operates anywhere near it.
>
> **Species-innate (S-3) substrate stands outside both layers.** Immunity to cultivation suppression is **anatomical, not mechanical** — it does not depend on what is doing the suppressing. Part 6 Area 12.3.4(a) exempts S-3 moves from the Firmament Lock *and from all cultivation-energy suppression mechanisms that operate on S-1 substrate*; the Olympus Suppression Seal is one such mechanism, so the exemption reaches it. No cultivation suppression of any origin touches an S-3 move. Only the physical counters of 12.3.4(b)–(d) do. Which moves are S-3 for a given character, and the specific counters that reach them, are registered in that character's own ledger (`bison.md` §3) and are not restated here.
>
> **Deliberately left open — do not invent:** how the Seal was laid, who holds it, and what would break it in full; and, separately, the Firmament Lock's own origin and conditions of release. None of these are canon.

**Two-Layer Ceiling Model (Locked Canon — Do Not Conflate):**

> *Not to be confused with the **Layered Suppression Model** above, which is about Lock versus Seal. This block is about physical ceiling versus narrative ceiling.*
>
> The Sol Star System operates under **two distinct ceilings** that must never be conflated. The first is a permanent **physical ceiling** imposed by the Firmament Lock that applies across all narrative Phases. The second is a transient **narrative ceiling** imposed by the story's current Phase per Part 30 — which sits well below the physical ceiling during Phase 1 and rises as later Phases unfold.
>
> **Layer 1 — System Physical Ceiling (All Phases, Until Lock Weakens / Breaks):**
> - **Cultivation:** Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid (the absolute physical cap any cultivator born or residing inside the Sol Star System can reach under the Firmament Lock).
> - **Technology:** Tier 6.
> - This ceiling is the property of the Sol Star System as a world-physical fact. It does not change Phase to Phase — only a canonical weakening or breaking of the Firmament Lock alters it.
>
> **Layer 2 — Phase 1 Narrative Ceiling (Part 30 — Phase 1 Only):**
> - **Cultivation:** **Ruler Level 9 early** — the upper narrative bound for any character (protagonist, antagonist, or background NPC) appearing in Phase 1 prose set inside the Sol Star System. No character appearing in Phase 1 prose may be rendered above Ruler Level 9 early, save the one bounded exception of Part 30 Law P30-L1 (Hercules's single Catastrophic-type move on the Section 8 voyage, through his true Demigod cultivation, which the Seal punishes); the Monarch realm and everything above it (up to the Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid physical cap) exists in-world but is narratively off-screen during Phase 1.
> - **Technology:** Tier 3 operational (per Part 30; Tier 4 reachable if narrative events drive it).
> - This ceiling rises Phase by Phase per Part 30 governance; Phase 2 and beyond will lift this narrative ceiling toward (and eventually through) the physical ceiling as the Firmament Lock arc develops.
>
> **Why Both Matter:**
> - The Physical Ceiling governs **what exists in the world** — it is the answer to "what is the maximum cultivation any in-system being could theoretically reach?"
> - The Narrative Ceiling governs **what appears on the page during this Phase** — it is the answer to "what is the maximum cultivation any character in Phase 1 prose may be rendered at?"
