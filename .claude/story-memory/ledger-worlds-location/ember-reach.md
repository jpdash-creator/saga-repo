**Physical Description:** The western volcanic coast of the Nine Suns Continent and its inland ridges. Fronts the Western Reach ocean. Volcanic ridges define the western cultural frontier with the Central Highlands' paired-oath cultures. Home to fire-affinity and stone-affinity polities. The boundary between cultivation and technology is unusually merged in this region. **Eastern rim (`[ASKED]` Author ruling, 2026-10-18 (map consultation)):** the low volcanic ridge with the Boundary Towers marks Azure Sky's border and is the eastern rim of the Ember Reach region, which stretches west from it toward the coast and Cindervast (§5.1).
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** A third pattern — not cultivation-dominant in the Gale sense, not dual-track balanced in the Azure Sky sense; the Ember Reach polities represent the single-element-but-technology-dominant variant; fire as both the cultivation substrate and the manufacturing engine
