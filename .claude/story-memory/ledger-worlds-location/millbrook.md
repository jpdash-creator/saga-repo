## Section 5 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

### 5.1 — Economy and Its Consequence for Leave

Grain and lumber move downriver to Azorion on a **predictable** season — one *"nobody argued about."* The consequence is the reason the town entered the story at all: **a Millbrook man can be told to the day when his leave would be worth having**, because the town's working year is legible in advance. This is a **fact about the town's calendar**, established through a sergeant's leave arithmetic rather than through description.
