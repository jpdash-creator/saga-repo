## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** The Southern Sea
**Common Name / Aliases:** Southern Sea
**Location Tier (Part 13):** Sub-World — continental ocean; bounds the Nine Suns Continent on its southern face
**Cosmological Area (Part 13):** Oceanic feature of Saha; adjacent to Nine Suns Continent (cross-ref: nine-suns-continent.md)
**Part 13 Character:** Maritime — the Lyraen River empties into this sea at the delta mouth where Saltmere sits; border kingdoms of the Southern March face this sea
**Sacred Ground Status:** Not yet established — to be defined in prose
**Containing World:** Saha (cross-ref: saha.md)
**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:** The ocean that borders the southern plains of the Nine Suns Continent. The border kingdoms of the Southern March sit between the continent's agricultural heartland and this sea. The continent's longest river (the Lyraen) empties into it at the delta where Saltmere sits. Water-affinity cultivation and maritime technology are dominant in kingdoms facing this sea. **Ruling, 2026-10-18 (`[ASKED]` Author ruling, 2026-10-18 (map consultation)):** the Southern Sea is "the same water the Lyraen delta empties into, one continuous southern shoreline from Saltmarsh east to Saltmere and Ironshore", and it is the sea the Azure Sky Kingdom's southern coast (the Tide March, with Farrowharbor and Saltmarsh) faces. The delta lies about 200 km east of Farrowharbor along the coast (`ledger-worlds-distance/_index.md` DIST-015).
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** Maritime; water-affinity cultivation kingdoms (Saltmere, Ironshore) face this sea; trade and fishing economy implied

**Controlling Faction(s):** No single controlling faction — open sea; multiple Southern March kingdoms have coastal presence:
