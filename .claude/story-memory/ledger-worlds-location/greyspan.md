## Section 5 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

### 5.1 — A Border That Argues Rather Than Fights

Greyspan's establishing fact is a **posture**, not an event: two kingdoms who dispute a jurisdiction, sign about it, fight about it, and sign again, without the matter resolving or escalating. **The town is what a long unresolved legal argument looks like when people have to live inside it.** No battle, no siege, no incident and no casualty is established, and none may be read out of *"about as often as they fought."*
