## Section 5 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

**Schema:** Part 28 Area 17.2 — Location Domain (Sub-World waypoint)

**Audit Hook:** ALCA dimension AL-10 (`guardrails/audit/guardrail-alca.md`)

### 5.1 — The Ford and Its Parts (axis 12)

Seen at first light on 1247.1.9 / PD 9 (Ref: 1.1.1.005), in a ground mist that hides the ford from the eye and not from a Warrior's sense. On the near side the Seam Road comes down a cutting to the water, with a ditch that runs out of the cutting at the edge of the mist; above the cutting a **shale ledge** is where the escort's captain halts every file, and the **draw** above the ford, "the dry gully that climbed the slope", runs up from it. The stream is crossed by a **gravel bar**, "the ridge of gravel the stream had piled across the ford", which "has crept east with the thaw", so that the wagons go straight along it; the water is shin-deep, and cold enough to be felt through boots. On the far bank stand willows on soft ground. On the near bank trees stand along the bank, and the company's long hafts were planted butt-down in the soft ground at the trees' edge. The air smells of cold stone and wet wool. No bird called before the raid; one bird of the far bank called twice after it. Stones on the bar in front of where the captain stood were dark all across where the raiders fell (Area block 06).

### 5.2 — The Ground, as the Lieutenant Read It (Area block 05)

Reported to the Court on 1247.1.14 / PD 14; the lieutenant gives it as a count and says it orders nothing. About two dozen men stood in the trees on both banks: the round butt-marks of long hafts are in a row in the soft ground at the trees' edge, and the boot-tracks come to about the same number. A train of pack-mules came down the draw above the ford and went up it again, and its track is lost on the shale, where the patrol followed it until the shale stopped giving it anything. There were strips of sacking in the gravel and on the thorn, each torn off in a single pull. On the third wagon's tailboard hung a torn strip of plain undyed cloth, and in the shallows against the lead wheel lay one plain brown coat with no cord or badge or colour on it, of a kind sold at any market in the kingdom. There was blood on the gravel that was not Gale's and a dark trail of it going up the draw, and none of the raiders' dead was left behind. The ground gives what they wore, what they carried, about how many they were and which way they went; it gives no name, mark or kingdom. Two of these traces were shown in Area block 01 before the lieutenant reads them (the coat left to the current and the sleeve left on the tailboard).

### 5.3 — Hazards and Dangers to a Visitor (axis 14)

**Established:** ground mist that hides the far bank; a gravel bar that moves with the thaw; soft ground under the willows; cold shin-deep water; and, on 1247.1.9 / PD 9, a company waiting in the trees on both banks, with its rear rank across the stream, and a pack-train waiting in the draw behind it. **Not established:** any other danger (OPEN-AREA).

### 5.4 — Climate and Seasonal Texture (axis 13; Part 13 Area 27)

**Established:** early spring: the thaw has moved the bar, the snow is only now going off the slopes below Shalegate Pass, and a first-light mist lies on the stream and the road (1247.1.9 / PD 9). **Not established:** the ford's own climate, its flood and low-water seasons, and what each does to it (OPEN-AREA).

On a misty first-light morning, 1247.1.9 / PD 9, a company waiting in the trees took the Whistledeep caravan there, killed its escort and its hands, took the ore on pack-mules and the book and papers in a coat, and left the four wagons standing in the water.
