## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** Farrowharbor
**Naming etymology (established, immutable — author-supplied at spec build, delivered in prose):** named for **a *farrow* of rocky islets** guarding the approach.
**Containing Kingdom:** Azure Sky Kingdom (cross-ref: `azure-sky-kingdom.md`) — **southern coast.**
**Physical Description (author-authored, pre-prose — immutable):** The kingdom's **fleet home port**. **The keels go down here and the war fleet winters here.** The approach lies **behind a farrow of rocky islets that no man can find a way in through unless he has been sailing it since he was a boy.**
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** A working naval port. Not separately established beyond that.

### 5.1 — The Approach
