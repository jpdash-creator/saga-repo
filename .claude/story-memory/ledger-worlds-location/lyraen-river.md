**Full Name:** The Lyraen River
**Common Name / Aliases:** The Lyraen; The Riverway (folk register — barge trade); Lyraen delta (the river's mouth zone)
**Location Tier (Part 13):** Sub-World — continental waterway; the Nine Suns Continent's longest river
**Cosmological Area (Part 13):** Waterway feature of Nine Suns Continent; flows from Windward Range headwaters to the Southern Sea (cross-ref: nine-suns-continent.md)
**Part 13 Character:** Multi-kingdom shared resource; trade artery; water-affinity cultivation present along the basin; governed by the Lyraen River Compact
**Sacred Ground Status:** Not yet established — to be defined in prose
**Containing World:** Saha (cross-ref: saha.md)
**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:** The continent's longest river. Flows from Windward Range headwaters south to the Southern Sea, emptying at the Lyraen delta where Saltmere (at the mouth) and Ironshore (eastern bank) are sited. The river forms the eastern boundary of the Azure Sky Kingdom's territory — the Lyraen River is the eastern edge, with the far bank lying within Gale Kingdom's sphere of customary use. The Lyraen River Compact governs the entire corridor from headwaters to sea.
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** The continent's primary trade artery; multi-kingdom river compact framework; water-affinity cultivation concentrated along its basin; the Riverway (Lyraen barge trade south) is a principal trade route
