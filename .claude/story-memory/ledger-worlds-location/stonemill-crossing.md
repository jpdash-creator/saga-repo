## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** Stonemill Crossing
**Common Name / Aliases:** Stonemill Crossing; "the Crossing"
**Location Tier (Part 13 Area 2.1):** Sub-World — the point where the descent road from the Windward Reach joins the Sapphire Road (cross-ref: azure-sky-kingdom.md §5.11 — Sapphire Road, "capital to Gale via the Lyraen crossings")
**Physical Description:** A water trough and staging point where wagons stand three deep; northbound traffic carries blue-steel bar stock bound for Gale's storm-cavalry, southbound traffic carries wind-affinity ingredient down from the Aerie Plateau. The Crossing is a waypoint on the Sapphire Road; the corridor-split where the Gale road goes east and the Azure Sky road goes south is the **Twinpost** (`twinpost.md`).
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** A working trade/dispersal waypoint, not a ceremonial site — carters, forge-crews, and Gale-side loads pass through in the ordinary business of the Sapphire Road trade.
