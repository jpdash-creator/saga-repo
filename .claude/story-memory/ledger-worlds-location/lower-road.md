## Section 3 — Faction/Civilization Control (B/L)

**Controlling Faction(s):** The Azure Sky crown holds a post on the road, "the patrol's post", a body of soldiers under Captain Elowen Tregear (the Skysworn's, by `ledger-people-society-faction/azure-sky-military.md`, Section 5). After the raid of 1246.12.8 / PD -19, Bastion Crowe of the Skysworn "doubled the post on the lower road" and gave up the month's exercise there to pay for it. Grand Marshal Ossian Kade says, on 1247.1.2 / PD 2, in answer to the Court's closing question, that the doubled post, eight pairs in all, would hold the road from the ford to the last toll-house with a rider at each end, and that no file would leave the mine's yard without a pair walking beside it (Ref: 1.1.1.003). ## Section 5 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

**Schema:** Part 28 Area 17.2 — Location Domain (Sub-World road)
**Depth Standard:** Proportionality Exception (Part 28 Area 17.3): populated to what the page shows; every other axis reads not established.
**Audit Hook:** ALCA dimension AL-10 (`guardrails/audit/guardrail-alca.md`)

### 5.1 — The Road and Its Two Sides (axis 12)

Seen on the evening of 1246.12.8 / PD -19 (Ref: 1.1.1.003). The road is pale and runs down a slope; to the left of a man walking down it lies a ditch, and to his right the first trees of Skyvein Forest (`skyvein-forest.md` §5.14). The ruts are frost-hard and ring under the iron on the wheels. Five wagons nose to tail, with their horse teams, go down it in file, and the breath of each team stands over it in a cloud the evening does not move. A guard set on "the ditch side" stands ahead of the first wheel; a guard set on "the forest side" stands a spear's length in from the first trunks. The road comes out of a last bend at the ford, and below the ford the slope drops into the narrow.

### 5.2 — The Narrow

A stretch where the road is tight enough that "two carts could not have passed": sixty paces long, and the five wagons nose to tail fill fifty of them. It lies below the ford and above the last toll-house. Frost lies in its ruts, a shade side is spoken of ("on both sides of that narrow, or only on the shade side?"), and on the mine's previous passage the lead wagon's driver's off wheel "went out from under" him there. The rule Roke gives that driver that evening is that the lamps go up only at the mouth of the narrow and the team is walked in, not driven (one night's instruction; no custom is stated). The toll-house slip names the narrow: "Narrow at dusk, yard by full dark." The guards were set at its mouth, the file stood in it with five lamps lit down its length, and the fight took place there: at the gap between the third and fourth wagons the ditch and the trees closed everything else.

### 5.3 — The Lower Ford

A ford on the lower road, above the narrow. "The road came out of the last bend at the ford, and below it lay the narrow." The relay hut stands by it (§5.5). Chapter 001 Area 02 (Ref: 1.1.1.001.02) gives it one sentence: in the clerk's report to King Varys at first light on 1247.1.1 / PD 1, "The lower ford went under water at dusk, and every toll-house below it wanted to say so", so the ford can go under water and more than one toll-house lies below it. In Chapter 003 a man in a good cloth coat was seen walking away toward the ford, far up the slope behind the last wagon; the patrol came down from that side; and Kade's stated plan (1247.1.2 / PD 2) keeps every soldier from crossing it: "No soldier would cross the ford, because that would be an offensive act, and such an act is yours and the queen's to sign together." What lies across the ford is Brackenhold ground, by the author's ruling of 2026-10-18 (map consultation), `[ASKED]`: "Brackenhold the kingdom across the ford to the north-west" (cross-ref: `ledger-worlds-kingdom-state/brackenhold.md`). The ford's width and depth, and how it comes to go under water, are not shown (OPEN-AREA).

### 5.4 — The Last Toll-House

Stands below the narrow. The text gives it one use: its keeper's yard is where the file "would lie tonight" (1246.12.8 / PD -19). A boy from the toll-house ran up the slope beside the front wagon with the slip, and ran back down the road. Kade says the carters' route "was written out at the last toll-house" and that he does not know whether it was copied. A slip like that evening's had come the month before ("It is the same as last month, near enough word for word", the lead wagon's driver said). The toll-houses send each other notices about a file along the wire (§5.5). In Chapter 001 Area 02 (Ref: 1.1.1.001.02) the toll-keeper's notice for the carters was the only one of the toll-house notices about the ford that came down the wire broken. How many toll-houses stand on the road, their names, the keeper's name and the toll are not established (OPEN-AREA).

### 5.5 — The Relay Hut by the Lower Ford, and the Wire

A relay hut stands by the ford, and the old wire runs under its floor. At a date the text does not give, between the raid and the report of 1247.1.2 / PD 2, Grand Marshal Ossian Kade went to it and found "under the floor" an iron clamp fixed to the wire: a pair of iron jaws no longer than his hand with a screw to close them, cast iron scraped bright where they bit the wire, the screw a common one "that any smith in any kingdom keeps in a drawer". He put his hands on it, so that the third wagon, the banner pole and the clamp are all "confirmed". The squad leader of the raiding company knew of it: his thought before the raid was that a clamp on the wire at a relay hut by the ford meant "every notice the toll-houses sent each other about the file was read before it was delivered". The post's own book shows its first report of the raid sent down the old wire on the night of the raid, and the next relay's book shows nothing received; Kade says the report was lost to the clamp, which is why the Court heard of the raid three weeks late, and that the clamp is unconfirmed as the means by which the raiders learned the road and the hour. The post took the silence for the old wire's usual fault and sent its second report by rider. Whether any other relay hut carries a clamp, who set this one and how long it hung there are not established (OPEN-AREA).

### 5.6 — What the Road Carries

The page shows one kind of traffic: the Bluecleft mine's file. On 1246.12.8 / PD -19 it was five wagons drawn by horse teams, five carters and a clerk who did not fight, eight guards and Roke, with two chests of shard strapped under the covers of the first two wagons and a paired banner on the last wagon's pole (`bluecleft-mine.md`). The keeper's boy runs a slip up the slope to meet it. Whether other traffic uses the road is not shown.

### 5.7 — Hazards and Dangers to a Visitor (axis 14)

**Established:** frost on the narrow, with a shade side where a wheel can go out from under a carter; the narrow's width, where two carts cannot pass; and, on 1246.12.8 / PD -19, the first trees along the road hid a company that struck at the narrow, with the frost and the hanging *Skyveil* leaves dulling a Warrior's soul sense there (`skyvein-forest.md` §5.14). After the raid the post is doubled. **Not established:** any other danger (OPEN-AREA).

**Narrative summary (as of Chapter 003).** The lower road is the cart road down from the Bluecleft mine: a pale road on a slope, with a ditch along one side and the first trees of Skyvein Forest along the other. Below the lower ford, where a relay hut stands over the old wire, the slope drops into the narrow, sixty paces where two carts cannot pass, and below the narrow stands the last toll-house, in whose yard the mine's file lies at night. On a frost-hard winter evening (1246.12.8 / PD -19) the mine's file of five wagons was taken at the narrow by a company of about twenty in plain coats, and a patrol from the post up the road saved the rear wagons. Three weeks later the Court hears the news, finds a clamp on the wire under the relay hut's floor, and doubles the post from the ford to the last toll-house (Ref: 1.1.1.003).
