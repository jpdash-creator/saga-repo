### 5.1 — Geography

The Windward Pass is a high cut in the southern flank of the Windward Range: a long, narrow channel of bare stone that climbs from the Twinpost, where the last trees stop, to a saddle on the crest line. The channel is confined, with steep walls close on both sides, and that shape is why the Pass is one of the nine convergence-risk formations of the Nine Suns Continent: the perimeter works on the two opposite shoulders feed the same stretch of floor between them, and the density there builds instead of thinning out. It is stone to the sky, with no trees and little soil: scree, bare ledge and old snow in the north-facing hollows. Weather comes down the channel off the crest and does not leave. The Windward Range baseline is Class 4 to 5. The Azure Sky works stand on the west shoulder and the Gale works on the east shoulder; the middle of the channel belongs to neither kingdom and is held together under the Northern Accord. The Twinpost stands at the foot, where the corridor splits and the two home roads begin. The ground north of the saddle is not described in Phase 1 (Part 30 Law P30-L8). The Pass's length and its distances to the Twinpost and to Azorion are held in the Distance Ledger (Registry B) and are not repeated here.

### 5.2 — Three Landmarks

- **The Shoulder Stations** (proposed working name). The two perimeter stations that face each other across the channel, the Azure Sky station on the west shoulder and the Gale station on the east. Each runs its own perimeter array and passes its full monitoring dataset to the other under the Windward Protocol.
- **The Midpoint Array** (proposed working name). The array on the channel floor between the two stations, where the two perimeters meet. Both kingdoms have access to it, and any bilateral discharge operation must be coordinated through both sides' access under the Protocol; none has taken place. Of all the Pass's places, it is where the density builds.
- **The Rotation Camp** (proposed working name). The one camp where the whole combined formation lives out a rotation, banked with stone in the lee of the shoulder wall. Shelters stand on timber frames lashed down against the pass wind, which eats rope, so lashings are re-turned before dark. It is the place a rotation is served and the place a rotation ends: the soldiers go down the channel to the Twinpost and part there. It is one camp, not two. *(Gate 2 of 2026-10-23, Chapter 009, Ref: 1.1.1.009, Area block 04: "the Rotation Camp" is on the page as the place the two runners go to "tell the other seventy-six of the Wing"; the page says nothing else of it, so the name is no longer only a working name and nothing else about the camp is set by the page.)*

### 5.3 — Sensory Signature

Cold stone and moving air. The pass wind comes down the channel steadily and is hard on gear: it eats rope, it cracks canvas, and the creak of lashed frames is the sound of the night. Underfoot the scree ticks and slides. The rock is grey and black, with snow glare by day and a hard, clear light. The air is thin, and breath shows even at midday. The smells are wet leather, wood smoke from small fires of carried wood, cold iron, and damp, because a wet sock is the thing that hurts people here. The watch is changed in low voices. At the midpoint, a cultivator feels the Class 4 to 5 air as a weight in the chest that does not thin with distance as it should.
