**Full Name:** The Hammer-and-Hilt Gate
**Common Name / Aliases:** The Hammer-and-Hilt Gate
**Location Tier (Part 13 Area 2.1):** Sub-World — Azorion's northern gate, the entry point from the Sapphire Road
**Naming register note (Part 28 Area 20.3):** "Hammer" evokes the technology/forge tradition, "Hilt" evokes the cultivation/sword tradition — a paired-duality-coded gate name consistent with Azure Sky's naming logic (cf. the Skysworn / the Azure Guard [Azure Sky's two military orders; the kingdom's paired-duality naming pattern], Sword Court / Signet Court).
**Physical Description:** Opens inward on the second stroke of the paired morning bells (brass, then resonant alloy). A muster stone stands just inside the gate, staffed by wardens who process returning military personnel by name, rank, and formation before they are permitted into the city proper. On opening, the whole waiting mass of carts and crews and apprentices goes in through it like water finding a channel.
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** The gate's opening is timed to the paired bells — a small daily civic ritual, not a ceremonial event in itself, but structurally bound to the kingdom's paired-duality timekeeping.
