### 5.1 — What the Mine Sends Out

Shard, "the hard crystal of packed qi the Bluecleft mine digs out", in chests. On 1246.12.8 / PD -19 two chests went down the road, one under the cover of each of the first two wagons, strapped down, banded and sealed; the mine's clerk carried the book and answered for the seals ("the book is in order, and the seals are whole"). How much the mine digs, how often a file goes down, the chests' weight and the shard's grade are not established. How the page's "shard" stands against the energy-substance states of Part 11 is not established (OPEN-AREA).

### 5.2 — The Mine's People and Its File

The file of 1246.12.8 / PD -19 was five wagons with horse teams, five carters and a clerk who did not fight, and eight guards under Ansel Roke, whose title on the page is that he "commanded the mine's guard". The guards were Seniors under one Warrior, Roke, as the page shows them (their levels are held in `ledger-character-side/_index.md`). A slip from the last toll-house told the file where to be at dusk and at full dark; a slip like it had come the month before. The mine's yard is where the file sets out (Kade, 1247.1.2 / PD 2).

**Narrative summary (as of Chapter 003).** Bluecleft is the shard mine at the top of the lower road. It has a guard of its own under Ansel Roke, a clerk with a book and seals, carters and a yard, and it sends its shard down the road in banded and sealed chests in a file of wagons under a paired banner. On 1246.12.8 / PD -19 the file was taken at the narrow and its two head chests were lost; four of the mine's people are counted dead of the raid: Davin Tarn, Wil Harker and Edric Gower on the road, and the clerk Perrin Lisle in the kingdom hospital in the night before 1247.1.2 / PD 2. The Court now keeps a doubled post on the road, and the mine's file is not to leave its yard without a pair walking beside it (Ref: 1.1.1.003).
