## Section 5 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

### 5.1 — The Land Grants, and What They Say About the Crown

Fallowfield's establishing fact is an **institutional** one rather than a scenic one: the crown discharges its obligation to **Twin Oath veterans** in land that has no other taker. The town is what that policy produces when it is run for long enough. **The Twin Oath itself is `azure-sky-kingdom.md` §5.2's and is cross-referenced, not restated.** **Nothing is established here about whether the practice is thought just, by anyone** — the prose renders the arrangement and passes no verdict on it, and none is added.
