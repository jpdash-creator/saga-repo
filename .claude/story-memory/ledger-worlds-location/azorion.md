**Physical Description:** Azure roofs, thousands of them, tier on tier, take the new light and throw it back in a colour that no other city on the continent could make. Approached from the north via the Sapphire Road, the city rises out of the basin's morning haze below the Royal Palace of Twin Courts, which stands on its own shoulder of stone above the city proper. The Hammer-and-Hilt Gate is the city's northern gate, opened on the second stroke of the paired morning bells (brass, then resonant alloy). Inside the gate: dense forge-crew and apprentice-cohort traffic; every formal doorway carries two inscription bands, cultivation-script above and technology-schematic below, cut into the lintel by two different hands. The Bellway is the principal thoroughfare rising from the gate toward the palace plaza.

### 5.1 — Geographic Extent
Azorion sits at the center of the Sapphire Basin, Azure Sky Kingdom's central agricultural-urban heartland (cross-ref: sapphire-basin.md, azure-sky-kingdom.md §5.1). The city is approached from the north by the road descending from the Windward Reach via Stonemill Crossing and the Sapphire Road, and rises out of the basin's morning haze below the Royal Palace of Twin Courts, which occupies its own shoulder of stone above the city proper.

**Shown in Area 01 (Ref: 1.1.1.001.01): the city and the plain, seen from the palace's west-edge platform.** In the last dark hour Azorion lies nearer than the plain, under the basin's haze, which rises only to the tallest chimneys and lets them stand out of it. The roofs step down below roof as the benches of a quarry step, and the lanes between them show as seams of deeper black, with a few yellow windows pricked through. The outermost district lies low, where the roofs fray out into the fields, and one wide street (unnamed in the Area) climbs from it toward the palace, empty to its last turning. North of the city, between it and the Windward Range, lies the Azure Sky Kingdom's plain, quiet to its last field. Its fields lie in strips running toward the Range with the grain of the land, frost silvering the nearer ones, and a pale road crosses them at a slant, the one line that cuts against the grain; along the road stand a field wall, a barn roof and a bend of ditch. The Area does not name the road, the district or the street, and it does not place the Hammer-and-Hilt Gate, the Bellway or Stonemill Crossing.

**Shown at Chapter 009 (Ref: 1.1.1.009), Area block 05: the city from the north road, on foot, 1247.3.27 / PD 86 (instances, not customs).** From the last rise before the plain the haze lies on the plain and the roofs of Azorion rise out of it "tier on tier toward the palace's shoulder of stone"; the plain's strips run toward the city with the grain of the land, with the pale road cutting across them, a field wall, a barn roof, a bend of ditch, a pump and a well (the features Area 01 gives, met from the other end); "at the plain's edge the low outer district, which the carters called Lowfield, frayed out into the fields" (Section 3); the Hammer-and-Hilt Gate stands before the morning crowd, the carters' yards (Wagonyard) lie inside it with the stone of the city beyond them, and the Bellway climbs from the Gate region to the top of its street, where the palace comes clear above the city and the Paired Banners Plaza lies under it (Sections 3 and 5.8). So the page now places Stonemill Crossing, the plain, the Gate and the Bellway in that order along a walk from the north. It does not place the Gate against the river, and it gives no bearing and no length for the walk from the Crossing to the Gate (the Crossing's file holds a half hour's ride, `stonemill-crossing.md`) or for the Bellway.

**The river (`[ASKED]` Author ruling, 2026-10-18 (map consultation)).** The city stands on the Azor (`azor-river.md`), the river that rises in the Windward Reach, runs south through Millbrook and Azorion, then east or south-east to join the Lyraen. Where the river runs against the palace, the gate, the plain and the quarters, and its crossings, are not established (OPEN-AREA).

**Doctrinal cross-note — the deadlock corollary as capital street knowledge:** in Azorion the dual-mark rule is not only known but understood down to its consequence. Ordinary citizens on the Bellway can state, unprompted and without ceremony, what happens when the two courts disagree: nobody wins — the courts agree, or the matter waits. The full adjudication is recorded at `bellway.md` §1: it is an entailment of Paired Legitimacy (`ledger-people-society-faction/azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §7.2), it does not conflict with the ratification function of the Twin Courts Bridge, the central reconciliation chamber (the span and the chamber are one structure; §5.8 cross-ref; `azure-sky-palace.md` §1), and it introduces no new entity. Useful for future capital-set scenes as a register fact: constitutional mechanics are civic small talk here, delivered in the flat tone of the obvious, and the ordinary citizen's private opinion of the arrangement may differ from their acceptance of it.

### 5.6 — Technology Profile
Home to the Azure Crafters' Concord (weapons/armor guild, cross-ref: azure-sky-kingdom.md §5.6) and the Paired Forges (twin-furnace metallurgy). The forge quarter's dual-flux solder smell, sharp and faintly sweet, carries out over the surrounding fields from the quarter itself and reaches a traveller on the north road before the city does: the capital announces itself by smell before sight.

**Shown in Area 01 (Ref: 1.1.1.001.01).** In the last dark hour the old wire is heard from the palace platform as a key worked by hand somewhere below it (`nine-suns-continent.md` §5.6, Telegraph): short, dry strokes with a longer gap where a word ends, in a practised hand; the key thins and stops as the dark begins to thin. As the dark thins the forge quarter wakes, seen and heard from the platform: a thread of coal smoke climbs from it without a bend, a bellows draws and draws again, a spark shows at a window and goes out, a hammer rings once on an anvil and is still, and the sweet, sharp solder smell comes up thicker as the fires take (the smell itself is on record above). The Area gives the quarter no street and no name beyond its function. At the plain's edge a pump handle creaks and a bucket rings once on a stone rim, and farther out a chain runs down a well wheel link by link; in the lower streets a cart goes over cobbles.

### 5.12 — Architectural and Sensory Signature
Azure roof tiles take the new light and throw it back in a colour that no other city on the continent could make; the paired morning bells sound brass first, then the resonant alloy a half beat behind, with that long bright edge on the note; the dual-flux solder smell from the forge quarter is sharp and faintly sweet; paired inscription bands mark every formal doorway.

**Shown in Area 01 (Ref: 1.1.1.001.01): the first light.** On the first morning of the year the light comes white, with no gold in it. The ridge-tiles catch it first, each one a hair of white fire; then the faces of the tiles below take it and give it back as blue. The blue falls down the roofs a tier at a time, faster than a man can walk, and every roof it reaches goes from black to a deep blue with a green edge where the glaze has pooled along the lip, the colour coming up out of the clay and not lying on it. The windows flash one after another down the slope; the haze between the chimneys lights from within and turns blue, with the coal smoke hanging in it bright; every chimney draws a long shadow down the roof behind it. The first loud noise of the morning is shutters going back along every lane. Before the light the city shows only as stepped roofs under the haze, with seams of deeper black for lanes and a few yellow windows; once the light is up the road across the plain shows pale as chalk where the dark had held it. The paired morning bells are not heard in Area 01.

**Shown in Area 04 (Ref: 1.1.1.001.04): the paired morning bells, rung and past, and the city's morning noise heard from the Sword Court.** The two morning bells open the Courts. On the first morning they had rung long before the king reached the Sword Court: the brass one first and the other right behind it with a long, bright ring (the page gives no metal for the second bell and no interval). The page does not give the hour they rang, only that the sun was by then well up, and they are not heard on the page. From the Sword Court's open pillars on the east side the city's blue roofs fall away down the hill, and the city's morning noise comes up into the hall: voices, a seller in the street below calling the same words over and over, and hooves on stone. Every item is an instance and not a custom.

**Shown at Chapter 004 (Ref: 1.1.1.004), Area block 01: the capital on foot, in the afternoon and at dusk of PD 2 (1247.1.2).** From the school the way to Aster House is "long": "four corners to the broad street, nineteen paired banners on the poles of its left side alone, and the sharp, sweet smell of solder" (the paired banners of §5.4 and the solder smell of §5.6 and §5.12, met as instances; the broad street is unnamed). At the school gate the street goes past the queen, waiting on the road with her case, "without looking up", and a man with a ladder swings it across her path and she steps around it. At dusk "the lamp-boys were lighting the street", and Varya counts lit windows on the walk home and loses the number twice. Every item is an instance and not a custom.

**Shown at Chapter 007 (Ref: 1.1.1.007), 1247.2.21 / PD 49 (instances, not customs).** The Academy's stone passages are lit by lamps at first light, the Academy's first bell sounds over its roofs, and its halls open on it (Area block 01). From the covered span at dusk "the roofs of Azorion went away under it in their tiers toward the dark", with the basin below and the Windward Range ahead; the palace lights its lamps by hand ("a taper moved from lamp to lamp") and a gate groans below (Area block 06). The city is otherwise not described.

**Shown at Chapter 009 (Ref: 1.1.1.009), Area block 05: the city entered from the north on foot at first light, 1247.3.27 / PD 86 (instances, not customs).** The forge quarter's solder smell reaches a traveller on the north road before the city does ("like hot metal and a little honey in the air", on the last rise before the plain); the roofs rise "tier on tier toward the palace's shoulder of stone" and send the new light back blue; the plain's strips, pale road, field wall, barn roof, ditch, pump and well are as in Area 01; the paired banners (a cultivation sigil and a technology sigil side by side) begin on every wagon-pole and gate-pillar, "so many that the eye gave up on them within two wagons"; the Gate's crowd waits packed before it, with the water sellers working through it; the Bellway carries forge crews, apprentice cohorts walking in step, guild carts and water sellers calling the same two words, and every formal doorway has its pair of strips; the smoke of the forge quarter leans over the roofs "with the bellows drawing and drawing behind it". Every item is an instance and not a custom.

**Narrative summary (as of Area 04).** Azorion at the last dark hour is a stepped mass of roofs under a low haze, lit by a few yellow windows and smelling of coal smoke with the forge quarter's sharp, faintly sweet solder under it. It is quiet but for one key working the wire; then, as the dark thins, the forge quarter's bellows and hammer, a pump at the plain's edge and a cart in the lower streets begin. It lies under the palace's shoulder of stone, with the plain, a pale road and the Windward Range's dark line to its north. At the first light of the first spring morning the roofs take the light from the ridge-tiles down, white at the ridge and blue below with a green edge on the glaze, and the city's first loud noise is its shutters going back. With the sun well up, the paired bells have long since rung to open the Courts, and the city's morning noise (voices, a seller crying the same words over and over, hooves on stone) comes up the hill and in through the Sword Court's open pillars (Area 1.1.1.001.04).

**Narrative summary (as of Chapter 004, Ref: 1.1.1.004).** By day the capital is a long walk of corners to a broad street hung with paired banners and smelling of solder, where a queen goes by on foot and the street does not look up; at dusk the lamp-boys light the street.
