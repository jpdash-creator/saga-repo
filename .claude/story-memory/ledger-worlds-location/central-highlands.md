## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** The Central Highlands
**Common Name / Aliases:** The Highlands
**Location Tier (Part 13):** Sub-World — continental region-scale; one of five canonical internal geographic regions of the Nine Suns Continent
**Cosmological Area (Part 13):** Sub-region of Nine Suns Continent (cross-ref: nine-suns-continent.md)
**Part 13 Character:** Cultivation-dominant with dual-track exceptions — cultivation-dominant kingdoms cluster here given high ambient energy density rewarding the investment; Azure Sky Kingdom (Balanced) is the notable dual-track exception
**Sacred Ground Status:** Not yet established — to be defined in prose
**Containing World:** Saha (cross-ref: saha.md)
**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:** The mid-continent plateau and mountain belt of the Nine Suns Continent. Occupies the center of the continent. Bounded on the north by the Windward Range; contains the Aerie Plateau. Home to Azure Sky Kingdom and Gale Kingdom. Cultivation-dominant kingdoms cluster here due to high ambient energy density.
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** Cultivation-dominant culture predominates; dual-track integration (Azure Sky) and wind-affinity depth (Gale) are the region's two defining cultural expressions; paired-versus-singular oath ideology is the continental cultural axis anchored here
