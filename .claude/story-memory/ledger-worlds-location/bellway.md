## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** The Bellway
**Common Name / Aliases:** The Bellway
**Location Tier (Part 13 Area 2.1):** Sub-World — Azorion's principal thoroughfare, running from the Hammer-and-Hilt Gate up toward the plaza beneath the Royal Palace of Twin Courts
**Physical Description:** Dense with forge crews, apprentice cohorts, guild carts, and water sellers; every formal doorway along it carries paired inscription bands. The street opens out where the ground rises, and the crowd thins against the plaza wall; its upper end gives the first clear view of the Royal Palace of Twin Courts above the city.
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** Named for its proximity to (or processional relationship with) the paired morning bells; the street where ordinary citizens narrate the kingdom's constitutional facts to each other in passing (a woman explaining the dual-signature edict rule to a child, in the bored voice of a person reciting something obvious).

**Disagreement-handling doctrine as ordinary street knowledge:** what the street understands about a deadlock between the two courts is that when the courts disagree, nobody wins — the courts agree, or the thing waits; and the child, hearing it, would have preferred a winner. Three governance notes:
- **Adjudicated a legal entailment, not new canon:** it follows from Paired Legitimacy (`ledger-people-society-faction/azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §7.2 — "no kingdom-wide edict is lawful without dual signature") plus the established dual-mark rule. A rule that makes a single mark unlawful already makes deadlock into delay rather than victory; the street's phrasing states the consequence, it does not create it.
- **No conflict with the reconciliation-chamber record.** `ledger-worlds-location/azure-sky-palace.md` §5 and `ledger-worlds-location/azure-sky-kingdom.md` §5 describe the central reconciliation chamber as the venue where dual-signature edicts are *ratified* — a place for formalizing agreement already reached, not a tiebreak court that decides between disagreeing sovereigns; the Reconciliation Tribunal (`ledger-people-society-faction/azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §7.3) is likewise judicial and cross-track, not an executive tiebreaker. Both records stand. The span and the chamber are one structure, the Twin Courts Bridge (`azure-sky-palace.md` §1).
- **No character entry created for the woman.** She is street texture belonging to this location record, not a side character. Do not promote her to `ledger-character-side` on the strength of this row.
