**Physical Description:** Named for the kingdom's sapphire-flecked bedrock. Sapphire Basin stone is blue-flecked and dense, laid without mortar in the old highland style. The central agricultural-urban heartland of Azure Sky Kingdom. The capital city, **Azorion**, sits at its center. Contains the Royal Palace of Twin Courts. The azure roof tiles that give the kingdom its name catch light in this basin. The Paired Forges (industrial quarter) emit the smell of dual-flux solder.
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** The cultural heart of Azure Sky Kingdom; the paired-duality tradition is most densely expressed here; annual Festival of the Twin Oath centers on the Twin Oath Shrine; Paired Banners Plaza holds the capital's ceremonial gatherings; the Sapphire Road connects this basin to Gale Kingdom
