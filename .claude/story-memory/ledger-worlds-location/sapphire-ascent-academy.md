### 5.1 — Function and Jurisdiction

Sapphire Ascent Academy is the first of the kingdom's three cultivation academies and its primary foundation school, in Azorion (`azure-sky-kingdom.md` §5.5; `azorion.md` §5.5), under the Nine Halls (`nine-halls.md` §6.1a). Its Headmaster is Grandmaster Yun Ashvale. It teaches from the unawakened to the end of the Senior realm and it has nine halls (§5.2) *(author amendment 2026-10-20)*. Its foundation yard is a place of its own, with its own file and its own code block (`sapphire-ascent-foundation-yard.md`).

### 5.3 — The quest levels and the points *(author amendment 2026-10-20)*

The Quest Hall sets six quest levels, and the Library's six levels follow the same six. The quest level a pupil may take follows the pupil's stage:

- The Skin, Muscle and Tendon stages take first-level quests.
- The Bone, Blood and Marrow stages take second-level quests.
- The Soul stage takes third-level quests.
- A Senior of Level 1 to Level 4 takes quests up to the fourth level (the peak for those Levels).
- A Senior of Level 5 to Level 8 takes quests up to the fifth level.
- A Senior of Level 9 takes the sixth level.

Quests give points. Points are exchanged for pills, weapons and other items, which the Lower Treasure Hall sells for points only and never for currency. *(Recorder's note, one word one meaning: this file says "quest level" for the Quest Hall's six levels and "Level" for a Senior's cultivation Level, and the two are not run together.)*

### 5.4 — The three academies and the Warrior-and-Champion halls *(author amendment 2026-10-20)*

The Nine Halls is the umbrella, with its seat in Azorion (`nine-halls.md` §6.1a), over Sapphire Ascent Academy (the foundation, from the unawakened to the end of the Senior realm), Lyraen Heights Academy (the middle academy, for Warriors and Champions) and Windward Reach Academy (the apex, for Rulers). **The Warrior and Champion level has its own separate nine halls**, with similar levels, focused on Warriors and Champions "in a different way", and its people may also join the army (the army's enlisted floor is held at `ledger-people-society-faction/azure-sky-military.md` §6.2b and is not copied here). The author has not listed those halls and none is listed here. The stages each academy teaches are recorded at `nine-halls.md` §6.1a, and the two other academies are described at `azure-sky-kingdom.md` §5.5.

The form is the pupils' form and sets nothing for the Head Elder, the other elders, the keepers or the monitors, whose forms stay open (`nine-halls.md` §6.5 item 14).
