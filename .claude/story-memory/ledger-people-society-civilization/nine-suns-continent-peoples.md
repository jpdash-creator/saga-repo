# NINE SUNS CONTINENT PEOPLES — Civilization Ledger

**Governing Canon Parts:** Parts 21, 25, 31, 8, 3, 13, 30, 28 (Area 17)
**Civilization Type:** Human Cultural Group — the peoples of the Nine Suns Continent taken as one civilization under Part 21 Area 20: several kingdoms bound by a shared calendar, overlapping treaties and shared cultural patterns, with no one government over all of them (the structural control of the whole is read as the sum of what its members administer and defend, P21-7). The Dragon-Human royal lineage of the Gale Kingdom is recorded in the Gale ledgers, not here.
**First Area Appearance:** Not yet established — to be defined in prose
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-05
**Ledger Version:** 10.0

## Section 1 — Identity (B)

**Full Name:** The Peoples of the Nine Suns Continent (ledger designation only; an in-world name for the whole is Negotiable Canon, OPEN-AREA)
**Common Name / Aliases:** Not yet established. "Nine Suns" and "the Continent" name the land (nine-suns-continent.md), not the peoples.
**Civilization Type (Part 21):** BP-Type (Balanced-Path) — see Section 2
**Geographic Range:** The Nine Suns Continent on Saha (cross-ref: ledger-worlds-location/nine-suns-continent.md): the Central Highlands, the Southern March, the Ember Reach, the Eastern Windward and the Northern Frozen Marches. No other continent of Saha belongs to it.
**Member Polities (each keeps its own kingdom-state ledger; not repeated here):** Azure Sky Kingdom and Gale Kingdom (cross-ref: ledger-worlds-kingdom-state), Saltmere, Ironshore and Cindervast (cross-ref: ledger-worlds-kingdom-state), the Border Kingdoms of the Southern March as a grouping, and further polities not yet named. Sect-held valleys of the Northern Frozen Marches are a Phase 2+ matter (Part 30 Law P30-L8; ledger reference only).
**Population:** Low-to-mid hundreds of millions (nine-suns-continent.md Section 5.10)
**Primary Language / Script:** The common tongue. It is one of the languages in use in Phase 1, with the Minotaur tongue and, from Section 6, the World Tree's ancient cultivation language (Part 29 Rule 29.18.3.6); its name is not established and is not named in prose. Script not yet established — OPEN-AREA.
**Dominant Worldview:** Pluralist — a stable plurality of cultivation-dominant, technology-dominant and balanced kingdoms held together by treaty and a shared calendar (nine-suns-continent.md Sections 5.3 and 5.4)
**Historical Origin:** The current order of sovereign kingdoms crystallized in the founding era of Theron the Twinned and Aerion the First-Winged, absorbing three earlier confederations (the Sapphire Confederation, the Lyraen Basin Council, the proto-Aerie clans). No empire has ever ruled the whole continent (nine-suns-continent.md Section 5.2). What "Nine Suns" refers to is deliberately left open there (Section 5.13).

## Section 3 — Cultural Architecture (B/L)

**Core Cultural Values:** There is no single continental culture; the cultural register is pluralist by treaty. Three patterns cross kingdom lines: the contrast between paired oaths (Azure Sky's Twin Oath) and a single oath (Gale's Oath Under Wind), with the Ember Reach fire polities a third pattern (one element, technology-dominant); the shared Nine Suns calendar; the Windward Range pilgrimage (nine-suns-continent.md Section 5.4).
**Cultural Paradigm Class (Part 25 Area 3.1):** CPC-3 Labor-Stratified (one to one with CCT-3) — artisan guilds and a labor hierarchy, guild culture, craft pride. The named guilds (Lyraen Engineering Guild, Azure Crafters' Concord, Skylight Analytic Hall, Aerie Artificers' College, Long Coast Shipwrights' Order) fit it.
**Cultural Paradigm Type (Part 25 Area 9):** Not set — OPEN-AUTHOR. The Part 21 to Part 25 trend for a Balanced-Path civilization is CPT-5 Syncretic-Adaptive; a trend is not an assignment.
**Religious / Philosophical Tradition:** No continental pantheon or church is established. Three readings of "Nine Suns" are held by different scholarly and religious traditions (nine-suns-continent.md Section 5.2); the oath doctrines above are the living philosophies.
**Governing Political Form:** A plurality of sovereign kingdoms. No empire-tier polity exists at story start. Three treaty frameworks: the Northern Accord, the Lyraen River Compact and the Southern March Framework. No continental defense pact, technology authority or cultivation authority.
**Primary Institutions:** The three treaty frameworks (cross-ref: ledger-people-society-affiliation); the Nine Suns Reckoning Stone, where the calendar is kept by a rotating diplomatic delegation; the Windward Range pilgrimage routes, protected together under the Northern Accord. Guilds and academies belong to the kingdom ledgers.

## Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

*(Civilization-domain Lore axes per the `_index.md` schema mandate. Populated from existing canon only; open axes are flagged, not invented.)*

1. **CCT Tier and Civilization Type:** Industrial (CCT-3), Established; BP-Type — Section 2.
2. **Dominant Languages and Writing Systems:** The common tongue (name not established, not named in prose). Script not yet established — OPEN-AREA.
3. **Calendar System:** The Nine Suns calendar, a reckoning by the "nine-sun cycle" whose origin is contested; every kingdom uses it for treaties, trade and diplomatic records, and the current year is set at the Nine Suns Reckoning Stone. **Set by the author's calendar ledger:** the year is 365 Saha days in twelve months with a seven-day week, the saga opens in Year 1247, and a Saha day is 26 standard hours; all of it is held in `ledger-worlds-calendar/_index.md` (Part 41, Calendar Law). The origin and length of the "nine-sun cycle" stay contested, OPEN-AREA, and the calendar names no cycle length.
4. **Religious/Philosophical Framework:** See Section 3. No pantheon, cosmology beyond the open "Nine Suns" question, or sacred text is established.
5. **Kinship Structure:** Not yet established beyond the two founding lineages of the Azure Sky and Gale crowns — OPEN-AREA.
6. **Economic Paradigm:** Pluralist by kingdom, with trade arteries across kingdom lines: the Sapphire Road, the Southern March Trail, the Riverway, the Windward Trail and the Long Coast Reach (nine-suns-continent.md Section 5.11).
7. **Technology Stance:** No continental technology authority; standards are set by kingdom-level guilds and inter-kingdom trade agreements (nine-suns-continent.md Section 5.6). The per-domain Phase 1 technology profile is held in the two kingdom-state ledgers (Technology Profile).
8. **Cultivation Stance:** No continental cultivation institution; authority sits with kingdoms and sects. Wind-affinity practice is densest in the Eastern Windward and the Central Highlands, fire in the Ember Reach, water along the Lyraen basin and Southern Sea (nine-suns-continent.md Section 5.5).

**Depth Test status (Part 28 Area 17.3):** PARTIAL — enough for a scene of continental travel, cross-kingdom diplomacy or a multi-kingdom council from this file plus the member-kingdom ledgers; not enough for scenes that need a continental name, script or kinship custom (the year length is set in the calendar ledger).
