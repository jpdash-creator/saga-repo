## Section 2 — Classification (B/L)

**Origin Archetype (Part 16 Area 18.2):** **COMBINED — Ancestral-Divergent (18.2.1) + Post-Awakening-Evolved (18.2.5)** (author ruling, ruling 1). The Minotaurs are an ancient lineage that diverged in the deep cosmological past AND became sentient through a species-level cultivation awakening from a pre-sentient bovine ancestor. They are simultaneously very old and self-made: **personhood was achieved, not given.** The species is explicitly NOT Uplifted Origin (18.2.2) and NOT Created/Constructed (18.2.3) — no external agent granted sentience. Recorded consequences:
- A mythic-continuity culture resting on unbroken descent (the Ancestral-Divergent inheritance).
- The awakening as the founding event of species identity (the Post-Awakening-Evolved inheritance).
- The existence of non-awakened bovine cousins of the ancestral line as a live cultural fact and species-cultural tension axis (per Area 18.2.5).

**Anatomical Class (Part 16 Area 18.3):** **Augmented-Bipedal (18.3.2)** — already canon: Area 18.3.2 names the Minotaur as its own reference example — human-baseline anatomy augmented with horns, tail, and augmented musculature; the anatomical substrate of the species' S-3 innate moves **Chaprachup** and **Chalakudhaal** (Part 16 Area 18.3.2; Part 6 Area 12.3).

**SADS Positioning (Part 16 Area 18.5.1–18.5.2):** The species uses the standard species-agnostic SADS ladder (SADS-1 Juvenile through SADS-5 Legendary) with SADS-3 Mature as full species-typical anatomical maturity and full S-3 baseline output. SADS is orthogonal to cultivation realm (18.5.2). Canon-attested positioning: Bison at SADS-3 Mature (per `bison.md` Innate Moves registration). Species-specific scaling values above SADS-3 (Peak and Legendary expressions) not yet established — to be defined by author ruling.

## Section 4 — Species Cultural Carriers (Part 16 Area 18.4) (B)

### 4.1 — Species Language (Rule 18.18.4.1) — THE OLD TONGUE, SURVIVING IN THE MOVES

*(Author ruling, ruling 2 — this is the fact whose absence formally blocked Bison prose appearances; it is hereby established.)*

- **State of the language:** Ancient and largely lost even among Minotaurs. Daily speech retains only fragments — oaths, exclamations, ritual phrases. It persists as a **martial and ritual register**, not a conversational one.
- **What survives whole:** The technique-names — because **the name IS the technique**. To learn **Chalakudhaal** is to learn the word; the word cannot be lost while the technique is taught, and the technique cannot be taught without the word. This is why the martial register outlived the conversational language.
- **Canon-attested vocabulary (complete, as of this entry):** **Chaprachup** (tail-strike) and **Chalakudhaal** (horn-charge) — Species-Cultural Proper Names per Part 6 Area 12.3.2, the canonical reference words for Rule 18.18.4.1.
- **Startle-fragments (oath and exclamation register; author ruling, closing open item 11):** **Hrakudh**, **Dhapru**, **Ulchak**, **Orrul** and **Duth**. They are fragments said when startled, not technique-names, and not a vocabulary: no other word, phrase, address form or spelling of the old tongue is on record. Each is printed whole, in italics, untranslated (N16-14), never glossed and never respelled. What each is said to mean is kept in this file's lore for consistency and is never printed.
- **Naming convention note (grounded in the two attested words, per the glosses in `bison.md`):** Old-tongue technique-names are percussive compounds — they encode the rhythm and physical event of the technique in the word itself (*Chaprachup*: onomatopoeic terminal snap of the tail-whip; *Chalakudhaal*: *chala*, directed force-through-horn, fused with *kudhaal*, the impact-gathering charge — one word naming both windup and delivery). New old-tongue coinages must follow this convention.
- **The startled oath draws on the startle-fragments above and on nothing else.** A drafting agent does not coin any other oath-word, exclamation, fragment or address form of the old tongue; a new word needs an author ruling and follows the percussive-compound convention above, Part 28 Area 20 and Gate 1 Check #17. The language's proper name is still **NOT established. Do not invent.** In prose the fragment stands as one whole italic word in the character's spoken line. The narration may say how it sounded and what it did to the people who heard it (how loud, how low, whose head turned); it never says what it means (Rule 29.19.3.4) and never repeats it in a second, translated line (Rule 29.19.3.7). The private senses in this file's lore stay in the ledger.
- **Private senses, never printed:** Hrakudh, "by the line" (the horn-line of his First Horns); Dhapru, "the path remembers" (the practice that carries the ancestors); Ulchak, "hold the horns" (the lethal reflex held back); Orrul, "the weight is mine" (the capacity to kill carried personally); Duth, no sense (a sound the body makes). None invokes a god, a ruler or an empire.
- **Proper name of the language:** **NOT established. Do not invent.** Canon has no proper name for it; the ledger designation is "the old tongue." The proper name is flagged **Negotiable Canon — to be defined by author ruling** (Part 28 Locked/Negotiable Canon framework).
- **Bison-specific usage (cross-ref `bison.md`; ledger fact for his separate registration):** He thinks in the old tongue when he fights and swears in it when startled; he has not held a full conversation in it since the banishment.
- **Prose rendering:** Per N16-14 and Part 6 N6-14 — species-language terms appear in italics (bold for Named Moves) without in-prose translation.

### 4.2 — Species Lineage Rites (Rule 18.18.4.2) — THE FIRST HORNS

*(Author ruling, ruling 3.)*

- **The rite:** When a young Minotaur's horns finish growing, they are taken to be taught what the horns are FOR — the **Chalakudhaal** instruction, given by an elder of their own line, **once, never repeated.**
- **What the rite means:** It is the moment a Minotaur becomes responsible for their own capacity to kill. It is coming-of-age, technique-transmission, and moral induction in a single event.
- **Governance position:** The First Horns is the canonical species-cultural transmission protocol that Part 16 Area 18.6.3 anticipates ("a Minotaur receives **Chalakudhaal** instruction at a specific coming-of-age event") — the rite-of-maturity transmission mode, now formally established.
- **Bison-specific consequences (cross-ref `bison.md`; ledger facts for his separate registration):** Bison received the First Horns. **Exile bars him from ever GIVING it** — an exiled Minotaur may never stand as the elder of a line and transmit the rite. This is a permanent, structural consequence of the banishment, distinct from and additional to his suppressed cultivation.

### 4.3 — Species Collective Memory (Rule 18.18.4.3) — CARRIED IN THE BODY, RETAINED

*(Author ruling, ruling 4.)*

- **The species capability:** Minotaur collective memory is encoded in the cultivation itself — an ancestral record reached through one's own practice. Because the species cultivated its way into personhood (Section 2), **the path remembers**: to practice the species' cultivation is to touch the generations who practiced it before. This is the cultivation-encoded ancestral record mode that Rule 18.18.4.3 enumerates.
- **Severability:** Banishment CANNOT sever it. It is internal — no exile, distance, or cultural suppression removes what is carried in the body.
- **Bison's degraded access (cross-ref `bison.md`; ledger fact for his separate registration):** The Olympus Suppression Seal has made Bison's access faint and hard to reach. This is a second, quieter grief that nobody around him can see: **he is slowly losing his ancestors and cannot say so.** Both facts are canon — the species capability is retained in him, AND his access to it is degraded for as long as the Seal holds. *(P30-L8: the Seal's origin is Empire-of-Gods material — ledger fact only in Phase 1; the felt experience of faint ancestral access is Phase-1-safe so long as its cause is not surfaced.)*

### 4.4 — Species-Cultural Depth Preservation (Rule 18.18.4.4; N16-12, N16-13, N16-14)

Prose involving a Minotaur character must render the species-cultural layer alongside the cultivation-world layer: horn-lineage, old-tongue fragments, lineage-rite obligations, and the sensory reality of horns, tail, and mass in a human-scaled world. A Minotaur in prose is not a human in costume (N16-13). Flattening to cultivation-world standard is an N16-12 violation.

## Section 5 — Cultivation Access Profile (Part 16 Area 18.5.3) (B)

*(Author ruling, ruling 5 — BODY-FIRST, THE REST UPHILL. All five components ruled.)*

1. **Species-typical affinity clusters (Part 4 Area 1):** Earth-, body-, and strength-adjacent affinities are species-typical and common. **Wind is NOT species-typical, and Sovereignty-level attainment in it is not either** — Bison (Wind affinity at Affinity Level 8, Sovereignty) is an outlier among his own people and always was. Rare and absent affinity lists beyond this not yet established — to be defined by author ruling.
2. **Meridian architecture variations:** Minotaur meridians deviate from human-standard patterns: they are adapted to dense musculature and physical-path conduction. Recorded deviation: **favors body conduction** over human-standard energy-channel balance.
3. **Soul cultivation accessibility (Part 4 Area 2):** Slower and harder than human-standard. Soul cultivation is accessible but uphill for species-typical members.
4. **Body cultivation advantages:** **CONFIRMED — dense Minotaur musculature provides a strong body-cultivation affinity.** (Part 16 Area 18.5.3 already half-writes this as its own example — "a Minotaur's dense musculature provides a body-cultivation affinity"; the author ruling completes and confirms it.)
5. **Technique-learning modifiers (Part 6/7 learning speed):** Energy-path technique work is effortful for species-typical members; physical-path technique work is fast and deep.

**Significance note (canon consequence of the profile):** Bison is doubly remarkable against his own species baseline — a **Wind Sovereign (Affinity Level 8)** from a species built for earth and muscle, AND **perfectly balanced with technology (60%/60% CS/TS)** from a species whose typical members find non-body paths uphill. He is atypical twice over. (Author ruling, ruling 5; cross-ref `bison.md`.)

### 6.1 — **Chaprachup** (tail-strike)

- **Move Name:** **Chaprachup** (old-tongue Species-Cultural Proper Name, Part 6 Area 12.3.2)
- **Anatomical Derivation:** Minotaur tail — dense, muscular, whip-capable combat appendage; force from tail musculature contraction, rotational delivery through spine-to-tail linkage, terminal velocity at tail tip (per `bison.md`)
- **Canon Mechanics (attested via Bison's registration, Rule 18.18.6.2):** Move Type Intermediate (Part 6 Area 2); Combat Function Attack — Strike; Substrate S-3 Species-Innate (pure); Firmament-Lock immune per Part 6 Area 12.3.4(a); realm-independent — functional at all realms, output scaling by SADS anatomical maturity only; attested exemplar at Mastery grade, SADS-3 Mature (Bison)
- **SADS Availability Profile:** Accessible from SADS-1 at reduced output; full species-typical baseline at SADS-3 Mature; Peak/Legendary scaling not yet established
- **Species-Cultural Significance:** One of the two words of the old tongue that survive whole — the name is the technique (Section 4.1)
- **Transmission Protocol (Area 18.6.3 / Area 12.7):** Not yet established — to be defined by author ruling (the First Horns is specifically the **Chalakudhaal** transmission; **Chaprachup**'s protocol is not ruled)
- **Register row:** `ledger-named-moves/_index.md`, the row for Chaprachup (Part 6 Rule 6.5.2)

### 6.2 — **Chalakudhaal** (horn-charge)

- **Move Name:** **Chalakudhaal** (old-tongue Species-Cultural Proper Name, Part 6 Area 12.3.2)
- **Anatomical Derivation:** Minotaur horns paired with neck musculature and charging leg architecture; full-body momentum channeled through shoulder-neck linkage into the horn's impact face (per `bison.md`)
- **Canon Mechanics (attested via Bison's registration, Rule 18.18.6.2):** Move Type Intermediate (Part 6 Area 2); Combat Function Attack — Penetrating Attack; Substrate S-3 Species-Innate (pure); Firmament-Lock immune per Part 6 Area 12.3.4(a); realm-independent — output scaling by SADS only; attested exemplar at Mastery grade, SADS-3 Mature (Bison)
- **SADS Availability Profile:** Accessible from SADS-1 at reduced output; full species-typical baseline at SADS-3 Mature; Peak/Legendary scaling not yet established
- **Species-Cultural Significance:** The rite-technique — the word taught once at the First Horns; learning the word and becoming responsible for one's capacity to kill are the same event (Section 4.2)
- **Transmission Protocol (Area 18.6.3 / Area 12.7):** **The First Horns** — instruction by an elder of the recipient's own line, once, never repeated (author ruling, ruling 3)
- **Register row:** `ledger-named-moves/_index.md`, the row for Chalakudhaal (Part 6 Rule 6.5.2)
