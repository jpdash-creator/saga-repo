> **Purpose:** This folder is the one generic place for every Named Move in the Starward Saga. Each move is recorded here once, by name, whoever uses it and even if no one does yet. Where a person uses a move, it is also recorded in that person's own character-ledger entry (Section 3 of the hero, villain and side-character ledgers, in the `Signature Named Moves` or `Innate Moves` subsection), and the row here and the move block there point at each other. A move used by a group and not by one person (a species-innate move, a lineage or school technique) is pointed at from the species, faction or school entry that holds it. This is the "generic place for all registered moves and a used one with the character ledger" that the author asked for.
>
> It is a register of names with pointers. It is not a second home for what a move is or does: the user's entry owns every value of the move (Type, Function, mastery grade, substrate, derivation, availability and the rest), and this register holds none of them.

> **Manual-borne moves (Part 42).** The Named Moves that the manuals of `ledger-manuals/` carry are registered in that ledger by ID (`MV-…` and `PER-…`), in its CSV files, and are not listed row by row here. A manual-borne move becomes a full row of this register when its name is first written in prose, at the Gate 2 of that Area (Part 6 Rule 6.5.5), and its First Area Appearance is then also entered in its CSV row. The Pre-Write Ledger Check and the Part 6 Rule 6.2 collision scan read the Manual Ledger by ID and by name. Query: `manual_ledger_query.py`.

> **Where the author's directive and the rule live:** the author's words are quoted in Part 37 Area 3.9. This index states the scope and the schema for the people who keep the register. Part 6 Rule 6.5 stands on any difference with this index.

> **Scope — what is a Named Move.** A Named Move, Named Technique or Named Cultivation Practice as Part 2 Rule 3.2.1 and Part 6 define it: a technique, combat move or practice that bears a proper name (Part 6 Rule 6.3, Stage 4). Its kind is one of:
> - `Signature Named Move`: a move recorded under a character's `Signature Named Moves` subsection (any substrate except S-3).
> - `Innate Move`: an S-3 species-innate move (Part 6 Area 12), recorded under a character's `Innate Moves` subsection and in the species entry's innate move catalog.
> - `Lineage or school technique`: a move held by a lineage, school or sect and taught onward (Part 6 Area 7.2).
> - `Practice movement (unawakened)`: a body or weapon movement given a proper name for practice at a school (a stance, a drill, a form, a weapon pattern), taught to people who are unawakened and not yet tied to a stage or a realm. Once named it is a Named Move like any other. Its Move Type, Combat Function and substrate read `OPEN-AREA` in its defining block until an Area ties them; the rule gives it no power and no access it does not already have (Part 6 Rule 6.5.3).
> - `Other`: any Named Move that fits none of the above, flagged to the author.
>
> **Scope — what is not here.** A named item (a device, a tool, a remedy and the like) is not a Named Move; it is italic and is recorded in `ledger-named-items` (Part 28 Rule 20.8). A Named Relic or Artifact is a named item: it is italic (Part 12 Rule 2.2.3) and is registered in `ledger-named-items`, with its home entry in `ledger-energy-relics-artifacts`. A cultivation level designation is not a name of a move. The in-world registration of Part 6 Rule 6.3, Stage 5, which a sect or clan makes, is a different thing from this register.
>
> **Why a folder of its own, and not a kind in the named-item register.** A Named Move is excluded from named items: its governing Part is 6, its form is bold-italic (the named-item form is italic), its classification fields belong to Part 6, and each move has users whose own entries hold its values.
>
> **Move-Naming Law (Canon Amendment 2026-10-08).** A move name belongs to the move and never to the situation in which it is called: one or two words (three only as Part 42 Rule 42.12.2.3 allows), tied to the weapon type and to the level and grade of the move, fitted to its function, and never a place, a setting, a tactic or an enemy. One row written before the amendment does not meet it and is grandfathered: ***Open Guard Reading Cut*** (Chapter 001, frozen). A row is never renamed here; a stock name that is replaced keeps its old name in the `notes` cell of the move's manual row (Part 42 Rule 42.12.2.3(g)).
>
> **Form.** Named Moves keep their bold-italic form (Part 2 Rule 3.2.1), inside and outside dialogue, with no dialogue exception. The italic rule for named items and its roman break inside an italic passage do not apply to a move. The Entity Name cell below is written in the prose form, `***Name***`.

> **Entry schema (Part 37 Area 3.9).** One table below, one row per Named Move name. A move used by several people is one row with several users. The six standard columns of Part 37 Area 2.11 come first, in their order; three more follow. The B and L marks follow Part 37 Area 5.
>
> | Column | What it holds |
> |---|---|
> | Entity Name (B) | the move's name in the prose form, `***Name***` |
> | Classification (B/L) | the kind (above) |
> | First Area Appearance (B) | the Area that first wrote the name, `Area NN — Title (Ref: P.S.N.CCC.AA)`, or `Not yet established` |
> | Entity File (L) | the entry that holds the move's defining block, with the section: the first user's character-ledger entry, or the species, faction or school entry for a move no single person owns |
> | Status (L) | `Active`, `Dormant`, `Lost` or `Superseded` (below) |
> | Last Updated (L) | `YYYY-MM-DD` |
> | Users (L) | every person or group that uses the move, each as a pointer to the entry that holds that user's block, separated by semicolons, or `none yet` |
> | Naming Checks (B) | the Part 6 Rule 6.2 checks settled at the time: the register the name follows, the collision scan and its date, distinctiveness and, for a name made from 2026-10-08, the Move-Naming Law of Part 42 Rule 42.12.2.3; or `pre-register entry; checks not recorded` for a row entered by backfill |
> | Aliases (B) | other names, separated by semicolons, or `none` |

> **Status words.**
> - `Active`: the move is known and available to someone.
> - `Dormant`: the move is known and no one uses it now.
> - `Lost`: no living practitioner is known.
> - `Superseded`: the move has an evolved successor (Part 6 Rule 6.4); the successor has its own row.
>
> A row is never deleted. When the Area that first wrote a move is removed by a rewind, a redraft or a reset, the reconciliation returns that row's First Area Appearance to `Not yet established` and keeps the row. A change of name is a new Named Move with a new row (Part 6 Rule 6.4).

> **Update Protocol:** Per `guardrails/ledger/guardrail-ledger-update-law.md`, Part 37 Threshold O-5 and Part 6 Rule 6.5:
> - **New Row Trigger:** any Area that writes a Named Move, including a move named once, requires a new row at Gate 2, made by the Post-Write Ledger Update after `[CONFIRM WRITE]`, in the same pass in which the move is entered in the user's entry, each pointing at the other. A move the author sets directly in a ledger (a `/new-character` or `/update-ledger` pass) gets its row in that same pass, its First Area Appearance reading `Not yet established`. A move named only in a spec, or in a draft discarded by `[SKIP WRITE]`, is not entered (Part 38 Rule 1.3.2).
> - **Pre-Write Ledger Check:** the check reads this register for every move the Area will use, so the name is written exactly as registered. The Rule 6.2 collision scan of a new move name reads this register too.
> - **One owner:** the user's entry owns every value of the move; the row mirrors only name, kind, users, first Area and status. On a difference the entry stands, and the difference is a finding at the next ALCA pass.
> - **Update Trigger:** a new user, a change of status, a Type Upgrade that produces a successor (the old row becomes `Superseded`), or a new alias updates the row.

> **Mandatory Update Triggers:**
> - A Named Move written for the first time in a confirmed and written Area, or set in a ledger by the author
> - A new user of a registered move (update Users, and add the move block with its `Register row` line to the new user's entry)
> - A status change
> - A Type Upgrade that gives a move a successor (Part 6 Rule 6.4)
> - A rewind, redraft or reset that removes the Area that first wrote a move (First Area Appearance returns to `Not yet established`)

> **Marker Legend:** These markers appear in the entries of this ledger. Open cells otherwise carry `OPEN-AREA` or `OPEN-AUTHOR` (Part 37 Area 5).
> - `[ASKED]`: the author chose this value.
> - `[DERIVED]`: the drafter inferred this value from cited evidence and the author reviewed it in the digest without overriding it (Part 39 Rule 39.3.3.1); it is pending only when the entry also says flagged for author confirmation.
> - `[AUTHOR-PENDING]`: a question about this value has been raised and is not yet answered.
> - `[MARKED SLOT]`: a slot left empty on purpose until the author rules.
> - "flag, do not invent": when a scene needs the value, the prose may establish one small fact at a time under Part 28 Area 17, logged in this ledger and offered to the author as a draft value at the next Section close. A name or a major entity (Part 28 Area 20.4) goes to the author first, through the Decision Consultation Gate.

## Part 28 Area 17 — Lore note (Named-Move Register)

A row is a register entry, not a lore file. The Part 28 Area 17 lore of a move is the lore of its defining block in the user's entry. This register carries no lore of its own.

**Depth Test:** "Can a scene that uses this move be written without inventing what it is?" The defining block in the user's entry answers it; this register only says where to find it.

**Silent Overwrite Prohibition (Part 28 Area 17.5):** Once a move is registered, its name and kind are fixed under the Establishment Threshold; later Areas may enrich it and never contradict it.
