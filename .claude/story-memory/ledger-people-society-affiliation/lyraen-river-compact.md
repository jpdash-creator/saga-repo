## Section 3 — Terms (B)

**Canonical Terms (pre-prose kingdom-lore cross-reference; not yet directly established in an authored Area):**

The Compact governs the Lyraen River from headwaters (Windward Range) to the Southern Sea. Principal known function: customs and trade coordination among signatory kingdoms. Specific terms (tariff schedules, dispute resolution, transit rights, enforcement mechanisms) not yet established in prose.

The Riverway (Lyraen river-barge trade south) is the principal trade artery governed by the Compact (cross-ref: azure-sky-kingdom.md §5.11).

Lyraen customs-sharing between Azure Sky and Gale is also referenced under the Northern Accord (cross-ref: northern-accord.md Section 3 Provision 3) — the relationship between the Northern Accord's customs clause and the Compact's customs provisions not yet established in detail in prose.
