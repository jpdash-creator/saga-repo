## Section 3 — Terms (B)

**Core Canonical Provisions:**

1. **Combined Patrol of Windward Range Passes** — Azure Sky and Gale share patrol jurisdiction of the Windward Range passes under a combined corridor, held by **a single combined bilateral formation, the Northern Accord Expeditionary Wing, containing both Azure Sky and Gale soldiers serving together**, under **paired command — one Azure Sky officer and one Gale officer, neither outranking the other**; patrol-jurisdiction questions are governed by session protocol (see Provisions 5 and 6 below)

2. **Mutual Right of Asylum** — each kingdom is obligated to extend asylum to recognized nationals of the other in defined emergency circumstances; terms not yet established in prose beyond named existence

3. **Customs-Sharing on the Lyraen** — a customs and tariff coordination clause for the Lyraen River crossing shared between the kingdoms; cross-ref: Lyraen River Compact (separate multi-party instrument)

4. **Mutual Military Aid Against External Aggressors** — each kingdom is obligated to render military assistance to the other against external attack; triggers and thresholds not yet established in prose beyond named existence

5. **Amendment Protocol:**
   - Sovereign co-signatory required on both sides at least 2 hours before any patrol-jurisdiction session
   - The amendment protocol governs what can be addressed in a given session format

6. **Session Format Categories:**
   - **Review Session** — limited-agenda bilateral meeting under the Northern Accord; governs patrol-jurisdiction discussion and data review; does not constitute a full treaty event
   - **Full Treaty Event** — broader-scope treaty activation or amendment; requires additional protocol; not yet established in detail

**The Windward Protocol (Sub-Instrument):**

The Windward Protocol is a named sub-instrument of the Northern Accord governing bilateral data-sharing in the Windward Range passes. Key canonical provisions:
- Both sides exchange full monitoring datasets from their respective perimeter arrays
- Both sides have midpoint array access within the combined patrol corridor
- The Windward Protocol provides the bilateral verification mechanism for any future coordinated discharge operation in the combined patrol corridor: both sides' midpoint array access must be coordinated under the Protocol for any bilateral discharge operation — no such operation has yet occurred

## Section 6 — History (B)

**Pre-Story Foundation:** The Northern Accord predates the story — established at an unspecified historical moment as a formal bilateral treaty; it is structural background at story start, not a newly created agreement

**Second Bilateral Infrastructure Accord:** A prior historical treaty that commissioned both the Northern Accord corridor infrastructure and the Windward Protocol relay stations in the same construction period; exact date not yet established in prose; it precedes story start by at least enough time for the relay infrastructure to be established and operational

**Prior State:** The relationship between Azure Sky and Gale predates the Northern Accord — the original bilateral relationship produced the conditions for the formal treaty; historical details not yet established in prose

**Pair History:** Both kingdoms trace their founding to the same generational window — Theron the Twinned (Azure Sky founder) and Aerion the First-Winged (Gale founder); this shared founding-generation context is part of the historical basis for the bilateral relationship (nine-suns-continent.md §5.2). They are neighboring kingdoms of the Central Highlands that share the Windward Range as a common border zone; the patrol corridor is their primary shared geography. At story start the relationship is stable and active, with no known recent strain or shift. The pair's AFF position, axis positions, public layer and covert layer are the pair's row in Registry B of `_index.md`.

**Trigger Events:**
- Toward a stronger alliance (AFF-1 or AFF-2): a shared crisis in the Windward Range corridor that the two kingdoms resolve together, should one arise (none established in prose), which would show their crisis-resolution capability and may deepen the relationship; an external threat to either kingdom that triggers the mutual aid clause.
- Toward a weaker alignment: failure of a bilateral combined operation in the corridor, should one occur (none established in prose), with escalation and blame; unilateral action in the combined patrol corridor; violation of the Northern Accord amendment protocol.
