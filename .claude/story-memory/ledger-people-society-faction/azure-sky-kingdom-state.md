### 7.1 — Founding Event

The ruling state was founded by **Theron the Twinned** (first sovereign, seven generations ago — see azure-sky-kingdom.md §5.2 for the continent-scale founding narrative). The founding event of the ruling state specifically — as a political body distinct from the territory — is the **Twin Oath Compact**, a ratified instrument that binds the sovereign, the council, and the principal institutions of the kingdom to the dual-track obligation. The founding Compact is canonically preserved in two identical copies — one in the Sword Court archive, one in the Signet Court archive — and the kingdom's constitutional law requires both copies to be physically present at every accession.

### 7.2 — Doctrine

The Azure Sky ruling state's foundational doctrine is the **Twin Oath**: cultivation-side fealty and technology-side fealty, sworn simultaneously and equally weighted. Doctrinal corollaries canonically derived from the Twin Oath:
- **Paired Legitimacy** — no kingdom-wide edict is lawful without dual signature.
- **Proportional Investment** — kingdom budget, military investment, academic funding, and guild subvention must be allocated proportionally between cultivation and technology tracks, with deviations requiring formal dual-signature exception.
- **Dual-Track Nobility** — nobility rank is tied to demonstrated service in one track with honorable standing in the other; single-track service is ceiling-capped.
- **Non-Aggression Default** — the doctrine discourages expansionist war; Azure Sky's historical military posture is defensive-plus-diplomatic, with offensive action requiring dual-signature ratification at the highest threshold.

### 7.3 — Governance Structure

The state is a constitutional monarchy with three principal organs:
- **The Crown-in-Twin** — the sovereign pair (Varys + Valia) acting jointly; the primary executive and lawgiving authority.
- **The Four Great Officers** *(author-set)* — four parallel offices, each answering directly to the Crown-in-Twin rather than sitting in a two-caucus council: **Grand Marshal Ossian Kade** (military — sole command of both the Skysworn and the Azure Guard), **Chancellor Maren Cole** (civil administration — treasuries, registries, provisioning, provincial affairs), **Grandmaster Yun Ashvale** (Grandmaster of the Nine Halls — the crown's cultivation authority (anyone can learn, 2026-10-07); guardian and disciplinarian of the training halls training halls where structured instruction is given; others can train and be trained as well (anyone can learn, 2026-10-07); second only to the King and Queen; treats cultivation as both a craft to be taught and a danger to be governed), and **Grand Artificer Bramwell Cog** (Grand Artificer of the Iron Dawn — the crown's chief inventor and engineer; commands the forges, workshops, and experimental works pushing Azure Sky's craft beyond tradition alone; answers directly to the King and Queen on invention, infrastructure, and the machinery of war and peace alike). None of the four outranks another; each is parallel in power. (Cross-ref: ledger-character-side/ossian-kade.md, maren-cole.md, yun-ashvale.md, bramwell-cog.md.)
- **The Twin Tribunals** — the paired judicial bodies; the **Sword Tribunal** handles cultivation-related legal matters (academy discipline, cultivation disputes, cultivation-inflected criminal cases) and the **Signet Tribunal** handles technology-related legal matters (guild disputes, charter enforcement, technology-inflected criminal cases); matters spanning both tracks go to the **Reconciliation Tribunal**, which sits en banc. *(The Tribunals are a judicial, not executive, organ, and are not tied to any Great Officer's portfolio.)*

**Portfolio mapping:** all military-command authority (the Skysworn and the Azure Guard) is unified under **Grand Marshal Ossian Kade** alone (§7.4); the academy and training-hall function sits with **Grandmaster Yun Ashvale**; the guild and technology-invention function with **Grand Artificer Bramwell Cog**; the treasury-reserve-minima function with **Chancellor Maren Cole** (§7.6). No office named "Master of Arms", "Master of Charters", "Grand Marshal of Swords" or "Grand Marshal of Signets" exists.

**Interim Regency Succession (provisional — pending formal establishment of a permanent regency/succession plan):** if the King and Queen are both incapacitated, Regency passes first to **Chancellor Maren Cole** (civil counterpart to the throne), then to **Grand Marshal Ossian Kade** (author-set). This is an explicitly provisional/interim arrangement, not a finished constitutional settlement.

Administrative infrastructure below the Crown is a uniform provincial structure, coordinated through Chancellor Cole's civil-administration portfolio (which explicitly includes provincial affairs): each province has a paired administrative seat (a Sword Office and a Signet Office) under a paired Governor-and-Chartermaster team.

**Deputies' attendance custom:** when the Four Great Officers sit with the King, their seconds sit with the Queen. On ordinary mornings the custom runs as often the other way about — the Chancellor herself at the Queen's table and her Vice Chancellor across the span. It is unwritten practice rather than constitutional rule: nothing in the Twin Oath Compact or the Great Officers' portfolios compels it, and no instrument records it. Named seconds: **Vice Chancellor Edran Voss** (runs Chancellor Cole's office day-to-day) and **Marshal-Lieutenant Isolde Farrow** (the Grand Marshal's second for the Queen's table and road/dispatch traffic) (cross-ref: ledger-character-side/edran-voss.md, isolde-farrow.md).

**Second domain of the same custom:** the custom governs not only *attendance* but **the discharge of chartered offices**. When the Grand Artificer is with the King, the chartered office of **craft witness** comes to the **Vice Artificer**. The named third second is therefore **Vice Artificer Selene Marrow**, the Grand Artificer's second. Full treatment at **§7.11**; her character file belongs to `ledger-character-side/` and is not written from here.

### 7.4 — Military Command Structure

The kingdom's military is organized as **paired orders**, both now commanded by a single unified office rather than two separate marshals *(author-set)*:
- **The Skysworn** the cultivation-side military order; Azure Sky's front-line combat order, the blades that carry the kingdom's fight to the field; directly sworn to the cultivation-side oath; bound by personal oath rather than birthright — the sword-hand of the crown made real in steel and drill.
- **The Azure Guard** the technology-side military order; the signet-bearing order entrusted with the crown's sealed authority, standing watch over court, capital, and command itself; directly sworn to the technology-side oath. Where the Skysworn fight the kingdom's wars, the Azure Guard protects the throne that sends them.
- Both orders are headed by **Grand Marshal Ossian Kade** — a single, unified military office (cross-ref: ledger-character-side/ossian-kade.md), replacing the earlier paired "Grand Marshal of Swords" / "Grand Marshal of Signets" structure.
- **The Twin Host** — the combined-arms reserve formation that deploys both orders in integrated tactical configurations; doctrinally reserved for existential-tier conflict; last mobilized in the reign of Mireya the Stormbound (three generations ago, per royal annals).

**Naming disambiguation (critical — do not conflate):** "The Skysworn" and "The Azure Guard" are the two military ORDER names. They are entirely distinct from King Varys's personal cultivation AFFINITY, **Sovereignty — Swords (ceiling Level 8)** (cross-ref: varys.md Section 2, Locked Canon) and from his regalia, the "Zenith oath-blades" (§7.8). The affinity keeps the name "Sovereignty — Swords"; the regalia is named Zenith; the military orders share neither name. The relationship is one of flavor only (Varys wields a sword-affinity and, as King, sits above the chain of command that includes the Skysworn), not identity. Valia's affinity "Sovereignty — Healing", and the latent, unestablished affinities of Varya ("Sovereignty — Creation") and Aegon ("Sovereignty — Darkness"), are likewise unrelated to the order names.

Frontier-specific formations: the **Ember Reach Watch** (paired joint garrison of the western boundary towers); the **Northern Accord Expeditionary Wing** — per the author ruling, **ONE combined bilateral formation** carrying both Azure Sky and Gale soldiers on the combined Windward Range patrols, under **paired command: one Azure Sky officer and one Gale officer commanding together, neither outranking the other** `[ASKED]`. **Gale's side of the same formation is not written, derived or implied from here.** **The Wing carries its own faction file — `ledger-people-society-faction/northern-accord-expeditionary-wing.md`** — which is its home record and is not duplicated here; **Azure Sky's own ranks, rungs and the Ninth's place inside the Wing are at `azure-sky-military.md` §6.6.** The Expeditionary Wing musters in oath-bound **squads** — among them **the Ninth**, a squad of eight pairs — **sixteen soldiers** — commanded by a **Sword-Sergeant** (Corin Vale). ### 7.7 — Reputation and Myth

Continental reputation: Azure Sky is regarded as the exemplar of the dual-track ideal — respected for doing genuinely well what most kingdoms only attempt. Reputation for reliability (the kingdom's treaties are honored), for restraint (non-expansionist by doctrine), and for a distinctive paired aesthetic. In folk speech, "to hold with the Twin" is a continental idiom for a reliable partner; "to break the Twin" is the inverse.

Internal mythology: the **Ascending Twinship** — a popular belief (not doctrinally endorsed) that Theron the Twinned did not die but ascended, and that the founding Compact is therefore still spiritually enforced by Theron's unseen presence. The crown neither affirms nor refutes this belief.

### 7.9 — Iron Dawn and Nine Halls: Operational Capacity

Both institutions delivered their capacity reports jointly to King Varys, Chancellor Cole, Grand Marshal Kade, and clerk Doran Fell at the Sword Court.

**The Iron Dawn (Grand Artificer Bramwell Cog):**
- Forty complete blue-steel kit sets (kitted, tempered, both hands laid on every blank) achievable between the report date and first snow — a figure Cog states as fixed and refuses to round to forty-one.
- The bottleneck is not ore but inscription: blue-steel requires a cultivation-hand inscription laid into the grain while the metal still remembers being hot, and a signet-hand lamination set into the same blank before the temper closes over both; the two hands must be matched in timing and trust or the blank is ruined.

### A16.4a — ⚠️ THE TRADE IS THE TITLE (author ruling) — ADDRESS BELOW THE OFFICES

**THE RULING, VERBATIM.** `[ASKED]`

> **"The trade IS the title."**
>
> *"Porter, Keeper, Carter, Miller — the work fills the office slot exactly as Chancellor or Grandmaster does. Invents no new word, satisfies §13.11's role-title requirement, and is already what Fell's locked canon describes him doing."*

**THE GAP IT CLOSES, stated so no later pass reopens it.** **§13.11's Government Body modifier reads *"official role-title for all below sovereign."*** That instruction is complete for a Chancellor, a Grandmaster, a Governor or a clerk of record, because each holds an office the rule can name. **It was empty for everyone else.** A petitioner on the court floor, a porter in the colonnade, a stall-keeper at a gate, a carter on the levy road — **each of them is *below sovereign* and each of them holds no office**, so the rule reached them and had nothing to hand them. **No term existed, and the whole population of the kingdom below its registered offices had no address form at all.** **That is the slot this ruling fills.**

**THE MECHANISM, and it is one sentence.** `[ASKED]` **A person's TRADE OR FUNCTION occupies the role-title slot.** The word for the work **is** the role-title, and **§13.11 is satisfied on its own terms** — not waived, not excepted, not worked around. **A trade is a role.**

**THE FORM, built exactly as the office forms of A16.4 are built.** `[ASKED]`

| | Direct address | With the name | Built the same way as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A person whose trade is known** | **The trade word alone** — "Porter," "Keeper," "Carter," "Miller" | **The trade word plus the name** — "Porter [Name]," "Keeper [Name]" | **"Chancellor" / "Chancellor Cole"**; **"Grandmaster" / "Grandmaster Ashvale"** |

- **NO WORD IS COINED, AND THAT IS THE RULING'S OWN STATED GROUND.** `[ASKED]` **Porter, keeper, carter, miller are the ordinary nouns the language already carries for the work.** **Part 28 Area 20's Entity Naming Law is not engaged**, because nothing is named — an existing common noun is placed in an existing slot. **A pass that invents a word here has done the one thing the ruling was chosen to avoid.**
- **THE LIST IN THE RULING IS ILLUSTRATIVE, NOT A CLOSED SET.** `[DERIVED]` *Porter, Keeper, Carter, Miller* are four examples of the mechanism, not four permitted words. **Whatever the person's work is, that word is the title.** Flagged for confirmation at the next Check #21.
- **THE FORM DOES NOT VARY WITH THE SPEAKER'S RANK.** `[DERIVED]` A trade word is a role-title and behaves as one: **§13.11's rule is about the ADDRESSEE's role, not the speaker's standing**, exactly as it is for an office. A sovereign, a Great Officer and another porter all reach the same word.
- **IT IS AN ADDRESS TERM AND NOTHING ELSE.** `[IMMUTABLE]` It confers **no office, no rung, no standing in any institution, no precedence and no right of audience.** **Being addressed as a keeper does not make a person a keeper of anything in law.**

**⚠️ WHERE THE TRADE IS NOT KNOWN — WORKED OUT, BECAUSE IT IS THE NEXT QUESTION A DRAFTING WRITER HITS, AND THE SECOND HALF OF IT IS LEFT OPEN.** The ruling hands the slot to **the work**. So the question becomes: **is the person's work known in this scene?** Three cases, and they do not have the same answer.

| Case | Form | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The trade is known** — stated, visible, or on the record | **The trade word, with the name where the name is known** | `[ASKED]` — the ruling itself |
| **2. No trade is known, but the person's FUNCTION IN THE MATTER is** — the petitioner in a petition, the witness in a hearing, a party bound by the terms | **The function word, on the same construction** | `[DERIVED]` — **flagged, and it does not rest on the ruling alone.** The ruling assigns the slot to *the work*, and **a function in a matter is work.** **Canon already addresses people this way through Rule P29-13.8.4**, which gives the sovereign a petitioner *"by name where the file carries the name, by the matter where it does not"* (A16.2 CAT-6) — **a designation by the matter is what this row generalises** |
| **3. NEITHER a trade NOR a function in a matter is known** — a stranger in a corridor, a face in a crowd, a person with no work and no business in hand | **NOTHING. NO TERM EXISTS AND NONE MAY BE COINED** | `[AUTHOR-PENDING]` — **routed to the author as open item 14** |

**Case 3 in full, because it is the one a pass will be tempted to close.** `[AUTHOR-PENDING]` **The ruling reaches the work, and where there is no work it supplies nothing.** Nothing else in canon supplies it either: **§13.11's U-4 fixes the LEVEL — the most formal applicable register — and never the WORD**; **§13.5.3's *"my lord" / "my lady"* is registered only as the CAT-3 child-to-peer-child pre-introduction default** (A16.2 CAT-3) and is not extended here; and **A16.2 CAT-2 records that no Azure Sky fictive-kinship designation exists.** **A drafting agent who needs a word for case 3 must stop and say so** (Part 28 Area 20; the `ledger-character-side/_index.md` drafting bar).

**⚠️ THE COHERENCE CHECK THE RULING ITSELF NAMES, and it is a check rather than a registration.** `[ASKED]` The ruling's own reason records that this is *"already what Fell's locked canon describes him doing."* **`ledger-character-side/doran-fell.md` §1 carries, author-locked:** *"Everyone he speaks to receives the complete form of the court's own address system, and he attaches the trade or the function every time."* **That line is CITED HERE AS THE COHERENCE CHECK AND IS NOT RE-REGISTERED, NOT RESTATED AS NEW, NOT WIDENED AND NOT MOVED.** It is Part 39 conduct on his own file and it stays there; **what this subsection registers is the kingdom's Area 16 TERM, which that conduct was already using and which had no home in canon until now.** **The check passes: the kingdom's rule and that locked conduct describe the same act.** `doran-fell.md` was **not edited for this check.**

**FOUR THINGS THIS RULING DOES NOT DO, listed because each is a reach a later edit will be tempted to make.** `[ASKED]`
1. **IT BUILDS NO INSTITUTIONAL RUNG.** It answers **what a person is called**; it does not answer **what offices an institution has.** The Nine Halls' ladder below the Head Elder and the class of elders (*Author ruling, 2026-10-17*), the Iron Dawn's inscriber pairs, the provinces' clerks and the levy weighing's keepers are **structure questions**, and **open item 6 is untouched and still open.** Where such a person's work is known, the address form is available **without any rung being registered**, and **using it asserts nothing about the institution.**

4. **⚠️ IT IS SCOPED TO THIS KINGDOM AND THE GENERALISATION IS NOT DECIDED HERE.** `[AUTHOR-PENDING]` **The ruling arose from this kingdom's §13.11 gap and this kingdom's canon, and it is written into this kingdom's system only.** **Whether it generalises beyond this kingdom is NOT decided, NOT assumed and NOT implied**, and it is **routed to the author as open item 13.** **No other realm's address system is read from, written into, extended or contrasted with anywhere in this block** — this block is written on this kingdom's terms alone, and **another realm's system may run on an entirely different mechanism without any conflict arising.**

**FCIA (§13.14) — the failures this subsection adds.** **MAJOR** — **a coined word used where the trade or the function was already available** (the ruling's whole point is that no coinage is needed); **a case-3 person given a term at all.** **MINOR** — a trade word carried upward into a CAT-5, CAT-1 or CAT-4 slot; a trade word written as though it registered an office or a rung.

**Placement and threshold note** — *why this block is here, in the style of the §7.9, §7.11 and Collective Behavior Code notes above.*

**Area 16 assigns these slots to the KINGDOM, not to a character.** **Rule P29-13.7.1** says it in those words: *"Azure Sky Kingdom has its own lower-to-sovereign address vocabulary."* **Rule P29-13.3.3**'s unfilled slot is captioned *"Azure Sky Kingdom"*, not *"Varya."* A kingdom-wide system recorded on any single character's file would become that character's, and would then have to be duplicated onto every other file that needs it — a direct conflict with **Part 37's No Duplication Across Ledgers rule**, and the same reasoning that put the **counter-signature law at §7.10** and the **standards-authority record at §7.11** on this file rather than on the Queen's.

**The per-character receiving palettes stay exactly where they are.** `varys.md` §7, `valia.md` §7 and `varya.md` §7 record **how others address that one person**, and the Locked palette is one of them. **This block is the kingdom's system**; the two are **cross-referenced, never merged**, and **no character ledger was edited by this block** — the §7 address-block additions the waiting files need are a **separate character-side task**, listed as its own task and not performed here. **The CAT-5 two-layer ruling makes that separation load-bearing rather than merely tidy:** the Locked palette is now **the governing everyday layer of CAT-5**, and it governs **from `varys.md` §7 and `valia.md` §7, where it already sits.** **Those two files are not edited by this block either**, and **a later edit that copies their palettes into this block, or re-assigns their terms across settings from here, is duplicating and overriding Locked canon** (Part 37, No Duplication Across Ledgers).

**Not a separate file.** A standalone `ledger-people-society-faction/azure-sky-address.md` was considered and **declined on the §7.9 / §7.11 precedent**: this is the ruling state's own institutional law, the same class of content as its counter-signature mechanics and its standards authority, and **it tracks no new entity.** Part 37 **Threshold F-1** is not triggered — no organization, guild, unit, bloc or association is created by an address system, and every institution named above is already tracked, either in this file's §7.3 or in `nine-halls.md`.

**Why after the Collective Behavior Code block.** Both are **addenda to the Part 37 Area 2.6 Faction Ledger template** rather than new template Sections, and the template's own sections run **1 through 7** in this file. The Collective Behavior Code block above was **read for cross-reference only** — its provisions, its member list, its per-member table and its open items are **untouched** by this block.

**Four things this block deliberately does not do.** It writes **no military address** (A16.5). It writes **no Part 29 Voice Register or Prosodic Signature content and implies none** — **Rule 39.2.3.1**: address **TERMS** are Area 16's and are set here; naming **HABIT** is **Part 39 Layer L10**, lives in the character ledgers' `B39-L10-NAME-*` lines, and those lines were read for boundary only and are **untouched**. It activates **no Phase 2+ rule** — **Part 30 Law P30-L8** and **Rule P29-13.9.2** are honored absolutely and **no Moon Sect address form appears.** And it contains **no cross-character comparison** (author ruling; the rule concerns characters only, confirmed): naming a character to say what they are called is a registration; setting one character's forms against another's is the thing forbidden, and it does not occur above; nor does this block set one kingdom's forms against another's.
