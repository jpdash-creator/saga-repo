### 6.1 — Standing at the Levy Weighing

The Concord's established institutional function is its position at **the levy weighing**, the standing venue below the palace where the kingdom's blue-steel export lots are weighed for the crown's principal revenue (`azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §7.6). The Concord weighs the export lots with its own proof-beam, and the levy office keeps its beam beside it. The arrangement is **two instruments, neither subordinate**. What happens when the two disagree is OPEN-AREA.

### 6.2 — Legal Standing Under the Charter

The Concord is a **charter counterparty of the crown, not a subordinate of it.** Which charter this is, what it provides for a standards dispute, and which body granted it are **OPEN-AREA**; do not name or infer them from this file.

### 6.3 — Conduct Record

No proceeding involving the Concord is established. Its conduct record is OPEN-AREA and is neither a scandal nor a vindication until an Area shows one.
