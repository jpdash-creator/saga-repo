### 6.1 — What the Wing Is

The Northern Accord Expeditionary Wing is the standing frontier formation that holds the **Windward corridor** for both crowns at once. Canon fixes the ground and the instrument: the Windward Range passes are **patrolled together under the Northern Accord**, **not contested between the two kingdoms**, and the corridor carries **bilateral monitoring under the Windward Protocol** with both sides exchanging full datasets and both sides holding midpoint array access (`windward-range.md` §3; `northern-accord.md` §3).

**What the ruling fixes, and it is the whole shape of this entity.** `[ASKED]` The Wing is **one formation containing soldiers of both kingdoms, serving together** — not two same-named forces working in parallel. **A soldier of the Wing is a soldier of the Wing.** They remain a subject of their own crown, sworn where they were sworn, paid where they are paid and going home where they go home; what the ruling settles is that **the formation they stand in is single**, and that its command, its code and its patrols are the single formation's, not a negotiated meeting of two.

**What this does not change** `[DERIVED]`: nothing about either kingdom's sovereignty, either kingdom's own military orders, or either soldier's own crown-side standing. Corin Vale's Azure Sky binding, and Brannic Ost's and Vaneth's Gale bindings, all stand exactly as their files hold them — see §4.

**On "the Ninth."** The one unit whose interior canon establishes is Corin Vale's: **eight pairs, sixteen soldiers, commanded at a non-commissioned rank.** **The rung is fixed by the governing table rather than chosen — Part 22 Area 5.4 puts the Specialist Grade (Sergeant) at Squad scale, so the Ninth is a squad.** **"The Ninth" remains its proper name, unchanged.** **Every other rung of the ladder is another pass's to write** — **[UNIT-RUNG NAME — MARKED SLOT]**. **The code below does not depend on the Ninth's composition**, because it binds **the patrol party**, not the unit roster.

### 6.3 — The Corridor's Arithmetic — Why the One Provision Costs Something Every Day

The corridor's staffing shortfall is canon, and it is the King's own synthesis at `azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §7.9, quoted here as the **ground of the code's cost**: the Windward corridor **"will continue to be held by the same seven [squads] doing the work of nine."** *(The bracketed word is the standard unit word; §7.9 is not edited from here. **⚠️ WHOSE SOLDIERS THOSE SEVEN AND NINE COUNT — AUTHOR-RULED, AND THE QUESTION IS CLOSED.** `[ASKED]` Two military passes read this figure and disagreed about it; the author has ruled. Verbatim selected option: ***"The whole corridor — Wing is company scale."*** — ***"Seven squads now, nine at establishment, both kingdoms included. The Wing is a company of roughly 112–144. **What the ruling fixes, in four lines** `[ASKED]`:

- **Current strength: seven squads, 112 soldiers.** **Establishment: nine squads, 144 soldiers.** The shortfall is **two squads, thirty-two soldiers**, missing from the corridor rather than from one kingdom's share of it.
- **The Wing's rung is COMPANY** — a combined company of roughly 112–144.
- **Why the figure lives in an Azure Sky record, and this is the reasoning that makes the numbers cohere.** `azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §7.9 is a record of **Azure Sky's kit production** and the cohorts raised to carry it.
