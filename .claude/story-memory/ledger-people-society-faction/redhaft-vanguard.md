## Collective Behavior Code (Part 39 Layer L19) (B/L)

*(Part 37 Area 2.6 Collective Behavior Code addendum; block schema per Part 39 Area 41.4; governing rules 39.23.1.1 through 39.23.1.5. **Authored from the plan facts of Chapter Spec 1.1.1.003, confirmed 2026-10-08.** Provenance is marked field by field: `[DERIVED]` marks a provision worked out from those plan facts and flagged for author confirmation at the next Gate 1 Check #21 touch; `OPEN-AREA` and `OPEN-AUTHOR` mark a field left open on purpose. No provision here is author-locked. This code is written on its own terms only: no comparison to, contrast with or derivation from any other faction's code, collective or person appears in this block.)*

**Code Status:** **Registered 2026-10-08** *(pre-draft registration, Part 39 Rule 39.23.1.5; derived fields flagged)*
**Members currently bound:** Wulfric Rask (`ledger-character-villain/wulfric-rask.md` §11.5; he leads the whole Vanguard) · Hadric Stoll (Tier C; his conformance line is his own registration's) · the company's line raiders and skirmisher (Tier D; the code shows as their conformance, not as a membership registration, Part 39 Rule 39.3.5.4). **No other collective code binds them in the plan.**

**Shared conduct — what every member displays:**

- **NO MARK.** `[DERIVED]` The company carries no colour, no banner and nothing that names a kingdom, and its hafts stay wrapped in sacking until the sign. *(Evidence: Chapter Spec 1.1.1.003 Area 01 anchor 4 and Area 02 anchors 1 and 2: plain clothes, hafts coming out of sacking.)*
- **ONE BODY.** `[DERIVED]` The line breaks cover and works together; no member goes in alone or out of step. *(Evidence: Area 01 anchor 4 and Area 02 anchor 3: the line comes through the hedge as one body.)*
- **THE SIGN IS THE LEADER'S.** `[DERIVED]` The company starts on the raid leader's sign, holds on the raid leader's count and order, and breaks off on the raid leader's signal; no member ends the raid on their own. *(Evidence: Area 02 anchors 2 and 4 and Area 03 anchor 5. The raid leader's own registered Signature carries the same sign and count, `wulfric-rask.md` §11.2.)*
- **THE LINE IS QUIET.** `[DERIVED]` No war-cry, no name called and no colour named on the road; what is said is the short order. *(Evidence: the plan shows the company seen from outside and silent, with a few line raiders speaking short orders.)*
- *Provision removed 2026-10-18 on the author's ruling: NO SOLDIER'S BLOOD (added 2026-10-17). The Vanguard may kill soldiers; the code no longer bars it. The written Chapter 003 (Ref: 1.1.1.003) still carries the king's order in its narration until that Chapter is revised.*

*The group-scene test (Rule 39.23.1.3): where three or more members stand together, the shared conduct shows before any member's deviation from it; each provision is shown at least once in a scene that carries the company. Where a subordinate member and the raid leader are both present, the subordinate's modulation shows before any dialogue acknowledges the arrival (Rule 39.23.1.4).*

**Shown at Chapter 003 (Ref: 1.1.1.003), provision by provision. This note changes no provision, tag or level; the tags above stand as they were.**

- **NO MARK.** The company wears "brown and grey and undyed wool" in coats "bought at three markets", its hafts "bound in rag along the grip" under sacking, and on none of them "a badge, a cord, a colour or a word"; the sacking comes off the hafts in one pull at the sign.
- **ONE BODY.** Five men step out of the trees "side by side, in one stride" and go for the front of the file "as if they shared one pair of legs"; Stoll's squad pulls the wrap together ("seven pulls made one long tearing sound of cloth") and the three at the front swing "as one from the trees to the ditch"; the rear squad rises out of the trees together at the third wagon.
- **THE SIGN IS THE LEADER'S.** Rask's right hand rises to the height of his shoulder and comes down once to send the first six; a second lift sends the rest; the order the squad leader gives is that "when the hand goes up and does not come down, you stop, whatever you have in your hands". The butt of Rask's halberd struck once on the road draws the company back to the trees; his hand raised and kept up closes the raid. Stoll's own order breaks at the soldier's horn, and it is Rask who calls the count of the runners and orders the front held and the third wagon taken.
- **THE LINE IS QUIET.** "There will be no cries on the road and no names"; the company takes the answering horn "without a sound". The words spoken are the squad leader's order before the sign, Rask's count and order, and one dry voice that says to leave the officer for last and break the boards first. The text's own phrase for the company's silence is "the line took it as it had taken everything, without a sound".
- **Greeting and deference; in-group markers; conduct toward outsiders.** No greeting is shown, and none is set. Deference is shown as moving, holding and withdrawing on the raid leader's sign, as the code has it. The unwrapped haft, the plain coats and the quiet are shown as in-group markers. The company gave no mark and no name on the road and withdrew on the sign with its spoil and its fallen, as the code has it for outsiders; no parley, demand or terms were shown.

**Greeting and deference ritual:** `OPEN-AREA` — no greeting, salute or form of address is set, and none is coined. **Deference** `[DERIVED]`: shown by moving, holding and withdrawing on the raid leader's sign, and by nothing else that is established.
**In-group markers:** `[DERIVED]` the haft wrapped in sacking and bare of colour; plain clothes; the line's quiet under the sign. **Not established, deliberately:** any badge, token, mark, banner, colour or spoken form belonging to the formation. None is invented (Part 28 Area 20).
**Conduct toward outsiders:** `[DERIVED]` the formation gives no mark and no name; it takes what a raid is for and withdraws on the sign, and does not stay to be known. **Not established:** any parley, demand, terms or reply to an outsider; none is invented.
**Conduct when the group is criticized:** `OPEN-AREA`
**Induction conduct:** `OPEN-AREA` — no rite, oath or term is set. The formation's written texts are the Manual Ledger's (`ledger-manuals/kingdoms/brackenhold.csv`, the rows whose institution cell names the Vanguard).
**Enforcement:** `OPEN-AREA` — the sign and the count are the raid leader's; no penalty, rite or officer of discipline is set.

**Registered deviations by member:** Wulfric Rask — conformance `B39-L19-CONFORM-01` (exemplary; holds the code more exactly than it requires) and seam `B39-L19-SEAM-02` (he walks while the line runs, against ONE BODY, and has never explained why; registered 2026-10-18, `ledger-character-villain/wulfric-rask.md` §11.5).
