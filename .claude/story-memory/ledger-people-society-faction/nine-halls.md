**First Area Appearance:** Chapter 004 — The Measure of a Gift (Ref: 1.1.1.004), Area block 02 *(the Nine Halls are named on the page there, and "the halls" and "her halls" are Yun Ashvale's own words in its opening paragraph; their head and the crates for "the school" had been on the page in Chapter 003 (Ref: 1.1.1.003) without the name)*
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-21

> **Why this file exists (short form; full record at the end).** The threshold note at `azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §7.9 kept the Nine Halls out of its own file and said the call **"should be revisited rather than treated as permanently closed"** if the institution ever acquired *"a named subordinate staff roster."* The author established **Elder Kellan Rothe**, the Nine Halls' first named subordinate, and gave **explicit direction** to create this file — satisfying Part 37 Area 1.4 (an author-directed faction file). The §7.9 **operational capacity record stays in that file and is cross-referenced here, never duplicated.**

> **Two canon facts this file honours exactly, and never softens.** **(a)** Grandmaster Yun Ashvale is the crown's cultivation authority (anyone can learn, 2026-10-07) (`azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §2, §7.3), and *sole* is taken at its word: **Elder Rothe holds no cultivation authority of his own, and no other elder of the class holds any** *(Author ruling, 2026-10-17: the class of elders leaves this fact unchanged)* — no certification power, no crossing decision, no independent cultivation ruling, anywhere in this file. **(b)** The Nine Halls is the umbrella institution over the kingdom's three named cultivation academies — Sapphire Ascent, Lyraen Heights and Windward Reach (`ledger-worlds-location/azure-sky-kingdom.md` §5) — by author ruling (`nine-halls.md` §6.1a); nothing is invented here beyond that ruling.

> **AMENDED 2026-10-17 — THE NINE HALLS AMENDMENT (Author ruling, 2026-10-17; the author-directed ledger step made before Chapter Spec 1.1.1.004 is drafted, Ref: 1.1.1.004).** Each element it changes is marked *Author ruling, 2026-10-17* where it stands. In short: **(1) the quiet is kept only in cultivation and meditation areas; elsewhere the floors are loud with talk, excitement, joking, banter and taunting, to foster competition, under a guard line:** the taunting is the students' own and is never the halls' lever, no instructor or office shames a student for pace, and *"not yet"* keeps its no-shame reading. **(2) The SHOWN, NOT TOLD element is dropped altogether:** the wordless demonstration is Kellan Rothe's own singular behavior, and he sometimes explains as well; "a verbal explanation is a last resort" and "you are handed no manual" go with it; explanation is shared and sought freely in the Nine Halls and the Academy, with no rigidity, the Grandmaster included; students receive manuals in their studies (implied, and off the page). **(3) Grandmaster Yun Ashvale is also Headmaster of Sapphire Ascent Academy.** **(4) The elders are a class:** Kellan Rothe is the **Head Elder**, and the other elders each hold a subject (weapon, cultivation, powers, affinity and others). The code's "two locked elements" are therefore **one**: NOBODY IS FORCED ACROSS. **Not touched by the amendment:** header fact (a) above; the author-locked premise that cultivation itself cannot be controlled; the Grandmaster's register (the future indicative, no words of permitting or forbidding); every address form (the registered "Elder" and "Elder Rothe" stand, and how the Head Elder and the other elders are addressed is an open address item, §6.5 item 14). Where this file says "the Elder" and means Kellan Rothe, it reads the Head Elder.

> **AMENDED 2026-10-20 — THE NINE HALLS OF SAPPHIRE ASCENT ACADEMY (author amendment 2026-10-20; the author-directed ledger step made before Chapter 1.1.1.007 is drafted, Ref: 1.1.1.007).** Each element it changes is marked *author amendment 2026-10-20* where it stands. In short: **(1) Sapphire Ascent Academy, which teaches from the unawakened to the end of the Senior realm, has nine halls** (the Library, the Quest Hall, the Lower Treasure Hall, Cultivation and Breathing, then one hall each for the Skin, Muscle and Tendon stages, the Bone, Blood and Marrow stages and the Soul stage, and two Senior halls); the list, what each hall is for, the quest levels and the points are held in `ledger-worlds-location/sapphire-ascent-academy.md` §5.2 and §5.3 and are not copied here. **(2) The Warrior and Champion level has its own separate nine halls**, with similar levels, focused on Warriors and Champions "in a different way", and its people may also join the army. **(3) The umbrella stays:** the Nine Halls is the umbrella, with its seat in Azorion, over Sapphire Ascent (the foundation, from the unawakened to the end of the Senior realm), Lyraen Heights (the middle academy, for Warriors and Champions) and Windward Reach (the apex, for Rulers). **(4) Also recorded at this step (author ruling 2026-10-20): "Teacher" is the pupils' form of address for an instructor** (an instructor and not an elder). **Not touched by the amendment:** header facts (a) and (b); the author-locked premise that cultivation itself cannot be controlled; the Grandmaster's register; the registered "Elder" and "Elder Rothe"; the address forms of the Head Elder and the other elders (open item 14); and the order of the Head Elder's circuit.

### 6.1 — What the Halls Are

The Nine Halls are the crown's cultivation training institution — canonically, **"training halls where structured instruction is given; others can train and be trained as well (anyone can learn, 2026-10-07)"** (`azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §7.3). Their head sits among the kingdom's Four Great Officers, none of whom outranks another, each answering directly to the Crown-in-Twin. The institution's governing posture is given in the same passage: cultivation is treated **"as both a craft to be taught and a danger to be governed."** Those two halves — craft and danger — are the whole of what canon says the halls are for, and the Collective Behavior Code below is that pair rendered as conduct.

**On the name.** *(Author amendment 2026-10-20.)* **Sapphire Ascent Academy has nine halls**, each with its own purpose: Hall 1 the Library; Hall 2 the Quest Hall; Hall 3 the Lower Treasure Hall; Hall 4 Cultivation and Breathing, with several classrooms; Hall 5 for the Skin, Muscle and Tendon stages; Hall 6 for the Bone, Blood and Marrow stages; Hall 7 for the Soul stage; Hall 8 for the Senior realm and the deeper use of cultivation and energy; and Hall 9 for the Senior realm, other cultivation methods and different ways. The quest levels, the points and each hall's purpose are held at `ledger-worlds-location/sapphire-ascent-academy.md` §5.2 and §5.3 and are not copied here. The Warrior and Champion level has its own separate nine halls (§6.1a), which are **not listed anywhere**: no drafting agent may invent a list for them or for the apex academy, and what the umbrella's own name "the Nine Halls" denotes is open (§6.5 item 4). *(The Elder's registered morning round covers "every hall, same order" — `kellan-rothe.md`, author lock 8b — which establishes that the halls are plural and walkable in one morning. The amendment of 2026-10-20 sets the Academy's halls and does not set the order of the circuit, which stays open.)* *Shown at Chapter 007 (Ref: 1.1.1.007), Area block 01 (an instance, not a custom): the Head Elder's round passes the nine halls in numeric order before the first bell, the Library first, then the passage where the Quest Hall and the Lower Treasure Hall face each other, then the Fourth to the Ninth Hall, and the first bell begins while he stands in the Ninth. Nothing else about the circuit is set by the page.* *(History: before 2026-10-20 this paragraph held that it was not established what the name denotes, that no canon listed the halls, and that no list could be invented.)*

### 6.1a — THE UMBRELLA RULING — author directive

**The ruling.** `[ASKED]` **The Nine Halls is the umbrella institution over all three of the kingdom's named cultivation academies** — **Sapphire Ascent** (primary foundation school, in the capital), **Lyraen Heights** (middle academy, on the Windward Reach near the Lyraen headwaters) and **Windward Reach Academy** (apex academy, at the kingdom's highest monastic altitude) — as those three stand at `ledger-worlds-location/azure-sky-kingdom.md` §5.5, **which is unaltered by this ruling**. **The seat of the Nine Halls is in Azorion.** *Shown on the page (Chapter 004, Ref: 1.1.1.004, Area block 02): "This was Sapphire Ascent Academy, the first of the kingdom's three, and above it stood the Nine Halls, which her mother said were the roof over all of them." The page gives the umbrella as the queen's saying, as Varya remembers it, and places the Academy as the first of three; the other two are not named on the page.*

**What that settles, and it settles the file's principal question.** Of the five candidate readings of the relationship — *"a fourth and separate establishment, the crown's own halls alongside the academies, an umbrella over them, a stage before or after them, or the internal name for part of one of them"* — **the author took the third.** The other four are **closed and are not to be revived.**

**Three consequences, stated so they are not re-derived later:**
1. **The Nine Halls is not a fourth academy**, and it is **not** another name for Sapphire Ascent. It is the institution **above** the three.
2. **The three academies keep their established stage roles unchanged** — foundation, middle, apex. **The umbrella does not flatten them**, and nothing in this ruling merges, renames or relocates any of the three.
3. **The full ladder runs at every hall** `[ASKED]` — the student ladder of §6.1b is the institution's, not any one academy's, so a trainee and a graduate are both meaningful terms wherever in the Nine Halls a person stands.

**What is still NOT established, and is not inferred:** how a student **moves** between the three academies; whether the Grandmaster's authority reaches inside the day-to-day of Lyraen Heights Academy and Windward Reach Academy or only the institution above them; whether those two academies keep heads of their own, and what those heads are called; and how the **Academy-Guild Accord** (`azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §7.5), which binds *"the three academies and three guilds"* and does not name the halls, sits against an umbrella that now stands over its signatories. **That last one is a real question and it is flagged, not answered** — open item 10 at §6.5.

**Closed for Sapphire Ascent Academy** *(Author ruling, 2026-10-17)*. Grandmaster Yun Ashvale is also the **Headmaster of Sapphire Ascent Academy**: the Academy keeps a head of its own, and that head is the Grandmaster herself. Her authority reaches inside the Academy's day-to-day as its Headmaster. She rules the first practice bouts of an intake herself, and on other days instructors or elders rule. This answers, for this one Academy, the question of whether the Grandmaster's authority reaches inside an academy's day-to-day and whether an academy keeps a head of its own (open item 11 at §6.5); it changes nothing about Lyraen Heights Academy or Windward Reach Academy, and it changes no fact of the sole-authority line (header fact (a)).

**The stage each academy teaches, and the Warrior-and-Champion halls** *(author amendment 2026-10-20; the reconciliation the author chose)*. The umbrella stands exactly as ruled above, with its seat in Azorion, over **Sapphire Ascent Academy** (the foundation, from the unawakened to the end of the Senior realm; its nine halls are at §6.1), **Lyraen Heights Academy** (the middle academy, for Warriors and Champions) and **Windward Reach Academy** (the apex, for Rulers). Certification, the crossing decision, and every cultivation ruling in Azure Sky terminate with her office and nowhere else.

**The Head Elder** (Kellan Rothe) enforces the standard and holds none of that authority, and **no other elder of the class holds any of it** *(Author ruling, 2026-10-17: the elders are a class and each holds a subject; certification, the crossing decision and every cultivation ruling stay with the Grandmaster's office alone)*. The distinction is load-bearing and is not to be blurred in prose: **the Elder can require a movement repeated a hundred times and cannot pass anyone through a threshold.**

### 6.3 — The Threshold Doctrine

The institution's governing doctrine on crossings is recorded at `azure-sky-kingdom-state.md` §7.9 and is quoted here as the ground of the code, with the capacity record itself left in that file: **eleven cultivators stand at Warrior Level 9 Peak, "close enough to the threshold that a forced crossing could push all eleven across within a month — but Ashvale will not force the crossing."** Her stated reason is that an unset foundation **"does not fail cleanly," it cracks silently and the failure surfaces a year or two later, sometimes on the mountain itself** — and her own framing is that **she would rather deliver six Champions she trusts than seventeen she does not trust to survive their second winter on the pass.**

The doctrine has an exact counterpart in her registered conduct, and the two are consistent rather than merely similar: her immutable `B39-L08-LINE-99` is **THE CHOICE IS NEVER HERS** — "she does not permit and does not forbid — the crossing belongs to the crosser; she will not take the choice from its owner, not for rank, not for need, not to save a life she has already read the ending of" (`yun-ashvale.md` §11.2). The halls therefore neither push a cultivator across nor bar one: **the crossing belongs to the crosser, and the institution's whole art is what it arranges around the choice** — her `B39-L16-TEACH-99`, SHE ADJUSTS THE CONDITIONS.

The institution is measured internally by who is still standing in their second winter, not by who crossed this season — the Grandmaster's own stated arithmetic at §7.9. `[DERIVED]` from the locked code element (NOBODY IS FORCED ACROSS; the second element was dropped, *Author ruling, 2026-10-17*) and the §7.9 doctrine; flagged for author confirmation.

What is established is what the place costs those who run it: the Grandmaster's `B39-L29-WATCH-99`, **THE NOT-HELPING** — "she will watch a student fumble for an hour rather than steal the learning, within arm's reach the whole time, and the not-helping is **visible effort**" — and the Elder's `L16-DEPFAIL-99`, **THE EMPTY HALL**, the unlogged hour after everyone has gone.
