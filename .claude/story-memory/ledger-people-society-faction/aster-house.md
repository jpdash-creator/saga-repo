### 6.1 — What the House Is

Aster House is the Azure Sky Kingdom's healing house, the hospital of the capital, and it heals by **technology medicines and by cultivation together**. It has one head and no deputy leader: Queen Valia, as **Healer Prime of Azorion**. Its healers stand at three strengths, and **none stands at the Senior realm**: five primary healers at the late Champion realm; ten to fifteen at the early Champion realm and the late Warrior realm; thirty and more at the early Warrior realm. It keeps **branches in other Azure Sky cities**, and the prime healers visit and support them. All of this is the author's ruling of 2026-10-17. **What the page has shown of it so far (Chapter 004, Ref: 1.1.1.004, Area block 01):** a ward, the Healer Prime at the bedside, one primary healer (Champion Level 8 Mid), one young healer ("a Warrior less than a year"), one unnamed healer in the first room, and a glass pipe beside a shining palm. The page does not show the composition beyond those, any branch, any prime healer visiting a branch, or the source of any technology medicine.

**Two words, not yet settled as one.** The author's wording uses **primary healers** (the five at the late Champion realm) and **prime healers** (who visit the branches). Whether the two name one office is **OPEN-AUTHOR**; the ledger keeps both words as the author gave them and merges nothing. *On the page of Chapter 004: "primary healers" is written (Berta Thaxter is "one of the house's five primary healers"), "Healer Prime" is the queen's title, and "prime healers" in the plural is not written.*

**The kingdom hospital of Chapter 003.** What the Court called "the kingdom hospital" in Chapter 003 (Ref: 1.1.1.003) is, from the author's ruling of 2026-10-17, the ward of Aster House. The Chapter 003 words stay true as a descriptor and are history; the pointer is at `ledger-worlds-location/azure-sky-kingdom.md` §5.16 and in the registry row of `ledger-worlds-location/_index.md`.
