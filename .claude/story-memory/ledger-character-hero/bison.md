### 11.3 — Contradiction and Cost (B)

**Registered theme:** ***the craftsman in exile, the sworn brother, and the wind that means freedom — a sovereign of the freest force in the world, sealed into a village, mending it with his hands.***

**Collides:** he roots himself deeper into the village with every repair while everything in him wants sky, road and an unsealed life. The mended pump, the squared gate, the loom that runs true — each is another argument for staying in a home he is not sure he is allowed to want. **Contradiction Pair CP-2 — THE OPEN AIR vs THE WALLS** `[ASKED]`
