**⚠️ THE CONSEQUENCE, AND IT IS NOT SMOOTHED. **(a) What the room sees, every time.** `[DERIVED]` In this kingdom **the change of name-term is itself the whole signal**, and everyone present knows it in the same instant — it needs no scaffolding to be legible (§G16.1.2). Outside an official court ruling or the three named rites the first name is the ordinary default and is not a refusal to be bound (kingdom §G16.1.3). The room notices a sovereign who keeps even a sworn word in the household voice, because the wind-name was open to her, and it remarks it; nothing is lost in law by it.** **This is why the ruling says people notice: they cannot fail to. The shift is legible by law, and so is its absence.**

**(b) Why it bites HERE and would not bite in a kingdom that wrote things down.** `[DERIVED]` The address system is not decorative in Gale. **The record IS the hearing** (§G16.2.1) — nothing binding is written, there is no register kept somewhere else, and **the kingdom has no somewhere else.** A state that kept paper could let the naming be a courtesy and let the instrument be the document. **Gale's naming IS the instrument.** So the ruling's phrase is exact and must be read literally rather than as a figure: **she is declining to put a matter fully on the record BY HOW SHE NAMES SOMEONE**, and there is no second mechanism standing behind the first to catch what the naming lets through. (b) applies as written only to an official court ruling. Outside one, the instrument is the act (kingdom §G16.1.6(iii)), so how she names someone does not decide whether a matter is on the record; the room still remarks it.

**(c) Her completion is where it lands.** `[DERIVED]` §2 above records that the completion is **the whole of the discretionary power the system gives her over address**, and that the name she chooses **sets the register in front of the entire hall.** Inside the perimeter that choice is already made — **by a rule about closeness, applied to a question about the record.** The two are not the same question, and **the system cannot see which one she was answering.
