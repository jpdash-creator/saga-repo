> **Purpose:** This folder tracks the villains and the side villains of the Starward Saga, arranged by the four tiers of depth of record that Part 19 Area 1.2 sets for side characters (Part 18 Area 6.3). A Tier A or Tier B villain is a primary villain/antagonist and has one ledger file on the thirteen-section Character Ledger Template defined in Part 37 Area 2.2. Section 5 (Psychological Profile) uses Part 18's five villain psychological archetypes (The Armored, The Consumed, The Calcified, The Fractured, The Hollow). Section 9 (Arc Status) records villain arc phase (Part 18), defeat type (Part 18), and survival status. A Tier C or Tier D villain is a side villain and has an entry or a row in this index.

> **Tiers (Part 18 Area 6.3; the tiers are depth of record, not power, danger or arc type):**
> - **Tier A and Tier B:** full Villain Ledger files. **Reserved until the author maps them from the macro story;** no agent creates one on its own initiative. **One is mapped: Wulfric Rask, Tier B, by the author's ruling of 2026-10-18.** The `Villain Narrative Tier` field (Arc, Series, Faction or Thematic Villain) is a different axis and stays.
> - **A tier is named only when the author maps one** (Part 18 Rule 18.6.3.3). A villain whose tier the author has not mapped reads UNMAPPED: it is created as a full file on the Part 37 Area 2.2 template, as before, is registered in full, and is listed in the "Tier not yet mapped" table below. The villain Part 30 plans for Phase 1 (Section 7) stays unmapped until the author maps it. No agent names a tier.
> - **Tier C — side villain:** a short entry in the Tier C table below, with its Part 39 behavioral line (the mandatory-core six Layers and one Signature).
> - **Tier D — side villain:** one row in the Tier D table below, carrying a Part 39 Section 3.5 compact code.
> - A **side villain** is a minor antagonist (Part 18 Rule 18.1.1.3) or a Sub-Villain (Part 18 Rule 18.8.1.2). Nothing promotes automatically: recurrence never promotes, and on genuine consequence the character is flagged to the author, who rules every time (Part 19 Rule 19.1.2.4).
> - **Moved from the monster ledger:** a sentient monster classified Villain has one canonical file here; the monster index keeps a pointer row marked `migrated` and its Kind stays in `ledger-character-monster` (Part 40 Rule 40.12.3.1). The pointer table below records each move. A non-sentient monster is never entered here, and its hostility is not qualifying behaviour for Threshold C-2 (Part 40 Rule 40.12.2.1).

> **Critical Note:** Per Part 18 Rule 18.1.3.1, villain characters have no survival protection — death is a canonical outcome. When a villain's status changes to "Dead," the ledger file is preserved as permanent canon record but is marked Inactive.

> **Update Protocol:** Per the ledger update law (`guardrails/ledger/guardrail-ledger-update-law.md`), this index is updated every time a new villain ledger file is created or an existing villain's status changes. The Pre-Write Ledger Check reads villain ledger entries before any Area where the villain appears. The Post-Write Ledger Update updates villain ledger entries after any accepted Area that changes villain state.

> **Mandatory Update Triggers:** any trigger of Part 18 Rule 18.6.1.3 (including a CSS-3+ consequence event, PIS-4+ and IE-3+); villain power shift; arc phase progression; defeat event; resource or faction position change; ideology shift; CS%/TS% triggering events per Part 31 Area 21.4.2 (Integration Model change, realm breakthrough, major CI Technology access change, Sacred Ground access gained/lost).

> **CS%/TS% Tracking:** CS% (Cultivation Synergy %) and TS% (Technology Synergy %) for each villain are Living Information (L) stored in Character Ledger Section 2. Values range 0%–100% per Part 31 Area 21. The _index.md registry includes CS%/TS% columns for quick-reference audit. An Integrated Practitioner villain with high CS% and TS% (e.g., CS% 75%+, TS% 70%+) represents one of the most dangerous villain configurations per Part 31 Area 12.

> **Marker Legend:** These markers appear in the entries of this ledger. Open cells otherwise carry `OPEN-AREA` or `OPEN-AUTHOR` (Part 37 Area 5).
> - `[ASKED]`: the author chose this value.
> - `[DERIVED]`: the drafter inferred this value from cited evidence and the author reviewed it in the digest without overriding it (Part 39 Rule 39.3.3.1); it is pending only when the entry also says flagged for author confirmation.
> - `[AUTHOR-PENDING]`: a question about this value has been raised and is not yet answered.
> - `[MARKED SLOT]`: a slot left empty on purpose until the author rules.
> - "flag, do not invent": when a scene needs the value, the prose may establish one small fact at a time under Part 28 Area 17, logged in this ledger and offered to the author as a draft value at the next Section close. A name or a major entity (Part 28 Area 20.4) goes to the author first, through the Decision Consultation Gate.

## Part 39 — Behavioral Signature Registration

**Governing Law:** Part 39 (`character-behavioral-synthesis-law`), Areas 3, 4, 41, 45.
**Schema:** Part 37 Areas 2.1/2.2/2.3 — Character Ledger **Section 11** (Behavioral Signature, B/L) and **Section 12** (Behavioral Evolution Log, L). The character ledger template is **thirteen sections** (Section 13, Current Condition).
**Audit Hook:** Gate 1 **Check #20** (Behavioral Conformance, Part 39 Area 43) and **Check #21** (Behavioral Change Detection, Part 39 Area 44). Gate 1 runs **22** checks (Check #22, Plain Language and Narrative Impact).
**Command:** `/character-behavior <name>` runs the registration interview and writes Sections 11 and 12.

**Villain-specific behavioral law.** The Part 18 psychological archetype recorded in Section 5 — The Armored, The Consumed, The Calcified, The Fractured, The Hollow — **constrains without determining** the villain's Part 39 Layer L03 (Emotional Expression Grammar), L08 (Moral Reflex), L27 (Stress-State Modulation) and L28 (Outcome-Response) registrations, per Part 39 Standard N39-12. The archetype is where the villain's coherence lives, and it becomes visible in conduct rather than in menace: the five archetypes each *wait* differently, *eat* differently, and *correct a subordinate* differently. A villain rendered as generalized threat rather than as a specific person behaving from inside a total worldview has failed both Part 18 and Part 39.

**Registration timing (Part 39 Rule 39.4.3.4).** Part 37 Threshold C-2 fires the villain-classification question at a character's first appearance in an Area. Where the author confirms Villain classification, the registration of the tier the author rules (the full Part 39 registration for a Tier A or Tier B villain; the core-six line for Tier C; the code for Tier D) is completed **before that character's second acting appearance** — not before the first, because the first is what establishes them. A non-sentient monster never raises the question (Part 40 Rule 40.12.2.1).

**Villain arc phases are drift triggers.** A villain's six Part 18 arc-phase transitions are registered as trigger class **T-10 (Positional)** in the Conditional Drift Map, and the villain's defeat — whichever of the five Part 18 defeat types it is — is a mandatory mapped entry. A villain who is Physically defeated and one who suffers Consequence Collapse do not behave the same way on the way down, and the map says which in advance.

**Requirement for every Tier A and Tier B villain in this folder:** all **36 Behavioral Layers** (`L01`–`L36`) addressed, **twelve Signatures** (the guide) drawn from about six or more Layers and about three or fewer from any one, **two contradiction pairs** (guide), **one costly behavior** (guide), and the **six usual Conditional Drift Map entries** (first kill, first significant defeat, first PIS-5, next realm or tier crossing, next age-band crossing, loss of the most significant registered relationship), per Part 39 Rules 39.4.1.1 and 39.3.3.5.

**Requirement for a side villain (Part 39 Area 4.1 and Area 41.3):** a **Tier C** side villain carries the mandatory-core six Layers and one Signature, stored as a one-paragraph behavioral line beneath the Tier C table; a **Tier D** side villain carries the compact code of Part 39 Section 3.5 in the Code cell of its row and no Layer register.

**Blocking rule (Part 39 Rule 39.4.3.1):** a character may not be drafted into an Area in which they act or speak until their registration is complete. A character merely named in passing is not "acting" for this purpose.

**Mandate:** Every Tier A and Tier B villain ledger file must populate Sections 1–10 to at least **comprehensibility-sufficient depth** per Part 28 Area 17.3 and Part 18. The Depth Test for villains: "Can a reader, from the ledger alone, understand *why this villain does what they do* — not merely that they oppose the heroes?" If yes, depth is satisfied.

**Required Axes — Villain-Specific Focus (beyond standard character axes):**

1. **Psychological Archetype** — Armored / Consumed / Calcified / Fractured / Hollow (Part 18) with named origin event.
2. **Ideological Core** (Part 20) — what the villain believes, and what personal cost proves they believe it.
3. **Capacity and Resources** — Part 3 realm, Part 5 combat profile, faction backing, Part 12 relics, Part 33 weapons, Part 32 vehicles.
4. **Known Victims and Consequences** (Part 27 CSS) — named prior consequences the villain has caused, with CSS tier where known.
5. **Defeat Conditions** (Part 18 Area 5) — what would constitute the villain's defeat type (Physical Defeat / Ideological Defeat / Consequence Collapse / Transcendence-Conversion / Containment-Exile, Rule 18.1.3.3).
6. **Open Tensions** — at least one unresolved question that future prose can resolve (revelation pending, relationship reversal possible, protégé poised to inherit).
7. **Innate Moves (Part 6 Area 12)** — required Section 3 subsection for all villain ledgers per Part 37 Area 2.2. Villains who are non-species-innate (pure-human or non-hybrid cultivator villains) record "(none registered — character is non-species-innate type)". Villains who are hybrid sentients (Part 16 Area 17), bipedal sentient species members (Part 16 Area 18), or other sentient beings (Part 16 Area 19) register their S-3 and S-8-with-S-3 moves with full Part 6 Area 12.3.5 field set. Villain S-3 moves carry the same Firmament-Lock immunity as hero S-3 moves (Part 6 Area 12.3.4) — this is a substrate fact, not a character-moral fact.
8. **Species Classification (Part 16 Areas 17–19)** — if applicable, villain's species designator (e.g., Minotaur-Lineage Balanced-Fusion Hybrid; Bipedal Sentient Species — Avian-Lineage), SADS position for species-innate-capable villains, and species-cultural affiliations.

**Silent Overwrite Prohibition (Part 28 Area 17.5):** Once villain lore is recorded, it is fixed under the Establishment Threshold. Post-Part 18 Area 1.5 villain redemption arcs require a formal Canon amendment before ledger rewriting.
