**1. THE BARE FIGURE, AND NOTHING ELSE — the syntax of letting the number do the work.** `[ASKED]`. Registered as a §4.2 **Social Position and Information State** element and **deliberately not claimed as one of the nine menu determinants**; menu items 2 and 9 sit adjacent to it, neither was ruled, and both are fenced below.
**Anchor:** `B39-L10-REFUSE-99` `[SIGNATURE]` `[IMMUTABLE]` — *SHE SAYS THE TRUE NUMBER ALOUD: in every room, **at the moment of most enthusiasm, she states the actual figure — flatly, without argument or apology — and lets it do the work; she does not debate, she reports**; the room's mood collapses and the decision improves; she knows exactly what it costs her socially and considers the cost part of the job.*

- **THE VALUE. She says the number. There is no sentence around it, no explanation, and no softening clause.** The immutable already registers **that** she states the figure and **that** she lets it do the work. **This element is the syntax of letting**, and it is the reason the immutable is renderable at all: *letting it do the work* is not a mood a narrator can assert, it is an absence of words that an auditor can count.
- **THE COUNTABLE FORM, and it is a single pass.** `[ASKED]` for the value; `[DERIVED]` for the counting procedure. **Take her utterance at the peak of a proposal. The utterance is the figure, optionally with its unit, and nothing else.** Count the constituents that are neither the figure nor its unit. **The count is zero.** No preface, no *the trouble is*, no *which leaves us*, no *I am afraid*, no restatement of what was asked, no consequence named, no alternative offered, no attribution of the figure to a source, and no verb of saying attached to it by her.
- **⚠️ BINDING ON EVERY DRAFTING AGENT: A SUPPORTING CLAUSE BREAKS THE IMMUTABLE.** Not weakens it, not writes her slightly off. **A supporting clause is an argument, and the immutable's own words are *she does not debate, she reports*.** The moment the figure acquires a because, the room has been given something to answer, and the mechanism the author locked — the number standing alone until the enthusiasm dies against it — has been replaced by an exchange. This is a Rule 39.35.1.1 matter, reported at Check #20 and asked at Check #21, as well as a Check #19 reading. **There is no scene, no stake and no emergency that licenses it.**

- **THE SCOPE, WORKED OUT AND STATED EXPLICITLY, BECAUSE A CHANCELLOR SPEAKS ALL DAY AND THE ELEMENT WOULD BE UNWRITABLE IF IT GOVERNED EVERYTHING.** `[DERIVED]`
  1. **The scope comes from the immutable's own wording and is not invented here:** *at the moment of most enthusiasm.* The bare figure is **the form of the reporting act** — the moment where a want meets its price. It is absolute inside that scope and it governs nothing outside it.
  2. **Her ordinary connected speech is not thereby unregistered.** It is governed by item 1 below (short and complete) and item 5 (no hedging), and by element 3(a), which is where she does build sentences.

> ### ⚠️ THE DRAFTING RULE ELEMENT 1 IS UNUSABLE WITHOUT: HOW A BARE FIGURE CARRIES ITS STAKES TO A READER WHO DOES NOT KNOW THEM. `[DERIVED]` *(reasoned from the registered material; flagged for author confirmation)*
> **The problem, stated plainly.** A number carries nothing on its own. *Eleven* means nothing to a reader, and the immutable forbids her the clause that would explain it. **The stakes therefore cannot come from her line, and if they cannot come from her line they must come from somewhere, or the most important speech act in her register is illegible on the page.**
> **THE RULE, IN THREE BEATS WITH THREE DIFFERENT OWNERS, AND SHE OWNS ONLY THE MIDDLE ONE.**
> 1. **The want and its quantity are established BEFORE she speaks, by the enthusiast.** `B39-L10-TURN-99` makes this structural rather than optional: she takes her turn last, so a proposal, its scale, and usually its own arithmetic are already in the air when she opens her mouth. **The antecedent is the other speaker's, not hers.**
> 2. **She states the figure.** Bare, per the countable form above.
> 3. **The room supplies the reading.** `B39-L10-REFUSE-99` registers the effect in its own words — *the room's mood collapses and the decision improves* — and `B39-L15-ROOM-99` registers why the room is listening at all: *what she says next determines budgets, and rooms quiet accordingly.* **The meaning of the number is legible in what happens to the faces, and that beat is already registered conduct rather than an invention of this field.**
> **THE COMMENSURABILITY TEST, countable, and it is the half a drafting agent will get wrong.** **Her figure must be in the same unit as the quantity already in the air, or in one the room has just used.** A figure that cannot be laid against something already said has no bridge to the reader and lands as noise. If the enthusiast has been counting wagons, her figure is wagons. If the enthusiast has been counting weeks, her figure is weeks. **The collapse is legible only because the reader can perform the subtraction the room performs.**
> **⚠️ THE FAILURE MODE, NAMED: THE NARRATOR EXPLAINING THE NUMBER AFTERWARD.** If the prose must tell the reader, after her line, what the figure meant, **the scene is built wrong and the fix is upstream, never a clause for her.** Move the information into the enthusiast's mouth, or into what the room was already arguing about. **A narrator who glosses her figure has converted an enacted refusal into an asserted one**, which is precisely what Rule 29.4.4.2 refuses to credit and what the Prose Vitality Law is written against. Narration may render the collapse; it may not supply the arithmetic.
> **THE ONE PERMITTED EXCEPTION, and it is a different act rather than an exception to the syntax.** Where she is **asked directly for a figure**, the figure is still bare, and the stakes are carried by the question. The asker has supplied the frame. **The syntax does not change; only the owner of beat 1 does.**
> **⚠️ AND NO FIGURE IS SUPPLIED BY THIS FIELD.** No quantity, unit, reserve level or minimum is established anywhere in this register, and none may be invented from it. **The field registers a shape and supplies no number.**
