#### 2 — What He Says: The Acknowledgment, and His Depth

**Depth — constituent count: THREE. He speaks the acknowledgment at its HIGHEST depth.** `[DERIVED]` **Rule P29-13.7.3** puts the **commoner petitioner in formal court** at the highest formality floor, and §G16.2.4 expresses that floor as **all three constituents**. He is an **unranked ranker** of a cliff-harbor fishing household — not a seated official, not palace staff, nothing about him on any standing record — so the whole of it is his to constitute:

> ***"[Office], I stand in hearing. I am [wind-name]. Heard by [wind-name], and by [wind-name]."*** Read [wind-name] as [name] outside an official court ruling or the three named rites: his given name, Brannic, or his wind-name if he chooses; the family name never enters (1A). He still holds a wind-name, and the depth stays THREE constituents (kingdom §G16.1.6)

| # | Constituent | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vocative + standing clause** | Locates the hearer by **office-noun**, and declares him **audible, present on his feet, and answerable.** The standing is literal — *a promise is made on the feet* |
| 2 | **Self-naming** | Enters him **personally**, by the term that is his true name in sworn contexts. Without it he has entered only his function; outside an official court ruling or the three named rites the term may be his birth name; he must still HOLD a wind-name |
| 3 | **Witness clause** | Constitutes the record at the moment of speaking, naming aloud who can afterwards be produced. **At this depth it is HIS OWN PROTECTION and not a courtesy to the crown** |

**Four conditions, all necessary, none of them the addressee's assent** (§G16.2.5): it must be **heard by at least one wind-named PERSON other than him who can afterwards be named and produced** `[ASKED]`; **spoken in the open**; **in his own voice** — no herald, no messenger, no delegate, no token, no seal; and made **on his feet.**

**IT IS AN ENTRY ACT AND NOT A PER-LINE TAG.** `[DERIVED]` Made **once, on entering hearing**; after it he uses ordinary name-terms and office-nouns as the sentence needs, **or nothing at all.** Re-made in exactly two situations: he left hearing and returned, or **the matter moved from ordinary weight to sworn weight** (§G16.2.7). **This is the most likely place to get his lines wrong**, and it is listed as an error class at §5.

**Depth is a FLOOR, not a ceiling.** `[DERIVED]` Three constituents is the top of the scale. **Going shallower is legible and must be written as legible** — below the floor his standing sets, written as correct, is a **MAJOR** error (§G16.2.4).

**How the acknowledgment is completed** (§G16.2.8): the addressee either **names him back**, which **enters** him: in an official court ruling the wind-name is used; elsewhere the act sets the weight, and the addressee's choice of the wind-name is an escalation; or answers ***"Speak,"*** which **hears him without entering him** — **no rebuke, no heat**, a decision about the record taken in public in one word. `[DERIVED]`

**⚠️ AND WHEN IT IS NOT HEARD, THE THREE CASES ARE NOT ONE CASE** (§G16.2.6). `[DERIVED]` **Case A — spoken and genuinely not heard:** a non-event, not an insult and not a fault; **he simply says it again, and keeps saying it, with no rise in temperature and no stigma whatever.** **Case B — heard, but by nobody who counts:** the exchange proceeds and nothing is void, **but nothing said in it can afterwards be relied on.** **Case C — WITHHELD where one was due:** the real breach; because there is no title to omit, **it cannot be written as coldness or bad manners — what is present is an absence**, landing in the class the kingdom calls **"to leave a silence."** `[AUTHOR-PENDING]` **No penalty is set here** (kingdom open item 3).

**⚠️ THE WIND IS NOT A RECORD-KEEPER.** `[ASKED]` Author ruling: the palace-wind witnesses at the **Sovereign Ascension Oath** and the **Wind Crossing** because those rites say so, **and nowhere else.** It **does not extend by analogy.
