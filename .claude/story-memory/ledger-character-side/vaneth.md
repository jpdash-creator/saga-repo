| # | Constituent | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vocative + standing clause** | Locates the hearer by **office-noun**, and declares him **audible, present on his feet, and answerable.** The standing is literal — *a promise is made on the feet* |
| 2 | **Self-naming** | Enters him **personally**, by the term that is his true name in sworn contexts. Without it he has entered only his function; outside an official court ruling or the three named rites the term may be his birth name; he must still HOLD a wind-name |
| 3 | **Witness clause** | Constitutes the record at the moment of speaking, naming aloud who can afterwards be produced. **At this depth it is HIS OWN PROTECTION and not a courtesy to the crown** — a petitioner who omits it has protected nobody |

**Four conditions, all necessary, none of them the addressee's assent** (§G16.2.5): it must be **heard by at least one wind-named PERSON other than him who can afterwards be named and produced** `[ASKED]`; it must be **spoken in the open**; it must be **in his own voice** — no herald, no messenger, no delegate, no token, no seal; and it must be made **on his feet.**

**Order is fixed** — vocative, standing, self-naming, witnesses — and out-of-order constituents are a MINOR error unless the disorder is written as characterization (§G16.2.3). `[DERIVED]`

**IT IS AN ENTRY ACT AND NOT A PER-LINE TAG.** `[DERIVED]` Made **once, on entering hearing**; after it he uses ordinary name-terms and office-nouns as the sentence needs, **or nothing at all.** Re-made in exactly two situations: he left hearing and returned, or **the matter moved from ordinary weight to sworn weight** — in which case it is re-made at the depth the sworn matter requires (§G16.2.7).

**Depth is a FLOOR, not a ceiling.** `[DERIVED]` Three constituents is the top of the scale and he cannot go deeper. **Going shallower is legible and must be written as legible** — a speaker's acknowledgment below the floor their standing sets, written as correct, is a **MAJOR** error (§G16.2.4).

**One derivation, marked and flagged.** `[DERIVED]` §G16.2.4 records that the self-naming clause **stops being required on the day the room can produce the name without the speaker.** Applied to the one office he has stood in front of repeatedly rather than once, that reads as a descent from the highest depth to the high depth — **all three constituents on first entry, and the witness clause thereafter carried only where the matter is one he needs to be able to rely on later.** **This is derived, not ruled, and is flagged for author confirmation before first use in an Area. Nothing is written as settled on it.**

**How the acknowledgment is completed** (§G16.2.8): the addressee either **names him back**, which **enters** him: in an official court ruling the wind-name is used; elsewhere the act sets the weight, and the addressee's choice of the wind-name is an escalation; or answers ***"Speak,"*** which **hears him without entering him** — **no rebuke, no heat**, a decision about the record taken in public in one word. `[DERIVED]`

**⚠️ AND WHEN IT IS NOT HEARD, THE THREE CASES ARE NOT ONE CASE** (§G16.2.6). `[DERIVED]` **Case A — spoken and genuinely not heard:** it did not happen; it is a non-event, not an insult and not a fault; **he simply says it again, and keeps saying it, with no rise in temperature and no stigma whatever.** **Case B — heard, but by nobody who counts:** the exchange proceeds and nothing is void, **but nothing said in it can afterwards be relied on** and the Stone Tribunal will not adjudicate on it. **Case C — WITHHELD where one was due:** the real breach; because there is no title to omit, **it cannot be written as coldness or bad manners — what is present is an absence**, and it lands in the class the kingdom calls **"to leave a silence."** `[AUTHOR-PENDING]` **No penalty is set here** (kingdom open item 3).

**⚠️ THE WIND IS NOT A RECORD-KEEPER, AND THIS BEARS ON HIM MORE THAN ON MOST.** `[ASKED]` Author ruling: the palace-wind witnesses at the **Sovereign Ascension Oath** and the **Wind Crossing** because those rites say so, **and nowhere else.** It **does not extend by analogy.
