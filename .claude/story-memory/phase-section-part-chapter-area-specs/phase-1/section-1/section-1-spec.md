## 2 — Section Arc as Planned

**How this Section realises the Part 30 arc:** The year opens in spring on two lines that do not meet, the capitals and the border village, braided from the first page: in the capitals two warm houses raise two children who learn together at Sapphire Ascent in Azorion, and in the village two exiles live under the Seal. All eight protagonists arrive in the opening part, each already wanting something, and the world and its lore are met in use and never toured. The pressure of the world rises each part on both lines and on both kingdoms' households, from small daily costs, through the alliance shown to the world in personal and diplomatic exchanges, to raids from several rival kingdoms, including Cindervast, Ironshore, Saltmere and a new kingdom, Brackenhold, and their cities and castles. The raids are the alliance's first test: each kingdom's own border officers answer on their own front, and the two answering together closes the year; the strain comes only from outside pressure, with no standing dispute between the houses, so the exchanges between the courts grow more intense and lead to action. Technology is not second fiddle and not hidden: two innovations, one in communication between the kingdoms and one in medicine and training at the school, are introduced and then used in both kingdoms, the technology state improving across the year, led by Valia in deeds (block 6). The village sees only the far edge of the trouble. The year closes as spring returns, with the children at the threshold: the First Awakening begins in the last Chapter and finishes in the first Chapter of Section 2.
**Escalation order:** ordinary days, each line with its own want and small cost, all eight arriving, both innovations in quiet use, the first named techniques (the floor's practice movements and a named technique for each adult at practice, never explained, and a second move for Valia shown once, in Chapter 4) (Novel-Part 1, which also brings the Azure Sky court a full report, in labelled parts, of one raid from before Day One, with its source unnamed, and shows one early raid on a Gale mine caravan on Phase Day 9, which the Gale court learns of as plain-clothes raiders and no more); the alliance shown and asked for something, the first personal and diplomatic exchange, named techniques seen again, further quiet signs from outside, both innovations used in both kingdoms (Novel-Part 2); the raids begin and are the alliance's first test, each kingdom answering on its own front, the king's ruling, the exchanges more intense, each exile's first named move, the far edge reaching the village, both innovations relied on (Novel-Part 3); the exchanges lead to action, the two answer together and the first test closes, the children's pressure peaks, the four rulers gather, and the Awakening begins in the last Chapter (Novel-Part 4).
