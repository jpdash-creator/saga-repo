## 1 — Thematic Movement

From days taken for granted to a first sense of what they rest on. Plain, not empty: every line of the part opens on what its person wants and is pulled by it, and the want carries the scene. A small cost rides beside each want and stays light and incidental: the children's "not yet" among older pupils, the exiles' strength kept hidden while the village needs it. The pressure on both lines is a little higher by the close, no more, except for one early step: a raid on a Gale mine caravan on Phase Day 9 (Chapter 005), outside pressure that comes early in the Novel-Part and is not repeated in it. The first sense of what the days rest on comes in Chapter 003 as a full report, in labelled parts, of one raid, the first blood of the peace, heard three weeks late and with no source named; it comes again in Chapter 005, where a raid in the story's present is shown and its news reaches the Gale court five days later, with no source named. Both innovations appear in quiet use, led by Valia in deeds and nothing explained. The opening does not read as a tour: the world is met in use. The heavier cost belongs to Novel-Part 2.
