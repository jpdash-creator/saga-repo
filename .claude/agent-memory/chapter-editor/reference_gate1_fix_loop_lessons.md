Applied in the Chapter 004 fix loop (A01 to A06); reuse the pattern when a later Chapter returns the same findings.

- **Naming Tier D speakers the draft left unnamed.** Give the full name once, in the place a reader would hear it said (a junior announcing a senior: "Healer Berta Thaxter is waiting"; the superior directing the children: "with Ida Norcott"; a runner giving his name to the teacher: "that he was Rafe Ingram and that the queen asked..."). After that: children say "Healer" + surname, the Healer Prime says the given name, narration uses "Healer" + surname. Echo test before choosing: whole-word Grep for zero hits in ledgers, specs, skills, projects, `.claude` (not `_archive`, not `agent-memory`), then a stem check against the existing Tier D list in `ledger-character-side/_index.md` (a rejected name can be clean by whole word and still echo a stem: -wan, Talan/Talbot, Pasco/Pascoe).
- **Near-twin titles.** "Healer Prime" (Valia) beside "the primary healer" read as one person; a name for the second fixes it with no added fact.
- **Crowd shouts as indirect speech.** A bystander shout in a child-POV block becomes "someone at the rail shouted for Kerra to hit him"; it cannot add a speaker against the Area's speaker cap or the TIER D block.
- **Deep POV (F7).** Cut narrator-asserted thoughts, expectations and sureness of non-viewpoint people unless a body cue carries them: "finding nothing in them to read", "which he had not expected", "He took as long as it took to be fair", "sure that the gap had shrunk", "heard her name late", "which she plainly expected", "waiting for it to rise", "hearing how quiet she had gone". Replace with what moved: hands went still, looked up late, nodded at the result, leaning in. Also cut narrator habit clauses ("as she always did", "Aegon always had the answers to the loud questions", "which she never did in front of people").
- **Varya cannot read a face (B39-L02-BLIND-02); she reads hands and body (B39-L02/PEOPLE-01).** In her narration and her own lines swap face-reading for hands, shoulders, voice: "the healer's hands went still", "her shoulders", "and her body went quiet and her head tipped", "his voice came out warm", "Her hand went to her stomach". Aegon may read faces. A smile or grin as a bare event was left; a named emotion on a face was not.
- **Ward geometry.** Roke's bed is at the window end; the children must sit at the door end ("go back up the ward to the bench by the door"). "Far end" meant both; use "door end" and "window end" only.
- **One name for one place.** The seat of the intake child nearest the ring is "the near bench" everywhere (not "third bench" / "nearest bench"); "the nearest ring" is the ring. Kerra's "Not yet" must reach Varya at arm's length.
- **Stopping-place ordinal.** Count on the page: Mother's "the how is not for me" (1), her low answer to the Captain (2), the healer's folded cloth (3); the later list in Varya's speech must run in that order.
- **Rotation arithmetic.** Loop [Wynne, Jenkin, Eira, Arthen, Dewi], front pair slides one place per call, loop closes on call 5 and 10, call 12 is "Jenkin out, Arthen in" with Arthen resting at the rear on call 10. Put "twelfth" on the page at that call so "twelve calls" / "twelve changes" in the Grandmaster's reading are true. Match words too: Dewi's "on the beat we have drilled" is echoed by "on the beat" in the reading.
- **The item name versus the hold.** Where the item *First Grip* is meant write it in full (italic in narration, roman inside italic speech, article outside); where the hold is meant say fingers, wrist, or "the cord on his fist". Never the bare word "grip".
- **Packed-speaker paragraphs** (three or four speakers in one paragraph): split at the speaker change with a tag or a small beat already on the page; merge runs of one-line paragraphs in pairs with a beat (foot tapped faster, chin up).
- **Count recipe that worked (6 calls):** probe the 6 tokens around each marker at predicted offsets (predicted = previous total adjusted by the hand delta); a miss of 1 means a multi-word swap was miscounted ("hearing how quiet she had gone" is 6 words, not 5).
