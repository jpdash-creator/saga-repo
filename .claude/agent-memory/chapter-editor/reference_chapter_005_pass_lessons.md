Seen in Chapter 005 (1.1.1.005, Areas 01 to 06, three scenes with a POV change in each). Check each by Grep before reading for taste.

- **Substring bans.** The author-removed concept list (hum, frequency, vibration, resonance, bedrock) is checked by plain substring, so "thumb", "human", "humor" must not stand anywhere in the text. Grep `-i` `hum|vibrat|resonan|bedrock|shard|frequenc` returns nothing on a clean file. "shard" is the earlier Chapters' cargo word but a later spec can bar it: read the spec's Power Crystal line.
- **`read` policy.** Per Area, one sense. Where a hero's Signature is "reads the air/the room" (Aeries Area 03, Aalia Area 04), sensing keeps `read` and the letter becomes "went through it", "looked at the last words", "went down it with one finger". Where an Area has no sensing (05, 06), the letter keeps `read`. A ground-reading line ("the order we read it") becomes "went over it".
- **Hero voice checks while editing.** Aeries: no maxim ("a wall is easier to agree about when..." recast to a concrete reason), no weather word in calm speech ("the winter of the late thaw" cut; the alarm line "the air has gone flat" is the one), a fragment only inside the plain humor (one-word echo "Counted them and been right" made a full sentence). Aalia: interior is imagery and placement; letters and answers short, answer first; check her letters for count slips (a draft said "five carters" for four carters and a clerk).
- **Mirror beats.** Two pilot landings and a letter read three times (Aeries, Aalia, Aeries) need different staging verbs and a different look-away construction ("broke the seal himself" / "cracked the wax"; "found something to look at" / "studied the planks"). Give the second reader a different focus (Aalia stops on the boot line, not on the sleeve girl and "not yet").
- **POV-change seam.** The new block's first sentence restated the old block's last ("did not go back" twice). Cut the restating clause in the new block; keep the fact in the block that owns the event. Keep the new mind in the first sentence (Aalia "placed the Court in her").
- **Split-speech tags.** Merge `"A," X said, "B."` into one speech with the tag at the end, or lead with an action beat that names the speaker; vary "said Aeries" / "he said". Leave a tag between two sentences where the second is a dry or humorous turn.
- **`did not` and `count` density.** 45 "did not" and 28 count-words in a 11,700-word draft; thinned to 19 and 16 by positive phrasing ("stayed where she was", "for good", "unsealed") and by keeping count-words for the registered habit (Rask) and one report sense (Gale "count"). Count the replacement words.
- **Time-skip and date-line forms.** Typed time-skip mark is `* * *`; the script's own test file puts the `<!-- AREA 01 -->` marker before the first date line, so marker-then-date-line is valid.
- **Count recipe.** `\S*\w\S*` with `-o -n`, offset probes: last token index gives total tokens; probe 6 to 12 tokens at each predicted `AREA` marker; words in an Area = AREA-token index of the next marker minus the first-word index (date line 6 tokens, marker 2). Three probes per round were enough.

Gate 1 fix pass (same Chapter) added these:
- **Compress a replay, spend on new events.** When a ruling says "open on the last breaths before the walk-out and compress the recollection", keep the planned callbacks (Ch.003 "counted the file in with his lips shut", "a count of three", right hand to shoulder height), move the live action to the first sentence, and fold the replay into two short paragraphs; the freed words go to staged beats the spec already carries (the order, the squad's repeat, the mid-fight reads), never to new facts. A short Area can fall under 1,650 after compression, so re-count before the next edit.
- **Naming Tier D recurring speakers.** Scan each candidate surname and first name by whole-word `-i` Grep over `.claude` (not agent-memory), story-memory, skills, guardrails, commands, agents and `projects`, plus a stem check against the character ledgers; avoid initials that pair with an author name (Egil Hurn vs Eskil Haug, so Haug became Ottar Haug). Put the level clause at the first mention in the Area where the person is seen ("a stout unawakened man", "a Senior of the middle Levels"), then use the bare name everywhere.
- **Level reading by position.** A first-rank reading "some of the fifth and the rest of the fourth" must match the spec table by Part 44 position = 4 x (Level - 1) + stage 0 to 3; write it sensed ("of the fifth Level, low and middle"), never as arithmetic.
- **Single-sense words, second sweep.** After a rewrite, re-Grep `line`, `post`, `count`, `weight`, `knees` per Area: a new sentence ("line fighters") can add a second sense. "Outpost" and "post" are different words; "husband" matches a `band` Grep.
- **Rounded numbers in speech.** "A dozen of ours dead" for eleven reads as a changed figure; use the exact number the Chapter already carries.
- **Sameness at the end.** Thin one of a pair of `did not` in an Area when a positive phrase serves ("she kept her step"); keep a parallel closing pair ("He did not move, and she did not need him to").
