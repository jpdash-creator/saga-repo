Seen in Chapter 006 (1.1.1.006, seven Areas, three scenes, Fast fight Areas 01 to 04). Check each by Grep before reading for taste.

- **Read the written Chapters before cutting an odd phrase.** "with no fur on it" (the rope), "Is it sweet this morning, or iron?" / "Sweet.", "the quiet of his boots owed nothing to it", "as if the square were a room he had not been asked into" are exact callbacks to Chapter 002 (`projects/.../Chapter 002 - The Well at First Light.md`, lines 50 to 60 and 204). Grep the written Chapter for the quoted words first; a planned callback in the spec is kept. The same for the man's earlier sightings (Chapter 003 line 26, Chapter 005 line 26).
- **Report-form speech that the narration calls "in full".** A budget list ("Doors, the forge, ... Benches, eleven. A length of ...") and a plan list ("A watch ... The hedge mended ... Stores laid in ...") are fragment chains and fail Rule 29.2.1.7a(3). Recast into connected sentences with the same items and the same counts (Pasco keeps "I make it", "nobody lost" said twice; the Chief gets "We will set ... We will mend ... we will put ..."). Tally-lines ("Five and five", "Sixteen and eleven") become "That makes five for each of us" and so on.
- **Geography check of a protect clause.** "The gate and the loft were behind him" was false: the gate stands on the far side of the square from the lane mouth where he stands. Fix the clause to what is behind him (the lane and its people), and report it.
- **Kind names must be on the page at first sight.** The six Kind names are the author's; "Bone Fox" was missing (the text said "foxes ... the colour of old bone"). Add the name in the Kind clause ("Bone Foxes, no bigger than dogs, ..."). Grep every Kind name before the report.
- **Cross-block fact contradiction.** The man's recollection said "the broader of the two men turn his head" while Area 04 shows Hercules looking and Bison laughing. Use a neutral form ("one of the two men"). Ledgers hold no height for the two heroes, so never assign it.
- **Unplanned witness.** Gwenna said Ebrel drew "a tall man in a coat down to his boots": spec Area 04 says no villager sees the man. Cut the clause and report it (author may restore); Ebrel's row holds ROLE-CHILD, silent, pebble (B39-L01-OBJ-06), nothing about drawing.
- **Figure doubles.** A clear figure plus a second stacked "as ..." in one sentence ("as a hall answers a thing it has heard said before in other words, ... as water closes over a stone"): cut the less clear one. Also a second figure for one idea in a paragraph ("do the sum" then "adding him up").
- **Single statement inside one speech.** The man said twice that two men were not in his count; keep one delivery. Selyf's shout repeated the Crag Buck trait the narration had just given; shorten the shout to the shock line.
- **Compact repeat of a Named Move.** Second use of ***Immovable Stand*** was near-verbatim of the first ("the whole of him one post", "gave it back through the open palm in one blow"): compress to the act, one sense detail, the result (Part 28 DP-9, repeat use). Move the two price signs apart (held breath and shaking hand for the first; burning chest for the Advanced finisher). Add a cause clause for ***Staggering Blow*** when the draft shows only the opening ("A plain blow would only have glanced off that hide.").
- **22-9 swaps used (all zero or negative cost):** step (pace vs doorstep vs stair) became paces / doorway; head (body vs "head of the slide") lost two body uses; crown (bear vs treetops) became "above the treetops"; border (frontier vs field edge) became "beside"; account (report vs matter) became "matters"; read (interpret vs appear) became "look the smallest"; hands (body vs workers) became "workers"; stoop (raven dive vs bending) became "bending"; edge (hand's edge vs forest edge vs move name Edge Gust) became "the side of his hand"; floor (square) became "stretch".
