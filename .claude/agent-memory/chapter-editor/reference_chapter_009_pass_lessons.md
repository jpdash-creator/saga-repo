- **Read the Tier B ledgers before editing their lines** (`ledger-character-side/gunnar-stavik.md`, `magnus-dalsen.md`). Stavik: PSM-1 middle sentences, long formal clause ending in the witness list only when constituting an act; PSM-5 blunt on what he saw, bounded on causes ("this is for the Marshal"); PSM-6 no epigram; scope excludes speech about his own grief, so "glad to set them down, and sorry to have them to set down" was cut to "and I set them down here". His thought is first person, verbal (`L07-MODE-01`, `SELF-01`). `I am angry about it` (Orm) is Signature `L03-DECAY-09`, keep. Dalsen: measured, "what I would ask" on force.
- **Tier C trait invention.** Halric Mear's ledger is core-6 plus one Signature (name first, hands to the roll); "eyes went to a man's hands before his face" was unregistered and was cut; his title in the ledger is senior gate warden (used "a warden of the Gate" to avoid Senior-realm vs senior-office doubling and to anchor A06's "the warden's runner").
- **Thumb contains "hum".** Kade's thumb is a spec-mandated closing image (and a Ch.003 Signature), so it stays; incidental thumbs (Corin's finger-joints count, Wenna's hub pin, the king's grip on the strip) were swapped. Report it as a likely substring false positive.
- **22-9 splits that worked (zero or negative cost):** `company` = raiders everywhere except Host units in A08 (raiders -> "fifty raiders", "they", "the raiders"); `in company` -> "together" in A06 (A05 keeps it, no raider sense there); `post` place vs people -> "detachment" in three places; `band` cloth vs carved doorway bands -> doorway "strips" (Ch.003 says "strip of writing / strip of plans") and Colm's "band of red" -> "red cloth"; `hold/held` (spoon kept, blade raised, "bit and stayed", "nursing a wound", "keep still", "I keep them", "put out his hand"); `level` (hafts low/lowered/slanted/grounded/crosswise, voice steady/even); `floor`; `line` (trail of drops, made an entry); time-unit `breath` -> instant, heartbeat, long moment, behind his teeth; `wall` (rock shoulder); `senior hand` -> senior man.
- **Spec self-contradiction.** Spec A04 folds the strip into the book; spec A05 says "strip in his coat"; A06 takes it from the book's gutter. Draft A05 now reads "the book under his arm and the strip folded in it". Stavik keeps his own strip in his coat (A08). Report such items.
- **Continuity fixes without new fact:** Colm's wet pair = boots (A01), so A04 "wet boot off" -> "left boot off"; dawn on Corin's left while heading south = east (stars go out "east side", not north); Orm "four strides short" then fell across Stavik's feet -> he crawled the four strides; "a company of fifty" -> "fifty raiders"; the king's "no word on the wire" -> "from the post" (Gale has no wire beyond the corridor); watch-order "pair of the other kingdom's" -> "another mixed pair"; night pair waiting to relieve a south approach whose evening pair had not come in.
- **Plain-language swaps:** scree -> gravel, runnel -> stream, "the Declaration" -> "the bounds of the fight" (Battlefield Declaration stays in the spec only), "storm-keeper" glossed by the king's "Aalia, the storm-keeper", "thimbles" -> "wire thimbles", "the change" -> "a watch being changed".
- **Flowing-dialogue recasts:** elliptical commands got verbs ("Go to the bank", "get down behind the near pole", "Get up ... and keep the stone at your backs", "put your boot on and then go to Marden's left"); list speeches joined (Corin's carry list, the Marshal's Stirrup Ford list); Tier D maxims cut (Marden "A man who is hit says something", Finn "A man who is running leaves his dead" -> reasoned sentence); Haldor's tally "four of them ... nine of ours" dropped because it conflicted with the 23-on-feet count.
- **Duplicate beat across scenes.** A05 (Colm) and A07 (Sten) both said "four months ... never heard of raiders"; reworded Sten. Not cut, because the spec plans both.
- **Seam recipe.** Continuing join A05/A06: first sentence takes up the door ("The door let him into a hall ..."). Visible-break joins A02/A03: replace the bare "He" with "The tall man" so the new mind stands alone. Split a 200-word beat paragraph after the release line.
- **Count method.** Total from the last token, then 20-token probes at each predicted `AREA` index; hand delta tallies were off by 0 or 1 in three Areas; the probe is the figure.
- **Left alone, reported:** spec treats Aeries's "go to the gate" as already public, but Ch.005 and Ch.008 show it only privately (text follows the spec).

**Gate 1 local-fix pass (same day; final 2,074 / 1,676 / 1,693 / 1,745 / 1,767 / 1,690 / 1,676 / 2,103 = 14,424)**
- **Narrator may not state a registration as a standing property.** Cut or turned into an act: "who stood square at all times, found that he stood squarer" -> "Stavik stood squarer."; "and the sentence had been a rule he had never found a reason to break" (cut, the italic remembered sentence stays); "who counted without deciding to, took the first of three" -> "Corin's free hand counted off the first of three on its finger-joints" (his registered finger-joint count, `L01-HANDS-06`); "as he had turned it to every wind since he was a boy" (cut). Grep the file for "who ... always|every ... since|at all times|without deciding" after a draft.
- **A death inside the Area that counts the living.** If a head-count precedes a death named in the same Area, the count must include the dying man (19 living then 18), say so ("Reuben Gask among them"), and the dying man needs a shown hurt earlier in the Area (one sentence in the push that opens the block). Re-sum every number word in the later Areas with one Grep over the number words.
- **Testimony phrases must be true of each item:** "as I saw them fall" is false for a man found dead; use "as they were found". The same claim in narration ("counted them fall") became "in the order they had fallen".
- **A term the room has not heard** (Crowe's "on the shelf" before anyone in the room said it) takes the room's word ("at the post").
