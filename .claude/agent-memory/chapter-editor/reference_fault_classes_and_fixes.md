Seen in Chapter 004 (1.1.1.004) and likely again in any bout or address Chapter. Check each by Grep before reading for taste.

- **Crowd metonyms (22-9).** "the yard", "the floor", "the rail" used for both the place and the people. Policy used: the place keeps "yard"/"rail"; the people become "the crowd" (zero words). The fixed salute phrase became "with the crowd for witness". "call" carried two senses in one Area (judge's call and the five's rotation call): the judge's became "ruling" there.
- **Time-unit "breath".** "a breath late / before / for a breath" beside the literal breath (22-9 and sameness tally). Swap the time-unit uses for "heartbeat", "instant", "moment" (one each per Area), keep respiration literal.
- **Canon facts in a child's mouth.** Mortal Tier 1 order is Skin, Muscle, Tendon, Bone, Blood, Marrow, Soul (skill `cultivation-power-and-lifespan-law` 1.2.1); "Blood is the sixth stage" is wrong unless Unawakened is counted. Drop the ordinal. Also check "three academies" (nine-halls ledger carries it).
- **Level wording vs gap.** A 4-minor-step Shut gap read "a little below him" on the page; sensed wording should be "well below". Never write steps or bands.
- **Judge and Grandmaster register.** Yun's call is a raised hand and at most a word ("Drawn", "Yours"); no physical strain tells (pale knuckles, jaw muscle) since STILL-99 is settled stillness and the spec lists no off-register act; no balanced maxim ("The power comes to a foundation that is ready for it, and it breaks one that is not"), no "or the third" hedge, no "could not" modal. "can" in her definitions was removed too.
- **Seam restatement.** Next Area re-narrated the closing state (the hush falling, "two near and three further back" then "two came forward, three stopped behind"). Keep the fact in the Area that owns the event; the next Area opens in motion.
- **Slip names.** A deliberate "wrong name" gag ("Arthur" for Arthen) is a name outside the names file. Use another of the five's names and say "at the wrong name" in the narration so the beat reads at once.
- **Implied canon.** "Aunt Aalia would have told me the how" implies Gale could know the raid; recast to a manner ("answered me straight"). "his flat sentences came true" invents a hero trait; "last year the youngest stood at the rail, sixteen of them" invents last year's arrangement. Cut or recast.
- **POV leaks in a child's block.** "He felt it to the elbow" (another person's sense) became "His arm shook to the elbow"; qi causes stay as seen effects ("as if a little qi had hardened it").
- **Sameness patterns worth a Grep:** `Then` as a narration opener, `because` in one speaker's lines (Max had five), "which is ..." tags across several speakers, finger/hair/teeth/sleeve/post repeated measures and gestures, person-type similes ("like a man ..."), food gags across several characters, numbers (eleven, fourteen, twenty-one) repeating across Areas, "found that she" openers, "stayed there".
- **Two bold-italic move names per sentence is fine; three in one sentence** was split into two sentences (jargon-wall guard).

Method note: per-Area counts by Grep probe worked in four calls: total from the last line, then probe the `AREA NN` token two before each marker; subtract 8 (first date line and marker) for Area 01 and 8 more per later marker plus date line. Use `head_limit` 1 to 8, never 250 (the output costs more than the count).
