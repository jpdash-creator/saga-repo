**Locked Canon versus ledger over-reading.** Law P30-L8's exception sentence (Part 30, the "ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION" block of P30-L8) already lets "the word Olympus, the Seal, the reasons for the banishment of Hercules and Bison, and their promise to clear their name" be spoken or thought in Phase 1 prose. Part 3 Rule 1.7.2(a) names Hercules and Bison as its example for Xiantian words (Demigod) used as memory or self-identity. The hero ledgers (canon decisions 1, 3 and 7 and `B39-L14-FORBIDDEN-99` in `hercules`, the father row and the imposer-unnamed cells in `bison`) cite P30-L8 for walls the law text does not make. An author ruling that opens those walls collides with ledger text, not with Locked Canon: write the exception blocks at the Gate 2 and flag a strict-reading risk for Check #11 element 11-3. Never write a father's name in a spec ("the row of his own father").

**Edit-list drafts for the parents and a completion write.** Give OLD/NEW substring pairs, number them (SS-, PS-, E-) so the Chapter Spec's Variance notes can cite them, and Grep-count every OLD (regex-escaped, count mode) before the hand-off. The new parent groups are appended at block 12 of the Section Spec (CRLF file) and block 11 of the Novel-Part Spec. A substantial parent amendment needs a stated status line: CONFIRMED only if the orchestrator relays the author's sign-off (precedent 2026-10-18 and 2026-10-20), else AMENDED-PENDING-RECONFIRM; give both versions of the line and the registry row.

**Body traps found.** (1) "each of these is planted in the written Chapter" belongs only after the Gate 2 sync. (2) Strip "Open note" and "section 7" pointers from Area blocks (header lines may carry them). (3) Typos in plan text ("authors'") slip through: grep. (4) When the closure report is not on disk (the session `tasks/*.output` files were empty), ask `main` by SendMessage to paste the edit list.

**Ledger facts that saved time (re-verify before use).** `hercules` `B39-L07-SELF-99` licenses "I am Demigod Level 1 Mid" privately; the Owed Beats `B39-L08-UNSEEN-*` and `B39-L18-WEIGHT-*` are OPEN for six heroes (11.3a of each file); the Aerie Palace code THE OPEN EDGE is Registered 2026-10-18 and the village code is None by the author's ruling of 2026-10-06; `hercules` 11.4a holds the Olympus exception block and the reversion trigger deferral; the hero index "Pending author confirmations" table (about lines 45 to 74) lists the Check #21 items that fall due; kingdom position lines: `brackenhold` north-west across the lower ford, `cindervast` the western volcanic coast beyond the Boundary Towers ridge, `ironshore` the eastern bank of the Lyraen delta on the southern coast.

**Swapping in a one-line author ruling after the handback (2026-10-21, "They spoke and were not believed").** (1) Grep all three drafts for the old verbs (fought, contested, undone, undid) AND the noun forms ("a fight and an exile", "the fight"): the Scene Map arc cell, the Budget-stretch reason, the first Open note's list and the Variance Key-events cell all carried the old words. (2) Open the ledger before a Gate 2 duty names its home: "convicted unheard" stands literally only in the origin cell of Signature 6 in `hercules` (it cites canon decision 1, which does not say it); `bison` has no literal match (nearest `B39-L15-DISSENT-09`, "convicted without a record in his favor"); neighbours "the hearing he never got" and "the proceeding he never got" need a read in the same pass. (3) A coordinator's "settle by your recommended reading" goes to the Skipped group (planner chose), never Settled; a matter the coordinator routes to a later Check is an Open note and never a Deferred note, because only the author may defer (the light audit moved my routed Deferred note to Open). (4) When an Open note becomes a Skipped note, strip every "(the Open note asks the author)" and "pending the author" pointer elsewhere (grep "Open note" and "pending the author"). (5) A Settled note ending "(the author, date)" must not also carry a planner sentence about what is "not decided": detach it.
