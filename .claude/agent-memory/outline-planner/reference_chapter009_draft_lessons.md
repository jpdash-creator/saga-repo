Facts and traps from the Chapter 009 ("The Pass Holds") draft. Chapter facts live in the specs; these are only the working lessons.

- **Part 44 positions (Rule 44.2.3.1).** Count from Level 1 Low of the realm as 0: four times (Level minus one) plus 0 to 3; the step from one realm's Level 9 Peak to the next realm's Level 1 Low counts one, so a Champion's positions start 36 after a Warrior's. Warrior and Champion lie in one Major Realm (Shut for 3 or more, never Barred). Write the positions in the spec Audit note only; the Fight lines carry gaps. Do not invent "realm bases" (an earlier draft used 36 and 72, harmless for gaps but wrong as a statement).
- **Lawful shape of a held post.** Rule 44.6.2.1 (the ambush's first strike reaches the unready once, then the bands return) plus the last bullet of Rule 44.7.4.1 (stronger anchors wound only those they reach in the time they have) justify a lower side that breaks half a post and loses to the rally. Give a hero-grade anchor's wound to a line fighter (named offsets: numbers, shielding) so no lower fighter wounds a higher anchor; the limited strike that spares a Shut leader is a strain item with a priced reason.
- **Casualty tables.** Keep one cumulative table in the battle statement (section 5.0b) and recount every Area ledger against it: per-phase deaths by kingdom, groups at the start and end of each phase, survivors; check that each group's loss sums to the phase figure.
- **Parent amendments.** Variance notes of earlier groups quote the old line, so an OLD string must carry leading or trailing context to match once (Grep each one with `-o` and line numbers before listing it; the hits on the Chapter 008 group lines were the trap). Part 35 Area 3.5 line 319 says "no formal set-piece battle is fought" and Part 30 row 11 (S2 cell) says "officers at Champion level enter the battlefield": both need the Canon Amendment.
- **Codes quoted correctly.** The Aerie Palace code is THE OPEN EDGE with two provisions (NO WALL AND NO WAITING ROOM; SAID STANDING, IN THE OPEN). The Azure Sky code's rank-blind act is "and if it came tomorrow", asked at the end of any plan or report. The Gale code is FEW RULES, ABSOLUTE and SPOKEN INTO THE WIND. The company's code is NO MARK, ONE BODY, THE SIGN IS THE LEADER'S, THE LINE IS QUIET (a hidden red band needs a NO MARK note). The Wing's two posts are empty by the ledger.
- **Scratch notes.** A long multi-file draft can outrun the context; keep a short scratch note of fixed numbers and open questions in the scratchpad folder and overwrite it to a one-line stub at the end.
- **Re-ordering Areas with no copy tool (second-round rework).** Insert the modified copy of the block to move before the next heading first (so the content is never only in context), then delete the old block in chunks: first chunk starts at the old unique heading and becomes a placeholder token; every later chunk is "token + next lines" replaced by the token, because lines identical to the new copy are not unique alone; the last chunk ends with the next heading, renamed in the same Edit. About 15 Edits for 150 lines. Then re-point cross-references by grepping `Area 0[5-8]`, `Areas 0[1-8]`, `Day NN` and `Scene S2N`.
- **Immutable pause of Aeries.** `B39-L18-WEIGHT-99` (aeries.md line 35): a long silence before any real commitment; past the pause the thing is permanent (`B39-L12-AFTER-04`). A closing line in which he says he will go to the gate is therefore a canon commitment, and Gale's custom (said standing, outdoors, witnesses present) also makes it a promise; Chapter 008 left the going undated and unpromised. Plan it as the planner's pick with the alternative (close inside the pause) put to the author. Outcome (author, 2026-10-22): "Aeries and Aalia will discuss during the visit to Azorion", so the king commits nothing alone, Aalia stays off the page and the Marshal's remark on the cut wire carries the hand-off; a solo commitment by a sovereign whose Signature is a pause was the wrong pick, so offer the shared-decision shape first next time.
- **Moving Open notes to Settled after a blanket acceptance.** Keep the ids of the notes that stay (so the author's own references still work), move each answered note to Settled marked "formerly On", remove the Skipped notes it replaced, re-point every "Open note On" pointer in Deferred and Audit notes, and add one Audit note listing the move. Re-grep the body for "(Open", "the author rules", "the planner proposes" and "for the author to confirm" and rewrite each to "accepted by the author on the date".
- **Corin's kill state.** `corin-vale` holds "has not killed" at `B39-L25-KILL-01` (Provisional), in the drift table (first kill, THE NAME IN THE BOOK) and in repeated notes (lines near 194, 429, 688, 878, 913). When the author rules otherwise, plan a ledger correction at the Gate 2, retire drift row 1 with a "not applicable" row (do not delete the table), drop "sabre never used" lines, and flag that the Maker reads the ledger first.
- **Gale Marshal.** Ruler Level 1 Low is position 72 on the Part 44 count (Warrior, Champion and Ruler lie in one Major Realm); `gale-kingdom-military` holds the Marshal as the army's peak cultivator with the sovereign above him, which fits.
- **Final greps that paid off.** Contractions (`n't` and `'s/'re/'ll/'ve/'d/'m` after pronouns), unwrapped named items and moves (`[^*]Name|Name[^*]`), leftover sentinel strings in every file, "Chapter 010" and fixed last-Chapter wording, Grep for Tier D personal names.
