> **Purpose:** This folder tracks named technology growth entities introduced in prose that have canonical continuity significance under Part 9 (Technology Growth Law). Two categories:
> - **Named Research Programs** — active, ongoing technology research initiatives that have been introduced with enough canonical detail to require continuity tracking. Example: a named CPM-technology integration project, a named FTL research initiative, a named AI development program.
> - **Named Discovery Events** — paradigm-shifting technology discoveries (Part 9 Area 2 — Experimental Accident; Part 9 Area 1.3 — Accidental Discovery; paradigm-shift events) that have been named and characterized in prose. These are historical events that define the technology timeline of a civilization or faction. Example: "The Sunforge Breakthrough of Year 342" (the discovery that enabled Tier 5 energy infrastructure in Azure Sky Kingdom).
>
> This ledger ensures that when a research program or breakthrough is referenced in a later prose unit, it is described consistently — same timeline, same parties, same scope, same status.

> **Scope Boundary:** This ledger tracks *research activities and discovery events*. The institutions conducting them are tracked in `ledger-technology-scale`. The civilizations they belong to are tracked in `ledger-people-society-civilization`. This ledger is the activity layer; the actor layers are elsewhere.

> **Update Protocol:** Per Part 1 Area 6:
> - **New Program File Trigger:** Any prose unit that names a research program (gives it a proper name or internal designation) and describes its scope or timeline requires a new `[program-name].md` file at Gate 2.
> - **New Discovery File Trigger:** Any prose unit that names a past or present paradigm-shift discovery event requires a new `[discovery-name].md` file.
> - **Update Trigger:** Research program status change (breakthrough, setback, termination, acceleration); timeline shift; scope expansion or contraction; new institution joining the program.

> **Mandatory Update Triggers:**
> - Named research program introduced in prose with scope and timeline
> - Research program achieves breakthrough (tier advancement event)
> - Named discovery event referenced for the first time with historical detail
> - Research program terminated, stolen, or disrupted
> - Cross-Civilizational Transfer (Part 9 Area 2.4) of named research results
> - Accidental discovery event named and characterized in prose

## Part 28 Area 17 — Lore Section Schema (Technology-Growth Domain)

**Governing Law:** Part 28 Area 17 (Lore Creation Law). **Audit Hook:** Part 1 Area 8.13 ALCA dimension AL-10.

**For Named Research Programs — Required Minimum Lore Fields:**

1. **Program Name** — canonical name or internal designation as established in prose.
2. **Conducting Institution(s)** — cross-reference to `ledger-technology-scale` institution files.
3. **Technology Domain** — Part 8 Type 1–14.
4. **Current Tier / Target Tier** — the tier the program is researching toward.
5. **Growth Mechanism** — Part 9 Area 1 mechanism type (Systematic Research, Experimental Accident, Cultivation-Assisted, Cross-Civilizational Transfer).
6. **Timeline** — estimated duration, current phase, known acceleration or deceleration events.
7. **Secrecy Status** — Part 24 classification of the program's existence and results.
8. **Prerequisite Status** — which Category I (scientific), II (resource), III (institutional) prerequisites are satisfied and which are outstanding (Part 9 Area 4).
9. **Current Status** — active / suspended / completed / stolen / terminated.

**For Named Discovery Events — Required Minimum Lore Fields:**

1. **Discovery Name** — canonical name as established in prose.
2. **Discoverer** — named individual or institution; cross-references to relevant ledger files.
3. **Domain** — Part 8 Type domain; knowledge layer achieved (L1–L4, Part 10 Area 1).
4. **Tier Impact** — which tier transition this discovery enabled or enabled progress toward.
5. **Discovery Mechanism** — Part 9 Area 2 mechanism (Systematic Research, Accident, etc.).
6. **Date / Era** — narrative timeline of when this occurred.
7. **Diffusion** — how widely this discovery has spread; who now has access to it.
8. **Paradigm Impact** — whether this was a paradigm-shift event (Part 10 Area 1.3 — Phase 3 paradigm-shift event); if so, which paradigm it replaced.

**Depth Test:** "Can a scene referencing this research program or discovery event be written accurately from only this ledger file?" If yes, depth is satisfied.
