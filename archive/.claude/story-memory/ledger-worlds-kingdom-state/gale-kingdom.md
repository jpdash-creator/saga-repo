## Section 1 — Identity and Founding (B)

**Full Name:** Gale Kingdom
**Common Name / Aliases:** Gale; The Gale Kingdom; Gale Crown
**State Type:** Kingdom — K-4 Cultivation-Dominant; cultivation is the dominant paradigm; technology is present and respected but secondary; the Dragon-Human royal lineage gives the kingdom a distinct species-cultural identity rooted in cultivation power
**Governing Philosophy:** Cultivation-dominant with secondary technology integration — the Oath-Under-Wind binds the throne to the wind as sovereign element; the monarch is the wind's human-bearing vessel; singular legitimacy is held by the primary wind-bearer
**Founding Event:** The Gale Ascent — Aerion the First-Winged ascended; the wind followed; the plateau clans understood; oath sworn at Aerion's Landing Stone; gathered mountain communities, Aerie Plateau clans, and Long Coast harbor-lords under a single wind-sworn oath
**Founder:** Aerion the First-Winged — first sovereign; Dragon-Human lineage; wind affinity of a depth recorded as beyond individual comparison in court annals
**Founding Oath / Charter:** The Gale Oath-Binding — a single oath: one element, one kingdom, one line of succession through those who could carry the wind; oath text transmitted orally rather than written; wind itself is the Compact's medium of record
**Current Rulers:** King Aeries (primary wind-bearer; Dragon-Human tri-form; Ruler Level 2 Mid — cross-ref: aeries.md); Queen Aalia (storm-keeper; Dragon-Human tri-form; Ruler Level 1 Mid — cross-ref: aalia.md)
**Territorial Extent:** Eastern edge of the Central Highlands; descending from the Aerie Plateau in the west to the Long Coast in the east
**Capital / Seat of Power:** The Aerie Palace — has no walls; wind-cultivation wards define its perimeter and shelter
