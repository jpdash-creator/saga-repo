## Section 1 — Identity and Founding (B)

**Full Name:** Azure Sky Kingdom
**Common Name / Aliases:** Azure Sky; The Azure Kingdom; Azure Sky Crown
**State Type:** Kingdom — K-5 Balanced Kingdom; one of the rarest kingdom types on the Nine Suns Continent; operates at genuinely high level in both cultivation and technology simultaneously
**Governing Philosophy:** Balanced — dual-track integration at constitutional depth; neither cultivation nor technology is secondary; the Twin Oath binds the throne to both tracks equally
**Founding Event:** The Twin Oath — a structured oath-binding undertaken by Theron the Twinned, unifying three predecessor polities (Sapphire Confederation, Lyraen Basin Council, mountain pass fief-holders) without conquest; the founding Compact is the constitutional document of the kingdom
**Founder:** Theron the Twinned — first sovereign; dual-cultivator; swore dual fealty simultaneously; title in royal annals: *First Sovereign of the Twin Oath*
**Founding Oath / Charter:** The Twin Oath Compact — every ruling sovereign swears dual fealty: once to the cultivation tradition (binding to the academies and the Windward Reach), once to the technology tradition (binding to the guild halls and the Sapphire Basin forges); failure to honor either oath is throne-voiding
**Current Rulers:** King Varys (cultivation-side regalia; sword-bearer; Ruler Level 3 Mid; 35 years old — cross-ref: varys.md); Queen Valia (technology-side regalia; charter-bearer; Ruler Level 1 Mid; 33 years old — cross-ref: valia.md)
**Territorial Extent:** Temperate highland plateau, central-western Nine Suns Continent; bounded by the Ember Reach ridge to the west, the Lyraen River to the east, the Windward Range passes to the north; southern frontier is open plain transitioning to the Lyraen lowland basin
**Capital / Seat of Power:** Royal Palace of Twin Courts, Sapphire Basin capital (capital city not yet named in prose)

**Military Organization:** Paired orders — the Sovereignty-Swords (cultivation-side combat force; directly sworn to the cultivation-side oath; headed by the Grand Marshal of Swords) and the Sovereignty-Signets (technology-side combat force; directly sworn to the technology-side oath; headed by the Grand Marshal of Signets); the Twin Host is the combined-arms reserve; frontier formations include the Ember Reach Watch and the Northern Accord Expeditionary Wing
**Cultivation Force Tier:** High — Ruler-realm king at apex; cultivation-trained military with kingdom-wide cultivation culture
**Technology Force Tier:** Tier 3 active — technology-equipped military orders; Paired Forges produce blue-steel dual-inscription armaments
**Active Conflicts:** Windward Pass CPM accumulation crisis — bilateral formation with Gale Kingdom; not yet an open conflict; in active bilateral assessment protocol
**Political Posture:** Non-expansionist by doctrine (Non-Aggression Default); defensive-plus-diplomatic; offensive action requires dual-signature ratification at the highest threshold
