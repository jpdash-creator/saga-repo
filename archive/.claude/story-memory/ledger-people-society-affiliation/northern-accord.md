## Section 3 — Terms (B)

**Core Canonical Provisions:**

1. **Joint Patrol of Windward Range Passes** — Azure Sky and Gale share patrol jurisdiction of the Windward Range passes under a joint corridor; each kingdom contributes a standing force (Azure Sky: Northern Accord Expeditionary Wing; Gale: Northern Accord Expeditionary Wing — named symmetrically); patrol-jurisdiction questions are governed by session protocol (see Provision 3 below)

2. **Mutual Right of Asylum** — each kingdom is obligated to extend asylum to recognized nationals of the other in defined emergency circumstances; terms not yet established in prose beyond named existence

3. **Customs-Sharing on the Lyraen** — a customs and tariff coordination clause for the Lyraen River crossing shared between the kingdoms; cross-ref: Lyraen River Compact (separate multi-party instrument)

4. **Mutual Military Aid Against External Aggressors** — each kingdom is obligated to render military assistance to the other against external attack; triggers and thresholds not yet established in prose beyond named existence

5. **Amendment Protocol:**
   - Sovereign co-signatory required on both sides at least 2 hours before any patrol-jurisdiction session
   - The amendment protocol governs what can be addressed in a given session format

6. **Session Format Categories:**
   - **Review Session** — limited-agenda bilateral meeting under the Northern Accord; governs patrol-jurisdiction discussion and data review; does not constitute a full treaty event
   - **Full Treaty Event** — broader-scope treaty activation or amendment; requires additional protocol; not yet established in detail

**The Windward Protocol (Sub-Instrument):**

The Windward Protocol is a named sub-instrument of the Northern Accord governing bilateral data-sharing in the Windward Range passes. Key canonical provisions:
- Both sides exchange full monitoring datasets from their respective perimeter arrays
- Both sides have midpoint array access within the joint patrol corridor
- The Windward Protocol enables the bilateral verification mechanism for the CPM accumulation crisis response: both sides' midpoint array access must be coordinated under the Protocol for any bilateral discharge operation

## Section 5 — Violation Profile (B)

**Known Violation Sensitivity:**
- Unilateral action in the joint patrol corridor without Northern Accord session authorization would constitute a violation — the Windward Pass CPM accumulation crisis response required Windward Protocol data-sharing precisely because unilateral action is not the operative mode under the Accord
- Amendment protocol breach (proceeding without sovereign co-signatory acknowledgment) would violate Provision 5

**CSS Consequence Class:** Not yet formally established in prose; mutual aid violation would presumably trigger significant CSS consequences given the AFF-3 formal alliance level
