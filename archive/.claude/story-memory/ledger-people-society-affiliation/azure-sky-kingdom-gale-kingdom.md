## Section 5 — Relationship History (B)

**Pre-Foundation:** Both kingdoms trace their founding to the same generational window — Theron the Twinned (Azure Sky founder) and Aerion the First-Winged (Gale founder); this shared founding-generation context is part of the historical basis for the bilateral relationship (nine-suns-continent.md §5.2)

**Northern Accord Establishment:** The primary formalization of the relationship; specific historical moment and prior-accord relationship state not yet established in prose

**Second Bilateral Infrastructure Accord:** A prior historical treaty that commissioned both the Northern Accord corridor infrastructure and the Windward Protocol relay stations; preceded the current story by enough time for the infrastructure to be established and operational; demonstrates the depth of the bilateral relationship's historical investment

**At Story Start:** The relationship is stable and active; no known recent strain or shift

- Unilateral action in the joint patrol corridor
