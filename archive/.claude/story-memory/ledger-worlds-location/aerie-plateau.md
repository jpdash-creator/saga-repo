## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** The Aerie Plateau
**Common Name / Aliases:** Aerie Plateau; The Plateau
**Location Tier (Part 13):** Sub-World — highland plateau; the western extent of Gale Kingdom territory; within the Central Highlands region
**Cosmological Area (Part 13):** Sub-region of Central Highlands; sub-region of Gale Kingdom territory (cross-ref: central-highlands.md, nine-suns-continent.md)
**Part 13 Character:** Wind-affinity highland; cultivation-dominant; the Aerie Plateau is the upland foundation of Gale Kingdom's wind-cultivation identity; Aerion the First-Winged ascended from here
**Sacred Ground Status:** Potentially — the Gale Oath-Binding was sworn here (at Aerion's Landing Stone within the plateau precinct); formal sacred ground designation to be established in prose
**Containing World:** Saha (cross-ref: saha.md)
**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:** The western highland extent of the Gale Kingdom's territory. The kingdom descends from this plateau in the west to the Long Coast in the east. Aerion the First-Winged gathered the Aerie Plateau clans here at the Gale founding. The Aerie Palace is situated here. The plateau is cultivation-terraced (modest yield). Home to the Aerie Plateau Governor (a direct appointee of the Wind-Crown). Wind-academy monasteries and the Oath-Voices oral tradition are grounded here.
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** Wind-affinity cultivation; the sacred origin of Gale Kingdom; the Returning Wing belief (Aerion's wind-spirit still circles the Windward Range) is broadly held on the Aerie Plateau; Dragon-Human lineage culture
