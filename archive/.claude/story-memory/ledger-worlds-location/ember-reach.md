## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** The Ember Reach
**Common Name / Aliases:** The Reach; Ember Reach
**Location Tier (Part 13):** Sub-World — continental region-scale; one of five canonical internal geographic regions of the Nine Suns Continent; also: the specific ocean face (Western Reach) is a separate location entry
**Cosmological Area (Part 13):** Sub-region of Nine Suns Continent (cross-ref: nine-suns-continent.md)
**Part 13 Character:** Fire-affinity cultivation with industrial thermal technology integration — fire-affinity and stone-affinity polities; the region's distinctive character is the merger of cultivation fire with industrial thermal technology
**Sacred Ground Status:** Not yet established — to be defined in prose; volcanic terrain may carry sacred ground designations
**Containing World:** Saha (cross-ref: saha.md)
**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:** The western volcanic coast of the Nine Suns Continent and its inland ridges. Fronts the Western Reach ocean. Volcanic ridges define the western cultural frontier with the Central Highlands' paired-oath cultures. Home to fire-affinity and stone-affinity polities. The boundary between cultivation and technology is unusually merged in this region.
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** A third pattern — not cultivation-dominant in the Gale sense, not dual-track balanced in the Azure Sky sense; the Ember Reach polities represent the single-element-but-technology-dominant variant; fire as both the cultivation substrate and the manufacturing engine
