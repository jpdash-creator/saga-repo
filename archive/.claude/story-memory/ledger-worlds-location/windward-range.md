## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** The Windward Range
**Common Name / Aliases:** The Range; Windward Mountains; The Range (informal)
**Location Tier (Part 13):** Sub-World — continental mountain system; the spine separating the Central Highlands from the Northern Frozen Marches
**Cosmological Area (Part 13):** Mountain system of Nine Suns Continent; shared jurisdictionally between Azure Sky Kingdom and Gale Kingdom (cross-ref: nine-suns-continent.md §5.8)
**Part 13 Character:** High-elevation mountain system; wind-affinity ambient energy concentration; convergence-risk geography; jointly patrolled under Northern Accord
**Sacred Ground Status:** Not yet established — mountain passes are sites of Windward Range pilgrimage tradition (non-sect cultural rite); to be formally established in prose
**Containing World:** Saha (cross-ref: saha.md)
**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:** Visible from the Azure Sky Palace platform on clear mornings as *"a dark and irregular line above the plain."* The Windward Range's silhouette *"hardened from suggestion to definite line"* as dawn light arrived. The air above the passes moves in patterns that experienced cultivators can read for weather prediction. The range is the source of the Lyraen River's headwaters. The nine convergence-risk formation sites of the continent are distributed across the continent's major mountain systems; the Windward Range is one confirmed site (the Windward Pass).
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** A pilgrimage tradition — citizens of many kingdoms undertake a pilgrimage to one or more Windward Range passes as a personal threshold rite; routes jointly protected under the Northern Accord; the range is the northern boundary of civilization on the continent's central spine
