## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** The Southern March
**Common Name / Aliases:** Southern March; Border Kingdoms of the Southern March
**Location Tier (Part 13):** Sub-World — continental region-scale; one of five canonical internal geographic regions of the Nine Suns Continent
**Cosmological Area (Part 13):** Sub-region of Nine Suns Continent (cross-ref: nine-suns-continent.md)
**Part 13 Character:** Technology-dominant with cultivation integration — technology-dominant kingdoms are more common here given raw material abundance and river infrastructure supporting manufacturing at scale; water-affinity cultivation co-present
**Sacred Ground Status:** Not yet established — to be defined in prose
**Containing World:** Saha (cross-ref: saha.md)
**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:** The broad southern plain and river basin of the Nine Suns Continent, descending to the Southern Sea. Holds kingdoms by the dozen. Contains the mouth of the Lyraen River (delta at Saltmere). Border kingdoms sit between the continent's agricultural heartland and the water. Raw material abundance and river infrastructure support manufacturing at scale.
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** Technology-dominant sub-culture predominates; water-affinity cultivation is present and distinctive along the Lyraen basin; the region is bounded by the agricultural heartland of the continent and by open water
