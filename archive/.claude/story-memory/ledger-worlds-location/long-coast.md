## Section 1 — Identity and Classification (B)

**Full Name:** The Long Coast
**Common Name / Aliases:** Long Coast; The Coast
**Location Tier (Part 13):** Sub-World — coastal zone; the eastern maritime frontier of the Gale Kingdom; faces the Greater Eastern Ocean
**Cosmological Area (Part 13):** Sub-region of Eastern Windward; coastal zone of Nine Suns Continent (cross-ref: nine-suns-continent.md)
**Part 13 Character:** Wind-affinity maritime coast; the Long Coast Fleet (Gale Kingdom's naval arm) operates from this zone; Long Coast Warden (non-governor title acknowledging maritime character)
**Sacred Ground Status:** Not yet established — The Silent Tower (named Long Coast landmark with canonical "no wind inside" status) may carry sacred designation; to be established in prose
**Containing World:** Saha (cross-ref: saha.md)
**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:** The long eastern coast that is the eastern extent of the Gale Kingdom's territory. Gale Kingdom descends from the Aerie Plateau in the west to the Long Coast in the east. Approximately 300 miles from the Azure Sky Palace platform in the northwest (*"over the Long Coast direction, over the three hundred miles of air and highland terrain between the palace platform and the sea"*). The coast faces the Greater Eastern Ocean. The Long Coast Shipwrights' Order and the Long Coast Fleet are based here.
**Cultural Association (Part 25):** Maritime; Gale Kingdom's wind-affinity coastal culture; Long Coast harbor-lords were among those sworn at the founding Gale Oath-Binding; maritime service and trade character; the Long Coast Fleet (Wind-Sail Cutters) represents the kingdom's naval identity
