**Common Name:** Saha / Planet Saha / The World of Saha
**Location Tier (Part 13):** Tier 7 — World

**Part 13 World Class:** W-5 Mixed World — a large-scale world with a dual-track civilizational character; cultivation-heavy regions and technology-heavy regions coexist across the multiple continents and island groupings; no single affinity dominates at the planetary scale
**Sacred Ground Status:** Not yet established — to be defined in prose
**Containing Star System:** Sol Star System (cross-ref: sol-star-system.md)
**Physical Description:**
Saha is a very large planet by galactic standards. It completes its rotation once every twenty-six standard hours. It is the third world inward from its star within the Sol Star System — one of eight orbital bodies around a single yellow star. It hosts multiple continents and island groupings of significant geographic and political diversity. The planet sustains multiple civilizational paradigms simultaneously — some regions are cultivation-dominant, others are technology-dominant, and some (notably the Nine Suns Continent, home of Azure Sky Kingdom and Gale Kingdom) operate at high-level dual integration. The planet is orbited by one large habitable moon (the Moon of Saha — unnamed; cross-ref: moon-of-saha.md).

**Named Continents / Major Geographic Divisions (B — partial, to be expanded in prose):**

**Cultural Association (Part 25):** Mixed planetary culture — cultivation-dominant and technology-dominant sub-cultures coexist; overall planetary identity is dual-track capable but uneven in distribution

**Special Zone — FIRMAMENT LOCK (inherited from Sol Star System):**
The Firmament Lock suppression field (see sol-star-system.md Section 2) applies at full force across all of Saha. Technology ceiling: Tier 6 (current: Tier 3). Cultivation ceiling (system-wide): Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid.

**Saha Planetary Cultivation Ceiling (Locked Canon):**
> While the Sol Star System's Firmament Lock permits cultivation up to **Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid** system-wide, **Saha itself** — as a planet — imposes a tighter planetary environmental cap. The maximum cultivation realm that can be reached and sustained *on the surface of Saha* is **Absolute God Level 8 High** (Xiantian apex sub-level). Cultivators who advance beyond Absolute God Level 8 High cannot remain on Saha's surface without destabilizing their own cultivation or the planetary environment; further advancement requires off-world cultivation (e.g., deeper into the Sol Star System, within specialized formations on Hoth, or in the Moon of Saha's sect-governed upper-tier environment). The Core realm (Core Formation, Core Assimilation) is not accessible on Saha's surface; it is accessible only on Hoth within the Sol Star System.
>
> **Phase 1 Narrative Ceiling on Saha (Part 30):** Ruler Level 9 early — far below the planetary ceiling. Phase 1 prose set on Saha does not render any character above Ruler Level 9 early; higher in-world realms up to the Absolute God Level 8 High planetary cap exist but are off-screen during Phase 1. See `sol-star-system.md` Section 2 for the full two-layer ceiling model.

**Planetary CS%/TS% Profile:**
- **Cultivation Synergy % (CS%) (Part 31 Area 21):** 65% — Saha exhibits strong cultivation affinity at the planetary scale; 65% of the planetary population/infrastructure is oriented toward or integrated with the cultivation paradigm
- **Technology Synergy % (TS%) (Part 31 Area 21):** 65% — Saha simultaneously exhibits strong technology affinity at the planetary scale; 65% orientation toward or integration with the technology paradigm

> **Note on Dual 65% Values:** CS% and TS% are independent axes (Part 31 Area 21). A world can be simultaneously cultivation-dominant and technology-dominant if different populations and regions drive each axis. Saha's dual-65% profile indicates a planet where both tracks are robustly present, though different areas may skew more heavily toward one or the other. This is a rare but canon-valid configuration for a balanced-integration world.
