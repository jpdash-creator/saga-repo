## Section 5 — Lore (Part 28 Area 17)

**Schema:** Part 28 Area 17.2 — Location Domain (royal seat tier)

### 5.1 — Architectural Signature

The Azure Sky Palace is a paired architectural complex: two physically separated audience halls (Sword Court for cultivation petitions; Signet Court for technology petitions) bridged by a central reconciliation chamber where dual-signature edicts are ratified. The Sapphire Basin capital's azure roof tiles are visible from the palace platform, catching first light in the particular way that gives the kingdom its name.

### 5.2 — The Platform

The west-edge upper courtyard platform is the location of Varys's daily pre-dawn contemplation practice — 11 continuous years of mornings, as established in Prose 00001. No railing. Built for sight. Sapphire Basin bedrock, blue-flecked, laid without mortar. The stone was chosen for flatness and acoustic quality — the Sapphire Line's frequency conducts from below without distortion. Three inscription bands on the north face: cultivation-script (top), technology-schematic (middle), old foundational hand (bottom — predates both scripts). The view north: the Windward Range's silhouette on clear mornings. The view east: the Long Coast direction, ~300 miles.

### 5.3 — Sapphire Line Integration

The palace is one of the primary nodes in the Sapphire Line network (cross-ref: ledger-technology-scale/sapphire-line.md). The bedrock beneath the palace foundations carries the frequency. The residential corridor approaching the platform conducts at thin, high pitch shaped by acoustic geometry. The pitch drops to the low register at the platform threshold. The frequency is present before dawn and settles below the sternum — even without pressure, occupying the boundary between sound and sensation.
