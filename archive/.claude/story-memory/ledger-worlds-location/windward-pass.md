### 5.1 — The Convergence Formation

The Windward Pass is classified as one of nine known convergence-risk formation sites in the Nine Suns Continent's major mountain systems (per nine-suns-continent.md §5.7). The two-source convergence geometry is the defining risk: the specific pass topography creates a bilateral convergence effect where two adjacent perimeter cultivation sources simultaneously feed a midpoint accumulation zone. Midpoint density rises non-linearly (not dispersing on its own). The standard bilateral monitoring architecture — designed for single-source accumulation — was structurally unequipped to detect this from perimeter data alone. A midpoint field survey was required.
