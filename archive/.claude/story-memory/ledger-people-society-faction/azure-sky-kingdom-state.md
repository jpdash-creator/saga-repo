### 7.1 — Founding Event

The ruling state was founded by **Theron the Twinned** (first sovereign, seven generations ago — see azure-sky-kingdom.md §5.2 for the continent-scale founding narrative). The founding event of the ruling state specifically — as a political body distinct from the territory — is the **Twin Oath Compact**, a ratified instrument that binds the sovereign, the council, and the principal institutions of the kingdom to the dual-track obligation. The founding Compact is canonically preserved in two identical copies — one in the Sword Court archive, one in the Signet Court archive — and the kingdom's constitutional law requires both copies to be physically present at every accession.

### 7.2 — Doctrine

The Azure Sky ruling state's foundational doctrine is the **Twin Oath**: cultivation-side fealty and technology-side fealty, sworn simultaneously and equally weighted. Doctrinal corollaries canonically derived from the Twin Oath:
- **Paired Legitimacy** — no kingdom-wide edict is lawful without dual signature.
- **Proportional Investment** — kingdom budget, military investment, academic funding, and guild subvention must be allocated proportionally between cultivation and technology tracks, with deviations requiring formal dual-signature exception.
- **Dual-Track Nobility** — nobility rank is tied to demonstrated service in one track with honorable standing in the other; single-track service is ceiling-capped.
- **Non-Aggression Default** — the doctrine discourages expansionist war; Azure Sky's historical military posture is defensive-plus-diplomatic, with offensive action requiring dual-signature ratification at the highest threshold.

### 7.3 — Governance Structure

The state is a dual-signatory constitutional monarchy with three principal organs:
- **The Crown-in-Twin** — the sovereign pair (Varys + Valia) acting jointly; the primary executive and lawgiving authority.
- **The Paired Council** — the deliberative council that advises the Crown; composed of a Cultivation-Side caucus (led by the **Master of Arms** — head of cultivation-side military and academy matters) and a Technology-Side caucus (led by the **Master of Charters** — head of technology-side guild and institutional matters); the two caucus heads are ex-officio Regents in the event of sovereign incapacity.
- **The Twin Tribunals** — the paired judicial bodies; the **Sword Tribunal** handles cultivation-related legal matters (academy discipline, cultivation disputes, cultivation-inflected criminal cases) and the **Signet Tribunal** handles technology-related legal matters (guild disputes, charter enforcement, technology-inflected criminal cases); matters spanning both tracks go to the **Reconciliation Tribunal**, which sits en banc.

Administrative infrastructure below the Paired Council is a uniform provincial structure: each province has a paired administrative seat (a Sword Office and a Signet Office) under a paired Governor-and-Chartermaster team.

### 7.4 — Military Command Structure

The kingdom's military is organized as **paired orders**:
- **The Sovereignty-Swords** — the cultivation-side military order; the primary cultivation combat force; directly sworn to the cultivation-side oath; headed by the **Grand Marshal of Swords** (position, not yet named in prose).
- **The Sovereignty-Signets** — the technology-side military order; the primary technology-equipped combat force; directly sworn to the technology-side oath; headed by the **Grand Marshal of Signets** (position, not yet named in prose).
- **The Twin Host** — the combined-arms reserve formation that deploys both orders in integrated tactical configurations; doctrinally reserved for existential-tier conflict; last mobilized in the reign of Mireya the Stormbound (three generations ago, per royal annals).

Frontier-specific formations: the **Ember Reach Watch** (paired joint garrison of the western boundary towers); the **Northern Accord Expeditionary Wing** (the kingdom's standing contribution to the joint Windward Range patrols with Gale).

### 7.5 — Oath Network

The kingdom is bound by the following canonical oaths and agreements (each is a Part 23 Oath-and-Binding instrument):
- **The Twin Oath Compact** (internal constitutional — §7.1).
- **The Northern Accord** (external; with Gale Kingdom; covers Windward passes, mutual asylum, mutual aid; co-signatory of record: King Varys).
- **The Lyraen River Compact** (external; multi-party; governs the Lyraen basin).
- **The Sovereign Accession Oath** (sworn at every accession, binding the incoming sovereign to the Twin Oath under penalty of throne-voiding).
- **The Academy-Guild Accord** (internal; binds the three academies and three guilds to the proportional investment doctrine).

### 7.6 — Economic Base

The ruling state's fiscal base draws from: a blue-steel export levy (principal revenue — Azure Crafters' Concord-mediated); phase-crystal oscillator export duties (Skylight Analytic Hall); a uniform agricultural tithe from the Sapphire Basin; Lyraen River customs (shared with Gale under the Lyraen River Compact); academy-and-guild service fees; and a treasury reserve maintained at constitutional minima by the Master of Charters.

### 7.7 — Reputation and Myth

Continental reputation: Azure Sky is regarded as the exemplar of the dual-track ideal — respected for doing genuinely well what most kingdoms only attempt. Reputation for reliability (the kingdom's treaties are honored), for restraint (non-expansionist by doctrine), and for a distinctive paired aesthetic. In folk speech, "to hold with the Twin" is a continental idiom for a reliable partner; "to break the Twin" is the inverse.

Internal mythology: the **Ascending Twinship** — a popular belief (not doctrinally endorsed) that Theron the Twinned did not die but ascended, and that the founding Compact is therefore still spiritually enforced by Theron's unseen presence. The crown neither affirms nor refutes this belief.
