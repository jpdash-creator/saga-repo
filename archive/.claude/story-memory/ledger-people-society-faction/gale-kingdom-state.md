### 7.1 — Founding Event

The ruling state was founded by **Aerion the First-Winged** (first sovereign, same generational era as the Azure Sky Twin Oath — see gale-kingdom.md §5.2 for the continent-scale founding narrative). The founding event of the ruling state specifically is the **Gale Oath-Binding**: following the Gale Ascent circumnavigation, Aerion gathered the mountain communities, Aerie Plateau clans, and Long Coast harbor-lords on the stone that became **Aerion's Landing Stone** and bound them by a single oath sworn under the wind. The founding oath text is transmitted orally rather than written, a deliberate choice that has canonically survived seven generations — Gale law treats the wind itself as the Compact's medium of record.

### 7.2 — Doctrine

The Gale ruling state's foundational doctrine is the **Oath-Under-Wind**: a single, absolute fealty sworn to the wind as sovereign element, with the monarch as the wind's human-bearing vessel. Doctrinal corollaries:
- **Singular Legitimacy** — sovereign authority is held by whichever of the ruling pair is the primary wind-bearer; Aeries is currently the primary wind-bearer, Aalia the primary storm-keeper, and edicts are issued by Aeries with Aalia's counter-sign as storm-keeper only for military-force matters.
- **Dragon-Lineage Precedence** — canonically, full-blooded Dragon-Human cultivators of Aerion's descent hold automatic noble standing; partial-lineage standing is graded by cultivation realm and wind-oath performance.
- **Single-Affinity Depth Preference** — the state doctrinally favors deep single-affinity mastery over breadth; dual-track or cross-affinity cultivators hold honored-guest status but not noble standing under Gale rules.
- **Honored Restraint** — the doctrine commits the kingdom to defensive posture with formal respect for the Windward Range neighbors (the Northern Accord is the expression); offensive war requires public wind-sworn declaration at the Wind Crossing.

### 7.3 — Governance Structure

The state is a single-sworn monarchy with a complementary regent-pair in the reigning couple; three principal organs:
- **The Wind-Crown** — the reigning sovereign (Aeries) as primary wind-bearer; the executive authority.
- **The Storm-Counsel** — the storm-keeper sovereign (Aalia) as counter-sign authority for military-force matters, plus the **Wind-Council** (a body of named wind-academy elders, Dragon-lineage elders, and Long Coast harbor-lords that advises the Wind-Crown).
- **The Stone Tribunal** — the judicial body, canonically held on a named ring of stones at the Aerie Palace precinct; adjudicates wind-oath violations, dragon-lineage disputes, and inter-clan matters.

Administrative infrastructure below the Wind-Council is a **governor-per-zone** structure: the Lyraen Frontier Governor, the Aerie Plateau Governor (a direct appointee of the Wind-Crown), the Tempest Valleys Governor, and the Long Coast Warden (a non-governor title acknowledging the maritime character of the eastern zone).

### 7.4 — Military Command Structure

The kingdom's military is organized as **wind-orders**:
- **The Stormwing Order** — the elite cultivation combat force; full-blooded Dragon-Human cultivators form the inner cadre; partial-lineage and ordinary wind-cultivators form the outer cadre. The Stormwing is the Wind-Crown's standing guard and the kingdom's rapid-response force.
- **The Windward Host** — the standing conventional military; wind-cultivation-trained infantry and Aerie Flightcraft messenger wings; garrisoned at Stormbreak Citadel and the Aerie Plateau.
- **The Long Coast Fleet** — the maritime arm; Wind-Sail Cutters crewed by Long Coast Shipwrights' Order mariners.
- **The Wind-Sworn Militia** — zonal militia raised by wind-sworn muster in each governance zone; mobilized by Storm-Counsel counter-sign.

Frontier-specific formations: the **Northern Accord Expeditionary Wing** (paired with Azure Sky's contribution for joint Windward patrols); the **Stone Vigil** (ceremonial-plus-operational garrison of the Aerion's Landing Stone precinct).

### 7.5 — Oath Network

The kingdom is bound by the following canonical oaths (Part 23 instruments):
- **The Gale Oath-Binding** (internal foundational — §7.1).
- **The Northern Accord** (external; with Azure Sky Kingdom; co-signatory of record: King Aeries). Key provisions established in Prose 1–7: (a) Amendment Protocol — supplementary records touching on patrol jurisdiction require sovereign co-signatory signature no less than two hours before any session in which patrol jurisdiction is discussed; (b) Session Format Categories — the Accord distinguishes a "review session" from a "full sovereign-to-sovereign treaty event"; Aeries chose the review format for the Prose 4 bilateral as accurate to what the session was; (c) **Windward Protocol** — sub-instrument governing bilateral monitoring data exchange; both sides commit to full dataset exchange from their respective monitoring stations; both sides maintain midpoint array access for real-time verification; Protocol relay infrastructure built under the Second Bilateral Infrastructure Accord in the same construction period as the Northern Accord corridor infrastructure.
- **The Lyraen River Compact** (external; multi-party).
- **The Wind-Naming Rite Oath** (internal; sworn by every wind-named citizen at age 14; binds the named wind to the citizen's civic identity).
- **The Sovereign Ascension Oath** (sworn at every accession, at the Wind Crossing, witnessed by palace-wind).

### 7.6 — Economic Base

The ruling state's fiscal base draws from: a wind-affinity cultivation ingredient export duty (principal revenue); Long Coast maritime service levies; a distinctive **Flight-fee** charged to non-Gale citizens requiring Aerie Flightcraft transit of the kingdom; Aerie Plateau agricultural tithe (modest — the plateau is cultivation-terraced and low-yield); Lyraen River Compact customs share; academy and guild service fees. The kingdom maintains a smaller treasury reserve than Azure Sky, reflecting its lower technology tier and cultivation-dominant resource allocation.

### 7.7 — Reputation and Myth

Continental reputation: Gale is regarded as the exemplar of wind-affinity cultivation; the Dragon-Human royal lineage is continental prestige; the kingdom's word-is-wind reputation makes Gale diplomatic commitments highly valued. In folk speech, "wind-sworn" is a continental idiom for a commitment beyond ordinary guarantee; "to leave a silence" is the idiom for breaking a wind-oath.

Internal mythology: the **Returning Wing** — a popular belief that Aerion did not die in the last storm but became a wind-spirit that still circles the Windward Range, and that certain rare ambient-wind conditions are Aerion's direct presence. The crown neither affirms nor refutes; the belief is broadly held on the Aerie Plateau and near the Long Coast.
