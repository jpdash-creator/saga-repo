## Section 2 — Capability Profile (B)

**Primary Function:** Kingdom-wide communication relay — transmits signal through the capital's foundation bedrock using phase-crystal oscillators at every circuit junction; the cultivation-amplified component provides a clarity and resonance character that pure technology transmission does not achieve

**Capability Ceiling:** Tier 3 (Part 8); cannot exceed Azure Sky Kingdom's active technology tier without kingdom-level technology advancement; Firmament Lock caps the ultimate ceiling at Tier 6

**Established Capabilities:**

1. **Foundation Routing:** Runs beneath the capital's foundations through bedrock older than the city's name — the routing pre-dates or is co-foundational with the city itself; the bedrock layer is the structural medium
2. **Phase-Crystal Junction Resonance:** Phase-crystals set into every circuit junction translate the current's passage into a low, even resonance — something the ear can almost but not quite resolve; the resonance settles below the sternum, steady and without pressure; frequency occupies the boundary between sound and sensation; it is not a sound in the conventional sense but not entirely subsensory either
3. **Capital Ambient Signature:** The hum is an ambient signature of the capital itself — audible (in the subsensory sense described above) throughout the capital precincts; it occupies the predawn silence before any surface noise begins
4. **Palace Floor-Stone Conduction:** Within the Royal Palace of Twin Courts, the Sapphire Line's resonance is conducted through the floor-stone at a thinner, higher pitch than outside — the acoustic geometry of each corridor and chamber shapes the transmission character; the residential corridor pitch is canonically described as thin and high relative to the external ambient
5. **Dawn Load-Transition Behavior:** Transitions from overnight load to operational load at dawn — the frequency itself holds constant regardless of load level, but a faint additional texture appears at the transition point, as if the system announces the shift from overnight to operational without altering the primary signal; Varys has observed this behavior over years of residence; it is not formally documented in the kingdom's technical records

**Dual-Track Integration Note:** The Sapphire Line is the kingdom's primary example of dual-track (cultivation + technology) infrastructure at the system level — it is not merely a technology system with a cultivation-adjacent function but is architecturally dual-track: the signal transmission is technological (bedrock routing, phase-crystal junctions, circuit architecture) and the signal clarity/resonance is cultivation-amplified (the phase-crystals function as cultivation-interface components, not purely mechanical transducers). This dual-track integration is consistent with Azure Sky Kingdom's K-5 Balanced Kingdom classification.
