# Guardrail: Content Density Law (CDL-2)

**Source:** Part 35 (Content Density Law CDL-2) · full detail: `ssc-narration-p35-*` skills
**Applies to:** Prose Scorer (primary owner); Prose Maker (must satisfy the floors)

## The rule

Every prose unit is scored across **10 content-density categories** (Combat, Named Moves &
Techniques, Cultivation Growth, Technology, Lore & World-Building, Dialogue & Social,
Information & Intrigue, Consequence & Psychology, Travel & Discovery, Political & Faction),
tracked both per-prose and as a running Section aggregate against a Phase 1 Section×Category
target table (Low–Anchor–High ranges).

- **Universal Anchor Floors**: Combat, Cultivation Growth, Dialogue & Social, and Lore &
  World-Building each carry a **≥20% per-Section minimum** — these four are marked ★ and may
  never be quietly under-served across a Section even if other categories run high.
- The Completeness Indicator (Gate 2 Block A) reports this scoring every time, with an
  ON TRACK / ⚠ WARN / ✗ FAIL status per category against the target range — this is not an
  end-of-Section-only check.

## Hard rules

- Scoring is the Prose Scorer's job specifically — it should be run with consistency, not
  chased for maximal depth on any single pass (this is why the Prose Scorer runs at `high`
  effort rather than `xhigh`: consistency across many scoring passes matters more than
  squeezing extra depth from any one of them, and v1→v2 comparisons must stay reliable).
- CDL-2 supersedes the retired CDL-1 framework entirely — never reference CDL-1 category
  names or thresholds.
- A category running persistently low against its target is reported plainly in the running
  Section aggregate, not smoothed over or excused by strong performance elsewhere.

## Escalation

If a Section is trending toward an Anchor Floor violation, flag it before the Section closes,
not after — there's no fixing an Anchor Floor miss retroactively without a rewrite.
