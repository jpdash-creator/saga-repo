## Area 14 — Growth Law Interface with Part 10 (Science Law — Fully Authored)

### 14.1 — Part 10 Provides the Scientific Prerequisite Content

Part 10 (Technology Science Law) is fully authored as of 2026-04-12 (Areas 1–16, v1.0). Part 9's advancement prerequisite tables (Area 4.2 and 4.3) include Category I — Scientific Knowledge Prerequisites — previously marked *[P10 stub]*, now superseded by Part 10 Area 3. The following have been resolved by Part 10's authoring:

1. **Tier-by-Tier Scientific Prerequisites** — Part 10 Area 3 provides specific scientific discovery prerequisites for all 20 tier transitions, using the Four-Layer Knowledge Stack (L1–L4) framework; all entries previously marked *[P10 stub]* in Area 4.2 now reference *[Part 10, Area 3]*
2. **Domain-Specific Science Tables** — Part 10 Area 4 provides Domain-Specific Science Tables for all 14 domains at threshold levels 5, 8, 11, 15, and 20; these tables are the definitive Category I prerequisite reference for domain-specific tier transitions
3. **Acceleration Calibration** — Part 9's Cultivation-Assisted Discovery mechanism (Area 2.3) and cultivation acceleration percentages (Area 6.6) are calibrated against Part 10 Area 7's Soul Sense Resolution table by realm and Part 10 Area 13's integration advantage multipliers by domain; the governance split is: Part 9 governs the acceleration percentage and mechanism; Part 10 governs the scientific data quality produced by the cultivation contribution
4. **FTL Science Prerequisites** — Part 10 Area 5 (FTL Physics Framework) provides the definitive scientific prerequisites for Transportation Type 9 Level 9+ (FTL capability); the Minimum Physical Duration (2–5 generations) governed by Part 10 Area 5 is the key constraint on FTL timeline, not resource availability
5. **AI Sentience Scientific Classification** — Part 10 Area 6 governs the AI Sentience Ontology; Part 9's advancement tables for AI (Type 4, Domain Type 4) are calibrated against Part 10's S-Classification thresholds

### 14.2 — Governance Split: Part 9 Governs Mechanics; Part 10 Governs Content

The governance split is active and bidirectional: Part 10 states WHAT scientific knowledge is required for each tier transition. Part 9 states HOW that knowledge is discovered — through which growth mechanism (Mechanisms 1–4 in Area 2), at what standard timeline, and under what acceleration or deceleration conditions. Neither Part is sufficient alone: knowledge without discovery mechanics is a prerequisite list with no process; discovery mechanics without knowledge content is a process without a destination. Both Parts must be consulted together for all technology advancement governance.

### 14.3 — Part 10 Principles That Directly Govern Part 9 Mechanics

**P10-1 (Science Cannot Be Created in a Vacuum)** directly reinforces Part 9's D1 Scientific Depth requirement — the scientific foundation must be built layer by layer; Part 9's "borrowed foundations" failure mode is the mechanical expression of P10-1.

**P10-5 (Accumulation Principle)** governs Part 9's Re-Advancement mechanics — the reason re-advancement is 5–50% faster than initial advancement is that scientific knowledge (L1–L4 layers) is non-reversible in foundation; even when physical infrastructure is lost, the theoretical framework that Part 10 governs may persist in practitioner memory and records.

**P10-6 (Scale of Experiment)** establishes why Part 9 acceleration mechanisms cannot compress timelines below the minimum thresholds — high-tier scientific prerequisites require physical experiments at the scale of the phenomenon being studied; resource investment cannot substitute for the time required to conduct and validate those experiments.

**P10-10 (Constraint Principle)** governs the ceiling on Part 9's Cultivation-Assisted Research acceleration — cultivation observation generates Layer 1 empirical data, not validated theory; the theoretical integration step (Layer 3) and predictive validation (Layer 4) still require the full institutional framework, which is why cultivation acceleration percentages in Part 9 Area 6.6 have a ceiling (45% maximum) and are domain-specific.
