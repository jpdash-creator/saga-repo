## Area 5 — Weapon Damage Architecture

### 5.1 — The Five Damage Vectors

Every weapon delivers destructive effect through one or more of five Damage Vectors. The damage vector determines what type of damage the weapon inflicts, what defensive countermeasures are relevant, and how the damage interacts with cultivation healing and technology repair.

| Vector | Designation | Damage Nature | Cultivation Healing Applicable? | Technology Repair Applicable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DV-I | Kinetic-Structural | Physical mass displacement; bone fracture, organ disruption, structural collapse | Yes (for biological targets); dependent on realm for speed | No (for biological); yes for technology objects |
| DV-II | Thermal-Radiant | Heat and radiation delivery; burns, cellular disruption, material fusion | Yes (partial — internal qi circulation needed for radiation); yes for technology shielding repair | Yes |
| DV-III | Electromagnetic | Electrical, magnetic, or EM-spectrum disruption; neural disruption, technology system damage | Limited (nerve damage); yes for technology systems | Yes — specialized EM shielding repair |
| DV-IV | Spiritual-Field | Qi field disruption, soul architecture damage, formation disassembly | Cultivation healing only; technology repair inapplicable | No — requires cultivation restoration |
| DV-V | Systemic | Propagating damage through host vector; biological, nanite, cognitive — spreads beyond initial delivery | Specialized cultivation healing (meridian purge required); specialized antidote technology | Limited (containment possible but rarely reversal) |

### 5.2 — Weapon Damage Multiplier by Target Type

Weapons interact differently with different target categories. The following table defines the Damage Vector efficiency (DE) for each target type. DE-1.0 is baseline; DE above 1.0 means the weapon is more effective against that target type; DE below 1.0 means it is less effective.

| Target Type | DV-I (Kinetic) | DV-II (Thermal) | DV-III (EM) | DV-IV (Spiritual) | DV-V (Systemic) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Biological (unaugmented non-cultivator) | DE-1.0 | DE-1.2 | DE-1.0 | DE-0.1 | DE-1.5 |
| Cultivator (active cultivation defense) | DE-0.3 to 0.8 (realm-dependent) | DE-0.5 to 0.9 | DE-0.8 | DE-1.5 | DE-0.6 |
| Technology Vehicle / Vessel | DE-0.8 to 1.2 | DE-1.0 | DE-1.5 | DE-0.1 | DE-0.3 |
| Formation Array / Relic | DE-0.2 | DE-0.5 | DE-0.8 | DE-2.0 | DE-0.1 |
| Planetary Infrastructure | DE-1.2 | DE-1.0 | DE-0.8 | DE-0.0 | DE-1.3 |
| Stellar Object | DE-0.5 | DE-1.5 | DE-0.3 | DE-0.0 | DE-0.0 |

### 5.3 — Stacking Damage Vector Rules

A weapon may deliver multiple damage vectors simultaneously. When multiple vectors stack on a target:

1. Each vector is assessed independently against the target's defenses.
2. Secondary vectors may find gaps in defenses that the primary vector cannot exploit (e.g., EM damage to a cultivator whose formation defense blocks DV-I but not DV-III).
3. Spiritual-field damage (DV-IV) from T-FORM or T-PSI weapons stacks additively with physical damage and cannot be blocked by the same defense that blocks DV-I.
4. Systemic damage (DV-V) once initiated propagates independently and cannot be stopped by destroying the weapon after delivery.
