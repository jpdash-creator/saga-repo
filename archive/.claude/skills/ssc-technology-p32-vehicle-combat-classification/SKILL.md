## Area 11 — Vehicle Combat Classification

### 11.1 — Vehicle Combat in the Part 5 Framework

All vehicle combat occurs within Part 5's engagement and escalation framework. Part 32 governs the vehicle capabilities; Part 5 governs the legitimacy, escalation, and outcome structure of all combat involving vehicles. The following rules govern the integration:

**Rule 11.1.1 — Vehicle Tier Maps to Force Category:** Part 8, Area 8.2's Technology Force Category table applies to all vehicles. The table determines what cultivation-equivalent combat output a vehicle of a given tier produces. A vehicle does not have a different Force Category from its tier — the tier IS the Force Category.

**Rule 11.1.2 — Configuration Code Governs Engagement Legitimacy:** Under Part 5, Force Legitimacy Law, a vehicle's Configuration Code determines its status:
- MIL and MIL-CAP vessels are legitimate combat targets in any active E3+ engagement.
- NC-configured vessels (FRT, PAS, EXP, COL, MED, DIP) are protected civilian targets. Engaging them outside defined combat contexts is an unlawful use of force under Part 5.
- DU-configured vessels are MIL-equivalent once they activate weapons systems; NC-equivalent when weapons are decommissioned.

**Rule 11.1.3 — Scale Class Determines Escalation Floor:** Vehicles above SC-IV automatically engage at E4 minimum when deployed as combat assets, regardless of the initial escalation context. SC-VI and SC-VII vehicles automatically engage at E6 minimum. This prevents narrative use of massive military vessels in "small skirmish" framing.

### 11.2 — Vehicle Combat Escalation Reference

| Vehicle Class | Combat Configuration | Minimum Escalation Tier | Maximum Normal Escalation Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| ST-Band vehicles (any) | MIL | E1–E2 (Tier 1–2); E3–E4 (Tier 3–5) | E5 formation deployment |
| OS-Band SC-II–III (SWC-1/2) | MIL/PAT | E3 | E5 |
| OS-Band SC-IV–V (SWC-3–5) | MIL | E4 | E6 |
| OS-Band SC-VI (FSW-1/2) | MIL-CAP | E6 | E7 |

| CHV-D/E | SYN/MIL | Per technology tier + cultivation enhancement | +1 escalation tier above equivalent technology-only vessel |

### 11.3 — Vehicle Destruction Consequences

**Rule 11.3.1 — Soul-Interface Damage (CHV-C/D):** As stated in Rule 7.5.1, destruction or severe damage of a CHV-C or CHV-D vessel while a pilot is soul-interfaced causes automatic soul damage (Part 4, Area 2 governs injury). Severity scales with the abruptness of the disconnection: gradual power-down causes minor to moderate soul injury; catastrophic hull destruction while fully interfaced causes severe to critical soul damage.

**Rule 11.3.2 — Formation Array Vessel Collapse:** Destruction of a CHV-B or CHV-D Formation-Primary vessel causes formation array collapse. Formation array collapse releases all stored CPM energy in the array — this is a combat area effect event governed by Part 11, Area 9 (CPM combat applications) and Part 12, Area 7.5 (Formation Array collapse profiles). Narration must account for the formation collapse release.
