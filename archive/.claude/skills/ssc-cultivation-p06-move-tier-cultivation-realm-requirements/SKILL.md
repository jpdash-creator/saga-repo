## Area 4 — Move Tier and Cultivation Realm Requirements

> **Governance boundary — Part 6 Area 4 vs. Part 3 Area 16.2:**
> Part 6 Area 4 is the *classification-side* Move Access Table — it governs what Move Types are standardly, possibly, or rarely accessible at each tier within Part 6's taxonomy framework. Part 3 Area 16.2 is the *physical-side* Move Access Table — it governs the actual physical reason why those access levels exist (meridian structure, dantian capacity, energy output type). Both tables must agree, and they do: Part 3 establishes the physical ceiling; Part 6 classifies what that ceiling means for technique taxonomy. When any conflict appears, Part 3 Area 16.2's physical-ceiling explanation governs. Part 6's "Rare" designations reflect Part 3's physical explanations of why exceptional talent or conditions can extend the ceiling modestly; Part 6's "—" entries reflect Part 3's categorical physical impossibilities (not enough qi output, inactive meridians, absent dantian).

### 4.1 — Access Table

The following table defines the accessibility of each Move Type at each cultivation tier. "Standard" means the type is routinely achievable by cultivators at this tier. "Possible" means achievable by the exceptional few under specific conditions. "Rare" means achievable by the extraordinarily gifted under exceptional circumstances. "—" means categorically inaccessible.

| Move Type | Mortal Tier 1 | Mortal Tier 2 | Xiantian | Core Realm | Divine Realm |
|-----------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Basic | Standard | Standard | Standard | Standard | Standard |
| Intermediate | Rare | Standard | Standard | Standard | Standard |
| Advanced | — | Possible | Standard | Standard | Standard |
| Elite | — | Rare | Standard | Standard | Standard |
| Signature | — | Possible | Standard | Standard | Standard |
| Fusion | — | — | Possible | Standard | Standard |
| Catastrophic | — | — | Rare (peak only) | Standard | Standard |
| Mythic | — | — | — | Rare (peak only) | Standard |

### 4.2 — Access Principle Rules

**Rule 4.2.1 — Realm Minimum, Not Realm Guarantee:**
Reaching a cultivation realm does not automatically grant access to that realm's move type ceiling. A Xiantian Demigod who just entered the realm has Standard access to Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced — but Elite, Signature, and above are still individual achievement targets that require talent, practice, and cultivation experience beyond realm position.

**Rule 4.2.2 — Mortal Tier 1 Physical Exception:**
Mortal Tier 1 cultivators near the Soul stage who are physical martial arts specialists may develop Intermediate physical-architecture techniques. This is the only Type 2 access at Mortal Tier 1, and it requires that the cultivator has achieved near-peak physical refinement.

**Rule 4.2.3 — Fusion Physical Prerequisite:**
Fusion moves require two distinct controllable power streams. Mortal Tier 2 cultivators with both qi circulation AND an awakened soul cultivation (early-awakening cases from Part 4, Area 2.5) may develop physical-soul Fusion techniques. This is extremely rare and narratively significant.

**Rule 4.2.4 — Mythic Reality Threshold:**
The Mythic type's "reality-bending" nature requires a cultivation level where the cultivator's power genuinely interacts with the fabric of reality — which begins at the transition from Core to Divine. A Core Solidification peak cultivator approaching the Divine Transformation threshold may develop and attempt Mythic techniques, but even at their most favorable, the metaphysical barriers remain formidable.

**Rule 4.2.5 — Type Downward Compatibility:**
A Divine Realm cultivator can and should still use Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced moves. Move types are not replaced by higher types as cultivation advances — they are retained and remain valuable as reliable, efficient tools. A Divine cultivator who relies solely on Mythic and Catastrophic moves is tactically limited. The depth of a cultivator's move repertoire across all types is a measure of their combat sophistication.
