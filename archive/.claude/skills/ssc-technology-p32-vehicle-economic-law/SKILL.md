## Area 12 — Vehicle Economic Law

### 12.1 — Vehicle as Economic Asset

Vehicles are primary economic assets in every technology civilization. Their acquisition, ownership, operation, and disposal are governed by the economic law framework of Part 22 (Faction Law — for faction-scale economic assets) and implied by Part 21 (Civilization Law — civilization tier determines vehicle production capacity).

**Rule 12.1.1 — Vehicle Value Scales Exponentially by Tier:** The cost to construct and maintain a vehicle scales approximately geometrically with tier and scale class. A Tier 10 SC-V vessel is not merely 50× more expensive than a Tier 5 SC-V vessel — it is thousands of times more expensive in absolute terms because of the multi-domain prerequisites it requires.

**Rule 12.1.2 — Vehicle Fleet as Power Proxy:** For IS-Band and above vehicles, the size and tier composition of a faction's or civilization's vehicle fleet is a direct proxy for its true power projection capability — more accurate than any single metric. Part 22's Faction Power Tier (FPT) implicitly incorporates vehicle fleet quality as a component of its assessment. Authors must not allow factions to have vehicle fleets inconsistent with their established FPT.

### 12.2 — Vehicle Market Structure

| Vehicle Class | Market Type | Typical Buyers |
|---|---|---|
| ST-Band (Tiers 1–4) | Open market; widely available | Any individual or faction at appropriate tier |
| ST-Band (Tier 5) / OS-Band | Limited civilian market; significant cost | Government bodies, major commercial factions (MTF, STF) |
| IS-Band (Tier 8, near-c) | Government and major faction only | CCT-8+ civilization government; major SCL-8+ factions |
| IS-Band (Tier 9 FTL) | Restricted civilian + military procurement | CCT-9 civilian licensed markets; military procurement contracts |
| IS-Band (Tier 10 gate-capable) | Military procurement; very limited civilian | Military factions, government, Apex commercial entities |
| GT-Band | Government and civilization-level only | CCT-11+ civilization government; Apex-class factions only |
| CHV-A/B | Specialty market; cultivation civilization only | Cultivation-integrated factions; major cultivation sects with resources |
| CHV-C/D/E | Extreme scarcity; no open market | Specific specialized orders; civilization-level programs only |
