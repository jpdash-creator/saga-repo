## Area 9 — Authorization Protocol

### 9.1 — The Two-Tier Authorization Structure

Creative acts in the Starward Saga fall into two authorization categories:

**Tier 1 — Free Creative Acts:** All creative acts that involve inventing instances within governed categories. These require no special authorization — they require only simultaneous compliance with all governing Parts. The vast majority of all creative choices fall in this category: naming, behavior, emotion, specific tactical choices, cultural invention, dialogue content, and prose expansion.

**Tier 2 — Part 28 Authorization Required:** Creative acts that involve elements of such magnitude or Canon-disruption potential that they require explicit narrative preparation, foreshadowing, and structural grounding before execution. See Area 9.2.

### 9.2 — Mandatory Part 28 Authorization Items

The following creative acts require Part 28 authorization — meaning they must be narratively prepared before they appear:

**From Part 3:**
- Narrative participation of a character at the Divine Sea Realm or above
- Deployment of a Primordial-realm entity (Part 3 explicitly designates this as requiring Part 28 review)

**From Part 7:**
- Narrative deployment of a Grand Convergence fusion practitioner
- Narrative deployment of an Origin Convergence practitioner
- Level 4 Cultivation-Technology Integration (all four paths simultaneously)
- Transcendent-ceiling fusion of any type

**From Part 10:**
- Narrative deployment of DC-2 digital consciousness systems
- Narrative deployment of S3+ AI sentience at exceptional ontological boundary cases

**From Part 12:**
- Narrative deployment of any Tier 5 Primordial Grade relic, artifact, or formation array
- Any formation array with effect radius at light-year+ scale (Part 12 Area 6.1 designates this as Part 28 territory)

**From Part 21:**
- Direct narrative participation of CCT-15+ Apex civilizations in the main story (as opposed to historical reference)

**From Part 33:**
- Narrative deployment of WS-VII Existential Class weapons
- Narrative deployment of any Absolute Prohibition weapon category

**From Part 32:**
- CT-Band Tier 15–20 vehicle narrative deployment in active story events (not merely as backdrop)

**The Authorization Standard:** Part 28 authorization means the creative act has been: (a) foreshadowed across at least two prior scenes; (b) structurally grounded in established world-state (the power, object, or civilization is known to exist before it acts); (c) consequentially integrated — its appearance produces CSS and IE events appropriate to its magnitude; and (d) consistent with all governing Parts for the element in question.

### 9.3 — The Authorization Failure Consequence

A Tier 2 creative act executed without fulfilling the Authorization Standard is a Canon violation of the highest severity — because these acts involve elements of world-altering magnitude that cannot be retroactively supported once executed. Their appearance without preparation produces a structural inconsistency that undermines reader trust and narrative coherence. The author must prevent this by always completing the Authorization Standard before executing any Tier 2 act.
