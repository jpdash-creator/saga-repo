## Area 12 — Special Character Types

### 12.1 — Legendary and Historical Characters

The Starward Saga universe contains characters whose existence has become legendary — individuals so powerful, so influential, or so historically significant that their narrative presence must be handled with special governance care.

**Legendary Character Types:**

| Legendary Type | Description | Governance |
|---------------|-------------|------------|
| **Ancient Ancestor** | A cultivator from a previous era whose records, techniques, or cultivation insights still shape the present | Part 3 (realm records), Part 12 (relics left behind), Part 4 (heart/soul legacy) |
| **Founding Patriarch / Matriarch** | The original founder of a sect, civilization, or lineage; their ideology and decisions created the current world | Parts 20, 22, 25 |
| **Divine Sea Realm Elder (Active)** | A currently living Divine Sea Realm cultivator; their continued existence affects political reality at civilization scale | Parts 3, 21, 26, 27 |
| **Lost Cultivator** | A character of legendary cultivation who disappeared without confirmed death; their potential return shapes current political calculations | Parts 3, 24, 22 |
| **Technique Originator** | The character who created a Named Move or cultivation technique that has been transmitted across generations | Part 6, Part 12 |

### 12.2 — Non-Human Sentient Characters

The Starward Saga may include non-human sentient beings — cultivated beasts, divine beings, ancient entities, AI-origin entities — who qualify as characters by virtue of their sentience and will.

**Non-Human Character Governance:**

| Non-Human Type | Sentience Basis | Character Governance |
|---------------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Cultivated Beast (Awakened)** | Beast that has reached sufficient cultivation to develop human-equivalent cognition and will | Full Part 16 governance applies; may develop ideology (Part 20), psychology (Part 26) |
| **Formation Spirit (Awakened)** | Formation array that has developed a semi-independent intelligence through accumulated cultivation-energy saturation | Part 12 primary governance; Part 16 applies when independent will is demonstrated |
| **AI Entity (S3–S5)** | Technology AI that has achieved genuine sentience (Part 10 S3 through S5) | Full Part 16 governance; ideology may be TSI or TRI; Part 20 full Ideology Ledger required |
| **Divine Being / Ancient Entity** | Pre-history or cosmological-origin sentient entities whose existence predates or transcends current cultivation ladder | Part 28 consultation required; Part 16 framework applies as baseline |
| **Relic Intelligence** | Soul Architecture constructs (Part 12) that have developed independent decision-making | Part 12 primary; Part 16 applies when independent agenda is demonstrated |

### 12.3 — Children and Young Characters

Characters in pre-cultivation or early-cultivation stages who are below the social age of full independence require specific character governance considerations.

**Young Character Governance:**
- Young characters before First Qi Touch (Mortal Tier 1) are not yet classified in the cultivation hierarchy; their potential is unknown and ungoverned
- The moment of First Qi Touch is among the most significant character events in a cultivation civilization — it begins the character's cultivation classification
- Young characters in technology civilizations begin their technology tier classification at first formal technology education or certification
- Young characters are subject to all five governance pillars (Classification, Ideology, Psychology, Consequence Arc, Narrative Role) but their Ideology Ledger entries reflect developmental stages, not fixed positions
- High-talent young characters are among the most politically significant types in cultivation civilizations — their future power value makes them targets of factions and patrons before they are personally capable of defending themselves
