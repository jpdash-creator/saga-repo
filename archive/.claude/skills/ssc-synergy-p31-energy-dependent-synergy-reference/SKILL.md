## Area 9 — Energy-Dependent Synergy Reference

### 9.1 — CPM Grade and Synergy Capability (Complete Reference)

This Area provides the complete reference for how CPM grade (Part 11) gates cultivation-side synergy depth. Cross-reference with Part 8, Area 6.4 for technology-side CI Technology level requirements.

| CPM State | Grade Range | CI Tech Min | Conversion Efficiency | Synergy Output Character | Affinity Preservation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Power Crystal | Grades 1–5 | Level 3 | 20–40% | Generic qi-power; high loss; supplementary fuel only | No — affinity character lost at minimum CI level |
| Gem | Grades 6–10 | Level 4 | 40–55% | Moderate qi-power; suitable for low-intensity CI systems | Partial — affinity preserved at Level 5 (one above minimum) |
| Shard | Grades 11–14 | Level 5 | 55–70% | High qi-power density; primary fuel for specialist CI systems | Yes — at Level 6 (one above minimum); full affinity-typed outputs achievable |
| Core | Grades 15–19 | Level 8 | 70–85% | Very high density; Core energy damages Level 5–7 systems; primary fuel for advanced symbiotic systems | Yes — at Level 9; Class 3 relic integration enabled |
| Divine Liquid | Grade 20 | Level 11 | 85–95% | Near-theoretical maximum; interfaces with spacetime structure; Level 10 and below containment fails | Yes — at Level 12; energy substrate merging begins at Level 16+ |

**Rule 9.1.1 — Grade Below Minimum Is Unusable in That CI System:** CPM below the minimum grade for a CI Technology system produces no useful output — the energy density is insufficient to drive the system. A Level 8 CI system fed with Gem CPM does not produce degraded Core-conversion output; it produces near-zero output. The minimum grade is a hard threshold, not a scaling input.

**Rule 9.1.2 — Grade Above System Ceiling Is Dangerous:** CPM of a grade above what the CI Technology level can safely handle causes conversion failure with energy release proportional to the CPM grade. Attempting Divine Liquid CPM in a Level 7 system is a catastrophic containment failure — the energy release is violent and immediate.

**Rule 9.1.3 — Affinity Preservation Upgrade:** Affinity preservation requires CI Technology one level above the minimum for the CPM grade. Authors depicting affinity-typed technology outputs must verify that the CI Technology level supports affinity preservation; at the minimum level, all outputs are generic qi-power with the affinity character stripped.

### 9.2 — Technology Energy Level and Synergy Access

Technology Energy domain level (Part 8 Domain 7) gates the technology-side energy contribution to synergy. Higher Energy Technology levels enable larger-scale and higher-quality cultivation environment effects.

| Energy Technology Level | Cultivation-Side Synergy Support |
|---|---|
| Level 1–4 | No cultivation energy interface; energy systems cannot interact with or produce qi-compatible outputs |
| Level 5 | CPM-powered systems producing qi-compatible outputs can power cultivation infrastructure; cultivation chambers and formation arrays deployable at planet scale |
| Level 6–7 | Multi-world cultivation infrastructure; inner-to-outer-system cultivation zone support; energy systems sustaining formation array networks across stellar distances |
| Level 8–10 | Stellar-scale energy systems interacting with ambient cultivation energy field; technology-generated Sacred Ground-equivalent zones at planetary and stellar scale; 40–60% ambient qi enrichment of home worlds |
| Level 11–15 | Galactic energy systems producing galactic-scale ambient qi enrichment; distinction between technology-generated energy and natural cultivation energy field begins to dissolve; all galactic zones enrichable |
| Level 16+ | Technology energy and cultivation energy are the same substrate; galactic enrichment is automatic; civilizations at this tier operate fundamentally different synergy physics |

### 9.3 — Affinity-Typed Technology Outputs (Complete Reference)

When CI Technology affinity preservation is achieved (one level above CPM grade minimum), technology systems produce outputs with the affinity character of the CPM fuel. This Area governs what affinity-typed technology outputs mean and how they interact with cultivation affinity systems (Part 4).

**Affinity-Typed Output Definition:**
An affinity-typed technology output is a technology output that carries the affinity character of the CPM used to produce it. This means the output interacts with affinity-sensitive phenomena (cultivators, formation arrays, relic systems, Sacred Ground energy fields) as if it were a cultivation technique of that affinity type — while maintaining the physical scale and consistency of technology output.

**Practical Effects of Affinity-Typed Outputs:**

- **Affinity Resonance with Cultivators:** Cultivators with matching affinity can interact more efficiently with affinity-typed technology environments and systems — training in affinity-matched CI Technology zones produces higher cultivation speed gains than affinity-neutral zones
- **Affinity Advantage on Opposing-Affinity Targets:** Affinity-typed technology weapons produce the Affinity Advantage effect (Part 4, Area 1.5) against targets with opposing affinity — the technology output interacts with the target's cultivation architecture in ways generic technology force does not
- **Formation Array Interaction:** Affinity-typed technology outputs can power and activate formation arrays with matching affinity requirements — formation arrays that require specific affinity CPM can be powered by affinity-typed technology outputs from a distance
- **Sacred Ground Resonance:** Affinity-typed outputs matching a Sacred Ground's natural affinity character interact with the Sacred Ground's cultivation energy field, producing the Sacred Ground Synergy Step-Up effect described in Area 2.3
