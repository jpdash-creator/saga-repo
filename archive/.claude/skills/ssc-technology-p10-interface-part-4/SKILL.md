## Area 12 — Interface with Part 4 (Affinity, Heart and Soul Law)

### 12.1 — Joint Governance Areas

Part 4 and Part 10 share governance over the following domains:

**AI Sentience and Cultivation Soul:** Part 10 classifies AI sentience (Area 6). Part 4 defines cultivation soul properties. The canonical determination (Area 6.3): S3+ AI systems have genuine phenomenological consciousness but not cultivation soul properties absent explicit cultivation-side engagement. Neither Part governs this question alone; any narrative deployment of AI with cultivation soul properties requires both Part 10 and Part 4 authorization under Part 28.

**Digital Consciousness and Soul Continuity:** Part 10 governs the science of consciousness transfer (Area 6.3). Part 4 governs whether the cultivation soul transfers with cognitive patterns. The canonical determination: cognitive pattern transfer does not transfer the cultivation soul. The soul of the original practitioner remains with the original physical substrate. DC-1 copies are new entities. DC-2 (soul transfer) requires Part 4 authorization for any narrative deployment.

**CI Technology Level 11–15 Scientific Prerequisites:** The Type 12 domain (CI Technology, Area 4.13) at Level 11–15 requires scientific understanding of soul-level phenomena. Part 10 governs the technology-science side of this understanding; Part 4 governs the cultivation-science side. Joint governance is required for any canonical claim about technology that interfaces with soul phenomena.

**Tier 17+ Science Intersection:** At Tier 17 and above, technology science begins to approach what cultivation at the highest realms has accessed through entirely different means. Part 10 governs the technology-science side of these intersections; Part 4 governs the cultivation side. The canonical principle (P10-11): at these tiers, technology and cultivation are two different vocabularies for the same underlying reality. Specific canonical determinations about what Tier 17+ science discovers about the nature of existence, consciousness, or the multiverse require joint Part 10 and Part 4 governance, authorized under Part 28.
