## Area 20 — Information Rights and Personal Cultivation Privacy

### 20.1 — The Privacy of Cultivation

The cultivation realm and technique of an individual practitioner occupies a unique zone in information governance: it is simultaneously a strategic asset of the faction they belong to, a personal achievement of the individual, and a vulnerability that their adversaries want to exploit.

**Privacy Rules:**
- **P24-Priv-1 — Realm Revelation is Voluntary (Below Divine Realm):** A cultivator below Divine Realm has no legal or governance obligation to reveal their true cultivation level to parties who do not have a governing authority relationship with them (teachers, sect heads). Actively misrepresenting it for fraud is governed by Part 25 (social contract).
- **P24-Priv-2 — Soul Scanning Without Consent is a Violation:** Active soul scanning of an unwilling target (Area 8.3) is treated as a grave violation across most moral spectrum doctrines. Even SD-2 (Strategic Secrecy) practitioners who would soul-scan enemy captives recognize that scanning a civilian or sect member who has not been declared a hostile target is a violation. The severity of the violation scales with scan depth.
- **P24-Priv-3 — The Passive Surveillance Exception:** Passive soul sense surveillance (Area 8.2) is not treated as a violation because it is a natural feature of cultivation existence — a high-realm cultivator cannot prevent their soul sense from operating any more than they can prevent themselves from hearing. What they do with that passive information is governed by their moral doctrine.

### 20.2 — Cultivation Lineage Privacy

A cultivator's technique lineage — the chain of masters who taught them — is personal information with both personal and factional dimensions. Revealing an opponent's technique lineage in combat is a significant intelligence act. Concealing lineage is standard practice for deep-cover operatives.
