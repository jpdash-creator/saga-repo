## Rule Text (Verbatim Source — Authoritative)
## Area 1 — Moves and Cultivation

### 1.1 — Cultivation Realm and Moves

A cultivator's realm position on the ladder (Part 3, Area 1) is the primary structural constraint on what moves are possible. This operates on three levels: access (which move types are available), output (how powerful a move's effect is), and expression (how the move physically manifests).

**Mortal Tier 1 — Physical Architecture Moves:**
Mortal Tier 1 cultivators have no qi circulation and thus no energy-based moves. All Named Moves at this tier are physical-architecture techniques — precise applications of refined body mechanics. These moves are not weak. A Soul-stage Mortal Tier 1 cultivator's **Iron Bone Shattering Stamp** is 128 times stronger than an Unawakened human's equivalent action. What limits these moves is their ceiling — without energy, they cannot produce environmental effects, ranged attacks, or persistent phenomena. Move types accessible at Mortal Tier 1: Basic and Intermediate (Advanced rarely, through exceptional physical mastery). Fusion, Catastrophic, and Mythic are categorically inaccessible — they require energy substrates that do not exist at this tier.

*The move expression of a Mortal Tier 1 Named Move is always physical: strikes, grips, sweeps, throws, kicks, footwork patterns, weapon handling techniques, and refined body-movement sequences. Every Named Move at this tier is ultimately a pattern of biomechanical excellence.*

**Mortal Tier 2 — Qi-Physical Hybrid Moves:**
Mortal Tier 2 introduces qi circulation. Moves at this tier combine physical structure with energy expression, producing techniques that can project force beyond physical contact, create brief environmental disturbances, and begin interacting with the world beyond the cultivator's body. This is the tier where affinity first becomes relevant (typically from Senior onwards, though genuine affinity expression is most pronounced at Warrior and above).

Move type ceiling at Mortal Tier 2: Basic through Elite as standard; Signature achievable for exceptional cultivators; Fusion rare and unstable (requires extremely refined dual-stream control that is almost anomalous at this tier); Catastrophic possible only at peak Monarch level with extraordinary talent and specific conditions; Mythic categorically inaccessible.

*Named Moves at Mortal Tier 2 begin to have environmental signatures — light effects, pressure waves, qi tremors, brief elemental discharges. These environmental signatures become more pronounced as the cultivator advances through the tier.*

**Xiantian Realm — Energy Expression Moves:**
The Xiantian Realm (Demigod through Absolute God) represents the first tier where pure energy expression makes moves capable of affecting the environment at meaningful range without physical contact. Named Moves at this tier can reshape terrain, interact with atmospheric forces, and produce effects measurable at distances the cultivator does not physically occupy. Soul moves become fully accessible at this tier (requiring soul cultivation — Part 4, Area 2).

Move type ceiling at Xiantian: All types through Catastrophic as standard; Mythic accessible only at the extreme peak of Absolute God under extraordinary conditions — it is the rarest achievement at this tier.

*Named Moves in the Xiantian Realm have strong environmental expression that aligns with the Heavenly Scale Grade signatures (Part 3, Area 3.3). A Void God's Attack moves generate the "wall of pressure" effect described at Grade 7. A True God's moves interact with atmospheric forces consistent with Grade 8.*

**Core Realm — Core-Powered Moves:**
The Core Realm (Core Formation through Core Solidification) fundamentally transforms technique execution. Because the Core functions as an internal reactor rather than a finite tank of qi, Core Realm moves can be sustained, repeated, and executed at output levels impossible for cultivators still drawing on circulating qi. The Core's rhythmic output becomes audible and physical to those in range — a Core Realm cultivator's techniques carry the distinctive pulse described in Part 3, Grades 10–12.

Move type ceiling at Core: All eight types are accessible. Catastrophic moves become routine combat tools (not just emergency deployments) at Core Solidification. Mythic moves become achievable at Core Solidification peak, though they remain genuinely difficult even there.

*Named Moves in the Core Realm carry the Core's distinctive resonance — the deep internal pulse that witnesses feel in their bones. High-tier Core Realm techniques produce geological-scale effects at their peak.*

**Divine Realm — Reality-Interaction Moves:**
Divine Realm moves do not merely apply force — they interact with the fundamental laws of reality. Named Moves at the Divine Realm level can manipulate space, time, causality, and the fabric of existence. At Divine Transformation, techniques begin bending space-time locally. At Sea Formation, moves interact with dimensional layers. At Heaven Treading, spatial techniques become second nature. At Divine Ascension and Divine Sea Realm, moves operate at scales that register as weather events, geological shifts, and cosmological disturbances.

All eight move types are accessible and fully deployable at the Divine Realm. Mythic moves — reality-bending by definition — are most naturally produced here, though even Divine Realm cultivators face the 10% base success rate challenge for Mythic-type executions.

*Named Moves in the Divine Realm have consequences that persist beyond the immediate combat. The world is changed by their use. A Part 27 consequence event is automatically triggered by any Divine Realm Named Move at Advanced or above that operates in a populated or historically significant area.*

**Move Output Scaling Rule:**
A Named Move's output scales directly with the cultivator's Heavenly Scale Grade (Part 3, Area 3.3). The same named technique — say, **Heaven Piercing Thrust** — executed by a Grade 3 cultivator versus a Grade 10 cultivator produces categorically different results. The move's TYPE remains constant (it is the same technique). Its FUNCTION remains constant (it is still a Penetrating Attack). Its OUTPUT is entirely different. This is a critical canon principle: technique inheritance does not confer the power of the original creator.

### 1.2 — Cultivation Affinity and Moves

Affinity (governed in full by Part 4, Area 1) is the resonance relationship between a cultivator's qi and a specific force of nature or metaphysical principle. It shapes moves in two distinct ways: it enables affinity-typed moves, and it amplifies the execution of any technique aligned with the cultivator's affinity.

**Affinity-Typed Moves:**
A cultivator with Fire Affinity can develop Named Moves that embody fire's principles — combustion, expansion, consuming light, transformative heat. These are not simply "fire-effect" moves; they are moves that draw on the cultivator's genuine resonance with fire as a universal force. An affinity-typed move at high affinity level does things that energy without affinity cannot replicate — a Fire Affinity move at Harmony level (Level 6+) does not merely produce fire; it produces fire whose behavior is governed by the cultivator's will at a level of precision that non-affinity fire techniques cannot match.

**Affinity Level and Move Quality:**
The nine affinity levels (Null through Origin Convergence — Part 4, Area 1.2) determine how powerfully affinity expression shapes a move:
- **Null / Whisper / Echo (Levels 0–3):** Affinity does not meaningfully distinguish the move from standard techniques. The cultivator can develop affinity-typed moves but they show only surface-level alignment — primarily visual and atmospheric.
- **Resonance / Attunement (Levels 4–5):** Affinity begins to alter the move's mechanical behavior. A Water Affinity move flows around obstacles differently from a standard move. Techniques gain a secondary resonance effect that interacts with affinity-compatible environments.
- **Harmony (Level 6):** The Affinity Advantage modifier (Part 3, Area 7.2; Part 4, Area 1.5) activates. Affinity-aligned technique output is amplified up to one Cultivation Realm equivalent for technique execution in aligned contexts. Affinity-typed moves at this level are distinctly superior to non-affinity equivalents in the same domain.
- **Communion / Sovereignty (Levels 7–8):** Affinity-typed moves begin to interact with the affinity's environmental expression — a Fire Affinity at Communion level turns environmental fire into an extension of the technique rather than background noise. Sovereignty-level moves in the affinity domain are effectively incomparable to standard techniques; they operate by the rules of the affinity type, not merely by cultivation qi.
- **Origin Convergence (Level 9):** Techniques at this level transcend the affinity as a category and embody its principle directly. A Space Affinity Origin Convergence cultivator does not use space techniques — they enact spatial law. Named Moves at this level are unique in the universe; no one else's technique can replicate them.

**Multi-Affinity Moves:**
A cultivator with multiple affinities (Affinity Depth of 2 or more — Part 4, Area 1.4) can develop moves that draw on more than one affinity simultaneously. Multi-affinity moves are categorically classified as Fusion-type moves in the Move Type system, because merging two affinity streams in a single technique is an act of fusion. All Fusion move rules (Area 10) apply. Multi-affinity moves are among the most complex and unpredictable techniques in existence.

**Non-Primary Affinity Moves:**
A cultivator can use techniques that belong to an affinity they do not possess or possess weakly. However, such techniques operate without the amplification, resonance precision, and environmental interaction that affinity provides. A cultivator without Water Affinity using a water-based technique is producing water-effect qi expression, not genuine affinity interaction. The move is valid but significantly weaker in the affinity domain than the equivalent technique used by a genuine Water Affinity cultivator.

### 1.3 — Cultivation Power Density and Moves

Power Density (Part 3, Area 3) is the concentration of cultivated force per unit of the cultivator's will deployment. It affects moves in ways distinct from realm position: two cultivators at the same realm can have different power densities and thus produce meaningfully different outputs from the same Named Move.

**Density and Move Output:**
Within the same realm, a cultivator at Level 9 Peak has 1.5× the power density of a cultivator at Level 8 Peak (the sub-level scaling law from Part 3, Area 3.2). This means the same Named Move executed by these two cultivators produces visibly different outputs. **Thunderstep Advance** by a Level 9 Peak Void God hits harder, reaches farther, and creates a more pronounced environmental response than the same technique executed by a Level 5 Mid Void God.

**Density and Move Success Rate:**
Power density directly modifies the success rate of move types above Basic:
- A cultivator with **high power density relative to the move's requirements** (strong energy foundation) gains a success rate bonus of up to +5% on Intermediate through Catastrophic moves
- A cultivator with **low power density relative to the move's requirements** (stretching beyond natural energy capacity) suffers a success rate penalty of up to −10% on the same types
- Mythic moves are not modified by power density in the same way — their uncertainty is metaphysical, not energetic

**Density and Move Sustainability:**
A cultivator with exceptionally high power density for their realm can execute techniques that lower-density cultivators cannot sustain. Channeled techniques (Area 5) that require sustained energy output are directly limited by the cultivator's energy reserve, which scales with density. A Core Assimilation cultivator at Level 9 Peak can sustain channeled techniques far longer than a Core Assimilation cultivator at Level 1 Low — the same move, but incomparably different utility in extended combat.

**Density-Based Move Variants:**
Some Named Moves have density-dependent variations — the same technique that produces a Minor variant at low density produces a Major or Grand variant at high density. These are not separate moves — they are the same move, recognized by the same name, but their scale of expression varies with the cultivator's density. A move's **maximum variant tier** is established when the move is created or registered.

### 1.4 — Cultivation Root and Moves

The Meridian Root (Part 3, Area 5) governs the efficiency of energy absorption, circulation, and storage. While it does not define what moves a cultivator can learn, it profoundly affects how well they execute them and how many they can deploy in sustained combat.

**Root and Execution Efficiency:**
A higher-quality Meridian Root translates to less energy wasted per technique execution. A Golden Root cultivator executing **Crimson Spiral Burst** wastes perhaps 15% of the technique's energy to inefficiency. An Iron Root cultivator executing the same technique wastes 40–60%. The technique looks the same in its core expression but the Iron Root cultivator depletes their reserves far faster in sustained combat.

**Root and Move Learning Speed:**
Higher-quality Roots absorb technique patterns faster. A Heavenly Root cultivator observing a technique once and practicing it briefly may achieve Practiced mastery in days. A Raw Root cultivator studying the same technique diligently for years may only achieve Novice mastery within a practical timeframe. The Roots do not change what moves are possible — they determine how quickly and thoroughly those moves can be learned.

**Root and Move Innovation:**
Transcendent Spectrum Roots (Star through Infinite — Part 3, Area 5.3) have unique properties that create corresponding unique move capabilities:
- **Void Root:** Can absorb hostile techniques during combat and incorporate their principles into original moves. A Void Root cultivator who experiences an opponent's **Sky Piercing Lance** may, over time, develop a variant that incorporates its penetrating principle. This absorption is not instantaneous — it is a learning process that occurs between fights.
- **Eternal Root:** The perpetual cultivation means technique fundamentals deepen continuously even without deliberate practice. An Eternal Root cultivator's basic techniques become increasingly precise over time, even if the cultivator never deliberately drills them.
- **Celestial Root:** Techniques aligned with cosmic timing (celestial events, tidal forces, planetary positions) express amplified output during cosmically significant moments. A Celestial Root cultivator's moves are not constant — they are at their weakest in ordinary moments and incomprehensibly powerful at cosmically significant junctures.
- **Infinite Root:** No hard ceiling on technique depth. Every move an Infinite Root cultivator masters continues to deepen and evolve indefinitely — the technique ceiling that others hit does not apply.

**Root and Move Mastery Caps:**
Raw through Bronze Root cultivators face mastery caps that are slightly below the theoretical maximum for Fusion, Catastrophic, and Mythic moves. This reflects the reality that the energy architecture required for these move types is difficult to sustain through lower-quality meridian channels. Specifically: Raw Root maximum mastery for Fusion is 30% (vs. 50% for others); Iron Root is 35%; Bronze Root is 40%. Silver Root and above achieve the standard maximum mastery rates defined in Area 2.

**Eternal Root and Soul Move Ceiling (Rule 1.4.5):**
The Eternal Root's perpetual cultivation property interacts uniquely with soul moves. Because the Eternal Root continuously deepens technique fundamentals without deliberate drilling, soul moves developed by an Eternal Root cultivator benefit from continuous passive deepening — even during physical cultivation sessions. The Eternal Root soul move ceiling is the same as any other root (constrained by soul rung per Area 1.6), but the **time cost to advance mastery grades is significantly reduced**: an Eternal Root cultivator advances soul move mastery at the same continuous-deepening rate as physical moves. Additionally, the Eternal Root's perpetual cultivation does not create soul force on its own — soul cultivation must still be awakened deliberately (Part 4, Area 2.5). The Eternal Root accelerates mastery speed for soul moves already being cultivated; it does not auto-generate soul techniques or soul rung advancement.

### 1.5 — Cultivation Heart and Moves

The Cultivation Heart (Part 4, Area 3) governs how much of a cultivator's theoretical maximum capability is accessible when pushed to ultimate limits. It interacts with moves not through power modification but through **performance consistency under pressure** and **crisis access**.

**Heart and High-Pressure Move Execution:**
When a cultivator is under extreme combat pressure — physiologically damaged, emotionally stressed, facing death — their effective cultivation access drops. A cultivator without an awakened Heart may be operating at 40–60% of their true capacity under these conditions. Their Named Moves execute with correspondingly reduced output and elevated failure rates. The Heart prevents this drop from occurring.

The access percentages by Heart level interact directly with move success rates under ultimate pressure:
- **No Heart / Ember Heart:** Under maximum pressure, success rates for Advanced and above moves drop by 10–20% from base. The cultivator's execution is compromised by their internal state.
- **Iron Heart:** Under maximum pressure, Advanced move success rates hold; Elite and Signature see 5% reduction. Basic and Intermediate maintain full rates.
- **Blazing Heart:** Under maximum pressure, all move types up to Elite maintain base success rates. Signature sees 5% reduction; Fusion/Catastrophic see 10% reduction.
- **Unbreakable Heart:** All move types up to Signature maintain base success rates under maximum pressure. Fusion and Catastrophic see only 5% reduction. Additionally, aura suppression from opponents reduces effectiveness by 1 Grade less than it otherwise would (Part 4, Area 3.5) — meaning technique output is less degraded by overwhelming opponent presence.
- **Sovereign Heart:** All move types maintain their mastery-level success rates even under maximum pressure. The cultivator accesses their true ceiling regardless of circumstances. Aura suppression reduces effectiveness by 2 Grades less. A Sovereign Heart cultivator executing a Mythic move when facing death performs exactly as well as they would in optimal conditions.

**Heart and Signature Moves:**
The Cultivation Heart has the most profound specific interaction with Signature moves. Signature moves are identity-bound techniques that draw on the cultivator's deepest personal truth. A cultivator without any Heart awakening may technically have a Signature move, but its expression is shallow — it relies on cultivated proficiency rather than genuine personal resonance. A cultivator with an awakened Heart, particularly at Blazing level or above, finds their Signature moves expressing something beyond technique — a lived truth that manifests as power. This is not a mechanical modifier; it is a narrative quality that the prose must render at the appropriate depth.

**Heart and Move Evolution:**
The Heart state directly influences the likelihood of a technique evolving to the next type. A move evolution event (Section 9.3) is significantly more likely during states of genuine Heart engagement — moments of profound crisis, genuine emotional reckoning, or the exact kind of lived experience that causes Heart advancement. This creates a narrative structure where a character's inner journey and technique development are intertwined, not parallel.

**Heart State × Mastery Grade Interaction (Rule 1.5.6):**
Heart-based success rate reductions under pressure apply on top of mastery grade bonuses — they are not cancelled by mastery. A cultivator with Expert mastery (+20% success rate bonus) still loses that bonus to Heart-based pressure penalties. The net result:
- **No Heart / Ember Heart** under maximum pressure: Advanced moves drop 10–20% from base *after* mastery bonus is added. Example: Expert mastery Advanced move = Base 60% + 20% bonus = 80%. Under Ember Heart maximum pressure, 80% − 15% = 65% effective success rate.
- **Iron Heart** under maximum pressure: Advanced moves hold; Elite/Signature see 5% reduction applied after mastery bonus. Mastery bonuses still meaningfully matter.
- **Blazing Heart and above**: Mastery bonuses apply cleanly in all conditions up to the Heart's pressure ceiling — Heart state removes the need to "fight through" the pressure drop, allowing mastery bonuses to realize their full value.
- **Sovereign Heart**: Mastery bonuses apply at their full value in every condition. A Sovereign Heart cultivator's Expert mastery Advanced move always executes at 80% or above regardless of combat conditions — this is the complete "what you train is what you get" standard that Sovereign Heart achieves.

**Practical implication:** Mastery improvement is most valuable to cultivators with higher Heart stages, because a lower Heart stage sees a portion of that mastery investment negated under real combat pressure. A cultivator with No Heart / Ember Heart benefits from mastery training but will consistently underperform their theoretical ceiling when it matters most. A Sovereign Heart cultivator realizes the full return on every hour of practice.

### 1.6 — Cultivation Soul and Moves

Soul cultivation (Part 4, Area 2) creates an entirely distinct layer of Named Moves that operate on the metaphysical dimension rather than (or in addition to) the physical dimension. Soul moves do not replace physical moves — they add a second combat axis that interacts with the physical in complex ways.

**The Nature of Soul Moves:**
Soul moves are Named Moves that deploy soul force — the metaphysical energy developed through soul cultivation — rather than or alongside qi. Their targets may be physical (the soul force damages the physical body through the metaphysical layer), metaphysical (the soul force interacts with the target's soul cultivation directly), or environmental (the soul force affects the landscape of the metaphysical layer that all cultivators inhabit to varying degrees).

Soul moves follow all the type classification rules (Area 2) and combat function rules (Area 3) of this Part. A soul move can be Basic or Mythic in type. It can be an Attack or Control in function. The distinction is the energy substrate (soul force vs qi) and the targeting layer (metaphysical vs physical).

**Soul Move Access Requirements:**
Soul moves require active soul cultivation. A cultivator who has not yet awakened their soul cultivation (Part 4, Area 2.5) has no soul moves regardless of their physical cultivation level. Early-awakening cultivators (possible from Mortal Tier 2 Monarch peak) may develop Basic and Intermediate soul moves before reaching the Xiantian Realm. Late-awakening cultivators develop their soul move repertoire more quickly after awakening because they are compensating for lost time.

**Soul Move Types in Relation to the Eleven Soul Technique Types:**
The eleven soul technique types defined in Part 4, Area 2.3 (Weapon, Defense, Trap, Convert, Phantom, Devour, Healing, Bind, Resonate, Nullify, Sense) map to the combat function classification of Area 3 as follows:
- **Weapon Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Attack sub-function (Soul Strike)
- **Defense Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Defense sub-function (Soul Shield)
- **Trap Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Control sub-function (Soul Trap)
- **Convert Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Control sub-function (Soul Convert) — the most dangerous soul move category
- **Phantom Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Support sub-function (Soul Illusion)
- **Devour Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Attack sub-function (Soul Devour) — damages by absorbing
- **Healing Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Support sub-function (Soul Restoration)
- **Bind Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Control sub-function (Soul Bind)
- **Resonate Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Combination sub-function (Soul Resonance)
- **Nullify Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Defense sub-function (Soul Nullification)
- **Sense Soul techniques** = Soul-Specific Support sub-function (Soul Perception)

**Soul Moves and Physical Defense:**
The defining property of soul moves is that they bypass the cultivator's physical defensive layer. A Core Solidification cultivator's body may be effectively indestructible by physical attacks — but a sufficiently powerful soul attack can still damage their soul structure, which then produces real consequences for physical cultivation. Conversely, soul defenses do not protect against physical attacks. Soul and physical combat operate simultaneously but on different targeting layers.

**Simultaneous Physical and Soul Moves:**
A cultivator with both physical cultivation and soul cultivation can execute a Named Move on both layers simultaneously — one physical technique and one soul technique in the same combat action. This is the most advanced form of cultivation combat and is almost universally described as terrifying to face. The naming convention for simultaneous dual-layer techniques typically reflects both dimensions: **Crimson Flame Strike — Soul Invasion** indicates a physical fire attack accompanied by a soul penetration attempt.

**Soul Rung and Move Ceiling:**
The cultivator's soul rung (Part 4, Area 2.2 — Soul Wisp through Eternal Soul) constrains the maximum complexity of soul moves accessible, in the same way cultivation realm constrains physical move access. The soul rung ceiling by move type:
- **Soul Wisp / Soul Shade (Rungs 1–2):** Basic and Intermediate soul moves only
- **Soul Form / Soul Echo (Rungs 3–4):** Up to Advanced; Elite possible
- **Soul Anchor / Soul Domain (Rungs 5–6):** Up to Elite; Signature soul moves achievable
- **Soul Fortress / Soul Ignition (Rungs 7–8):** All types through Catastrophic
- **Soul Sea / Heaven Soul (Rungs 9–10):** All types; Mythic soul moves accessible
- **Ascending Soul / Eternal Soul (Rungs 11–12):** Full access; Mythic soul moves achievable with high mastery

**Soul Move Success Rate Modifiers:**
Soul moves use the same base success rates and mastery grades as physical moves (Area 2.1). However, soul moves have additional success rate modifiers specific to the soul layer that apply on top of the standard modifiers (Area 2.1 combat condition modifiers):

| Soul Move Modifier | Effect on Success Rate |
|---|---|
| Target has no active soul cultivation | +15% — undefended souls are easier to affect |
| Target's soul rung ≥ attacker's soul rung | −15% to −30% depending on differential — soul defense naturally resists comparable or superior attacks |
| Target has active Defense Soul or Nullify Soul technique | −10% to −25% — active soul defense significantly reduces soul move effectiveness |
| Attacker's soul is damaged (fragmentation, exhaustion) | −10% to −20% — soul damage degrades move execution precision |
| Attacker is in a high soul energy environment (Sacred Ground S-Grade 3+) | +5% to +10% — soul energy density improves soul move execution |
| Target is distracted or emotionally destabilized | +5% to +15% — emotional turbulence weakens soul defense coherence |
| Attacker attempts soul move while physically attacking simultaneously | −5% per additional simultaneous physical technique — split attention reduces soul move precision |

These modifiers stack with the standard combat condition modifiers from Area 2.1 (injury, energy depletion, opponent disruption). Soul moves with a combined modifier below −30% from all stacked sources are at high failure risk even at Expert or Master mastery.
