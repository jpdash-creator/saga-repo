## Area 16 — Domain Ownership and Disambiguation

### 16.1 — This Part vs. Part 11

**Part 11 governs:** The raw CPM substance — what it is, how it forms, its states and grades, its usage rules, its hazard profiles, its currency function.

**Part 12 governs:** The constructed objects that use CPM as raw material — relics, artifacts, formation arrays. When the CPM is embedded in a relic, it ceases to be governed by Part 11 as an independent substance and becomes governed by Part 12 as a component of the constructed object. The constructed object's power ceiling, usage safety, assessment, and narration are Part 12's domain.

**Joint governance area:** The moment of CPM integration into a relic during construction — which grade of CPM is appropriate for what tier, and what happens when too-high-grade CPM is forced into an insufficiently prepared base material during construction. This is jointly governed: Part 11 defines the substance tier; Part 12 defines the construction material ceiling.

---

### 16.2 — This Part vs. Part 6

**Part 6 governs:** Named Moves that produce formation-like effects (Formation-function moves, Area 3.6.2). The key distinction: a Part 6 Formation-function move is a cultivator-executed technique that creates a temporary zone effect requiring the cultivator's ongoing energy. When the cultivator stops channeling or is disrupted, the technique ends.

**Part 12 governs:** Permanent or semi-permanent formation arrays installed as physical infrastructure. These continue operating without the cultivator's ongoing technique execution.

**The interaction:** Formation-function Named Moves can be used to activate, amplify, or disrupt formation arrays (Part 12 objects). A **Formation Activation Technique** is a Named Move (Part 6) that triggers a Part 12 formation array's full output. A **Formation Disruption Strike** (Part 6, Control/Disrupt function) may specifically target a formation array's energy flow. The Named Move governs the technique; the formation array governs the object.

---

### 16.3 — This Part vs. Part 13

**Part 13 governs (fully authored, Areas 1–23):** The spatial properties of Formation Zones (Part 13 Area 14 — Passive Zones, Vortex Zones, Sacred Ground Zones) — where they are geographically, what their spatial characteristics are, what their boundary enforcement rules are, how overlap and zone priority work, and what collapse conditions terminate them.

**Part 12 governs:** What objects — artifacts and formation arrays — are constructed in Formation Zones, using the high-energy environment for their production.

**The interaction:** Part 13 defines the location; Part 12 defines what can be made there and the advantage the location provides to construction.

---

### 16.4 — This Part vs. Part 8

**Part 8 governs:** The technology civilization tier prerequisites required to construct Technology-Relic Hybrids — specifically the minimum levels in the Materials Science, Energy Technology, and Cultivation Integration Technology domains (Part 8, Area 6.3). Part 8 also governs the technology-side interface architecture: how CPM energy from relics is routed, channeled, and converted within technology systems (Part 8, Area 7.2).

**Part 12 governs:** The Technology-Relic Hybrid object itself as a constructed physicalized energy structure — its Tier classification, material ceiling (P12-2 applies: relic component grade sets the ceiling), Foundational Principles compliance, construction mechanics (Area 3), functional classification (Area 5, Class J — Technology-Relic Hybrids), soul bonding (Area 11), and assessment/authentication (Area 10).

**The key rule:** Part 8 sets the door (whether the technology civilization can attempt the construction). Part 12 governs what is built once the door is open. A civilization that meets Part 8's tier thresholds but constructs a Hybrid that violates Part 12's Foundational Principles has satisfied Part 8 and violated Part 12 simultaneously — both are independent satisfaction requirements.

**Joint governance area:** The moment of construction itself — determining whether the civilization's current technology tier can produce the specific Hybrid being attempted, while simultaneously satisfying Part 12's construction material ceiling, formation rules, and Tier classification. Neither Part alone governs the complete construction event.
