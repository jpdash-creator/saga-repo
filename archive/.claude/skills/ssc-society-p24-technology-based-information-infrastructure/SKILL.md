## Area 9 — Technology-Based Information Infrastructure

### 9.1 — Communication Networks by CCT Tier

Technology-based information infrastructure is the dominant information channel for civilizations above CCT-4 (Technology-Sovereign type) or above CCT-6 in general. Its governance is the joint responsibility of Part 8 (capability definition), Part 14 (distance and lag), and Part 24 (governance and secrecy rules).

The canonical communication tiers and their information governance implications:

**Communication Tier 1 (Pre-Industrial, CCT-1 to CCT-2):**
Information moves at human walking/riding speed. A message crossing a 500 km territory takes 3–10 days. Decision-making loops are slow; military coordination is limited to visual range. This is the courier and herald paradigm. No encryption exists — all messages are subject to interception by anyone who captures the courier.

**Communication Tier 2 (Early Industrial, CCT-3):**
Electronic communication (telegraph, radio) allows near-instantaneous planetary communication. This changes governance fundamentally: central authorities can know of and respond to events on the far side of a planet within hours. The first encryption systems emerge; signals intelligence becomes a distinct military function.

**Communication Tier 3 (Advanced Technology, CCT-4 to CCT-5):**
Planetary and inner-system communication networks. Quantum encryption makes all messages effectively unreadable to same-tier adversaries. Information saturation begins — so much data is transmitted that analysis becomes a bottleneck. AI-assisted analysis emerges.

**Communication Tier 4 (FTL Communication, CCT-7 to CCT-8):**
The threshold at which interstellar governance becomes possible. FTL communication requires the Part 10 FTL physics framework; it does not come for free with FTL travel. Once achieved, civilizations can maintain coherent governance across multiple star systems in real time. Pre-FTL communication civilizations governing multiple systems operate in a perpetual command lag environment.

**Communication Tier 5 (Galactic, CCT-11+):**
Galactic communication fabric maintains near-zero effective latency across kly-scale distances. This requires a distributed relay infrastructure whose maintenance is itself a strategic military and political asset. Destruction of key relay nodes is a canonical act of civilizational warfare.

### 9.2 — Surveillance Infrastructure

Surveillance capability scales with Part 8 Type 13 (Sensory/Detection Technology):

| Type 13 Level | Surveillance Capability | Information Output |
|--------------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Level 1–3 | Human observers, basic optics | Manual sighting; no real-time synthesis |
| Level 4–5 | Planet-wide sensor grids | Real-time planetary awareness; individual tracking |
| Level 6–8 | System-scale sensor networks | Full star system monitoring; ship tracking at AU range |
| Level 9–11 | Multi-system sensor networks | Interstellar movement tracking; FTL transit detection |
| Level 12+ | Galactic surveillance fabric | Civilization-scale pattern analysis; civilization intention modeling |

**Governance Rule P24-Surv-1:** Surveillance capability creates information but does not process it. At high tiers, the volume of surveillance data exceeds all human analytical capacity. AI analysis (Part 8 Type 4) is required to extract actionable intelligence from high-tier surveillance systems. This creates a dependency: a civilization with Level 12 surveillance but Level 8 AI analysis has more data than it can use.

### 9.3 — Encryption and Counter-Intelligence Technology

**Encryption Architecture:**
- Pre-Level 4: Symmetric key encryption; vulnerable to cryptanalysis by same-tier adversaries
- Level 4–6: Quantum encryption; unbreakable by conventional computation at any tier
- Level 7+: Distributed quantum encryption fabric; breaking requires physically compromising every node simultaneously
- Level 11+: Reality-substrate encryption; information encoded into physical constants; requires Apex physics to approach

**Counter-Intelligence Technology:**
- Signal analysis: Detection of anomalous communication patterns that indicate covert activity even without content decryption
- Behavioral pattern AI: Analysis of movement, resource procurement, and social network patterns to identify infiltrators and operatives
- Cultivation signature scanning: Technology sensors that detect and catalog cultivation signatures, enabling cross-reference with known IEF operative signatures
- Formation detection arrays: Technology systems capable of detecting formation array activation signatures, alerting security systems to clandestine soul transmission
