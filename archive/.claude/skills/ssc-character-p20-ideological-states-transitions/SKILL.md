## Area 8 — Ideological States and Transitions

### 8.1 — Ideological Stability States

Every character's ideology occupies one of five stability states at any narrative moment:

| Stability State | Description | Narrative Indicators |
|----------------|-------------|----------------------|
| **IS-1 — Secure** | The character's ideology is functioning coherently; no active contradictions; Ledger is current | Consistent behavior; clear motivation; ability to state their position when challenged |
| **IS-2 — Pressured** | The character's ideology is under challenge; active IE-2 events occurring | Defensiveness; over-explanation of positions; inconsistent response to challenge |
| **IS-3 — Strained** | One or more Active Contradictions are unresolved; IE-4 or IE-5 potential | Avoidance of specific situations that would force the contradiction to surface |
| **IS-4 — Fractured** | Core Ideology is under active attack; IE-6 potential present | Emotional volatility around specific ideological topics; uncharacteristic behavior |
| **IS-5 — Collapsed** | IE-7 has occurred; the character is in ideological void | Absence of characteristic motivation; inability to make decisions; loss of the usual self-explanatory framework |

### 8.2 — Ideological Void

An **Ideological Void** is the state following IE-7 (Ideology Collapse). The character's organizing framework has been destroyed, and no new framework has yet emerged.

**Characteristics of the Ideological Void:**
- Actions become reactive and immediate — driven by basic survival, basic attachment, basic sensation — rather than principled
- The character experiences the world as radically unstable, because their interpretation framework has collapsed
- Other characters' values appear arbitrary and incomprehensible, because the void character has lost their own framework for evaluating values
- The character may appear to adopt multiple new ideological frameworks temporarily and discard each — they are trying on new structures to see if any fit
- The character is highly susceptible to ideological adoption from authority figures during this period — cults, mentors with strong ideological frameworks, and charismatic leaders are the primary risks

**Rule 20.8.2.1 — Void Duration Is Proportional to Polarity:** A character who collapsed from Polarity-5 ideology faces a longer and more severe void than one who collapsed from Polarity-2. Higher polarity means more of the character's identity was organized around the ideology; more of the self must be reconstructed.

**Rule 20.8.2.2 — Void Characters Are Not Neutral:** The Ideological Void is not ideological neutrality — it is ideological devastation. These characters are not free from ideology; they are ideology-homeless. The difference is between a person who has no strong opinions (Polarity-1 fluid) and a person who has lost every framework that gave their life meaning.

### 8.3 — Ideological Reconstruction

Following IE-7, the character undergoes ideological reconstruction — a narrative process by which they develop a new organizing framework. This process is governed jointly by Part 20 (ideology) and Part 26 (psychological) because reconstruction is both an intellectual and an emotional process.

**Reconstruction Pathways:**

| Pathway | Description | Resulting Ideology Profile |
|---------|-------------|---------------------------|
| **Integration Pathway** | The character integrates the experiences that destroyed their old ideology into a more complex framework that can contain them | Modified version of prior ideology at lower Polarity with new depth |
| **Opposition Pathway** | The character builds their new ideology in direct opposition to what destroyed the old one | Often produces high-Polarity new ideology that is the mirror image of the collapsed framework |
| **Transplant Pathway** | The character adopts a ready-made ideology from a significant figure or institution they encounter in the void | Rapid ideological stabilization at high risk of producing dependence on the ideological source |
| **Minimalist Pathway** | The character reduces to the barest possible ideological commitments — a small number of principles they can verify through direct experience | Low-Polarity, PCI or LBI (personal, minimal) framework; vulnerability to later IE-6 from accumulated complexity |
| **Transcendence Pathway** | The character uses the void as the cultivation Heart encounter it is, and emerges with TSI or Dao-navigation TRI that is genuinely new | Rare; requires specific cultivation conditions; produces the most transformed characters in the series |
