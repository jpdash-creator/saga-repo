## Area 17 — Tier Transition Indicators

### 17.1 — Narrative Signals of Tier Transition

When civilizations undergo tier transitions within a narrative arc, certain narrative signals mark the transition as a world-law event rather than merely a plot beat.

**Rule 17.1.1 — Tier Transition Is Civilizational, Not Individual:** No individual decision or discovery moves a civilization from one tier to the next. Tier transitions are institutional — they require all three categories of prerequisites (Area 10) to be satisfied across the civilization's infrastructure. A single brilliant inventor cannot build a Tier 8 civilization; the civilization must grow into Tier 8.

**Rule 17.1.2 — Tier Transitions Have Narrative Weight Proportional to the Tier:** The transition from Tier 1 to Tier 2 is a local event in civilizational history — significant but not world-altering in the cosmic sense. The transition from Tier 10 to Tier 11 is a galactic-civilization-scale event — the kind of threshold that other civilizations notice, respond to, and in some cases fear. Authors scale the narrative weight of tier transitions to the tier being crossed.

**Rule 17.1.3 — The Eight Intermediate Tier Transitions:** The transitions to the eight new intermediate tiers (6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15, 17) each have their own distinctive narrative signature. Tier 6 transition (inner solar system mastery) is most visible as: multi-world habitation becomes normalized; the home planet begins to feel like one node rather than the civilization. Tier 9 transition (multi-stellar) is most visible as: FTL travel becoming operational and the first genuine interstellar commerce and politics beginning.

### 17.2 — Tier Transition Table for Narrative Identification

| Transition | Primary Visible Signal | Narrative Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1→2 | Writing, organized government, metal weapons | Local historical event |
| Tier 2→3 | Industrialization; factory systems; national-scale energy | National historical event |
| Tier 3→4 | Nuclear power; global digital network; space launch | Planetary historical event |
| Tier 4→5 | Fusion power; AGI threshold; orbital habitation standard | Planetary civilizational threshold |
| Tier 5→6 | Multi-world habitation normalized; inner system industry | Inner-system civilizational threshold |
| Tier 6→7 | Outer system development; complete stellar system integration | Stellar-system civilizational threshold |
| Tier 7→8 | Dyson sphere construction begins; stellar energy access | Stellar-scale civilizational transformation |
| Tier 8→9 | FTL travel operational; second star system habitation | Interstellar civilizational threshold |
| Tier 9→10 | Stellar cluster unified logistics network | Cluster-scale civilizational transformation |
| Tier 10→11 | Galactic-scale governance and energy network operational | Galactic civilizational transformation (Type III Kardashev) |
| Tier 11→12 | First cross-galactic expansion; Local Group reach | Local Group civilizational threshold |
| Tier 12→13 | Galaxy cluster operational domain; dark matter energy begins | Galaxy cluster transformation |
| Tier 13→14 | Supercluster reach; dark energy partial harnessing | Supercluster civilizational threshold |
| Tier 14→15 | Observable universe fringe reach; expansion logistics | Near-universal civilizational threshold |
| Tier 15→16 | Universal energy access; physics as engineering material | Universal civilizational transformation |
| Tier 16→17 | First trans-universal operation; other universe access | Universal boundary crossing |
| Tier 17→18 | Multi-universal operational domain | Multi-universal civilizational threshold |
| Tier 18→19 | Dimensional layer operations | Reality-architecture civilizational threshold |
| Tier 19→20 | Absolute civilizational scale | Narrative-mythological limit |
