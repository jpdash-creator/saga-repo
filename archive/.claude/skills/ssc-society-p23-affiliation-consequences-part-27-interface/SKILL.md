## Area 22 — Affiliation Consequences and Part 27 Interface

### 22.1 — Affiliation Events as Part 27 Consequence Triggers

All major affiliation events at AFF-2 or deeper (including all hidden affiliation discoveries, all betrayals, and all structural affiliation collapses) are Part 27 Consequence Law events. The following affiliation events trigger Part 27 consequence assessment:

| Affiliation Event | Consequence Tier | Part 27 Assessment Required |
|---|---|---|
| Full alliance formation at FPT-6+ | Tier 3 | Yes — regional political impact |
| Alliance betrayal at FPT-5+ | Tier 4 | Yes — reputation cascade; military response |
| Vassal compact dissolution | Tier 2–4 (scale-dependent) | Yes |
| Hidden affiliation discovery | Tier 3–5 (scale of hidden relationship) | Yes — trust cascade across full web |
| Puppet faction liberation | Tier 4 | Yes — immediate political and military consequences |
| Elimination campaign declaration | Tier 5 | Yes — civilizational-scale response |
| Trans-civilization faction network exposure | Tier 5 | Yes — multi-civilization diplomatic crisis |
| Galaxy-branch village sect activation | Tier 4 | Yes — the village world is suddenly transformed |

### 22.2 — Long-Tail Affiliation Consequences

Affiliation events produce consequences that extend far beyond the immediate event horizon:

**Reputation Effects:** A faction known to have betrayed affiliations will find future affiliation formation more expensive — higher proof thresholds, shorter terms, more verification requirements. A faction known to honor its affiliations under pressure gains affiliation premium — partners will offer better terms.

**Third-Party Effects:** When two factions change their relationship, all third parties who had relationships with both must recalibrate their entire affiliation analysis.

**Generational Effects:** An affiliation betrayal or alliance formation in one generation shapes the affiliation landscape that the next generation inherits. The cultivation lifespan system (Part 3) means that long-lived senior cultivators may personally remember affiliation events from multiple generations ago, giving them unique perspective on current relationships.
