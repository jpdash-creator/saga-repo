## Area 11 — Synergy Interface with All Cultivation Parts

### 11.1 — Part 3 (Cultivation Power Law) Synergy Interface

**What Part 3 Provides to Synergy:**
- The cultivation realm ladder (Part 3, Area 1) establishes what cultivation tier a practitioner contributes to synergy — higher realm = higher cultivation-side synergy contribution
- The realm ceiling (Part 3, Area 6.3) is the absolute bound on cultivation-energy synergy output — no synergy exceeds this ceiling on the cultivation side
- CPM absorption physiology (Part 3, Area 18) governs how cultivators process the CPM that eventually feeds technology systems — the physiological basis of CPM availability is cultivation-side governance
- The 3-Level Rule (Part 3, Area 7.2) remains active in all synergy combat contexts — technology augmentation does not override this for individual cultivation combat tier comparison
- The Categorical Impossibility Law (Part 3, Area 17.3) applies to synergy outputs on the cultivation side — synergy cannot produce cross-Major-Realm cultivation-energy effects that cultivation alone cannot produce
- Breakthrough requirements (Part 3, Area 4) govern the cultivation development that produces high-realm CPM and high-realm cultivation contributions to synergy — technology cannot shortcut the cultivation development side

**What Synergy (Part 31) Provides to Part 3:**
- Part 31 governs how technology environments (Area 4.1) support cultivation advancement — Part 3 governs the advancement mechanics; Part 31 governs the environmental quality that technology provides
- Part 31 governs how technology medicine (Area 4.2) addresses the physical-substrate dimension of cultivation injuries — Part 3 governs the injuries themselves
- Part 31 governs how technology information systems (Area 4.3) enhance cultivation research and education — Part 3 governs cultivation practice and advancement

**Rule 11.1.1 — Part 3 Ceiling Laws Are Not Synergy Boundaries:** The realm ceiling (Part 3, Area 6.3) bounds cultivation-side outputs in synergy. It does not bound technology-side outputs in synergy. Part 31 governs synergy as both tracks contributing; Part 3 governs only the cultivation-side ceiling.

### 11.2 — Part 4 (Affinity, Heart, Soul Law) Synergy Interface

**Affinity in Synergy:**
The 40+ affinity types (Part 4, Area 1.2) are the classification taxonomy for affinity-typed CPM and affinity-typed technology outputs (Area 9.3). Affinity Advantage (Part 4, Area 1.5) applies when affinity-typed technology outputs interact with opposing-affinity targets in combat. Soul cultivation (Part 4, Area 2) governs the soul-side contribution of practitioners to Model C and D integration — the Soul Bond (Part 12, Area 11.2) required for Cultivation-Relic Integration (Part 7, Area 4) is a soul-cultivation threshold that contributes to the practitioner's integration capability.

**The Heart System in Synergy:**
A cultivator's Heart state (Part 4, Area 3) affects their integration capability in subtle ways. A Sovereign Heart reduces qi fluctuation during integration operation, improving CI Technology system stability for the practitioner. A Shattered Heart may produce irregular qi patterns that interfere with CI Technology calibration, reducing effective integration model access below the technical threshold.

**Rule 11.2.1 — Affinity Classification Is Part 4's Domain:** The taxonomy of what affinities exist, how they interact (Affinity Resonance, Affinity Opposition), and what the Affinity Advantage modifier value is — these are Part 4's governance. Part 31 uses these definitions for affinity-typed synergy outputs but does not modify or extend the affinity classification.

### 11.3 — Part 5 (Combat Law) Synergy Interface

Part 5 governs the combat framework within which all combat synergy operates. Part 31, Area 7 provides the synergy-specific combat applications. The relationship is:

- Part 5 adjudicates all force classifications, escalation tiers, and engagement outcomes
- Part 31 provides the synergy output classifications that Part 5 receives as inputs for adjudication
- When synergy produces combined-force outputs, Part 31 defines the output; Part 5 adjudicates the outcome

**Rule 11.3.1 — Part 5 Is the Adjudication Authority:** No synergy output governance in Part 31 overrides Part 5 combat adjudication. Part 31 describes what the combined-track output is; Part 5 determines what that output produces in a combat context.

---

### 11.4 — Part 6 (Move Taxonomy Law) Synergy Interface

**Synergy Techniques:**
Named Moves (Part 6) executed through a synergistic system (Model C or D) retain their Part 6 classification and governance. The technique is still a Named Move; it is classified under Part 6's taxonomy. What changes is the delivery medium and output scale. A technique delivered through a Model D integrated system may have a physical reach, energy output, or affinity-character delivery that exceeds the same technique executed without integration — but the technique classification, success rate governance, and collision rules (Part 6, Area 8) remain Part 6 governed.

**Rule 11.4.1 — Synergy Does Not Create New Move Categories:** Part 31 does not define new Named Move categories. When a technique is delivered through a synergistic system, it remains in its existing Part 6 classification with the physical output parameters modified by the integration model's capability. Authors should apply Part 6 classification and then note the synergy-modified output parameters in parenthetical reference to Part 31, Area 5.

### 11.5 — Part 7 (Fusion Law) Synergy Interface

Part 7 governs the cultivation-side thresholds for cultivation-technology integration. Part 31 governs the synergy outputs. Part 8 governs the technology-side thresholds.

**The Three-Part Joint Governance Structure:**
For any cultivation-technology integration event:
1. Part 7, Area 6.2 — provides the cultivation-side threshold table (what cultivation realm is required for each integration model)
2. Part 8, Area 6.2 — provides the technology-side threshold table (what CI Technology level is required for each integration model)
3. Part 31, Area 5 — provides the synergy output description (what each model produces when both thresholds are met)

No part of this governance is complete without the others. Part 31 does not duplicate Part 7 or Part 8 threshold tables — it references them and governs only the synergy output produced when both are satisfied.

**Fusion Path and Synergy:**
Fusion cultivators (Part 7) can achieve deeper synergy at any given CI Technology level than single-path cultivators, because their dual-path cultivation architecture has higher compatibility with the multi-architecture CI Technology systems at Model C and D. This is a synergy output governed by Part 31; the fusion path mechanics remain Part 7's domain.

---

### 11.6 — Part 11 (Energy Resource Law) Synergy Interface

Part 11 governs CPM as a physical substance — its formation, grades, properties, hazard profiles, and affinity typing. Part 31 governs CPM in its synergy role as a cross-track energy bridge.

**Joint Governance for CPM in Technology Systems:**
When CPM is used as technology fuel:
- Part 11 governs: what CPM is, what grades exist, what hazards it presents, what affinity types it carries
- Part 8, Area 6.4 governs: what CI Technology level is required to interface with each CPM grade
- Part 31, Area 9 governs: what synergy capabilities the CPM-technology interface enables at each grade and CI Technology level combination

All three Parts must be simultaneously satisfied. Part 31 does not override Part 11's CPM substance governance and does not override Part 8's technology-side interface requirements.

---

### 11.7 — Part 12 (Relics and Artifacts Law) Synergy Interface

Part 12 governs the object-layer properties of relics, formation arrays, and Technology-Relic Hybrids. Part 31 governs the synergistic capabilities produced when relics and technology operate together.

**Technology-Relic Hybrid Classes in Synergy:**
The three Hybrid Classes (Part 12, Area 5.1; Part 7; Part 8, Area 6.3) are the primary object category where relic-technology synergy is physically instantiated. Part 12 governs the object; Part 8 governs the technology-side prerequisites; Part 7 governs the cultivation-side threshold where fusion process is involved; Part 31 governs what the hybrid class produces in terms of synergy output and capability.

**Formation Array-Technology Synergy:**
Formation arrays (Part 12, Area 7) integrated with technology infrastructure are a primary non-combat synergy mechanism (Area 3.2, Area 6.2). Part 12 governs the formation array as an object; Part 31 governs the synergy produced when formation arrays and technology operate as co-operative infrastructure.
