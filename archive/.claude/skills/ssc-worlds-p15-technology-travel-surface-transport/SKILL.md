## Area 9 — Technology Travel: Surface Transport (Tiers 1–5)

### 9.1 — Tier 1 Surface Transport

**Vehicles:** Foot travel, animal riding (horses, oxen, pack animals), primitive watercraft (dugout canoes, reed boats), basic wheeled carts (wooden axle, no road infrastructure).

| Mode | Speed | Range per Day | Terrain Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walking | 4–5 km/h | 30–40 km | Any passable terrain |
| Horse riding | 10–15 km/h sustained, ~50 km/h burst | 50–80 km | Road, trail, open terrain |
| Oxcart | 5–7 km/h | 20–30 km | Road or flat terrain required |
| Primitive watercraft | 5–10 km/h | 50–100 km (river/coast) | Water only |

**Infrastructure:** None formalized; travel is terrain-constrained.

### 9.2 — Tier 2 Surface Transport

**Vehicles:** Horse-drawn carriages, sailing ships, organized road networks, bridges, river ferries, early canal systems.

| Mode | Speed | Range per Day | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horse-drawn carriage | 15–25 km/h on road | 100–200 km on road network | Road quality dependent |
| Sailing ship (open ocean) | 5–15 km/h | 200–400 km per day | Wind and current dependent |
| River ferry | 8–20 km/h (current-assisted) | 150–300 km | River navigation |
| Military horse (courier) | 30–50 km/h | 300–500 km (relay system) | Organized relay post system |

**Infrastructure:** Organized road networks, bridge systems, relay post stations.

### 9.3 — Tier 3 Surface Transport

**Vehicles:** Steam trains, steamships, early automobiles, early military aircraft, canal systems.

| Mode | Speed | Range per Day | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steam train | 80–150 km/h | 2,000–5,000 km | Rail network dependent |
| Steamship | 25–45 km/h | 600–1,200 km | Open ocean |
| Early automobile | 60–120 km/h | 500–1,500 km | Road required |
| Early aircraft | 150–400 km/h | 2,000–5,000 km per day | Atmospheric |

**Infrastructure:** National rail networks, canal systems, organized port infrastructure.

### 9.4 — Tier 4 Surface Transport

**Vehicles:** Commercial aviation, high-speed rail, private vehicles, early orbital rockets, submarines.

| Mode | Speed | Range/Day | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial aircraft | 800–1,200 km/h | 8,000–20,000 km | Global reach |
| High-speed rail | 350–600 km/h | 8,000–12,000 km | Rail network |
| Early rockets (orbital) | 7.9 km/s (orbital velocity) | — | Launch to orbit in ~8 minutes |
| Submarine | 50–80 km/h | 1,200–2,000 km | Submerged |

**Infrastructure:** Global aviation network, high-speed rail networks, early space launch infrastructure.

### 9.5 — Tier 5 Surface and Orbital Transport

**Vehicles:** Suborbital and orbital spacecraft, interplanetary early missions, hypersonic atmospheric transport, maglev/hyperloop planetary networks, permanent space stations.

| Mode | Speed | Transit Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maglev/hyperloop (planetary surface) | 1,000–5,000 km/h | Any point on planet in hours | Planetary tunnel-tube network |
| Orbital shuttle | 7.9–11 km/s | Surface to orbit: 8–30 minutes | Atmospheric to orbit |
| Early interplanetary (subluminal) | 10–50 km/s | Adjacent planet: 3 months to 2 years | Chemical/ion drive; mission-scale journey |
| Hypersonic atmospheric transport | 3,000–10,000 km/h | Any point on planet in 1–3 hours | Atmospheric only |

**Infrastructure:** Planetary launch networks, orbital stations as transit hubs, early interplanetary relay stations.
