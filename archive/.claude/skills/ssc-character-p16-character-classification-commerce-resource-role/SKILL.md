## Area 7 — Character Classification by Commerce and Resource Role

### 7.1 — Commercial Character Architecture

Commercial characters are essential in every civilization type. Their function is the flow of resources, technology, cultivation materials, and information between producers and consumers.

**Canonical Commercial Character Types:**

| Commercial Role | Description | Governing Parts |
|----------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Market Trader / Street Merchant** | Individual commerce operators; small scale; W-Class Stratum 1–2 | Parts 25, 22 |
| **Shop Owner / Establishment Merchant** | Fixed-location commerce; small-to-medium scale; CPM-grade materials common stock | Parts 11, 25, 22 |
| **Guild Merchant / Journeyman Trader** | Guild-affiliated commerce; operates trade routes; medium-scale resource flows | Parts 22, 25 |
| **Senior Merchant / Trade Factor** | Manages regional trade networks; significant W-Class position | Parts 22, 25 |
| **Merchant Prince / Trade Lord** | Controls major resource supply chains; W-Class Stratum 4–5; geopolitical influence | Parts 21, 22, 25 |
| **Commerce Guild Director** | Leads major mercantile faction (Part 22 MTF); institutional commercial authority | Parts 21, 22, 25 |
| **Resource Monopolist** | Controls exclusive access to a critical CPM grade or technology tier resource; geopolitically significant | Parts 11, 21, 22 |
| **Alchemist / Refiner** | Produces CPM-grade substances, pills, and cultivation materials; specialized commerce | Parts 11, 22 |
| **Banker / Financial Operator** | Controls capital flow; FBF faction equivalent (Part 22); funding military and faction operations | Parts 22, 25 |
