## Area 13 — Technology Applications (Reserved — Pending Technology/Energy Integration Law Amendment)

### 13.1 — Reserved Domain Notice `[DEFERRED — PENDING]`

This Area is formally reserved for future authoring under a dedicated Canon Part update (Technology/Energy Integration Law — pending authoring). The current version of Part 11 governs only cultivation-related applications of energy substances. Technology applications — how energy substances power technological devices, energy conversion systems, civilization-scale infrastructure, and enhancement equipment — will be authored as a coordinated amendment spanning Part 11 (Area 13), Part 8 (Technology Scale Law), Part 9 (Technology Growth Law), and Part 10 (Technology Science Law).

**Deferral rationale:** Authoring technology-substance interaction rules requires joint-scope governance across four Parts simultaneously. Attempting to author Area 13 in isolation would produce inconsistent interfaces with Parts 8/9/10. Deferral to a dedicated CAT allows the interaction surface to be designed coherently.

**Deferral tracking:** This deferral was formalized during Canon v4.14 (2026-04-19). The deferral remains open until the Technology/Energy Integration Law amendment is scheduled and authored.

No technology-substance interaction rules are currently active. Until this Area is authored, narrative involving technology use of energy substances must declare a Canon Variance (Part 1, Section 3.3.2) pending the formal governance of these interactions.

### 13.2 — Reactivity Established (Placeholder Principle)

While governance is reserved, Principle P11-1.5's reactivity principle is active: energy substances are reactive to technological systems with equivalent sensitivity and consequences as their biological reactivity. A technological system interfacing with a substance above its compatible grade tier experiences structural damage analogous to biological meridian damage. This principle is established here as a foundation — future technology authoring does not require a Part 11 amendment for this fundamental principle. It was present from the beginning.
