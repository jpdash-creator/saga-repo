## Area 11 — Weapon Combat Classification and Part 5 Integration

### 11.1 — Weapon Events as Part 5 Combat Events

Every weapon discharge in a combat context is simultaneously a Part 33 weapon event and a Part 5 combat event. The two governance frameworks apply in parallel. Part 33 governs: what the weapon is, what it can do, and whether it functions correctly. Part 5 governs: whether the deployment is legitimate, what escalation tier it implies, and what consequences flow from its use.

**Rule 33.11.1 — Weapon Use Triggers Force Legitimacy Assessment:** The first use of a weapon in a confrontation constitutes an active hostile act under Part 5, Area 4, Condition CR-3 (overt hostile act by any party), subject to the same force legitimacy rules as named cultivation techniques. A character drawing and firing a weapon initiates combat under Part 5 rules regardless of whether cultivation techniques are used.

**Rule 33.11.2 — Escalation Tier Violation:** Deploying a weapon whose WS class exceeds the contextually appropriate escalation tier is a canon error equivalent to deploying a Catastrophic cultivation technique in an E1 Personal Combat. WS-V weapons cannot be legitimately deployed in E3 tactical skirmishes. Unilateral escalation through weapon deployment follows Part 5's unilateral escalation consequence rules.

### 11.2 — Weapon Scale Class to Escalation Tier Mapping

| Weapon WS Class | Minimum Escalation Tier for Legitimate Deployment | Maximum Natural Escalation Ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| WS-I | E1 (Personal Combat) | E4 |
| WS-II | E1 (Personal Combat, multi-target) | E4 |
| WS-III | E2 (Skirmish Scale) | E5 |
| WS-IV | E3 (Tactical Engagement) | E5 |
| WS-V | E4 (Strategic Operation) | E6 |
| WS-VI | E5 (Full Faction Conflict) | E7 |
| WS-VII | E7 only — Absolute Escalation | E7 (ceiling) |

### 11.3 — Weapon vs. Cultivation Technique — Part 5 Priority Adjudication

When a cultivator is struck by a technology weapon and simultaneously uses a defensive cultivation technique, Part 5 adjudication occurs in the following order:

1. **Cultivation defense force category comparison first:** Is the weapon's force category below, equal to, or above the cultivator's realm?
2. **Damage vector assessment:** Which DV vectors does the weapon deliver, and which is the cultivator's defense aligned against?
3. **WS class vs. cultivation aura spatial footprint:** If the weapon's WS radius exceeds the cultivator's aura radius (Part 3, Area 20 table), the cultivator cannot defend using aura alone.
4. **Outcome adjudication:** If weapon tier force category exceeds cultivation realm AND WS class exceeds aura footprint → cultivation defense provides no meaningful protection. Otherwise, cultivation defense reduces damage proportionally.
