## Area 20 — Galaxy Cluster, Galaxy Supercluster, Universe, and Multiverse Classification

At the scale of Galaxy Clusters and above, the classification system follows the same 10-class framework established for lower cosmic tiers. The columns remain: Ambient Energy Profile | Affinity Profile | Special Zone Status | Dimensional Properties. The classes (1–10) represent the same spectrum: barren through sparse through common through affinity through rich through vortex through sacred (minor/major/apex) through dimensional.

For brevity, these tiers are not expanded with full class tables in this Part, as the governing principles are identical to Areas 19 (Galaxy), 18 (Star Quadrant), 17 (Star Cluster), and 16 (Star System) — the scale increases but the classification logic is the same. Authors and ledger maintainers apply the 10-class framework directly to Galaxy Cluster, Galaxy Supercluster, Universe, and Multiverse tiers using the same column definitions.

**Galaxy Cluster (GC-1 through GC-10):** Multiple Galaxies gravitationally associated.
**Galaxy Supercluster (GSC-1 through GSC-10):** Multiple Galaxy Clusters forming cosmic web structures.
**Universe (U-1 through U-10):** A single spacetime manifold containing Galaxy Superclusters and all below.
**Multiverse (MV-1 through MV-10):** Multiple Universes, each potentially with different physical constants and cultivation energy profiles.
