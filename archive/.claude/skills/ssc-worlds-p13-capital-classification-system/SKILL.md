## Area 6 — Capital Classification System

A Capital is the political, administrative, and often cultivation or technology apex of a governance structure — the center from which authority radiates. Capitals carry symbolic, historical, and power-concentration functions that exceed their physical size alone. Every Kingdom and Empire has a Capital. Large Cities may serve as Capitals.

### 6.1 — Capital Class Table

| Class | Designation | Administrative Scope | Ambient Energy | Affinity Profile | Special Zone | Historical Depth | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K-1 | District Capital | A cluster of Villages or a minor territory | Class 1–3 | Neutral | None | Variable | Administrative center for a small cluster of settlements. First point of formal governance. Basic court functions. |
| K-2 | County Capital | A district of Towns | Class 2–3 | Neutral | None | Established | Governs a district containing multiple Towns. Court-level governance established. Formal tax collection, basic legal systems. |
| K-3 | Regional Capital | A full region of Cities and Towns | Class 2–3 | Neutral or mild | None | Long-established | A major administrative hub for a large region. Multi-tier governance machinery, standing military garrison, formal courts. |
| K-4 | Provincial Capital | Multiple regions under common governance | Class 2–4 | Neutral or affinity | Possible | Centuries | Governs a full province. Near-imperial administrative machinery. Major faction presence as power anchor. Grand infrastructure. |
| K-5 | Kingdom Capital | A single Kingdom | Class 3–4 | Neutral or affinity | Possible | Long history | The seat of a Kingdom. Full court, military command center, treasury, and spiritual or cultivation authority. Symbolic center of the Kingdom's identity. |
| K-6 | Grand Capital | A major Kingdom or multi-Kingdom authority | Class 3–5 | Neutral or affinity | Possible: Formation Zone | Deep history | The seat of a major Kingdom or early multi-Kingdom authority. Grand architecture, major formation defenses, population over a million. |
| K-7 | Imperial Capital | An Empire spanning multiple Kingdoms | Class 3–5 | Neutral or affinity | Possible: Formation Zone or Sacred Ground grade 1–3 | Ancient — centuries to millennia | The seat of an Empire. Multiple subordinate Kingdoms. Grand formation arrays. Enormous historical continuity. The Empire's identity radiates from this location. |
| K-8 | Grand Imperial Capital | A major multi-continental Empire | Class 4–5 | Neutral or affinity | Sacred Ground grade 1–4 possible | Ancient — millennia | The capital of a dominant Empire spanning an entire continent or multiple continents. Grand Sacred Ground presence possible. Known across its World. |
| K-9 | World Capital | A World-spanning authority | Class 4–6 | Variable | Likely Sacred Ground | Ancient | The single administrative capital of a World. The entire planet acknowledges its authority. Its formation arrays and energy environment are legendary within its Star System. |
| K-10 | Cosmic Capital | A multi-World or Star System-spanning authority | Class 5–7 | Variable | Sacred Ground grade 4–8 | Ancient — millennia or more | The capital of a civilization spanning multiple Worlds or a Star System. Its energy environment, dimensional properties, and historical depth are beyond what any single-World capital achieves. Known across its Star Cluster or beyond. |
