## Area 6 — Growth Speed

### 6.1 — Governing Principle

No two cultivators advance at exactly the same rate. Growth speed is a function of talent (Area 5), cultivation method quality, environmental resources (Part 11), guidance received, and the individual's mental and emotional engagement with cultivation.

### 6.2 — Growth Speed Factors

**Factor 1 — Meridian Root Efficiency (Primary):**
The direct multiplier to advancement speed. As defined in Area 5, Root classification determines how efficiently energy converts into cultivation progress. A Golden Root is baseline (1×); roots below are fractional; roots above are multiplied.

**Factor 2 — Method Quality:**
Not all cultivation methods are equal. A poor method wastes energy even through a good Root. An excellent method maximizes efficiency regardless of Root quality. Method quality is governed by technique availability, faction access (Part 22), and external guidance (teachers, manuals, ancient formations).

**Factor 3 — Environmental Richness (Part 11):**
Cultivation in a location saturated with spiritual energy, near an energy source or sacred ground, dramatically accelerates progress. This is why sects and major factions compete intensely for territory with high ambient energy density.

**Factor 4 — Mental and Emotional Engagement:**
A cultivator in a state of clarity, intent, and philosophical alignment advances faster than one who cultivates mechanically without understanding. This is why Heart cultivation (Part 4) directly affects growth speed — emotional clarity and intent align the cultivator's mind with their cultivation, reducing internal friction. Conversely, extreme emotional disturbance (trauma, grief, unresolved conflict) can arrest advancement entirely.

**Factor 5 — Age and Foundation:**
Young cultivators advance faster at early stages. As cultivation deepens, the foundation must be proportionally stronger to support the next level. A cultivator who advances too fast without building foundation will accelerate early and hit severe walls later. A cultivator who builds slowly and thoroughly often overtakes faster cultivators in the long run.

### 6.3 — Realm Ceiling Constraint

Cultivation advancement cannot skip breakthroughs. No amount of CPM, relic support, external energy, or talent shortcut replaces the breakthrough event required to cross a realm boundary (Area 4). A cultivator may approach a realm ceiling rapidly, but cannot cross it without a valid breakthrough. This is a physical cultivation law, not a narrative restriction — the energetic and biological requirements of the system enforce it.
