## Area 4 — Weapon Tier Reference Table

### 4.1 — Weapon Tier Map to Part 8 Type 8 Domain

The following master reference table maps every weapon tier to its source in Part 8's Type 8 Military & Defense Technology domain, its paradigm band, its force category equivalence, and its minimum cultivation defense threshold.

| Weapon Tier | Part 8 Type 8 Source Level | Paradigm Band | Technology Force Category (Part 8 Area 8.2) | Cultivation Defense Threshold | Highest Cultivation Defense |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Level 1 | TW-Band (TB-Alpha) | Force-1: Mortal T1 equivalent | None required | Any cultivator |
| Tier 2 | Level 2 | TW-Band (TB-Alpha) | Force-2: Mortal T2–T3 equivalent | Minor body cultivation hardening | Mortal T1+ |
| Tier 3 | Level 3 | TW-Band (TB-Beta) | Force-3: Mortal T4–T5 equivalent | Core Realm (Tier 1) body cultivation | Core Realm+ |
| Tier 4 | Level 4 | TW-Band (TB-Beta) | Force-4: Gold Core equivalent | Core Realm peak body cultivation | Gold Core Realm+ |
| Tier 5 | Level 5 | TW-Band (TB-Gamma) | Force-5: Void Origin equivalent | Void Origin Realm peak (WS-I only) | Void Origin peak only for WS-I |
| Tier 6 | Level 6 | OW-Band (TB-Delta) | Force-6: Spirit Sea equivalent | Cultivation defense insufficient for WS-III+ | Spirit Sea cap at WS-I–II |
| Tier 7 | Level 7 | OW-Band (TB-Delta) | Force-7: Supreme equivalent | Cultivation defense fails at WS-III+ | Supreme peak cap at WS-I |
| Tier 8 | Level 8 | IW-Band (TB-Epsilon) | Force-8: Divine Liquid equivalent | Cultivation defense fails at WS-II+ | Divine realm defense only for WS-I |
| Tier 9 | Level 9 | IW-Band (TB-Epsilon) | Force-9: Divine Sea equivalent | Cultivation defense fails entirely | Technology shielding only |
| Tier 10 | Level 10 | IW-Band (TB-Epsilon) | Force-10: Divine Peak equivalent | Cultivation defense fails entirely | Technology shielding only |
| Tier 11 | Level 11 | GW-Band (TB-Zeta) | Force-11: Absolute Realm T1 equivalent | Cultivation defense fails entirely | Technology shielding only |
| Tier 12–14 | Levels 12–14 | GW-Band (TB-Zeta) | Force-12–14: Absolute Realm T2–T4 equivalent | Cultivation defense fails entirely | Technology shielding only |
| Tier 15–20 | Levels 15–20 | CW-Band (TB-Zeta) | Force-15–20: Primordium equivalents | Cultivation defense fails entirely | Technology shielding only |

**Canonical Rule (Rule 33.4.1 — Cultivation Defense Failure Threshold):** This table is the authoritative source for cultivation defense thresholds against technology weapons, expanding Part 8's Rule 15.8.4. Above Tier 8, technology weapons exceed Divine-realm cultivation defensive capability entirely. Above Tier 12, technology weapons exceed all known cultivation defensive capability including Absolute Realm defenses — technology shielding at an appropriate tier is the only viable defense.
