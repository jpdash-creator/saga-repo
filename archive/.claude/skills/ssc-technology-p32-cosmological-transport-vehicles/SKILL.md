## Area 6 — Cosmological Transport Vehicles (CT-Band, Tiers 15–20)

### 6.1 — Scope and Deferred Governance

CT-Band vehicles (Tiers 15–20) operate at universe-scale and beyond. As with Part 15, Area 8 and Part 13's Primordial-scale governance, full CT-Band vehicle specifications are **partially deferred scope** — detailed governance requires Part 28 (Apex Narrative Law) authorization for narrative use.

The following outlines the canonical framework without full mechanical specification:

### 6.2 — CT-Band Vehicle Categories (Framework Only)

**Tier 15–16: Near-Universal Transit**

*CT-Class Celestial Ark:* SC-VII civilization-scale vessels capable of universe-scale transit. A Celestial Ark is effectively a mobile world — self-contained planetary civilization in transit form. Governance: Tier 15–16 construction; requires Part 28 for narrative deployment.

*CT-Class Universal Warship:* SC-VII military vehicle with universe-scale weapons capability. Classified MIL-CAP. Requires Part 28 for all active narrative use. A Universal Warship's existence alone is an E7-equivalent fact in any narrative context where it is known to exist.

**Tier 17–20: Dimensional and Absolute Transit**

At these tiers, "vehicle" as a concept stretches to its limits — travel at Tier 17–20 increasingly involves existence-tier transitions rather than conventional propulsion. These are governed by the principle that dimensional transit vehicles are jointly governed by Part 7 (Fusion — for the integration of consciousness and space-transit), Part 10 (Science — for the physics prerequisites), and Part 28 (for the narrative authorization). Full specification is deferred.
