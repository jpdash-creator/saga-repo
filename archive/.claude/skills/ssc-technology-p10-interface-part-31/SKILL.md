## Area 13 — Interface with Part 31 (Cultivation-Technology Synergy Law)

### 13.1 — Synergy's Scientific Contribution

Part 31 (Area 3.5) governs the specific synergy output of cultivation-assisted scientific research — the cultivation-side enhancement to research programs that accelerates scientific prerequisite satisfaction. Part 10 governs what those contributions achieve on the scientific-knowledge side: what specific scientific prerequisites they satisfy (or partially satisfy), at what rate relative to the standard accumulation timeline, and what their ontological classification is (Layer 1, 2, or 3 scientific knowledge).

The governance split: Part 31 owns HOW the cultivation contribution enters research programs and WHAT synergy output it represents; Part 10 owns WHAT scientific knowledge is produced and what tier-transition prerequisites that knowledge satisfies.

### 13.2 — Integration Civilizations' Scientific Advantage

In civilizations with active cultivation-technology integration (Part 31 Integration Models A–D), the Science-Cultivation Interface (Area 7) is institutionalized and systematic rather than opportunistic. This produces a structural scientific advantage:

- Type 12 CI Technology prerequisites advance 20–40% faster than in non-integration civilizations (cultivation science and technology science are building on each other systematically)
- Type 5 Medical and Type 14 Materials prerequisites advance 10–25% faster (cultivation knowledge of biology and material properties accelerates framework construction)
- Type 7 Energy and Type 10 Space prerequisites benefit from soul-sense observational contributions at rates governed by the highest-realm practitioners participating in integration programs

The specific acceleration percentages are Part 9 (timing) governance; the specific scientific knowledge content produced is Part 10 (content) governance.
