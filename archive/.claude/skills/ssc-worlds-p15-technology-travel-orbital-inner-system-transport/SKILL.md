## Area 10 — Technology Travel: Orbital and Inner-System Transport (Tiers 5–7)

### 10.1 — Tier 5–6 Inner System Transport

At Tier 5–6, a civilization's transport infrastructure extends from a single world to a multi-world inner system. Subluminal high-thrust drives (fusion or advanced ion) make routine transit between inner planetary bodies practical in days to weeks.

| Mode | Speed | Transit Time (1 AU) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 5 interplanetary (fusion drive) | 100–500 km/s | 3–10 days per AU | Practical inner system travel |
| Tier 6 inner system rapid transit | 500–2,000 km/s | 20–85 hours per AU | Routine commerce between worlds |
| Tier 6 passenger liner (inner system) | 200–800 km/s | 2–8 days per AU | Passenger service with acceleration comfort |
| Tier 6 cargo freighter (inner system) | 100–300 km/s | 6–17 days per AU | High cargo efficiency |

**Infrastructure (Tiers 5–6):**
- Orbital stations at all inhabited bodies
- In-system beacon and navigation network
- Refueling stations at orbital distances
- Traffic control systems for inner system lanes
- Emergency rescue infrastructure (ships, medical stations)

### 10.2 — Tier 7 Full Stellar System Transport

At Tier 7, the entire stellar system — including outer planets, ice giants, and Kuiper-equivalent zones — becomes routine transit space. Drive performance reaches the level where even multi-AU outer system destinations are weeks-scale journeys.

| Mode | Speed | Transit Time (50 AU outer system) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 7 rapid transit craft | 2,000–10,000 km/s | 6–30 days | Outer system passenger routes |
| Tier 7 cargo freighter | 500–2,000 km/s | 1–4 months | Economic outer system freight |
| Tier 7 survey vessel | 1,000–5,000 km/s | 2–6 weeks | Deep system exploration |

**Infrastructure (Tier 7):**
- Full stellar system beacon network (outer system navigation)
- Deep system habitation platforms (outer planet moons, asteroid stations)
- Long-range fuel depot chains throughout the stellar system
- Stellar system emergency response infrastructure
