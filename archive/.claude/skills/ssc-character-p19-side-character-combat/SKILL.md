## Area 8 — Side Character in Combat

### 8.1 — Combat Governance for Side Characters

All side character combat scenes are governed by Part 5 (Cultivation — Combat and Fight Law) and Part 6 (Move Taxonomy Law). The cultivation power and technique mastery of side characters follow the same Canon laws as heroes and villains.

**Rule 19.8.1.1 — Side Character Power Must Be Consistent:** A Tier A side character who is established as a Core Formation cultivator does not suddenly perform at Divine Sea level without a canon-valid explanation (breakthrough, Part 28 variance). Narrative convenience cannot override power law.

**Rule 19.8.1.2 — Side Character Combat Serves Narrative Function:** Side character combat scenes must serve the character's narrative function. A Mentor's combat shows mastery the hero is not yet at. An Ally's combat shows a different approach, a different style, a different cost structure. A Rival's combat shows the gap that must be closed. Combat that exists only to show the side character is "impressive" without connecting to narrative function is a narration waste.

**Rule 19.8.1.3 — Side Character Deaths in Combat Are Governed Events:** When a Tier A or major Tier B side character dies in combat, this death is a Crucible Event candidate for the hero (Part 17 Area 7) and a major CSS event for all connected characters. The combat scene producing this death must be given sufficient weight for the death to carry its full narrative impact.
