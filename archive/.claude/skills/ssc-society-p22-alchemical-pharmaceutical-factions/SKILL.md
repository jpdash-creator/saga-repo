## Area 8 — Alchemical and Pharmaceutical Factions

### 8.1 — Overview: Alchemical and Pharmaceutical Factions (ALF)

Alchemical and Pharmaceutical Factions are organized institutions whose primary purpose is the refinement of substances — CPM processing, pill crafting, elixir creation, medical substance production, poison development, and the broader application of alchemical knowledge to cultivation advancement and medical treatment.

**Defining Characteristics:**
- **Primary Purpose:** CPM substance refinement; medical products; cultivation enhancement consumables
- **Core Resource:** CPM raw materials; refinement furnaces; alchemical knowledge; Formation Arrays for controlled synthesis
- **Cultivation Component:** Alchemy requires specialized cultivation knowledge — alchemists are typically cultivators with specific affinity types advantageous for heat control and substance manipulation (Fire, Earth, Wood/Life affinities are classic alchemical affinities)
- **Natural Scale Range:** Village (SCL-1) through Galaxy (SCL-11)

### 8.2 — Alchemical Faction Classifications

| Alchemical Type | Primary Output | Scale Range | CPM Grade Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Village Herb Merchant | Basic medicinal herbs; low-grade CPM raw material | SCL-1 to SCL-2 | Grade 1–3 |
| Town Apothecary Guild | Processed medicinals; basic CPM concentrates | SCL-2 to SCL-3 | Grade 3–7 |
| City Alchemy Association | Refined pills; mid-grade CPM processing; basic elixirs | SCL-3 to SCL-5 | Grade 7–12 |
| Kingdom/Empire Pill House | High-grade pills; Core-level CPM processing; medical distribution networks | SCL-5 to SCL-7 | Grade 12–16 |
| Grand Alchemy Sect | Complete alchemical tradition; all grades; technique transmission | SCL-6 to SCL-10 | Grade 16–20 |
| Divine Liquid Refiners (Rare) | Divine Liquid containment; highest-grade CPM processing | SCL-8 to SCL-11 | Grade 20 |
| Pharmaceutical Corporations (Tech-integrated) | Technology-assisted drug synthesis; CPM-tech hybrid products | SCL-6 to SCL-11 | Varies; CI Level 8+ for tech integration |
| Poison & Toxin Guilds | Offensive alchemical agents; counter-poison agents | SCL-4 to SCL-9 | Specialized; often criminal adjacency |

### 8.3 — Alchemical Hierarchy

| Rank | Title | Capability |
|---|---|---|
| Grand Alchemist | Grand Pill Master / Sage Alchemist | Grade 18–20 CPM processing; legendary technique access |
| High Alchemist | Pill Master / Senior Alchemist | Grade 14–18 CPM processing |
| Intermediate Alchemist | Journeyman Alchemist | Grade 9–14 CPM processing |
| Novice Alchemist | Apprentice Alchemist | Grade 3–9 CPM processing |
| Assistant | Herb Sorter / Furnace Assistant | Raw material handling; no independent refinement |
