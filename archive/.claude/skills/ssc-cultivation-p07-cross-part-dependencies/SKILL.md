| Canon-Part 25 | Part 7 → Part 25 | Fusion achievement is one of the highest-prestige cultural milestones in cultivation civilizations — it is not merely a power event but a cultural event with deep social ramifications governed by Part 25. Stage 5 (Deep Fusion) achievement creates a cultural dynasty within the sect — descendants of a Stage 5 practitioner hold inherited cultural prestige (L-Class elevation in Part 25's Lineage Class social axis) that persists for generations regardless of those descendants' own cultivation. Fusion lineages (cultivation families or sect branches that have consistently produced Stage 3+ fusion practitioners) constitute a distinct cultural sub-class in integration-capable civilizations: a family that has produced three consecutive generations of Stage 4+ practitioners is recognized as a cultural institution by Part 25's lineage culture governance. Model D integration practitioners (Supreme Integration — Area 6.4) occupy a position of mythological cultural status in civilizations that know of their existence: they are the subject of cultural stories, the recipients of CPT-1 and CPT-6 quasi-religious reverence, and the targets of CPT-3 faction recruitment at any cost; their public identity is a cultural event that reshapes the social landscape of any civilization they inhabit |
