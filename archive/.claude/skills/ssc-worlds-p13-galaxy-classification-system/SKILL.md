## Area 19 — Galaxy Classification System (Cosmic Tier)

A Galaxy is a gravitationally bound collection of Star Quadrants — the fundamental large-scale structure of the physical universe. Galaxy class reflects the overall energy character, Sacred zone density, and dimensional properties of the entire galactic structure.

### 19.1 — Galaxy Internal Structure

Galaxies contain millions of Star Quadrants organized into: Galactic Core (densest region, highest cultivation energy density at galactic scale), Galactic Arms/Spiral Structure (most Star Quadrants, most civilizations), Galactic Halo (sparse outer regions, older Star Clusters, isolated populations), and Satellite Dwarf Galaxies (smaller gravitationally bound satellite galaxies, each classifiable independently).

### 19.2 — Galaxy Class Table

| Class | Designation | Ambient Energy Profile | Affinity Profile | Special Zone Status | Dimensional Properties | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G-1 | Barren Galaxy | Class 1 dominant | Neutral | None at Galaxy scale | Standard | A Galaxy with no meaningful aggregate energy character at galactic scale. Matter only — no civilization-significant energy profile. |
| G-2 | Sparse Galaxy | Class 1–2 | Neutral | None at Galaxy scale | Standard | Low galaxy-scale energy profile. Individual Quadrants may vary but the Galaxy as a unit is unremarkable. |
| G-3 | Common Galaxy | Class 2 | Neutral or mild | None at Galaxy scale | Standard | The baseline inhabited Galaxy. Standard in all regards at galactic scale. |
| G-4 | Affinity Galaxy | Class 2–3 | Active affinity across significant Quadrant density | Quadrant-distributed Formation Zones | Standard | A Galaxy with a dominant affinity character that manifests across multiple Star Quadrants. The Galaxy has a recognized energy identity across its Universe. |
| G-5 | Rich Galaxy | Class 3–4 | Neutral or affinity | Formation Zones distributed across constituent Quadrants at notable density | Standard | An energy-rich Galaxy. The aggregate Formation Zone density across its Star Quadrants makes it noticeably favorable. |
| G-6 | Vortex Galaxy | Class 4–5 | Affinity aligned | Active Vortex Zones across multiple Star Quadrants; Possible: Galaxy-scale energy phenomenon | Standard | A Galaxy with distributed high-energy Vortex Formation Zones across constituent Quadrants. |
| G-7 | Sacred Galaxy (Minor) | Class 6–7 in Sacred zones | Affinity aligned | Sacred Grounds distributed across multiple Star Quadrants | Minor dimensional alteration in sacred zones | A Galaxy where Sacred Ground presence is distributed across multiple constituent Star Quadrants. The Galaxy has sacred significance at Universe scale. |
| G-8 | Sacred Galaxy (Major) | Class 7 in sacred zones | Affinity aligned | High-grade Sacred Grounds (S5–S7) across multiple Star Quadrants; Galaxy-scale energy convergence | Moderate dimensional alteration | A Galaxy of major sacred significance at Universe or Multiverse scale. High-grade Sacred presence distributed across constituent Quadrants. |
| G-9 | Sacred Galaxy (Apex) | Class 7 (transcendent) | Affinity aligned | S7–S8 Sacred Grounds across multiple Star Quadrants; Galaxy-scale sacred convergence | Significant dimensional overlap across the Galaxy | An apex-sacred Galaxy. The density of the highest-grade Sacred Grounds makes the entire Galaxy a sacred structure of Multiverse significance. |
| G-10 | Dimensional Galaxy | Class 4–7 | Variable | Dimensional feature of Galaxy-scale magnitude — inter-quadrant or inter-galactic Stable Rift structure, Galaxy-scale Resonance Plane access, or confirmed dimensional layer overlap at Galaxy scale | Active Galaxy-scale dimensional structure | A Galaxy defined by a major dimensional feature operating at galactic scale. |
