## Area 6 — Space Forces and Exploration Factions

### 6.1 — Overview: Space Forces (SPC)

Space Forces are specialized military and exploratory factions whose primary operational environment is the void — interplanetary space, interstellar space, and above. They require FTL-capable or at minimum sub-FTL-capable technology infrastructure (Part 15 Tiers 5+) or cultivation space-travel capability (Core Formation+ from Part 3) to function in their primary environment.

**Operational Prerequisites:** Space Forces cannot function below Part 15 Orbital/Inner-System paradigm (Tiers 5–7 technology) unless their personnel include Core Formation+ cultivators capable of independent vacuum operation (Part 15 Area 4).

**Characteristic FPT Range:** FPT-6 (Solarian) through FPT-14 (Superclusteral)

### 6.2 — Space Force Classifications

| Space Force Type | Operational Domain | Scale | Technology Floor | Cultivation Component |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orbital Patrol Fleet | Low orbit; orbital station defense | SCL-7 | Tier 5–6 | Optional |
| System Defense Navy | Full star system; inner and outer system | SCL-8 | Tier 7–8 | Core Formation useful |
| Interstellar Strike Fleet | Multi-star-system offensive operations | SCL-9 | Tier 8–9; sub-FTL and first FTL | Core Solidification+ preferred |
| FTL Combat Fleet | FTL-capable military; star-to-star projection | SCL-9 to SCL-10 | Tier 9; practical FTL | Heaven Treading for scouts |
| Gate Fleet | Star Gate network-dependent military logistics and combat | SCL-10 to SCL-11 | Tier 10+ | Varies |
| Galactic Armada | Galaxy-scale military projection | SCL-11 | Tier 11–14 | Divine Ascension commanders |
| Cosmic Navy | Multi-galaxy / cosmological-scale naval force | SCL-12 to SCL-13 | Tier 14–16 | Divine Sea navigators |
| Vanguard Expeditionary Corps | Beyond-frontier exploration-military hybrid | SCL-8 to SCL-12 | Tier 9–12 | Heaven Treading mandatory |

### 6.3 — Exploration and Frontier Factions (EFF)

Exploration factions are non-military (or paramilitary) organizations whose primary purpose is expanding the known map — charting unmapped void regions, establishing contact with uncatalogued civilizations, locating new Sacred Grounds, discovering anomalous regions, and pioneering settlement in frontier space.

**Defining Characteristics:**
- Small-team structure: exploration factions favor highly capable mobile teams over mass formations
- Self-sufficiency: extended void operations require faction members to be largely self-sufficient
- Cultivation-technology balance: most exploration factions are BP-Type (Part 21 terminology equivalent at faction level) — combining cultivation space-travel with technology life support

**Key Exploration Faction Types:**

| Type | Focus | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Void Cartographers' Guilds | Mapping unmapped space; stellar surveying | SCL-8 to SCL-11 |
| Sacred Ground Seekers | Locating new Sacred Grounds and high-energy zones | SCL-5 to SCL-10 |
| First Contact Corps | Diplomatic exploration; encountering new civilizations | SCL-9 to SCL-12 |
| Anomaly Research Expeditions | Investigating spatial anomalies, dimensional rifts (Part 13 Area 15) | SCL-7 to SCL-11 |
| Pioneer Settlement Corps | Establishing new civilization outposts on frontier worlds | SCL-6 to SCL-9 |
| Relic Recovery Expeditions | Locating and recovering ancient Ruins World artifacts (Part 13 Area 8) | SCL-5 to SCL-10 |
| Trans-Universal Survey Teams | At Divine Sea Realm and CCT-17+ — exploring multiverse boundaries | SCL-14 to SCL-15 |
