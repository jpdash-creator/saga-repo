## Area 6 — Cultivation and Power Consequences

### 6.1 — Adverse Cultivation Consequences

Actions can produce permanent or lasting adverse consequences on a character's cultivation capacity:

**Cultivation Path Damage:**
Extreme physical injury (Part 3, Area 8.3), severe soul damage (Part 4, Area 2.7), or specific cultivation miscalculations can produce lasting damage to cultivation pathways. Part 3 governs the physical reality of this damage; Part 27 governs its status as a consequence of a specific action — the causal chain from action to outcome must be traceable.

**Realm Regression:**
Under specific circumstances — extreme physical damage, severe soul injury, catastrophic Qi Deviation, or violation of fundamental cultivation laws — realm regression is a canonical consequence. Realm regression is always a consequence (something caused it) and never a narrative convenience.

**Heart Ceiling from Moral Injury:**
Unresolved moral injury (Part 26, Area 3.3.3) creates a permanent Heart advancement ceiling. This is a consequence of the moral transgression — Part 27 governs it as such. The ceiling is removable through the resolution pathways defined in Part 26, Area 9.2 (Pathway 4 — Active Accountability and Atonement), but it cannot be bypassed without resolution.

**Technique Prohibition:**
A character who uses cultivation techniques for atrocity — particularly soul techniques (Part 4, Area 2) used for non-combat purposes against non-combatants — may find that the technique's soul resonance is permanently altered. The technique that was used to violate now carries the memory of that violation in its execution architecture. The character may still execute the technique, but its interaction with their own soul architecture produces psychological consequences (Part 26) every time they use it.

### 6.2 — Enabling Cultivation Consequences

Not all cultivation consequences are adverse. Genuine growth produces enabling consequences:

**Heart Awakening as Consequence of Genuine Experience:**
The Cultivation Heart (Part 4, Area 3.3) does not awaken through desire — it awakens through earned experience. The Heart awakening event is always a consequence of a PIS-5+ psychological event that was faced with genuine presence rather than suppression or avoidance. The Heart awakenings that produce the greatest power advances are the ones that were earned through the deepest consequences.

**Breakthrough Enabling from Genuine Challenge:**
Major cultivation breakthroughs are enabled — not produced, but enabled — by the accumulated experience of genuine difficulty, consequence, and survival. A cultivator who has been tested, who has faced real consequences for their actions and their choices, who has carried real weight — is more capable of breakthrough than a cultivator who has advanced in protected circumstances. The consequence of having genuinely faced difficulty is a cultivation capacity that safety cannot provide.

**Power Depth from Earned Understanding:**
Beyond raw power advancement, the depth and quality of a cultivator's power — how fully it can be expressed, how precisely controlled, how genuinely theirs — is a consequence of the totality of their cultivation journey. Power earned through genuine difficulty and consequence is qualitatively different from power accumulated through resource advantage alone.
