## Area 3 — Weapon User Classification

### 3.1 — The Six Weapon User Classes

All weapon users are classified into one of six Weapon User Classes that determine which weapons they may legally operate, what training prerequisites apply, and how their weapon use is classified under Part 5's combat framework.

| User Class | Designation | Who Qualifies | Maximum Authorized Weapon Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| WU-I | Individual Operator | Single cultivator or technology practitioner; unaugmented individual | WS-I–II standard; WS-III with Individual Heavy Weapon certification |
| WU-II | Crew-Operated Platform | Multi-operator team (2–20 operators); crewed vehicles at SC-I through SC-III; emplacement crews | WS-II–IV |
| WU-III | Vehicle-Hardpoint System | Vehicle-mounted weapons operated from integrated fire-control; SC-I through SC-IV vehicle classes | WS-III–V (limited by vehicle configuration code) |
| WU-IV | Capital Installation | Heavy warship, orbital fortress, or fixed planetary installation; SC-V through SC-VII vehicle classes | WS-IV–VI |
| WU-V | Faction Strategic Asset | Weapons operated at faction level under Part 22 strategic authorization; civilization-class weapons by faction designation | WS-V–VII (Strategic Ordnance authorization required) |
| WU-VI | Civilization Strategic Weapon | Weapons operated at civilization level under Part 21 authorization; civilizational-scale only | WS-VI–VII (Absolute Authorization Protocol required) |

### 3.2 — Individual Operator Weapon Sub-Classes

WU-I Individual Operator weapons are subdivided by the operator's primary capability profile — whether they are pure technology operators, cultivators, or integrated practitioners. This determines which weapon types within WS-I–II they may access.

| WU-I Sub-Class | Designation | Operator Profile | Accessible Weapon Types |
|---|---|---|---|
| WU-I-A | Standard Operator | Non-cultivator technology practitioner; military or civilian training | T-KIN, T-RAY, T-BURST (WS-I–II only) |
| WU-I-B | Combat Specialist | Non-cultivator with advanced weapons training; military elite | T-KIN, T-RAY, T-BURST, T-VEC (WS-I–III with Heavy Weapon cert) |
| WU-I-C | Cultivation Weapon User | Cultivator using technology weapons alongside cultivation techniques | All types WS-I–II; T-FORM WS-I–III |
| WU-I-D | Integrated Weapon Practitioner | Integrated Practitioner (Part 31) using synergy weapons | T-SYN (all certified levels); all other types up to Integration Model ceiling |
| WU-I-E | Autonomous Combat Agent | AI-directed weapons platform operating at individual scale (drones, autonomous sentinels) | T-KIN, T-RAY only (standard); T-FORM under special restriction |

### 3.3 — Weapon Accessibility by Civilization Tier

A civilization's technology tier determines which weapons its population can access through standard manufacturing and distribution channels. Weapon accessibility follows the Technology Ceiling law (Part 8, P8-2) — a civilization cannot distribute weapons it cannot construct.

| Civilization Tier | Standard Weapon Access | Special Weapon Access |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1–2 | TB-Alpha weapons only (Shard-Throwers through Iron-Lances) | None |
| Tier 3–4 | TB-Alpha through TB-Beta (Powder-Shot through Hardstrike Round; Thunder-Charge; Fission Core at Tier 4) | Tier 4 only: Fission Core requires Special Authorization |
| Tier 5 | TB-Gamma full access; T-SYN Qi-Amplifier Frame accessible if Integration practitioners exist | Strategic ordnance (Orbital Impactor, Fusion Crown) requires Special Authorization |
| Tier 6–7 | TB-Delta full access; OW-Band weapons standard military issue | WS-V+ requires Strategic Ordnance authorization |
| Tier 8–10 | IW-Band weapons constructible; antimatter, Annihilation Beam, Soul-Sever class | WS-VI+ requires Absolute Authorization |
| Tier 11–20 | GW/CW-Band weapons; galactic and cosmological classes | WS-VII requires Civilization-Level Authorization |
