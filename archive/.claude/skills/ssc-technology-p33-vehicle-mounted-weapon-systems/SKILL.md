## Area 9 — Vehicle-Mounted Weapon Systems

### 9.1 — Vehicle Weapon Integration Governance

Weapons integrated into vehicle hardpoints (VHM configuration code) are jointly governed by Part 32 and Part 33. The governing principle is: Part 32 determines what weapon mounting capacity a vehicle has; Part 33 determines what weapons can be mounted and how they function.

| Vehicle Scale Class (Part 32) | Weapon Mounting Capacity | Maximum Mounted Weapon WS |
|---|---|---|
| SC-I (Personal Scale) | 1–2 light weapon hardpoints (WS-I–II) | WS-II maximum |
| SC-II (Light Scale) | 2–4 standard weapon hardpoints (WS-I–III) | WS-III maximum |
| SC-III (Medium Scale) | 4–8 hardpoints; 1 medium weapon bay | WS-III maximum; WS-IV with heavy configuration |
| SC-IV (Heavy Scale) | 8–16 hardpoints; 2–4 weapon bays | WS-IV maximum; WS-V with ORD configuration |
| SC-V (Capital Scale) | 16–64 hardpoints; dedicated weapon decks | WS-V maximum; WS-VI with Strategic Ordnance authorization |
| SC-VI (Fleet Scale) | Full weapon city integration; hundreds of hardpoints | WS-VI maximum |
| SC-VII (Civilization Scale) | Civilization-level weapon integration; weapon systems are part of vessel architecture | WS-VII — requires Absolute Authorization |

### 9.2 — Vehicle Configuration Code and Weapon Law

Weapon mounting on vehicles follows the vehicle's Part 32 configuration code:

| Part 32 Config Code | Weapon Mounting Rule Under Part 33 |
|---|---|
| MIL (Military) | Full weapon mounting up to SC class maximum; WS ceiling applies |
| MIL-CAP (Military-Capital) | Same as MIL with addition of ORD weapon bays for WS-V–VI |
| COL (Colony) | Non-combat; maximum WS-II defensive weapons only (point defense); no offensive weapons |
| FRT (Freight) | Non-combat; no weapon mounting permitted without reclassification |
| PAS (Passenger) | Non-combat; emergency WS-I point defense only |
| EXP (Exploration) | WS-I–II defensive weapons only; no offensive ordnance |
| DUL-H (Dual-Use Hybrid) | WS-I–III depending on civilian or militarized operational mode; required to declare mode before engagement |
| PAT (Patrol) | WS-II–III standard patrol armament; WS-IV with extended patrol authorization |

### 9.3 — CHV Soul-Interface Weapon Rules

Cultivation-Hybrid Vehicles (CHV-A through CHV-E, Part 32 Area 7) carry unique weapon governance rules because their formation arrays create spiritual-field interactions that function as T-FORM weapons when directed offensively.

**Rule 33.9.1 — CHV Formation Weapon Status:** When a CHV's hull formation arrays are actively directed at an external target in an offensive configuration, they are classified as T-FORM weapons of the CHV's tier level and governed under Part 33 weapon law simultaneously with Part 32 vehicle law.

**Rule 33.9.2 — CHV Soul-Interface Attack Classification:** A soul-interface attack using a CHV's hull-bond against an external target's spiritual architecture is classified as a T-PSI / WS-I weapon event under Part 33, simultaneously classified as a soul-technique escalation event under Part 5, Area 7.6. The two governance frameworks apply simultaneously.

**Rule 33.9.3 — CHV Weapon Maximum WS by CHV Class:**

| CHV Class | Maximum Offensive Weapon WS | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CHV-A | WS-I–II (standard vessel T-FORM via formation array) | Basic formation arrays only |
| CHV-B | WS-II–III (enhanced formation weapon arrays) | Expanded formation array scope |
| CHV-C | WS-III–IV (capital-grade formation offensive systems) | Advanced unified field weapons |
| CHV-D | WS-IV–V (civilization-level CHV offensive capacity) | Apex formation weapon integration |
| CHV-E | WS-V–VI (Absolute-tier CHV weapons) | Highest CHV weapon class |
