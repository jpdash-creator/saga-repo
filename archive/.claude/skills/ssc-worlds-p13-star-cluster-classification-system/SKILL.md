## Area 17 — Star Cluster Classification System (Cosmic Tier)

A Star Cluster is a gravitationally associated group of Star Systems sharing a regional spatial neighborhood — typically hundreds to thousands of Star Systems forming a coherent regional unit.

### 17.1 — Star Cluster Internal Structure

A Star Cluster contains: Cluster Core (highest-density central region, typically the political and cultivation center), Cluster Middle Regions (standard inhabited zone, most Star Systems and civilizations), Cluster Periphery (lower-density outer Star Systems, frontier space), and Cluster Boundaries (defined spatial extent of gravitational association). Large-scale Formation Zones, spatial anomalies, and dimensional thinning zones may be present at Cluster scale.

### 17.2 — Star Cluster Class Table

| Class | Designation | Ambient Energy Profile | Affinity Profile | Special Zone Status | Dimensional Properties | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SC-1 | Barren Cluster | Class 1 dominant | Neutral | None | Standard | A Star Cluster with no meaningful ambient energy profile at the cluster scale. Individual Star Systems may vary but the Cluster as a unit has no special character. |
| SC-2 | Sparse Cluster | Class 1–2 | Neutral | None | Standard | Minimal cluster-level energy profile. Standard in all regards. The most common unexceptional Star Cluster type. |
| SC-3 | Common Cluster | Class 2 | Neutral or mild | None | Standard | The baseline inhabited Star Cluster. Adequate ambient conditions, no special zone presence at cluster scale. |
| SC-4 | Affinity Cluster | Class 2–3 | Active affinity across significant portion of Cluster | Possible: cluster-distributed Formation Zones | Standard | A Star Cluster where a dominant affinity character manifests across multiple constituent Star Systems. The Cluster has an overall energy personality. |
| SC-5 | Rich Cluster | Class 3–4 | Neutral or affinity | Cluster-distributed Passive and Vortex Formation Zones | Standard | A notably energy-rich Star Cluster. Formation Zones distributed across constituent Star Systems at meaningful density. |
| SC-6 | Vortex Cluster | Class 4–5 | Affinity aligned | Active Vortex Zones across multiple Star Systems; Possible: Cluster-scale energy confluence | Standard | A Star Cluster with multiple active Vortex Formation Zones distributed across its constituent systems. High energy density at cluster-wide scale. |
| SC-7 | Sacred Cluster (Minor) | Class 6–7 in Sacred zones | Affinity aligned | Sacred Ground(s) of S1–S4 present in multiple Star Systems | Minor dimensional alteration near Sacred zones | A Star Cluster housing Sacred Grounds across multiple constituent Star Systems. The Sacred presence is distributed through the Cluster, not concentrated in one system. |
| SC-8 | Sacred Cluster (Major) | Class 7 in Sacred zones | Affinity aligned | Multiple S4–S7 Sacred Grounds across multiple Star Systems; Cluster-scale energy anomaly | Moderate dimensional alteration | A Star Cluster where Apex-grade Sacred presence is distributed across multiple Star Systems. Known across its Star Quadrant and into the Galaxy as a sacred region of extraordinary power. |
| SC-9 | Sacred Cluster (Apex) | Class 7 (transcendent) in sacred zones | Affinity aligned | S7–S8 Sacred Grounds in multiple Star Systems; Cluster-scale energy convergence | Significant dimensional overlap within the Cluster | An exceptional Star Cluster where the density of Apex-grade Sacred Grounds creates a cluster-wide sacred environment. The Cluster itself functions as a super-zone. Known across its Galaxy. |
| SC-10 | Dimensional Cluster | Class 4–7 | Variable | Dimensional feature of Cluster-scale magnitude — inter-system or inter-cluster Stable Rift network, Cluster-scale Resonance Plane access, or confirmed dimensional layer overlap at Cluster scale | Active dimensional layer overlap or cross-cluster connection structure | A Star Cluster distinguished by a major dimensional feature operating at Cluster scale. The dimensional structure defines the Cluster's significance independent of all other properties. |
