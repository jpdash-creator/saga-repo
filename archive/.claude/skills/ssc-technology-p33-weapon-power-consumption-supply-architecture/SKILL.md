## Area 7 — Weapon Power Consumption and Supply Architecture

### 7.1 — Weapon Energy Classification

Every weapon requires an energy source. The energy classification determines what fuel, power source, or activation mechanism the weapon uses and which governance rules apply.

| Energy Class | Designation | Source | Part 11 / Part 7 / Part 8 Cross-Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| WE-I | Mechanical Energy | Muscle power, stored mechanical tension; bow, sling, crossbow | No special governance; Tier 1–2 only |
| WE-II | Chemical Energy | Gunpowder, conventional explosive, fuel combustion | No special governance; Tier 3–4 |
| WE-III | Nuclear Energy | Fission, fusion power generation for weapon systems | Restricted access (Tier 4+); Technology authorization |
| WE-IV | Directed Energy Power System | High-output electrical/magnetic/plasma generation system; requires energy technology Type 7 support | Technology power grid or vehicle power plant; Tier 5–7 |
| WE-V | CPM Energy | Condensed Power Material as weapon fuel; graded per Part 11 | Part 11 governance (CPM grade = weapon fuel grade); Tier 3–10 hybrid |
| WE-VI | Formation Energy | Qi-derived formation array energy driving the weapon effect | Part 12 formation rules; Tier 3–11 hybrid |
| WE-VII | Annihilator Drive Energy | Matter-antimatter or exotic energy conversion at civilizational scale | Civilization-level energy infrastructure; Tier 8–14 |
| WE-VIII | Cosmological Energy | Dark matter, dark energy, vacuum energy, spacetime fabric energy | Tier 15+ only; deferred governance |

### 7.2 — CPM Weapon Fuel Grade Requirements

T-FORM, T-SYN, and some T-PSI weapons use CPM as their primary fuel source. CPM grade requirements are governed jointly by Part 11 and Part 33.

| Weapon Tier | Minimum CPM Grade Required | Part 11 Grade Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 3–4 | Power Crystal (Grade 1–4) | Part 11 Grade 1–4 |
| Tier 5 | Power Gem (Grade 5–8) | Part 11 Grade 5–8 |
| Tier 6–7 | Power Shard (Grade 9–12) | Part 11 Grade 9–12 |
| Tier 8–9 | Power Core (Grade 13–16) | Part 11 Grade 13–16 |
| Tier 10–11 | Power Heart (Grade 17–20) | Part 11 Grade 17–20 |
| Tier 12+ | Substance above Power Heart tier | Part 11 expansion governance |

**Rule 33.7.1 — CPM Weapon Fuel Incompatibility:** A CPM-powered weapon that receives CPM below its minimum grade will fail to fire. It does not downgrade to a lower tier — the formation matrix or energy interface simply does not activate. Attempts to force activation with sub-grade CPM may cause internal energy feedback and weapon destruction.

**Rule 33.7.2 — CPM Weapon Overcharge Risk:** Loading CPM above the weapon's designed maximum grade creates overcharge conditions. Overcharge at +1 grade above maximum: 10% explosion probability. At +2 grades: 50% probability. At +3 grades: weapon destruction with uncontrolled CPM detonation classified as Part 11 Area 9.3 terrain hazard event.

### 7.3 — Ammunition and Magazine Governance

Technology weapons with consumable ammunition (T-KIN, T-BURST sub-types) or finite energy magazines operate under magazine governance rules.

**Rule 33.7.3 — Magazine Drain Rate:** Magazine-governed weapons must have specified ammunition loads for extended engagement narration. Unlimited-ammunition narration is only permissible for WE-IV (energy beam) weapons drawing from continuous power plant supply, and only if that power plant has been established to have operational energy reserves.

**Rule 33.7.4 — Resupply Doctrine:** Weapon resupply operations during extended campaigns are Part 22 faction logistics events, not incidental narrative details. Stockpile depletion is a strategic condition with concrete governance consequences.
