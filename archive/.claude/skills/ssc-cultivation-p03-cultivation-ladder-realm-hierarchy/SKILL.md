## Area 1 — Cultivation Ladder and Realm Hierarchy

### 1.1 — Structural Overview

Reality in the Starward Saga is divided into layered existence tiers. Each tier does not merely change the scale of a cultivator's strength — it changes the physical rules under which they operate. A higher realm is not a stronger version of a lower realm. It is a different mode of existence with different constraints, different possibilities, and different relationships to the physical universe.

The complete cultivation ladder consists of four Major Realms, each subdivided into named Cultivation Realms, each further subdivided into Levels and Sub-Levels.

**Full Ladder Overview:**

```
MORTAL REALM
  ├── Tier 1 — Human (8 stages, no energy)
  └── Tier 2 — (5 realms × 9 levels × 4 sub-levels)
      Senior → Warrior → Champion → Ruler → Monarch

XIANTIAN REALM
  └── (4 realms × 9 levels × 4 sub-levels)
      Demigod → Void God → True God → Absolute God

CORE REALM
  └── (3 realms × 9 levels × 4 sub-levels)
      Core Formation → Core Assimilation → Core Solidification

DIVINE REALM
  └── (5 realms × 9 levels × 4 sub-levels)
      Divine Transformation → Sea Formation → Heaven Treading → Divine Ascension → Divine Sea Realm
```

---

### 1.2 — Mortal Realm

**Governing Principle:** The Mortal Realm represents biological existence constrained by the durability of matter. Cultivators in this realm are flesh-and-bone beings whose strength comes from physical refinement, not from drawing upon external or internal energy sources in meaningful ways.

#### 1.2.1 — Mortal Tier 1 — The Human Stages

**The Eight Human Stages:**
```
Unawakened → Skin → Muscle → Tendon → Bone → Blood → Marrow → Soul
```

**Nature of Progression:** These stages refine the body. They do not alter metaphysical structure. A cultivator in Mortal Tier 1 is still a biological human whose capabilities are governed by the laws of physical matter. They run faster, hit harder, and endure more than an ordinary person — but they do this through pure physical excellence, not through energy manipulation.

**Allowed Capabilities in Mortal Tier 1:**
- Running, jumping, climbing, swimming
- Weapon use at full physical proficiency
- Unarmed combat and martial techniques
- Cultivation moves that rely on pure physical execution (no energy cost)
- **Energy usage is explicitly forbidden** — a Mortal Tier 1 cultivator cannot channel, emit, or sense qi as a combat resource

**Progression Logic — The Doubling Law:**
Each stage in Mortal Tier 1 is exactly **2 times stronger** than the previous stage in raw physical power output.

| Stage | Relative Power (Unawakened = 1) |
|-------|-------------------------------|
| Unawakened | 1× |
| Skin | 2× |
| Muscle | 4× |
| Tendon | 8× |
| Bone | 16× |
| Blood | 32× |
| Marrow | 64× |
| Soul | 128× |

A Mortal Tier 1 Soul cultivator is 128 times stronger in raw physical output than an Unawakened human. This represents the upper limit of what pure biological refinement can achieve.

**Transition:** Mortal Tier 1 is exited by the body achieving its maximum biological refinement threshold and beginning to channel ambient energy for the first time — the moment called "First Breathing" or "Qi Touch." This is the transition into Mortal Tier 2.

---

#### 1.2.2 — Mortal Tier 2 — The Energy Initiation Stages

**The Five Realms:**
```
Senior → Warrior → Champion → Ruler → Monarch
```

**Nature of Progression:** Mortal Tier 2 represents the mastery of body coordination with energy flow. The cultivator's physical frame, already refined by Tier 1 work, learns to circulate qi through its meridians. Power in this tier comes from the synergy between refined physical structure and the cultivator's ability to compress and direct energy. The difference between Tier 2 realms is **control efficiency and raw force**.

**Sub-Level Structure (applies to all Tier 2 realms and beyond):**
Each Realm contains 9 Levels. Each Level is subdivided into four Sub-Levels:
```
Level N — Low → Mid → High → Peak → [Level N+1 — Low]
```

The complete granular progression within a single Tier 2 realm is thus:
- Level 1 (Low, Mid, High, Peak) → Level 2 (Low, Mid, High, Peak) → ... → Level 9 (Low, Mid, High, Peak)
- 9 levels × 4 sub-levels = 36 discrete progression steps per Realm

**Inter-Realm Power Scaling:**
- **Entry of Senior (Level 1 Low)** is **5× stronger** than Mortal Tier 1 Soul. The leap from Tier 1 to Tier 2 is a category transition, not a linear step.
- **Each full Realm advance** (Senior → Warrior, Warrior → Champion, etc.) multiplies base power by **3×** at the entry boundary. That is: Warrior Level 1 Low is 3× Senior Level 1 Low in base power.

**Realm Entry Powers (in multiples of Unawakened):**
| Realm | Realm Entry Multiplier (vs Unawakened) |
|-------|---------------------------------------|
| Senior Level 1 Low | 128 × 5 = **640×** |
| Warrior Level 1 Low | 640 × 3 = **1,920×** |
| Champion Level 1 Low | 1,920 × 3 = **5,760×** |
| Ruler Level 1 Low | 5,760 × 3 = **17,280×** |
| Monarch Level 1 Low | 17,280 × 3 = **51,840×** |

### 1.3 — Xiantian Realm

**Governing Principle:** The Xiantian Realm breaks biological constraints. The body no longer merely stores and circulates qi — it becomes a channel that transmits and amplifies power that exceeds what physical matter can contain. Xiantian cultivators can output force that no physical structure unaided could generate or survive. Power and damage tolerance increase exponentially. A Xiantian cultivator can shatter rock, redirect rivers, and collapse structures without physical contact through pure energy expression.

**The Four Xiantian Realms:**
```
Demigod → Void God → True God → Absolute God
```

**Sub-Level Structure:** Identical to Mortal Tier 2 — 9 Levels per Realm, each Level with Low/Mid/High/Peak sub-levels.

**Inter-Realm Power Scaling:**
- **Entry of Xiantian (Demigod Level 1 Low)** is **5× stronger** than Mortal Tier 2 Monarch Level 9 Peak. The entire Mortal Tier 2 summit is dwarfed by the base of Xiantian.
- **Each sub-level advance within Xiantian** multiplies power by **1.5×**.

**Realm Natures:**
- **Demigod:** First break from biological limits. The cultivator's qi begins to exist outside the body as an extension of will. Aura begins to manifest physically.
- **Void God:** Qi can be projected as destructive force at significant distances. The cultivator's body passively reinforces itself against damage.
- **True God:** Cultivation energy begins to interact with environmental forces — wind, water, earth, fire. The cultivator is no longer just a powerful body; they are a localized force of nature.
- **Absolute God:** The summit of Xiantian. The cultivator's existence begins to subtly distort the space around them. Lesser cultivators feel physical pressure just from proximity. The Absolute God is the boundary between human-scale and cosmic-scale power.

---

### 1.4 — Core Realm

**Governing Principle:** Energy becomes internalized into a stable, condensed source — the Core. Cultivators in the Core Realm stop exhausting stamina in conventional ways. A Mortal or Xiantian cultivator expends qi like burning fuel; a Core cultivator has internalized an energy reactor. The Core is both source and amplifier, making sustained high-output combat possible in ways impossible at lower realms.

**The Three Core Realms:**
```
Core Formation → Core Assimilation → Core Solidification
```

**Sub-Level Structure:** Identical — 9 Levels per Realm, each with Low/Mid/High/Peak.

**Inter-Realm Power Scaling:**
- **Entry of Core Realm (Core Formation Level 1 Low)** is **5× stronger** than Xiantian Absolute God Level 9 Peak.
- **Each sub-level advance within Core** multiplies power by **1.5×**.

**Realm Natures:**
- **Core Formation:** The dantian condenses all accumulated qi into a single point — the nascent Core. This is one of the most dangerous transitions in cultivation, as an unstable Core will rupture and kill the cultivator. The Core begins as fragile but grows rapidly if the foundation is solid.
- **Core Assimilation:** The Core stabilizes and begins to harmonize with the body's meridian network. Power output becomes continuous and controllable. The cultivator can sustain combat at full output indefinitely within the limits of Core capacity.
- **Core Solidification:** The Core reaches its maximum density for the current existence tier. It becomes indestructible by any force below the Divine Realm. At this stage, the only way to meaningfully harm a cultivator is to target the Core directly — which is extraordinarily difficult.

---

### 1.5 — Divine Realm

**Governing Principle:** The Core breaks apart — deliberately, through a transcendent act of will — and its condensed energy disperses through the entire being to create divine energy. Divine energy is qualitatively different from cultivation qi. It can interact with the fundamental laws of reality: space, time, causality, embodiment. A Divine Realm cultivator does not simply fight — they rewrite the rules of the engagement.

**The Five Divine Realms:**
```
Divine Transformation → Sea Formation → Heaven Treading → Divine Ascension → Divine Sea Realm
```

**Sub-Level Structure:** Identical — 9 Levels per Realm, each with Low/Mid/High/Peak.

**Inter-Realm Power Scaling:**
- **Entry of Divine Realm (Divine Transformation Level 1 Low)** is **5× stronger** than Core Solidification Level 9 Peak.
- **Each sub-level advance within Divine** multiplies power by **1.5×**.

**Realm Natures:**
- **Divine Transformation:** The Core shatters. Divine energy floods the cultivator's body. This transformation is catastrophic if not carefully managed — many cultivators die at this threshold. Those who survive emerge as beings that no physical object or energy below the Divine Realm can meaningfully harm.
- **Sea Formation:** Divine energy extends beyond the body, forming a permanent "divine sea" — an external reservoir of metaphysical power that surrounds and protects the cultivator. This sea can be weaponized, used for sensing at vast distances, and deployed as a spatial barrier.
- **Heaven Treading:** The cultivator's feet no longer need to touch ground. Gravity is optional. Space itself bends slightly around their presence. They can step through localized spatial folds to cover extreme distances instantaneously.
- **Divine Ascension:** The cultivator begins to transcend individual existence. Their presence becomes a force that affects reality at large scale — weather patterns shift, animals flee, lesser cultivators' qi systems destabilize involuntarily. Death from this cultivator is not always caused by direct action.
- **Divine Sea Realm:** The apex of the current ladder. A Divine Sea Realm cultivator is a singular existence — an entity whose power has no meaningful comparison to anything below the Divine Realm. Their continued existence in the mortal world is itself a distortion. The universe strains to accommodate them.

### 1.7 — Phase-Appropriate Realm Vocabulary Mandate

**Rule 1.7.1 — Realm Vocabulary Is Bounded by Location Power Ceiling.**
Prose must not reference cultivation realms, sub-realms, or named cultivation stages that are inaccessible to the in-scene power ceiling of the location where the prose is set. The in-scene power ceiling is established by the governing location ledger (per Part 13 and the location's entry in `ledger-worlds-location/`).

**Rule 1.7.2 — Three Allowed Exceptions.**
A reference to a realm above the in-scene ceiling is permitted only under one of the following exceptions, and the exception must be identifiable from the prose context:
- **(a) Verified In-Canon Exposure:** The character speaking, thinking, or remembering has verified in-canon exposure to those realms. Example: Hercules and Bison, formerly of Olympus, may reference Xiantian and Divine vocabulary as memory of their pre-banishment lives. The ledger entries for such characters must list a **Permitted External Realm References** block.
- **(b) Flashback / Vision / External-Realm Sequence:** The prose is explicitly rendering a flashback, vision, scrying, dream, prophecy, or sequence set in a location whose ceiling permits those realms. The shift of setting must be narratively clear.
- **(c) Canonical Plot-Point Reveal of Ceiling Itself:** The reference is a canonical plot-point reveal about the power ceiling (e.g., the Firmament Lock's existence, an NPC explaining why higher realms are inaccessible, a prophecy that names higher realms as the reader's future horizon).

**Rule 1.7.3 — Phase 1 / Sol Star System Application (Locked Canon — Two-Layer Ceiling Model).**

The Sol Star System operates under **two distinct ceilings** that must never be conflated for the purpose of Rule 1.7 prose-vocabulary enforcement:

**Layer 1 — System Physical Ceiling (All Phases, per `sol-star-system.md` Firmament Lock):**
- **Cultivation:** Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid — the absolute physical cap for any cultivator born or residing in the Sol Star System, imposed by the Firmament Lock.
- **Per-World Variants within the System:** Saha planetary cap = Absolute God Level 8 High; Moon of Saha local cap = Core Formation Level 3 High; Hoth local cap = Core Assimilation Level 3 Mid (matches the system ceiling).
- These physical ceilings govern **what exists in-world** and do not change Phase to Phase.

**Layer 2 — Phase 1 Narrative Ceiling (per Part 30 Phase 1 governance):**
- **Cultivation:** **Ruler Level 9 early** — the upper narrative bound for any character (protagonist, antagonist, or background NPC) appearing in Phase 1 prose set inside the Sol Star System.
- This ceiling governs **what appears on the page during Phase 1** and is the ceiling used for Rule 1.7 prose-vocabulary enforcement during Phase 1 drafting.

**Rule 1.7 Application During Phase 1 Prose Drafting (Sol Star System Settings):**

In-system POV characters in Phase 1 prose may reference:
- **Mortal Tier 1** — Unawakened, Skin, Muscle, Tendon, Bone, Blood, Marrow, Soul
- **Mortal Tier 2** — Senior (L1–L9), Warrior (L1–L9), Champion (L1–L9), Ruler (L1–L9 early only)

Forbidden in Phase 1 prose (Sol Star System settings, no active exception):
- **Mortal Tier 2 above the narrative ceiling:** Ruler Level 9 Mid through Ruler Level 9 Peak; all Monarch levels
- **Xiantian:** Demigod, Void God, True God, Absolute God
- **Core Realm:** Core Formation, Core Assimilation, Core Solidification
- **Divine Realm:** Divine Transformation, Sea Formation, Heaven Treading, Divine Ascension, Divine Sea Realm

*Note: Monarch and the higher bands of Ruler exist in-world within the Sol Star System up to the physical ceiling; they simply do not appear on the page in Phase 1 prose. Abstract reference to "the Firmament Lock's ceiling" or "the realms beyond" without naming specific forbidden realms is permitted under Rule 1.7 exception (c). Naming a specific forbidden realm still requires an exception.*

Later Phases will raise the Layer 2 narrative ceiling toward (and eventually through) the Layer 1 physical ceiling per Part 30 expansion; Rule 1.7.3 will be extended Phase-by-Phase as Part 30 is authored.

**Rule 1.7.4 — ALCA Audit Coverage.**
This rule is audited under the **AL-6 Cultivation Alignment** dimension of the Action Law + Ledger Correctness Audit (Part 1 Area 8.13). A prose unit that references a forbidden realm without qualifying under an allowed exception constitutes an ALCA Mode A failure and must be corrected before the prose is committed to `.docx` / `.md` output files.

**Rule 1.7.5 — Pre-Write Check Requirement.**
Before drafting any prose unit, the Pre-Write Ledger Check (Part 1 Area 6.2) must include an explicit statement of: (i) the in-scene location, (ii) the location's Power Ceiling, (iii) the Permitted Realm Vocabulary for the unit, and (iv) any operative Permitted External Realm References derived from the in-scene POV character's ledger.

**Rule 1.7.6 — Bold-Italic Formatting Mandate for Cultivation Level Designations in Prose.**
Every qualifying cultivation level designation that appears in the Prose body must be rendered in ***bold-italic*** (triple-asterisk in Markdown; character-level bold + italic simultaneously in .docx). This is a companion mandate to Rule 1.7.1 (vocabulary scope) — not only must the vocabulary be in-ceiling, but when it appears, it must be formatted correctly. The four qualifying categories are governed by Part 2 Area 3 Rule 3.2.1a and Part 29 Rule 29.2.1.1d:

- **Full realm+level+sub-level strings** — e.g., ***Ruler Level 3 Mid***, ***Champion Level 7 Peak***
- **Full affinity+level designation strings** — e.g., ***Sovereignty-Swords Level 8*** (partial refs without level number are plain text)
- **Tier designations** — e.g., ***Mortal Tier 2***, ***Mortal Tier 1 Skin Stage***
- **Named meridian designations** — e.g., ***Void Root***

**Standalone realm names** (e.g., Champion, Warrior, Ruler, Monarch, Core Solidification, Champion-realm) are plain text in all prose contexts — both when characterizing a character's cultivation level and when used in technical power-level descriptions. They are not bold-italic and are not checked by Gate 1 Check #12 or Gate 2 Rule 10.5.8.

A qualifying cultivation level designation that appears in Prose body without bold-italic formatting is a Gate 1 Check #12 FAIL (Part 1 v2.23) and blocks `[CONFIRM WRITE]`.
