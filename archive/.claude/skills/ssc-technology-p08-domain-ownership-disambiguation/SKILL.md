## Area 13 — Domain Ownership and Disambiguation

### 13.1 — What Part 8 Governs

Part 8 is the governing authority for:

- The complete 20-Tier Civilization-Technology Scale and all tier definitions
- The fourteen technology type classification domains and their twenty internal levels
- Civilizational tier classification (Civ-1 through Civ-20) and its correspondence to sustainable technology tiers
- The technology-side thresholds for cultivation-technology integration (complementing Part 7's cultivation-side thresholds)
- Technology-side requirements for Technology-Relic Hybrid Classes
- Technology's interface with CPM (technology-side rules; Part 11 governs the substance itself)
- Technology Force Category classification in combat (technology-side; Part 5 governs the combat framework)
- Technology narration standards and capability rendering requirements
- The Soft Correspondence Tendency Table between technology tiers and cultivation realms

### 13.3 — Disambiguation: Boundary Cases

**Q: Who governs a cultivator using a Tier 7 weapon in combat?**
A: Part 5 governs the combat framework and force legitimacy. Part 8 governs the weapon's tier classification and its cultivation-equivalent combat output (Area 8.2). Part 6 governs whether weapon use constitutes a Named Move analog. All three apply simultaneously.

**Q: Who governs CPM being used to power a Tier 8 machine?**
A: Part 11 governs the CPM substance. Part 8 governs the technology-side interface rules (Area 7). Part 7 governs whether this constitutes cultivation-technology integration and at what model. All three apply; Part 8 and Part 11 jointly govern the energy interface; Part 7 governs the integration classification.

**Q: Who governs whether a civilization's Technology-Relic Class 3 system is valid?**
A: Part 7 governs the fusion process rules. Part 8 governs the technology-tier prerequisites (Area 6.3). Part 12 governs the relic object layer. All three must be satisfied simultaneously.

**Q: Who governs whether a civilization can advance from Tier 7 to Tier 8?**
A: Part 9 governs the growth mechanics and timeline. Part 10 governs the scientific prerequisites. Part 8 (Areas 5 and 10) governs the civilizational and resource prerequisites.

**Q: Is a Tier 8 technology civilization REQUIRED to have Divine-realm cultivators?**
A: No. Part 8 P8-2.1 explicitly establishes that the Technology Ceiling is never determined by cultivation realm. A civilization of entirely Mortal-tier cultivators can operate at Tier 8 technology if it has satisfied the civilizational prerequisites (Area 10). The Soft Correspondence Tendency Table (Area 4.2) describes what tends to co-occur in typical civilizations — it does not prescribe what must co-occur. An all-Mortal-cultivation Tier 8 civilization is exceptional and narratively significant, but not a rule violation.

**Q: Does a higher cultivation realm give a cultivator control over higher-tier technology?**
A: Higher cultivation realm gives a cultivator greater capacity to interface with Cultivation Integration Technology (Type 12) systems designed for their realm. It does not automatically grant the ability to operate arbitrary high-tier technology. A Divine-realm cultivator in a Tier 3 civilization has no special ability to use Tier 8 weapons systems — they simply don't exist in that civilization's Technology Ceiling. If a Tier 8 weapon is present, the Divine cultivator can attempt to use it (as any Tier 8 system operator would), but their cultivation realm provides no automatic advantage in operating technology outside their civilization's CI Technology architecture.

**Q: Who governs the ambient energy density of a technologically enhanced cultivation environment?**
A: Part 8, Area 7.3 establishes what technology tier can produce what ambient energy effects. Part 13 (Location Law — fully authored, Areas 1–23) governs the spatial structures involved: Energy Formation Zones (Part 13 Area 14) define the spatial architecture of cultivation-enhanced environments; Sacred Ground grade (Part 13 Area 13) is the natural benchmark that technology-enhanced environments approximate. Part 11 governs any CPM component. The technology-side of the effect is Part 8; the spatial-structure side is Part 13; the substance-side is Part 11; the synergy output is Part 31 Area 10.

**Q: Does the new 20-Tier scale invalidate existing narrative arcs using the old 12-tier references?**
A: Existing narrative content that referenced specific tier numbers using the 12-tier scale should be understood as referencing the anchor tiers that remain in the 20-tier scale. Old Tier 6 (Solar System) = new Tier 8. Old Tier 7 (Galactic) = new Tier 11. Old Tier 8 (Multi-Galactic) = new Tier 13. Old Tier 9 (Universal) = new Tier 16. Old Tier 10 (Multi-Universal) = new Tier 18. Old Tier 11 (Dimensional) = new Tier 19. Old Tier 12 (Absolute) = new Tier 20. The intermediate tiers represent new narrative and world-building space between the anchor points.
