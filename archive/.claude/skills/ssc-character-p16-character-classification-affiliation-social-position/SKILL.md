## Area 11 — Character Classification by Affiliation and Social Position

### 11.1 — Affiliation-Based Character Types

A character's affiliation position (Part 23) and social class (Part 25) combine to determine their structural position in the social order. This creates a distinct set of character types defined not by what they can do but by where they stand.

**Affiliation-Based Character Types:**

| Affiliation Type | Description | Narrative Potential |
|-----------------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Sect Loyalist** | Full AFF-1 alignment with their sect; their identity is their affiliation | LBI-primary ideology; identity crisis if sect is destroyed |
| **Independent Wanderer** | No active faction affiliation; operates solo | FSI-primary ideology; information class dependent on personal reputation |
| **Alliance Member** | Belongs to inter-faction alliance; dual loyalty navigation | LBI + OMI tension; alliance obligation creates story constraints |
| **Vassal Member** | Member of a vassal faction; subordinate obligation | LBI/SCI covenant tension; power asymmetry is their daily reality |
| **Spy / Double Agent** | Surface affiliation contradicts true affiliation | Strategic hypocrisy ideology; Part 24 governance; highest narrative tension |
| **Ghost Branch Member** | Unknown member of a distant parent faction | Part 23 ghost branch mechanics; A-Class elevated but invisible |
| **Exiled Character** | Expelled from all affiliations; all social structures have rejected them | IS-4/IS-5 risk; often VAI or ADI reconstruction ideology |
| **Rogue Cultivator** | Cultivator without sect; may have stolen techniques; operates outside sect social norms | FSI or ADI; complex legal status |

### 11.2 — Social Class Character Profiles

The six social class axes (Part 25) produce distinct character profiles when combined:

| Dominant Class Axis | Character Type Profile | Examples |
|--------------------|----------------------|---------|
| **CC-Class dominant** | Power defines everything; cultivation realm is the primary identity | Combat cultivators, Sect Masters, martial culture figures |
| **W-Class dominant** | Wealth is the organizing principle; resources command respect | Merchant Princes, Commerce Guild Directors, Resource Monopolists |
| **L-Class dominant** | Lineage and bloodline govern social reality | Noble House members, Sect lineage disciples, Royal families |
| **I-Class dominant** | Information access is power; knowledge governs | Intelligence Directors, Sage-level scholars, Tier I information custodians |
| **A-Class dominant** | Network position defines the character's reality | Diplomatic envoys, alliance brokers, political operators |
| **Ac-Class dominant** | Achievements and discovery define standing | War heroes, first-discovery scientists, legendary technique creators |
