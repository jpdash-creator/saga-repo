## Area 8 — Empire Classification System

An Empire is a multi-territory political entity under unified imperial authority, spanning greater territorial reach and wielding greater organizational power than a Kingdom at equivalent class. Empires are defined by the scope of their governance mandate and the character of their power.

### 8.1 — Empire Class Table

| Class | Designation | Territorial Scale | Dominant Character | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E-1 | Nascent Empire | Continental | Any | A newly unified Empire still consolidating absorbed Kingdoms. Administrative infrastructure forming. Legitimacy still being established. |
| E-2 | Consolidated Empire | Multi-continental | Any | Full continental governance established. Multiple Kingdoms under direct authority. Imperial army and bureaucracy consolidated. |
| E-3 | World Empire | Full World | Any dominant character | An Empire spanning its entire World. All major competing polities absorbed or subordinated. World-level politics orbit this Empire. |
| E-4 | System-Adjacent Empire | World + orbital | Technology-augmented or Hybrid | Controls a World plus its orbital bodies, moons, and near-space infrastructure. Approaching Star System scope. |
| E-5 | System Empire | Full Star System | Any dominant character | A true Star System-spanning Empire. Multiple Worlds under imperial governance. The dominant power in its Star System. |
| E-6 | Multi-System Empire | 2–10 Star Systems | Any dominant character | Controls multiple Star Systems. Known across its Star Cluster as a major power. Interstellar military projection capability established. |
| E-7 | Cluster Empire | Star Cluster | Any dominant character | A Star Cluster-spanning Empire. Among the defining political entities of its Star Quadrant. Ancient institutional history. |
| E-8 | Multi-Cluster Empire | Several Star Clusters | Any dominant character | One of the dominant Empires in its Star Quadrant. Multiple legendary Star Systems under governance. |
| E-9 | Quadrant Empire | Full Star Quadrant | Any dominant character | A Star Quadrant-spanning Empire. History spans tens of thousands of years of institutional development. One of the great powers of its Galaxy. |
| E-10 | Galactic Empire | Galaxy-scale | Any dominant character | A Galaxy-scale Empire of mythological political weight. Its history and the Galaxy's history are intertwined. Encountered in narrative as a background epoch-defining force or direct antagonist at the highest tier. |
