## Area 14 — Ruins Zone Governance

A Ruins Zone is any location that contains significant physical, energetic, or informational remnants of a prior civilization or cultivation tradition at a higher development level than what currently occupies the location.

### 14.1 — Ruins Zone Properties

- Ruins Zones may contain dormant formation arrays (Part 12) that were constructed by prior occupants.
- They may contain residual ambient cultivation energy from prior occupants — ancient cultivation residue that slowly dissipates over millennia.
- They may contain artifacts, relics, or CPM deposits that have accumulated since the prior civilization's departure or collapse.
- Their classification grade reflects the developmental level of the prior civilization, not the current occupants.

### 14.2 — Ruins Zone Narrative Rules

**Rule 14.2.1 — Ruins Are Discovery Zones:** A Ruins Zone is always a potential narrative discovery site. What remains is not always immediately visible. Excavation, research, and high-level formation sense are required to fully assess a Ruins Zone's contents.

**Rule 14.2.2 — Dormant Formations May Reactivate:** Ancient formation arrays in a Ruins Zone may reactivate if triggered — by qi exposure of the right affinity, by deliberate activation, or by external energy events. Reactivation of a major ancient formation is a CSS-5+ consequence event.

**Rule 14.2.3 — Ruins Cannot Be Fully Depleted:** A Ruins Zone's informational content (cultural records, historical inscriptions, architectural knowledge) cannot be exhausted — only lost if the physical ruins are destroyed.
