## Area 12 — Side Character Information Architecture

### 12.1 — What Side Characters Know and Don't Know

Every side character operates within the information constraints of their position — governed by Part 24 (Information and Secrecy Law). A Tier A side character who is a Core Disciple of a mid-tier sect knows certain things about the world, their faction, and their arc context; they do not know things outside their information access.

**Rule 19.12.1.1 — Side Character Information Must Be Position-Appropriate:** A side character cannot know things that their cultivation realm, faction position, and information access do not permit them to know. Information they should not have constitutes either a Part 24 breach event (they obtained it through intelligence means) or a canon violation.

**Rule 19.12.1.2 — Side Character Knowledge Gaps Are Narrative Assets:** What a side character does not know is as important as what they know. A Mentor who does not know about a threat the villain represents cannot prepare the hero for it — which is a valid and valuable structural gap. Side character knowledge gaps create story problems that the hero must solve independently.
