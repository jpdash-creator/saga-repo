## Area 22 — Cross-Scale Information Governance

### 22.1 — Information Governance at Cosmological Scale

As faction and civilization scale increases (SCL-9 through SCL-16, CCT-10+), information governance takes on qualitatively different properties:

**Galactic-Scale (SCL-11, CCT-11):** Information infrastructure at galactic scale requires distributed relay networks whose maintenance is itself a strategic military priority. A galactic faction that loses its core information relay infrastructure loses coherent governance capability within years. The destruction of relay nodes is accordingly treated as an act of civilizational warfare rather than tactical sabotage.

**Galaxy Cluster and Above (SCL-12+, CCT-12+):** At this scale, the information lag between cluster-separated territories (Mly distances) creates de facto governance autonomy for regional sub-factions. Even with FTL communication, the sheer volume of information flowing across cluster-scale empires exceeds any central analysis capability. AI analysis infrastructure (Part 8 Type 4 at Level 11+) becomes critical — the civilization is governing based on AI-synthesized summaries rather than primary data review.

### 22.2 — Information Governance Below CCT-3

At CCT-1 through CCT-2 (Subsistence through Industrial), information governance is simple and brutal:

- The courier and herald system carries all information
- The most powerful information resource is a cultivator with a large soul sense domain — their passive surveillance represents the most advanced intelligence capability available at this tier
- Technique secrecy is maintained by master-disciple loyalty culture and the physical security of formation jade slips
- Most factions at this tier have no formal intelligence function — intelligence is gathered by cultivators who happen to use their soul sense during normal activity, and by social networks of informants maintained through personal loyalty relationships
