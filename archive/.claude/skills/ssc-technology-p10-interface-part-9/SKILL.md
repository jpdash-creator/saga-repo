## Area 10 — Interface with Part 9 (Growth Law)

### 10.1 — The Governance Split Restated

Part 10 governs the WHAT of scientific knowledge. Part 9 governs the HOW of scientific discovery. This split is fundamental and must be maintained rigorously to prevent governance confusion:

| Question | Governing Part |
|---|---|
| What theoretical understanding is required to advance to Tier X? | Part 10 |
| How long does it take to achieve that understanding? | Part 9 |
| What events accelerate achieving that understanding? | Part 9 |
| What events prevent achieving that understanding? | Part 9 |
| What specific discoveries mark reaching that understanding? | Part 10 |
| What cultivation contributions are possible to that discovery process? | Part 9 (timing) + Part 10 (content) + Part 31 (synergy outputs) |

### 10.2 — Updating Part 9's Category I Tables

With Part 10 now authored, Part 9's Tier Transition Prerequisite Reference Table (Area 4.2) Category I entries are fully active. The *[P10 stub]* notations in Part 9 Area 4.2 are superseded by this Part's governance. Authors using Part 9 to determine advancement prerequisites should consult both Part 9 (for timelines and mechanics) and Part 10 Area 3 (for Category I scientific knowledge content).

### 10.3 — Part 9 Areas That Reference Part 10

The following Part 9 areas have direct Part 10 governance interfaces:
- **Part 9, Area 2.1 (Systematic Research):** The "Hypothesis Formation" step references existing knowledge and scientific prerequisites; the specific existing knowledge standards are Part 10's domain.
- **Part 9, Area 2.2 (Experimental Accident):** The "discovery must still be integrated into a theoretical framework" requirement is Part 10's domain.
- **Part 9, Area 2.3 (Cultivation-Assisted Discovery):** The observation must be integrated into a Part 10 scientific framework; Part 10 Area 7 governs the specific integration rules.
- **Part 9, Area 4.2 (Tier Transition Prerequisite Table):** All Category I entries are now governed by Part 10 Area 3.2.
- **Part 9, Area 4.3 (Domain-Specific Notes):** Domain-specific science prerequisites are now governed by Part 10 Area 4.
- **Part 9, Area 6.3 (Accidental Discovery Acceleration):** The specific threshold of what constitutes a "paradigm-shifting" discovery is Part 10's domain (Area 2.3 Phase 4 event).
