## Area 10 — Side Character and the World

### 10.1 — Side Characters as World Architecture

Beyond their individual narrative functions, the ensemble of side characters in the Starward Saga collectively forms the **human architecture of the world**. Through the side character ensemble, the world is populated by individuals rather than types — by specific people whose specific existence in specific factions and specific relationships makes the world feel inhabited.

**Rule 19.10.1.1 — The Side Character Ensemble Must Span the World's Scale:** The side character ensemble should not all come from the same faction, the same cultivation tradition, or the same social class. The ensemble must represent the breadth of the world's systems — cultivation and technology, elite and civilian, sect-based and independent, high-CCT civilization and low-CCT civilization.

**Rule 19.10.1.2 — Side Characters as Civilizational Representatives:** Many Tier B and Tier C characters are representatives of larger systems — the village elder who represents an entire civilization's relationship to cultivation, the refugee who represents a civilization destroyed by the villain, the sect archivist who represents an institution's accumulated knowledge. These characters make abstract systems human and immediate.

### 10.2 — Side Characters and the Hero's World Expansion

Part 17 Growth Arc Phase 3 (The Widening World) is structurally supported by side characters — it is through new side characters encountered at higher scales that the hero's world expands. New Mentors at higher realms, new Allies from larger factions, new Rivals from civilizations the hero has never encountered — these are the mechanism by which Phase 3 is narratively delivered.

**Rule 19.10.2.1 — World Expansion Side Characters Must Be World-Specific:** A new side character encountered in Phase 3 must reflect the specific qualities and concerns of the higher-scale world the hero has entered. A Core Formation cultivator from a galaxy-spanning sect carries a different relationship to their cultivation, their world, and their ideology than a Core Formation cultivator from a village sect. The specificity of their world must be present in their character.
