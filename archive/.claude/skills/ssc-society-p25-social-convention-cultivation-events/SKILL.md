## Area 20 — Social Convention for Cultivation Events

### 20.1 — Breakthrough Social Protocol

Breakthroughs are the most important social events in cultivation civilization. The following social conventions surround them:

**Pre-Breakthrough:**
- The cultivator announces their cultivation session to their master or senior peers (obligation in CPT-1 cultures; courtesy in CPT-2; optional in others)
- Guards are posted to prevent interference (violation of guard post is equivalent to assault)
- The sect or community enters a state of quiet anticipation (in CPT-1 and CPT-6 cultures)

**During Breakthrough:**
- Absolute non-interference obligation (P25, Universal Taboo, Area 11)
- In CPT-1 cultures: no qi use nearby that might interfere with breakthrough energetics
- In CPT-2 cultures: protective watching as honor duty
- Breakthrough in a Sacred Ground: community holds vigil and records the event in the Chronicle

**Post-Breakthrough:**
- The new breakthrough level is formally announced and publicly acknowledged
- In CPT-1 cultures: realm-ascending ceremony; presentation of new-realm appropriate resources (CPM grade, cultivation technique, relic)
- In CPT-2 cultures: immediate challenge from a peer of the new realm (to test and acknowledge the new capability)
- In CPT-6 cultures: covenant renewal ceremony (the breakthrough is interpreted as divine confirmation)

### 20.2 — Death and Funeral Conventions

Death convention in cultivation civilizations is far more complex than in mortal-lifespan societies because the expected lifespan, the social roles of the deceased, and the cultural weight of their accumulated experience vary enormously by realm.

**Mortal Realm Death:** Conventional by world culture (varies by CPT). Short-form mourning (3–7 days standard).

**Core Formation Death:** Significant community loss. Formal mourning period of one moon cycle. Chronicle entry mandatory. Master-disciple chain obligation: the surviving disciples must collectively document their master's techniques and teaching before the mourning period closes.

**Core Solidification and Above:** Civilizational event. Extended mourning period (one year formal, one generation informal). Major Chronicle entry. In CPT-1 cultures, the cultivation site of the deceased becomes a minor sacred location. The cultivation technique lineage enters formal protection status.

**Heaven Soul and Divine Sea Death:** Cosmos-scale cultural event. Multi-civilization mourning protocol. The cultivation site may become a Sacred Ground (Part 13 Area 14.3). Cultural canonization is immediate in CPT-1 and CPT-6 cultures.
