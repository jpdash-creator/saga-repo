## Area 16 — Consequence Governance in Non-Cultivator Characters

### 16.1 — Ordinary Person Consequences

Non-cultivating characters follow the same consequence framework as cultivators but without cultivation-specific consequences (no realm regression, no Heart ceiling, no soul technique consequence) and with proportionally lower power amplification. Their consequence profiles are nonetheless complete — the relational, psychological, and systemic consequence domains apply fully.

**Specific Non-Cultivator Consequence Amplifications:**
- Physical vulnerability: ordinary people bear more severe physical consequences for physical violence because they have less physical resilience
- Social consequence amplification: in power-stratified societies (Part 21), ordinary people have more severe social consequences for any action that brings them into conflict with cultivators or high-technology actors — the power asymmetry means their consequence ceiling is higher in adverse scenarios
