## Area 13 — Narration Standards

**N28-1 — The Full-Canon Pre-Execution Standard.**
Before any significant creative act is committed to prose, conduct the full simultaneous multi-Part check as specified in Area 10. No creative act of narrative consequence may bypass this check.

**N28-2 — The Prose Depth Standard.**
Every prose unit must simultaneously satisfy all ten Named Depth Pillars (DP-1 through DP-10) of the Pacing-Appropriate Depth Standard (Area 4; 3500%–5000% range) and must meet the pacing-determined word count floor (minimum 2000 words; per Part 2 Area 6.2.1). Thin prose in any dimension — compressed combat (DP-1), dropped subtext (DP-2), expository lore delivery (DP-3), sensory vacuum (DP-4), flat emotional assertion (DP-5), imprecise power rendering (DP-6), consequence-free events (DP-7), cultural register slip (DP-8), single-sentence Named Move (DP-9), or orphaned facts (DP-10) — is a Canon violation identical in severity to a power-ceiling continuity error. The check for prose depth across all ten Pillars is as mandatory as the check for power ceiling compliance.

**N28-3 — The Name-Consistency Standard.**
All invented proper nouns — locations, factions, characters, techniques, events, customs — must be consistent with the linguistic and cultural register of the producing culture per Part 25. Names that impose anachronistic contemporary Earth linguistic patterns are prohibited.

**N28-4 — The Establishment-Immutability Standard.**
Once Negotiable Canon content is established in prose, it is immutable. The author must track established Negotiable Canon content and prevent contradiction. The Hero Ledger, Villain Ledger, and Side Character Profile are the primary tracking mechanisms for character-level Negotiable Canon.

**N28-5 — The Emotion-Grounding Standard.**
Every emotional state must be rendered with its causal roots visible in prior prose, its full physical and behavioral texture, and its appropriate duration. Asserted emotion is prohibited. Causally disconnected emotion is prohibited. Instantaneous resolution of significant emotional wounds is prohibited.

**N28-6 — The Tactical-Consistency Standard.**
Every tactical choice must be consistent with the character's established power level, psychological state, ideology, and the environmental and situational context of the scene. Tactical surprise is required; causally unsupported surprise is prohibited.

**N28-7 — The Cultural-Consistency Standard.**
Every invented cultural element — event, custom, tradition, taboo, governance form, political conflict — must be consistent with the Part 25 CPT of the culture producing it. Cultural content that contradicts the fundamental logic of its producing CPT is prohibited.

**N28-8 — The Retroactive-Enrichment Standard.**
Retroactive enrichment of earlier established content must be consistent with all prior implications of that content. Enrichment that feels retroactively contradictory to what was established is prohibited even when it does not formally violate a specific Canon law.

**N28-9 — The Authorization-First Standard.**
Any creative act designated as Tier 2 under Part 28's Authorization Protocol (Area 9) must complete the Authorization Standard — foreshadowing, structural grounding, consequence preparation — before execution. Tier 2 acts executed without authorization preparation are the most severe class of Canon violation.

**N28-10 — The Behavioral-Complexity Standard.**
No character may be rendered as a simplified execution of their dominant trait. Every character must be rendered with the full complexity of their governed profile — the gap between ideology and emotion, between public behavior and private experience, between what they say and what they mean. Robotic behavioral rendering is prohibited.

**N28-11 — The Scene-Completeness Standard.**
Every scene must achieve at minimum: one character revelation, one relationship development, one world-building contribution, and one consequence. Incomplete scenes must be extended or merged until the completeness standard is satisfied.

**N28-12 — The Cascade-Consequence Standard.**
When a creative act produces a consequence cascade — CSS events triggering IE events triggering PIS events triggering behavioral changes — the full cascade must be rendered in the prose. Consequence chains may not be truncated at the first link for the sake of narrative pace. Pace is built through prose craft, not through consequence omission.

**N28-13 — The Novel-Register Standard (Em-Dash and Contraction Prohibition).**
The Starward Saga is a novel, not a code comment, a journalism article, or a governance document. Its prose must read with the weight, formality, and cadence of a novel-grade literary series. Two typographical and lexical habits belong to non-novel registers and are therefore categorically prohibited in the Prose body:

- **No Em-Dash (—).** The em-dash character is prohibited across all Prose body text. When the authorial impulse reaches for an em-dash, the prose must instead construct a full clause, open a colon where the second clause specifies the first, use a semicolon where two clauses are closely related, use a comma where the interruption is light, use parentheses where the interruption is a genuine aside, or restructure the sentence to remove the need for any interruption. Em-dashes compress what novel-grade prose must render through completed clause architecture; their pervasive use is a register collapse into the cadence of commentary.
- **No Contractions.** Apostrophe-elided forms (*haven't, isn't, don't, I'm, you're, it's, that's,* and all equivalents) are prohibited across all Prose body text, dialogue and narration alike. Every contracted form must be rendered in its full expanded form. The expanded form carries the weight appropriate to a cultivation epic; the contracted form carries the casual register of contemporary conversational journalism and is categorically incompatible with the Starward Saga voice.

N28-13 is authored in Part 29 Rules 29.2.1.1b and 29.2.1.1c and cross-referenced in Part 2 Rule 2.3.7. Audit Pass 1 and Audit Pass 2 (Part 1 Rule 7.1.1(k)) both FAIL if the em-dash character or any prohibited contraction is found in the Prose body. Scope of "Prose body" is the text between the Prose Title Block and end of the Prose file; governance artefacts (Position Header Blocks, Chapter Header Blocks, Area Dividers, Prose Title Blocks, Audit Reports, ledger tables, and all Part-file structural content) are outside scope and may continue to use the em-dash and contractions where structural clarity benefits from them.

**N28-14 — The Dialogue Format Standard (Double Quotation Marks and Italics).**
All direct speech in the Prose body is rendered in **double straight quotation marks AND italics simultaneously** per Part 29 Rule 29.2.1.1 and Part 2 Rule 2.2.2. The canonical rendering is *"the exact spoken words"* with the quotation marks inside the italic span. Italics-only dialogue is prohibited. Double-quoted dialogue without italics is prohibited. Single quotation marks for primary speech are prohibited (they are reserved for nested reported speech per Part 29 Rule 29.2.1.1a). N28-14 is a character-voice standard and applies to every Prose under every prose-producing command; violation is an Audit Pass 1 and Pass 2 FAIL under Part 1 Rule 7.1.1(k).
