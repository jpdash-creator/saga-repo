## Area 15 — World Classification System (Cosmic Tier)

A World is a self-contained planetary body — complete or capable of supporting civilization. World class is determined purely by the intrinsic properties of the World itself: its energy class, affinity profile, special zone status, and dimensional character. Who lives on the World, what they have built, or what political structure governs it does not determine World class.

### 15.1 — World Type Taxonomy

Before World class assignment, each World carries a Type designation describing its dominant characteristic.

| World Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Green World | Biosphere-dominant. Rich ecosystems, diverse life, high biological productivity. Neutral or Life/Wood affinity dominant. |
| Cultivation World | Cultivation energy-rich. The World's natural energy profile makes cultivation natural and productive. Often Class 3+ ambient energy without any special zone. |
| Technology World | Resource-rich for technological development. Dense mineral deposits, stable physics, high-energy materials. Technology civilizations emerge naturally here. |
| Beast World | Dominated by powerful beast populations. Human civilization exists in tension with beast civilizations. Beast cores are major resources. |
| Elemental World | Defined by extreme elemental conditions — volcanic, perpetual storm, deep ocean, frozen tundra, pure desert. Strong single-affinity dominance. |
| Ruins World | Hosted a higher-tier civilization now significantly degraded. Ancient ruins, dormant formations, historical artifacts, residual energy. |
| Void World | Minimal ambient energy — naturally energy-depleted. May have resources but requires significant effort to cultivate here. |
| Fractured World | Unstable spatial-dimensional properties. Reality inconsistent — safe zones alternate with dimensional shear zones. Extreme danger but potentially extreme resources in stabilized pockets. |
| Integration World | Both cultivation and technology have achieved genuine co-development at high levels. Exceptionally rare. |
| Sacred World | Sacred Ground conditions spread across a significant portion or majority of the planet. The most powerful cultivation World type. Extremely rare. |

### 15.2 — World Class Table

| Class | Designation | Ambient Energy Class | Affinity Profile | Special Zone Status | Dimensional Properties | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W-1 | Barren World | Class 1 | Neutral | None | Standard | No meaningful ambient energy. No special zones. A raw planetary body with basic physical properties only. Resource value possible but cultivation environment negligible. |
| W-2 | Sparse World | Class 1–2 | Neutral | None | Standard | Low ambient energy. Basic conditions for survival. No Formation Zones of note. Standard physical laws. |
| W-3 | Common World | Class 2 | Neutral or mild | None | Standard | The baseline inhabited World. Adequate ambient energy for ordinary cultivation without special advantages. Standard in every regard. |
| W-4 | Affinity World | Class 2–3 | Active affinity | None or Passive Formation Zone | Standard | A World with a pronounced environmental affinity that defines its character. The affinity shapes every system on the planet. |
| W-5 | Rich World | Class 3–4 | Neutral or affinity | Passive Formation Zone(s) | Standard | A notably energy-rich World. Passive Formation Zones distributed across its geography. Favorable cultivation environment without unusual spatial properties. |
| W-6 | Vortex World | Class 4–5 | Affinity aligned | Active Vortex Formation Zone(s) | Standard | A World with active Vortex Formation Zones — concentrated, potent energy confluences. The Vortex sites define the World's political and cultivation geography. |
| W-7 | Sacred World (Minor) | Class 6–7 | Affinity aligned | Sacred Ground (S1–S3) | Minor dimensional alteration near Sacred Ground | A World hosting a confirmed Sacred Ground of lower grade. The Sacred Ground's influence zone transforms a portion of the World. The rest of the World exists in its shadow. |
| W-8 | Sacred World (Major) | Class 7 | Affinity aligned | Sacred Ground (S4–S6) | Moderate dimensional alteration in Sacred Ground zone | A World where a major Sacred Ground of high grade has fundamentally shaped the World's cultivation geography. Known across its Star System and Star Cluster. |
| W-9 | Sacred World (Apex) | Class 7 (transcendent) | Affinity aligned | Sacred Ground (S7–S8) | Significant dimensional overlap in Sacred Ground zone | A World hosting an Apex-grade Sacred Ground. The Sacred Ground's influence zone covers vast portions of the planet. The World is a destination known across its Star Quadrant or Galaxy. Physical reality in the Sacred Ground zone operates under partially non-standard laws. |
| W-10 | Dimensional World | Class 5–7 | Variable | Special dimensional feature (Stable Rift, Resonance Plane Access, or Extradimensional Convergence) | Active dimensional layer overlap or connection point | A World distinguished by a major dimensional feature independent of Sacred Ground status. A stable dimensional rift, a Resonance Plane access point, or a confirmed overlap of physical and qi dimensions at planetary scale. Dimensional Worlds are uniquely significant regardless of their energy class. |
