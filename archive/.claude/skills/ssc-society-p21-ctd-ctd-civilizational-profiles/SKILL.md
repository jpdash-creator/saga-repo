## Rule Text (Verbatim Source — Authoritative)
## Area 8 — CTD-by-CTD Civilizational Profiles

Each Civilization Tier Designation has a detailed civilizational profile — the characteristic social structure, narrative texture, and world-building identity that that tier represents.

---

### CCT-1 — Subsistence

**Social Structure:** Tribal; elder-based; kin-network governance. Population ranges from dozens to low thousands. Every individual is known to every other. Cultivation is instinctual or inherited from elders — no formal training structures. Technology is hand-crafted, pre-industrial, seasonally dependent.

**Narrative Texture:** The world is immediate, personal, and dangerous. The horizon is a mystery. Cultivation power is magic to non-practitioners. Technology is hand-tool scale. Authority is personal reputation and elder status.

**Cultivation Character:** Scattered individuals may have achieved Mortal Tier 2–3; no formal cultivation inheritance. CPM is unknown or treated as sacred found-objects.

**Technology Character:** Stone tools through early bronze/iron equivalents (Tier 1). No systematic knowledge preservation.

**Energy Character:** Fire, wind, water, animal labor. CPM unknown.

---

### CCT-2 — Formative

**Social Structure:** City-states, early kingdoms, organized religion as governance. Formal law beginning. Trade networks between settlements. Population in low millions. Cultivation begins its first institutional expression: small sects, cultivation families.

**Narrative Texture:** The world is growing — cities rise, roads connect settlements, trade brings strangers into contact. Cultivation practitioners are local legends. Technology is increasingly systematic. The first cultivation academies are appearing.

**Cultivation Character:** Mortal Tier 3–4 practitioners known; Xiantian rare and legendary. First formal sects transmitting cultivation techniques.

**Technology Character:** Early industrial equivalents (Tier 2–3). Written records. Early metallurgy mastered.

**Energy Character:** Early CPM (Power Crystal Grade 1–2) known as rare resources, possibly not yet understood as cultivation fuel.

---

### CCT-3 — Industrial

**Social Structure:** Nation-states, empires, established governance hierarchies. Bureaucracy functions. Inter-state conflict and diplomacy at scale. Population in hundreds of millions. Cultivation sects are major social institutions — comparable in influence to religious bodies.

**Narrative Texture:** The world is industrial. Cities have millions. Information moves faster than armies. Cultivation sects compete, form alliances, and influence politics. Technology is disrupting traditional power structures. The first significant CA/TA integration experiments are occurring.

**Cultivation Character:** Xiantian Grade practitioners are common among cultivation families; Core Formation rare but known. Cultivation academies teach structured techniques.

**Technology Character:** Industrial through early digital (Tier 3–4). Standardized production, national infrastructure.

**Energy Character:** Power Crystal (Grades 1–5) known as cultivation resources; early CPM as technology fuel experiments beginning.

---

### CCT-4 — Planetary (Type I)

**Social Structure:** Global or continental governance. The first planetary-scale institutions. International law beginning. Cultivation and technology are both established as societal pillars; neither has defeated the other. Population in billions.

**Narrative Texture:** The world is both vast and connected. Space travel is beginning. The planet feels smaller. Cultivation sects operate globally and compete for planetary-scale territories. Technology threatens traditional cultivation power structures — a Tier 4 army can threaten a Core Formation cultivator under the right conditions.

**Cultivation Character:** Core Formation accessible to dedicated practitioners; Divine Realm legendary. Large-scale cultivation academies with multi-generational inheritance.

**Technology Character:** Global digital infrastructure through early space access (Tier 4–5). Nuclear and early fusion equivalents.

**Energy Character:** Power Crystal reliable at civilization scale; early Gem-grade CPM known.

---

### CCT-5 — Multi-World (Type I+)

**Social Structure:** Multi-world governance frameworks. The first interplanetary administration. Population spans multiple worlds. Cultivation sects establish satellite academies on new worlds. Technology companies and cultivation sects begin the first systematic partnerships.

**Narrative Texture:** Characters can be born on different worlds than they live on. Travel between worlds is expensive but achievable. Sacred Ground construction becomes a major institutional goal. Technology is now a genuine partner to cultivation rather than a competition.

**Cultivation Character:** Core Formation widely known; early Divine Realm presence. Planet-scale cultivation academies with interplanetary chapters.

**Technology Character:** Interplanetary transportation, early interstellar probes (Tier 5–6). Automation, life extension beginning.

**Energy Character:** Gem-grade CPM accessible at faction scale; early CPM-technology power systems deployed.

---

### CCT-6 — Solarian (Type II−)

**Social Structure:** Inner stellar system governance. Administration of orbital habitats, asteroid belts, inner planets. The void between worlds is routine transit, no longer a frontier. Population of hundreds of billions across inner system.

**Narrative Texture:** Space is a texture of daily life — not a frontier. Sacred Grounds are being constructed and competed over across the inner system. Technology-relic hybrid devices are standard equipment for elite practitioners and high-tier military units.

**Cultivation Character:** Divine Realm practitioners represent an accessible elite. Divine Sea Realm known but rare. Formation-level techniques being pioneered.

**Technology Character:** Inner solar system complete mastery (Tier 6–7). Megastructures beginning. Partial stellar energy capture.

**Energy Character:** Gem-grade civilization-wide; Shard-grade accessible at major faction level.

---

### CCT-7 — Stellar (Type II)

**Social Structure:** Full stellar system governance. Outer system reaches administered. The civilization is recognizably unified at stellar scale — a single civilization-entity with stellar-scale identity. Cultivation and technology have achieved deep mutual investment; Integrated Practitioners (Part 31, Area 12) are a recognized social class.

**Narrative Texture:** The sun is an owned thing — captured, managed, optimally output. The outer reaches of the stellar system are mapped and governed. Sacred Grounds exist on multiple worlds and orbital platforms.

**Cultivation Character:** Divine Realm broadly accessible to cultivation-focused practitioners. Early Divine Sea Realm practitioners known. Formation-level cultivation taught at major academies.

**Technology Character:** Full stellar system mastery; Dyson-scale energy capture (Tier 7–8). Megastructures operational.

**Energy Character:** Shard-grade CPM civilization-wide; Core-grade accessible at major institutions.

---

### CCT-8 — Interstellar (Type II+)

**Social Structure:** First interstellar reach. The civilization has extended its governance to at least one neighbor star system. The awareness that the universe contains other civilizations is now a lived reality, not a philosophical hypothesis. Diplomatic or military first contact protocols are active.

**Narrative Texture:** The civilization is waking to the scale of the universe. FTL travel is achievable (Tier 8+ technology per Part 10 FTL Framework). Communication lag to the new star system is real and politically significant. The expansion frontier is alive with conflict, discovery, and opportunity. This is the dawn of interstellar identity.

**Cultivation Character:** Divine Sea Realm practitioners present and institutionally important. High Divine Realm techniques in active development.

**Technology Character:** Early interstellar travel at FTL threshold (Tier 8–9). Part 10 FTL Physics Framework prerequisites met.

**Energy Character:** Core-grade CPM accessible at major factions; Shard-grade reliable civilization-wide.

---

### CCT-9 — Expansionary

**Social Structure:** Multi-stellar governance spanning 6–50 Star Systems. The civilization is flooding across the void — too large to centrally govern, requiring federated or distributed authority structures. Inter-stellar administration is the primary governance challenge. Communication infrastructure at light-year to kly scale (Part 14 CMS) defines the limits of what can be centrally governed.

**Narrative Texture:** Different stars have different sub-cultures, different dominant cultivation paths, different technology priorities. The central authority is distant — weeks or months of FTL travel away. Frontier star systems develop independent identities. The civilization is in the process of becoming vast.

**Cultivation Character:** High Divine Realm cultivators known; Primordial Realm individuals beginning to appear in legends and records.

**Technology Character:** Multi-stellar transportation networks (Tier 9–10). Advanced FTL logistics.

**Energy Character:** Core-grade CPM civilization-wide; limited Divine Liquid access at exceptional Sacred Grounds.

---

### CCT-10 — Clusteral (Type IIb)

**Social Structure:** Stellar cluster governance. The civilization commands thousands to hundreds of thousands of star systems. Sub-civilizational political entities exist within the civilization's territory — faction-states at Stellar or Interstellar scale, governing star systems within the larger Clusteral entity. The civilization's total identity is a complex mosaic of sub-civilizations.

**Narrative Texture:** The civilization is a cosmos in itself. Internal diversity is staggering — cultivation traditions from a thousand worlds, technology schools from a hundred sub-civilizations, multiple languages and cultural identities maintained within the larger unity. Authority and legitimacy are constantly negotiated.

**Cultivation Character:** Primordial Realm cultivators known and institutionally present. Divine Sea Realm represents a significant leadership class.

**Technology Character:** Cluster-scale infrastructure (Tier 10–11). First galactic technology beginning.

**Energy Character:** Core-grade CPM civilization-wide; Divine Liquid accessible at exceptional locations.

---

### CCT-11 — Galactic (Type III)

**Social Structure:** Galactic civilization. A single governance entity administering a complete galaxy. The Galactic tier implies the apex of normal civilizational ambition — most civilizations across the universe exist at CCT-1 through CCT-10, and the Galactic is seen as the peak of "normal" civilizational development. Model D synergy operational at civilization scale.

**Narrative Texture:** The civilization is a universe unto itself. Internal cultures, cultivation traditions, and technology schools represent evolutionary branches that diverged over millions of years of civilizational history. The Galactic civilization may be older than the recorded history of most other civilizations.

**Cultivation Character:** Primordial Realm accessible to elites. High-Primordial individuals known. Phase Realm is a theoretical discussion.

**Technology Character:** Galactic-scale infrastructure (Tier 11–12). Reality-manipulation technology beginning.

**Energy Character:** Divine Liquid accessible civilization-wide. Near-theoretical CPM efficiency.

---

### CCT-12 through CCT-20 — Deep Tiers

Tiers 12 through 20 represent civilizational scales that exceed what standard narrative can depict without Part 28 authorization for direct participation. These tier levels are world-building context — the background of the universe within which the Starward Saga's stories occur.

**CCT-12 (Intergalactic):** Spans multiple galaxies in the Local Group. Governance architecture that can administer tens of galaxies simultaneously. CPM production at Divine Liquid scale civilization-wide.

**CCT-13 (Cosmological):** Galaxy cluster governance. A repository of civilizational history and diversity. The civilization has absorbed or subsumed multiple former Galactic-class entities.

**CCT-14 (Superclusteral):** Supercluster scale. The distances that once defined civilizational limits are now administrative corridors bridging what was once thought uncrossable.

**CCT-15 (Liminal-Universal):** The observable universe becomes the territory. CCT-15 civilizations stand at the threshold of cosmological governance. Part 21 P21-1 comes into full expression here — civilization is the structural expression of collective capability, and at CCT-15, that capability approaches cosmological scope.

**CCT-16 through CCT-20:** Successive transcension of universal (Type IV), trans-universal, multi-universal (Type V), dimensional, and absolute scales. At CCT-20 (Absolute), civilizational existence is indistinguishable from the substrate of reality. These tiers are named and described; they are not narratively accessible without Part 28 authorization.
