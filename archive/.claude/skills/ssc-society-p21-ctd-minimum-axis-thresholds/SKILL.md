## Area 5 — CTD Minimum Axis Thresholds

| Industrial | 3 | CA-3 | TA-3 | EA-3 | LA-3 | SA-0/1 | Industrial forging; first expansion |
| Planetary | 4 | CA-4 | TA-4 | EA-4 | LA-4 | SA-1 | Planetary/continental governance |
| Multi-World | 5 | CA-5 | TA-5 | EA-5 | LA-5 | SA-2 | First lift-off; multi-world beginning |

| Stellar | 7 | CA-7 | TA-7 | EA-7 | LA-7 | SA-4 | Full stellar system administration |
| Interstellar | 8 | CA-8 | TA-8 | EA-8 | LA-8 | SA-5 | Early interstellar; first great reach |
