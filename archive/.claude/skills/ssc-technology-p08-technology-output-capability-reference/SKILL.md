## Area 9 — Technology Output and Capability Reference

### 9.1 — The Technology Narration Benchmark System

As the Heavenly Scale (Part 3, Area 3) provides narration benchmarks for cultivation power in prose, the Technology Narration Benchmark System provides equivalent reference anchors for technology capability descriptions. Authors use this system to calibrate technology narrative descriptions against established world-law capability parameters.

Technology output is described across three narration categories: **Structural Output** (what the technology can create or destroy physically), **Energy Output** (what energy-level effects the technology produces), and **Information Output** (what information-processing scale the technology operates at).

### 9.2 — Structural Output Reference by Tier

| Tier | Structural Destruction Benchmark | Structural Creation Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cannot destroy stone structures without exceptional effort and time | Simple shelters; primitive fortifications |
| 2 | Can breach stone walls with sustained effort | City-scale stone architecture; aqueducts |
| 3 | Can destroy large stone fortifications; industrial explosives | Industrial cities; railways; national infrastructure |
| 4 | Can destroy any conventional structure; nuclear weapons cause localized planetary scarring | Skyscrapers; dams; global infrastructure networks |
| 5 | Significant craters in planetary crust; early orbital bombardment | Space elevators; orbital stations; arcologies |
| 6 | Affects inner planetary body geology; multi-world simultaneous strike capability | Inner solar system habitats; terraforming initiation |
| 7 | Can damage outer planetary moons and deep-space structures | Outer system habitats; complete stellar system infrastructure |
| 8 | Can affect or destroy stellar-scale structures; stellar detonation possible | Dyson sphere components; terraformed worlds; Ringworld prototypes |
| 9 | Can affect multiple stellar systems simultaneously | Multi-stellar megastructures |
| 10 | Can affect stellar cluster-scale phenomena | Stellar cluster infrastructure |
| 11 | Galaxy-scale structural effects; galactic topology alteration | Galactic megastructures; complete galaxy infrastructure |
| 12 | Local Group-scale structural effects | Cross-galactic megastructures |
| 13 | Multi-galactic structural effects | Galaxy cluster infrastructure |
| 14 | Supercluster-scale effects | Supercluster-spanning megastructures |
| 15 | Near-universal structural effects | Cosmological-scale engineering |
| 16 | Universal-scale structural effects | Spacetime architecture modifications |
| 17 | Trans-universal structural effects | Cross-universal architecture |
| 18 | Multi-universal structural effects | Multi-universal megastructures |
| 19–20 | Dimensional and absolute effects | Dimensional and absolute construction |
