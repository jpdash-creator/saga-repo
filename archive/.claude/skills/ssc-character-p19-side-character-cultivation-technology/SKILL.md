## Area 13 — Side Character Cultivation and Technology

### 13.1 — Side Character Power

All side character cultivation power and technology tier follow the same Canon laws as heroes and villains. There are no governance exceptions for side characters.

**Rule 19.13.1.1 — Mentor Power Credibility:** A Mentor figure must have cultivation or technology capability that genuinely exceeds the hero's in the relevant domain at the time of mentorship. A Core Formation cultivator who is mentoring a Xiantian True God hero is credible in cultivation terms. A Core Formation cultivator who is presented as capable of teaching a Divine Sea hero without an explicit exception (special area of mastery, formation array expertise, etc.) is not credible.

**Rule 19.13.1.2 — Side Character Breakthroughs Are Canon Events:** When a Tier A side character achieves a cultivation breakthrough or technology tier advancement, this is a canon event. It must be the product of the same requirements as hero breakthroughs (Part 3) and must be narratively present — not simply noted in passing.

**Rule 19.13.1.3 — Side Character Power Calibration Shapes the World's Scale:** The cultivation and technology levels of the side character ensemble collectively calibrate the reader's understanding of the world's power scale. Mentors who are three realms above the hero establish what "experienced" looks like. Rivals who are one realm above establish what "achievement" the hero is striving toward. The ensemble's power levels must be internally consistent with the world's cultivation and technology governance.
