## Area 14 — The Flexibility Audit Protocol

### 14.1 — Purpose of the Flexibility Audit

The Flexibility Audit is the structured process by which the author evaluates a planned creative act against the full SSC Canon before committing it to prose. It is not a bureaucratic formality — it is the discipline that makes creative freedom safe. A creative act that clears the Flexibility Audit with full simultaneous-compliance verification is a creative act that the Canon supports. An act that fails the Audit is an act that must be modified before execution.

### 14.2 — The Five Audit Questions

Before executing any significant creative act, the author applies five questions:

**Audit Question 1 — Category or Instance?**
Is this creative act inventing a new instance within an existing governed category (permitted), or is it inventing a new category not authorized by the Canon (prohibited)? If the answer is "new category," the act must be referred back to the Canon for authorization.

**Audit Question 2 — What Parts govern this act?**
List every Part that has jurisdiction over this creative act. Be exhaustive. Then verify that the act complies with each governing Part simultaneously.

**Audit Question 3 — What does this act establish?**
Identify every piece of Negotiable Canon content that this act would fix in place. Verify that none of this content contradicts previously established Negotiable Canon. Verify that all of it is consistent with the Locked Canon of all governing Parts.

**Audit Question 4 — What consequences does this act trigger?**
Identify all CSS events, IE events, and PIS events that this act produces. Verify that all triggered consequences will be rendered in the prose with appropriate specificity and duration. Identify any cascade effects that must also be rendered.

**Audit Question 5 — Is Part 28 Authorization required?**
Is this act on the Tier 2 Authorization list (Area 9.2)? If yes, verify that the Authorization Standard has been satisfied before execution.

### 14.3 — Audit Failure Responses

When the Flexibility Audit reveals a compliance failure:

**Minor Failure:** The creative act violates one governing Part in a way that can be corrected by adjusting the act without changing its essential narrative purpose. Correct the adjustment and re-audit.

**Major Failure:** The creative act violates multiple governing Parts or violates a locked governance principle in a way that cannot be corrected without fundamentally changing the act. Return to narrative design and approach the same story goal through a different, compliant creative path.

**Authorization Failure:** The creative act is Tier 2 and the Authorization Standard has not been satisfied. Halt execution, return to prior scenes to build the required narrative preparation, and re-audit when the Authorization Standard is satisfied.

### 14.4 — Author Quick Reference — Common Compliance Checks

| Creative Act | Primary Governing Parts | Common Failure Modes |
|---|---|---|
| Character cultivation breakthrough | Parts 3, 4, 16, 17/18/19, 26, 27 | Bypass of breakthrough requirements; failure to trigger PIS/IE/CSS events; failure to update Ledger |
| New Named Move deployed | Parts 3, 4, 5, 6, 29 | Power exceeds realm ceiling; affinity incompatible; escalation tier not matched; announcement dialogue violates Part 29 |
| New faction introduced | Parts 22, 25, 21, 16, 23 | No CPT assigned; SCL inconsistent with location tier; FPT implausible against CCT; affiliation implications not addressed |
| Character arrives at new location | Parts 13, 15, 14 | Travel time inconsistency; location-class capability violated; CMS distances not honored |
| Tactical surprise deployed | Parts 5, 3, 8, 26, 29 | Power used exceeds established capability; psychological state makes tactic implausible; escalation tier violated |
| Cultural event described | Parts 25, 22, 21, 29 | CPT inconsistency; venue inconsistent with location tier; dialogue register does not match ceremonial context |
| Emotional reversal rendered | Parts 26, 20, 17/18/19, 27 | No causal grounding in prior prose; PIS impact not triggered; IE event not triggered; Ledger not updated |
| Alliance shift between factions | Parts 23, 22, 20, 27 | Establishing events not rendered; ideological motivation implausible; CSS consequence not triggered |
| Weapon deployed | Parts 33, 5, 14, 26 | WS class exceeds available technology tier; range implausible against Part 14; escalation tier not honored; Part 26 psychology not reflected in operator behavior |
| Relic used or revealed | Parts 12, 3, 4, 27, 28 | Tier 5 Primordial deployed without Part 28 authorization; power output exceeds realm ceiling; Soul Bond consequence not tracked |
