## Area 23 — Cross-Location Interaction Law

Effects, perceptions, and energies do not cross location boundaries without defined mechanisms. This Area governs those mechanisms.

### 23.1 — Perception Across Location Boundaries

- Soul sense range by cultivation realm is governed by Part 14 (Distance Law, Area 4.1). It does not automatically cross World or Star System boundaries — perception is bounded by range.
- Technology sensor range by tier is governed by Part 14 (Area 5.1). Technology sensors at sufficient tier can perceive across Star System and Star Cluster scales.
- Sacred Ground ambient energy does not extend beyond its influence zone (Area 9.2). Sacred Ground presence cannot be perceived at arbitrary distance.

### 23.2 — Energy Exchange Across Location Boundaries

- Ambient energy does not "leak" from one Star System to another without a specific energy conduit — a Formation Zone river, a dimensional rift, or a deliberately constructed cultivation infrastructure.
- Formation Zone substance formation is bounded by the Formation Zone's spatial extent. Resources do not flow across boundaries without extraction.

### 23.3 — Cross-Location Combat and Consequence

- Combat effects are bounded by range (Part 14) and technique scope (Part 5).
- Cultivation aura effects extend within the active range of the cultivator's soul sense — they do not propagate beyond that range unless a specific technique governs the extension.
- Consequence of events at one location can affect other locations only through defined causal chains — political consequence, economic consequence, cultivation energy distribution change.
