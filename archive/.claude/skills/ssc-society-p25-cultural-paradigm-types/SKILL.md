## Area 9 — Cultural Paradigm Types

### 9.1 — The Seven Canonical Cultural Paradigm Types

Across the Starward Saga cosmos, seven fundamental Cultural Paradigm Types (CPT) repeat at every scale — from village to galaxy. Each CPT is a coherent cultural logic: a set of values, a prestige economy, a taboo architecture, and a social convention system that hang together as a whole. Most cultures are hybrids of two or three CPTs; pure single-CPT cultures are rare and tend to be extreme.

### 9.2 — CPT-1: Hierarchical-Sacral

**Core Logic:** Power from above (cultivation realm, divine mandate, ancestral authority) flows downward and must be honored by those below. The hierarchy is sacred — not merely political but metaphysically justified.

**Values:** Deference to superiors; ancestor veneration; ritual purity; hierarchical loyalty; cultivation advancement as spiritual achievement.

**Prestige Economy:** Cultivation realm first; lineage second; institutional position third.

**Taboo Architecture:** Direct challenge to superior (unless formal challenge protocol invoked); questioning the sacred justification of the hierarchy; cultivation deception (claiming higher realm than one has); master betrayal.

**Social Conventions:** Bow depth scales to realm gap; no uninvited qi-sense use on superior; always stand when superior enters; offer seat before speaking; address by full title and realm designation on first address.

**Common In:** Cultivation Sects (CS-F); Theocratic civilizations; Ancient-tradition lineages.

### 9.3 — CPT-2: Meritocratic-Martial

**Core Logic:** Position and prestige are earned through demonstrated excellence, especially combat excellence. No birth advantage is permanent — it must be backed by achievement. The worthy defeat the unworthy.

**Values:** Combat excellence; demonstrated ability; honest challenge; respect for the genuinely skilled regardless of origin; rejection of unearned privilege.

**Prestige Economy:** Recent achievement first; current demonstrated capability second; reputation for consistent excellence third.

**Taboo Architecture:** Claiming victories not one's own; refusing an honest challenge from a peer; hiding behind position rather than ability; purchasing rank without earning it.

**Social Conventions:** Formal challenge protocol (must be accepted or acknowledged); post-combat healing obligation to defeated; battlefield commendation formula; do not interrupt another's cultivation session.

**Common In:** Military/Security Factions (MSF); Martial cultivation sects; Meritocratic governments.

### 9.4 — CPT-3: Mercantile-Exchange

**Core Logic:** All value — social, spiritual, and political — is ultimately exchangeable. Relationships are investments. Honor is credit. Betrayal is default. The market determines worth.

**Values:** Contract fulfillment; calculated fair dealing; reputation as the primary asset; profitable relationships; pragmatic flexibility.

**Prestige Economy:** Wealth and credit rating first; network value second; contract fulfillment record third.

**Taboo Architecture:** Contract violation without remedy; false weight or measure; reputation exploitation; debt refusal.

**Social Conventions:** Contract sealing ceremony before major dealings; explicit statement of terms before agreement; gift-as-investment transparency; price negotiation as social ritual (it is rude to accept the first price).

**Common In:** Mercantile factions (MTF); Trading civilizations; Port and gate-city cultures.

### 9.5 — CPT-4: Isolationist-Purity

**Core Logic:** The community's unique identity and cultivation path must be preserved against contamination from outside. Cultural purity is the highest value; contact with the outside world is managed carefully or resisted entirely.

**Values:** Lineage purity; tradition preservation; self-sufficiency; suspicion of outside influence; guardianship of exclusive knowledge.

**Prestige Economy:** Length of unbroken lineage first; depth of tradition mastery second; record of cultural preservation third.

**Taboo Architecture:** Adopting outside practices without traditional sanction; marrying outside the community; sharing exclusive cultivation methods with outsiders; accepting gifts that create obligation to outsiders.

**Social Conventions:** Extended evaluation period before trusting outsiders; no shared meals until trust is established; complex and opaque courtesy system that outsiders invariably violate; silence as a legitimate response to questions about internal affairs.

**Common In:** Extreme Specialization civilizations; Ancient isolated sects; Sealed Sacred Ground communities; Extinction-survivor lineages.

### 9.6 — CPT-5: Syncretic-Adaptive

**Core Logic:** The strongest culture is one that selectively absorbs the best of every tradition it encounters while maintaining a flexible core identity. Cultural boundaries are permeable; synthesis produces strength.

**Values:** Open inquiry; flexible identity; learning from the defeated as from the victorious; multiple cultural frameworks held simultaneously; synthesis as mastery.

**Prestige Economy:** Breadth of mastered traditions first; synthesis achievement second; cross-cultural bridge-building capacity third.

**Taboo Architecture:** Cultural chauvinism (claiming one's native tradition is inherently superior); refusing to learn from a tradition one has defeated; cultural mimicry without genuine understanding.

**Social Conventions:** Deliberate adoption of the guest culture's greeting form when meeting; explicit acknowledgment of cultural learning sources; no permanent cultural allegiance oaths (flexibility must be preserved).

**Common In:** Balanced Practitioner (BP) civilizations; Exploration factions (EXF); Diplomatic factions (DPF); Gate-network cosmopolitan cultures.

### 9.7 — CPT-6: Theocratic-Covenant

**Core Logic:** The community exists in covenant relationship with a higher power — divine, primordial, or cosmic. Adherence to the covenant terms (doctrinal, ritual, behavioral) is the supreme social obligation. All other values are subordinate to covenant maintenance.

**Values:** Doctrinal purity; ritual precision; covenant renewal; divine service; martyrdom as ultimate honor; heresy as ultimate crime.

**Prestige Economy:** Doctrinal authority first; ritual mastery second; covenant service record third.

**Taboo Architecture:** Apostasy (leaving the covenant); heresy (corrupting the doctrine); ritual impurity; covenant-breaking behavior; consorting with the covenant's declared enemies.

**Social Conventions:** Ritual greeting formula (must be performed correctly); doctrinal affirmation before major decisions; communal prayer/meditation ceremony; exclusion of covenant violators from social participation.

**Common In:** Religious/Ideological factions (RIF); Theocratic civilizations; Sacred lineage traditions.

### 9.8 — CPT-7: Anarchic-Sovereign

**Core Logic:** Individual sovereignty is the supreme value. No authority not individually consented to is legitimate. Community exists as voluntary association. The strongest rule, but only by the consent of those who recognize them.

**Values:** Individual capability; self-sufficiency; voluntary association; non-coerced loyalty; strength respected, weakness accepted.

**Prestige Economy:** Individual demonstrated capability first; voluntary following size second; reputation for honoring voluntary commitments third.

**Taboo Architecture:** Coercing loyalty; claiming authority not individually recognized; punishing individuals for refusing association; betraying voluntarily given loyalty.

**Social Conventions:** Explicit consent before cooperation; no assumed obligation from association; direct communication of terms; combat challenge as the ultimate dispute resolution.

**Common In:** Frontier civilizations; Criminal underworld (CUF, TSF) factions; Isolated sect survivors; Galaxy-edge frontier communities.
