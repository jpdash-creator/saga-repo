## Area 3 — Cultivation Travel: Mortal Tier 1 and Mortal Tier 2

### 3.1 — Mortal Tier 1 Travel

**Travel Type:** Pure physical enhancement — no qi, no flight, no environmental interaction beyond normal biology.

| Stage | Base Overland Speed | Peak Burst Speed | Jump Height | Notable Capability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unawakened | ~5 km/h walking, ~15 km/h sprint | N/A | Normal human | No cultivation-enhanced movement |
| Skin | ~6 km/h walk, ~18 km/h sprint | N/A | Slightly above human | Enhanced endurance |
| Muscle | ~7 km/h walk, ~22 km/h sprint | N/A | 1.5× human | Sustained sprint capability |
| Tendon | ~8 km/h walk, ~28 km/h sprint | ~40 km/h | 2× human | Spring-like movement efficiency |
| Bone | ~9 km/h walk, ~35 km/h sprint | ~55 km/h | 3× human | Shock-absorption in movement |
| Blood | ~10 km/h walk, ~42 km/h sprint | ~70 km/h | 4× human | Oxygenation sustains long-distance sprint |
| Marrow | ~10 km/h walk, ~50 km/h sprint | ~90 km/h | 5× human | Marrow energy provides burst capability |
| Soul | ~10 km/h walk, ~60 km/h sprint | ~120 km/h | 8× human | Summit of pure-physical movement |

**Travel Notes:**
- Mortal Tier 1 cultivators use horses, vehicles, and watercraft for long-distance travel exactly as normal humans would
- No aerial capability; no water-surface travel without conventional means
- Energy cost: physical fatigue only; food and water requirements

### 3.2 — Mortal Tier 2 Travel

**Travel Type:** Qi-assisted physical movement, developing toward aerial capability at upper realms.

| Realm | Overland Speed | Peak Burst Speed | Aerial Capability | Environmental Mobility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Senior | ~150 km/h sustained | ~300 km/h burst | Leaps carry seconds; cannot fly | Water-surface running (brief); wall-running |
| Warrior | ~300 km/h sustained | ~600 km/h burst | Aerial recovery; qi-assisted hop-gliding | Sustained water-surface stride; vertical surface running |
| Champion | ~500 km/h sustained | ~1,000 km/h burst | Short aerial travel (seconds of true flight) | Environmental traversal unimpeded |
| Ruler | ~800 km/h sustained | ~1,500 km/h burst | Brief true flight (tens of seconds); hover with effort | Aerial sprint across gap distances |
| Monarch | ~1,200 km/h sustained | ~2,500 km/h burst | Sustained low-altitude flight; leaves afterimages at peak | Movement creates visible wind effects |

**Travel Notes — Mortal Tier 2:**
- **Champion and below:** Still rely primarily on ground-based travel for long journeys; aerial capability is burst-only
- **Ruler:** First realm capable of genuine cross-city aerial travel; flight is possible but costly in qi
- **Monarch:** First realm where sustained aerial travel over hundreds of kilometers is operationally viable
- **Energy cost:** Moderate qi expenditure for sustained burst speeds; high expenditure for flight; normal walking/running is essentially free
- **Travel range per day at combat speed:** Senior ~2,000 km; Warrior ~4,000 km; Monarch ~15,000 km at sustained speed (not maximum burst)
- **Infrastructure use:** Mortal Tier 2 cultivators still rely heavily on horses, carriages, road networks, and watercraft for transcontinental travel — their own speed is superior for short-medium distances but infrastructure beats them over weeks-long journeys for efficiency and resource cost

### 3.3 — Mortal Tier 2 Qi Cost for Travel

**Rule 3.3.1 — Normal Pace Is Free.** Walking, casual running, and slow overland travel cost no cultivation qi. The body's physical refinement handles normal movement without tapping cultivation energy reserves.

**Rule 3.3.2 — Sprint Speed Costs Qi.** Moving at 50%+ of maximum speed begins tapping qi reserves. A Senior-realm cultivator sprinting at peak speed for sustained periods will begin to deplete their qi reserve within hours.

**Rule 3.3.3 — Flight Always Costs Qi (Mortal Tier 2).** Any aerial travel at Mortal Tier 2 is an active qi expenditure. A Ruler-realm cultivator in sustained flight will exhaust their qi reserve in minutes of combat-speed flight or hours of slower travel flight.

**Rule 3.3.4 — Depleted Travel Capacity.** A cultivator who has exhausted their qi through high-speed travel cannot immediately engage in combat at full capacity. Part 31's synergy rules and Part 3's qi recovery mechanics govern recovery rate.
