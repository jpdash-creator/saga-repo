## Area 2 — The Three Pillars of Synergy Capacity

### 2.1 — Civilization Tier as Synergy Gate

Civilization tier — classified as a **Composite Civilization Tier (CCT) with Civilization Tier Designation (CTD)** under Part 21 (Civilization Law, fully authored) — determines which categories of cultivation-technology synergy are institutionally achievable. No individual effort, regardless of cultivation realm or technology level, can access synergy categories beyond what civilization-level infrastructure supports. The CTD name (e.g., Stellar, Clusteral, Galactic) is the canonical in-world term; CCT is the structural identifier. All prior Civ-N and Hold Designation notation is superseded by CTDs per Part 21 authority.

| CCT / CTD | SA Axis (Part 21) | Accessible Synergy Categories |
|---|---|---|
| CCT-1 — Subsistence | SA-0 to SA-1 | None — no institutional capacity for integration; cultivation and technology coexist without meaningful interface |
| CCT-2 — Formative | SA-0 to SA-1 | Coordination synergy only: cultivators applying physical strength and affinity to construction; early natural selection for CPM-positive terrain |
| CCT-3 — Industrial | SA-1 to SA-2 | Basic augmentation: early CPM use as technology fuel at Power Crystal grade; cultivator expertise in precision manufacturing; soul-sense research applications |
| CCT-4 — Planetary | SA-2 to SA-3 | Model A–B threshold: CPM-powered devices; cultivation biometric monitoring; early formation array + technology hybrid infrastructure |
| CCT-5 — Multi-World | SA-2 to SA-3 | Model B fully operational: CPM-powered technology at industrial scale; cultivation-state responsive systems; Relic Class 1–2 achievable; planet-scale technology-supported cultivation environments |
| CCT-6 — Solarian | SA-3 to SA-4 | Expanded Model B: multi-world cultivation integration systems; inner-system-scale CPM infrastructure; improved affinity preservation in conversion |
| CCT-7 — Stellar | SA-3 to SA-5 | Model B–C transition: emerging symbiotic capability; outer-system integration networks; early Relic Class 3 prototypes; soul-sense research at stellar scale |
| CCT-8 — Interstellar | SA-4 to SA-6 | Model C fully operational: Relic Class 3 achievable; high-fidelity CPM conversion; cross-realm cultivation interface architecture; stellar-scale technology-enhanced cultivation zones |
| CCT-9 — Expansionary | SA-5 to SA-6 | Expanded Model C: multi-stellar integration networks; CPM conversion 72–80%; multi-stellar Sacred Ground simulation at technology infrastructure |
| CCT-10 — Clusteral | SA-5 to SA-7 | Model C–D transition: advanced symbiotic standard; early unified integration at cluster scale; CPM conversion 80–88% |
| CCT-11 — Galactic | SA-6 to SA-8 | Model D fully operational: full unified cultivation-technology systems; galactic-scale integration; CPM efficiency 85–95%; galaxy-scale ambient qi enrichment possible |
| CCT-12 — Intergalactic | SA-7 to SA-9 | Model D extended to civilization scale; synergy capabilities scale with civilization reach; CPM at near-theoretical maximum; energy substrate merging begins |
| CCT-13 — Cosmological | SA-8 to SA-10 | Deep unified integration: cultivation and technology share substrate-level energy fabric; SA-10 required for cross-dimensional synergy applications |
| CCT-14 — Superclusteral | SA-9 to SA-10 | Full multi-dimensional synergy architecture; civilization-scale energy fabric integration; CPM at theoretical maximum for non-Apex civilization |
| CCT-15+ (Apex Tier) | SA-10 (required) | Synergy transcends model classification; requires Part 28 narrative authorization; energy, cultivation, and technology operate as a unified substrate — see Part 21 Area 7 for Apex governance |

**Rule 2.1.1 — Individual Access Bounded by Civilization:** A Divine-realm cultivator in a CCT-5 (Multi-World) civilization cannot access Model C synergy regardless of personal cultivation power. The CI Technology infrastructure enabling Model C is not available to a Multi-World civilization. Individual capability cannot substitute for civilizational infrastructure in synergy access.

**Rule 2.1.2 — Civilization Tier Gap and Synergy Ceiling:** When practitioners from different civilization tiers interact, the synergy available is bounded by the lower civilization tier's infrastructure. A Divine-realm practitioner traveling from a CCT-11 (Galactic) civilization to a CCT-5 (Multi-World) civilization can access only the synergy categories Multi-World infrastructure supports. Their cultivation realm is unchanged; their synergy access is constrained by the available technology infrastructure.

**Rule 2.1.3 — Part 21 Authority (Active):** Part 21 (Civilization Law) is fully authored. The CTD / CCT (Part 21) is the canonical civilization tier classification for all synergy access determinations in this Part. The SA Axis (Part 21 Area 2) maps directly to Integration Model accessibility. Part 8 Area 5's prior Civ-N notation and all former Hold Designation names are superseded. Part 31's synergy categories remain governed by this table; civilization tier input is now governed by Part 21.

### 2.2 — Energy Tier as Synergy Depth Gate

Within each civilization tier's accessible synergy categories, the actual depth and power of synergy achievable is gated by energy access on both tracks simultaneously. The energy gate operates independently on the cultivation side (CPM grade, Part 11) and the technology side (Energy Technology domain level, Part 8 Domain 7).

**Cultivation-Side Energy Gate (CPM Grade):**
CPM grade determines which cultivation energy densities are available for technology interface. The higher the CPM grade, the deeper the synergy possible through the cultivation-technology energy interface.

| CPM State (Part 11) | Synergy Depth Enabled |
|---|---|
| Power Crystal (Grades 1–5) | Foundation synergy: CPM as supplementary technology fuel; basic cultivation energy detection by technology; cultivation-speed monitoring; minimal affinity interaction |
| Gem (Grades 6–10) | Augmentative synergy: reliable CPM-powered technology at meaningful output; affinity-responsive technology outputs; cultivation-environment technology enhancement begins |
| Shard (Grades 11–14) | Symbiotic threshold: CPM providing primary power for specialized high-value technology systems; affinity-preserving conversion enables affinity-specific technology outputs; formation-array-equivalent technology zones achievable |
| Core (Grades 15–19) | Deep symbiotic synergy: Core energy density enables technology systems with cultivation-realm-equivalent output classifications; technology-enhanced cultivation environments approaching lower Sacred Ground conditions; Relic Class 3 achievable with Core CPM supply |
| Divine Liquid (Grade 20) | Unified synergy: Divine Liquid energy interfaces with spacetime at micro-scale; Tier 11 CI Technology at this grade enables outputs that approach the fundamental energy substrate both cultivation and technology draw from at their highest tiers; Sacred Ground conditions simulatable by technology at this grade |

**Technology-Side Energy Gate (Energy Domain Level):**
Technology's ability to interact with cultivation energy, distribute CPM, and enhance cultivation environments scales with Energy Technology domain level.

| Energy Technology Level | Technology-Side Synergy Enabled |
|---|---|
| Level 1–4 | No meaningful cultivation energy interaction; technology cannot detect or interface with cultivation energy; CPM produces only physical/thermal effects when interacting with these systems |
| Level 5–7 | CPM-powered energy systems that produce qi-compatible outputs can power cultivation infrastructure (formation arrays, cultivation training chambers, sacred ground enhancement) as effectively as direct CPM use; this is the primary civilization-scale cultivation infrastructure application |
| Level 8–10 | Stellar-scale energy systems produce outputs of sufficient density to interact directly with the ambient cultivation energy field at macroscopic scales — equivalent to artificially generating mid-tier Sacred Ground conditions across a planetary or stellar region; technologically enhanced cultivation environments at civilizational scale become achievable |
| Level 11–15 | Galactic and supercluster-scale energy systems interact with the fundamental energy structure of the cosmos — the same substrate cultivation draws on at its deepest levels; galactic-scale ambient qi enrichment becomes possible; the technology-generated energy field becomes functionally indistinguishable from naturally elevated cultivation environments |
| Level 16+ | At these tiers, energy systems interact with the foundational energy substrate underlying all cultivation; the two tracks — technology energy and cultivation energy — become expressions of the same underlying force; Part 7 Models C and D become vastly more powerful and natural at these tiers because the integration interfaces are, effectively, joining two channels of the same substrate |

**Rule 2.2.1 — Both Gates Must Be Open:** The effective synergy depth is bounded by the lower of the two energy gates simultaneously — cultivation-side (CPM grade accessible) and technology-side (Energy Technology level). Access to Divine Liquid CPM in a Level 5 technology civilization does not unlock Level 11+ synergy — the technology-side energy gate is the binding constraint.

**Rule 2.2.2 — Energy Gate and Integration Model:** The energy gate constraints described here are aligned with the integration model thresholds (Area 5; Part 7 Area 6.2; Part 8 Area 6.2). They represent the same governing principle from the energy dimension: deeper integration requires higher energy access on both sides.

### 2.3 — Location as Synergy Amplifier

Location conditions (Part 13 — Location Law, fully authored Areas 1–23) modify the effective synergy available to practitioners and civilizations beyond what civilization tier and energy access alone determine. Part 13 Area 14 governs Sacred Ground formation, S-Grade 1–8 classification, and all spatial properties; Part 13 Area 14 governs Energy Formation Zone spatial governance; Part 13 P13-5 (Location Class Determines Environmental Ceiling) is the foundational spatial rule that caps the location-side synergy amplification. Part 31 Area 10 governs the synergy mechanics that operate within those locations.

**Sacred Grounds and Synergy Amplification:**
Sacred Grounds (Part 13, Area 14) are locations of naturally elevated cultivation energy. This elevated ambient energy interacts with cultivation-technology synergy in three ways:

- **Cultivation Output Amplification:** Cultivation practitioners operating in a Sacred Ground achieve higher effective cultivation output. When cultivation output feeds into a synergistic system, the Sacred Ground amplifies the cultivation-side contribution.
- **CPM Quality Enhancement:** CPM formed in or near Sacred Grounds tends toward higher grades (Part 11, Area 3). Higher CPM grade increases energy-gate accessibility for synergy.
- **Technology Synergy Resonance:** Technology systems designed for CI Technology interface (Domain 12) operating within a Sacred Ground draw on the elevated ambient cultivation energy in addition to their CPM fuel supply, effectively increasing the operational CPM availability without additional substance consumption.

**The Sacred Ground Synergy Step-Up Rule:**
When the cultivation-technology integration model threshold is being assessed for systems operating within a Sacred Ground of sufficient tier, the effective CI Technology level is treated as one level higher than the actual level for synergy depth assessment purposes only. This does not change the technology system's actual Domain 12 level or its capability in non-synergy applications. The step-up applies only to synergy depth within the Sacred Ground.

*A Tier 7 CI Technology system that would normally achieve Model B–C transition synergy achieves Model C synergy while operating within an appropriate Sacred Ground.*

**Technology Enhancement of Cultivation Environments:**
Technology can artificially produce conditions that approximate Sacred Ground cultivation environments. The quality of approximation scales with technology tier and CI Technology level:

- Tier 5–7 Energy Technology with Level 5 CI Technology: produces cultivation environments equivalent to training-grade elevated areas — approximately 15–25% above ambient cultivation speed
- Tier 8–10 Energy Technology with Level 8 CI Technology: produces environments equivalent to low-grade Sacred Ground conditions — approximately 40–70% above ambient cultivation speed
- Tier 11+ Energy Technology with Level 11+ CI Technology: produces environments that approach mid-grade Sacred Ground quality for broadly distributed cultivation use — beyond this tier, the ambient qi enrichment effects begin to rival naturally occurring Sacred Ground conditions

**Rule 2.3.1 — Natural Sacred Grounds Remain Superior at Equivalent Tier:** Naturally occurring Sacred Grounds of equivalent Grade (as defined by Part 13, Area 13 [citation note: Sacred Ground Grade is defined at Part 13 Area 14, per this file's own 2.3 section above]) produce cultivation effects superior to technology-generated equivalents. Technology-generated cultivation environments are consistent, scalable, and deployable on demand — but they do not replicate the resonance properties unique to naturally formed sacred sites. Natural Sacred Grounds retain irreplaceable advantages for certain cultivation breakthroughs that specifically require natural formation energy.

**Rule 2.3.2 — Combined Sacred Ground + Technology Synergy:** Operating technology-enhanced cultivation infrastructure within a natural Sacred Ground produces multiplicative rather than merely additive benefits. The natural Sacred Ground's ambient cultivation energy enriches the technology-delivered cultivation environment, and the technology system's stable energy delivery strengthens the Sacred Ground's formation-energy patterns. This is the highest-quality cultivation environment achievable through combined means.
