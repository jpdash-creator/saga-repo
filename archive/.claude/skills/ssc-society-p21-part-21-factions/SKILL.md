## Area 20 — Part 21 and Factions (Part 22 Interface)

### 20.1 — Civilization vs. Faction

Part 21 governs civilizations. Part 22 governs factions. The distinction:

**Civilization (Part 21):** An entity with unified governance structure, defined territory, and multi-generational institutional continuity. Assessed by CCT Designation.

**Faction (Part 22):** An organized power group operating within or across civilizations. Factions may achieve CCT-equivalent power outputs but are structurally different — they lack the population-wide, territory-wide administrative reach that defines a civilization.

**Rule 20.1.1 — Faction CCT Proxy:** For narrative purposes, powerful factions within a civilization may be given a "Faction Power Tier" using the same CTD names — but this is a power proxy, not a formal CCT assessment. A Solarian-class faction within a Galactic civilization is a faction at the power output level of a Solarian civilization; it is not a civilization.

### 20.2 — Factions Operating at Civilization Scale

When a faction controls territory, population, and institutions at the scale of a full civilization and operates independently of any larger civilization governance structure, Part 22 refers to Part 21 for its CCT classification. The boundary between "powerful faction" and "independent civilization" is governed by Part 22.
