### 13.3 — Part 7 vs. Part 6 Fusion Disambiguation

The same phenomenon — fusion — is governed at two different levels:

| Level | Governing Part | What It Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Technique Level | Part 6, Area 10 | The execution of a single Named Move that merges two streams; temporary merger in a technique; success rates; failure modes; mastery grades |
| Path Level | Part 7 | The permanent architectural merger of cultivation systems; the development process; the long-term changes to the cultivator; the unique capabilities that only arise from architectural integration |

**A Part 7 fusion cultivator uses Part 6 Fusion Move Type techniques.** The two are not alternatives — they are the same phenomenon at two levels of analysis. Part 7 governs the deep architecture; Part 6 governs the surface expression. A cultivator cannot have Part 7 fusion without Part 6 fusion expressions, and the best Part 6 fusion expressions come from Part 7 fusion architecture.
