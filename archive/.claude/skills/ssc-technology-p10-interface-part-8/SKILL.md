## Area 11 — Interface with Part 8 (Scale Law)

### 11.1 — Scientific Prerequisites and Tier Definitions

Part 8 defines what each technology tier looks like — the capabilities it includes, the domains it encompasses, the civilization classification associated with it. Part 10 defines what must be known before those capabilities can be built. This relationship means:

- Part 8 governs the destination: "Tier 8 includes stellar energy capture capability at Dyson sphere scale."
- Part 10 governs the key: "Achieving stellar energy capture requires Layer 4 stellar physics, Layer 4 megastructure structural science, and Layer 3 self-replicating manufacturing science."
- Part 9 governs the journey: "Achieving these scientific prerequisites takes 5–20 generations under standard conditions, reduced by specific acceleration mechanisms."

### 11.2 — Part 8 Scientific Prerequisite Footnotes — Authoritative Update

With Part 10 now authored, Part 8 footnote references to Part 10 as "stub" are superseded. The specific Part 8 rules that defer to Part 10 are now fully active:
- **Part 8, P8-5.1 (Operation Without Understanding):** Governed jointly by Part 8 (operation rules) and Part 10 (what understanding means for each domain)
- **Part 8, P8-5.2 (Reverse Engineering Requires Scientific Prerequisite):** The scientific prerequisite is now specifically governed by Part 10 Area 3 and Area 4 (domain-specific tables)
- **Part 8, Area 10, Category 1:** Governed by Part 10; the specific content for each tier and domain is found in Part 10 Areas 3 and 4
- **Part 8, Area 15.2.5 (AI Sentience Threshold):** Governed by Part 10 Area 6 (AI Sentience Ontology)
- **Part 8, Area 15.3.2 (Robotic Agency and Cultivation Soul):** Governed by Part 10 Area 6.4
- **Part 8, Area 15.5.3 (Consciousness Transfer):** Governed by Part 10 Area 6.3
- **Part 8, Area 15.9.1 (FTL Prerequisites):** Governed by Part 10 Area 5
