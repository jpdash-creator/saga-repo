## Area 7 — Cultivation Travel: Flight Mechanics

### 7.1 — Flight Mechanics Governance

Cultivation flight across all realms follows consistent governing rules, regardless of speed or realm. Authors use these rules to render flight scenes consistently.

### 7.2 — Flight Posture and Movement Expression

**Flight is active, not passive.** A cultivator in flight is continuously expending cultivation energy to maintain their elevation and vector. They are not gliding — they are continuously sustained. The character of their flight reflects their realm:

| Cultivation Stage | Flight Character | Environmental Signature |
|---|---|---|
| Mortal Tier 2: Ruler/Monarch | Visible qi compression beneath feet; slight strain visible; feet often remain in natural standing posture | Subtle wind disturbance; leaves rustle in radius |
| Xiantian | Qi field forms visible aura; smooth, apparently effortless; body stabilizes automatically | Wind wake and qi disturbance proportional to speed |
| Core Realm | Core's energy field is visible as faint luminescence around the body; movement is frictionless and silent | Atmospheric compression wave at burst speeds; sonic effects at extreme velocity |
| Divine Transformation / Sea Formation | Body radiates divine energy; flight is weightless and fluid | Local atmospheric response to presence; minor environmental changes in close proximity |
| Heaven Treading | Flight becomes spatial navigation — the cultivator doesn't "fly" so much as relocate through space | Space itself shows slight distortion near their path |
| Divine Ascension and above | The concept of flight is obsolete — the cultivator moves as a force of nature through space | Environmental transformation in their wake |

### 7.3 — Flight Altitude Governance

**In-atmosphere altitude:**
- Mortal Tier 2: Ruler/Monarch — functional up to ~10 km altitude (upper troposphere)
- Xiantian: Demigod through Absolute God — functional through full atmosphere; vacuum begins at ~100 km altitude
- Core Realm and above — no atmospheric altitude limit; full vacuum operation

**Space altitude (orbital and above):**
- Governed by vacuum survival rules in Areas 4.2 and 5.3.

### 7.4 — Flight Speed Context (Atmospheric Reference)
| Speed | Reference |
|---|---|
| ~300 km/h | Light aircraft speed (Mortal Tier 2: Senior burst) |
| ~1,200 km/h | Sound barrier in Earth-like atmosphere |
| ~12,000 km/h | Mach 10 (Mortal Tier 2: Monarch level) |
| ~3,000 km/h | Normal Xiantian Demigod sustained flight |
| ~100,000 km/h | Absolute God burst (~83 km/s) |
| ~300,000 km/s | Speed of light (Heaven Treading spatial fold overcomes this) |
