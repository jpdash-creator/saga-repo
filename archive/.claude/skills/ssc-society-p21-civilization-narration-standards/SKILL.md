## Area 14 — Civilization Narration Standards

### N21-1 — CTD Primacy in Prose

All references to a civilization's tier in narrative prose use Civilization Tier Designations, not axis values or interim notation:
- **Correct:** "The Stellar civilization of the Veil Cluster..."
- **Correct:** "An Interstellar-tier empire that had recently crossed the void..."
- **Correct:** "A Clusteral power — the greatest the northern quadrant had ever seen."
- **Incorrect:** "The CCT-7 civilization..."
- **Incorrect:** "A CA-8, TA-7 civilization..."

Axis values (CA, TA, EA, LA, SA) appear in world-building governance documents, academic references within the narrative, and canon reference materials — not in narrative prose.

### N21-2 — Type Designation in Prose

Civilization Types may appear in prose as background flavor:
- **Correct:** "A Cultivation-Sovereign Stellar civilization" / "a CS-Type Stellar entity"
- **Correct:** "a Balanced-Path civilization" / "they had pursued the Balanced-Path since the great unification"
- Types are not mandatory in narrative prose — they are available color, not required identification.

### N21-3 — Cross-Book Consistency

A civilization's CTD must be consistent across books unless a CCT advancement or regression event has been narratively depicted and authorized. Authors must flag any tier-level change to ensure canon consistency.

### N21-4 — Avoiding False Precision

Authors should not attempt to derive exact axis values from prose descriptions. CCT and Civilization Tier Designation are the canonical precision level for in-story civilizational assessment. Axis values are governance tools, not narrative tools.

### N21-5 — The Tier Gap and Narrative Realism

When depicting interaction between civilizations of significantly different CCT levels, the narrative must reflect the power asymmetry. A CCT-4 (Planetary) civilization interacting with a CCT-9 (Expansionary) civilization cannot be depicted as diplomatic equals. The tier gap creates a realistic power relationship that Part 28 is required to override.
