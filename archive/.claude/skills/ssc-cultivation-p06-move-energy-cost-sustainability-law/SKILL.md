## Area 5 — Move Energy Cost and Sustainability Law

### 5.1 — The Energy Cost Principle

Every Named Move that involves cultivation energy depletes the cultivator's energy reserves. The cost is governed by: the move's Type (complexity), the move's Function (what it does), the cultivation realm of the user (which determines the total reserve and the cost-to-power ratio), and whether the technique is instant, channeled, or cast.

**Mortal Tier 1 Exception:** Physical techniques at Mortal Tier 1 have no energy cost — they are metabolic actions. However, they have a **stamina cost** governed by physical exertion. Sustained physical technique deployment depletes stamina rather than qi.

### 5.2 — Deployment Forms

Every Named Move is deployed through one of three forms:

**Instant Deployment:** The technique is formed and released in a single action. No sustained energy management is required after activation. The cost is a one-time depletion at the moment of use. Most attack moves are instant. Most Basic and Intermediate moves are instant.

**Channeled Deployment:** The technique requires ongoing energy investment to maintain its effect. The cultivator must continue directing energy into the move for as long as it is active — Formations, sustained Seals, ongoing Suppressions, and extended Healing are typically channeled. The cost-per-second of a channeled technique is lower than the equivalent instant technique's total cost, but the cumulative cost over time can be enormous. A cultivator who is channeling a technique is also managing their reserves in real time.

**Cast Deployment:** The technique requires a preparation phase during which energy is accumulated and structured, followed by a release. The preparation phase is vulnerable — a Disrupt move landing during casting forces the failure table for that technique's type. After release, the technique operates independently of the cultivator (like a Projectile) or creates an instant effect. Most Catastrophic and Mythic techniques have a Cast deployment form due to the enormous energy structuring required.

### 5.3 — Energy Cost by Move Type

| Move Type | Instant Cost | Channeled Cost/Cycle | Cast Accumulation | Reserve Impact |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|------------------|----------------|
| Basic | Minimal | Very Low | — | Negligible |
| Intermediate | Low | Low | — | Minor |
| Advanced | Moderate | Moderate | Low | Noticeable |
| Elite | High | High | Moderate | Significant |
| Signature | Variable | Variable | Variable | Significant to Extreme |
| Fusion | Very High | Very High | High | Major |
| Catastrophic | Extreme | — | Very High | Depleting |
| Mythic | Reality-level | — | Extreme | Potentially Lifespan |

**"Reserve Impact"** describes the percentage of a cultivator's total reserves a single deployment consumes:
- Negligible: less than 1%
- Minor: 1–5%
- Noticeable: 5–15%
- Significant: 15–30%
- Major: 30–50%
- Depleting: 50–80%
- Potentially Lifespan: may exceed energy reserves and draw on essence/lifespan

### 5.4 — Sustainability Rules

**Rule 5.4.1 — Core Realm Advantage:**
Core Realm cultivators (Part 3, Area 1.4) have an internal reactor rather than a finite qi reservoir. This means their energy cost-to-reserve ratio is fundamentally better than Xiantian cultivators. A Core Formation cultivator can sustain Elite moves at a pace that would exhaust an Absolute God cultivator's reserves.

**Rule 5.4.2 — Fatigue Accumulation:**
Even Core Realm cultivators face fatigue limits. Extended combat at maximum output depletes the Core's output capacity even though the Core is continuously generating energy. Part 3, Area 8.4.3 governs Core fatigue. For Named Move purposes: a cultivator at less than 30% effective capacity suffers −10% success rate on Intermediate and above moves.

**Rule 5.4.3 — Technique Efficiency and Mastery:**
Higher mastery levels reduce energy cost as well as improving success rates. A Master-level Basic technique uses perhaps half the energy of a Novice-level execution of the same technique. Mastery does not reduce Catastrophic or Mythic costs significantly — the energy requirement for those types is fundamental to their nature.

**Rule 5.4.4 — External Resource Enhancement:**
Energy resources (Part 11 — Energy Resource Law) can temporarily expand effective reserve capacity, accelerate recovery between techniques, or directly power specific techniques. A cultivator using a high-grade spirit stone to fuel a Catastrophic technique may achieve the same output at reduced personal reserve cost. Technique-specific resource usage and its governance are defined in Part 11.
