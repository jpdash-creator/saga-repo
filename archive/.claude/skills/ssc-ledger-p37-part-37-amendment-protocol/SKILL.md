## Rule Text (Verbatim Source — Authoritative)
## Area 6 — Part 37 Amendment Protocol

When new entity types are discovered in prose and their schemas are authored under Area 3:
1. The new schema is written as a new Area 3 subsection (e.g., Area 3.1, Area 3.2)
2. The Ledger Folder Registry in Area 4 is updated to reflect `Active` status for the new folder
3. Part 34 Area 20 (Ledger Architecture Map) is updated
4. Part 34 Area 23 (Version Registry) records the Part 37 version increment
5. Part 1 Canon Version is updated per the Canon Amendment Process (Part 1 Area 3.4)
