## Area 6 — Relationship to Retired CDL-1 Framework

### 6.1 — CDL-1 Retirement Statement

The Combat Density Law (CDL-1), which governed all Sections across all Phases with a hard 70% universal combat floor (Law CDL-1 in Part 30 Area 3, Canon v4.24), is **retired effective Canon v4.26**. CDL-1 is superseded in its entirety by Part 35 CDL-2. All future authorship of Starward Saga prose is governed by CDL-2 exclusively for content density enforcement.

**What CDL-1 protected, CDL-2 preserves and extends:**
CDL-1's fundamental insight — that the Starward Saga is, at its core, a cultivation-technology-fight novel series and that combat must be quantifiably central — is preserved and extended in CDL-2. The Universal Anchor Floor for COMBAT (≥20% Anchor minimum at every Section-close) ensures that combat remains a mandatory presence in every Section. The COMBAT target Anchors across Phase 1 range from 30% (Section 8 consolidation) to 85% (Section 7 war climax) — demonstrating that combat is the single most tracked category in the framework. CDL-2 does not reduce combat. It gives combat the company it needs — lore, dialogue, consequence, cultivation growth — to land with full pacing-appropriate immersive force (fast-paced combat at 3500%–4000%; slow-paced climax combat at 4500%–5000%).

**What CDL-1 overconstrained, CDL-2 corrects:**
CDL-1's 70% universal floor was blind to story context. A diplomatic Section, a relational coda, or a world-discovery Section cannot and should not run 70% combat prose without degrading the story's foundations. CDL-2 provides Section-specific targets: Section 8 runs at 30% COMBAT Anchor because Part 30 mandates it as an airship-and-discovery coda; Section 7 runs at 85% COMBAT Anchor because Part 30 mandates it as the war climax. The story's identity is preserved; its architecture is honored.

### 6.2 — Cross-References to CDL-1 in Other Parts

All references to CDL-1 and the 70% combat floor in other Canon Parts and ledger files are treated as superseded by Part 35. When encountered in prose authorship context, any CDL-1 citation should be internally redirected to the CDL-2 framework. The original text in Part 30 Area 3 is retained as a retirement notice (per the Part 30 v1.7 update) for historical reference.
