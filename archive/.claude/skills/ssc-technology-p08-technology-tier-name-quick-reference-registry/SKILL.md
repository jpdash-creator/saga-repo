## Area 39 — Technology Tier Name Quick-Reference Registry

**Purpose:** Complete cross-domain reference table of all canonical technology tier names for all 14 domains across all 20 levels. Use this table when writing prose to identify the exact canonical tier name for any technology being described. The tier name is used in prose narration, ledger entries, and character dialogue to identify technology without resorting to the generic "Level N" notation.

**Usage in Prose:** A character or narrator describing a technology system may reference it by tier name rather than numeric level. Example: "The Azure Sky Kingdom's industrial base had advanced to **Molecular Nexus** — perhaps the pinnacle of what any Planetary-class civilization could achieve before expanding beyond a single world." The numeric level (Level 5 in this case) remains the governance reference; the tier name is the narrative expression.

**Narration Rule:** When referencing a specific technology tier in prose or dialogue, the canonical form is: **[Tier Name]** (Type [N], Level [X]). In casual prose where the domain is obvious from context, the tier name alone is sufficient. In governance contexts (audit reports, ledger entries, Canon citations), the full form is required.

**Phase 1 Technology Context:** The Azure Sky Kingdom and Gale Kingdom operate primarily at Industrial **Molecular Nexus** (Type 1, Level 5), Information **Quantum Lattice** (Type 2, Level 5), and Transportation **Combustion Drive** (Type 9, Level 5). Their military capability sits at **Electronic Warfare** (Type 8, Level 5). The Firmament Lock caps the Sol Star System at approximately Level 6 operational ceiling across all domains.
