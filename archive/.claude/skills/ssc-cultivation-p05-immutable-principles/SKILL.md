## Area 3 — Immutable Principles

The following principles are immutable. They cannot be overridden by narrative intent, creative variance, faction authority, or any mechanism below a formal Canon Amendment per Part 1, Area 3.4.

**IP-1:** Combat is deterministic in mechanics. Every combat event has a mechanically defined structure. No combat event may exist without classification under this Part.

**IP-2:** Escalation must be categorized. No conflict may exist in an undefined or unclassified state. If a conflict cannot be immediately classified, it defaults to the lowest applicable tier until reclassification conditions are met.

**IP-3:** Combat cannot bypass cultivation advancement conditions. No combat outcome — victory, defeat, near-death, or breakthrough stress — may produce advancement that bypasses the methods and stability requirements defined in Part 3, Area 4. Combat may create breakthrough conditions; it does not override breakthrough requirements.

**IP-4:** Combat cannot override restrictions in Location Law (Part 13). Protected zones, sacred ground rules, and dimensional boundaries defined by Part 13 remain in full force during combat. A battle cannot "happen" in a protected zone by narrative fiat.

**IP-5:** Combat does not change narrative weight. The significance of a character or event is determined by their role and impact in the story. Combat outcomes do not grant or remove narrative weight — they produce consequences (Part 27) that may affect narrative weight through their downstream effects.

**IP-6:** Combat must remain mechanically interpretable even in chaotic battlefield conditions. When multiple factions, cultivation levels, and spatial scales converge, the deterministic structure of this Part must still be applicable. If it cannot be applied, the conflict must be narratively resolved through the chaos dissolution rules in Area 10.

**IP-7:** All combat actions are validated under Part 1 and Part 2. Part 1 governs the validity of all narrative output. Part 2 governs how combat is rendered in prose. Both remain active during all combat events.

**IP-8:** Escalation tier classification should be coherent with the current in-world power and political scale. Area 15 provides guidance for assessing escalation coherence and what narrative development may be needed before larger-scale conflicts can be earned within the story.

**IP-9:** Combat intensity operates inside escalation tier boundaries. A higher-level cultivator engaging in a lower-tier conflict must operate at the intensity ceiling of that tier. Exceeding the tier's intensity ceiling constitutes an escalation violation with defined consequences.
