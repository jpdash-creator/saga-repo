## Area 14 — Synergy Advancement Requirements

### 14.1 — Prerequisites for Accessing Deeper Synergy Categories

Advancing from a lower integration model to a higher one requires satisfying prerequisites across three dimensions:

**Cultivation-Side Prerequisite (Part 7, Area 6.2):**
The cultivation practitioner side must reach the realm threshold for the target integration model. These are Part 7's governance:
- Model B: Xiantian realm minimum
- Model C: Core Formation minimum
- Model D: Core realm established minimum

**Technology-Side Prerequisite (Part 8, Area 6.2):**
The CI Technology level must reach the threshold for the target integration model. These are Part 8's governance:
- Model B: CI Technology Level 3–5
- Model C: CI Technology Level 6–8
- Model D: CI Technology Level 8–20

**Synergy Infrastructure Prerequisite (Part 31):**
Beyond the threshold requirements, actually achieving a higher integration model requires:
- CPM of sufficient grade to power the target model (Area 9.1)
- Energy Technology sufficient to support the target model's cultivation environment quality (Area 9.2)
- Civilization tier sufficient to have built and maintained the CI Technology infrastructure (Area 8)
- Integrated Practitioner training enabling personal operation of the target model's architecture

**Rule 14.1.1 — All Three Simultaneously Required:** All three prerequisite dimensions must be satisfied simultaneously. Cultivation realm advancement beyond the threshold does not automatically unlock the next model — technology and infrastructure must advance in parallel. The integration model is the joint product of all three prerequisites, not the product of any single one.

---

### 14.2 — Synergy Breakthrough Events

When a civilization crosses from one integration model threshold to the next, it experiences a Synergy Breakthrough — a civilizational transformation event comparable to a cultivator's Major Realm breakthrough.

**Model A → Model B Transition:**
The first successful CPM-powered technology deployment at industrial scale marks the Model B threshold. Civilization changes: CPM trade economy emerges; cultivation practitioners gain economic value beyond combat; technology infrastructure begins producing cultivation environment benefits; the first Integrated Practitioners emerge as a recognized category.

**Model B → Model C Transition:**
The first Technology-Relic Class 3 successful construction marks the Model C threshold. Civilization changes: CI Technology becomes a strategic priority rather than a secondary concern; formation-array-integrated technology infrastructure transforms urban planning; Integrated Practitioners become rare institutional assets rather than curiosities; Sacred Ground + technology combinations become civilization strategic investments.

**Model C → Model D Transition:**
The first successful Model D unified system operation marks the D threshold. Civilization changes: the distinction between cultivators and Integrated Practitioners shifts from architecture to degree; CI Technology systems become co-equal civilization infrastructure with energy and military systems; galactic-scale ambient qi enrichment becomes a policy goal; the civilization's cultivation advancement rate accelerates by synergy effect at civilizational scale.

---

### 14.3 — Synergy Degradation Conditions

Civilizations can lose synergy capability through several mechanisms:

**CI Technology Infrastructure Degradation:**
If a civilization's CI Technology domain level drops (through war, resource depletion, population loss, or institutional collapse), synergy capability degrades to the level the remaining CI Technology supports. Cultivation capability and CPM access may remain intact, but without the technology-side interface, integration cannot proceed above the supported model.

**CPM Supply Disruption:**
Disruption of CPM supply (loss of cultivation practitioners, loss of CPM formation zones through environmental change, CPM extraction infrastructure destruction) degrades the cultivation-side energy input to synergy. Technology systems operating on CPM switch to conventional energy at lower output, or shut down. Synergy capability drops by CPM grade.

**Sacred Ground Loss:**
If a Sacred Ground used as a synergy amplification location is disrupted or destroyed, synergy effects dependent on it degrade to non-Sacred-Ground baseline. Technology Sacred Ground approximations may partially compensate but cannot fully replace natural Sacred Ground synergy amplification.

**Integrated Practitioner Attrition:**
Loss of Integrated Practitioners through death, defection, or disability degrades personal synergy capability at the practitioner level. Civilization-level CI Technology infrastructure remains, but the operators who can achieve deep personal integration are scarce. Rebuilding Integrated Practitioner capability requires the same cultivation + technology parallel development that produced the first generation — it cannot be accelerated without the cultivation baseline.
