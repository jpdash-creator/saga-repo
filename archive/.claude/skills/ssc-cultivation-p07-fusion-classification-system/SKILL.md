## Area 1 — Fusion Classification System

### 1.1 — The Five Fusion Domains

Part 7 governs fusion across five distinct domains, each with its own architecture, requirements, and expressions:

**Domain 1 — Affinity Fusion:** The merging of two or more cultivation affinities within a cultivator's qi-based cultivation path. Governs how Fire and Lightning, Life and Death, Space and Time, and all other affinity pairings from Part 4's 40+ affinity system integrate at the path level. This is the most common fusion domain and the first fusion most cultivators encounter. Fully governed in Area 2.

**Domain 2 — Cultivation Path Fusion:** The merging of two fundamentally different cultivation architectures within a single cultivator — qi cultivation and body cultivation, qi cultivation and soul cultivation, body cultivation and soul cultivation, or all three. Each path has its own structural logic, advancement ladder, and energy type. Path fusion merges these at the root level. Governed in Area 3.

**Domain 3 — Cultivation-Relic Integration:** The permanent integration of a relic into a cultivator's cultivation architecture, transforming the relic from an external tool into a living extension of the cultivator's own system. This goes beyond Soul Bonding (Part 12, Area 11) into a deeper, permanent architectural merger. Governed in Area 4.

**Domain 4 — Technology-Relic Fusion:** The fusion of technological systems with relic energy architecture, producing hybrid objects that function according to neither pure technology rules nor pure relic rules. This domain is the material bridge between the cultivation world and the technological world. Governed in Area 5.

**Domain 5 — Cultivation-Technology Integration:** The integration of cultivation power with technological systems at the operational level — the ways cultivators and technology interact, augment each other, merge into hybrid capabilities, and develop genuinely integrated systems that neither could produce alone. The cultivation-side rules are governed here; the technology-side rules are governed by Parts 8–10. Governed in Area 6.

### 1.2 — The Fusion Spectrum

All fusion — across all five domains — exists on a spectrum from incomplete to complete integration:

**Stage 1 — Parallel:** Two systems coexist in one architecture without genuine integration. This is the Coexistence state defined in P7-5. No fusion has occurred. The Coexistence Ceiling applies.

**Stage 2 — Contact:** The two systems have begun to influence each other. There is interference, resonance, and minor interaction effects. The cultivator experiences the instability of Contact without yet producing genuine fusion expressions. This is the dangerous transitional stage — the systems are neither separate nor fused.

**Stage 3 — Partial Fusion:** Genuine fusion has begun but is incomplete. The two systems share some architecture but retain significant separate structure. Partial fusion expressions are possible but unstable. Partial fusion is better than Contact instability but represents an incomplete state that creates ongoing cultivation complications.

**Stage 4 — Stable Fusion:** The fusion has reached architectural completion at the cultivator's current realm. The merged architecture is the new default. Fusion expressions are accessible with the base success rates governed by Part 6 for technique-level expression, and the cultivator's capability envelope reflects their fusion architecture. This is the target state.

**Stage 5 — Deep Fusion:** The fusion has penetrated all levels of the cultivator's architecture — meridians, Core/Dantian, soul, and intent. The Integration Principle has been deeply cultivated and the fused expression is more natural than the original components would have been. Deep Fusion cultivators are among the most powerful within their realm because their fusion depth allows them to operate near the realm ceiling more consistently.

### 1.3 — Cultivation Level Requirements for Fusion

| Fusion Domain | Minimum Entry Point | Stable Fusion Typical Achievement | Deep Fusion Typical Achievement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Affinity Fusion (Compatible) | Xiantian Demigod with Level 4 (Resonance) in both affinities | Xiantian Void God to True God | Core Formation to Assimilation |
| Affinity Fusion (Opposing) | Core Formation with Level 5 (Attunement) in both affinities | Core Assimilation to Solidification | Divine Transformation and above |
| Affinity Fusion (Transcendent) | Core Solidification with Level 6 (Harmony) in both affinities | Divine Transformation | Divine Heaven Treading and above |
| Path Fusion (Qi + Body) | Mortal Tier 2 Senior for Contact; Xiantian Demigod for Stable | Xiantian True God | Core Formation to Assimilation |
| Path Fusion (Qi + Soul) | Xiantian Demigod (soul rung 1) for Contact; Xiantian True God for Stable | Core Formation | Core Solidification to Divine Transformation |
| Path Fusion (Body + Soul) | Core Formation for Contact; Core Solidification for Stable | Divine Transformation | Divine Sea Formation and above |
| Triple Path Fusion | Divine Transformation for Contact; extraordinarily rare for Stable | Near-mythological | Primordial — realm ceiling constraint |
| Cultivation-Relic Integration | Xiantian Demigod with Soul Bond (Part 12, Area 11.2) | Core Formation | Divine Transformation |
| Technology-Relic Fusion | Governed by Part 8 and Part 12 jointly | Governed by Part 8 | Governed by Part 8 |
| Cultivation-Technology Integration | Governed by cultivation-technology threshold table (Area 6.2) | — | — |

### 1.4 — The Fusion Priority Rule

When a cultivator has achieved fusion in multiple domains simultaneously (e.g., a cultivator who has achieved both Affinity Fusion and Cultivation-Relic Integration), the domains operate as a unified architecture. The interactions between different fusion types on the same cultivator are governed by the most specific applicable rule. When no specific rule exists, the following priority order applies:

1. Realm Ceiling (governed by Part 3, Area 6.3 and breakthrough requirements)
2. The 3-Level Rule (Part 3, Area 7.2) — fusion does not exempt cultivators from this
3. The Realm Categorical Impossibility Law (Part 3, Area 17.3)
4. This Part's specific domain rules
5. Part 6 technique rules for expression-level mechanics
