## Area 10 — Interface with Part 3 (Cultivation Power Law)

### 10.1 — Joint Governance with Part 3 Area 20

Part 3 Area 20 (Heavenly Scale Spatial Footprint Reference) and Part 14 Area 4 (Soul Sense Active Range) are jointly authoritative for different aspects of cultivation distance governance:

| Governance Question | Primary Authority |
|---|---|
| Passive aura radius (how far the cultivator's ambient presence extends without active technique) | Part 3, Area 20 |
| Environmental distortion radius (how far passive environmental effects extend) | Part 3, Area 20 |
| Harmful zone radius (how far passive aura causes harm to lower-realm beings) | Part 3, Area 20 |
| Maximum active soul sense range (how far the cultivator can extend active perception) | Part 14, Area 4 |
| Soul sense range modifiers (ambient energy, suppression, dimensional) | Part 14, Area 4.2 |
| Cultivation technique maximum projection range | Part 14, Area 4.3 (jointly with Part 6) |
| Combat footprint governance in battlefield context | Part 3, Area 20.3 (using Part 14 range values as input) |

---

### 10.2 — Soul Sense vs. Physical Distance

Soul sense range (Part 14 Area 4) and physical technique range (Part 14 Area 4.3) are separate capabilities. A Grade 10 Core Formation cultivator can perceive up to 300 km through soul sense but can only directly project a technique to a maximum of ~1,000 km for the most powerful projections. Soul sense detects the target; technique range determines if the cultivator can act on that detection.

**Rule P14-P3-1 — Detection Precedes Interaction:**
A cultivator must first detect a target (soul sense range, technology sensor range, or visual) before any direct interaction technique can be applied. Detecting a target at the edge of soul sense range does not automatically mean the cultivator can strike that target — technique range may be shorter than detection range for lower realms.
