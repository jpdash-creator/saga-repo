## Area 6 — Character Classification by Governance and Administrative Role

### 6.1 — Governance Character Architecture

Every civilization, faction, and settlement requires governance characters — individuals whose primary role is the exercise of institutional authority. Governance characters are distinct from military characters (who exercise force) and academic characters (who manage knowledge).

**Canonical Governance Character Types by Scale:**

| Scale | Governance Character Types |
|-------|--------------------------|
| **Village / Settlement Scale** | Village Elder, Settlement Head, Local Magistrate, Council Member, Village Guard Captain |
| **City / Capital Scale** | City Governor, Mayor, Urban Prefect, City Council, Capital Administrator, Court Official |
| **Kingdom / Empire Scale** | King / Queen, Crown Prince / Princess, Court Ministers, Grand Vizier, Imperial Officials, Regional Governors, Royal Advisors |
| **Civilization Scale (CCT-5+)** | President / Chancellor / Supreme Leader, Legislative Body, Judicial Authority, Cabinet Ministers, Diplomatic Corps |
| **Multi-Civilization Scale (CCT-10+)** | Alliance Directors, Council Representatives, Cross-Civilization Trade Commissioners, Diplomatic Treaty Authorities |

### 6.2 — Sect Governance Characters

Within cultivation faction governance (Part 22):

| Governance Role | Faction Type | Description |
|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Sect Master / Patriarch** | Cultivator Sect | Absolute sect authority; cultivation floor: Core Solidification+ |
| **Council of Elders** | Cultivator Sect | Collective governance body; each Elder is Grand Elder equivalent or higher |
| **Head Steward / Logistics Elder** | Cultivator Sect | Resource management authority; often lower realm but deep institutional knowledge |
| **Mission Hall Elder** | Cultivator Sect | Oversees disciple mission assignments; intelligence function within sect |
| **Treasury Elder** | Cultivator Sect | Manages sect resource allocation; highest W-Class within sect structure |
| **Enforcement Elder** | Cultivator Sect | Internal discipline; punishment authority; law-enforcement function within sect |
| **Outer/Inner Hall Managers** | Cultivator Sect | Administrative officers for disciple populations |
