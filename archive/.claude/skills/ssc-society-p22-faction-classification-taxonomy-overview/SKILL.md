## Area 2 — Faction Classification Taxonomy Overview

### 2.1 — The Twenty-Two Faction Categories

Part 22 governs twenty-two primary faction categories. Each category is defined by its primary purpose, characteristic membership, natural scale range, internal governance archetype, and cross-Part dependencies. Categories are not mutually exclusive — hybrid factions that combine characteristics of multiple categories are common and are governed by the **Primary Function Rule**: a hybrid faction is classified by its dominant primary function for governance purposes, with secondary-function characteristics as modifiers.

**The Twenty-Two Primary Faction Categories:**

| Category | Short Code | Primary Purpose | Natural Scale Range | Governing Areas |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Cultivator Sects | CS-F | Cultivation advancement, transmission | Village → Galaxy | Area 3 |
| 2. Government Bodies | GOV | Political administration, law, order | Village → Universe | Area 4 |
| 3. Military & Security Forces | MSF | Armed defense, offense, enforcement | Village → Universe | Area 5 |
| 4. Space Forces | SPC | Void, stellar, galactic military operations | Star System → Universe | Area 6 |
| 5. Academic & Research Factions | ACR | Knowledge, discovery, science, cultivation theory | Village → Galaxy | Area 7 |
| 6. Alchemical & Pharmaceutical Factions | ALF | Substance refinement, medicine, CPM processing | Village → Galaxy | Area 8 |
| 7. Science & Technology Factions | STF | Technology development, engineering | Village → Universe | Area 9 |
| 8. Mercantile & Trading Factions | MTF | Commerce, trade networks, finance | Village → Multiverse | Area 10 |
| 9. Financial & Banking Factions | FBF | Capital, credit, monetary systems | Kingdom → Multiverse | Area 11 |
| 10. Artisan & Craft Factions | ACF | Relic crafting, artifact construction, engineering | Village → Galaxy | Area 12 |
| 11. Intelligence & Espionage Factions | IEF | Information, infiltration, surveillance | Kingdom → Universe | Area 13 |
| 12. Assassination & Hunting Factions | AHF | Targeted elimination, bounty hunting | Village → Universe | Area 14 |

| 22. Judicial & Legal Factions | JLF | Law, arbitration, inter-faction dispute resolution | Kingdom → Universe | Area 4 |

### 2.2 — The Primary Function Rule for Hybrid Factions

Most real-world factions blend multiple categories. A powerful cultivation sect may also operate a pharmaceutical arm, manage trade routes, and maintain an intelligence bureau. The Primary Function Rule states: **the category that defines the faction's founding Charter Purpose and controls the majority of its institutional investment is the governing classification.** Secondary functions are governed by the secondary-category rules as modifiers. A cultivation sect with a pharmaceutical arm is still a Cultivator Sect (CS-F) — the pharmaceutical function follows Alchemical Faction rules as a subsidiary arm, not as a primary classifier.

### 2.3 — Faction Scale Code System

All factions are also coded by their **maximum operational scale** — the largest cosmological scale at which the faction has meaningful organizational presence and operational capability:

| Scale Code | Cosmological Tier | Operational Footprint |
|---|---|---|
| SCL-1 | Village | One or a cluster of villages |
| SCL-2 | Town | Town and surrounding villages |
| SCL-3 | City | City and region |
| SCL-4 | Capital | Capital and its administrative domain |
| SCL-5 | Kingdom | Kingdom-scale territory |
| SCL-6 | Empire | Empire-scale territory |
| SCL-7 | World | Single planet/world |
| SCL-8 | Star System | Single star system |
| SCL-9 | Star Cluster | Stellar cluster |
| SCL-10 | Star Quadrant | Star quadrant |
| SCL-11 | Galaxy | Full galaxy |
| SCL-12 | Galaxy Cluster | Galaxy cluster scale |
| SCL-13 | Galaxy Supercluster | Supercluster scale |
| SCL-14 | Universe | Universal scale |
| SCL-15 | Multiverse | Multiversal scale |
| SCL-16 | The Primordium | Absolute/Primordial scale |

A faction's full designation combines its Category and Scale Code: e.g., **CS-F SCL-8** (a Cultivator Sect operating at Star System scale) or **MTF SCL-12** (a Mercantile Faction operating at Galaxy Cluster scale).
