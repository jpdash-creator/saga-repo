## Area 17 — Noble Houses and Aristocratic Factions

### 17.1 — Overview: Noble and Aristocratic Houses (NAH)

Noble and Aristocratic Houses are hereditary power structures whose authority derives from bloodline — typically cultivation bloodlines, ancient lineage, historical military service, or royal grant. They are distinguished from other faction types by their explicit hereditary nature: membership is by birth or formal adoption, not by achievement, application, or commercial relationship.

**Defining Characteristics:**
- **Primary Purpose:** Preservation of bloodline power; territorial administration; political influence
- **Core Resource:** Hereditary land; cultivated bloodline advantages (talent, technique inheritance, ancestral relics); political relationships
- **Authority Source:** Historical recognition + current cultivation/political power
- **Natural Scale Range:** Village (SCL-1) through Empire (SCL-6); exceptional houses may reach World (SCL-7)

### 17.2 — Noble House Classifications

| House Type | Source of Status | Power Base | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Village Elder Clan | Seniority + cultivation talent | Local respect; village resource control | SCL-1 to SCL-2 |
| Minor Noble House | Royal grant; military service | Landed estate; minor cultivation tradition | SCL-3 to SCL-5 |
| Major Noble House | Ancient lineage; high cultivation bloodline | Multiple estates; notable cultivation tradition; court influence | SCL-4 to SCL-6 |
| Royal House | Sovereign bloodline | Kingdom authority; royal cultivation sect | SCL-5 to SCL-7 |
| Imperial Bloodline | Ruling dynasty of an empire | Imperial authority; peak cultivation; historical relics | SCL-6 to SCL-7 |
| Ancient House (Remnant) | Pre-civilization or pre-war ancient lineage | Degraded authority but exceptional knowledge/relic holdings | SCL-3 to SCL-6 (remnant status) |

### 17.3 — Bloodline Cultivation Advantages

Noble Houses may possess institutional cultivation advantages unavailable to unaffiliated cultivators:

- **Ancestral Techniques:** Cultivation techniques preserved across generations — often of higher quality than publicly available manuals
- **Talent Bloodline:** Some noble families carry heritable cultivation talent traits — not a guarantee of genius, but a higher probability floor of talent class
- **Ancestral Relics:** Relics crafted for or used by powerful ancestors; Part 12 relic governance applies; ancestral relics may be above the current generation's ability to fully activate
- **Bloodline Formation Arrays:** Formation arrays keyed to bloodline energy — others cannot activate them; useful for estate defense and resource protection
- **Lineage Sacred Ground Access:** Some noble families maintain private Sacred Grounds (Part 13 Area 14) restricted to bloodline members
