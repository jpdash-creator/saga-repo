## Area 2 — Physical Consequences

### 2.1 — Combat and Violence Physical Consequences

Every combat event governed by Part 5 produces physical consequences within the framework of Part 27:

**Injury Consequences:**
Physical injuries from combat (governed by Part 3, Area 8) produce consequence cascades:
- **Immediate (IC-1):** Degraded combat capability; pain; possible incapacitation
- **Short-term (IC-2):** Recovery requirements that remove the character from activity; resource consumption for healing
- **Medium-term (IC-3):** Scarring, meridian damage, or technique restriction (Part 3, Areas 8.3 and 13.4) that permanently modifies the character's capability profile
- **Cultivation consequence:** Severe injuries can create permanent cultivation ceilings (Part 3 governs the ceiling; Part 27 governs the pathway from action to ceiling)

**Death Consequence Cascades:**
The death of a significant character produces consequence cascades in every domain:
- *Physical:* The body must be dealt with — buried, honored, or left to circumstances according to cultural practice (Part 25)
- *Relational:* Every relationship the deceased character held is now modified — bonds are severed, grief activates in connected characters, power dynamics shift
- *Systemic:* If the character held a significant position (sect elder, faction leader, mentor), their death creates a power vacuum with systemic consequences
- *Cultivator death in combat vs. murder:* The manner of death has different consequence profiles for all parties involved — a death in legitimate combat under Part 5 produces different systemic consequences than assassination, execution, or collateral killing

**Environmental Damage:**
High-power cultivation events (escalation tiers E3 and above — Part 5) and high-WS weapon discharges (Part 33) produce physical environmental consequences:
- Location damage (Part 13 governs location integrity; Part 27 governs the consequence process)
- Sacred Ground desecration (Part 13, Area 14): the most severe environmental consequence category — sacred ground degradation carries spiritual, political, and cultural consequences far beyond the physical damage
- Ecosystem disruption: sustained high-power combat in inhabited areas produces ecological consequences that affect local populations for years
- CPM contamination: fractured or detonated CPM substances (Part 11, Area 9.3) create persistent terrain hazards with multi-year consequence timelines

### 2.2 — Technology Use Physical Consequences

**Weapon Discharge Consequences:**
Deployment of weapon systems (Part 33) produces physical consequences scaled by Weapon Scale Class:
- WS-I through WS-II: Precision consequences — the intended target affected, minimal or no collateral
- WS-III through WS-IV: Area consequences — the target zone affected; adjacent areas at risk; debris and secondary effects (IC-1 through IC-2)
- WS-V: Regional consequences — the region affected for IC-2 through IC-3 timescales; significant collateral impact
- WS-VI: Civilizational consequences — multiple-world-scale physical damage; IC-3 through IC-5 recovery requirements
- WS-VII: Existential consequences — stellar-scale or beyond; CSS-9 to CSS-10

**Vehicle Consequence Cascades (Part 32):**
Large vehicle destruction events (CHV scale and above) produce physical consequence cascades: debris fields, energy release, structural damage to surrounding installations, environmental contamination from drive system failures. These are joint Part 32/Part 27 governance.
