## Area 16 — Star System Classification System (Cosmic Tier)

A Star System is the complete spatial unit surrounding a star — all Worlds, celestial bodies, dimensional phenomena, and spatial properties within the star's gravitational influence. Star System class reflects the aggregate energy profile, zone density, and dimensional character of the entire system.

### 16.1 — Star System Internal Structure

A Star System is centered on one or more stars and contains: Primary Star(s) — the gravitational anchor; Habitable Worlds of any type; Gas Giants, uninhabitable worlds, asteroid fields, and comets; Dimensional Anomalies where present at higher-energy systems; and Star System-scale Formation Zones where applicable.

### 16.2 — Star System Class Table

| Class | Designation | Ambient Energy Profile | Affinity Profile | Special Zone Status | Dimensional Properties | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SS-1 | Barren System | Class 1 throughout | Neutral | None | Standard | A Star System with no meaningful ambient energy. Celestial bodies only. No civilization has taken root or survived. |
| SS-2 | Sparse System | Class 1–2 | Neutral | None | Standard | Minimal ambient energy. Basic physical properties. Habitable worlds possible but no energy advantages. |
| SS-3 | Common System | Class 2 | Neutral or mild | None | Standard | The baseline inhabited Star System. Adequate ambient energy, no special zones. Standard in all regards. The most numerous Star System type. |
| SS-4 | Affinity System | Class 2–3 | Active affinity | Possible: World-level Passive Formation Zone | Standard | A Star System with a dominant affinity character across multiple Worlds. The star's radiation profile or the system's geometry produces a consistent affinity environment. |
| SS-5 | Rich System | Class 3–4 | Neutral or affinity | Passive Formation Zones across multiple Worlds; Possible: System-level Formation Zone | Standard | A notably energy-dense Star System. Formation Zones distributed across its Worlds and in celestial body concentrations. Favorable throughout. |
| SS-6 | Vortex System | Class 4–5 | Affinity aligned | Active Vortex Zones on multiple Worlds; Possible: System-scale Vortex | Standard | A Star System with multiple active Vortex Formation Zones across its Worlds and potentially at the Star System scale. Exceptionally energetic. |
| SS-7 | Sacred System (Minor) | Class 6–7 in Sacred zones | Affinity aligned | World-level Sacred Ground (S1–S4) on at least one World | Minor dimensional alteration near Sacred Grounds | A Star System hosting at least one World with a confirmed Sacred Ground. The Sacred World is the system's defining feature. |
| SS-8 | Sacred System (Major) | Class 7 in Sacred zones | Affinity aligned | Multiple Worlds with Sacred Grounds (S3–S6); Possible: Star System-scale energy anomaly | Moderate dimensional alteration | A Star System where multiple Worlds carry Sacred Ground designations. The density of Sacred presence makes the entire system exceptional. Known across its Star Cluster. |
| SS-9 | Sacred System (Apex) | Class 7 (transcendent) in core zones | Affinity aligned | Apex-grade Sacred Ground (S7–S8) present; System-scale energy resonance | Significant dimensional overlap across portions of the system | A Star System organized around an Apex-grade Sacred Ground. The Sacred Ground's influence reaches beyond its host World to shape the entire system's energy profile. Known across its Star Quadrant or Galaxy. |
| SS-10 | Dimensional System | Class 4–7 | Variable | Dimensional feature of System-scale magnitude — Stable Rift connecting across Star Systems, Resonance Plane access at System scale, or confirmed dimensional layer overlap | Active dimensional layer overlap or cross-system rift connection | A Star System distinguished by a major dimensional feature that operates at Star System scale. The dimensional feature defines the system's strategic and cultivation significance independent of its energy class. |
