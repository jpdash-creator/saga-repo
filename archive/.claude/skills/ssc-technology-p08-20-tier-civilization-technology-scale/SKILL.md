## Rule Text (Verbatim Source — Authoritative)
## Area 3 — The 20-Tier Civilization-Technology Scale

The Starward Saga uses a 20-Tier Civilization-Technology Scale built on the Kardashev Scale's foundational energy-measurement framework, extended to cover the full power range from primitive settlement to absolute existence. Each tier measures civilizational mastery of energy and its translation into technological capability across all fourteen domains.

### 3.1 — Scale Principles

**Energy Mastery Is the Foundation:** Each tier is defined primarily by the civilization's total accessible energy budget — the aggregate amount of energy it can harness, store, convert, and deploy. All other technological capabilities follow from energy mastery. The tier description specifies the floor, not the ceiling, of what that energy budget enables.

**Scale Is Geometric:** Each tier is a geometric leap, not a linear step. The lower tiers (1–5) span energy budgets across roughly 14 orders of magnitude. The intermediate tiers (6–15) break up what were previously enormous jumps in the 12-tier system into finer granularity. The upper tiers (16–20) represent qualitative transformations beyond any finite energy quantity.

**Tier Is a Floor, Not a Portrait:** Reaching a technology tier means a civilization has achieved the minimum capabilities described for that tier across the majority of its domains (at least 7 of 14). The description is the threshold, not a complete portrait. Exceptional civilizations may substantially exceed a tier's floor in specific domains.

**Tiers 6–7 and 9–10 Are Genuine Thresholds:** The eight new intermediate tiers are not merely interpolations — they represent real civilizational thresholds. A Tier 6 civilization has genuinely crossed the barrier from planetary to multi-planetary capability. A Tier 9 civilization has genuinely crossed from single-stellar to multi-stellar operation. Each new tier marks a structural shift in what the civilization's infrastructure can do, not merely in the numbers on the energy budget.

**Cultivation and Technology Tiers Are Independent:** The 20-Tier Technology Scale and the cultivation realm ladder are two parallel scales. A civilization's position on one scale does not determine its position on the other. The Soft Correspondence Tendency Table (Area 4) describes what tends to co-occur — it does not prescribe what must co-occur. Authors should treat the tendency table as a world-building reference for typical cases, not as a constraint on exceptional civilizations.

---

### 3.2 — Tier 1: Settlement Scale

**Energy Budget:** Human and animal muscular energy plus fire. Organized energy access: ~10^3 to 10^6 watts.

**Scale of Reach:** A single Village to a small settlement cluster. Technology cannot be extended beyond what the settlement population can physically carry, maintain, and organize.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Stone/bone/early-metal tools; fire for heat, cooking, and light; wind/water for simple mills; oral and early symbolic communication; herbal medicine; primitive shelters and basic fortifications; animal domestication.

**Civilization Requirement:** Tribal or small-settlement organization (TA-1 / CCT-1 Subsistence equivalent).

**Key Threshold:** The beginning of organized tool use and communal knowledge storage.

*Historical Analogue: Pre-Bronze Age to very early Bronze Age human societies.*

---

### 3.3 — Tier 2: City Scale

**Energy Budget:** Improved mechanical energy, animal power, and early combustion. Organized energy access: ~10^6 to 10^9 watts.

**Scale of Reach:** City-states to early kingdoms. Technology can support organized social structures, trade networks, division of labor, and early administrative bureaucracy.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Water/wind mills; systematic metallurgy (bronze/iron); organized architecture (fortresses, aqueducts, temples); writing and record systems; systematic medicine and pharmacology; early chemistry; organized military formations with metal weapons; sailing ships; road networks.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-2 / CCT-2 Formative equivalent (organized city-state or kingdom governance).

**Key Threshold:** The emergence of information storage and large-scale social coordination beyond tribal limits.

*Historical Analogue: Bronze Age through Renaissance-era human societies.*

---

### 3.4 — Tier 3: Industrial/National Scale

**Energy Budget:** Steam and early electrical power. Organized energy access: ~10^9 to 10^12 watts.

**Scale of Reach:** Nation-states with continental logistics and global trade networks.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Industrial manufacturing at scale; electrical generation and early grid; modern chemistry (pharmaceuticals, fertilizers, explosives); vaccines and antibiotics; steam/rail transportation; early aviation; mass-circulation media; national-scale military with firearms and artillery; telegraph/early radio communication; early CPM containment technology.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-3 / CCT-3 Industrial equivalent (nation-state or empire).

**Key Threshold:** Energy ceases to be a limiting constraint for most purposes within national boundaries. The nation becomes the primary civilizational unit.

*Historical Analogue: 19th–early 20th century human societies.*

---

### 3.5 — Tier 4: Continental/Advanced Industrial Scale

**Energy Budget:** Nuclear fission and advanced conventional fuels. Organized energy access: ~10^12 to 10^16 watts.

**Scale of Reach:** Full planetary coverage with globally integrated infrastructure systems.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Nuclear power; large-scale electrical grids; digital computers and early internet equivalent; early AI (pattern recognition, specialist decision systems); gene sequencing and early gene editing; organ transplants; modern military with precision guided weapons and nuclear deterrence; early satellites and space probes; advanced materials (polymers, composites, early nanotechnology); CPM-powered technology at augmentative integration level.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-4 / CCT-4 Planetary equivalent (global or continental governance architecture).

**Key Threshold:** The planet becomes a single integrated technological system. Nuclear capability creates existential risk awareness for the first time.

*Historical Analogue: Late 20th–early 21st century human societies.*

---

### 3.6 — Tier 5: Planetary Scale (Kardashev Type I Equivalent)

**Energy Budget:** Fusion power and complete planetary energy capture. Approximately 10^16 to 10^17 watts — the full energy budget of a planet-star system, fully harvested at the planetary surface and orbit.

**Scale of Reach:** Complete planetary integration with early interplanetary capability.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Fusion power standard; quantum computing; advanced AI approaching AGI threshold; full automation of manufacturing; molecular assembly and nanofabrication; precision gene editing and nanomedicine; lifespan extension to 200–500 years; orbital spaceflight as routine; early interplanetary capability; directed energy weapons; orbital defense systems; powered exosuits; Technology-Relic Class 1 and 2 hybrids achievable; Model B cultivation-technology integration achievable.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-5 / CCT-5 Multi-World equivalent (interplanetary governance beginning).

**Key Threshold:** The planet is fully mastered. The next threshold — moving beyond the home world as a primary operational domain — begins here.

---

### 3.7 — Tier 6: Inner Solar System Scale *(New Tier)*

**Energy Budget:** Inner planetary zone energy networks. Approximately 10^18 to 10^22 watts — the combined energy budget of inner planetary bodies, close stellar proximity collection, and inner-belt resource energy.

**Scale of Reach:** Inner solar system fully operational as a civilizational domain; outer system as active exploration frontier.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Interplanetary transport as routine commerce; vacuum and low-gravity manufacturing as standard; asteroid mining and inner-belt industrial operations; multi-world habitation standard; interplanetary defense and military networks; inner system sensor coverage in real time; early outer system exploration missions; lifespan extension to 10,000+ years becoming accessible.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-6 / CCT-6 Solarian equivalent (inner Star System governance beginning; at minimum, multi-World governance with interplanetary administration covering the inner Star System).

**Key Threshold:** The home world ceases to be the civilization's center of gravity. The civilization's infrastructure distributes across multiple worlds as a matter of standard operation, not exceptional effort.

---

### 3.8 — Tier 7: Outer Solar System Scale *(New Tier)*

**Energy Budget:** Full stellar system energy infrastructure including outer planets and deep space. Approximately 10^22 to 10^24 watts — total stellar system energy budget excluding direct stellar capture.

**Scale of Reach:** Full stellar system as operational domain; early interstellar reach as a research/exploration project.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Outer planetary and deep-space operations as routine; gas giant resource extraction at industrial scale; construction and habitation in deep solar orbit; complete stellar system logistics network; early stellar physics engineering (observation and small-scale manipulation); first interstellar probe missions; stellar system-scale military infrastructure; AGI systems deployed system-wide.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-7 / CCT-7 Stellar equivalent (full Star System governance beginning).

**Key Threshold:** The entire stellar system — including deep space and outer planets — becomes integrated as a single civilizational infrastructure. The civilization no longer depends on any particular planet; it depends on the system as a whole.

---

### 3.9 — Tier 8: Solar System / Stellar Scale (Kardashev Type II Equivalent)

> **FTL Governance Note — Tier 8 vs. Tier 9:** Tier 8 unlocks **FTL communication** (interstellar data transmission faster than light — Type 2 Information Technology; see Section 2.2 Level 8) but does NOT unlock standard FTL travel. FTL travel prototypes appear in **late Tier 8** (Part 15, Section 11.1), but sustainable FTL travel becomes standard at **Tier 9** (Part 15, Section 11.2). Writers should not depict a Tier 8 civilization as routinely using FTL starships for travel — they can communicate instantly across star systems but still rely on near-c ships and generation ships for physical transit. Prototype FTL research vessels exist at late Tier 8 but are not standard logistics infrastructure.

**Energy Budget:** Full stellar energy harnessed via Dyson sphere or equivalent swarm technology. Approximately 10^26 watts — the entire energy output of a sun-class star.

**Scale of Reach:** Full solar system development with early interstellar reach.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Stellar energy capture; true AGI and early ASI; full biological reconstruction and consciousness transfer; biological immortality technology; interplanetary travel as routine; early interstellar missions; planetary terraforming fully operational; megastructure construction (Dyson components, O'Neill cylinders at full scale); interplanetary weapons systems; planetary shielding; Technology-Relic Class 3 integrated hybrids; Model C cultivation-technology integration standard; CPM Divine Liquid interface possible.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-8 / CCT-8 Interstellar equivalent (early interstellar reach; first neighboring Star System governance beginning).

**Key Threshold:** The star itself becomes the civilization's primary energy infrastructure. The civilization's capability becomes effectively unconstrained within its own stellar system.

---

### 3.10 — Tier 9: Multi-Star System Scale *(New Tier)*

**Energy Budget:** Multiple full stellar energy budgets combined. Approximately 10^27 to 10^28 watts — the combined output of 2–10 fully harnessed star systems.

**Scale of Reach:** Multiple star systems as an integrated civilizational domain; stellar cluster as the civilization's medium-term expansion space.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** FTL travel becoming standard (warp or equivalent); multi-stellar manufacturing and logistics networks; multi-stellar military coordination; populations spread across multiple star systems as permanent infrastructure; stellar engineering (adjusting stellar output through direct intervention) at multiple sites; multi-stellar cultivation integration networks; ASI systems operating across multiple stars coherently.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-9 / CCT-9 Expansionary equivalent (multi-Star System governance).

**Key Threshold:** The civilization's operational domain no longer fits within a single star system. The fundamental shift from stellar to inter-stellar as the default scale of operation.

---

### 3.11 — Tier 10: Stellar Cluster Scale *(New Tier)*

**Energy Budget:** Stellar cluster total energy budget. Approximately 10^29 to 10^32 watts — the combined stellar output of dozens to hundreds of star systems.

**Scale of Reach:** An entire stellar cluster as the operational domain.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Stellar cluster transit infrastructure (FTL cluster-wide); cluster-scale manufacturing networks; cluster-scale military coordination; stellar engineering for cluster-wide energy optimization; cluster-scale sensor coverage; formation array networks spanning multiple stellar systems; cultivation integration infrastructure at cluster scale; computational intelligence spanning the full cluster.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-10 / CCT-10 Clusteral equivalent (Star Cluster-scale governance).

**Key Threshold:** The civilization's minimum viable unit of operation becomes the stellar cluster rather than the individual star system. It can lose individual star systems without strategic loss.

---

### 3.12 — Tier 11: Galactic Scale (Kardashev Type III Equivalent)

**Energy Budget:** Full galactic energy harnessing. Approximately 10^36 to 10^37 watts — the total stellar energy output of an entire galaxy.

**Scale of Reach:** The entire galaxy as operational domain.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** FTL travel as absolute standard capability; artificial superintelligence operating at galactic cognitive scale; biological immortality universally standard; megastructures at galactic scale (Ringworld-class, Matrioshka brains); stellar engineering controlling stellar processes across galactic arms; galactic navigation networks; stellar weapons capability; Model D unified cultivation-technology integration standard; CPM conversion efficiency 85–95%; galaxy-scale cultivation infrastructure.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-11 / CCT-11 Galactic equivalent (Galaxy-scale governance architecture).

**Key Threshold:** The galaxy itself is the civilization's operational unit. Stellar scale events become local events within the civilization's context.

---

### 3.13 — Tier 12: Local Group Scale *(New Tier)*

**Energy Budget:** Combined energy from multiple fully harnessed galaxies — the Local Group's total stellar output. Approximately 10^38 to 10^40 watts.

**Scale of Reach:** The Local Group of galaxies as a civilizational domain.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Cross-galactic transit as routine commerce; cross-galactic military networks; inter-galactic sensor systems; manufacturing coordinated across multiple galaxies; information networks spanning the Local Group; cultivation integration infrastructure spanning multiple galaxies; multi-galactic ecological management begins.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-12 / CCT-12 Intergalactic equivalent (multi-Galaxy / Local Group-scale governance).

**Key Threshold:** The civilization's operational scale has outgrown a single galaxy. Individual galaxies become administrative regions rather than civilizational boundaries.

---

### 3.14 — Tier 13: Multi-Galactic / Cluster Scale

**Energy Budget:** Multiple full galactic energy budgets plus galaxy cluster dynamics. Approximately 10^46+ watts.

**Scale of Reach:** Multiple galaxies or a galaxy cluster as operational domain.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Wormhole transit network infrastructure as standard inter-galactic transit; galaxy-scale megastructures; exotic matter production at industrial scale; multi-galactic AI networks; dark matter partial harnessing; systems capable of altering stellar topology at multi-galactic scale; CPM conversion efficiency approaching theoretical maximum across the cluster.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-13 / CCT-13 Cosmological equivalent (Galaxy Cluster governance).

**Key Threshold:** The galaxy cluster becomes the civilization's single unified operational domain.

---

### 3.15 — Tier 14: Galaxy Supercluster Scale *(New Tier)*

**Energy Budget:** Galaxy supercluster total energy budget — spanning superclusters containing thousands of galaxy clusters. Approximately 10^48 to 10^50 watts.

**Scale of Reach:** A galaxy supercluster as the operational domain.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Supercluster transit networks; coordinated operations across hundreds of galaxy clusters; weapons and defense systems at supercluster scale; manufacturing networks coordinated across supercluster distances; information architecture spanning the full supercluster; dark energy partial harnessing begins.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-14 / CCT-14 Superclusteral equivalent (Galaxy Supercluster-scale governance).

**Key Threshold:** The civilization's operational domain now spans structures that are at the upper edge of large-scale cosmic structure.

---

### 3.16 — Tier 15: Observable Universe Fringe Scale *(New Tier)*

**Energy Budget:** Access to cosmological-scale energy infrastructure near the edge of the observable universe. Approximately 10^52 to 10^58 watts.

**Scale of Reach:** The civilization operates across cosmological distances — its infrastructure reaches the fringe of the observable universe.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Near-universal transit networks; information systems spanning cosmological distances; dark energy substantial harnessing; manufacturing systems accounting for universal expansion in logistics; surveillance networks reaching the observable universe fringe; operations that must account for the universe's large-scale structure and expansion in all planning.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-15 / CCT-15 Liminal-Universal equivalent (near-universal governance framework).

**Key Threshold:** The civilization's operational horizon is the observable universe itself. It now must contend with cosmic expansion as an engineering constraint.

---

### 3.17 — Tier 16: Universal Scale

**Energy Budget:** Universal energy access — zero-point energy, dark matter/dark energy, spacetime vacuum fluctuation engineering. All energy available within the universe.

**Scale of Reach:** The entire universe as operational domain.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Universal travel technology; spacetime engineering as standard; manipulation of physical constants in localized regions; universal information fabric access; universal cultivation interface technology; universal AI at full operational capability; dark matter and dark energy full harnessing; total universal energy budget accessible.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-16 / CCT-16 Universal equivalent (Universe-scale governance).

**Key Threshold:** The civilization operates at the scale of the universe itself. Physics is an engineering material, not just a constraint.

---

### 3.18 — Tier 17: Trans-Universal Scale *(New Tier)*

**Energy Budget:** Energy drawn through trans-universal conduits from other universes. Exceeds single-universe energy budget.

**Scale of Reach:** Technology and operations begin to reach across universal boundaries.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Early trans-universal communication; manufacturing bridging two universes; early trans-universal transit routes; information architecture beginning to span universal boundaries; military capability reaching beyond a single universe; early cross-universal cultivation interface.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-17 / CCT-17 Transversal equivalent (trans-universal governance beginning).

**Key Threshold:** The civilization's home universe is no longer its operational boundary. Universal boundaries become navigable obstacles rather than absolute limits.

---

### 3.19 — Tier 18: Multi-Universal Scale

**Energy Budget:** Access to energy across multiple distinct universes simultaneously.

**Scale of Reach:** Multiple universes as operational domain simultaneously.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Reliable multi-universal transit; multi-universal manufacturing and computation; communication across universal boundaries; military systems capable of inter-universal operations; cultivation interface technology operating across universal substrates.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-18 / CCT-18 Multiversal equivalent (Multiverse / multi-universal governance).

**Key Threshold:** Universal boundaries are infrastructure elements rather than limits.

---

### 3.20 — Tier 19: Dimensional / Existential Scale

**Energy Budget:** Dimensional energy — energy from the architecture of reality itself, not from physical processes within it.

**Scale of Reach:** Technology that operates across dimensional layers of reality.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** Dimensional manufacturing, transit, warfare, sensing, and communication; technology whose existence spans dimensional layers; manipulation of the dimensional structure of reality; cultivation integration technology bridging dimensional layers.

**Civilization Requirement:** TA-19 / CCT-19 Dimensional equivalent (dimensional governance).

**Key Threshold:** The civilization operates not merely within reality but on the structure of reality itself.

---

### 3.21 — Tier 20: Absolute Scale

**Energy Budget:** The fundamental force underlying all existence. No finite quantity expresses this budget relative to lower tiers.

**Scale of Reach:** Technology that operates at the level of existence itself — beyond universe, beyond dimension.

**Core Capabilities Unlocked:** All twenty capability ceilings reached simultaneously. The theoretical maximum of what any technology can do or be.

*Narrative Status: Tier 20 technology is narrative-mythological — equivalent to Primordial Grade relics (Part 12) in narrative function. It cannot be an operational background element of a narrative arc. Authors may reference it as legend, cosmological fact, or distant horizon. It may not be deployed as a character-accessible tool within any narrative phase governed by the Technology Ceiling law.*
