## Area 11 — Financial and Banking Factions

### 11.1 — Overview: Financial and Banking Factions (FBF)

Financial and Banking Factions are organized institutions whose primary purpose is the management, storage, lending, exchange, and multiplication of capital — whether that capital is denominated in CPM substances, technological currency, precious materials, information assets, or abstract financial instruments. Banking factions underpin the economic infrastructure of civilizations at Kingdom scale and above; without stable financial institutions, large-scale commercial trade and government tax systems become operationally impossible.

**Defining Characteristics:**
- **Primary Purpose:** Capital management, credit provision, monetary system maintenance, financial services
- **Core Resource:** Capital reserves, depositor relationships, legal charter, vault security, political protection
- **Security Requirement:** Banking factions require exceptional security capability — their vaults contain the most concentrated material wealth in their operational area
- **Natural Scale Range:** Kingdom (SCL-5) through Multiverse (SCL-15)

### 11.2 — Financial Faction Classifications

| Financial Faction Type | Services | Scale | Capital Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Money Changers / Exchange Houses | Currency exchange; basic value storage | SCL-4 to SCL-5 | Precious materials; early CPM grade |
| Lending Houses | Credit provision; collateral loans | SCL-4 to SCL-6 | Land, goods, CPM Grade 1–8 collateral |
| Merchant Banks | Commercial banking; trade finance; investment | SCL-5 to SCL-8 | Diversified; trade route stakes |
| Central Banking Institutions | Monetary policy; government banking; currency issuance | SCL-5 to SCL-9 | State mandate; reserve CPM; tax revenue |
| Investment Corporations | Capital deployment to enterprises; return on equity | SCL-6 to SCL-10 | Diverse portfolio; fractional ownership stakes |
| Cultivation Resource Banks | Specialized CPM lending; pill futures; resource derivatives | SCL-6 to SCL-11 | CPM Grade reserves as core capital |
| Interstellar Financial Conglomerates | Cross-civilization banking; FTL-enabled capital movement | SCL-9 to SCL-12 | Multi-currency; cross-civilization asset base |
| Galactic Reserve Banks | Galactic-scale monetary governance; reserve currency management | SCL-11 | Star-gate-distributed vault networks |
| Universal Financial Authority | Universe-scale financial stability governance | SCL-14 | Universal reserve assets; Tier 15+ secure vaults |

### 11.3 — Financial Faction Governance Rules

**Rule 11.3.1 — Financial Faction Authority Requires Legal Charter:**
No banking institution can operate without formal legal recognition from the government body of the territory in which it operates. Unlicensed banking is a criminal act in CCT-4+ civilizations.

**Rule 11.3.2 — Vault Security Governance:**
Financial faction vaults are governed security architecture. At Kingdom scale and above, vault security requires formation arrays (Part 12 governance) rated at minimum Tier equal to peak CPM grade stored. A vault storing Grade 18 CPM requires Tier 3 formation array security at minimum.

**Rule 11.3.3 — Banking Factions Are Non-Military by Charter:**
Banking factions are institutionally non-combatant — they do not participate in territorial conflicts and do not maintain offensive military capability. However, they maintain proportional defensive capability (vault protection) and contract with MSF factions for security provision.

**Rule 11.3.4 — Cross-Civilization Banking Requires Diplomatic Framework:**
Banking factions operating across civilization boundaries require formal inter-civilization treaty recognition of their operating charter. Trans-civilization banking without treaty support is functionally illegal in CCT-8+ civilizations.
