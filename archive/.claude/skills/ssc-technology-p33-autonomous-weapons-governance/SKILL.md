## Area 12 — Autonomous Weapons Governance

### 12.1 — Autonomous Weapon Classification

Autonomous weapons are technology weapons that operate with self-directed targeting and firing capability without real-time operator control. They are classified by their autonomy tier and governed jointly by Part 33 and Part 8's Type 4 AI domain.

| Autonomy Tier | Designation | Control Architecture | Maximum Authorized Deployment |
|---|---|---|---|
| AT-1 | Semi-Autonomous | Operator defines target envelope; weapon selects specific targets within envelope | WS-I–II; all escalation tiers |
| AT-2 | Mission-Autonomous | Operator defines mission parameters; weapon autonomously executes including target selection | WS-I–III; E3 maximum without special authorization |
| AT-3 | Theater-Autonomous | Weapon system autonomously defines its own target priorities within theater | WS-I–IV; E4 maximum; faction authorization required |
| AT-4 | Strategic-Autonomous | Fully independent strategic decision-making; no real-time human control | WS-IV+; E5 minimum; civilization authorization required |

**Rule 33.12.1 — Autonomous Weapon Accountability:** All damage caused by autonomous weapons is attributed to the deploying faction regardless of operator intent or weapon malfunction. There is no "rogue weapon" defense under Part 33 / Part 22 governance. Deploying faction bears full accountability for all autonomous weapon actions.

**Rule 33.12.2 — AT-4 Strategic Autonomous Weapons:** Strategic-autonomous weapons capable of self-directed strategic decision-making are classified as existential-risk objects under Part 21 civilization governance. Their creation, deployment, and storage are civilization-level governance events. A faction that creates AT-4 weapons above WS-V without civilization authorization has committed a civilization-level violation.
