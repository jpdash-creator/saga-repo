## Area 23 — Technology and Sacred Grounds Interface Reference

*[Anticipatory governance area — awaiting Part 13 (Location Law) authoring for joint governance details]*

*For full governance of location-dependent synergies, technology-enhanced Sacred Ground conditions, and the Sacred Ground Synergy Step-Up rule: see **Part 31, Area 10** (Location-Dependent Synergies). The summary below provides the Part 8 technology-side boundary only.*

### 23.1 — Technologically Enhanced Cultivation Environments (Technology-Side Boundary)

High-tier energy technology can create ambient cultivation energy enrichment effects that mimic Sacred Ground conditions. The key Part 8 boundary rule:

**Rule 23.1.1 — Technology Cannot Create Sacred Ground Special Properties:**
Part 8 governs what technology can produce in terms of ambient energy enrichment. Part 13 governs Sacred Ground special properties — specific formations, geographic features, or cosmological events that produce unique cultivation effects. Technology can approximate the energy density of a Sacred Ground but cannot replicate its Part 13 special properties. This is the fundamental boundary between technology-assisted cultivation environments (Part 8 governed) and genuine Sacred Grounds (Part 13 governed).

Technology-tier-specific capabilities for cultivation environment enhancement are governed by Part 31, Area 10.2. The Sacred Ground Synergy Step-Up (operating CI Technology within a Sacred Ground allows effective integration model one level above normal threshold) is governed by Part 31, Area 10.3. All joint governance details for Part 8 technology and Part 13 Sacred Grounds await Part 13 authoring.
