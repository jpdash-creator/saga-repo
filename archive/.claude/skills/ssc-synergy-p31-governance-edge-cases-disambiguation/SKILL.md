## Area 20 — Governance Edge Cases and Disambiguation

### 20.1 — When Synergy Claims Conflict with Independence Principles

If a narrative or governance argument proposes that a civilization's technology tier should be limited by its cultivation tier (or vice versa), this conflicts with P31-4 (Synergy Does Not Violate Track Independence) and Part 8 P8-2.1 (Technology Ceiling Is Not Cultivation-Derived). The resolution: the tracks are independent; the Soft Correspondence Tendency Table (Area 13) describes tendency, not law. No narrative decision may create a governance requirement that one track's advancement is limited by the other's level without Part 28 authorization.

### 20.2 — When Synergy Appears to Exceed Canonical Limits

If a synergy configuration appears to produce an output exceeding the realm ceiling or Technology Ceiling, the resolution is:
1. Verify the integration model — is the claimed model actually supported by both thresholds?
2. Verify the CPM grade — is the claimed energy depth actually available?
3. Verify the civilization tier — is the claimed synergy category institutionally accessible?
4. Verify location — is a Sacred Ground Synergy Step-Up being incorrectly applied?

If all four verify correctly and the output still appears to exceed canonical limits, the claim requires Part 28 (Creative Flexibility Law) authorization.

### 20.3 — Synergy in Technology-Only or Cultivation-Only Civilizations

**Technology-Only Civilizations:**
Technology-only civilizations with no cultivation practitioners have no synergy capacity — not because synergy is denied to them, but because there is no cultivation track to contribute. CI Technology (Domain 12) requires cultivation practitioners as a precondition — without them, Domain 12 level is zero and all synergy categories requiring CI Technology are inaccessible. Technology-only civilizations operate fully on all 13 non-CI domains with zero synergy of any type.

**Cultivation-Only Civilizations:**
Cultivation-only civilizations with negligible technology have the cultivation track but not the technology infrastructure to interface with it. They may have informal Model A co-existence (cultivators using simple tools) but no integration models above A. Synergy in the formal sense requires CI Technology infrastructure; without technology development, synergy is not accessible.

### 20.4 — Synergy Governance Hierarchy

When any governance conflict arises between this Part and another Part on a synergy question:
1. Part 1 (Meta-Canon Apex Law) is supreme in all governance conflicts
2. Part 3 (Cultivation Power Law) governs all cultivation-side laws — its realm ceiling, breakthrough requirements, and physical laws are not overrideable by Part 31
3. Part 8 (Technology Scale Law) governs all technology-side laws — its Technology Ceiling and domain level requirements are not overrideable by Part 31
4. Part 31 governs synergy outputs — what the combination produces, not what either track's ceiling is
5. Part 7 and Part 12 govern the cultivation and relic object layers of integration respectively
6. Part 28 governs authorized exceptions to all of the above
