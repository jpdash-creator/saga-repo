## Area 25 — Author Quick Reference

### 25.1 — Five Questions Before Writing a Cultural Scene

1. **What CPT(s) govern this character's cultural orientation?** (Area 9 — seven canonical paradigm types)
2. **What is this character's dominant Class Axis position?** (Area 6 — six axes; which is dominant here?)
3. **What Formation Axis is most active in this scene?** (Area 2 — five axes; which governs this social interaction?)
4. **What specific social conventions apply here?** (Area 10 — greeting, honorific, dress, taboo)
5. **What cultural difference between the characters in this scene generates the most productive tension?**
