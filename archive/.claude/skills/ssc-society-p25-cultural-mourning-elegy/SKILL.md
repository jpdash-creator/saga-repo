## Area 18 — Cultural Mourning and Elegy

### 18.1 — Cultural Mourning Conventions

Cultural loss — the death of a tradition, the destruction of a cultural anchor site, the extinction of a cultural lineage — requires its own mourning protocol. Canonical mourning forms by CPT:

| CPT | Cultural Loss Mourning Form | Duration Convention | Prohibition During Mourning |
|-----|-----------------------------|--------------------|-----------------------------|
| CPT-1 | Three-day mourning fast; elegy recitation before the destroyed site or its direction | Three days formal; one cycle (year/season) informal | Celebration; new cultivation advancement ceremony |
| CPT-2 | Combat suspension for one day; naming of the lost tradition's greatest practitioners | One day formal | Challenge combat (combat honoring the fallen is permitted) |
| CPT-3 | Voluntary market closure; purchase of last available cultural artifacts | Market-day duration | New contracts (ongoing contracts continue) |
| CPT-4 | Total silence and seclusion; recording of everything still remembered | Duration of available memory | Any communication with those outside the community |
| CPT-5 | Gathering of all bearers of the lost tradition's cultural influence; documentation ceremony | Until documentation complete | Claims that the culture has "fully survived" |
| CPT-6 | Covenant-renewal ceremony; prayer for the cultural dead | One complete covenant cycle | Celebration; ordinary covenant business |
| CPT-7 | Open acknowledgment of loss; voluntary gathering of any who valued the culture | Duration of voluntary assembly | Coerced attendance; mandatory mourning |
