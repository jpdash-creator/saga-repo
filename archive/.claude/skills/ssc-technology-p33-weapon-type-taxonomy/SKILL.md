## Area 2 — Weapon Type Taxonomy

### 2.1 — Primary Weapon Type Categories

All weapons are classified into one of seven primary weapon type categories. Type determines the weapon's operational mechanism, energy requirement, cultivation interaction properties, and countermeasure profile. Types are mutually exclusive at the primary classification level; a weapon with multiple delivery mechanisms receives a primary type and one or more secondary type modifiers (e.g., *Lancefire-6/ShieldPierce* for a directed-energy weapon with a penetrating formation element).

| Type Code | Type Name | Mechanism | Cultivation Interaction Class |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-KIN | Kinetic Strike Type | Physical mass delivery — ballistic, railgun, mass driver, orbital kinetic | CIC-I (Physical) |
| T-RAY | Directed Ray Type | Focused energy beam delivery — laser, particle beam, plasma jet, maser | CIC-II (Energetic) |
| T-BURST | Detonation Burst Type | Explosive energy release at or near target — chemical, nuclear, antimatter, vacuum collapse | CIC-III (Area Energy) |
| T-FORM | Formation Array Type | Cultivation formation-matrix-derived weapon effect — qi distortion, field suppression, void collapse | CIC-IV (Spiritual Field) |
| T-SYN | Synergy Strike Type | Cultivation-technology hybrid weapon; requires SYN configuration; integrated practitioner activation | CIC-V (Dual Track) |
| T-PSI | Consciousness Targeting Type | Weapons that target soul architecture, mental continuity, or spiritual substrate rather than physical body | CIC-VI (Soul) |
| T-VEC | Vector Payload Type | Weapons that deliver a payload agent (biological vector, nanite swarm, CPM contaminator, ecosystem disruptor) | CIC-VII (Systemic) |

**Type Note:** Weapons with no cultivation interaction capability are CIC-I (physical) by default, as the weapon delivers purely physical force that cultivation defense must resist through raw physical blocking.

### 2.2 — Kinetic Strike Type (T-KIN) — Full Taxonomy

Kinetic Strike weapons deliver destructive force through the physical impact of mass. All weapons of this type obey the same underlying physics: kinetic energy scales with mass and the square of velocity. At technology Tier 7+, kinetic weapons begin to enter relativistic velocity regimes where relativistic mass increase becomes significant.

**Named Kinetic Strike Sub-Types (by tier range):**

| Sub-Type Name | Tier Range | Mechanism | Scale Class Typical |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shard-Thrower | Tier 1 | Stone, bone, or early metal projectile propelled by muscle power; slings, bows | WS-I |
| Iron-Lance | Tier 2 | Metal crossbow bolt, arrow, early trebuchet stone, spear-thrower | WS-I–II |
| Powder-Shot | Tier 3 | Gunpowder-propelled solid or fragmenting round; cannon, musket, rifle, artillery shell | WS-I–III |
| Hardstrike Round | Tier 4 | Modern firearms round; tank penetrator; guided kinetic munition; bunker-buster | WS-I–III |
| Orbital Impactor | Tier 5–6 | Tungsten rod or mass-driver projectile deployed from orbital altitude; early rods-from-god equivalent | WS-III–IV |
| Void Hammer | Tier 7 | Relativistic kinetic bombardment — mass accelerated to 0.1–0.5c, targeted from stellar system distances | WS-IV–V |
| Star-Lance | Tier 8–9 | Multi-stellar relativistic payload; mass accelerated to 0.5–0.99c; capable of planetary core penetration | WS-V–VI |
| Event Spike | Tier 10 | Near-lightspeed compressed mass payload with gravitational focus; pierces planetary magnetic fields; effective against stellar targets | WS-VI |
| Gravity Javelin | Tier 11 | Controlled micro-black-hole or neutron-star-density mass packet delivered at or near c; collapses stellar objects on impact | WS-VI–VII |
| Singularity Driver | Tier 12–14 | Engineered singularity payload with contained collapse trigger; activates on target; galactic topology disruption capable | WS-VII |
| Existence Hammer | Tier 15–20 | Absolute-force mass delivery at cosmological scale; mechanism partially deferred; WS-VII absolute category | WS-VII |

**Kinetic Defense Note:** Kinetic weapons below Tier 7 can be deflected by sufficiently advanced cultivation body-hardening (Diamond Body equivalent or above) or by Tier-appropriate technology shielding. Above Tier 7, kinetic energy delivery exceeds any cultivation physical defense threshold and requires dedicated technology shielding.

### 2.3 — Directed Ray Type (T-RAY) — Full Taxonomy

Directed Ray weapons deliver focused energy in a directional beam — laser light, charged particle stream, superheated plasma jet, or similar directed-energy mechanism. They are characterized by light-speed or near-light-speed delivery (for laser/EM weapons), high precision, and the ability to penetrate soft targets without explosive area effect.

**Named Directed Ray Sub-Types:**

| Sub-Type Name | Tier Range | Mechanism | Scale Class Typical |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spark-Lash | Tier 3–4 | Early military laser; low-power directed energy; anti-drone and optical/sensor blinding | WS-I–II |
| Burnline | Tier 4 | High-energy laser weapon; anti-vehicle and anti-materiel; metal cutting at significant range | WS-I–II |
| Particle Scorch | Tier 5 | Military particle beam — charged proton or electron beam with significant penetrating power | WS-I–III |
| Plasma Needle | Tier 5–6 | Magnetically confined plasma bolt; penetrating thermal and ionization damage; early ship armament | WS-I–III |
| Helio-Lance | Tier 6–7 | High-yield orbital or stellar system directed energy; precise beam capable of continental-scale damage at full output | WS-II–IV |
| Void Ray | Tier 8 | Interstellar-range focused energy weapon; crossing star-system distances with beam coherence maintained; WS-IV at point of impact | WS-IV–V |
| Annihilation Beam | Tier 9–10 | Matter-antimatter annihilation beam; pure energy delivery at near-total conversion efficiency; effective at stellar distances | WS-V–VI |
| Stellar Scalpel | Tier 11 | Galaxy-range coherent energy weapon capable of targeting stellar cores; triggers stellar events on hit | WS-VI–VII |
| Void Thread | Tier 12–14 | Spacetime-cutting directed weapon; severs local spacetime fabric along beam path; area effect along line | WS-VII |
| Absolute Ray | Tier 15–20 | Deferred cosmological energy projection; WS-VII absolute category | WS-VII |

### 2.4 — Detonation Burst Type (T-BURST) — Full Taxonomy

Detonation Burst weapons release large quantities of energy at or near a target through controlled or uncontrolled energetic reactions. They are characterized by area effect, omnidirectional energy release, and thermal/concussive/radiation delivery. This type includes chemical explosives, nuclear and thermonuclear devices, antimatter detonation, and exotic matter collapse events.

**Named Detonation Burst Sub-Types:**

| Sub-Type Name | Tier Range | Mechanism | Scale Class Typical |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire-Shard | Tier 1–2 | Early incendiary device; pitch fire, early explosive compounds; hand-thrown | WS-I–II |
| Thunder-Charge | Tier 3 | Gunpowder explosive; grenade, mine, artillery shell; early dynamite equivalent | WS-I–III |
| Fission Core | Tier 4 | Nuclear fission bomb; early atomic device; tactical to city-scale yield | WS-III–V |
| Fusion Crown | Tier 4–5 | Thermonuclear device; hydrogen bomb equivalent; continental-yield ceiling | WS-IV–V |
| Plasma Storm | Tier 5–6 | Controlled plasma detonation weapon; orbital deployment; stellar-atmosphere coupling possible at Tier 6 | WS-III–V |
| Void-Splitter | Tier 7 | Vacuum-energy collapse bomb; triggers localized spacetime energy release; WS-V minimum | WS-V–VI |
| Antimatter Burst | Tier 8–9 | Pure antimatter payload detonation; matter-antimatter conversion yields at planetary or stellar scales depending on payload mass | WS-V–VII |
| Stellar Igniter | Tier 9–10 | Weapon designed to trigger premature stellar fusion events or stellar collapse; affects entire star system | WS-VII |
| Galactic Bomb | Tier 11–12 | Galaxy-topology disruptive detonation; triggers cascade effects across galactic arms | WS-VII |
| Absolute Burst | Tier 13–20 | Escalating cosmological detonation classes; partially deferred for higher tiers | WS-VII |

**Burst Defense Note:** At WS-V+ yield, no individual cultivation defense can absorb or deflect Detonation Burst weapons. Survival at close range requires civilization-grade shielding technology at appropriate tier or higher. This is the weapon class most consistently lethal against Divine-tier and below cultivation practitioners.

### 2.5 — Formation Array Type (T-FORM) — Full Taxonomy

Formation Array weapons operate through cultivation formation matrices embedded in technology platforms. The weapon effect is spiritual-physical rather than purely energetic — it manipulates qi fields, disrupts spiritual substrate, creates vacuum voids in ambient qi, or projects formation-defined force shapes at targets. T-FORM weapons are jointly governed by Part 12 (formation array rules) and Part 33.

**Named Formation Array Sub-Types:**

| Sub-Type Name | Tier Range | Mechanism | Scale Class Typical |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qi-Scatter Emitter | Tier 3–4 | Disrupts ambient qi field in area; non-lethal to cultivators at early versions; disrupts lower-tier formations | WS-II–III |
| Spirit-Crack Array | Tier 4–5 | Formation array that projects a fragmentation wave through spiritual substrate; damages active qi circulation | WS-I–III |
| Void-Field Projector | Tier 5–6 | Creates localized void of qi; all qi-dependent techniques fail inside field boundary; suppression weapon | WS-II–IV |
| Formation Disassembler | Tier 6–7 | Technology platform broadcasting a counter-formation frequency; dismantles enemy formation arrays remotely | WS-II–IV |
| Soul-Resonance Cannon | Tier 7–8 | Projects resonance waves through spiritual substrate; resonance frequency matched to human soul-body interface; non-explosive soul disruption | WS-I–III |
| Heaven-Shatter Array | Tier 9–10 | Massive formation-grid weapon; projects formation-space distortion at stellar distances; dismantles cultivation infrastructure at range | WS-IV–VI |
| Primordium Splitter | Tier 11–14 | Formation weapon targeting the Primordium-level substrate; reality distortion in targeted zones | WS-VI–VII |
| Absolute Formation Weapon | Tier 15–20 | Deferred — formation weapons at cosmological tier; absolute-field disruption | WS-VII |

**Cultivation Interaction Note:** Formation Array weapons interact directly with cultivation at every tier. They are the primary weapon class that can harm cultivators through spiritual rather than physical mechanisms. Higher-tier cultivation defense trained specifically for qi-disruption is more effective against T-FORM than against T-KIN weapons of the same tier.

### 2.6 — Synergy Strike Type (T-SYN) — Full Taxonomy

Synergy Strike weapons are cultivation-technology hybrid weapons that require an Integrated Practitioner (Part 31 governance) as their primary operator. The weapon uses a technology platform to amplify, focus, and deliver cultivation energy in ways neither track can achieve alone. T-SYN weapons require SYN configuration code and are jointly governed by Part 31 and Part 33.

**Named Synergy Strike Sub-Types:**

| Sub-Type Name | Tier Range | Integration Model Required | Mechanism | Scale Class Typical |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qi-Amplifier Frame | Tier 4–5 | Integration Model A (Part 31) | Exosuit or handheld frame that channels operator's qi through technology amplifier coils; qi output ×3–×10 base amplification | WS-I–II |
| Resonance Gauntlet | Tier 5–6 | Integration Model B | Arm-mounted technology system that shapes and projects qi into precision strike forms; penetrating qi-needle or qi-blade effects | WS-I–II |
| Harmony Cannon | Tier 6–7 | Integration Model B–C | Shoulder/vehicle-mounted dual-track weapon; technology targeting and range; qi energy payload; no non-integrated operator capable of full operation | WS-II–IV |
| Unity Lance | Tier 7–8 | Integration Model C | Heavy vehicle or emplacement synergy weapon; unified qi-technology beam; energy output exceeds either track's solo ceiling | WS-III–V |
| Apex Confluence Weapon | Tier 8–10 | Integration Model D | The highest tier of synergy weapon; technology and cultivation unified into a single delivery system that exceeds both tracks' individual ceilings; only Apex Integration Practitioners can operate | WS-IV–VI |
| Transcendence Ordnance | Tier 11+ | Beyond current Integration Models | Deferred — post-Integration-Model-D weapons; partially governed by future Part 31 expansion | WS-VI–VII |

### 2.7 — Consciousness Targeting Type (T-PSI) — Full Taxonomy

Consciousness Targeting weapons are designed to attack the soul, mental continuity, or spiritual substrate of a target rather than its physical body. They do not necessarily produce physical destruction — a perfectly executed T-PSI weapon hit may leave the target's body physically unharmed while inflicting soul damage, memory erasure, spiritual fragmentation, or consciousness severance. These weapons are among the most tightly governed in the SSC Canon and require special authorization above WS-II.

**Named Consciousness Targeting Sub-Types:**

| Sub-Type Name | Tier Range | Mechanism | Governance Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Memory-Wipe Pulse | Tier 4–5 | Electrical/magnetic disruption of neural architecture; temporary to permanent memory loss at close range | Standard (WS-I) |
| Soul-Sting Array | Tier 5–6 | Formation array component tuned to spiritual substrate resonance; causes pain-state or confusion in cultivators without physical injury | Restricted (WS-I–II) |
| Spirit-Fracture Beam | Tier 6–7 | Targeted soul-layer disruption; does not damage Core but disrupts soul-body connection; cultivation techniques disabled for duration | Restricted (WS-I–II) |
| Core-Crack Projector | Tier 8–9 | Weapon capable of directly attacking cultivation Core structure via combined spiritual-technology interface; extreme range with precision targeting | High Restriction (WS-I) |
| Soul-Sever Lance | Tier 9–10 | Weapon designed to sever soul from physical body without killing the body; most feared weapon class among cultivators; use classified as extreme war crime under major faction codes | Absolute Restriction |
| Consciousness Erase Engine | Tier 11–14 | Mass-scale soul architecture disruption; entire planetary population can be consciousness-targeted simultaneously; civilizational-scale soul weapon | Part 21 / E7 governance only |

**Critical Governance Rule (Rule 33.7.1 [citation note: this Area-2 rule collides with the distinct Rule 33.7.1 "CPM Weapon Fuel Incompatibility" in `ssc-technology-p33-weapon-power-consumption-supply-architecture`, Area 7; following this Part's own Area-matched numbering convention, this rule would properly be Rule 33.2.1] — T-PSI Deployment Authorization):** All T-PSI weapons above WS-I require special authorization from the deploying faction's governing body (Part 22 governance). Soul-Sever Lance class weapons are considered an extreme war crime and will trigger E6+ escalation response from third-party civilizations under Part 21 civilization protection law regardless of faction alignment. Their use is a Part 21 civilization-level political event, not merely a battlefield one.

### 2.8 — Vector Payload Type (T-VEC) — Full Taxonomy

Vector Payload weapons deliver a payload agent rather than direct energy. The destructive mechanism is the agent — a biological organism, a nanite swarm, a CPM contaminant, an ecosystem disruptor, or an engineered cognitive vector. T-VEC weapons are the most heavily restricted class under Part 33 governance due to their uncontrolled propagation potential.

**Named Vector Payload Sub-Types:**

| Sub-Type Name | Tier Range | Mechanism | Governance Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plague Vial | Tier 3–4 | Biological pathogen delivery weapon; early battlefield disease vector; targeted or area-deployed | Special Authorization (WS-III+) |
| Cultivator-Bane Toxin | Tier 5–6 | Engineered biological agent specifically targeting meridian/qi-flow disruption; blocks cultivation temporarily or permanently | Faction High Authorization |
| Nano-Corrosive Swarm | Tier 5–7 | Nanite swarm delivered to target area; breaks down specific materials, blocks cultivation infrastructure, corrodes technology | Technology Authorization |
| CPM Poisoner | Tier 6–8 | Delivers CPM-contaminating agent into target CPM stockpiles or fuel systems; corrupts or destabilizes CPM at grade-level | Special Authorization |
| Ecosystem Eradicator | Tier 7–8 | Engineered biological vector targeting planetary biosphere; mass extinction-rate ecosystem disruption | Strategic Ordnance (WS-V+) |
| Cognitive Vector | Tier 8–10 | Engineered vector delivering artificial spiritual-substrate modification to target cultivator population; alters cultivation trajectory, loyalty architecture, or soul-alignment | Absolute Restriction |
| Reality Corruptor | Tier 11+ | Agents that corrupt the local physical substrate; alters local constants in target area; permanent area denial | Deferred — Absolute Restriction |

**Governance Rule (Rule 33.8.1 [citation note: following this Part's Area-matched numbering convention used everywhere else, this Area-2 rule would properly be Rule 33.2.2]  — T-VEC Propagation Liability):** T-VEC weapons that spread beyond their intended deployment zone create unlimited escalation liability for the deploying party. If a biological, nanite, or cognitive vector spreads to neutral parties or protected zones, the deployer assumes full escalation consequences under Part 5's E5+ escalation law regardless of intent. There is no "accidental deployment" defense for T-VEC weapon use above WS-III.
