## Area 13 — Restricted Weapons Governance

### 13.1 — Absolute Prohibition Weapons

The following weapon categories are classified as Absolute Prohibition weapons across all major factions and civilizations in the Starward Saga. Their production, storage, trade, or deployment constitutes a civilization-level war crime regardless of context:

1. **Soul-Sever Class weapons** (T-PSI, WS-I+) — any weapon designed to sever soul from physical body while leaving the body alive.
2. **Cognitive Vector weapons** (T-VEC Cognitive Vector sub-type) — weapons designed to permanently alter spiritual alignment, cultivation trajectory, or soul-level loyalty architecture.
3. **Consciousness Erase Engine** (T-PSI WS-VI) — mass-scale consciousness destruction weapons.
4. **Reality Corruptors** (T-VEC Tier 11+) — weapons that permanently alter local physical constants.
5. **Stellar Igniter class** (T-BURST Tier 9–10) — weapons specifically designed to trigger stellar collapse, deployed without evacuation protocol in inhabited systems.

**Rule 33.13.1 — Absolute Prohibition Enforcement:** Evidence of production, storage, or use of Absolute Prohibition weapons triggers mandatory civilization-level response under Part 21's collective security framework. Any faction possessing evidence of an Absolute Prohibition weapon is legally authorized to notify the relevant civilization authority and request E6-level military response, regardless of peacetime treaties.

### 13.2 — Restricted Weapons

The following weapon categories are Restricted — permissible only with special multi-level authorization and only in defined conflict contexts:

1. T-PSI weapons above WS-I (all soul-targeting weapons except Memory-Wipe Pulse in non-combat contexts).
2. T-VEC Plague Vial and Cultivator-Bane Toxin above WS-II.
3. T-BURST Stellar Igniter class — permissible only with civilization authorization and mandatory affected-party notification protocol.
4. T-VEC Ecosystem Eradicator — permissible only in uninhabited planetary targets with written Part 21 authorization.
5. AT-4 Strategic-Autonomous weapons — faction-level restriction; civilization authorization required.
