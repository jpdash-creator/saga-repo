## Area 3 — Side Character Ideology

### 3.1 — Side Character Ideology Requirements

**Rule 19.3.1.1 — Every Named Side Character Has an Ideology:** All named side characters appearing in three or more scenes have an ideology governed by Part 20. The ideology need not be mapped in the same depth as a hero's or villain's, but it must exist — it must be internally coherent, and it must be visible in the character's behavior and choices.

**Rule 19.3.1.2 — Side Character Ideology Must Be Independent:** A side character's ideology is their own, not a function of their relationship with the hero. An ally who shares the hero's ideology exactly is less narratively interesting than an ally whose own ideology produces a different relationship with the same events. Ideological alignment between hero and side character should be partial, not total.

**Rule 19.3.1.3 — Side Character Ideology Changes Through IE Events:** Side characters experience IE events (Part 20 Area 4). A mentor whose ideology is challenged by the hero's choices may shift. An ally who witnesses a major consequence event may experience an IE-4 recalibration. Side character ideological changes are not required to be tracked with the same detail as heroes, but they must be internally consistent and event-driven.

### 3.2 — Side Character Ideology Diversity

The most effective side character ensemble in the Starward Saga covers the full spectrum of ideological categories (Part 20 Area 2.1) without clustering all allies into a single ideological type. Heroes surrounded only by characters who share their exact ideology are in an ideological echo chamber — this is a narration violation under N19-5.

**Ideological Diversity Standard:** A hero's Tier A ally ensemble (all Tier A allies together) should, collectively, span at least four distinct primary ideology categories. This ensures that the hero regularly encounters ideological perspectives they must engage with genuinely rather than simply reinforce their own.
