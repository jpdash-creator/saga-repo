## Area 18 — Governing Boundaries — What Part 26 Does NOT Govern

The following are explicitly NOT within this Part's governance domain:

1. **Soul energetic mechanics and damage** → Part 4, Area 2 governs soul architecture, soul rungs, soul damage types and recovery mechanics
2. **Cultivation Heart power mechanics** → Part 4, Area 3 governs Heart states (Virtuous/Wicked/Demon/Void/Sovereign), their power effects and cultivation implications
3. **Physical Qi Deviation triggers and physical damage** → Part 3, Area 7.3 governs Qi Deviation as a physical cultivation event
4. **Move success rates as cultivation baselines** → Part 6 governs base success rates; this Part governs only the modifiers arising from psychological state
5. **Combat engagement structure** → Part 5 governs all structural combat mechanics; this Part governs only the psychological dimensions of combat
6. **Cultural norms and traditions** → Part 25 governs culture; this Part governs the psychological interface with culture
7. **External consequences of actions** → Part 27 governs consequence law; this Part governs internal psychological experience only
8. **Character ideology and belief systems** → Part 20 governs ideology; this Part governs the psychological substrate from which ideological frameworks arise
9. **Dialogue mechanics and dialogue law** → Part 29 governs dialogue; this Part governs the psychological states that dialogue expresses and responds to
10. **Faction and civilizational event mechanics** → Parts 21 and 22 govern these; this Part governs the psychological impact on characters who experience them
