## Area 21 — Faction Power Tier (FPT) System

### 21.1 — Faction Power Tier Definition

The **Faction Power Tier (FPT)** is the canonical measure of a faction's organizational strength. It uses the CCT scale (CCT-1 through CCT-20) as a proxy reference framework but is applied at the faction level with faction-specific modifiers. A faction at FPT-7 is comparable in organizational depth and coordinated power to a CCT-7 civilization's military-institutional component — but it is not a civilization.

**FPT is determined by five factors:**

| Factor | Weighting | Determination |
|---|---|---|
| Cultivation Peak | 25% | Peak cultivation realm of the faction's top members |
| Technology Depth | 25% | Highest sustained technology tier of faction's infrastructure |
| Territorial Footprint | 20% | Faction's operational territory per Part 13 classification |
| Resource Control | 20% | CPM grade access, financial assets, strategic resource holdings |
| Internal Cohesion | 10% | Loyalty, succession stability, internal conflict rate |

**Modified Base FPT Formula:**
Base FPT = (Cultivation Peak equivalent + Technology Depth equivalent + Territorial Footprint CCT equivalent) / 3, modified by Resource Control and Internal Cohesion factors (±2 maximum).

### 21.3 — FPT Gap Dynamics

**Rule 21.3.1 — The Three-FPT-Tier Gap Rule:**
When two factions are separated by 3 or more FPT tiers, their interaction is inherently asymmetric. The higher-FPT faction can compel, absorb, or destroy the lower-FPT faction at will. The lower-FPT faction's resistance is narrative heroism or strategic cleverness — it is not a direct power contest. This mirrors the Three-Tier Gap Rule in Part 21 (Civilization Law).

**Rule 21.3.2 — FPT Within Civilization:**
No faction permanently operating within a civilization can maintain FPT higher than the civilization's CCT + 1 without becoming effectively independent of the civilization. A single faction with FPT-3 higher than its containing civilization's CCT is functionally a state-within-a-state.

**Rule 21.3.3 — FPT Fluctuation:**
FPT is not static — it fluctuates with the faction's current operational condition. A sect that has suffered the death of its top three elders may drop from FPT-7 to FPT-4 instantly. A trading cartel that loses its primary trade route may drop from FPT-8 to FPT-5. FPT is a measure of current functional capability, not historical peak.

### 21.4 — Faction Scale Transition Law

Faction scale transitions — when a faction advances or declines across FPT tiers — are not administrative events. They are major narrative governance events with specific canonical requirements, consequence implications, and story-level significance. This Area governs how scale transitions occur and what they mean.

**Rule 22.21.4.1 — Scale Transitions Must Be Earned Through Narrative Events:** A faction cannot advance from FPT-3 to FPT-6 simply because "time has passed." Every FPT tier advancement requires canonical justification through one or more of the following event types:
- **Cultivation Peak Advancement:** A faction's top cultivators advance to the realm tier associated with the next FPT level
- **Territory Expansion:** The faction gains control of territory at the scale of the next FPT level through conflict, negotiation, or discovery
- **Resource Seizure or Development:** The faction acquires the CPM grade, technology tier, or resource base characteristic of the next FPT level
- **Institutional Integration:** The faction absorbs, allies with, or is recognized by the institutional structures characteristic of the next FPT level
- **Part 27 Consequence Cascade:** A major consequence event (CSS-7+) reorganizes the power landscape in a way that elevates the faction's relative position

**Rule 22.21.4.2 — Single-Tier Transitions Are Standard; Multi-Tier Transitions Require Part 28 Authorization:** A faction advancing 1 FPT tier through a single narrative arc is standard governance. Advancing 2 FPT tiers requires a compound of multiple event types (minimum two from the list above). Advancing 3+ FPT tiers in a single narrative arc is a Part 28 Tier 2 Authorization event — such accelerated ascension is a world-shaking event requiring explicit canon variance justification.

**Rule 22.21.4.3 — Scale Transition Has Internal Consequence:** When a faction advances a FPT tier, its internal structure is under pressure. The governance archetype, succession law, membership law, and resource distribution mechanisms that were appropriate at the previous FPT tier may not be suitable at the new tier. Scale transitions that are not accompanied by internal governance adaptation create **Post-Transition Instability** — a CSS-5 to CSS-6 ongoing consequence event where the faction's new scale exceeds its institutional capacity.

**Rule 22.21.4.4 — Downward Scale Transition (FPT Decline) Mechanics:** FPT decline is typically faster than FPT advancement and is usually triggered by sudden loss events:
- **Leadership Decapitation:** Loss of the top 1–2 cultivation peaks causes immediate FPT drop of 1–3 tiers depending on how concentrated the power was
- **Resource Severance:** Loss of a critical CPM source, trade route, or territory causes FPT drop of 1–2 tiers within the narrative Section
- **Alliance Collapse:** The loss of a faction alliance that contributed significantly to FPT can cause 1–2 tier drop
- **Information Exposure:** When a Hidden Faction's concealment is broken, their operational security FPT contribution is eliminated immediately

**Rule 22.21.4.5 — Faction Scale Transitions Are Part 27 Events:** Every FPT advancement or decline is a CSS-4 to CSS-7 consequence event for the changing faction (advancement produces positive CSS; decline produces negative CSS). Every FPT transition is also a consequence event for all factions within FPT-3 of the transitioning faction — the power landscape has changed in a way that reorganizes the relational and strategic context for all proximate actors. Faction ledgers for all affected factions must be updated after a scale transition event.

**Rule 22.21.4.6 — Protagonist Faction Scale Transitions Are Hero Arc Events:** When a faction that one or more heroes are affiliated with (Part 23 AFF scale) transitions FPT tiers, this is an IE event for every hero with AFF-6 (Deep Roots) or higher affiliation to that faction. The faction's advancement is a growth milestone for affiliated heroes; the faction's decline is a potential Crucible Event. The faction scale transition must be recorded in every affiliated hero's Hero Ledger Section 7.
