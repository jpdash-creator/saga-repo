## Area 16 — Vehicle Law and Part 15 Joint Governance Clarification

### 16.1 — Governance Boundary Statement

The governance boundary between Part 15 (Travel Law) and Part 32 (Vehicle Law) is defined as follows:

**Part 15 Governs:**
- What travel speeds and transit times are achievable at each technology tier
- What FTL drive types exist and how they physically operate (Area 14)
- What gate network mechanics govern gate transit (Area 13)
- What infrastructure exists and its travel effect (Area 16)
- Travel cost and resource consumption during transit (Area 17)
- Narration standards for depicting travel (Area 19)

**Part 32 Governs:**
- What specific named vehicle types exist at each tier
- How vehicles are classified, designated, and notated
- What scale classes vehicles belong to
- What construction prerequisites are required
- What propulsion systems power vehicles and how those systems are named
- What cultivation-hybrid configurations exist
- What the vehicle's internal operational and crew governance is
- What the vehicle's combat escalation floor is (Area 11)
- What the vehicle's economic and market position is (Area 12)

**Joint Governance:**
- All narrative scenes involving a technology vehicle in transit require both Parts simultaneously
- All CHV vehicle scenes require Part 12 (formation array objects) and Part 32 (vehicle) and Part 31 (synergy output) simultaneously
- Vehicle combat requires Part 5 (combat framework) and Part 32 (vehicle capabilities) simultaneously

### 16.2 — Conflict Resolution

If any specification in Part 32 conflicts with Part 15 regarding travel speeds or mechanics, **Part 15 is authoritative** — it is the domain authority for travel mechanics. Part 32 vehicle specifications may not exceed Part 15's capability tables for their corresponding tier.

If any conflict exists between Part 32's vehicle combat classification and Part 5's escalation framework, **Part 5 is authoritative** for the combat outcome. Part 32's escalation floor references (Area 11) are derived from Part 5, not independent of it.

If any conflict exists between Part 32's CHV classification and Part 12's formation array tier, **Part 12 is authoritative** for the formation array object. Part 32 governs the vehicle; Part 12 governs the formation.
