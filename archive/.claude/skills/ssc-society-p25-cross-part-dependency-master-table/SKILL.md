| Canon-Part 32 | Part 32 ↔ Part 25 — Bidirectional Active | Vehicle culture is one of the most pervasive cultural institutions in the Starward Saga and Part 32's vehicle taxonomy is the material foundation that Part 25's cultural governance operates on. Vehicle design aesthetic is a cultural institution: in CPT-2 Meritocratic-Martial civilizations, the aesthetic of warships (bristling with weapon arrays, deliberately menacing hull profiles) communicates martial identity and power; in CPT-3 Mercantile-Exchange civilizations, trading vessel aesthetics communicate reliability and wealth (ornate FRT vessels as floating prestige markers); in CPT-1 Hierarchical-Sacral civilizations, formation-array vessels (CHV classes) are regarded with religious reverence. Vehicle gifting traditions are governed by Part 25 as high-status social conventions: gifting a CHV-B Formation-Assisted vessel to an allied sect leader is a CPT-1 Hierarchical-Sacral honor ceremony; gifting a Tier 8 IS-Band Traversal Vessel to a disciple is a succession declaration in CPT-2 cultures. The cultural status of vehicle ownership by social class follows Part 25's class architecture: CC-Class (Cultivation Class) high-realm cultivators own CHV vehicles while lower-realm civilians use ST-Band transports; W-Class (Wealth Class) merchant magnates own large-scale SC-V/VI FRT configuration vessels; A-Class (Affiliation Class) diplomatic representatives use DIP configuration vessels (protective status markers). Surface transport paradigm culture (ST-Band, Part 32 Area 2) is the dominant transport culture for most of narrative history — carriage culture in CPT-1 sects (decorated palanquins, beast-drawn Yoke Carts for seniors, IWR trains for commoners), market transport culture in CPT-3 cities (Electroveil Cruisers and EVC as status markers), pilgrimage travel culture (ritual foot travel as cultural purity statement vs. vehicle use as cultural compromise). The Vehicle Prestige Scale (SC-I through SC-VII) is a Part 25 social status signal: riding a SC-I Skiff marks low status; commanding an SC-VI Titan-class vessel commands civilizational prestige |
