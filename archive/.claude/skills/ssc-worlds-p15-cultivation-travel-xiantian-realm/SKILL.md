## Area 4 — Cultivation Travel: Xiantian Realm

### 4.1 — Xiantian Travel Overview

The Xiantian realm represents the first fundamental break in travel physics. Qi can now be expressed externally as a sustained propulsive force rather than merely augmenting the body's physical movement. Xiantian cultivators can fly indefinitely, operate in vacuum (partially), and begin approaching velocities that make planetary-scale travel practical. The Xiantian realm also introduces the **first spatial awareness capabilities** that complement travel: True God and above can begin detecting spatial fold points usable for shortcutting.

### 4.2 — Xiantian Travel Capability Table

| Realm | Sustained Flight Speed | Burst Speed | Vacuum Capability | Spatial Capability | Travel Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demigod | ~3,000 km/h sustained | ~8,000 km/h burst | Low orbit reachable under strain; minutes in vacuum | None | Atmospheric flight fully sustainable; orbital escape is a max-effort event |
| Void God | ~8,000 km/h sustained | ~20,000 km/h burst (~5.5 km/s) | Low orbit comfortable; hours in vacuum with qi-sustained field | Brief spatial shortcuts (meters to hundreds of meters) | First spatial-shortcut capability; interplanetary still months |
| True God | ~25,000 km/h sustained | ~80,000 km/h burst (~22 km/s) | Full vacuum survival (hours to days without qi cost); space flight | Spatial shortcuts (km scale); fold-detect begins | Interplanetary travel enters the "months" range at sustained speed |
| Absolute God | ~100,000 km/h sustained | ~300,000 km/h burst (~83 km/s) | Indefinite vacuum; full space-flight at comfort | Spatial fold detection; shortcuts up to ~100 km | Planetary orbit traversal in hours; inner system transit in weeks |

### 4.3 — Xiantian Vacuum and Space Travel

**Void God Vacuum Protocol:** A Void God cultivator in vacuum must maintain an active qi field around their body. This is sustainable for hours but not indefinitely — they must return to atmosphere or a pressurized environment before their qi reserve depletes. It is not an emergency — it is a practical travel constraint.

**True God Vacuum Transition:** At True God, the qi field becomes semi-passive — it maintains vacuum protection at a fraction of the cost. A True God can sustain space travel for days without dedicated qi allocation to life support.

**Absolute God Space Operations:** Absolute God cultivators are functionally operational in space without restriction. Their qi density is high enough that vacuum survival is a passive background expenditure. They can engage in extended space operations — months of travel — without life-support concern.

### 4.4 — Xiantian Spatial Shortcuts

The Void God stage marks the first appearance of spatial shortcut capability. This is distinct from Heaven Treading's full spatial fold — Xiantian spatial shortcuts are:
- **Limited in range** — Void God: meters to hundreds of meters; True God: up to kilometers; Absolute God: up to ~100 km
- **Not true spatial folding** — the cultivator does not fold space; they detect and slip through naturally existing micro-folds in space
- **Detectable** — spatial shortcuts at Xiantian level produce a visible qi ripple that can be sensed by same-tier or higher cultivators
- **Combat-viable** — Xiantian spatial shortcuts are used in combat for evasion and repositioning, not long-distance travel

**Rule 4.4.1 — Xiantian Shortcuts Are Not Inter-System Travel.** No Xiantian spatial shortcut can bridge interplanetary distances. The range caps are governance constraints that cannot be exceeded without advancing to the Core Realm or Divine Realm.
