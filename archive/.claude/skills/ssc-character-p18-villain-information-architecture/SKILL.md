## Area 12 — Villain Information Architecture

### 12.1 — What the Villain Knows

The villain's information architecture — what they know, what they believe incorrectly, what they are missing, and what they are actively pursuing — is tracked in the Villain Ledger and governed jointly by Part 17 Area 11 principles and Part 24 Information and Secrecy Law.

**Rule 18.12.1.1 — Villains Have Information Asymmetries:** Villains typically know more than the hero knows at arc entry. Their intelligence apparatus (Part 24), their institutional positioning, and their greater experience typically provide superior information. This information asymmetry is part of their threat structure.

**Rule 18.12.1.2 — Villain Information Blindspots Are Exploitable:** Every villain's information architecture has blindspots — things they cannot see or believe. These blindspots are typically rooted in their ideology: the Strength Supremacist villain who cannot see threat from someone they consider weak; the Order Absolutist who cannot comprehend resistance from those who "should" accept order. Villain blindspots are narrative assets — they create the conditions under which a weaker hero can defeat a stronger villain.

**Rule 18.12.1.3 — Villain Intelligence Failures Are Consequence Events:** When a villain acts on false information and suffers material harm as a result, this is a CSS event (Part 27) and may be a PIS event (Part 26) if it exposes a deep blindspot. Information warfare against the villain (Part 24) is a legitimate strategic tool for heroes.
