## Area 8 — Propulsion Systems Taxonomy

### 8.1 — Propulsion Classification Framework

Every vehicle has a primary propulsion system that determines its operational physics, fuel requirements, and capability ceiling. Propulsion systems are classified into six categories, each with named subtypes unique to the Starward Saga universe.

### 8.2 — Category P-I: Chemical and Thermal Drives (Tiers 1–5)

*P-I-1 Biomechanical Drive:* Animal muscle power, human muscle power. ST-Band only. No fuel cost independent of biology.

*P-I-2 Aerodynamic Drive:* Wind and current exploitation via sail, rudder, and hull design. ST-Band, water and air environments.

*P-I-3 Combustion Drive:* Internal combustion, steam engine, diesel engine. Chemical fuel (refined hydrocarbons). Tiers 3–5. The first mechanical drive not dependent on weather.

*P-I-4 Rocket Drive:* Chemical rocket propellant generating thrust through directed exhaust. Tiers 4–6. The bridge between atmospheric and orbital transport.

### 8.3 — Category P-II: Fusion and Antimatter Drives (Tiers 5–8)

*P-II-1 Helion Fusion Drive:* Fusion reactor generating continuous thrust through controlled helium-isotope fusion. High thrust efficiency. Tiers 5–7. The standard inner-system propulsion of OS-Band civilizations. Named for the iconic helion isotope reaction cycle.

*P-II-2 Torchfire Drive:* Continuous-thrust fusion rocket with very high exhaust velocity. Tiers 6–8. Provides near-constant acceleration rather than impulse thrust — the "always-on" drive that makes outer-system transit practical.

*P-II-3 Annihilator Drive:* Matter-antimatter annihilation drive. Tier 7–8. Maximum specific impulse achievable with matter-based propulsion. Expensive to fuel (antimatter production costs); extraordinary performance. Near-c transit is achievable with Annihilator drives.

*P-II-4 Coherence Drive:* Advanced energy-to-thrust system using highly coherent electromagnetic energy as propulsion. Tiers 7–9. Fuel-efficient; high precision; often used in conjunction with FTL jump systems.

### 8.4 — Category P-III: FTL Drives (Tiers 8–12)

*P-III-1 Warpstream Drive:* Contracts space ahead of the vessel, expands space behind — the vessel "surfs" a spatial wave at apparent FTL speeds without locally exceeding light speed. High energy cost; continuous drive; good at long-haul sustained transit. The dominant FTL drive of Tier 9 commercial vessels.

*P-III-2 Riftjump Drive:* Creates a brief dimensional transition into an adjacent spatial layer with compressed distances, then returns to normal space at a displaced location. The "Hyperspace" equivalent. Instantaneous transit per jump but requires jump point identification; range limited by transition energy. Common in military doctrine for its unpredictability.

*P-III-3 Vaultfold Drive:* The Fold Drive equivalent — compresses space around the vessel through brute-force spatial manipulation, achieving effective FTL. Very energy intensive; range limited; high precision in arrival. Preferred for precision operations.

*P-III-4 Gravitation Jump Drive:* Exploits naturally occurring gravitational anomalies (jump points) for instantaneous point-to-point transit. Infrastructure-dependent (requires mapped jump points). Maximum range per jump ~20 ly (Tier 9 baseline). The Jump Drive vehicle class. Instantaneous transit but destination-constrained.

### 8.5 — Category P-IV: Formation Drive Systems (CHV Propulsion) (Tiers 5–20)

*P-IV-1 Spatial Step Array:* Formation array that generates localized spatial folding — the formation-drive equivalent of a Fold Drive. Drive range determined by inscriptor realm. The canonical propulsion of CHV-B and CHV-D vessels.

*P-IV-2 Void Compression Array:* Formation array generating continuous spatial compression in the vessel's direction of travel — formation equivalent of the Warpstream Drive but governed by formation laws rather than technology physics.

*P-IV-3 Dimensional Anchor Engine:* Formation array creating a gravitational anchor in the vehicle's destination space and pulling the vehicle toward it — range-limited by formation tier but with no fuel cost beyond CPM.

### 8.6 — Category P-V: Advanced and Post-FTL Drives (Tiers 11–20)

*P-V-1 Galactic Voidstream Drive:* High-efficiency FTL drive operating at 50,000–2,000,000× light. Tiers 11–14. Required for practical galactic-scale transit without gate reliance. High power consumption; requires Tier 11+ energy technology for continuous operation.

*P-V-2 Wormhole Transit System:* Not a drive — the vehicle modification that enables transit through constructed wormhole infrastructure. Tiers 13+. Requires compatible entry/exit alignment systems.

*P-V-3 Existence Transit Engine:* Tier 17–20 propulsion concept — the vehicle interacts with existence itself as a medium, rather than space as a substrate. Joint governance with Part 10 Science Law.

### 8.7 — Category P-VI: Gate Transit Systems

Gate transit is not a vehicle drive — it is an infrastructure access system. Vehicles transit through gates as passive objects; the gate generates the traversal. However, vehicles require a Gate Transit Interface (GTI) to safely pass through gate apertures:

*GTI-1 Standard Gate Interface:* Standard gate-compatible hull shielding and transition buffer systems for Tier 10 technology gates. Required for any vessel transiting a technology star gate.

*GTI-2 Formation Teleportation Interface:* Compatible hull modifications for CHV vehicles transiting Formation Teleportation Arrays (Part 12, Part 15). Governed by Part 12 (the FTA) and Part 32 (the vehicle's interface systems).
