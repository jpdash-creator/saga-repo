## Area 3 — Village Classification System

A Village is the smallest defined inhabited location unit — a single inhabited structure, compound, hall, or small settlement cluster. The classification system describes what kind of Village it is based on its intrinsic properties, not who lives there.

**Classification Columns:** Size/Population Scale | Ambient Energy Class | Affinity Profile | Special Zone | Historical Depth

### 3.1 — Village Class Table

| Class | Designation | Population Scale | Ambient Energy | Affinity Profile | Special Zone | Historical Depth | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-1 | Frontier Village | 5–50 | Class 1–2 | Neutral | None | New — decades or less | A newly established outpost, exploration camp, or resource extraction settlement. No accumulated history. Subsistence function. |
| V-2 | Nascent Village | 30–150 | Class 1–2 | Neutral | None | Young — under a century | A forming settlement with minimal organized structure. Commerce is barter-level. Community identity just beginning. |
| V-3 | Common Village | 100–500 | Class 2 | Neutral | None | Established — 1–3 centuries | The baseline Village. Multi-generation habitation, organized sustenance, local commerce, basic community governance. |
| V-4 | Rooted Village | 300–1,000 | Class 2–3 | Neutral or Mild | None | Settled — 3–10 centuries | A Village with deep local roots. Community identity strong. May have a recognized elder or local power structure. Minor local market. |
| V-5 | Prosperous Village | 500–2,000 | Class 2–3 | Neutral or Mild | None | Settled — centuries | A Village positioned near a resource deposit, trade route, or naturally productive environment. Noticeably better resourced than peers. |
| V-6 | Affinity Village | 200–1,500 | Class 3–4 | Active affinity | Possible: Passive Formation Zone | Variable | A Village with a pronounced environmental affinity — the location itself has a natural energy character that makes it attractive to practitioners of aligned paths. |
| V-7 | Energy Village | 500–3,000 | Class 3–4 | Neutral or affinity | Passive Formation Zone | Variable | A Village built on or near an active Passive Formation Zone. The energy environment is noticeably richer than surroundings. |
| V-8 | Vortex Village | 300–2,000 | Class 4–5 | Affinity aligned with vortex | Vortex Formation Zone | Variable — may be ancient or recent | A Village at or near an active Vortex Formation Zone. Exceptionally rich energy environment. The Vortex is the Village's defining feature and primary resource. |
| V-9 | Ancient Village | 100–2,000 | Class 3–5 | Variable | Variable — ruins, formation remnants, or accumulated history | Very old — over a millennium | A Village of great age. Accumulated cultivation residue, historical formation remnants, embedded cultural tradition. The location itself carries the weight of history. |
| V-10 | Sacred Village | 50–1,000 | Class 6–7 | Aligned with Sacred Ground affinity | Sacred Ground (any grade) | Variable | A Village within or immediately adjacent to a confirmed Sacred Ground. The location's energy environment is transformative. Access may be restricted. Legendarily significant regardless of population size. |
