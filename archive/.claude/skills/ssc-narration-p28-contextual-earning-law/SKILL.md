## Rule Text (Verbatim Source — Authoritative)
## Area 15 — The Contextual Earning Law

### 15.1 — Principle

Every unit of canon information delivered in prose — classification names, location names, faction references, character history, cultural customs, technological capabilities, cultivation mechanics, relationship state, timeline detail, and any other fact governed by the SSC Canon — must be **load-bearing in its scene**. Information is load-bearing when at least one of the following is true:

- It is the **consequence** of what the scene has just rendered (the scene earned the telling).
- It is the **cause** of what the scene is about to render (the telling earns the next beat).
- It **changes** what a character understands or decides within the scene (the telling is itself the event).

Information that satisfies none of these three conditions is **standalone information** and is prohibited in prose.

### 15.2 — The Character-Sheet Failure Mode

Standalone information reads as a character sheet: a label placed next to a character or a world-element for the reader's reference, not for the story's motion. The diagnostic test is deletion. If the sentence containing the information can be deleted without any subsequent sentence losing its cause, its sense, or its emotional charge, then the information was standalone and should not have been present.

A scene suffering the character-sheet failure mode is technically canon-compliant in every other respect — every fact may be accurate, every ledger state may be honored, every rule may be obeyed — and still read as stale, rigid, and lifeless. Accuracy without motion is not prose. It is a manifest.

### 15.3 — Who May Deliver What

Any character may voice any canon information, including technical classifications, deep-canon terminology, and specialized vocabulary, **provided Section 15.1 is satisfied**. The Law is not about speaker qualification. It is about scene-tissue.

- A child may name a cultivation realm if the scene has built the child's reason to know it and the naming drives the next beat.
- A villager may reference a distant faction if the villager's relationship to that faction is what the scene is about.
- A king may speak the name of a Heart state to his queen if the saying of it shifts what she does next, or if it resolves what just passed between them.
- A commoner may observe a technology tier if the observation is itself the moment of the scene — fear, recognition, refusal, decision.

The Law removes no vocabulary from any speaker. It removes only vocabulary that is not earned by its surrounding scene.

### 15.4 — The Three-Beat Test

For any piece of canon information in a prose unit, three beats must be locatable in the surrounding text:

- **Beat Before.** Within the preceding several sentences, something in the scene made this information possible, necessary, or loaded. A reason to know, a reason to ask, a reason to notice, a stake that makes the fact matter, a pressure that makes the naming land.
- **The Beat.** The information is delivered through one of the preferred mechanisms in Section 15.5 — sensed impression, rendered use, consequential dialogue, or earned exposition — not declared.
- **Beat After.** Within the following several sentences, something is caused, unlocked, changed, decided, or refused because the information was delivered. The telling produces motion.

If any of the three beats is absent or thin, the information is standalone and the prose fails the Law.

### 15.5 — Preferred Delivery Mechanisms

Even when contextually earned, information should prefer the mechanism that carries the most scene-work:

- **Sensed impression** (governed by Part 3 Area 3.3 Heavenly Scale for cultivation power; analogous sensory registers for other information categories) — the physical, environmental, and emotional experience of encountering the thing.
- **Rendered use** — a capability, relationship, or faction exercised in the scene such that its shape, cost, and consequence imply the classification without naming it.
- **Comparative behavior** — other characters responding, deferring, recoiling, posturing, calibrating; their reactions carry the naming.
- **Consequential naming** — a character names the thing because the naming itself does work: resolves a question, shifts a relationship, opens or closes a decision.
- **Earned exposition** — direct explanation, permitted when and only when a character in the scene needs the explanation as much as the reader does, and that need arose within the scene.

### 15.6 — Immersion Primacy

The immersion of the reader is the terminal value. A prose unit that is technically compliant with every other canon provision but reads as a recitation of facts has failed this Law regardless of those compliances. The test is not "was the information accurate" but "was the information *alive* in the scene."

This principle governs every tie-break between Contextual Earning and any other rule that would push the prose toward literal declaration. Where another rule appears to require a declaration that would violate Section 15.1, the declaration must be converted into an earned delivery, or the rule's underlying intent must be satisfied through an alternative mechanism that does not break immersion.

### 15.7 — Applicability Scope

This Law applies to all information categories in the canon system, including but not limited to:

- All classification vocabulary (cultivation realms and levels, Meridian Roots, affinities, soul talents, Heart Levels, technology tiers, CS%/TS% synergy states)
- All location references (star systems, worlds, continents, kingdoms, villages, sacred grounds, cosmological tiers)
- All faction, sect, civilization, and affiliation references
- All character relationships, histories, and backstory
- All cultural, ritual, and custom references
- All timeline and chronology references
- All power-system mechanics (cultivation steps, technology operation, combat techniques, Named Moves, fusion stages)
- All relic, weapon, vehicle, and artifact identification

No category of canon information is exempt.

### 15.8 — Failure Grades

Violations of the Contextual Earning Law are graded:

- **Minor** — one occurrence of standalone information, correctable by local revision without restructuring the scene.
- **Major** — multiple standalone units, or one standalone unit that labels a character, location, or faction without any surrounding scene-work.
- **Critical** — the scene's core structure consists of standalone information delivery. The character-sheet failure mode in full form. Such scenes must be redesigned, not revised.

### 15.9 — Relationship to Other Canon Provisions

This Area supersedes and extends two earlier provisions:

- **Part 2 Area 6.3 "Power Levels — Explicit or Sensed"** — that bullet's permission for "explicit identification" as co-equal with sensed impression is withdrawn. Explicit identification is a Section 15.5 "consequential naming" subcase only, subject to the Three-Beat Test.
- **Part 28 Area 7 Emotion-From-Experience Law** — Area 7 governs emotion specifically; Area 15 is its structural generalization to all canon information. Where the two overlap (emotional information deployed as fact), both apply; the Three-Beat Test is the operational check.

The Heavenly Scale system (Part 3 Area 3.3) remains the canonical delivery mechanism for cultivation-power information and is reinforced, not constrained, by this Law.

### 15.10 — Delivery Sequencing Under the Two-Audit Mandate

The Contextual Earning Declaration that follows every Prose unit is delivered in Gate 1 Chat Delivery immediately after the Prose body and immediately before the Gate 1 Post-Draft Audit Report (see Part 1 Rule 7.1.1 "Gate 1 Chat Delivery Order" and Rule 7.1.1(k) Two-Audit Mandate). The Gate 1 Position Header Block precedes the Prose body in chat; the Contextual Earning Declaration follows the Prose body; the Post-Draft Audit Report (Audit Pass 1 of 2) follows the Contextual Earning Declaration. The Post-Write Audit (Audit Pass 2 of 2) executes later, after Gate 2 file writes, and re-checks Contextual Earning compliance against the written `.docx` and `.md` files. A Prose unit that passes the Three-Beat Test at Post-Draft Audit but loses a Beat in the written files (e.g., truncated prose, dropped paragraphs) is a Post-Write Audit FAIL and must be corrected before the Post-Write Ledger Update may run.

The Gate 1 Position Header Block itself does **not** count as information delivery under the Three-Beat Test — it is a chat-only governance artefact outside the prose body and therefore exempt from Contextual Earning scrutiny. Only the Prose body is evaluated for Contextual Earning.
