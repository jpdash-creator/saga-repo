## Area 18 — Part 21 and Location / Distance (Parts 13, 14 Interface)

### 18.1 — LA Axis and Parts 13, 14

The LA axis is calibrated against Part 13's cosmological hierarchy and Part 14's CMS distance scale:
- LA-5 (single World) = Part 13 World (W-class) territory; Gm to AU scale (Part 14)
- LA-7 (full Star System) = Part 13 Star System (SS-6 to SS-10); light-minute to light-hour scale (Part 14)
- LA-10 (Star Cluster) = Part 13 Star Cluster (SC-class); light-year to kly scale (Part 14)
- LA-11 (Star Quadrant) = Part 13 Star Quadrant (SQ-class); 10–100 kly scale (Part 14)
- LA-14 (Galaxy fully administered) = Part 13 Galaxy (G-11 to G-15); full Mly galactic scale (Part 14)
- LA-16 (Galaxy Cluster) = Part 13 Galaxy Cluster (GC-class); 10–100 Mly scale (Part 14)
- LA-17 (Galaxy Supercluster) = Part 13 Galaxy Supercluster (GSC-class); 100 Mly to 1 Gly scale (Part 14)

**Rule 18.1.1 — LA Cannot Exceed Administrative Reality:** LA claims based on territory that is not structurally administered are invalid. A civilization that has sent probes to neighboring star systems but administers none of them has LA-5 (single World), not LA-8.

### 18.2 — Distance and CCT

Part 14's FTL distance governance (P14-FTL-1 through P14-FTL-4) constrains how quickly LA can be expanded. The FTL threshold at TA-8 corresponds to first interstellar reach (LA-8); before FTL is achieved, LA cannot advance beyond LA-7 (full Star System) regardless of other axis values.

**Rule 18.2.1 — LA-8 Requires FTL:** LA-8 or above requires FTL transportation capability (Part 10 FTL Physics Framework prerequisite satisfied; Part 8 Space Technology Level 9+). This creates a hard gate — TA minimum 8 is required for LA-8.
