## Area 14 — Assassination and Bounty Hunting Factions

### 14.1 — Overview: Assassination and Hunting Factions (AHF)

Assassination and Bounty Hunting Factions are specialized armed organizations whose primary service is the targeted elimination of specified individuals or the capture and delivery of specified targets. They occupy a morally ambiguous but structurally distinct position in the faction ecosystem — some are legally sanctioned (bounty hunting organizations licensed by government bodies), others operate in legal gray zones or as outright criminal organizations.

**Defining Characteristics:**
- **Primary Purpose:** Targeted elimination or capture of individuals for contracted compensation
- **Core Resource:** Highly skilled operatives; intelligence capability; cultivation specialization in lethal precision techniques
- **Membership:** Elite practitioners who have specialized in assassination techniques, tracking, concealment, and target-specific combat
- **Natural Scale Range:** Village (SCL-1) through Universe (SCL-14)

### 14.2 — Assassination and Hunting Faction Classifications

| Type | Primary Service | Scale | Legal Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Village Bandit Hunters | Local criminal elimination; rural threat suppression | SCL-1 to SCL-3 | Licensed |
| Kingdom Bounty Hunter Guild | Licensed bounty hunting; wanted criminal recovery | SCL-4 to SCL-7 | Licensed (government charter) |
| Assassination Guild | Contracted targeted elimination; gray/black market | SCL-4 to SCL-9 | Gray-to-illegal |
| Shadow Hunters | Elite cultivation-specialized assassins | SCL-5 to SCL-10 | Varies |
| Monster Hunting Corps | Dangerous beast and anomalous entity elimination | SCL-3 to SCL-9 | Licensed; public safety |
| Void Predators | Space-based target hunting; FTL-capable pursuit | SCL-8 to SCL-12 | Gray-to-illegal |
| Ghost House (Ancient) | Ancient assassination orders; historical lineage; extreme capability | SCL-7 to SCL-13 | Hidden; nominally illegal |
| Galactic Enforcement Marshals | Trans-civilization fugitive pursuit; licensed by Galactic authority | SCL-10 to SCL-12 | Licensed |

### 14.3 — Assassination Faction Governance Rules

**Rule 14.3.1 — Contract Sanctity:**
Assassination faction contracts are binding on both contractor and organization. An assassination organization that fails to complete a contracted elimination loses guild reputation. A contractor who fails to pay upon completion triggers enforcement clauses — and assassination factions are uniquely positioned to enforce payment.

**Rule 14.3.2 — Target Restriction Clauses:**
Major assassination factions maintain Target Restriction Lists — categories of targets they will not accept contracts against regardless of payment. Common restrictions: children below cultivation threshold; neutral diplomatic personnel; members of the organization's own patron faction.

**Rule 14.3.3 — Counter-Contract:**
Any targeted individual aware of an active contract against them may issue a Counter-Contract — a contract with any willing faction to neutralize the original contractor or the original assassination organization itself. Counter-contracts are a recognized and common response at Kingdom scale and above.
