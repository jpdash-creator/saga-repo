## Area 7 — Academic and Research Factions

### 7.1 — Overview: Academic and Research Factions (ACR)

Academic and Research Factions are organized institutions whose primary purpose is the systematic pursuit, preservation, and transmission of knowledge — cultivation theory, scientific discovery, historical documentation, philosophical inquiry, and technical innovation.

**Defining Characteristics:**
- **Primary Purpose:** Knowledge creation, preservation, transmission
- **Core Resource:** Scholars, libraries, laboratories, ancient texts, cultivation manuals, experimental infrastructure
- **Membership Profile:** Cultivators (theory-focused), scientists, historians, philosophers, formation-array theorists, inscriptors
- **Natural Scale Range:** Village (SCL-1) through Galaxy (SCL-11)

### 7.2 — Academic Faction Classifications

| Academic Type | Knowledge Domain | Characteristic Activities | Scale Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cultivation Theory Academies | Cultivation mechanics, realm theory, technique design | Theoretical cultivation research; manual authoring; disciple training | SCL-3 to SCL-9 |
| Scientific Research Institutes | Physical sciences, mathematics, materials science, space science | Laboratory research; experiment design; Part 10 scientific prerequisite satisfaction | SCL-5 to SCL-11 |
| Historical Archives & Memory Halls | Historical documentation, civilization records, lineage tracking | Record keeping; cultural preservation; chronicle authoring | SCL-2 to SCL-10 |
| Philosophical Schools | Cultivation philosophy, existence theory, moral frameworks | Teaching; debate; ideological publication | SCL-1 to SCL-9 |
| Formation Array Research Centers | Formation theory, inscription advancement, spatial architecture | Experimental array construction; theory development | SCL-4 to SCL-10 |
| Soul Science Institutes | Soul phenomenon research, soul cultivation theory, soul-technology interface | Joint governance with Part 4 and Part 10 | SCL-6 to SCL-11 |
| Technology Research Corporations | Technology development, engineering innovation, prototype construction | R&D programs; patent registration; technology transfer | SCL-5 to SCL-12 |
| Interdisciplinary Convergence Academies | Cross-domain synthesis of cultivation science and technology science | Integration of cultivation and technology knowledge traditions | SCL-7 to SCL-12 |
| Cartography & Cosmological Survey Institutes | Mapping, spatial documentation, dimensional research | Survey missions; mapping compilation; dimensional anomaly documentation | SCL-7 to SCL-11 |

### 7.3 — Academic Faction Internal Structure

Academic factions use **Expertise Hierarchy** as their primary organizational model: authority is derived from demonstrated knowledge contribution, not political appointment or combat capability.

| Rank | Academic Title | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Apex Scholar | Grand Master / Arch-Sage / Chief Sage | Institutional leadership; research direction; public face |
| Senior Faculty | Master Scholar / Professor Emeritus | Department leadership; senior research programs |
| Active Researcher | Research Fellow / Associate Professor | Primary research activities; supervision of junior researchers |
| Junior Scholar | Research Associate / Scholar | Structured research under senior supervision |
| Apprentice | Scholar Apprentice / Student | Foundational training; assisting senior research |
| Library/Archive Staff | Archivist / Curator | Preservation, cataloguing, and access management |
