## Area 7 — Distance and the Cosmological Hierarchy Interface

### 7.1 — Minimum Separation Requirements Between Cosmological Units

For the Starward Saga world-building to remain internally consistent, the following minimum separation distances apply between distinct units of each cosmological level:

| Pair of Units | Minimum Separation |
|---|---|
| Two distinct star systems | ~1 light-year |
| Two distinct Star Clusters | ~100 light-years |
| Two distinct Star Quadrants | ~1,000 light-years |
| Two distinct Galaxies | ~500,000 light-years |
| Two distinct Galaxy Clusters | ~5 Mly |
| Two distinct Galaxy Superclusters | ~50 Mly |
| Two distinct Universes | Trans-universal — not expressible in light-years |

**The Overlap Rule:** Two cosmological units of the same tier cannot spatially overlap. A star system boundary is where another star system cannot begin. Authors must not describe adjacent star systems as sharing space — they share a gravitational neighborhood (the Star Cluster) but are spatially distinct.

---

### 7.2 — Distance Within Classification-Class Ranges

Part 13 establishes 20 classification classes for each location type. Part 14 establishes that classification-class does not linearly correlate with physical size — a Village Class 15 may be smaller in physical diameter than a Village Class 3 while being classified far higher. Classification-class correlates with capability ceiling, cultivation density, and ambient energy — not with physical dimensions. Authors must not assume higher classification-class = larger physical area.

---

### 7.3 — Sacred Ground Distance Influence Zone

Sacred Grounds (Part 13, Area 13) have a defined maximum influence zone:

| Sacred Ground Grade | Core Effect Zone (Full Class 7 ambient energy) | Fringe Influence Zone (detectable ambient enhancement) |
|---|---|---|
| S-Grade 1 | ~1–5 km | ~10–20 km |
| S-Grade 2 | ~5–20 km | ~50 km |
| S-Grade 3 | ~20–50 km | ~100 km |
| S-Grade 4 | ~50–100 km | ~200–300 km |
| S-Grade 5 | ~100–300 km | ~500–1,000 km |
| S-Grade 6 | ~300–1,000 km | ~2,000–3,000 km |
| S-Grade 7 | ~1,000–2,000 km | ~5,000–8,000 km |
| S-Grade 8 | ~10,000 km or more | ~50,000–100,000 km (near full planetary influence) |

The fringe influence zone is where a cultivator can detect the Sacred Ground's ambient energy enhancement without being within the core effect zone. Detection occurs passively for cultivators at Grade 10+ (Core Formation); lower realms require active soul sense.
