## Area 3 — Canonical Distance Thresholds by Cosmological Scale

### 3.1 — Purpose

This Area establishes the canonical distance ranges within and between each level of Part 13's cosmological hierarchy. These ranges are the governance-level definitions — all narrative distance claims must be consistent with these ranges or require a documented explanation.

---

### 3.2 — Settlement Scale Distances (Village, Town, City, Capital)

| Location Tier | Internal Diameter | Separation from Nearest Same-Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Village (Class 1–7) | 10 m to 500 m (hamlet to village core) | 1–10 km | Small settlements cluster; higher-class have larger territories |
| Village (Class 8–20) | 500 m to 5 km | 5–50 km | Elevated Villages may span a small valley or district |
| Town (Class 1–7) | 1–20 km | 20–200 km | Towns govern surrounding Villages |
| Town (Class 8–20) | 20–100 km | 100–500 km | High-class Towns approach City scale in geography |
| City (Class 1–7) | 50–300 km | 200 km – 2 Mm | Cities govern surrounding Towns |
| City (Class 8–20) | 300 km – 2 Mm | 1–5 Mm | High-class Cities may span a sub-continental zone |
| Capital (Class 1–7) | 1–10 Mm (continental governor) | Variable (one per continent typically) | Capitals are the largest governance hubs on their planetary region |
| Capital (Class 8–20) | 10 Mm to full planetary scale | The sole governing seat of a multi-planet polity | Planetary and multi-planetary Capitals at highest tier |

---

### 3.3 — Polity Scale Distances (Kingdom, Empire, World)

| Location Tier | Territory Diameter | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kingdom (Class 1–5, Continental) | 500 km – 5 Mm | Governs a continent segment of a planet |
| Kingdom (Class 6–10, Multi-Continental) | 5–30 Mm | Single to multi-continental; spans most of a planet |
| Empire (Class 1–8, Planetary) | 30 Mm – full planetary scale | Multi-continental; spans most of a planet |
| Empire (Class 9–15, Multi-Planet) | 1 AU – several AU | Governs multiple planets in a star system |
| Empire (Class 16–20, System-Spanning) | 1–50 AU | Governs full star system and its bodies |
| World (Class 1–10) | Full star system scale | Governs star system as a unified polity |
| World (Class 11–20) | Up to several AU beyond system edge | Extended World control into near-interstellar space |

---

### 3.4 — Star System Scale Distances

| Scale | Canonical Distance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Planet surface to low orbit | 100–500 km | Standard orbital altitude range |
| Planet surface to moon (if present) | 200,000–500,000 km (~0.001–0.003 AU) | Moon distance varies by star system |
| Inner habitable zone | 0.5–1.5 AU | Within the star's habitable orbital band |
| Star to outer planetary system | 5–30 AU | Gas giants, ice giants |
| Star to system edge (heliopause equivalent) | 100–200 AU | Beyond which is interstellar void |
| Intra-system fastest transit (Tier 8+) | 1 AU: hours | FTL-capable; depends on Part 15 |
| Intra-system subluminal transit (Tier 5–7) | 1 AU: weeks to months | High-thrust subluminal |

---

### 3.5 — Interstellar to Cosmological Scale Distances

| Scale | Canonical Distance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Neighboring star systems | 1–10 light-years | Typical Star Cluster neighbors |
| Star Cluster diameter | 10–500 light-years | Varies by cluster density |
| Star Cluster to Star Cluster within a Star Quadrant | 100–2,000 light-years | Star Quadrant structure |
| Star Quadrant diameter | 1,000–10,000 light-years | Galactic arm section |
| Full Galaxy diameter | 50,000–150,000 light-years (~50–150 kly) | Standard spiral galaxy |
| Galaxy to Galaxy within a Galaxy Cluster | 500,000 – 5 million light-years (0.5–5 Mly) | Inter-galactic separation |
| Galaxy Cluster diameter | 2–20 Mly | Compact to extended clusters |
| Galaxy Cluster to Galaxy Cluster within a Supercluster | 10–100 Mly | Supercluster filament scale |
| Galaxy Supercluster diameter | 100–1,000 Mly | Full supercluster complex |
| Universe observable radius | ~46.5 Gly | Observable cosmic horizon |
| Universe diameter (full) | ~93 Gly | Full observable universe |
