## Area 10 — Ideological Alignment with All Other Parts

### 10.1 — Ideology and Combat (Part 5)

Every significant combat encounter is a simultaneous ideological event. The decision to fight, the intensity deployed, the choice to kill or spare — these are all expressions of the character's current ideological state.

- **Why a character fights** is an ideological question answered by their Ideology Ledger
- **How a character fights** (mercilessly, with restraint, with artistic investment, with pure function) expresses their ideology through combat behavior
- **The aftermath of killing** generates an IE-4 or IE-5 for characters whose ideology has not previously required them to account for killing as a fact of their life (joint governance with Part 26 Area 4 — Kill Psychology)
- **The decision to spare** an enemy is often the most ideologically complex act in combat — it reveals which of the character's ideological layers actually governs in extremis

### 10.2 — Ideology and Consequences (Part 27)

The character's ideology determines which consequences they are capable of genuinely registering. An SDI character cannot be genuinely moved by social consequence unless it threatens their power base. A JCI character is more affected by moral consequence than physical consequence.

- **Ideological Blindspot to Consequence:** Characters whose ideology cannot process a specific consequence type will appear narratively callous — not because they lack psychology but because their ideology does not assign weight to that consequence type
- **Consequence-Driven Ideology Change:** CSS-8 or higher consequence events (Part 27) frequently generate IE-5 or IE-6 ideology events when the consequence type is one the character's ideology does not have adequate framework for

### 10.3 — Ideology and Faction (Part 22)

- Every faction has a dominant institutional ideology — the collective expression of the faction's governing beliefs
- Character ideology interacts with faction ideology through alignment, performance, resistance, and transformation
- Faction leadership succession is frequently ideological — who inherits leadership may depend on who most convincingly embodies the faction's institutional ideology
- Cross-faction relationships carry the ideological tensions of their respective institutions

### 10.4 — Ideology and Culture (Part 25)

- Cultural paradigm types (CPT-1 through CPT-7) are the collective cultural expression of dominant ideological orientations — culture is the social precipitation of ideology
- Individual characters may hold ideologies that deviate significantly from their cultural context — these deviations are among the most productive narrative characterization tools
- Cultural conflict (Part 25) always has ideological content — cultures clash because their governing ideological orientations are incompatible
- The cultivation sect cultural axis (CuA-C) is particularly ideology-dense because cultivation philosophy is simultaneously an ideological framework

### 10.5 — Ideology and Psychology (Part 26)

- Psychological events (Part 26) generate Ideology Ledger events — trauma, grief, moral injury, identity crisis are all IE sources
- Ideological frameworks shape psychological interpretation — the same event (a first kill) produces different psychological experiences depending on whether the character holds SDI (functional, possibly confirmatory), JCI (profound moral-psychological event), or PCI (desperate psychological justification)
- Psychological healing (Part 26 Area 14) is also ideological reconstruction — the character who heals from trauma must also address the ideological framework that either produced the trauma or was shattered by it
- Maladaptive coping mechanisms (Part 26 Area 11.2) are frequently ideological performances — the character's ideology does not permit them to acknowledge the psychological wound, so it is managed through ideological channels instead

### 10.6 — Ideology and Information (Part 24)

- Information asymmetry produces ideological asymmetry — those with more accurate information have ideological advantages over those operating on misinformation
- Secrecy maintenance (Part 24) often requires characters to maintain Surface ideologies that contradict their Core — the spy, the double agent, the strategically performing sect elder
- Information revelation events (Part 24 Disclosure Events) are Ideology Ledger events by definition — learning something true about the world always carries ideological weight
