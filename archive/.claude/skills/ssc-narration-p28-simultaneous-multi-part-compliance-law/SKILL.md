## Area 10 — Simultaneous Multi-Part Compliance Law

### 10.1 — The No-Local-Decisioning Mandate

Local decisioning is the error of checking a creative act against only the most immediately relevant Part without checking it against the full Canon simultaneously. It is the most common source of Canon violations in complex world-building. Part 28 prohibits it absolutely.

**Why Local Decisioning Is Dangerous:**
A creative act that satisfies its most immediate governing Part may still violate five other Parts that bear on the same moment. A character who deploys a Named Move (Part 6 compliant) that draws on a cultivation affinity combination (Part 4 compliant) but whose power output contradicts their established realm ceiling (Part 3 violation) and whose deployment escalates the conflict to a tier that has not been narratively legitimized (Part 5 violation) and whose psychological state at PIS-7 makes such precise technique deployment implausible (Part 26 violation) — is a three-Part violation that only appears as a zero-Part violation if the check was local to Part 6.

### 10.2 — The Full-Canon Simultaneous Check

Every creative act must be checked against the following before execution:

| Check Category | Governing Parts | What to Verify |
|---|---|---|
| Power ceiling compliance | Part 3, Part 8 | Character's realm/tier authorizes this output |
| Classification compliance | Part 16 | Character's type classification is consistent with the act |
| Ideology compliance | Part 20 | Act is consistent with character's current ideology |
| Psychological compliance | Part 26 | PIS state makes this act plausible |
| Consequence trigger check | Part 27 | Does this act require CSS consequence generation |
| Ideology event trigger check | Part 20 | Does this act trigger IE events |
| Hero/Villain/Side Char Ledger check | Parts 17/18/19 | Does this require a Ledger update |
| Faction compliance | Part 22 | Act is consistent with faction governance |
| Affiliation compliance | Part 23 | Act is consistent with affiliation positions |
| Information compliance | Part 24 | Character has the information this act requires |
| Cultural compliance | Part 25 | Act is consistent with cultural norms |
| Location compliance | Part 13 | Location supports the act's requirements |
| Travel compliance | Part 15 | Arrivals/departures are travel-time consistent |
| Dialogue compliance | Part 29 | Speech is consistent with voice architecture |
| Tactical compliance | Part 5 | Escalation is consistent with force legitimacy |
| Synergy compliance | Part 31 | Any synergy outputs are consistent with integration model |

### 10.3 — The Cascade Check

When a creative act passes the simultaneous check, it may trigger further checks through cascade: the CSS consequence produced by the act requires its own ideological trigger check; the IE event triggered by the act requires a psychological impact check; the PIS change requires a subsequent combat performance check; the combat performance check requires a power ceiling re-verification. Cascade checking is not separate from the simultaneous check — it is the extension of it through the consequence chain.

### 10.4 — The Check Before the Act Rule

The simultaneous multi-Part check occurs before the creative act is executed in the prose, not after. Checking after execution creates the pressure to rationalize a completed act rather than to evaluate it fairly. The discipline of Part 28 is pre-execution discipline: the author conducts the simultaneous check, makes any necessary adjustments to the planned creative act, and only then renders it in prose.
