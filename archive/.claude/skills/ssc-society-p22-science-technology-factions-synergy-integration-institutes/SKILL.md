## Area 9 — Science, Technology Factions and Synergy Integration Institutes

### 9.1 — Overview: Science and Technology Factions (STF)

Science and Technology Factions are organized institutions whose primary purpose is the development, production, deployment, and maintenance of technology — spanning the full Part 8 Tier 1–20 technology scale. They range from a village blacksmith's guild to galaxy-spanning technology corporations that design and deploy Tier 13–14 engineering megastructures.

**Defining Characteristics:**
- **Primary Purpose:** Technology research, development, production, infrastructure maintenance
- **Core Resource:** Technical knowledge (Part 10 scientific prerequisites), manufacturing infrastructure, energy resources, personnel (engineers, scientists, AI systems)
- **Cultivation Component:** Optional but increasingly valuable with tier — at Tier 8+, CI Technology requires cultivation integration knowledge
- **Natural Scale Range:** Village (SCL-1) through Universe (SCL-14)

### 9.3 — Synergy Integration Institutes (SII)

Synergy Integration Institutes are specialized factions that exist at the intersection of cultivator sects and technology corporations. Their primary purpose is cultivating and producing **Integrated Practitioners** (Part 31, Area 12) — individuals who simultaneously satisfy both cultivation-side and technology-side thresholds for Model C or Model D synergy.

**Formation Prerequisites:**
- Must have access to at minimum CCT-7 (Stellar) civilization's institutional resources
- Must have access to Core Formation+ cultivators on the cultivation side
- Must have Tier 8+ CI Technology infrastructure on the technology side
- Must maintain both a cultivation training curriculum AND a technology training curriculum simultaneously

**Governance Note:** All synergy outputs produced by SII members are jointly governed by Part 31 (synergy mechanism) and Part 15 (synergy travel output if applicable). The SII faction itself is classified under Part 22 for organizational governance.

**SII Scale Range:** World (SCL-7) through Galaxy (SCL-11)
