## Area 12 — Technology Travel: Galactic Transport (Tiers 11–14)

### 12.1 — Tier 11 Galactic FTL Transport

At Tier 11, a civilization's travel infrastructure spans a full galaxy. FTL speeds reach levels where even galactic-scale distances become days-to-weeks journeys on established routes. Gate networks expand to galaxy-spanning infrastructure.

| Mode | Effective Speed | Transit Time (100 kly galaxy) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 11 standard FTL | 50,000–200,000× light | 6 months – 2 years | Off-lane travel |
| Tier 11 express galactic lane | 500,000–2,000,000× light | 2–8 weeks | Beacon-dense established corridor |
| Tier 11 galactic gate network | Near-instantaneous | Minutes | Gate infrastructure required |
| Tier 11 direct gate transit | Near-instantaneous | Minutes to hours (gate + approach) | Up to 10 kly range per gate pair |

**Tier 11 Gate Network Properties:**
- Gate pairs at Tier 11 can span up to 10,000 light-years (10 kly)
- Galaxy-spanning networks are achieved through gate chains (hop from gate to gate)
- A galactic crossing via gate chain: 10–20 gate hops, each taking minutes → total transit: hours
- Gate construction time at Tier 11: years of industrial investment per pair
- Gate network governance becomes a major political axis of Tier 11 civilizations

### 12.2 — Tiers 12–14 Intergalactic and Supercluster Transport

| Tier | Scale | Travel Mode | Transit Time (full scale) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 12 | Galaxy Cluster / Local Group (10 Mly) | Cross-galactic FTL lanes + intergalactic gate chains | Months on established routes; years on new routes |
| Tier 13 | Galaxy Supercluster (~500 Mly) | Multi-galactic gate networks; wormhole infrastructure | Weeks to months via established gate infrastructure |
| Tier 14 | Supercluster complex to near-universal | Supercluster-spanning transit architecture | Months to years via gate chains; years via raw FTL |

**Wormhole Technology (Tier 13):**
Tier 13 technology achieves stable wormhole construction — permanent spatial tunnels that are self-maintaining and do not require paired activation. Unlike star gates (which require both ends to be active simultaneously), wormholes are always-open once established. Wormholes become the standard infrastructure for inter-galactic-cluster travel at Tier 13+.
