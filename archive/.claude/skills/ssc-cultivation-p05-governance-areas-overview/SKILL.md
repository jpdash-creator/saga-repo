## Area 2 — Governance Areas Overview

The ten governance areas of Part 5 are:

**Governance Area 1 — Force Legitimacy (Area 4):**
Determines when violence is recognized as combat versus assault, assassination, or accident. Defines protected status, initiation conditions, and declaration doctrine.

**Governance Area 2 — Escalation Categorization (Area 5):**
Classifies all conflicts into seven deterministic escalation tiers (E1 through E7) plus the Formal Duel special classification. Defines the conditions under which each tier exists and how conflicts escalate between tiers.

**Governance Area 3 — Intensity Control (Area 7):**
Defines the upper and lower bounds of force expression permitted within each escalation tier. Governs proportional force, involuntary escalation, and escalation violations.

**Governance Area 4 — Engagement Recognition (Area 6):**
Determines when two or more parties are officially in combat — defining the moment of engagement entry, mutual hostility threshold, one-sided engagement conditions, and disengagement versus termination.

**Governance Area 5 — Third-Party Interference (Area 8):**
Defines the legitimacy of interruption, assistance, betrayal, proxy involvement, observer rights, and alliance activation in active combat.

**Governance Area 6 — Battlefield Containment (Area 9):**
Defines spatial and cosmological containment of combat events. Establishes protected zones, battlefield footprint by cultivation grade, cross-dimensional combat rules, and sacred ground interaction.

**Governance Area 7 — Termination Conditions (Area 12):**
Defines when combat ends mechanically. Classifies victory, defeat, draw, and surrender. Establishes post-combat obligations.

**Governance Area 8 — Authority Interaction During Combat (Area 11):**
Ensures faction structure, leadership succession, and command continuity function during combat. Governs leader death consequences, command transfer, and the leaderless faction state.

**Governance Area 9 — Multi-Actor Stabilization (Area 10):**
Prevents chaotic multi-faction escalation from breaking the deterministic structure. Defines chaos threshold, faction alignment tracking, opportunistic entry rules, temporary ceasefire protocols, and coalition formation.

**Governance Area 10 — Escalation Coherence Guidance (Area 15):**
Provides guidance for ensuring escalation tier classification feels coherent with the current in-world power and political scale. The arbitration framework in Area 15 helps authors assess whether a proposed conflict scope fits the story's current scale, and what narrative development may be needed before a larger-scale conflict can be earned.
