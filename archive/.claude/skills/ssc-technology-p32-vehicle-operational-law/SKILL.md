## Area 10 — Vehicle Operational Law

### 10.1 — Crew Governance Principles

**Rule 10.1.1 — Minimum Crew Certification:** Operating any vehicle above SC-II at IS-Band or above requires certified crew trained specifically for that vehicle class's drive systems, navigation architecture, and emergency protocols. Part 9's institutional prerequisite model (Area 3.2) governs crew training infrastructure.

**Rule 10.1.2 — Cultivator Crew and Vessel Tier:** A cultivator's realm does not substitute for technical training in operating IS-Band and above vehicles. A Divine Realm cultivator without navigation training cannot operate an LLV-3 Lightleaper Merchant's FTL drive safely. Cultivation enhances what trained crews can do; it does not replace the training. Exception: CHV-C and CHV-D vehicles where soul-interface directly substitutes for some conventional training requirements.

**Rule 10.1.3 — Realm Augmentation of Vehicle Performance:** Cultivators operating vehicles within their realm compatibility range can push vehicle performance beyond design specifications through direct qi/energy injection. However:
- Performance boost ceiling: +30% above design maximum for Xiantian; +60% for Core Realm; +150% for Divine Realm
- Each instance of performance boost causes structural stress (tracked narratively)
- Accumulated structural stress requires repair before the next boost; ignoring accumulated stress produces failure events
- Part 3's energy expenditure rules apply: qi-boosting a vehicle is an expenditure event

### 10.2 — Vehicle Resource and Fuel Governance

**Rule 10.2.1 — Fuel Is Not Optional:** Every vehicle has defined fuel requirements. Narrative ignoring of fuel cost is a Canon Variance (Part 1, Section 3.3.2). The fuel type and consumption rate for each vehicle class are defined by its propulsion category (Area 8).

| Propulsion Category | Fuel Type | Consumption Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| P-I (Chemical/Thermal) | Biological or refined chemical | Continuous consumption during operation |
| P-II (Fusion/Antimatter) | Fuel isotopes or antimatter | High burn during acceleration; minimal during coast |
| P-III (FTL Drives) | Exotic energy (Type 7 Level 6+) | Large consumption at FTL initiation; sustained during transit |
| P-IV (Formation Drive) | CPM (grade determined by array tier) | Continuous CPM consumption during formation drive operation |
| P-V/Gate Transit | Major energy infrastructure | Gate activation cost is enormous; individual vessel contributes zero — the gate is powered by civilization-scale infrastructure |

**Rule 10.2.2 — CPM Fuel Grade Compatibility (CHV Vehicles):** Formation Drive vehicles require CPM grade matched to their array tier per Part 11 and Part 12. Mismatched CPM grade causes absorption conflict equivalent (Part 11, Area 7.6) in the vehicle's energy systems — not in biological meridians, but in the formation energy pathways. This causes degraded performance and eventual formation damage.

### 10.3 — Vehicle Maintenance and Degradation

**Rule 10.3.1 — Technology Ceiling and Vehicle Degradation:** Per Part 8, P8-2, technology above the civilization's Technology Ceiling degrades. A civilization that loses one tier of technology advancement cannot maintain its highest-tier vehicles — they begin to degrade within years and become non-functional within decades without the industrial infrastructure to service them (Part 8, Area 8.2).

**Rule 10.3.2 — Formation Component Degradation:** Formation array components in CHV vehicles degrade differently from technology components. Formation arrays that are not re-inscribed by a qualified practitioner at their maintenance interval lose effectiveness at 5–10% per year until they fall below functional threshold. This is the cultivation-technology hybrid maintenance burden: you need both a qualified engineer AND a qualified formation master for sustained CHV operation.
