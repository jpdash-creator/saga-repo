## Area 3 — Orbital and Inner-System Transport Vehicles (OS-Band, Tiers 5–7)

### 3.1 — Vehicle Type Philosophy: OS-Band

OS-Band vehicles operate between planetary bodies within a single star system — from orbital altitude above a planet through the full reach of the outer system. They are subluminal vessels operating in the vacuum of space, powered by drives ranging from chemical rockets through fusion and antimatter systems. The OS-Band is where vehicles first become truly three-dimensional, where the concept of "a ship" transitions from a surface metaphor to a genuine three-dimensional space vessel, and where military vessel classes first develop the doctrine of space combat.

OS-Band vehicles are organized into four primary operational categories: **System Runners** (civilian transit), **System Warcraft** (military), **System Workers** (infrastructure and industrial), and **Deep Station Platforms** (fixed or near-fixed infrastructure).

---

### 3.2 — Tier 5–6 Inner System Vessels

**Category: System Runner Vessels (SRV)**

*SRV-1 Orbital Commuter:* Small personnel transit vehicle for low-orbit to station and station-to-station transit. SC-II. Speed: 7.9–50 km/s orbital range. Capacity: 4–20 passengers. The space-equivalent of a city bus — mundane but essential for orbital infrastructure workers and commuters. Crew: 1–2.

*SRV-2 Inner-System Clipper:* Passenger transit vessel for inner planetary body transit (planet to moon, inner to middle planets). SC-III to SC-IV. Speed: 100–500 km/s (fusion drive). Transit time: 2–10 days per AU. Capacity: 20–200 passengers. The standard short-to-medium range civilian people-mover.

*SRV-3 System Merchant:* Civilian cargo transport for bulk goods between inner planetary bodies. SC-IV to SC-V. Speed: 50–200 km/s. Cargo capacity: 5,000–50,000 tons. Transit time: 3–15 days per AU. The backbone of inner-system commerce.

*SRV-4 Luxury System Liner:* High-comfort passenger transit vessel with extended life support, entertainment and hospitality infrastructure, scenic routing capability. SC-IV. Capacity: 100–500 passengers. The transit vessel for wealthy individuals and diplomatic parties in inner-system circuits.

**Category: System Warcraft (SWC)**

*SWC-1 System Patrol Craft:* Small, fast military vessel for customs, law enforcement, and system patrol operations. SC-II to SC-III. Speed: 500–2,000 km/s (performance drive). Weapons: light direct-fire and guided munitions. Crew: 5–30. Configuration: PAT. The everyday law enforcement vehicle of a Tier 5–6 civilization's system authority.

*SWC-2 System Strike Fighter:* SC-I to SC-II single or two-person military attack craft optimized for speed, maneuverability, and weapons capacity in inner-system engagement ranges. Speed: 1,000–5,000 km/s burst. Combat doctrine: dogfighting, escort, strike. Part 5 combat: SWC-2 fighters operate in E3–E4 as individual units; coordinated squadrons reach E5.

*SWC-3 Corvette-Class Warship:* SC-III medium warship with balanced speed, firepower, and crew. The standard small military vessel of a Tier 5–6 star system defense force. Crew: 30–150. Armament: medium-range weapons array. Part 5 combat: E4–E5 individually.

*SWC-4 Destroyer-Class Warship:* SC-IV military warship with heavy weapons load and anti-capital-ship capability. Crew: 150–600. Speed: lower than a corvette but significantly higher firepower. The backbone combat vessel of most Tier 5–6 system defense fleets.

*SWC-5 Battleship-Class Warship:* SC-V capital warship — the apex Tier 5–6 military vessel. Crew: 600–5,000. Immense weapons capacity, heavy armor equivalent, flagship-capable command infrastructure. Part 5 combat: a single Battleship-class operates at E5–E6. Classified MIL-CAP.

**Category: System Workers (SWK)**

*SWK-1 Orbital Constructor:* Specialized vessel with extended manipulator arms, micro-thrust precision control, and construction material bays for assembly of orbital infrastructure. SC-III to SC-IV. Not a transit vehicle — an industrial vehicle that operates at the intersection of vehicles and infrastructure.

*SWK-2 System Tanker:* Bulk fuel and resource carrier, typically slower than passenger vessels but with enormous cargo capacity. SC-IV to SC-V. The vehicle that makes refueling stations viable.

*SWK-3 Salvage and Recovery Vessel:* Multi-role utility craft for space debris clearance, ship recovery, and emergency response. SC-III. The space-equivalent of a tow truck and ambulance combined.

*SWK-4 Deep Miner:* Mining vessel for asteroid belt and outer-planet resource extraction. SC-III to SC-IV. Heavy drill and processing equipment; extremely slow transit (mining-optimized, not speed-optimized). The primary resource extraction vehicle for civilizations whose planetary surface resources are exhausted.

### 3.3 — Tier 7 Full System Vessels

**Category: System Range Runners (SRR)**

*SRR-1 Deep System Explorer:* Survey vessel optimized for outer system transit — heliopause-capable, extended mission endurance, science instrumentation and probe launch capability. SC-III to SC-IV. Speed: 2,000–10,000 km/s. Mission duration: 1–5 years. Crew: 20–100. The vehicle of first exploration of a star system's full extent.

*SRR-2 Outer-System Freighter:* Heavy cargo vessel for outer-system resource depots and stations. SC-IV to SC-V. Speed: 500–2,000 km/s. Cargo capacity: 50,000–500,000 tons. Transit time for 50 AU route: 1–4 months. The economic lifeline for outer system industrial operations.

*SRR-3 Frontier Colony Ship:* SC-V to SC-VI vessel designed to transport a population colony of 5,000–100,000 to a destination within the same stellar system. Not yet capable of interstellar transit, but represents the maximum civilian transit unit of Tier 5–7 civilizations for within-system colonization.

**Category: Full System Warcraft (FSW)**

*FSW-1 Fleet Carrier:* SC-VI capital vessel carrying 100–1,000 SWC-2 Strike Fighters and supporting combat vessels. The full-system military apex platform of Tier 7 civilizations. Crew: 10,000–80,000. Part 5 combat: a Fleet Carrier with full wing represents E6–E7 level destructive output. Classified MIL-CAP.

*FSW-2 System Fortress:* Not a transit vessel — a heavily armed, lightly mobile orbital defense installation. SC-VI to SC-VII. Designed for permanent system denial rather than fleet maneuver. Armed with planet-facing and deep-space-facing weapons capable of engaging entire enemy fleets without moving significantly. Part 5 combat: E7 installation-scale.
