## Area 10 — Mercantile and Trading Factions

### 10.1 — Overview: Mercantile and Trading Factions (MTF)

Mercantile and Trading Factions are organized institutions whose primary purpose is the systematic movement of goods, resources, and services between locations for commercial profit. They are among the most wide-ranging faction types in the Starward Saga — capable of operating at SCL-1 (village peddler collectives) through SCL-15 (multiverse-crossing trading networks). Trading factions may deal in any goods: cultivation resources, technology products, CPM substances, relics, information, services, or living beings.

**Defining Characteristics:**
- **Primary Purpose:** Commercial trade; supply chain management; profit maximization
- **Core Resource:** Trade routes, capital, logistics infrastructure, faction reputation, market intelligence
- **Travel Dependency:** Trading factions are uniquely dependent on Part 15 travel infrastructure — their operational range is directly bounded by what travel paradigm is available (Part 15 P15-4 infrastructure multiplier principle applies most powerfully to trading factions)
- **Natural Scale Range:** Village (SCL-1) through Multiverse (SCL-15) — wider than any other faction type

### 10.2 — Trading Faction Classifications

| Trading Faction Type | Primary Goods | Scale | Travel Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Village Peddler Guild | Local produce, basic goods, tools | SCL-1 to SCL-2 | Foot and animal transport |
| Town Merchant Association | Craft goods, regional specialties, basic CPM | SCL-2 to SCL-4 | Wagon and sail routes |
| City Trade Cartel | Processed goods, luxury items, mid-grade CPM | SCL-3 to SCL-6 | Road and early industrial transport |
| Kingdom Merchant House | Kingdom-scale goods distribution; CPM Grade 5–12 | SCL-5 to SCL-7 | Rail; early orbital access |
| Inter-World Trading Corporation | Cross-planetary trade; mixed goods and technology | SCL-7 to SCL-9 | Orbital/Inner-System transport |
| Stellar Trade Fleet | Star-system spanning goods movement | SCL-8 to SCL-10 | FTL-adjacent/sub-FTL |
| Interstellar Trade Conglomerate | Multi-star-system trade network; CPM and technology | SCL-9 to SCL-11 | FTL transport; gate-access preferred |
| Galactic Merchant Empire | Galaxy-spanning trade empire; all goods categories | SCL-11 | Star gate dependent; Tier 10+ |
| Trans-Galaxy Trading Syndicate | Multi-galaxy; cross-civilization trade | SCL-12 to SCL-13 | Galactic gate grids (Tier 11–14) |
| Universal Trade Council | Universe-scale trade governance; standardized exchange | SCL-14 | Tier 15+ transport |
| Multiverse Cartel | Trans-universal trade; extremely rare | SCL-15 | Divine Sea Realm couriers; CCT-17+ gates |

### 10.3 — Trading Faction Internal Governance

Trading factions typically use a **Stakeholder Hierarchy** — authority is distributed based on capital investment and trade volume rather than cultivation realm or technical achievement:

| Rank | Title | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Grand Merchant Lord / Trading Patriarch | Controls majority share; ultimate authority | Faction identity; major trade route decisions; charter amendments |
| Senior Partner | Major stakeholder; board of directors equivalent | Trade policy; expansion decisions; alliance ratification |
| Active Partner | Mid-tier stakeholder; regional management | Regional route management; local contracts |
| Branch Manager | Non-equity management of regional operations | Day-to-day regional trade; personnel |
| Senior Trader | Experienced contract-active merchant | Mid-scale trade contract execution |
| Journeyman Trader | Contract-active merchant | Standard contract execution |
| Apprentice Merchant | Training; accompanied trade runs | Observation; limited contract support |
| Logistics Staff | Caravan guards, navigation crew, warehouse workers | Operational support; not equity-holding |

### 10.4 — Special Rule: Trading Faction Neutrality Claim

Trading factions at SCL-8 and above may invoke the **Trading Faction Neutrality Claim** — a formal assertion that the trading faction's commercial operations are neutral with respect to inter-faction conflicts within its operational area. Neutrality Claim:
- Does NOT provide immunity from taxation or regulation by legitimate government bodies
- DOES create an expectation of non-interference with trade operations during non-wartime disputes
- MAY be revoked by any party if the trading faction is demonstrated to be supplying a belligerent exclusively
- IS recognized and enforced in CCT-8+ civilizations by Judicial Factions as a customary commercial right
