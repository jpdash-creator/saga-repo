## Area 4 — Information Tier and Strategic Valuation

### 4.1 — Tier I: Sovereign Secrets

Sovereign Secrets are the highest-classification information in the canonical hierarchy. Their defining characteristic is that exposure creates a non-recoverable strategic shift. Examples with full governance implications:

**Sovereign Secret Category A — Location Secrets:**
- Sacred Ground exact location and grade (Part 13 Area 14 — knowledge of an S-Grade 5+ Sacred Ground enables military targeting or seizure attempt)
- Energy Formation Zone coordinates of undiscovered zones (Part 11 — CPM supply leverage)
- Apex relic storage locations (Part 12 Tier 4–5 relics)
- Galaxy-faction true headquarters location (Part 22 — the concealed operational center of an SCL-11+ faction)

**Sovereign Secret Category B — Person Secrets:**
- The true identity of the leader of a Hidden Faction (Part 22 Area 19.2)
- The cultivation realm of an Apex Faction's peak cultivator (FPT-13+ individuals)
- The existence and location of a Protected Heir before succession announcement
- The true cultivation technique lineage of a founding ancestor (technique source grants no legitimate claim alone, but reveals strategic priorities and weaknesses)

**Sovereign Secret Category C — Capability Secrets:**
- Proprietary formation array designs of Tier 4+ capability
- Fusion technique breakthrough outcomes (Part 7 — a successful Model D integration)
- A faction's FTL capability before adversaries know it exists
- A civilization's actual military strength as opposed to its publicly acknowledged strength

**Governance Rule P24-T1-1:** All Part 24 Tier I secrets, when exposed, automatically trigger Part 27 consequence law. The narrative must account for the consequence cascade. No Tier I exposure can be narratively treated as inconsequential.

### 4.2 — Tier II through Tier V

See Area 2.1 for the full classification table. The detailed governance rules for acquisition, protection, and weaponization of each tier are governed in Areas 15 (Acquisition Methods), 16 (Protection Architecture), and 17 (Weaponization).
