## Area 21 — The Primordium / Absolute Chaos

The Primordium / Absolute Chaos exists beyond the Multiverse. It is the existential substrate within which multiverses float. It does not conform to the classification systems of Part 13 for normal locations.

**P13-10 restated:** The Primordium does not have classes. Its sub-regions are governed by their own internal principles. "Absolute Chaos" is the term used when the Primordium is experienced in its most turbulent, unstructured form — the raw substrate before pattern emerges.

Narrative deployment of Primordium locations requires Part 28 (Creative Flexibility Law) Tier 2 Authorization. Authors may reference it as cosmological fact, legend, or distant horizon. It may not be deployed as a character-accessible setting within any narrative phase without explicit authorization.
