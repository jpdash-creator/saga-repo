## Area 19 — Part 21 and Energy Resources (Parts 11, 12 Interface)

### 19.1 — EA Axis and Part 11

EA is calibrated against Part 11's CPM substance states and Part 8's Energy Technology domain. The EA assessment table (Area 2.3) references specific CPM grades (Part 11) and technology energy equivalents (Part 8 Domain 7).

**Rule 19.1.1 — EA Does Not Grant CPM Access:** A civilization's EA value does not grant individual practitioners access to CPM grades beyond what they can safely absorb (Part 11 Realm Ceiling Constraint, P11-6). EA describes civilizational resource access; individual CPM use is governed by Part 11.

### 19.2 — Formation Arrays and EA

Formation Arrays (Part 12, Area 7) at high tiers create ambient cultivation energy environments that contribute to a civilization's effective EA. A civilization with extensive Tier 4 Formation Array infrastructure may have a higher effective EA than its raw CPM access alone would suggest.
