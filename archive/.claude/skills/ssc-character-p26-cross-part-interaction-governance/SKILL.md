## Area 15 — Cross-Part Interaction Governance

### 15.1 — Part 3 (Power and Lifespan Law) Interaction

**Governed jointly:**
- Physical Qi Deviation (Part 3, Area 7.3) → psychological aftermath (this Part, Area 3.1)
- Realm breakthrough identity disruption (this Part, Area 3.5)
- Extended lifespan isolation psychology (this Part, Area 2.5)
- Core Formation psychological confrontation (this Part, Area 11.1)
- Talent deprivation psychology (Part 3 defines talent levels; this Part governs the psychological impact of low-talent discovery)
- Physical injury and its psychological aftermath (Part 3 Area 8 governs the injury; this Part governs the psychology that follows)

### 15.2 — Part 4 (Affinity, Heart, Soul Law) Interaction

**Governed jointly:**
- Cultivation Heart as psychological architecture (Part 4 governs Heart power mechanics; this Part governs psychological pathways to Heart states)
- Soul cultivation psychological impacts (this Part, Area 3.2)
- Affinity awakening psychology (this Part, Area 3.4)
- Heart suppression as psychological event (this Part, Area 3.3; Part 4 governs Heart power consequences)
- Soul contamination psychological experience (this Part, Area 3.2; Part 4 governs soul defense and healing)
- Origin Convergence identity crisis (this Part, Area 3.4; Part 4 governs affinity mechanics)

**Governing Boundary:**
Part 4 governs what happens to the soul and Heart at the energetic/metaphysical level.
Part 26 governs what the character *experiences* psychologically as a result.

### 15.3 — Part 5 (Combat and Fight Law) Interaction

**Governed jointly:**
- Pre-combat psychological state modifiers on technique success rates (this Part, Area 10.2; Part 5 governs engagement structure)
- Post-combat psychological consequence (Part 5 governs the combat; this Part governs what follows)
- Psychological impact of escalation tier violations on witnesses and participants (joint)
- Kill impact psychology (this Part, Area 2.1.1; Part 5 governs legitimacy classification of kills)

### 15.4 — Part 6 (Move Taxonomy Law) Interaction

**Governed jointly:**
- Psychological state modifiers on move success rates (this Part, Area 10.1; Part 6 governs base success rates)
- Technique identity (a cultivator's Named Moves are part of their identity; technique-specific psychology is joint governance)
- Move failure psychological impact (this Part assigns PIS ratings to significant technique failures; Part 6 governs the failure mechanics)

### 15.5 — Part 20 (Ideology Law) Interaction

**Governed jointly:**
- A character's ideology is both a psychological framework (which shapes how they interpret and cope with events — this Part) and a governance domain in its own right (Part 20)
- Ideological crisis — when events fundamentally challenge a character's ideology — is a compound event governed by both Parts
- The relationship between psychological wounds and the development of ideological positions (chronic wounds often generate protective ideological frameworks — this Part explains the psychological origin of what Part 20 governs as belief)

### 15.6 — Part 25 (Culture and Social Law) Interaction

**Governed jointly:**
- Cultural grief and cultural trauma (joint Part 25/Part 26 domain — this Part governs the psychological mechanism; Part 25 governs the cultural dimension)
- Cultural expectations as sources of psychological pressure (this Part, Area 5.1)
- How cultural norms determine which coping mechanisms are available (Part 25 governs cultural norms; this Part governs their psychological impact)
- The psychological cost of cultural dissonance and forced assimilation (joint)

### 15.7 — Part 27 (Consequence Law) Interaction

**Governing boundary:**
- Part 26 governs the internal psychological experience of events (what the character experiences internally)
- Part 27 governs the external consequences of actions (what happens in the world as a result of actions)
- Psychological impacts are internal consequences — they are within this Part's domain exclusively
- The psychological impact of experiencing external consequences (witnessing what one's actions caused) is Part 26 domain (the psychology of confronting consequences)

### 15.8 — Part 29 (Dialog and Social Interaction Law) Interaction

**Governed jointly:**
- Every dialogue scene is a psychological event — the character's psychological state shapes how they speak, what they reveal, what they conceal, and how they receive what others say
- Dialogue as psychological revelation is joint Part 26/Part 29 governance
- Psychologically charged dialogues (confrontation, grief, moral conflict, confession) require both Parts to be satisfied simultaneously

### 15.9 — Part 31 (Cultivation-Technology Synergy Law) Interaction

**Governed jointly:**
- Integration psychology (cybernetic and cultivation-technology integration identity impacts — this Part, Area 4.2)
- The psychological experience of being an Integrated Practitioner (new-being-type alienation — this Part; synergy mechanics — Part 31)
- Technology-cultivation tension as a psychological phenomenon (the internal experience of this tension — this Part; the social/civilizational dimensions — Part 31 and Part 25)
