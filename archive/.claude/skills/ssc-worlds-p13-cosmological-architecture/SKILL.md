## Area 2 — Cosmological Architecture

### 2.1 — The Complete Cosmological Hierarchy

The Starward Saga universe is organized into the following hierarchy from smallest to largest. Each tier strictly contains only the tier immediately below it within the standard hierarchy.

**Tier 1 — Village:** The smallest defined inhabited location unit. A single inhabited structure, compound, hall, or small settlement cluster. The fundamental unit of settlement.

**Tier 2 — Town:** An organized inhabited cluster larger than a single compound. A small-to-medium settlement with social organization, basic commerce, and defined territory.

**Tier 3 — City:** A major urban center with complex governance, multiple administrative districts, a self-sustaining economy, and major cultivation or technology infrastructure.

**Tier 4 — Capital:** The political, administrative, and cultivation apex of a governance structure — the center from which authority radiates. Carries symbolic, historical, and power-concentration functions.

**Tier 5 — Kingdom:** An organized political territory with defined borders, a recognized governing structure, military capability, and a cultivation/technology character that defines the political unit. Scale ranges from minor principalities to single-world powers.

**Tier 6 — Empire:** A major multi-territory political entity spanning multiple Kingdoms or equivalent governance zones under unified imperial authority.

**Tier 7 — World:** A self-contained planetary body — complete or capable of supporting civilization. Contains all sub-World tiers (Villages through Empires) within it.

**Tier 8 — Star System:** A complete stellar unit — a star or stars and all bodies orbiting within their gravitational influence. Contains Worlds and all sub-World tiers.

**Tier 9 — Star Cluster:** A gravitationally associated group of Star Systems sharing a regional spatial neighborhood. Contains Star Systems and all below.

**Tier 10 — Star Quadrant:** Multiple Star Clusters forming a navigable region of a Galaxy. Contains Star Clusters and all below. A Star Quadrant exists within a Galaxy only.

**Tier 11 — Galaxy:** A gravitationally bound collection of Star Quadrants — the fundamental large-scale structure of the physical universe. Contains Star Quadrants and all below.

**Tier 12 — Galaxy Cluster:** Multiple Galaxies gravitationally associated. Contains Galaxies and all below. Does NOT directly contain Star Quadrants or Star Clusters.

**Tier 13 — Galaxy Supercluster:** Multiple Galaxy Clusters forming cosmic web structures. Contains Galaxy Clusters and Galaxies. Does NOT directly contain Star Quadrants, Star Clusters, or Star Systems.

**Tier 14 — Universe:** All observable physical reality in a single spacetime manifold. Contains Galaxy Superclusters and all below.

**Tier 15 — Multiverse:** Multiple Universes. Each Universe within the Multiverse may have different physical constants, dimensional properties, and cultivation energy profiles.

**Tier 16 — The Primordium / Absolute Chaos:** The medium containing the Multiverse. Not a "place" in the normal sense but the existential substrate within which multiverses float. Beyond classification.

### 2.2 — Rogue Locations (Exceptions to Containment)

Not all locations conform to standard containment. Recognized exceptions are classified normally by their tier but carry a "Rogue" governance annotation:

**Rogue World:** A world unbound from any star — drifting in inter-system space or dimensional anomaly pockets.

**Rogue Star System:** A star system not gravitationally associated with any Star Cluster, drifting in inter-cluster void.

**Rogue Star Cluster:** A cluster not contained within any Star Quadrant, occupying inter-quadrant void within a Galaxy.

**Rogue Star Quadrant:** A quadrant-equivalent region that is not part of any recognized Galaxy — an intergalactic formation or anomalous structure. Extremely rare.

Rogue locations are classified by their type and class normally. The Rogue designation is a governance annotation, not a separate classification tier.

### 2.3 — Ambient Energy Class System

All locations carry an Ambient Energy Class from 1 to 7. This class is an intrinsic property of the location — determined by the density of natural cultivation energy in the environment. It is NOT determined by who lives there.

| Class | Designation | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Class 1 | Barren | No detectable ambient cultivation energy. Raw, unformed environment. Cultivation is possible but receives no environmental support. |
| Class 2 | Sparse | Trace ambient energy. Ordinary inhabited worlds and settlements. Sufficient for basic cultivation but providing no meaningful acceleration. |
| Class 3 | Mild | Noticeable ambient energy density. Cultivation communities form naturally here. Recovery and sensing are moderately enhanced. |
| Class 4 | Rich | Dense ambient energy. Major cultivation centers. Recovery and advancement are meaningfully enhanced. Formation Zones may be present. |
| Class 5 | Concentrated | High ambient energy density. Rare naturally occurring conditions. Active Vortex Formation Zones likely present. |
| Class 6 | Intense | Very high ambient density. Environment visibly saturated with energy — perceptible to any cultivator. Natural Divine Liquid possible in rare pockets. |
| Class 7 | Transcendent | Maximum naturally sustained ambient energy density. Exclusive to Sacred Ground zones and their surrounding influence areas. Reality is visibly altered. |

### 2.4 — Affinity Profile System

Locations may have a natural affinity profile — the dominant cultivation energy type expressed in the environment. Affinity profile is a property of the place, not a ceiling on who can cultivate there.

| Affinity Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Neutral | No dominant affinity. Supports all cultivation paths equally. Most inhabited locations. |
| Fire | Volcanic, extreme heat, solar-proximate environments. Fire affinity cultivators advance faster here; opposing affinities (Ice, Water) may be suppressed. |
| Ice/Water | Polar, deep ocean, cold void environments. |
| Lightning | Perpetual storm zones, high electromagnetic activity. |
| Earth/Metal | Dense mineral formations, ancient mountain cores, metallic asteroid concentrations. |
| Wind/Space | High-altitude, orbital fringe, dimensional thinning zones. |
| Life/Wood | Biosphere-saturated, forest-world environments, ancient growth zones. |
| Death/Void | Ancient battlefields, collapsed star remnants, dimensional null zones. |
| Soul | Soul-resonant zones, ancient cultivator burial grounds, sites of great spiritual events. |
| Space/Time | Dimensional convergence zones, gravitational anomalies, temporal distortion sites. |
| Mixed | Two or more strong affinities present simultaneously — rare and typically highly unusual. |

### 2.5 — Special Zone Status

Every location may carry one or more Special Zone designations. These are the most governance-significant properties of any location.

| Special Zone | Description | Governed By |
|---|---|---|
| None | Standard location. No special spatial properties beyond ambient energy class. | — |
| Passive Formation Zone | Natural energy accumulation site producing lower-grade CPM substances over time. | Part 11, Part 13 Area 10 |
| Vortex Formation Zone | Active high-energy confluence. Produces mid-to-high grade CPM substances. Dangerous to the unprepared. | Part 11, Part 13 Area 10 |
| Sacred Ground (Grades S1–S8) | The highest special zone status. Formed through extended Divine Sea Realm cultivation presence. Graded S1 (weakest) through S8 (most powerful). | Part 13 Area 9 |
| Void Zone | A region of complete spatial absence — no ambient energy, no qi dimension activity. Natural or artificially sealed. Extremely hazardous. | Part 13 Area 11 |
| Dimensional Rift (Stable) | Persistent spatial connection between two non-adjacent locations or dimensions. High strategic value. | Part 13 Area 12 |
| Dimensional Tear (Unstable) | Uncontrolled spacetime opening. Dangerous, shifting, may expand or collapse. | Part 13 Area 12 |
| Resonance Plane | A location where a dimensional layer (Fire Plane, Soul Plane, etc.) is accessible from physical space. | Part 13 Area 13 |
| Extradimensional Pocket | A stable dimensional space detached from standard physical coordinates. Natural or artifact-created. | Part 12, Part 13 Area 12 |
| Ruins Zone | A location containing ancient structures, dormant formation arrays, or residual energy from a prior civilization. | Part 13 Area 14 |
