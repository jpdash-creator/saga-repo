Technology and cultivation are two independent parallel tracks — neither determines the other; the Soft Correspondence Tendency Table (Area 4.2) describes tendencies only. The 20-tier scale supersedes the prior 12-tier scale; the eight new intermediate tiers (6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15, 17) are genuine civilizational thresholds with their own prerequisites and capability floors.*
