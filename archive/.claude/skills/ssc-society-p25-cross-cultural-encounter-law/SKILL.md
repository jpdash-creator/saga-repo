## Area 16 — Cross-Cultural Encounter Law

### 16.1 — Encounter Outcomes

When two cultures make sustained contact, the encounter produces one of seven canonical outcomes:

| Outcome | Designation | Conditions | Cultural Result |
|---------|------------|------------|-----------------|
| Assimilation | CE-1 | Extreme power differential (3+ CCT gap); no resistance capacity | Dominated culture absorbed; remnant practices survive as folk memory |
| Coerced Syncretism | CE-2 | High power differential (2 CCT gap); partial resistance capacity | Dominated culture adopts dominant forms; retains internal private practices |
| Voluntary Syncretism | CE-3 | Moderate power differential (1 CCT gap); willing exchange | Both cultures adopt selected elements; hybrid form emerges |
| Parallel Coexistence | CE-4 | Roughly equal power; limited interdependence | Cultures maintain independence; mutual respect conventions established |
| Mutual Enrichment | CE-5 | Roughly equal power; high interdependence | Both cultures enriched by sustained exchange; robust hybrid forms |
| Cultural Dominance without Assimilation | CE-6 | High power differential; dominated culture has strong identity capital | Dominated culture maintains identity but adopts surface conventions of dominant culture |
| Cultural Resistance | CE-7 | Any power differential; dominated culture has revolutionary core | Dominated culture actively resists cultural imposition; underground culture forms |

### 16.2 — Cultural Contact Governance Rules

**Rule 16.2.1 — The Minimum Contact Threshold.**
A single encounter between two cultural representatives does not produce encounter outcomes. Sustained cross-cultural encounter requires at minimum: a shared physical space, ongoing resource exchange, and at least one generation of co-habitation or regular contact.

**Rule 16.2.2 — Hybrid Forms Are Irreversible.**
Once a genuine hybrid cultural form has emerged from sustained contact, it cannot be "undone" by separation of the source cultures. The hybrid exists as an independent cultural entity. Attempts to suppress a hybrid culture are treated as cultural destruction events (Part 27 consequences apply).

**Rule 16.2.3 — Cultural Resistance is Permanent.**
A dominated culture that enters CE-7 Cultural Resistance mode will maintain that resistance posture across multiple generations regardless of the practical futility of resistance. Cultural resistance becomes the primary identity mechanism, and the identity investment in resistance may exceed the identity investment in the original culture's content.
