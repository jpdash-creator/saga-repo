## Area 19 — Relic and Artifact Culture

### 19.1 — Relic as Cultural Anchor

Relics (Part 12) are not merely powerful objects — they are cultural anchors. A Named Relic carries the cultural identity of its originating tradition, its lineage of owners, and the significant events in which it participated. The cultural weight of a relic is distinct from and may exceed its combat or strategic value.

**Cultural Relic Tiers:**

| Cultural Significance | Relic Grade | Typical Cultural Status | Cultural Obligation of Owner |
|-----------------------|-------------|------------------------|------------------------------|
| Cultural Heirloom | Tier 1–2 | Family or local community identity marker | Maintenance; display at community ceremonies |
| Tradition Anchor | Tier 2–3 | Sect or faction cultural identity | Protection; ceremonial deployment; lineage transmission |
| Civilizational Icon | Tier 3–4 | Civilizational cultural identity | Pilgrimage destination; national ceremony; restricted access |
| Mythological Object | Tier 4–5 | Multi-civilizational cultural significance | Cross-civilizational governance obligation; Part 28 implications |

### 19.2 — Gifting Convention for Relics

Relic gifting is one of the highest cultural acts in cultivation civilizations. Governing conventions:

**Rule 19.2.1 — Relic Gifting Encodes Relationship Depth.** The tier of relic offered as a gift encodes the giver's assessment of relationship value and their own cultural investment in the relationship. A Tier 3 relic offered to a faction ally is a statement of civilizational-level trust.

**Rule 19.2.2 — Relic Gifting Requires Acceptance Ceremony.** A relic gift that is not formally accepted in ceremony is not socially binding. Informal acceptance of a relic gift creates ambiguous social obligation.

**Rule 19.2.3 — Relic Inheritance Supersedes Combat Capture.** In CPT-1 cultures, a relic that belongs to a lineage cannot be fully claimed by combat victory — the victor may hold the relic, but the original lineage retains a cultural claim to it that may be pressed for generations.
