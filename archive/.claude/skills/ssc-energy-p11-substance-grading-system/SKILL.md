## Area 4 — Substance Grading System

### 4.1 — The Grade Principle

Each substance state contains an internal grade system. Grades reflect the energy density, formation pressure, formation duration, and structural coherence of a specific specimen. Grade is intrinsic — determined at formation and unchanged through normal handling, storage, or trade. Grade can be altered only through:
- Extended proximity to higher-grade substances (passive upgrade over months to years)
- Deliberate refinement arts (accelerated, requires practitioner skill)
- Proximity to Divine Liquid (accelerated environmental upgrade for all lower-state substances)
- Energy depletion or structural damage (downgrade — see below)

**Downgrading:** A substance's grade reduces through energy depletion (over-use that exhausts its content) or structural damage (incorrect handling, exposure to corrosive energy environments). A fully depleted Crystal or Gem becomes an inert shell at Grade 0 — physically intact but with no cultivation utility.

### 4.2 — Grade Assessment

Cultivators assess substance grade through direct qi examination — projecting cultivation sense into the substance and reading the resonance feedback. Assessment accuracy requires:

**Rule 4.2.1 — Realm Minimum:** A cultivator must be at a minimum of the grade-corresponding cultivation tier to accurately assess within one grade margin. A Mortal T2 cultivator examining a Core substance knows only that it is "extremely powerful" — they cannot assess its specific grade.

**Rule 4.2.2 — One-Grade Margin:** Even skilled assessors have a one-grade margin of error without specialized assessment tools. Precision assessment below the one-grade margin requires advanced soul cultivation sense (Part 4) or assessment relics/formations (Part 12).

### 4.3 — Master Grade Scale

> **GRADE SYSTEM DISAMBIGUATION — CPM Grades vs. Heavenly Scale Grades:**
> The Starward Saga uses two distinct "Grade" systems that share similar terminology but measure completely different things:
> - **CPM Grade 1–20 (Part 11, this table):** The quality grade of a Condensed Power Material substance — how dense, coherent, and potent the energy content is. This is a substance property, not a cultivator property.
> - **Heavenly Scale Grade 1–17 (Part 3, Area 3.3):** The power density of a cultivator — how much force they can express. This is a cultivator property, not a substance property.
>
> These scales are correlated but NOT equivalent and do NOT have the same number of steps (CPM has 20 grades; Heavenly Scale has 17). The correlation is: a cultivator needs to be at or near the Heavenly Scale Grade corresponding to a CPM substance's Grade to safely use it. But "CPM Grade 10 Origin Gem" and "Heavenly Scale Grade 10" are different quantities measured on different scales. CPM Grade 10 corresponds to Core-and-above Heavenly Scale Grades (see the Cultivation Tier column in the table below). Writers must not assume that "Grade 10" in a CPM context means the same thing as "Grade 10" in a cultivator-power context.
>
> Part 12's relic Classification Tiers (Tier 1 Mortal Grade through Tier 4 Divine Grade) use the Part 11 CPM 20-Grade system as their material standard, not the Part 3 Heavenly Scale.
