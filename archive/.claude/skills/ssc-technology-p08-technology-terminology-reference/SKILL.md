## Area 26 — Technology Terminology Reference

### 26.1 — Standard Terminology

**Technology Tier:** The classification level (1–20) assigned to a civilization based on the majority-domain capability assessment. "Tier" specifically refers to the civilization-level classification.

**Technology Level:** The classification level (1–20) within a specific technology domain. Levels are domain-specific; tiers are civilization-level summaries. A civilization's Energy Technology is at "Level 8"; the civilization's overall technology classification is at "Tier 8" (if Energy is among the majority domains at that level).

**Technology Ceiling:** The maximum technology tier a civilization can sustainably build, maintain, and operate. Determined by the civilization's Civ tier (Area 5.1) through Rule 5.1.1.

**Civilization Tier (Civ-N):** The governance classification of a civilization on a 1–20 scale (Area 5.1), used to determine sustainable technology tier range.

**Majority-Domain Tier:** The technology level achieved in at least 7 of 14 domains. This is the civilization's overall technology tier.

**Peak Domain Tier:** The single highest technology level any one of the civilization's 14 domains has achieved.

**Technology Gap:** A domain where a civilization's level is more than 3 tiers below its majority-domain tier, representing a significant deficit.

**Soft Correspondence Tendency Table:** The reference table in Area 4.2 mapping cultivation realms to typical technology tier ranges. A tendency reference only — not a governance rule.

**CPM:** Condensed Power Material. The primary cultivation-side energy substrate accessible to technology (via Cultivation Integration Technology, Type 12). Governed as a substance by Part 11; governed as a technology energy source by Part 8, Area 7.

**Integration Model A/B/C/D:** The four levels of cultivation-technology integration, from fully parallel (A) to fully unified (D). Part 7 governs cultivation-side thresholds; Part 8, Area 6 governs technology-side thresholds. Both must be satisfied for a given model.

**Integrated Practitioner:** An individual who operates at Model C or D cultivation-technology integration, combining cultivation capability with advanced Cultivation Integration Technology operation.

**Technology-Relic Hybrid Classes (1/2/3):** Three classes of objects combining technology and relic components. Governed jointly by Part 7 (fusion process), Part 8 (technology tier prerequisites), and Part 12 (relic object layer).

**Energy Domain:** Type 7 in the 14-domain classification system; the foundational enabling domain for all other technology domains; the primary measure of civilization tier in the Kardashev-derived scale.

**Intermediate Tiers:** The 8 new tiers added to the 20-Tier Scale that did not exist in the prior 12-Tier Scale: Tiers 6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15, and 17.

**Anchor Tiers:** The 12 tiers that existed in the prior 12-Tier Scale, now mapped to new positions in the 20-Tier Scale. Old Tier 6→New Tier 8; Old Tier 7→New Tier 11; Old Tier 8→New Tier 13; Old Tier 9→New Tier 16; Old Tier 10→New Tier 18; Old Tier 11→New Tier 19; Old Tier 12→New Tier 20.

### 26.2 — Terminology Anti-Patterns (Concepts to Avoid)

**"Technology Phase Ceiling":** Incorrect. Technology is not bounded by narrative phases. The Technology Ceiling (P8-2) is a civilizational infrastructure constraint, not a Phase restriction. Use "Technology Ceiling" only.

**"Cultivation prerequisite for technology":** Incorrect framing. Cultivation is not a prerequisite for technology advancement. Only Cultivation Integration Technology (Type 12) requires cultivation practitioners as a precondition — and this is a domain-internal requirement, not a prerequisite for other domains or for overall technology tier advancement. Use: "CI Technology requires cultivation practitioners."

**"Technology tier is locked to cultivation realm":** Incorrect. Technology tier and cultivation realm are independent scales. The tendency table describes correlation, not causal lock. Use: "Technology tier and cultivation realm tend to correspond in typical civilizations" (not "are locked to").

**"Part 30 governs the technology ceiling":** Incorrect. Part 30 provides story rhythm guidance only. Part 8, P8-2 governs the Technology Ceiling as a world-law. Use: "The Technology Ceiling is governed by Part 8; Part 30 provides story rhythm guidance."

**"Tier X technology requires Realm Y cultivation":** Incorrect. No technology tier requires any cultivation realm. Use: "Tier X civilizations with cultivation practitioners tend to have cultivators of Realm Y" (if the tendency table supports it) or simply don't connect the two if no connection is narratively warranted.
