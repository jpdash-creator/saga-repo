## Area 22 — Environmental Interaction Law

This Area governs how a location's properties interact with the systems that operate within it.

### 22.1 — Ambient Energy and Cultivation

Location ambient energy class (Class 1–7) directly modifies the cultivation environment for practitioners within the location. The detailed interaction rules are governed by Part 3 (Cultivation Power Law, Area 14). Summary:
- Class 1: No environmental cultivation support. Internal resources only.
- Class 2: Basic support. Recovery at standard rate.
- Class 3: Mild enhancement. Faster recovery, marginally enhanced training.
- Class 4: Notable enhancement. Formation Zones support more advanced practice.
- Class 5: Major enhancement. Vortex zone conditions produce rapid recovery and accelerated advancement potential.
- Class 6: Intense enhancement. Natural Divine Liquid possible in pockets. Advanced breakthrough conditions.
- Class 7: Transcendent enhancement. Sacred Ground conditions — reality itself supports cultivation. The highest natural cultivation environment.

### 22.2 — Affinity and Cultivation Path Interaction

A location's affinity profile modifies the experience of cultivators with aligned or opposing affinities:
- Aligned affinity: cultivation of aligned path produces faster progress, richer sensory experience, and more available environmental energy for technique execution.
- Opposing affinity: cultivation of opposing path may be suppressed — slower progress, less environmental support, hostile ambient conditions for sensitive techniques.
- Neutral affinity: no modification in either direction.
- Mixed affinity: complex interactions determined by the specific combination.

### 22.3 — Special Zones and Technology Systems

Special zones interact with technology systems in ways governed by Part 8 and Part 31:
- Formation Zones: passive benefit to CPM-dependent technology (Model B–D per Part 31).
- Sacred Grounds: Sacred Ground Synergy Step-Up (Part 31, Area 10) — CI Technology within appropriate Sacred Ground grade achieves effective integration model one level above normal threshold.
- Void Zones: impairs all Model B–D technology that depends on ambient cultivation energy draw.
- Dimensional Rifts: technology navigation in rift-proximate space requires specialized sensor and propulsion capability (Part 8 Space Technology Type 10, Level 10+).

### 22.4 — Location and Combat

A location's properties modify combat conditions governed by Part 5:
- Sacred Grounds are protected zones — combat within them is governed by Part 5 protected zone rules.
- Void Zones suppress qi-based combat techniques — combatants are limited to internally stored energy.
- Affinity environments amplify aligned techniques and suppress opposing techniques.
- Dimensional Tears and Rifts create spatial instability that affects technique execution requiring spatial stability.
