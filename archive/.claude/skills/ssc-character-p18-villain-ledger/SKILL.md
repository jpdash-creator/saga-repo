## Area 6 — The Villain Ledger

### 6.1 — Villain Ledger Mandate

**Rule 18.6.1.1 — Every Named Villain Requires a Villain Ledger:** Every villain who appears in three or more scenes requires a canonical real-time Villain Ledger — a governance document tracking the villain's complete state at any point in the narrative. The Villain Ledger is the villain counterpart to the Hero Ledger (Part 17 Area 6).

**Rule 18.6.1.2 — The Villain Ledger Is a Canon Document:** The Villain Ledger is subject to Part 1 audit. Narration that contradicts a Villain Ledger entry is a canon violation. The Villain Ledger must be updated whenever a mandatory update trigger event occurs.

**Rule 18.6.1.3 — Mandatory Villain Ledger Update Triggers:** The Villain Ledger must be updated when any of the following events occur:
- Cultivation breakthrough or technology tier advancement
- Significant combat event (victory, defeat, significant injury)
- IE-4+ ideology event (Core Activation or above per Part 20)
- PIS-4+ psychological event (Active Wound category or above per Part 26)
- CSS-5+ consequence event (Major Consequence or above per Part 27)
- Significant subordinate loss, alliance fracture, or faction change
- Any event that permanently alters the villain's capabilities, ideology, or relationships

### 6.2 — The Ten Villain Ledger Sections

The Villain Ledger comprises ten canonical sections mirroring the Hero Ledger structure (Part 17 Area 6.2) but adapted for villain governance:

**Section 1 — Villain Identity Baseline:**
- Full name and aliases
- Arc type classification (Arc Villain, Series Villain, Mirror Villain, Tragic Villain, Institutional Villain, Ascending Villain)
- Current canonical status (Active Threat / Contained / Defeated / Converted / Deceased)
- Home civilization and location
- Arc phase (1–6, see Area 5.2)

**Section 2 — Cultivation and Technology Classification:**
- Current cultivation realm and CC-Class (Part 3, Part 16)
- Affinity type and development level (Part 4)
- Soul cultivation rung (Part 4)
- Heart level if applicable (Part 4)
- Technology tier if applicable (Part 8, Part 16)
- Integration model type if applicable (Part 31)
- Military rank and institutional position (Part 16)
- Weapon user class WU-class (Part 33)

**Section 3 — Power and Capability Snapshot:**
- Signature Moves and Fusion Moves (Part 6) — the villain's canonical combat technique set
- Key tactical capabilities and strategic tools
- Faction and institutional power dimension summary
- Information and intelligence apparatus summary
- Cultivation injuries, power limitations, or known vulnerabilities

**Section 4 — Villain Ideology Profile:**
- Primary Ideology Category and sub-variant (Part 20)
- Secondary and tertiary ideology categories if compound
- Moral Orientation (Part 20 Area 3)
- Ideology Polarity (Part 20 Area 4) — Polarity-1 Fluid through Polarity-5 Absolutist
- Current Ideology Stability State (IS-1 through IS-5) (Part 20)
- Core/Operational/Surface ideology distinctions
- Active ideological contradictions and blindspots
- Ideology Ledger event history (significant IE events)

**Section 5 — Villain Psychological Profile:**
- Psychological archetype (The Armored / The Consumed / The Calcified / The Fractured / The Hollow)
- Current PIS state (PIS-1 through PIS-10) (Part 26)
- Active psychological wounds — category and weight
- Primary coping mechanisms (adaptive or maladaptive) (Part 26)
- Pre-combat psychological state modifier (Part 26 Area 10.2)
- Psychological history — major PIS events that shaped the villain
- Psychological trajectory — worsening, stable, or inflection point

**Section 6 — Villain Consequence Arc:**
- Active consequences — category, severity (CSS-1 through CSS-10), immediacy class (IC-1 through IC-6)
- Consequence history — completed, active, and outstanding
- Consequence self-awareness level — does the villain know their consequence arc is accumulating?
- Outstanding consequence debts with specific named parties
- Consequence vulnerability points — which consequence exposures most threaten the villain's agenda

**Section 7 — Relationships, Subordinates, and Faction Web:**
- Current subordinate structure — named lieutenants, their loyalty levels, their cultivation/technology tier
- Faction web — which factions support, serve, fear, or oppose the villain
- Affiliation map (Part 23 AFF scale) for primary factional relationships
- Betrayal history — which relationships have been destroyed and how
- Rival and enemy relationships with named characters
- Hero-villain relationship status — which phase of antagonism

**Section 8 — Resources and Strategic Assets:**
- Key relics and artifacts (Part 12)
- CPM holdings and energy resource profile (Part 11)
- Territory control and spatial resources (Part 13, Part 14)
- Technology assets (weapons, vehicles, infrastructure) (Parts 8, 32, 33)
- Intelligence assets (Part 24)
- Unique strategic assets not covered above

**Section 9 — Villain Arc Status:**
- Current arc phase (1–6)
- Agenda objectives — what the villain is currently pursuing
- Active plans and schemes in motion
- Known vulnerabilities the hero could exploit
- Arc trajectory — is the villain advancing toward or retreating from their goals?
- Narrative role at current arc phase

**Section 10 — Canon Events Log:**
- All major events in the villain's history — IE events, CSS events, PIS events, major defeats or victories — recorded with arc phase and narrative context
- Backstory events that predate the story's beginning but shape the villain's present state
- Hero-villain encounter history
