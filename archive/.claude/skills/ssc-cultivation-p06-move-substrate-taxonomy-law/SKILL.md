## Area 12 — Move Substrate Taxonomy Law

### 12.1 — Purpose and Domain of the Substrate Taxonomy

Areas 1 through 11 of this Part classify Named Moves along three primary axes: **Type** (complexity, Areas 2 and 4), **Function** (combat role, Area 3), and **Dimensional Influence** (cultivation-dimension shaping, Area 1). What those eleven Areas do not formally define is the **substrate** from which a Named Move draws its operative principle — the underlying carrier (energy architecture, anatomical architecture, bloodline resonance, relic channel, weapon channel, technology channel, formation mediation, or hybrid composite) through which the technique expresses. Substrate has been mentioned in context — Area 6.3 requires an "energy substrate" field at registration; Area 7.2 describes bloodline resonance as a distinct technique property; Area 1.6 distinguishes soul force from qi as differing substrates — but no formal taxonomy has ever catalogued the full substrate space. Area 12 closes that gap.

This Area is additive. It does not replace, override, or renumber Areas 1 through 11. It does not introduce new Move Types and does not alter the Access Table (Area 4). What it does is classify **what every Named Move rests on** — the operative substance the technique is made of — and defines the governance rules that follow from substrate category, including the critical category of Species-Innate moves (moves derived from species-anatomy rather than from channeled cultivation energy), which back-certifies all previously registered species-innate Named Moves (the canonical reference case is the Minotaur-species moves **Chaprachup** and **Chalakudhaal** registered in the Bison ledger).

**Principle P6-6 (Substrate Classification Mandate):** Every Named Move has exactly one **primary substrate** drawn from the taxonomy in Area 12.2 and may have up to two **secondary substrates** where hybrid composition is established by Area 12.8. Primary substrate is an immutable property of the technique — a move whose primary substrate changes is a new technique requiring new registration under Area 6.

**Principle P6-7 (Substrate Governs Gating, Not Naming):** Substrate category governs *how a move's access, output, and suppression rules apply* (Realm gating, Firmament Lock interaction, cost scaling, collision rules). It does not govern *what a move may be named* — naming conventions (Area 6.2) remain substrate-agnostic, meaning a Species-Innate move may use any of the five naming patterns established in Area 6.2, including species-cultural proper names.

**Principle P6-8 (Ledger Substrate Field Mandatory):** Every Named Move recorded in Character Ledger Section 3 (Power and Capability Snapshot) must declare its primary substrate. This is a Part 1 Area 5.4 ledger schema obligation (v2.5+) and a Part 6 Area 6.3 registration-field obligation — both are simultaneously satisfied by a single Substrate Category entry in the move's ledger block.

### 12.2 — The Eight Primary Substrate Categories

The complete substrate space is partitioned into eight mutually exclusive primary categories. Every Named Move resolves to exactly one primary category.

**12.2.1 — Cultivation-Energy Substrate (Substrate Category S-1):**
The default substrate of the cultivation cosmos. The move's operative principle is **channeled cultivation energy** — qi, affinity-resonant force, soul force, heart-state projection, or the four combined. Almost every Named Move described in Areas 1–11 assumes this substrate: the Access Table (Area 4) is expressed in cultivation-realm terms because cultivation-energy substrate moves scale with realm; the energy-cost table (Area 5) is expressed in cultivation-reserve terms; the 3-Level Rule (Area 8 via Part 3 Area 7.2) is a cultivation-energy principle. **Firmament-Lock interaction:** fully suppressed by Firmament Lock and by all cultivation-energy suppression mechanisms (Part 3 Area 19, Part 4 Area 5 soul suppression, sealed domains per Part 14). **Realm gating:** full Access Table applies. **Naming:** any of the five conventions; most commonly Principle + Action or Natural Force + Form.

**12.2.2 — Bloodline-Resonance Substrate (Substrate Category S-2):**
The move's operative principle is **cultivation energy amplified or modulated by heritable genetic resonance** (Area 7.2). The substrate is still cultivation energy, but the energy's pattern is bloodline-specific — it carries signatures that non-descendants cannot replicate at the same efficiency. A bloodline-resonance move used by a descendant has reduced cost and amplified output; the same move mechanically executed by a non-descendant performs at 40–70% depth even with identical technique understanding, because the bloodline signature is part of the principle. **Firmament-Lock interaction:** suppressed as cultivation-energy is suppressed (S-2 rides S-1's suppression profile); bloodline resonance does not grant immunity. **Realm gating:** Access Table applies. **Naming:** frequently Creator Reference or Clan-Ancestor naming (Area 6.2 patterns 3 and 5). **Inheritance:** Part 6 Area 7.2 governs transmission restrictions — bloodline moves are the most tightly held lineage techniques in cultivation society.

**12.2.3 — Species-Innate Substrate (Substrate Category S-3):**
The move's operative principle is **species-specific anatomical architecture rather than channeled cultivation energy**. A Minotaur's horn-force attack (**Chalakudhaal**), a Naga's coil-crush (hypothetical example), an Avian-sentient's wing-beat concussion — all are outputs the species produces *by existing as that species*, not by channeling qi. Species-Innate moves are the anatomical expression of a sentient being's biology as a combat vector.

*This substrate is governed in extreme breadth in Area 12.3.*

**12.2.4 — Relic-Channeled Substrate (Substrate Category S-4):**
The move's operative principle is **a cultivation relic or artifact acting as the energy channel or amplifier** (Part 12 domain). The move's identity binds to the relic — remove the relic, and the move cannot be executed, because the relic is not merely a tool but the substrate through which the technique expresses. This is distinct from a cultivator using a relic to amplify an otherwise-cultivation-energy move (that remains S-1 with relic amplification). Relic-Channeled moves are classified under Part 6 taxonomy even though the Part 12 object is load-bearing — Part 6 governs the move identity; Part 12 governs the relic itself. **Firmament-Lock interaction:** partial — the relic's channel may persist through some cultivation suppression, depending on relic Tier (Part 12 Area 8); a Tier 5+ relic can often project through a Firmament-class suppression that would nullify an S-1 equivalent. **Realm gating:** the relic's Tier provides the effective ceiling regardless of wielder's realm, but only within the relic's authorized wielder profile (Part 12 Area 11). **Naming:** frequently incorporates the relic's name.

**12.2.5 — Weapon-Bonded Substrate (Substrate Category S-5):**
The move's operative principle is **a weapon acting as structural component of the technique** (Part 33 domain). Distinct from S-4: a weapon is not a relic — it is a non-cultivation artifact whose properties (edge, mass, WS-class discharge capability, signature-weapon bond per Part 17/Part 33 cross-reference) are the vehicle of the move. Pure weapon strikes are not Named Moves; Weapon-Bonded Named Moves occur when a cultivator's codified technique incorporates the weapon's structural properties as an inseparable element (a signature sword form whose footwork and cut-pattern are inseparable from the blade's geometry; a Named Move that only exists because the specific WS-VII discharge delivery completes its principle). **Firmament-Lock interaction:** the cultivation-energy layer is suppressed; the weapon-substrate layer persists (a sword remains a sword). **Realm gating:** the cultivation-energy component is realm-gated; the weapon-mechanical component is not. **Naming:** often references the weapon.

**12.2.6 — Technology-Aided Substrate (Substrate Category S-6):**
The move's operative principle is **a cultivator-executed technique channeled through a Tier-rated technology system** (Part 10 domain, Cultivation Integration Technology via Part 31). A Named Move delivered through a CI Tier 5 amplifier that could not exist without the technology layer is S-6. Pure technology weapon fire is not a Named Move — S-6 requires a cultivator-produced technique component. **Firmament-Lock interaction:** the cultivation-energy layer is suppressed; technology layer may or may not persist depending on integration depth (Part 31). **Realm gating:** Integration Resonance rolls operate in parallel with Part 6 base success rate; both must succeed for the S-6 output to manifest (Part 31 governance). **Naming:** any pattern; often references the technology designation or platform.

**12.2.7 — Formation-Mediated Substrate (Substrate Category S-7):**
The move's operative principle is **a Formation Array acting as the structural mediator of a Named Move** (Part 11 domain). Formation Arrays are architecture, not technique (P12-4); but a Named Move that requires a specific formation to express — where the formation is the scaffolding and the technique is the surge through it — is S-7. The formation is not a channel like a relic; it is a pre-structured domain that the move fills. **Firmament-Lock interaction:** varies by formation Tier; pre-built formations maintain structural integrity under cultivation suppression that would collapse cultivation-channeled equivalents. **Realm gating:** the formation's Tier provides the ceiling; the cultivator's realm provides the minimum keystone. **Naming:** often references the formation.

**12.2.8 — Hybrid-Composite Substrate (Substrate Category S-8):**
The move's operative principle is **two or more of the above substrates in inseparable combination**, where removing any substrate layer collapses the technique. S-8 is the substrate of Fusion Moves (Area 10) where the fusion is between substrate categories (not merely between affinity streams within S-1). A technique that fuses bloodline resonance with a species-innate anatomical component is S-2/S-3 composite; a technique that couples a relic channel with a technology amplifier is S-4/S-6 composite. The most common S-8 configurations are: S-1/S-2 (cultivation + bloodline), S-1/S-3 (cultivation + species-innate — the canonical form for hybrid sentient cultivators per Part 16 Area 17), S-1/S-4 (cultivation + relic), S-1/S-5 (cultivation + weapon), S-1/S-6 (cultivation + technology), S-1/S-7 (cultivation + formation), and rarer three-way composites. **Firmament-Lock interaction:** composite — each substrate layer's suppression profile applies to its component; a hybrid-composite move whose anchor is S-3 persists in reduced form when S-1 is suppressed, which is the structural reason hybrid sentients retain a minimum combat floor under cultivation-suppression environments. **Realm gating:** the most restrictive substrate's ceiling applies to the composite unless Part 28 Creative Flexibility authorization elevates the ceiling. **Naming:** composite often uses Principle + Action to reference the dominant substrate.

### 12.3 — Species-Innate Substrate — Extreme-Depth Governance

The Species-Innate Substrate (S-3) is the most consequential addition in Area 12 because it formalizes a category of Named Moves that the cultivation-centric Areas 1–11 structurally could not accommodate. This subsection establishes the complete governance envelope for S-3 moves in six sub-rules.

**12.3.1 — The Anatomical-Derivation Test.**
A Named Move qualifies as S-3 if and only if all four of the following conditions are met simultaneously:

*(a) The move's operative force vector is produced by species-specific anatomy* — a body part, physiological capability, or biological mechanism that is characteristic of the wielder's species and absent in species that lack the corresponding anatomy. A Minotaur's horn-force attack meets this test (horns are Minotaur anatomy); a human cultivator's bare-handed palm strike does not (palm strikes are available to all humanoid sentients and are governed by Mortal Tier 1 physical-architecture moves in Area 1.1).

*(b) The move is executable without conscious cultivation-energy channeling* — the wielder does not need to circulate qi, attune an affinity, mobilize soul force, or otherwise deploy cultivation reserves to produce the technique. The move is a refined biomechanical pattern plus species-anatomy, not a refined biomechanical pattern plus energy.

*(c) The move is not learnable by members of species that lack the underlying anatomy* — a non-Minotaur cannot learn **Chalakudhaal** by any Direct Transmission, Manual Study, Combat Observation, Experiential Discovery, or Void Root Absorption pathway (Area 7.1), because the pathway's endpoint requires anatomy the learner does not possess. Species-Innate moves are therefore exempt from Area 7's transmission taxonomy — they are inherited with the species, not transmitted through it.

*(d) The move expresses a culturally recognized codified pattern within the wielder's species* — idiosyncratic use of an anatomical feature (a random tail-slap) is not an S-3 Named Move; only codified species-cultural techniques with established naming, training traditions, and recognized execution forms qualify. This prevents S-3 from being a wildcard for any anatomical action and grounds the category in species-cultural canon (Part 16 Area 18 Bipedal Sentient Species Governance and Part 25 culture interaction).

Moves failing any of (a)–(d) are not S-3. A move that meets (a) and (b) but fails (c) is typically S-1 with anatomical flavor (governed by Area 1 as a Mortal-tier physical-architecture move or higher-realm qi-physical variant). A move that meets (a)–(c) but fails (d) is raw biological capability, governed as environmental/biological description, not as a Named Move (no Part 6 registration required).

**12.3.2 — Naming Rules for S-3 Moves.**
All five naming conventions of Area 6.2 are permitted. A sixth convention is added exclusive to S-3: **Species-Cultural Proper Name**. This pattern uses a word, phrase, or compound from the wielder species' own language or cultural lexicon that lacks direct translation into the standard Starward Saga common tongues. **Chaprachup** and **Chalakudhaal** are the canonical exemplars — both are Minotaur-language terms whose semantic content references the anatomical function (tail-snap / horn-force) in Minotaur cultural idiom. The Species-Cultural Proper Name convention is valid only for S-3 moves (and for S-8 composites with S-3 as a load-bearing substrate). Every Species-Cultural Proper Name registered under Part 6 Area 6.3 must be accompanied by a brief gloss in the ledger entry explaining the species-cultural referent — this preserves prose clarity without requiring in-prose translation (the bold-formatting mandate, P6-2, applies unchanged).

**12.3.3 — Realm Access and the Anatomical-Maturity Scaling Rule.**
S-3 moves are **not gated by the Move Access Table (Area 4)**. Area 4's table expresses what cultivation realms permit what Move Types; S-3 moves are not bounded by cultivation realm because they are not cultivation-energy techniques. What governs S-3 output scaling is **anatomical maturity** — a species-specific developmental curve established in Part 16 Area 18.5 (Species Cultivation and Anatomical Profile) and operationalized here through the following scaling principle:

*S-3 moves scale with the wielder's anatomical development (species-age, physical conditioning, musculoskeletal integration, injury state), not with the wielder's cultivation realm.* A Mortal-tier Minotaur and a Champion-tier Minotaur execute **Chalakudhaal** with output differences driven by horn mass, horn density, neck musculature, and footwork — not by cultivation rung. This does not mean S-3 output is flat with realm; it means realm affects S-3 output only through the incidental channel that higher cultivation tends to correlate with better body conditioning (an indirect coupling, not a gating rule). A cultivator who advances from Core Realm to Divine Sea Realm sees no automatic S-3 uplift without corresponding anatomical improvement.

*Complexity Types still apply.* An S-3 move is still Type-classified per Area 2 (Basic through Mythic). **Chaprachup** is Basic-Type S-3; an Awakened-Beast-Sovereign's planet-shaking species-innate roar would be Catastrophic-Type S-3. Type governs the execution-complexity risk envelope (success rates, failure modes per Area 2.2); substrate governs what the move is made of. Both apply simultaneously.

*Cross-reference:* Part 16 Area 18.5 defines the Species Anatomical Development Scale (SADS) for each catalogued bipedal sentient species; S-3 output scaling uses SADS as its primary axis.

**12.3.4 — Firmament-Lock and Cultivation-Suppression Immunity (The S-3 Exemption Rule).**
This is the sub-rule with the largest operational implication. **S-3 moves are categorically immune to Firmament Lock and to all cultivation-energy suppression mechanisms that operate on S-1 substrate.** The reasoning is structural: Firmament Lock (Part 3 cultivation cap; see Phase 1 Narrative Ceiling), soul-sense sealing (Part 4 Area 5), meridian-seal arrays (Part 11 formations), and cultivation-domain sealing (Part 14 Area 5) all operate on cultivation-energy channels. They cannot suppress anatomy. A Minotaur's horns exist whether the cultivator is under Firmament Lock or not; the charge that drives the horn-force is biomechanical, not qi-channeled; therefore **Chalakudhaal** remains executable.

*Scope of the exemption:*
- (a) **Full immunity** to cultivation-energy suppression (Firmament Lock, meridian seals, qi-suppression talismans, soul-sense sealing, affinity-dampening formations).
- (b) **Partial immunity** to environmental conditions that suppress both cultivation and biology — e.g., extreme cold immobilizes muscles regardless of substrate; toxin that paralyzes the wielder prevents all action regardless of S-3 status.
- (c) **No immunity** to anatomical damage — horn amputation disables **Chalakudhaal**; a severed tail disables **Chaprachup**. This is not suppression; it is literal destruction of the substrate. S-3 moves are more sensitive to anatomical damage than S-1 moves are to meridian damage, because the anatomy cannot regenerate on the cultivation-healing curve (Part 3 Area 11) — it heals on the species' biological curve per Part 16 Area 18.5.
- (d) **No immunity** to species-specific countermeasures — formations, substances, or techniques designed to specifically neutralize a species' anatomical capability (e.g., a Minotaur-specific horn-binding formation) can suppress S-3 moves that depend on that anatomy. These are classified as S-3-targeted suppression, a Part 11 / Part 12 design space.

*Narrative implication (captured in Area 12.3.6 narration rules):* the S-3 exemption is the structural reason hybrid sentient cultivators and bipedal-species cultivators remain threat-relevant under Firmament Lock and equivalent cultivation-suppression environments — a pure-human cultivator locked at Core Assimilation L3 Mid is cultivation-capped; a Minotaur locked at the same realm retains unrestricted S-3 access. This is a canon-level asymmetry that must be rendered truthfully in prose when Firmament Lock is a scene-active constraint.

**12.3.5 — Mandatory Ledger Registration Fields.**
Every S-3 Named Move must be recorded in Character Ledger Section 3 under the "Innate Moves (Part 6 Area 12)" subsection (see Part 1 Area 5.4 v2.5) with the following required fields:

- **Move Name** — bold in prose per P6-2
- **Substrate Category** — one of S-3 (pure species-innate) or S-8/S-3-composite (hybrid with S-3 as a load-bearing layer)
- **Anatomical Derivation** — specific species-anatomical feature producing the force vector (e.g., "Minotaur horns; forward-charging impact-delivery")
- **Firmament-Lock Interaction** — standard entry: "Immune per Part 6 Area 12.3.4(a). No suppression by Firmament Lock, meridian seals, qi-dampening arrays, or soul-sense sealing." — with any species-specific countermeasure notes per 12.3.4(d)
- **Power-Level Availability Profile** — SADS position plus realm-independent availability statement: "Functional at all realms; scaling driven by anatomical maturity per SADS"
- **Species-Cultural Naming Gloss** — required if the name uses Species-Cultural Proper Name convention (Area 12.3.2); a one-sentence gloss explaining the species-cultural referent
- **Move Type (Area 2)** — Basic through Mythic as normal
- **Move Function (Area 3)** — Attack / Defense / Mobility / etc., as normal
- **Mastery Grade (Area 2.1)** — Novice through Master as normal (S-3 moves undergo the same mastery progression; a juvenile Minotaur's **Chalakudhaal** at Novice differs mechanically from an adult warrior's Master execution even at identical anatomical maturity)

**12.3.6 — Collision and Interaction with S-1 Cultivation-Energy Moves.**
When an S-3 move collides with an S-1 cultivation-energy move of the same Combat Function (two Attack-function moves meeting in direct opposition), the Area 8 collision-outcome system applies with the following substrate-interaction modifiers:

- The 3-Level Rule (Area 8, via Part 3 Area 7.2) references cultivation realm differentials; for S-3 ↔ S-1 collisions, substitute the **Effective Force Rating** — a single scalar combining SADS maturity (for the S-3 side) and realm-plus-power-density (for the S-1 side), computed per the Part 3 Area 21 capability envelope cross-reference. This produces a substrate-neutral comparison.
- **Resonance collisions** (Area 8.2 outcome type) are rare at the S-3/S-1 boundary because resonance requires substrate compatibility; typical S-3/S-1 collisions resolve as Nullification, Cancellation, Partial Interaction, or Tangential, with Resonance reserved for specific hybrid/bloodline interactions.
- **Under Firmament-Lock or equivalent suppression**, the S-1 side's Effective Force Rating is computed at the suppressed ceiling; the S-3 side's EFR is unchanged. This asymmetry is the mechanical expression of the structural narrative point in 12.3.4.
- **Environmental aftermath** of S-3 moves is anatomically grounded: a Catastrophic-Type S-3 horn-force attack leaves impact craters, splintered architecture, and biomechanical residue — not qi tremors, elemental discharge, or affinity environmental response.

Narration Standards N6-12 through N6-14 (below in Area 12.9) formalize the prose rendering of S-3 executions.

### 12.4 — Bloodline-Resonance Substrate — Formalization

Section 7.2 introduced bloodline techniques at the level of transmission (who may learn them). Area 12.4 formalizes the substrate-level governance:

**12.4.1 — Bloodline Signature Field.** Every S-2 Named Move must declare a Bloodline Signature — the specific heritable lineage (clan, family, awakened bloodline, or species-subtype) whose genetic-energy resonance is required for the move's full expression. This is a required ledger field under Part 1 Area 5.4 Section 3 "Signature Named Moves (Part 6)" subsection.

**12.4.2 — Descendant-Distance Scaling.** S-2 output scales by genetic proximity to the bloodline founder or canonical bloodline anchor: Direct Descendant (100%), First-Collateral Descendant (90%), Distant Collateral (70%), Adopted-into-Bloodline (ritual descendant, 50%), Non-Descendant-With-Technique-Knowledge (40–60% depth even with perfect mechanical replication). The specific numeric profile is per-bloodline-canonical and must be entered in the bloodline ledger (ledger-character-hero/ledger-character-villain/ledger-character-side Section 3).

**12.4.3 — S-2 Transmission Restrictions.** Bloodline moves are the most tightly held lineage techniques in cultivation society (Area 7.2). Formal transmission occurs only through bloodline rites recognized by the lineage authority (Part 22 faction governance / Part 25 cultural governance). Unauthorized transmission to non-descendants is a Part 24 information-security violation and a Part 25 cultural violation simultaneously.

**12.4.4 — S-2 Firmament-Lock Profile.** S-2 rides S-1's suppression profile; bloodline resonance does not grant suppression immunity. A bloodline cultivator under Firmament Lock loses S-2 access as they lose S-1 access. This is the critical contrast with S-3 (12.3.4) and must not be conflated — bloodline is heritable cultivation-energy modulation; species-innate is heritable anatomical capability.

### 12.5 — Hybrid-Composite Substrate — Extreme-Depth Governance

S-8 (Hybrid-Composite) is the substrate of techniques that cannot be reduced to any single primary category. This subsection governs the most common composites in the canon, with particular attention to the S-1/S-3 composite — the canonical form for hybrid sentient cultivators (Part 16 Area 17 Hybrid Sentient Character Governance).

**12.5.1 — Composite Registration.** An S-8 move's ledger entry must list every constituent substrate in descending load-bearing order (the first-listed substrate is the primary load-bearing layer; remaining substrates are contributing layers). Removing any load-bearing layer collapses the technique; removing a contributing layer degrades but does not eliminate expression. Registration under Part 6 Area 6.3 requires all constituent layers be enumerated.

**12.5.2 — The S-1/S-3 Composite (Hybrid Sentient Cultivator Composite).** The most common and narratively important composite. A hybrid sentient cultivator (Part 16 Area 17 definition) who possesses both cultivation-energy capability (S-1) and species-innate anatomy (S-3) may develop composite moves where cultivation energy channels *through* the species-innate anatomical feature. A Minotaur cultivator who channels qi through his horn during **Chalakudhaal** to amplify the impact with qi-pressure is executing an S-1/S-3 composite variant, not pure **Chalakudhaal** — composite execution is a separately registered Named Move per 12.5.1.

*Firmament-Lock behavior of S-1/S-3 composites:* under cultivation suppression, the S-1 layer is suppressed; the S-3 layer persists; the composite **degrades to the underlying S-3 base move at full S-3 capacity**. This preserves the hybrid cultivator's minimum combat floor even under Firmament Lock — a canonically important asymmetry that distinguishes hybrid sentient cultivators from pure-human cultivators in suppressed-environment encounters.

**12.5.3 — Other Common Composites.**
- *S-1/S-2 (Cultivation + Bloodline):* the substrate configuration of most high-register clan techniques. Firmament-Lock suppresses both layers together (S-2 rides S-1).
- *S-1/S-4 (Cultivation + Relic):* a technique requiring relic channeling to complete. Partial Firmament-Lock resistance per 12.2.4.
- *S-1/S-5 (Cultivation + Weapon):* a signature weapon-bound technique. The weapon layer persists under Firmament Lock.
- *S-1/S-6 (Cultivation + Technology):* a CI-Technology-integrated technique. Partial persistence depending on integration depth.
- *S-1/S-7 (Cultivation + Formation):* a formation-mediated technique. Formation layer persists per formation Tier.
- *Three-way composites* (S-1/S-3/S-5, etc.) are governed by compound rules; each layer's suppression profile applies to its component.

**12.5.4 — Composite-Specific Type Bump.** An S-8 composite is always at least one Type-tier above the highest-Type constituent — the coordination load of simultaneously managing two substrate layers is itself a complexity increase. A Basic S-1 move fused with a Basic S-3 move produces an Intermediate S-8 composite at minimum. This applies the same structural logic as Fusion Moves (Area 10) to substrate-composite coordination.

### 12.6 — Updates to Area 6.3 Registration Fields

This subsection is a formal amendment to Area 6.3. The mandatory Registration Fields for every Named Move are extended to include:

- **Substrate Category** — one of S-1 through S-8 per Area 12.2.
- **For S-2 moves:** Bloodline Signature field (per 12.4.1).
- **For S-3 moves:** Anatomical Derivation field plus Firmament-Lock Interaction field (per 12.3.5).
- **For S-8 moves:** full Constituent Substrate enumeration in descending load-bearing order (per 12.5.1).

All Named Moves registered prior to Area 12's ratification remain valid; the Substrate Category is back-populated at next ledger update per Part 1 Area 6.3 Post-Write Ledger Update. Pre-ratification moves whose substrate cannot be determined from prior registration default to S-1 (Cultivation-Energy), with correction at next ledger audit.

### 12.7 — S-3 Non-Transmission Principle (Exception to Area 7)

Area 7 defines five learning methods for Named Moves. Area 12.7 formalizes the S-3 exception: **S-3 moves are inherited with the species; they are not transmitted between non-conspecific individuals.** The implications:

- *Direct Transmission (Area 7.1.1)* — an S-3 move cannot be taught to a non-conspecific learner by any transmission method, because the learner lacks the substrate.
- *Manual Study (Area 7.1.2)* — an S-3 move's technique manual can be read and understood by non-conspecifics, but the understanding does not produce executable capability without the anatomy.
- *Combat Observation (Area 7.1.3)* — a non-conspecific observer can learn to *counter* an S-3 move but not to execute it.
- *Experiential Discovery (Area 7.1.4)* — a conspecific youth may experientially discover an S-3 move from species instinct or cultural exposure; this is the primary S-3 acquisition pathway for juvenile conspecifics.
- *Void Root Absorption (Area 7.1.5)* — a Void Root cultivator can absorb the *principle* of an S-3 move but cannot instantiate it without the substrate anatomy; the absorbed principle may inform a different Named Move the Void Root practitioner develops in a substrate they do possess.

*Intra-species transmission* of S-3 moves follows species-cultural protocols established in Part 16 Area 18 (Bipedal Sentient Species Governance) and Part 25 (culture); it is not universally Direct Transmission — some species treat innate moves as secret, others as communal, others as rite-of-maturity instruction.

### 12.8 — Substrate Interaction with Combat Function (Area 3) and Move Type (Area 2)

Substrate is orthogonal to Combat Function and Move Type. All eight Combat Functions (Attack, Defense, Mobility, Control, Trap, Support, Soul-Specific, Combination) can exist in any Substrate Category, with one constraint: **Soul-Specific Function moves are constrained to substrates that include a soul-force layer** — functionally, this means S-1 (when the cultivator has soul cultivation per Part 4 Area 2), S-2 (bloodline-inherited soul techniques), S-4 (soul-active relics), S-7 (soul-formation arrays), and S-8 composites containing a soul layer. S-3, S-5, and S-6 cannot host Soul-Specific moves because the substrates lack a soul-force channel. This is a definitional constraint, not a restriction — and it has a narrative upside: it establishes that species-innate capability does not include soul-attack vectors unless the species also possesses cultivation-soul architecture.

All eight Move Types (Basic through Mythic) are accessible in all substrate categories **subject to each substrate's native ceiling rules** (S-1 is Area 4 Access Table; S-3 is SADS; S-4 is relic Tier; etc.). Catastrophic- and Mythic-Type moves in S-3 require exceptional species anatomy, species-cultural canonical standing, and narrative-event gravitas per Part 27 consequence weight; they are rare but not structurally forbidden.

### 12.9 — S-3 Narration Standards (N6-12 through N6-14)

**N6-12 — Anatomical Grounding in S-3 Prose.**
Every S-3 Named Move rendering must ground the technique's force vector in species-anatomical description. Prose for **Chalakudhaal** must reference the horn-anatomy — curvature, density, the shoulder-into-horn force transfer, the horn's angle-of-impact — because those anatomical features are the technique. A rendering that describes only the impact effect without the anatomical mechanism is in violation of N6-12 and must be revised. This is parallel to the affinity narration requirement (Area 11.7) — substrate-truthful rendering is mandatory.

**N6-13 — Firmament-Lock Scene Truthfulness.**
When a scene is under Firmament Lock or cultivation-suppression per Part 3, prose must render the substrate-differential honestly. S-1 moves are suppressed; S-3 moves are not. A hybrid sentient character executing an S-3 move under Firmament Lock is executing a move the locked environment cannot dampen — the prose must convey this asymmetry without commentary (no "despite the Lock..." narration; the move simply functions), because the character's embodied experience is that their body works even when their cultivation does not. Breaking this rule produces a world-coherence violation that undermines the substrate taxonomy's narrative weight.

**N6-14 — Species-Cultural Name Rendering.**
S-3 moves named under the Species-Cultural Proper Name convention (12.3.2) appear in bold in prose per P6-2. The species-cultural gloss appears in the ledger, not in the prose; the prose treats the name as self-standing (**Chalakudhaal** appears as **Chalakudhaal**, not as "**Chalakudhaal** [horn-force]"). If the narrative POV is a character who does not speak the wielder's species-language, the POV's internal recognition of the move may render non-linguistic (the observer recognizes the stance, the moment of commitment, the horn-angle, without knowing the name) — the name is still rendered in bold when the narrating consciousness acquires it, either through subsequent recognition or through the wielder's own POV.
