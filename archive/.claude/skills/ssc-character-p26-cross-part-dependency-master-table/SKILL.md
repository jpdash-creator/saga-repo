Psychological state — PIS level, active wounds, coping mechanism type, psychological archetype (villains), psychological history — is Living Information (L) tracked in Character Ledger Section 5 (Psychological Profile) across all character ledger types. PIS-4+ events are mandatory Post-Write Ledger Update (Part 1 Area 6.3) triggers for Section 5. The Pre-Write Ledger Check (Part 1 Area 6.2) reads current PIS level from Ledger Section 5 before any prose involving psychological pressure, violence, loss, moral injury, or identity crisis. [CONSEQUENCE AUDIT] (C-18) reads Character Ledger Section 5 current PIS state as part of its CSS-arc audit. PIS-7+ events that cross into identity-layer territory also trigger [IDEOLOGY AUDIT] (C-17). |

FCIA runs on every accepted prose unit and at session end; ALCA Mode A runs on every edit to this file; ALCA Mode B runs at session end. [RUN CANON AUDIT] (C-10) manually invokes the FCIA. | Canon-Part 18 | Part 26 ↔ Part 18 — Bidirectional Active | Part 26 governs all villain internal psychological experience — the five villain psychological archetypes (The Armored, The Consumed, The Calcified, The Fractured, The Hollow) are Part 26 PIS state and wound-pattern constructs applied to villain characters; PIS-4+ events are Villain Ledger update triggers per Part 18 Rule 18.6.1.3; Villain Ledger Section 5 (Psychological Profile) is the villain's cross-reference point into Part 26 — current PIS state, active wounds, coping mechanisms, and combat modifier are Part 26 governance translated into Villain Ledger format; villain psychological trajectory worsening under hero pressure (Part 18 Rule 18.3.3.1) follows Part 26 PIS escalation mechanics; villain PIS-8+ state produces unpredictable and maximally dangerous combat behavior per Part 18 Area 10.2 (governed by Part 26 combat modifier table); the psychological pathway from original wound to villain formation (Part 18 Villain Construction Protocol Step 2 — History) is entirely Part 26 governance — the original wound, first maladaptive response, point of no return, and full commitment events are all PIS events that Part 26 categorizes; Part 18 governs what the villain becomes; Part 26 governs the internal psychological reality of becoming it |
| Canon-Part 19 | Part 26 ↔ Part 19 — Bidirectional Active | Part 26 governs all Tier A side character psychology in full — PIS-5+ events are Side Character Profile update triggers per Part 19 Rule 19.6.1.2; hero PIS impact from a Tier A side character's death is a PIS-6 to PIS-8 Loss impact event (Part 26 Area 5.2) and must be tracked in both the side character's consequence record and the hero's psychological profile; Tier A side character psychological wounds are canonical governance constraints on their behavior — a mentor who carries a grief-wound (PIS-6) responds differently to the hero's choices than one who does not, and this difference is Part 26 governance applied to Part 19 character content; side character psychological development (PIS improvement through resolution pathways per Part 26 Area 11) is permitted and tracked in Side Character Profile Section 5; inter-side-character psychological dynamics are Part 26 domain — two Tier A allies with conflicting psychological profiles (one Armored, one Hollow) will interact through the lens of those profiles; Part 19 governs the side character's narrative function and depth requirements; Part 26 governs the internal psychological architecture that makes those functions real |
| Canon-Part 20 | Part 20 ↔ Part 26 — Bidirectional Active | Ideology (Part 20) and psychology (Part 26) are mutually shaping: psychological wounds (Part 26 PIS events) generate IE-1 through IE-7 Ideology Ledger events (Part 20); ideological frameworks (Part 20 Core/Operational/Surface layers) shape psychological interpretation — the same combat event produces different psychological experiences depending on the character's ideology; Ideological Crisis (IS-4 Fractured / IS-5 Collapsed in Part 20) and Ideological Void (Part 20 Area 8.2) are joint Part 26 (identity crisis, PIS-8 impact) and Part 20 (ideology collapse, IE-7) governance domains; Part 26 governs the internal psychological substrate from which ideological frameworks arise; Part 20 governs the belief systems built on that substrate; neither duplicates the other's domain |
