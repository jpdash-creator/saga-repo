## Area 6 — Formation Array Distance Governance

### 6.1 — Formation Array Effect Radius by Tier

Formation arrays (Part 12, Area 7) project their effects across bounded spatial zones. The maximum effect radius is governed by the array's Tier classification and the ambient energy density of its location.

| Array Tier | Standard Effect Radius | Maximum Achievable Radius (optimal CPM and placement) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 (Mortal Grade) | 1–50 m | ~200 m | Room to compound scale; cultivation chambers and sect protection |
| Tier 2 (Xiantian Grade) | 1–5 km | ~10 km | Sect ground to Town-scale; battlefield suppression arrays |
| Tier 3 (Core Grade) | 100–300 km | ~1,000 km | Regional-scale arrays; City and Capital protection formations |
| Tier 4 (Divine Grade) | 10,000–30,000 km | Full planetary scale | Planetary-scale energy networks; orbital array systems |
| Tier 5 (Primordial) | Star system scale | Multi-system scale | Beyond standard governance; Part 28 required |

**Ambient Energy Modifier on Array Radius:**
A Tier 3 array placed in a Class 7 (Sacred Ground) environment extends to the upper bound of its radius tier. A Tier 3 array placed in a Class 1 environment is limited to its minimum radius. The ambient energy class modifier applies to array radius as follows: Class 1 = minimum radius; Class 3 = standard radius; Class 5 = +30% radius; Class 7 = +70% radius (maximum achievable).

---

### 6.2 — Formation Array Sensing and Detection Range

The distance at which a formation array can actively sense and register intrusions, formations, and entities is generally 50% greater than its effect radius. A Tier 2 formation array with a 5 km effect radius can sense activity at approximately 7.5 km. Passive detection (registering strong cultivation output rather than active scan) extends to 150% of effect radius.
