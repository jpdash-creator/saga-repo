## Area 13 — Growth Law Interface with Part 31 (Synergy Law)

### 13.1 — Synergy as Growth Accelerator

Part 31 governs what cultivation-technology synergy produces when both tracks are present. Part 9 governs the growth mechanics that determine how quickly those synergy capabilities become available. The relationship: Part 31 defines the synergy outputs; Part 9 defines the growth trajectory that brings civilizations to the synergy thresholds.

**Rule 13.1.1 — Synergy Access Requires Growth Prerequisite Satisfaction:**
A civilization cannot access Model C synergy capabilities (Part 31, Area 5) simply because it has high-realm cultivators. It must also have achieved Tier 8 technology on the technology side (Part 8, Area 6.2 — technology-side Model C threshold). Part 9 governs how long it takes to reach Tier 8 and what growth events are required. Synergy is available only after growth prerequisites on both tracks are met.

### 13.2 — The Bidirectional Growth Effect

Part 31, Area 3 governs how cultivation assists technology growth (as an acceleration mechanism governed in Part 9, Area 6.6). Part 31, Area 4 governs how technology assists cultivation growth. The two growth mechanisms are bidirectional:

- Cultivation-assisted technology discovery (Part 9, Area 2.3; Part 31, Area 3) accelerates technology advancement
- Technology-supported cultivation infrastructure (Part 31, Area 4.1 — cultivation environments; Area 4.2 — medical support for cultivators) accelerates cultivation advancement in ways that feed back into faster technology advancement (more high-realm cultivators → more cultivation-assisted discovery → faster technology growth)

This bidirectional growth effect is the mechanical basis for the CPM Growth Cycle (Area 10.3 of this Part).
