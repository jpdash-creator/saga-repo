## Area 6 — Combat Applications

### 6.1 — Relic Use in the Part 5 Framework

All combat use of relics occurs within Part 5's engagement and escalation framework (Part 5, Dependency Section). This means:

**Rule 6.1.1 — Relic Use Does Not Bypass Force Legitimacy.**
Deploying an offensive relic against a target who has not engaged in legitimate combat (Part 5, Area 4) is classified by Part 5's Force Category law — it is assault, assassination, or accident depending on intent and context, regardless of the relic being used. The relic is the instrument; Part 5 governs the legitimacy of using it.

**Rule 6.1.2 — Relic Power Does Not Override Escalation Tier Intensity Ceilings.**
Part 5, Area 7 defines intensity containment boundaries by escalation tier. An E1 Personal Combat (personal-scale conflict) cannot legitimately escalate to continental-scale devastation simply because one party possesses a Tier 4 Formation Array or Tier 4 offensive relic. Using a relic to exceed the appropriate intensity ceiling for the combat's escalation tier is a Part 5 violation — an unauthorized escalation with the consequences defined in Part 5.

**Rule 6.1.3 — Named Relic + Named Move = Combined Output.**
When a cultivator activates a Named Move while using a Named Relic, both are present in the combat prose and the output is assessed at the effective ceiling of the combination. The Named Move's success rate (Part 6) is not modified by the relic, but the output ceiling of a successful execution reflects the relic's tier contribution.

---

### 6.2 — Relic-Triggered Terrain Hazards

High-tier offensive relics that produce massive force output or explosive CPM release create battlefield terrain effects that interact with Part 5's Battlefield Spatial Constraints and Part 11's CPM combat rules:

- A Tier 3 offensive relic detonated in an enclosed space creates a geological-scale force event. Part 5's Protected Zone and Collateral Damage rules apply.
- A Tier 2 offensive relic that shatters upon activation (sacrificial artifact) creates a burst CPM release equivalent to deliberate CPM substance detonation (Part 11, Area 9.3).
- Formation Array destruction in combat (see Area 7.5) creates the most severe terrain-altering effects — the collapse of a major Formation Array releases all stored energy immediately.

---

### 6.3 — Passive Combat Effects of High-Tier Relics

Tier 3 and above relics that a cultivator carries produce passive environmental effects in the same manner as the cultivator's own Heavenly Scale signature — because the relic's energy field interacts with the surrounding environment continuously. A Core-grade sword sheathed at a Warrior cultivator's hip produces a low-level Grade 10 Core Pulse effect around the cultivator at all times. This cannot be suppressed without sealing the relic. For powerful relics, carrying them is itself a combat declaration — their presence is detectable at range.
