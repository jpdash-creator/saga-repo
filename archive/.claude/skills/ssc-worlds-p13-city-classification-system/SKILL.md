## Area 5 — City Classification System

A City is a major inhabited location with complex governance, multiple administrative districts, a self-sustaining economy, major cultivation or technology infrastructure, and sufficient defensive capability to require siege warfare to breach.

### 5.1 — City Class Table

| Class | Designation | Population Scale | Ambient Energy | Affinity Profile | Special Zone | Historical Depth | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-1 | Frontier City | 30,000–150,000 | Class 1–2 | Neutral | None | New | A City on a growth edge. Recently established, still consolidating infrastructure. Organized governance but minimal historical depth. |
| C-2 | Common City | 80,000–400,000 | Class 2 | Neutral | None | Established | The baseline City. Complete governance, established economy, multi-district administration, standing defensive capability. |
| C-3 | Commercial City | 150,000–700,000 | Class 2–3 | Neutral | None | Established | A major commercial hub. Auction houses, merchant guilds, multi-faction trading networks. High economic throughput is its defining purpose. |
| C-4 | Military City | 100,000–500,000 | Class 2–3 | Neutral | None | Variable | A City built primarily for military function. Major defensive formation arrays, armories, training grounds, command infrastructure. |
| C-5 | Rooted City | 200,000–800,000 | Class 2–3 | Neutral or mild | None | Long-established — centuries | A City of significant age and deep cultural identity. Historical significance embedded in its architecture, districts, and social structure. |
| C-6 | Affinity City | 100,000–600,000 | Class 3–4 | Active affinity | Possible: Passive Formation Zone | Variable | A City where the environmental affinity shapes everything — architecture, commerce, culture, and what kinds of practitioners and institutions congregate. |
| C-7 | Energy City | 150,000–700,000 | Class 3–4 | Neutral or affinity | Passive Formation Zone | Variable | A City built around an active Passive Formation Zone. Energy resource economy drives the City's commerce and growth. |
| C-8 | Vortex City | 100,000–600,000 | Class 4–5 | Affinity aligned | Vortex Formation Zone | Variable | A City built on or around a major Vortex Formation Zone. The Vortex defines the City's identity, economy, and power. Competing factions may contest control of the Vortex. |
| C-9 | Ancient City | 50,000–700,000 | Class 3–5 | Variable | Variable — historical ruins, formation networks, accumulated residue | Very old — millennia or more | A City of immense age. Every district carries historical weight. Ancient formation arrays may still be active or dormant beneath its foundations. Known across its continent or World for its historical depth. |
| C-10 | Sacred City | 5,000–300,000 | Class 6–7 | Aligned with Sacred Ground | Sacred Ground (any grade) | Variable | A City within or immediately adjacent to a confirmed Sacred Ground. The most exceptional City location possible. Reality operates differently here. Access may require specific conditions. Known across its Star System or beyond. |
