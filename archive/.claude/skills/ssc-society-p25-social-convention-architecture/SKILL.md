## Area 10 — Social Convention Architecture

### 10.1 — Greeting Convention Systems

Social conventions around greeting are among the most culturally loaded social acts. Incorrect greetings cause offense disproportionate to their apparent triviality. The following governing principles apply:

**Rule 10.1.1 — Greeting Governs Before Combat Governs.**
In any encounter between parties who have not established combat hostility, greeting conventions take precedence over all other social acts. Even enemies meeting under truce protocols perform greeting.

**Rule 10.1.2 — Greeting Embeds Information.**
Every greeting system encodes status claims. The body position (who bows, how deeply), the first words spoken, and the title used all constitute status assertion. Greeting errors are not trivial — they are status challenges.

**Rule 10.1.3 — Greeting Style Is Culturally Legible.**
A skilled social actor can identify cultural origin, faction affiliation, and realm of cultivation from greeting style alone. This is a Part 24 identity disclosure event (soul-sense scan equivalent in social terms).

### 10.2 — Canonical Greeting Forms by CPT

| CPT | Primary Greeting Form | Status Encoding | Incorrect Greeting Response |
|-----|-----------------------|-----------------|----------------------------|
| CPT-1 Hierarchical-Sacral | Deep bow, hands clasped (depth = realm differential) | Bow depth = acknowledged status gap | Cold correction; formal rebuke; ignored if severe |
| CPT-2 Meritocratic-Martial | Open-palm gesture (no weapon concealed); direct eye contact | Equal palms = peer acknowledgment; eye contact = honest challenge acknowledgment | Challenge to prove status claim |
| CPT-3 Mercantile-Exchange | Extended hand (contract-ready gesture); item of value offered | Item value = assessment of relationship value | Polite refusal; counter-offer |
| CPT-4 Isolationist-Purity | Formal spoken formula in community tongue; no physical contact | Formula accuracy = community belonging signal | Silence; non-acknowledgment |
| CPT-5 Syncretic-Adaptive | Adopts the guest's own greeting form | Cultural recognition = respect for the other's tradition | Appreciation; opening of trust |
| CPT-6 Theocratic-Covenant | Covenant affirmation formula; ritual gesture | Correct formula = covenant membership signal | Doctrinal correction; exclusion risk |
| CPT-7 Anarchic-Sovereign | Verbal statement of purpose: "I am [name], I come for [purpose]" | Directness = honest sovereign presentation | No issue — all approaches acknowledged |

### 10.3 — Honorific Architecture

Honorifics encode social hierarchy in language. Every Culture in the Starward Saga has an honorific system scaled to its dominant Class Axis (Section 6.1). Canonical honorific structures by dominant class:

**Cultivation-Class Honorifics (CC-Class dominant):**
- Mortal Tier 1–3: First name only
- Core Formation: "[Name]-gē/jiě" (elder sibling honorific) or "[Name]-senior"
- Core Solidification: "Practitioner [Name]"
- Divine Realm: "Elder [Name]" or "[Name] Elder"
- Heaven Soul: "Grand Elder [Name]" or "Ancestor [Name]"
- Divine Sea: "Venerable [Name]" or "[Title] of [Territory]"

**Wealth-Class Honorifics (W-Class dominant):**
- Common: No honorific
- Merchant: "Trader [Name]"
- Rich: "[Name]-gongzi / xiaojie" (young lord / young lady) for wealthy families
- Wealthy Lineage: "[Name]-laoban" (master/boss)
- Grand Merchant: "Honored [Name]"
- Financial Apex: "[Name] of the [Institution]"

**Lineage-Class Honorifics (L-Class dominant):**
- Common: First name
- Gentry: "[Family Name]-[given name]"
- Nobility: "Lord/Lady [Family Name]"
- Highborn: "High Lord/Lady of [Territory]"
- Grand Lineage: "Grand Lord/Lady of [Domain]"
- Sacred Lineage: "Divine Lineage [Title]" — specific to each sacred tradition

### 10.4 — Dress Convention Systems

Cultural dress codes encode social position, faction affiliation, cultivation realm, and ideological stance simultaneously. Governing principles:

**Rule 10.4.1 — Dress Is Communication.**
In cultivation civilizations, clothing choice is a speech act. Wearing another faction's colors, a realm marker one has not earned, or materials whose cultural significance one does not hold is a social crime equivalent to identity theft in some cultures.

**Rule 10.4.2 — Dress Restrictions Are Class Architecture.**
Sumptuary law (what materials, colors, and styles each social stratum may wear) is one of the most culturally distinct expressions of a civilization's class architecture. CPT-1 cultures have the most elaborate sumptuary law; CPT-7 cultures have none.

**Rule 10.4.3 — Cultivation Dress Signals Readiness.**
In CPT-1 and CPT-2 cultures, how a cultivator is dressed signals their combat readiness state. Full-regalia dress signals ceremonial presence; travel dress signals readiness; partial armor signals alert state.
