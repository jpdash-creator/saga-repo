## Area 18 — Domain Ownership Disambiguation

### 18.1 — Part 33 Exclusive Domain

The following are Part 33's exclusive governance domain — no other Part governs these aspects:

1. Weapon type taxonomy (T-KIN, T-RAY, T-BURST, T-FORM, T-SYN, T-PSI, T-VEC and all named sub-types).
2. Weapon Scale Class (WS-I through WS-VII) — the footprint classification system.
3. Weapon Configuration Code (SDW, DEP, VHM, ORD, CRY, AUT, SYN, BIO).
4. Weapon Deployment Paradigm Bands (TW-Band through CW-Band).
5. Weapon User Classification (WU-I through WU-VI).
6. Weapon Damage Architecture (DV-I through DV-V, damage multiplier table).
7. Weapon Power Consumption governance (WE-I through WE-VIII energy classes).
8. Weapon Maintenance Doctrine.
9. Autonomous Weapon Classification (AT-1 through AT-4) and governance rules.
10. Weapon Market Classification (WM-I through WM-III).
11. Restricted and Absolute Prohibition weapon categories.
12. Weapon Prestige Scale (P-I through P-VII).

### 18.2 — Explicitly Not Part 33 Domain

The following are explicitly NOT governed by Part 33 — cross-references provided:

1. **Weapon range tables** → Part 14, Area 5.3 (authoritative; Part 33 does not duplicate).
2. **Combat engagement legitimacy** → Part 5 (authoritative; Part 33 weapon rules operate within Part 5's framework).
3. **Formation array object rules** → Part 12 (Part 33 references Part 12 for formation arrays embedded in weapons).
4. **Cultivation technique classification** → Part 6 (cultivation techniques that resemble weapons are Part 6 Named Moves, not Part 33 weapons).
5. **CPM substance properties and grades** → Part 11 (Part 33 references Part 11 for CPM fuel grades).
6. **Weapon delivery vehicle travel mechanics** → Part 15 (transit mechanics of weapon platforms).
7. **Weapon construction science prerequisites** → Part 10 (science prerequisites for weapon tier advancement).
8. **Weapon cultural meaning and prestige narratives** → Part 25 (social significance of weapons).
9. **Cultivation realm definitions and defensive capabilities** → Part 3 (Part 33 cross-references Part 3 for defense thresholds).
