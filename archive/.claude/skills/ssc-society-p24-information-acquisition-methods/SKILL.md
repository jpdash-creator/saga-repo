## Area 15 — Information Acquisition Methods

### 15.1 — Canonical Acquisition Methods

All information acquisition in the Starward Saga uses one or more of the following canonical methods:

**Method AM-1 — Soul Interrogation:**
Active soul scanning (Area 8.3) to extract information from a person. Ranges from Surface Impression Scan to Total Consciousness Penetration. The most direct method but requires soul cultivation and creates detectable evidence.

**Method AM-2 — Physical Interrogation:**
Non-cultivation coercive information extraction. Includes intimidation, deprivation, and physical pressure. Effective against targets without sufficient cultivation or willpower. Countered by cultivation-based mental discipline, soul compartmentalization (Area 6.1), and dead-man protocols (information that self-destructs on certain triggers).

**Method AM-3 — Social Engineering:**
Building access to a target's trust circle to receive information voluntarily, or exploiting social dynamics to extract information indirectly. The primary method of long-term deep-cover operations. Countered by soul-sense emotional authenticity detection (P24-ITC1-1) and background verification protocols.

**Method AM-4 — Physical Infiltration:**
Accessing information in its substrate by physically entering a secured location and copying, extracting, or memorizing the stored information. Countered by physical security, formation scanning arrays, surveillance systems, and access control.

**Method AM-5 — Technical Interception:**
Intercepting information in transit through a transmission channel. For technology channels: signals interception, network hacking. For formation channels: formation-wave frequency interception. For courier channels: courier capture or surveillance. Countered by encryption, channel obfuscation, and information segmentation.

**Method AM-6 — Cultivator Asset Development:**
Recruiting an existing authorized insider — a faction member, government official, or household servant who already has access — to provide information voluntarily (for payment, ideology, coercion, or personal loyalty). The most productive long-term method and the hardest to detect. Countered by regular soul-state monitoring of sensitive personnel, compartmentalization that limits what any single insider can access, and double-agent programs.

**Method AM-7 — Environmental Reading:**
Inferring information from ambient leakage (ITC-7): cultivation residue analysis, behavioral pattern analysis, social network mapping, resource procurement tracking. No direct intrusion required. Countered by operational security discipline — minimizing trace evidence, disguising resource procurement, and standardizing behavioral patterns to eliminate tells.
