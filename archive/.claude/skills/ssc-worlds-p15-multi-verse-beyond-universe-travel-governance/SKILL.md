## Area 18 — Multi-Verse and Beyond-Universe Travel Governance

### 18.1 — Scope Deferral Declaration

**The current primary series arc operates within a single universe, at scales from planetary through galactic and (potentially) to universal scale.** The following travel categories are acknowledged to exist in the lore but are **outside the governance scope of Part 15 v1.0** and are deferred to future authoring:

- Transit between distinct universes (multi-verse travel)
- Navigation within the Primordium / Absolute Chaos
- Dimensional transit (movement between dimensional layers of reality)
- Existence-substrate travel (movement at the level of what it means to exist)

### 18.2 — Cultivation Threshold for Deferred Travel

**Divine Sea Realm** is identified as the cultivation threshold at which multi-verse travel becomes theoretically possible. The mechanism is:
- The Divine Sea Realm cultivator's "divine sea" can, at its theoretical peak, extend beyond the boundary of a single universe — but this requires complete mastery of the Divine Sea stage.
- Controlled multi-verse transit is a capability above the Divine Sea Realm ladder summit — it belongs to realms beyond the current Part 3 cultivation ladder.
- When future cultivation realms (above Divine Sea) are authored, Part 15 will be updated.

### 18.3 — Technology Threshold for Deferred Travel

**CCT-17 (Transversal / Type IV+)** is identified as the technology threshold at which trans-universal transit capability begins to emerge (per Part 8 Type 9 Level 17 description). This is deferred scope until those civilization tiers become narratively relevant.

### 18.4 — Current Story Scope Boundary

For all current narrative purposes: the universe is the ceiling of meaningful travel governance. Any character or faction claiming multi-verse travel capability in the current story arc is operating beyond defined Part 15 governance and requires Part 28 (Creative Flexibility Law) authorization.
