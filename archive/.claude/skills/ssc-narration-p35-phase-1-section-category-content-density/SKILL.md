## Area 3 — Phase 1 Section × Category Content Density Target Table

### 3.1 — Table Architecture

The table below is the canonical CDL-2 content density target for every Section of Phase 1. Each cell contains **Low%–Anchor%–High%**:
- **Low:** Minimum acceptable Section-aggregate for this category. Falling below Low triggers Advisory WARN (non-Anchor) or Hard FAIL trajectory warning (Anchor floor).
- **Anchor:** Canonical target. The AI steers toward the Anchor as the optimal distribution point for the story this Section is telling.
- **High:** Maximum recommended Section-aggregate. Exceeding High triggers Advisory WARN — not a failure, but a signal that one category may be crowding others.

**Table Status:** Locked Guidance (Part 28 §2.3) — Canon v4.26 approved. Revisions require CDL-2 Section Override (Area 2.4).

**★ = Universal Anchor Floor category (must achieve ≥20% Anchor in every Section; Hard FAIL if not met at Section-close)**

### 3.5 — Section Derivation Notes

Each Section's CDL-2 targets are derived from Part 30's Section arc definitions. The narrative rationale for each Section's distinctive profile is recorded below.

**Section 1 — The Citadels of Morning (1yr)**
Root-planting. The world must be *shown*, relationships *built*, and the ensemble *established as people* before combat can carry weight. Lore & World-Building (55% Anchor) and Dialogue & Social (58% Anchor) dominate — these are the categories that make the world worth protecting. Combat is present through cultivation practice scenes and adult protagonist level-establishment moments (42% Anchor) but formal battles are absent. Named Moves are minimal (18% Anchor) — no formal technique taxonomy has yet been established. Political & Faction begins its cultivation (28% Anchor) through the diplomacy scene and alliance establishment.

**Section 2 — The First Trial of Blades (1.5yr)**
First testing. The tournament arc, training deepened, and First Awakening drive Combat (62% Anchor) and Named Moves (40% Anchor) to their first meaningful highs. Cultivation Growth rises sharply (50% Anchor) — the First Awakening at the S1→S2 boundary is Phase 1's first major cultivation event. Lore drops (32% Anchor) — the world is established; now it is tested. Dialogue & Social holds strong (42% Anchor) through training relationships and tournament social dynamics.

**Section 3 — Courts and Tensions (1.5yr)**
The political Section. Political & Faction reaches its Phase peak (65% Anchor) — the diplomatic summit, faction introduction, and Third Faction emergence make Section 3 the series' most politically concentrated Section. Dialogue & Social peaks to its second-highest Phase value (60% Anchor) — diplomatic exchange is by definition social interaction. Information & Intrigue surges (48% Anchor) — the Third Faction's hidden movements are the Section's dramatic engine. Combat holds (48% Anchor) through training and faction-friction events.

**Section 4 — The Fallen Demigods (1yr)**
Action-reset and ensemble completion. The border crisis and ensemble convergence produce the second-highest Combat anchor in Phase 1 (72% Anchor). Consequence & Psychology peaks to its highest anchor outside S7 (48% Anchor) — Hercules's arc of displacement-to-engagement and the suppressed-power-glimpse moment are Phase 1's most psychologically charged events. Lore holds at its minimum Anchor floor value (30% Anchor) through border territory rendering and Olympus backstory revelation.

**Section 5 — Guilds, Quests, and the World's Edge (1.5yr)**
Exploration. Travel & Discovery reaches its second-highest anchor (58% Anchor) — the entire Section is a continental quest-exploration arc. Combat (68% Anchor) and Named Moves (48% Anchor) remain strong through quest combat. Political & Faction rises (45% Anchor) — the guild ecosystem is a continental political-economic structure even in wilderness contexts. Cultivation Growth (50% Anchor) builds toward the Section 6 Second Awakening.

**Section 6 — The Seven World Trees (1.5yr)**
Phase 1's most category-dense Section — three major threshold events run simultaneously. Lore & World-Building ties its Phase peak (65% Anchor) with the World Tree discovery. Cultivation Growth hits its Phase peak (62% Anchor) — the Second Awakening is the largest single cultivation event in Phase 1. Political & Faction surges (60% Anchor) — wars begin and the political race for World Tree access drives the Section's conflict engine. Combat holds strong (65% Anchor) as wars erupt mid-Section.

**Section 7 — The World Tree's Trials and the Final War (0.75yr)**
War climax. Combat hits its Phase maximum (85% Anchor, High 92%) — consistent with the War Arc / Climax Arc intensity mandate. Named Moves (58% Anchor) and Cultivation Growth (55% Anchor) are both high as all Phase 1 end-states are delivered. Consequence & Psychology rises to second-highest in Phase (52% Anchor) — the war cost must be fully rendered. Dialogue & Social compresses (32% Anchor) — the war's pace limits extended social scenes but does not eliminate them. Travel & Discovery reaches its Phase minimum (18% Anchor) — the Section operates in a compressed geographic zone.

**Section 8 — Snow Town and the Edge of the World (0.25yr)**
Phase inversion and coda. Travel & Discovery peaks at its Phase maximum (78% Anchor, High 88%) — the entire Section is an act of discovery. Dialogue & Social reaches its Phase peak (68% Anchor) — the airship is explicitly a relational space per Part 30 Law P30-L7. Lore & World-Building hits its Phase peak (68% Anchor, tied with S6) — Snow Town, Snow Mountain, and the new continent are the Section's world-building engine. Combat compresses to its Phase minimum (30% Anchor) — consolidation only; no cultivation breakthroughs. Cultivation Growth holds just above its Anchor Floor minimum (28% Anchor) through Snow Mountain phenomena and protagonist cultivation reflection scenes. Technology reaches its Phase peak (45% Anchor) — Snow Town's technology culture and the airship's systems are front and center.
