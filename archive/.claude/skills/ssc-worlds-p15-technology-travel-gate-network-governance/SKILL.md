## Area 13 — Technology Travel: Gate Network Governance

### 13.1 — Gate Network Fundamental Rules

**Rule 13.1.1 — Both Ends Required.**
Star gate transit requires an active gate structure at both the departure and destination point. A gate transit cannot proceed if the destination gate is offline, destroyed, or not yet constructed.

**Rule 13.1.2 — Gates Cannot Be Forced.**
A gate cannot be activated from the outside by force. Gate access is controlled by the operating authority at each gate. Bypassing gate access control requires destroying the gate, which makes it unavailable to the attacker as well.

**Rule 13.1.3 — Gate Throughput Is Limited.**
Gates have a throughput limit governed by their energy supply and aperture size. A standard Tier 10 gate can transit one large vessel every 10–60 minutes. Mass transit of an army or fleet requires hours to days through a single gate.

**Rule 13.1.4 — Gate Networks Are Strategic Infrastructure.**
Control of gate networks is the primary strategic-level military and economic variable for civilizations at Tier 10+. Severing or capturing key gate nodes can isolate sectors of civilization from one another. Gate network security is a major governance domain for high-tier civilizations.

**Rule 13.1.5 — Gates Can Fail.**
Gate malfunction (power failure, deliberate sabotage, physical damage) strands transiting objects in mid-transit — which is uniformly catastrophic. Gate transit is only initiated when the destination gate status is confirmed active.

### 13.2 — Formation Array Gate Networks (Cultivation-Side)

Cultivation civilizations develop an analog to the technology star gate system through formation arrays (Part 12, Area 7): **Formation Teleportation Arrays** (FTAs). These are fixed spatial architecture structures that create a spatial fold bridge between two array nodes.

**Formation Teleportation Array Properties:**
- **Origin:** Core Formation-realm inscriptors or above can construct functional FTAs; Divine Realm inscriptors are required for multi-light-year range arrays
- **Range by inscriptor realm:**
  - Core Formation inscriptor: ~1 Mm to ~1 AU range
  - Core Assimilation: ~1 AU to ~1 light-hour range
  - Core Solidification: ~1 light-hour to ~10 light-years range
  - Divine Transformation+: ~10 light-years to ~100 light-years range
  - Heaven Treading+: Full interstellar range (100 ly+)
  - Divine Sea Realm: Effectively unlimited within-universe range
- **Transit time:** Near-instantaneous (seconds for the transit itself)
- **Activation cost:** High CPM grade energy (Part 11 interface); scales with range
- **Throughput:** Typically one person or small group per activation cycle (minutes between activations for personal-scale arrays; larger arrays at Tier 3 formation inscriptor level can transit entire companies)
- **Both-end requirement:** Like technology gates, both the departure and destination array nodes must be active

**Rule 13.2.1 — Formation Arrays and Technology Gates Are Not Intercompatible.**
A formation array portal cannot be connected to a technology star gate and vice versa. They operate on different physical principles (formation qi-architecture vs. energy-physics-based spatial bridge). Synergy Travel (Area 15) governs hybrid systems.
