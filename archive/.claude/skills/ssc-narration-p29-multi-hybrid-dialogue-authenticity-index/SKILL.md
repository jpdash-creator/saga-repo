## Rule Text (Verbatim Source — Authoritative)
## Area 17 — Multi-Hybrid Dialogue Authenticity Index (MHDAI)

**Purpose:** The MHDAI is the governing framework for calibrating dialogue authenticity across all variable dimensions that affect how a character speaks. It is a named-modifier stack system: each applicable factor applies named modifiers to a character's Base Register. The modifiers combine into an Effective Register (ER) code and a Scene Speech Profile (SSP) — a practical, per-scene list of observable rules the author must follow when writing that character's dialogue.

The MHDAI governs all characters whose Base Register is below BR-AU (Adult). It applies to every child and adolescent character in the Starward Saga. For adult characters, the MHDAI modifiers apply to emotional-state regression (ESM) and information-security concealment (ISM) only.

**Binding from:** Prose 4 (1.1.1.001.1.00004) forward. Proses 1-3 logged as Pre-Index Prose (see Section 14.6).

---

### 14.1 — Foundational Architecture: Four Steps

**Step 1 — Determine Base Register (BR)**
The Base Register is fixed by the character's confirmed biological age at the time of the prose unit. It is the unconditional floor of the character's speech.

**Step 2 — Apply Named Modifiers (NM)**
Each applicable factor applies one or more named modifiers. Modifiers are applied in stack order: CSM → KDM → PM → ESM. The ISM operates independently of the stack (see Section 14.2, Modifier Category 5).

**Step 3 — Calculate Effective Register (ER)**
The ER is the BR after all applicable modifiers. It is expressed as a named code (e.g., `ER-LC+PD`) and a profile of observable rules specific to that character in that scene.

**Step 4 — Generate Scene Speech Profile (SSP)**
The SSP is the practical authoring guide: 4-6 observable rules the author must follow when writing the character's dialogue in this scene.

---

### 14.2 — Base Register Table (BR)

| Band Code | Age Range | Band Name | Core Speech Profile |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|---------------------|
| BR-EC | 4–6 | Early Childhood | Simple sentences. Subject-verb-object dominant. Concrete vocabulary only. Questions focus on immediate experience. Very high emotional transparency — feeling is expressed before thought. Cannot sustain abstract reasoning. All conclusions are announced; none are framed. |
| BR-LC | 7–9 | Late Childhood | Compound sentences beginning. Abstract concepts accessible only when anchored to concrete reference. Vocabulary expanding but still child-lexicon. Excitement, pride, and frustration expressed more directly than adults. Logical reasoning is linear: A → B → conclusion. Not yet multi-layered. Prodigy children may display domain precision at this band while remaining anchored here emotionally and socially. |
| BR-PA | 10–12 | Pre-Adolescent | Abstract reasoning emerging. Can sustain multi-step arguments. Vocabulary expands rapidly. Emotional regulation beginning but still porous — composure costs visible effort. Social self-awareness develops: character begins noticing how they appear to others and adjusting accordingly. Can perform formality at a visible cost. |
| BR-AD | 13–16 | Adolescent | Near-adult cognitive capacity. Complex vocabulary available. Multi-layered reasoning. Emotional regulation practiced but volatile under high-stakes pressure. Strong social self-consciousness. Can sustain formal register convincingly, but authenticity-cracks visible under stress. |
| BR-AU | 17+ | Adult | Full cognitive range. Emotional regulation developed (varies by character disposition and circumstance). Can sustain diplomatic, measured, multi-layered discourse without visible cost. Formality is a chosen register, not a performance. |

### 14.3 — The Five Named Modifier Categories

#### Modifier Category 1 — Cultivation-Stage Modifier (CSM)

Cultivation accelerates the mind, but domain-specific speech improves first. Social and emotional maturation follow only with sustained practice.

| Cultivation Status | Modifier Code | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Unawakened | CSM-0 | No cultivation modifier. BR governs fully. |
| Newly Awakened (confirmed, first year of cultivation) | CSM-1 | +1 band on Cultivation-domain speech only. All other dimensions: no change from BR. |
| Established Cultivator (1+ years active cultivation) | CSM-2 | +1 band on Cultivation-domain speech. Social/emotional dimensions: partial uplift (+0.5 — observable as improved stress-regulation in cultivation-context scenes only, not in personal/domestic scenes). |
| High-Realm Cultivator (Ruler realm and above) | CSM-3 | +1 band across all dimensions when cultivation identity is salient in the scene. At this stage, cultivation has genuinely accelerated all cognitive and regulatory systems. |

**CSM Domain-First Rule:** CSM-1 and CSM-2 uplift cultivation-topic speech only. A newly awakened 9-year-old speaks with greater precision about cultivation theory than about social situations — the cultivation experience has taught them to reason in this domain, not in all domains.

---

#### Modifier Category 2 — Knowledge-Domain Modifier (KDM)

A character with demonstrably deep expertise in a named domain receives a speech-register uplift when actively reasoning within that domain during the scene.

| Condition | Modifier Code | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Character actively reasoning within confirmed expert domain | KDM+1 | +1 band on Vocabulary Level and Reasoning Display dimensions only. Emotional Transparency and Social Formality dimensions: no change from BR. |
| Character operating outside their confirmed expert domain | KDM-0 | No uplift. BR governs. |

**Domain-Only Rule:** The KDM+1 uplift activates and deactivates at the sentence level. A character mid-scene may shift from domain-active to domain-inactive as the conversation subject changes. This produces the authentic effect of a child who sounds precisely adult about their specialty and then suddenly sounds their age about something else. This is the intended behaviour — do not smooth it.

**Confirmed Domain Registry:** Confirmed expert domains are recorded in the character's Ledger file under the Prodigy/Expertise notation. New domains are established in prose and then logged; they cannot be assumed.

---

#### Modifier Category 3 — Prodigy Modifier (PM)

A character designated as an intellectual prodigy in their Character Ledger receives dimension-specific uplifts within their confirmed domain. The PM amplifies the KDM+1 effect and adds the Child-Anchor Requirement (CAR).

| Dimension | Modifier Code | Effect When Domain Active |
|---|---|---|
| Vocabulary Level | PM+1 | +1 band in prodigy's confirmed domain. Technical and precise vocabulary of the domain is available. |
| Reasoning Display | PM+1 | +1 band in prodigy's confirmed domain. Multi-step, domain-specific argument construction is available. |
| Emotional Transparency | PM-0 | No change. Prodigy status does not reduce emotional transparency. Prodigies feel; they are not stoic. |
| Social Formality | PM-0 | No change. Prodigies have domain intelligence, not social experience. Social register remains at BR. |

**Child-Anchor Requirement (CAR):** Every prose unit in which a prodigy child character has active dialogue must contain at least one Child-Anchor Event. A Child-Anchor Event is a moment in the dialogue where the character's delivery, framing, or response reveals their biological age despite their intellectual capability. The five valid anchor types:

| Anchor Type | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Pride Display** | The character signals their own intelligence or discovery without diplomatic moderation. Children announce conclusions; adults frame them as contributions. | "I found it before you" / "I already knew that" / "I worked it out last night" |
| **Linear Excitement** | A moment of genuine uncontrolled enthusiasm about an idea or finding that breaks through measured presentation. | Pace quickening; jumping to conclusion before setup is complete; "and also—" interrupting their own thought |
| **Emotional Leakage** | A moment where the emotional undercurrent of the character's experience shows through, unmediated by adult-style regulation. | Wanting validation that cannot quite be suppressed; a flash of hurt at being doubted; visible effort to seem calm |
| **Scale Error** | A minor misjudgment of the social weight of what they are saying — the character does not yet fully read the room. | Presenting a politically delicate finding with the same tone as a homework result; not registering that the listener is troubled |
| **Directness Without Diplomacy** | Children state conclusions plainly. Adults wrap conclusions in consideration for the listener's reaction. | "The interpretation is wrong" vs. "There may be an alternative reading worth examining" |

The CAR is a hard requirement for all prose units in which a prodigy child character speaks. Missing the CAR is a MAJOR MHDAI audit failure (see Section 14.5).

---

#### Modifier Category 4 — Emotional State Modifier (ESM)

Emotional intensity pulls the character's active ER DOWN toward the raw biological-age baseline. This is the age-regression pull — the single most powerful modifier in the stack.

| Emotional Intensity Level | Modifier Code | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline (calm, neutral, working focus) | ESM-0 | No modification. Active ER from steps 1-3 governs. |
| Mildly elevated (mild excitement, mild nervousness, quiet pride) | ESM-1 | Minor softening: sentence structure simplifies slightly, emotional transparency rises slightly. Domain precision remains mostly available. |
| Moderately elevated (genuine excitement, worry, pride surge, frustration) | ESM-2 | Vocabulary drops toward biological BR band. Reasoning Display loses one layer of complexity. Emotional Transparency rises — the character's feeling becomes clearly legible. KDM and PM are partially suspended: domain vocabulary remains but multi-step argument construction degrades. |
| Highly elevated (fear, grief, overwhelming joy, anger, shock) | ESM-3 | Full regression to biological BR. All KDM, PM, and CSM uplifts are suspended. The character speaks from their raw age. This is the unconditional floor. |

**ESM Precedence Rule:** The ESM takes precedence over all other modifiers. A prodigy child under ESM-3 speaks like a typical child of their biological age — the intellectual capability does not disappear, but the channel for expressing it is overwhelmed. This is the most realistic result and is the intended authoring outcome.

---

#### Modifier Category 5 — Information-Security Modifier (ISM)

When a character must conceal, strategically deflect, or withhold information, the manner of concealment is governed by their active **Base Register band** (BR), not by any uplifted ER. Domain expertise and prodigy intelligence do not elevate information-security capability. Concealment is a social skill, not a cognitive one, and social skills develop with age and experience — not with domain knowledge.

| Base Register Band | Concealment Profile |
|---|---|
| BR-EC (Early Childhood) | Subject change, silence, or direct refusal: "I do not want to say." No misdirection. The concealment is obvious to any attentive adult. |
| BR-LC (Late Childhood) | Topic deflection; answering an adjacent question rather than the one asked; partial truths; "I do not know" when the character does know. Evasions are detectable to attentive adults; invisible to inattentive ones. |
| BR-PA (Pre-Adolescent) | Technically true statements; delayed answers; changing subject with more natural transition. Less obvious but still readable to skilled observers. |
| BR-AD (Adolescent) | Practiced partial disclosure; misdirection by emphasis; calculated omission. Approaching adult sophistication. Skilled adults may still detect the seam. |
| BR-AU (Adult) | Full adult concealment toolkit: technically true statements, strategic reframing, silence as communication, calibrated omission, performing transparency to conceal. The seam, if present, is architectural. |

**ISM-Prodigy Interaction Rule:** A prodigy child's ISM profile is governed by their BR band, not their domain-uplifted ER. KDM and PM do not modify ISM. A Late Childhood prodigy (BR-LC) conceals with Late Childhood tools regardless of how sophisticated their legal analysis is. This produces the authentic result: the child's evasions are detectable to experienced adults who know how to watch for them.

### 14.4 — The Named Modifier Stack Calculation

**Calculation sequence:**
1. Confirm character's biological age → identify BR band
2. Identify active modifiers: CSM (cultivation status) → KDM (domain active or inactive) → PM (prodigy confirmed or not) → ESM (emotional state in this scene)
3. Apply modifiers in sequence; ESM takes precedence if highly elevated
4. Generate ER code
5. Write SSP: 4-6 observable authoring rules for this character in this scene
6. ISM is applied independently whenever concealment occurs in the scene

**ER Code Format:** `ER-[Band Code][+Modifier Flags][-ESM Flag]`

| ER Example | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ER-LC` | Unmodified Late Childhood — no uplifts active, baseline emotional state |
| `ER-LC+PD` | Late Childhood + Prodigy Domain active — KDM+1 + PM+1 on Vocabulary and Reasoning |
| `ER-LC+PD-E1` | Late Childhood + Prodigy Domain + ESM Level 1 (mildly elevated — minor softening) |
| `ER-LC-E2` | Late Childhood + ESM Level 2 (moderately elevated — domain precision degraded) |
| `ER-LC-E3` | Late Childhood + ESM Level 3 (highly elevated — all uplifts suspended; raw BR-LC) |
| `ER-PA+C1` | Pre-Adolescent + CSM Level 1 (newly awakened — cultivation domain speech uplifted) |
| `ER-AU` | Adult, no modifiers active |

### 14.5 — Varya Canonical MHDAI Profile

This section applies the MHDAI to Varya at story-start (Chapter 001, Phase 1). This is the reference application for the most frequently appearing child character in Phase 1.

**Character:** Varya (daughter of Varys and Valia, Azure Sky Kingdom)
**Biological Age at Story Start:** 8
**Base Register:** BR-LC (Late Childhood, age 7-9)
**Cultivation Status:** Unawakened → CSM-0 (no cultivation modifier)
**Confirmed Prodigy Domain:** Governance law (Northern Accord analysis, legal text interpretation, bilateral compact reading, treaty provision identification)
**Prodigy Confirmation Source:** Varys's interior acknowledgment, Prose 2 ("Before He Was the King")

**Varya's Modifier Stack at Story Start:**
- CSM: CSM-0 (Unawakened)
- KDM: KDM+1 when actively reasoning about governance / treaty law / legal text; KDM-0 otherwise
- PM: PM+1 on Vocabulary + Reasoning Display in confirmed domain; PM-0 on all other dimensions
- ESM: Assessed per scene (baseline to highly elevated depending on scene conditions)
- ISM: Late Childhood concealment profile (detectable evasions; no adult-level misdirection)

**Varya's Scene Speech Profiles:**

| Scene Type | Active ER | Core SSP Rules (4-6 rules the author must follow) |
|---|---|---|
| Casual domestic scene, no domain topic | ER-LC | (1) Compound but not complex sentences. (2) Child vocabulary — no adult abstractions. (3) Emotional state visible and unmoderated. (4) Conclusions announced as discoveries, not framed as contributions. (5) CAR: at least one of the five child-anchor types must appear in her dialogue. |
| Governance / legal domain discussion, calm | ER-LC+PD | (1) Precise legal vocabulary permitted. (2) Multi-step domain reasoning permitted. (3) Emotional transparency NOT reduced — domain precision and emotional legibility coexist. (4) Conclusion announced as discovery, not offered as diplomatic contribution: "I found it" not "This may be worth noting." (5) Direct confidence in domain findings, not adult-register epistemic modesty. (6) CAR required: at least one child-anchor. |
| Governance discussion, excited about finding | ER-LC+PD-E1 | (1) Precision domain vocabulary mostly present but sentence structure simplifies. (2) Excitement leaks through — pace may accelerate, setup may be incomplete. (3) The finding is announced with less controlled framing than ER-LC+PD. (4) CAR satisfied by the emotional leakage itself. |
| Governance discussion, highly excited or frightened | ER-LC-E2 or ER-LC-E3 | (1) All domain uplifts degraded or suspended. (2) Vocabulary drops to Late Childhood baseline. (3) Reasoning Display loses complexity — one step at a time. (4) Emotional state fully readable from the text. (5) CAR satisfied by the emotional regression itself. |
| Required to conceal information | ISM: BR-LC profile | (1) Deflect by topic change or answer an adjacent question. (2) Use partial truths, not lies — the evasion must be technically defensible. (3) The evasion is detectable to Varys; it may not be detectable to others. (4) No adult-level misdirection — no strategic reframing, no performing transparency to conceal. |

**CAR Standing Requirement for Varya:** Every prose unit in which Varya speaks must contain at minimum one Child-Anchor Event of any of the five types. If no natural anchor presents itself, restructure the dialogue so one does. A Varya who speaks without any child-anchor in a full dialogue scene is a Varya who has ceased to read as a believable 8-year-old — regardless of how accurate her domain precision is.

### 14.6 — Retroactive Compliance Record (Proses 1–3)

Part 29 Area 17 (MHDAI) was not in force during the authoring of Proses 1-3. These proses are logged as **Pre-Index Prose** and are not subject to retroactive rewrite. The annotations below identify dialogue that would be calibrated differently under the MHDAI, for continuity awareness in Prose 4 forward.

**Prose 1 — "First Light, Sapphire Basin" (1.1.1.001.1.00001):** Varya does not appear in active dialogue. No compliance issues.

**Prose 3 — "The Ruling Meridian" (1.1.1.001.1.00003):** Varya does not appear in active dialogue. No compliance issues.

**Prose 2 — "Before He Was the King" (1.1.1.001.1.00002):** Varya has two primary dialogue blocks. Both are logged.

---

**Prose 2 Dialogue Block 1:**
> *"I finished reading it last night. I am making notes. There are provisions in Part Seven governing joint patrol arrangements for the Windward Range passes. The initiation language is ambiguous: it says 'either crown may initiate' a joint patrol review, but does not specify whether 'initiate' means proposing the review or formally opening the proceeding. I wanted to note it."*

**MHDAI Analysis:** Domain active (governance/treaty law) → ER-LC+PD applies. Legal precision is within profile. The vocabulary and multi-step reasoning are calibrated correctly for a prodigy in-domain. **CAR gap:** "I wanted to note it" is adult-register diplomatic understatement. A BR-LC prodigy at ER-LC+PD would close with a Pride Display or Directness Without Diplomacy: "I thought you might not have found it yet" (competitive pride, checks whether the discovery is exclusive) or "I thought it was important" (direct announcement of significance, no hedging). The measured understatement registers as adult epistemic modesty and removes the child-anchor.

**CAR status:** CAR unsatisfied in Block 1 in isolation. Block 2 assessment follows.

---

**Prose 2 Dialogue Block 2:**
> *"You said it was a matter you wanted resolved quietly. The Moon Sect does not operate in the open passes; they have reason to avoid examination. A quiet resolution and a party that avoids examination align. The deduction seemed reasonable."*

**MHDAI Analysis:** Domain active (governance, political inference) → ER-LC+PD. The reasoning chain (quiet resolution → party avoiding examination → alignment) is multi-step domain reasoning within profile. **CAR gap:** "The deduction seemed reasonable" is adult-register epistemic modesty ("seemed reasonable" is an adult's way of not over-claiming). A BR-LC prodigy would say "I thought the deduction was right" (direct confidence in own reasoning, no hedging) or "Was the deduction correct?" (direct confirmation-seeking — the child wants to know if they were right, without the adult diplomatic buffer of presenting their conclusion as merely "reasonable").

**CAR status:** Neither block satisfies CAR. Pre-Index Prose — no rewrite. **From Prose 4 forward: Varya's dialogue must contain at minimum one Child-Anchor Event per prose unit.**

---

**Continuity Note for Prose 4+:** The character established in Proses 1-3 is correct in capability — Varya's domain intelligence is established canon. The MHDAI adds the missing dimension: the child-anchoring that grounds her intelligence in an 8-year-old's emotional world. Prose 4 forward should feel like the same Varya — precise where she knows the territory — but with a visible humanity that makes the precision legible as something extraordinary coming from a child, not merely correct coming from a small adult.

### 14.7 — MHDAI Audit Hook

The MHDAI adds an audit dimension to the Pre-Output Audit (Pass 1) and Post-Write Audit (Pass 2) for every prose unit containing dialogue from a non-adult character (BR below BR-AU).

**MHDAI Audit Checklist (per prose unit):**
1. Is the ER correctly identified for each non-adult speaking character in the scene?
2. Does the dialogue conform to the ER's observable SSP rules?
3. For prodigy child characters: is the CAR satisfied by at least one of the five anchor types?
4. Does any emotional escalation trigger appropriate ESM regression in the dialogue?
5. Does any required concealment conform to the ISM profile for the character's BR band?

**MHDAI Failure Classes:**

| Error Class | Severity | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Non-adult character speaks at full adult register with no modifiers justifying it | MAJOR | Most common error. Author defaults to adult voice. Results in characters who sound unnaturally sophisticated for their age. |
| Prodigy child missing CAR in a dialogue-active prose unit | MAJOR | Prodigy precision present; child humanity absent. The character reads as a small adult rather than an extraordinary child. |
| ESM not applied during emotionally elevated scene | MINOR | Character maintains domain precision under conditions that would strip it. Reads as flat, inhuman, or inconsistent. |
| KDM uplift applied outside confirmed expert domain | MINOR | Character speaks with domain precision about a subject that has not been established as their domain. |
| ISM concealment above BR band capability | MAJOR | Character performs adult-level misdirection that their age cannot support. The evasion rings false — it is too smooth for the character's developmental stage. |
| CAR anchor type used without genuine grounding in scene | MINOR | An anchor event is present but feels inserted rather than organic. Anchors must arise from the scene's actual emotional conditions — not be manufactured to satisfy the checklist. |
