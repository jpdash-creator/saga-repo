## Area 9 — Battlefield Spatial & Cosmological Constraints

### 9.1 — Spatial Containment Principle

Every combat event has a **natural spatial footprint** determined by the power levels of the participants and the escalation tier. At low cultivation levels, this footprint is small and self-contained. At high cultivation levels — particularly at Grade 8 and above — the spatial footprint expands dramatically, and the environmental responses defined in Part 3, Area 3.3 are non-voluntary.

The Spatial Containment Principle states: combat should be contained to the minimum spatial footprint necessary for the engagement, with explicit recognition that containment becomes increasingly difficult and eventually impossible at high power levels. Deliberate expansion of the combat footprint beyond what the engagement requires is a force legitimacy violation.

### 9.2 — Battlefield Footprint by Cultivation Grade

The following are the natural combat footprints at each Heavenly Scale Grade. These footprints represent the area that is realistically affected by the combat's energy discharge, aura expression, and technique execution — not the area the combatants must occupy:

| Heavenly Scale Grade | Combat Realm | Natural Combat Footprint |
|---------------------|-------------|--------------------------|
| Grade 0–5 | Mortal Tier 1–2 | Tens of meters; human-scale environments |
| Grade 6–9 | Xiantian | Hundreds of meters to several kilometers |
| Grade 10–12 | Core | Several kilometers to tens of kilometers |
| Grade 13–15 | Divine Transformation–Heaven Treading | Tens to hundreds of kilometers |
| Grade 16 | Divine Ascension | Hundreds of kilometers; regional scale |
| Grade 17 | Divine Sea Realm | Continental to planetary scale |

**Containment Expectation vs Containment Requirement:**
At Grade 0–9, spatial containment to the immediate engagement zone is expected and achievable. At Grade 10–12, containment requires deliberate effort and may not be fully achievable in extended combat. At Grade 13–15, full containment may be impossible — the environmental responses (Part 3, Area 3.3, Grades 13–15) occur non-voluntarily and cannot be suppressed. At Grade 16–17, combat is inherently a regional or planetary event; the concept of "containment" shifts from physical to tactical (ensuring non-combatants are not in the affected zone rather than limiting the zone itself).

### 9.3 — Protected Zones in Combat

The following zones retain protection during combat at all escalation tiers unless formally designated as military objectives under the rules of E4 and above conflicts:

**PZ-1 — Civilian Settlements Not on Declared Battlefield:**
Any inhabited location that has not been declared part of a battlefield (in E3+ conflicts) or that is not directly within the engagement zone (in E1–E2) retains full civilian protection. Deliberate attacks on non-declared civilian settlements are Force Legitimacy violations regardless of escalation tier.

**PZ-2 — Sacred Grounds:**
As defined by Part 13 (Location Law). Sacred Grounds have inherent properties — power floors, power ceilings, dimensional anomalies — that make combat within them carry unique risks and restrictions. Combat in Sacred Grounds must comply with the specific restrictions of that Sacred Ground as defined in Part 13. Deliberate attack on a Sacred Ground that has not been declared a military objective may have in-world consequences beyond normal combat governance.

**PZ-3 — Recognized Medical Treatment Zones:**
Any location visibly identified as a medical treatment zone — marked by recognized healer symbols, faction medical corps insignia, or common cultivation society conventions — is protected. Attacking a treatment zone is a maximum-severity Force Legitimacy violation and one of the few combat actions that produces universal condemnation across cultivation society (Part 25).

**PZ-4 — Diplomatic Zones:**
Locations where recognized diplomatic functions are occurring (peace negotiations, envoy reception, formal ambassador meetings) are protected from combat action. Violation of a diplomatic zone is equivalent to attacking diplomatic personnel (Area 4.5, PS-4) and is treated as an act of war.

**PZ-5 — Declared Evacuation Corridors:**
In E3+ conflicts where battlefield declaration has designated civilian evacuation routes, those corridors are protected from combat action for the duration of their declared function.

**PZ-6 — Historical and Cultural Preservation Sites:**
Sites with recognized historical, cultural, or cultivation heritage significance. These sites have partial protection — they cannot be deliberately targeted without specific military necessity justification in E4+ conflicts. Deliberate cultural destruction without military justification carries severe consequence weight under Part 27.

### 9.4 — Battlefield Declaration Protocol

In E3 and above conflicts, before major combat operations begin, a **Battlefield Declaration** must be established:

**Declaration Components:**
1. Designation of the primary combat zone
2. Identification of protected zones within or adjacent to the combat zone
3. Establishment of evacuation corridors
4. Notification to non-combatant parties that the designated zone is now a combat area

**Declaration Timing:**
Declaration must precede the opening of major operations by sufficient time to allow evacuation — in practice, hours to days depending on the scale of the protected zone and the speed of the combatants involved. Emergency combat conditions (defensive response to sudden invasion) may require declaration simultaneously with combat initiation, in which case the declaration requirement is met as soon as practically possible.

**Declaration Authority:**
Any party at E3 and above with command authority over their forces may declare a battlefield. Opposing parties need not agree to the declaration's scope, but the declaration binds the declaring party to respect the designated zones.

### 9.5 — Cross-Dimensional and Cross-Realm Combat

Combat that extends into different dimensional spaces (void spaces, spirit planes, the divine sea external layer, or dimensions defined by Part 13) is not fully governed by mortal spatial laws. The following rules apply:

**Rule 9.5.1 — Entry Legitimacy:**
Forcing a combat into a dimension where the opposing party cannot follow is a valid strategic action in E1–E4 but constitutes abandonment of the engagement by the party who entered the dimension alone. If only one party can access the dimensional space, the engagement cannot continue as mutual combat.

**Rule 9.5.2 — External Resolution:**
A combat that resolves entirely in another dimension produces its outcome in that dimension. Consequences (Part 27) apply in both the dimension where the combat occurred and in the mortal realm, as appropriate.

**Rule 9.5.3 — Return Obligation:**
A combat that enters a dimension does not terminate by virtue of dimensional crossing. If both parties survive the dimensional engagement, they may return to mortal space; the engagement status depends on the dimensional combat's termination conditions (Area 12).

### 9.6 — Sacred Ground Power Rules in Combat

Sacred Grounds (Part 13) may impose power expression restrictions that affect combat:

- **Power Ceilings on Sacred Grounds:** If a Sacred Ground limits power expression to a certain Grade, combatants of higher Grades cannot express their full power within that Ground. Combat within a power-ceiling Sacred Ground is automatically governed by the ceiling level for intensity purposes.
- **Power Floors on Sacred Grounds:** If a Sacred Ground has a minimum power floor for entry (only cultivators above a certain Grade can enter), then combat within is inherently at that minimum power level.
- **Sacred Ground Consequence Multipliers:** Some Sacred Grounds amplify the consequences of violence within them — death, injury, or collateral damage in such locations carries greater consequence weight under Part 27.
