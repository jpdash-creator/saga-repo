## Area 6 — Civilization Type Taxonomy

### 6.1 — Overview

Every civilization has a CCT Designation (tier) and a Civilization Type (structural profile). Types are not value judgements — they are structural descriptions of how a civilization distributes its axis investments and what civilizational philosophy it embodies. Types can change over time; some CCT-level transitions come with characteristic type shifts.

Types are named using the structure: **[Dominant Axis]-[Character]**.

### 6.2 — Cultivation-Sovereign Type (CS-Type)

**Definition:** CA is the civilization's leading axis, typically 2+ levels above TA. Technology exists but serves cultivation. Institutions are organized around cultivation advancement as the primary social value.

**Strengths:**
- Exceptional cultivation power density; peak cultivators may significantly outclass equivalently-tiered technology civilizations
- Sacred Ground formation capability at comparatively lower CCT levels
- CPM resource access; natural Divine Liquid sites often within territory
- Soul-sense observation, memory, and knowledge preservation advantages

**Vulnerabilities:**
- Technology infrastructure gaps create administrative and logistical vulnerabilities at large territorial scales
- Cultivation reproduction bottlenecks: talent gates limit CA distribution at population scale
- Economic infrastructure often underdeveloped relative to cultivation infrastructure
- Susceptible to technology-based warfare from equivalently-tiered or lower-tiered TA-dominant civilizations

**Characteristic Tiers:** CS-Type is most common at Solarian through Galactic for cultivation-rich regions. Above Galactic, SA requirements force CS-Type civilizations to develop TA or stagnate.

**Example SA Profile:** CS-Type at Stellar (CCT-7) typically has SA-3 to SA-4 — early coordination, CPM as fuel, but not deep integration.

---

### 6.3 — Technology-Sovereign Type (TS-Type)

**Definition:** TA is the civilization's leading axis, typically 2+ levels above CA. Cultivation exists but is a minority practice — perhaps even a cultural curiosity. Institutions are organized around technological advancement and scientific discovery.

**Strengths:**
- Exceptional technology infrastructure density; can sustain more technology domains at peak performance
- Industrial, information, and military technology outputs exceed equivalently-tiered cultivation civilizations
- Large population capacity — TA-dominant civilizations often have significantly larger populations
- Energy conversion efficiency at technological energy scale is unmatched

**Vulnerabilities:**
- Zero or minimal CI Technology — SA-0 or SA-1 by definition; locked out of synergy categories
- Cannot access CPM-based energy, soul-sense research advantages, or Sacred Ground environments
- Absolute upper CCT limit of CCT-14 (SA-0 cap rule, Area 4.1.3) unless cultivation is adopted
- Vulnerable to soul-sense intelligence and perception advantages of CS-Type civilizations at equivalent tier

**Characteristic Tiers:** TS-Type appears across all CCT levels. Many civilizations that have never encountered cultivation remain TS-Type indefinitely. Some TS-Type civilizations transition to Balanced-Path once they achieve first contact with cultivation knowledge.

---

### 6.4 — Balanced-Path Type (BP-Type)

**Definition:** CA and TA are within 2 levels of each other. Neither axis dominates. SA is actively invested in. The civilization pursues cultivation and technology as co-equal development paths and integrates them systematically.

**Strengths:**
- Full access to synergy categories at the highest SA levels; Model D achievable
- No single axis vulnerability — no large gaps exposed
- Economic and institutional flexibility: can leverage both tracks as circumstances demand
- The Starward Saga's primary civilizational model; most protagonists emerge from BP-Type civilizations

**Vulnerabilities:**
- No axis dominance means no axis peak: a BP-Type civilization at CCT-10 has CA-10 and TA-10, while a CS-Type at CCT-10 may have CA-13 with TA-7 — individual cultivation power peaks lower
- Integration investment is expensive: SA development requires simultaneous dual-track infrastructure
- Cultural tension: the coexistence of cultivation and technology values creates ongoing social conflict (Part 31 Area 15.4)

---

### 6.5 — Energy-Sovereign Type (ES-Type)

**Definition:** EA is the civilization's leading axis by 3+ levels. The civilization commands extraordinary energy resources — either through massive CPM deposits, advanced energy technology, or both — while other axes lag behind.

**Strengths:**
- Energy exports as the primary diplomatic and economic lever
- Formation Zone territories of exceptional density; Divine Liquid formation at comparatively low CA
- Technology development in Energy domain (Part 8 Domain 7) at peak levels; energy technology exports

**Vulnerabilities:**
- Resource-dependency — other civilizations covet the energy sources; military vulnerability
- TA and CA may be underdeveloped; energy wealth creates complacency
- Energy surplus does not automatically translate to CCT advancement without corresponding CA/TA/LA growth

**Characteristic Tiers:** ES-Type often appears as a transitional type — civilizations with exceptional resource territories briefly show ES-Type characteristics before developing other axes to match.

---

### 6.6 — Territorial-Sovereign Type (LS-Type)

**Definition:** LA significantly exceeds other axes by 2+ levels. The civilization controls vast territory relative to its cultivation/technology depth — perhaps through conquest, expansion, or inheritance from a greater predecessor civilization.

**Strengths:**
- Strategic depth — vast territory makes total conquest difficult
- Resource access across wide territory; potential energy and cultivation sites within large domain
- Political prestige of territorial scale

**Vulnerabilities:**
- Administrative overreach — LA well above CA and TA means much of the territory cannot be effectively governed
- Regression risk — held territory degrades when infrastructure cannot sustain it
- CCT hard-cap applies: LA-8 with CA-4, TA-5 means CCT is capped at CCT-6 regardless of territorial claims

**Characteristic Tiers:** LS-Type frequently appears post-conquest or in declining civilizations. A civilization that was once Balanced at CCT-8 may become LS-Type at CCT-5 as CA and TA decline while LA is maintained through political inertia.

---

### 6.7 — Convergence-Path Type (CP-Type)

**Definition:** All five axes are within 1 level of each other. Perfect or near-perfect axis balance. The civilization has invested deliberately in uniform development across all dimensions.

**Strengths:**
- Maximum CCT efficiency — no axis gap penalty; CCT equals the weighted average of axes
- Full synergy access; maximum resilience to single-axis degradation events
- Extremely rare; Convergence-Path civilizations at high CCT levels are among the most formidable entities in the universe

**Vulnerabilities:**
- Achieving axis uniformity is extraordinarily resource-intensive; most civilizations cannot sustain it
- No specialist axis advantages; a Convergence-Path CCT-9 cannot match the peak cultivation power of a CS-Type CCT-9
