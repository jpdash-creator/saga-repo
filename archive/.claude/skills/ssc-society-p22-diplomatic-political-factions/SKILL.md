## Area 18 — Diplomatic and Political Factions

### 18.1 — Overview: Diplomatic and Political Factions (DPF)

Diplomatic and Political Factions are organizations whose primary purpose is the management of relationships between major entities — civilizations, large factions, alliances — through negotiation, treaty, representation, and organized political advocacy rather than through military force.

**Natural Scale Range:** Empire (SCL-6) through Universe (SCL-14)

### 18.2 — Diplomatic Faction Classifications

| Diplomatic Faction Type | Primary Function | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Ambassador Corps | Formal state representation at foreign powers | SCL-6 to SCL-9 |
| Inter-Civilization Council | Formal multi-civilization deliberative body | SCL-8 to SCL-12 |
| Treaty Arbitration Body | Neutral treaty negotiation and implementation monitoring | SCL-8 to SCL-14 |
| Trade Diplomacy Missions | Commercial treaty negotiation; trade route governance | SCL-7 to SCL-12 |
| Peace Facilitation Corps | Active conflict mediation; ceasefire negotiation | SCL-7 to SCL-13 |
| Multi-Civilization Political Alliance | Political cooperation bloc; shared policy advocacy | SCL-9 to SCL-14 |
| Galactic Senate | Galaxy-scale representative political body | SCL-11 |
| Universal Diplomatic Authority | Universe-scale governance and inter-civilization law | SCL-14 |
