## Area 7 — Intensity Containment Boundaries

### 7.1 — The Tier-Relative Intensity Principle

Every escalation tier has an **intensity ceiling** — the maximum scale of destructive force that is structurally appropriate for that tier. This ceiling is not a physical limit on what cultivators can do; it is a canon governance limit on what they may do within a given tier's framework without triggering an escalation violation.

The intensity ceiling is defined by the spatial footprint, participant count, and scope of destruction that is consistent with the tier's classification. A conflict cannot maintain its classification as an E1 Personal Combat if the destructive footprint of its actions would affect thousands of people and alter terrain at continental scale. Either the tier reclassifies upward, or an escalation violation has occurred.

### 7.2 — Intensity Ceilings by Tier

**E1 — Personal Combat Intensity Ceiling:**
Destructive force is contained to the immediate combat space — typically no more than tens of meters in radius for standard combatants, up to hundreds of meters for very high-grade cultivators exercising significant restraint. No deliberate structural destruction of civilian infrastructure. No terrain alteration that affects third parties not present. The fight's effects end at the edges of the immediate engagement zone.

**E2 — Skirmish Intensity Ceiling:**
Destructive force may affect a distinct combat location — a building, a section of forest, a stretch of road. Limited terrain alteration is acceptable. No deliberate targeting of structures beyond the immediate tactical objective. Environmental damage is contained to the skirmish zone.

**E3 — Engagement Intensity Ceiling:**
Deliberate tactical destruction of the engagement zone is permitted. Buildings, formations, fortifications within the declared engagement zone may be destroyed. Civilian structures within the engagement zone that have been evacuated under Area 9 protocols may be destroyed for military necessity. Destruction outside the declared engagement zone is a containment violation.

**E4 — Battle Intensity Ceiling:**
Broad strategic destruction within the declared battlefield is permitted. Multiple locations may be destroyed. Terrain can be significantly altered within the battlefield. No deliberate attacks on civilian population centers outside the military objective. The battle's destructive effects are to remain within the theater of operations.

**E5 — Campaign Intensity Ceiling:**
Regional-scale destruction capabilities are deployable under campaign doctrine. Multiple theaters may sustain significant destruction. The campaign's destructive effects may alter regional geography. No deliberate planetary-scale attacks without E6 reclassification.

**E6 — War Intensity Ceiling:**
Continental-scale destruction capabilities are deployable. Divine realm combat may occur, bringing with it the inherent environmental consequences of that power level (Part 3, Area 3.3, Grades 13–17). No cross-dimensional attacks that could permanently damage cosmological structures unless the story's current scale and world-state support this level of conflict.

**E7 — Annihilation Conflict Intensity Ceiling:**
No intensity ceiling under this Part. Location Law (Part 13) remains applicable. Beyond these constraints, an E7 conflict authorizes unlimited destructive deployment consistent with the in-world power scale of participants.

### 7.3 — Proportional Force Rule

A cultivator whose cultivation capability exceeds the intensity ceiling of the current escalation tier must exercise deliberate restraint to remain within the tier. A Divine Realm cultivator in an E1 duel is expected to calibrate their force expression to E1 parameters. This calibration is:

- **Culturally expected** in most cultivation societies for duels and personal combat (Part 25)
- **Canon-required** to maintain the engagement's tier classification
- **Not always achieved** — a cultivator who loses control of their output triggers the Involuntary Escalation rule

**Proportional Force Rule Compliance:**
A cultivator who maintains E1 force output despite having E6 capability is demonstrating exceptional control and cultural maturity. This is narratively valid and does not require mechanical justification. A cultivator cannot be "forced" by the rules to express their full power.

### 7.4 — Escalation Violations

An **Escalation Violation** occurs when a cultivator expresses force at a scale inconsistent with the current tier's intensity ceiling, unilaterally and without the implicit consent of the other parties (which would constitute escalation by agreement).

**Consequences of Escalation Violations:**
1. The conflict is automatically reclassified upward to the tier that matches the actual intensity expressed
2. The violating party bears sole responsibility for all consequences of the reclassification under Part 27
3. Third parties who become engaged due to the escalation violation did not consent to the conflict; their participation is a consequence of the violation, not an independent choice
4. In cultivation society, escalation violations carry severe social and political weight (Part 25)

**Involuntary Escalation (see also Section 5.5.2):**
An escalation violation caused by loss of power control (breakthrough state, emotional breakdown, Core destabilization) is classified as Involuntary Escalation. The consequences under Part 27 still apply — a cultivator who cannot control their power is responsible for its effects — but the social and cultural penalty is reduced relative to deliberate escalation.

### 7.5 — Intensity Floors in Formal Duels

A Formal Duel may include an agreed **intensity floor** — a minimum level of power expression required for the duel to be considered genuine. Intensity floors are most common in:
- Honor Duels where the point is to demonstrate capability, not merely survive
- Wager Duels where one party suspects the other of sandbagging to minimize risk
- Duels in which power ceiling agreements are combined with power floor agreements for a balanced engagement

Intensity floor violations (fighting significantly below the agreed floor without cause) are treated as duel invalidation by the violating party.

### 7.6 — Soul Technique Escalation Tier Constraints

Soul techniques (Weapon Soul, Defense Soul, Illusion Soul, Convert Soul, Devour Soul, Bind Soul, Resonate Soul, Nullify Soul — governed by Canon-Part 4 Area 2.7) operate in the **metaphysical layer** rather than the physical layer. This creates a special governance relationship with the escalation tier framework:

**Soul technique intensity is calibrated against soul rung differential rather than spatial footprint:**
Because soul techniques do not produce terrain-altering physical destruction, the standard spatial footprint metric (Area 7.2) does not directly apply. Instead, soul technique intensity within a given escalation tier is governed by the soul rung differential between attacker and target:

| Escalation Tier | Maximum Soul Technique Intensity | Soul Rung Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| E1 Personal Combat | Disorientation, pain, brief consciousness disruption | Soul rung differential ≤ 3; no permanent soul damage |
| E2 Skirmish | Consciousness suppression (temporary), technique disruption | Soul rung differential ≤ 4; no soul structure damage |
| E3 Engagement | Short-term memory disruption, soul exhaustion, temporary power access block | Soul rung differential ≤ 5; minor soul structure damage permitted |
| E4 Battle | Soul fragmentation (recoverable), extended power access block, emotional suppression | Soul rung differential ≤ 6; moderate soul damage permitted |
| E5 Campaign | Severe soul fragmentation, partial erasure of recent memories, semi-permanent consciousness suppression | No rung differential limit; permanent soul damage possible |
| E6 War | Soul destruction, permanent memory erasure, identity dissolution | No rung differential limit; soul death possible |
| E7 Annihilation | No soul technique constraints | Governed by Part 28 |

**Devour Soul special constraint:**
Devour Soul (Part 4 Area 2.7) is governed by a stricter escalation constraint than other soul techniques — its use at E1 through E3 is limited to soul force absorption that does not permanently diminish the target's soul structure. Sustained Devour Soul that causes permanent soul damage constitutes automatic escalation to E4 regardless of the physical footprint of the conflict.

**Soul technique invisibility:**
Because soul techniques leave no visible physical trace in most cases, escalation violations involving soul techniques are harder to detect than physical escalation violations. A cultivator who soul-attacks a target beyond the current tier's constraint may not trigger immediate social recognition of the violation — the target's visible suffering may appear to have physical causes. This invisibility makes soul technique escalation violations a canonical form of plausibly deniable escalation.

**Governance boundary:** Part 4 governs what soul techniques do and their mechanics. Part 5 governs the escalation tier constraints on their use in combat. Both must be simultaneously satisfied.
