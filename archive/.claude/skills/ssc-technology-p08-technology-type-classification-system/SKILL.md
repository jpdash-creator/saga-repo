## Rule Text (Verbatim Source — Authoritative)
## Area 2 — Technology Type Classification System

The Technology Type Classification System organizes all technology into fourteen functional domains. Each domain has twenty internal levels corresponding to the twenty-tier civilization scale. A civilization's level in each domain is assessed independently. The civilization's overall technology tier equals the majority-domain level — the level achieved in at least seven of the fourteen domains simultaneously.

**Notation:** When technology combines multiple domains, it is classified by its primary functional domain with secondary domains noted: *Industrial-8/Energy-7* (an industrial system powered by Energy-7 technology). When a technology's tier level in its primary domain is ambiguous, Part 8 adjudication defaults to the domain governing its primary operational function. If the operational function is contested, Area 13 disambiguation applies.

| Level 7 | **System Forge** | Outer system industrial operations; gas giant atmospheric resource extraction at industrial scale; deep-space manufacturing stations operating without any planetary support infrastructure; construction of planet-scale habitat arrays in deep solar orbit; complete solar system as integrated supply chain |
| Level 8 | **Stellar Fabrication** | Atomic-scale fabrication (placing individual atoms into designed configurations); matter-energy conversion for manufacturing (printing solid objects from energy substrates); exotic material production (metamaterials, topological materials, room-temperature superconductors); full stellar system as manufacturing domain |

| Level 11 | **Galactic Forge** | Stellar-scale industrial operations; megastructure component fabrication (Dyson sphere panels, orbital ring segments at full-galactic infrastructure scale); neutron-density material fabrication and shaping; industrial systems producing material at rates exceeding the output of entire planetary civilizations per second |

| Level 19 | **Dimensional Nexus** | Dimensional manufacturing — creating objects that exist across dimensional layers simultaneously; production systems anchored in the architecture of reality itself, not merely in physical space |
| Level 20 | **Absolute Forge** | Absolute manufacturing — creation from fundamental existence principles; manufacturing that operates at the level of what it means for anything to exist; the ceiling of what any industrial system can achieve |
