## Area 2 — Surface Transport Vehicles (ST-Band, Tiers 1–5)

### 2.1 — Vehicle Type Philosophy: ST-Band

Surface Transport vehicles exist within the gravitational well and atmosphere of a single planetary body. They are bound by atmospheric physics, terrain constraints, and the energy limits of pre-orbital technology. The ST-Band spans from the first wheeled cart to the hypersonic orbital shuttle — from Tier 1 primitive transport through Tier 5, where the boundary between atmosphere and orbit begins to dissolve.

ST-Band vehicles are organized into five operational categories: **Ground Vehicles**, **Watercraft**, **Atmospheric Craft**, **Hybrid Ground-Atmospheric**, and **Sub-Orbital Transitionals**. Each category has defined naming conventions unique to the Starward Saga universe.

---

### 2.2 — Tier 1 Ground Vehicles (ST-1/SC-I to SC-II)

**Category: Earthbound Conveyances (EBC)**

*EBC-1 Trailwalker:* Single-person wheeled or skid-based cart, hand-pulled or animal-pushed. No mechanical propulsion. SC-I. Used for personal goods transport in pre-road civilizations. Speed: 3–6 km/h. Range: terrain-constrained.

*EBC-2 Beastdray:* Animal-drawn wheeled platform of variable size. The standard heavy transport of Tier 1 civilizations. SC-I to SC-II. Pulled by domesticated draft animals (oxen, horses, water buffalo equivalents, or alien-equivalent beasts). Speed: 5–8 km/h sustained. Load capacity: 0.5–2 tons. The backbone of agricultural and trading economies.

*EBC-3 Ridebeast:* Animal-mounted transport — the cultivator's horse equivalent. SC-I (rider only). Speed: 10–60 km/h depending on beast training and cultivation enhancement. An EBC-3 ridden by a powerful cultivator has speeds modified by the rider's cultivation ability.

*EBC-4 Riverskiff:* Primitive watercraft for river and coastal transit. Dugout, reed-hull, or plank-bound construction. SC-I to SC-II. Propelled by oar, pole, or early sail. Speed: 3–12 km/h.

*EBC-5 Coastal Runner:* Early sailing watercraft, ocean-capable but without deep-water navigation. SC-II to SC-III. Lateen or square sail configurations. Speed: 5–15 km/h wind-dependent. Range: coastal routes, 200–500 km per day in favorable conditions.

---

### 2.3 — Tier 2 Ground and Water Vehicles (ST-2/SC-II to SC-IV)

**Category: Road-Bound Vessels (RBV)**

Tier 2 introduces systematic road infrastructure, bridge networks, and the social institution of organized trade routes. Vehicles become standardized in a way that Tier 1's artisanal constructions are not.

*RBV-1 Express Carriage:* Four-wheeled enclosed passenger carriage, horse-drawn on organized road networks. SC-II. The personal transit vehicle of the Tier 2 noble class and wealthy merchant. Speed: 20–35 km/h on maintained roads. Comfort features: suspension systems, enclosed passenger cabin, weather protection. In-world cultural significance: express carriage ownership signals social status in CPT-1 through CPT-4 civilizations.

*RBV-2 Merchant Hauler:* Large horse-drawn or oxen-drawn goods wagon with reinforced frame and weatherproofing. SC-III. The workhorse of Tier 2 commerce. Cargo capacity: 2–5 tons. Speed: 10–18 km/h. Typically travels in caravans for security, making the *Merchant Hauler convoy* a standard feature of Tier 2 trade culture.

*RBV-3 Relay Post Rider:* Single-rider courier system utilizing organized relay horse stations at 50–80 km intervals. SC-I. Courier-speed transit across road networks. Not a vehicle class as such — it is a *system* of EBC-3 Ridebeasts and road infrastructure. Speed: 300–500 km/day via relay chains. This is the fastest Tier 2 information and small-package transit system.

*RBV-4 River Barge:* Large flat-bottomed river watercraft for bulk cargo transit, horse-pulled or sail-assisted upstream, current-assisted downstream. SC-III to SC-IV. The most cost-efficient Tier 2 freight system for river-connected regions. Cargo capacity: 20–100 tons. Speed: 8–20 km/h downstream; 4–10 km/h upstream (towed or sailed).

**Category: Blue-Water Vessels (BWV)**

*BWV-1 Deep-Sea Merchantman:* Ocean-going sailing ship with full deep-water navigation capability, multi-mast configuration. SC-III to SC-IV. The flagship vessel of Tier 2 maritime trade. Cargo capacity: 100–800 tons. Speed: 10–20 km/h in favorable conditions. Duration at sea: weeks without resupply. Range: global ocean transit via prevailing wind routes.

*BWV-2 War Galleon:* Military configuration deep-sea vessel, armed with long-range projectile weapons (ballistae, early cannon equivalents), reinforced hull, elevated firing platforms. SC-IV. The Tier 2 apex naval combat vessel. Combat range: 200–500 m. Crew: 100–400. The BWV-2 War Galleon is the primary platform for naval E3–E4 escalation events in Tier 2 civilizations.

*BWV-3 Fast Courier Sloop:* Light, swift sailing vessel optimized for speed over cargo. SC-II to SC-III. The diplomatic, courier, and scout vessel of Tier 2 naval operations. Speed: 25–40 km/h in ideal conditions. Small crew (10–30), minimal cargo, maximum sail-to-hull ratio.

### 2.4 — Tier 3 Industrial Vehicles (ST-3/SC-III to SC-V)

**Category: Ironway Runners (IWR) — Rail Vehicles**

The introduction of steam power at Tier 3 creates the first technology-driven (non-biological-propulsion) vehicle class, and with it, the first standardized mass-transit network.

*IWR-1 Ironhull Freight Train:* Steam or early combustion engine locomotive pulling a chain of enclosed cargo wagons on fixed rail track. SC-IV as a complete train unit. Cargo capacity per train: 500–2,000 tons. Speed: 50–100 km/h. The defining infrastructure of Tier 3 continental economic integration. An *Ironhull* train requires the rail network to exist — without the network, the vehicle has no operational territory.

*IWR-2 Passenger Express:* Steam/combustion locomotive configured for passenger transit with enclosed heated/cooled cabins, defined schedules, multi-class boarding. SC-IV. Speed: 80–160 km/h on premium rail. Capacity: 200–1,000 passengers per train. The *Passenger Express* is the first truly mass public transit system — and in many Tier 3 civilizations, it becomes the backbone of the national identity, connecting previously isolated regional cultures.

*IWR-3 Military Transport:* Rail vehicle configured for rapid troop and equipment deployment. SC-IV to SC-V. Reinforced chassis, wide-gauge military rail. Can deploy a full military division (10,000+) across continental distances in 24–48 hours via coordinated rail network use.

**Category: Road-Bound Engine Vehicles (REV)**

*REV-1 Personal Groundrunner:* Early internal combustion engine personal vehicle (equivalent to early automobile). SC-I. Speed: 40–80 km/h (Tier 3 early) to 120–200 km/h (Tier 3 advanced). Range: 300–800 km per fuel fill. The first personal mechanized transport — a status object in Tier 3 societies before becoming a commodity.

*REV-2 Cargo Hauler Truck:* Heavy combustion/diesel engine freight vehicle for road-based cargo transit. SC-II to SC-III. Cargo capacity: 5–50 tons. Speed: 60–120 km/h. Replaces the Merchant Hauler as the primary overland freight vehicle where rail is absent.

*REV-3 Armored Warcrawler:* Early military armored vehicle — tracked or wheeled, with direct-fire weapon systems and armor plating. SC-II to SC-III. The Tier 3 ground combat vehicle. Speed: 30–80 km/h. Part 5 combat: the Warcrawler functions as a heavy combat asset at E3–E4 escalation range; its technology Force Category maps to a mid-Mortal Tier 2 equivalent in destructive output. The Warcrawler is categorized MIL/PAT.

**Category: Sky-Bound Craft (SBC) — Early Atmosphere**

*SBC-1 Light Airskimmer:* Early propeller or early jet aircraft, single to twin engine, limited range. SC-I to SC-II. Speed: 150–500 km/h. Range: 1,000–5,000 km. The first viable atmospheric transit beyond the ground. Used initially for reconnaissance and courier roles before civilian adoption.

*SBC-2 Transport Skycarrier:* Multi-engine atmospheric aircraft designed for passenger or cargo transit. SC-III. Speed: 400–900 km/h. Capacity: 20–200 passengers or 5–30 tons cargo. The first instrument of practical intercontinental transit without maritime infrastructure.

*SBC-3 War-Wing:* Military-configured fixed-wing aircraft with weapons systems, from early fighters to early bombers. SC-I to SC-III depending on configuration. Part 5 combat: air warfare enters the E3–E4 range at Tier 3; formation-level War-Wing operations enter E5 at Tier 3 apex. Classified MIL.

**Category: Deep-Water Vessels (DWV)**

*DWV-1 Steam Ironhull:* Steam-powered iron or steel-hulled oceangoing vessel replacing sail. SC-IV. Speed: 25–50 km/h. Cargo capacity: 2,000–15,000 tons. The industrial-era freighter that makes truly global trade economically viable.

*DWV-2 Ironhull Warship:* Steel-hulled, steam-powered military warship with long-range projectile and torpedo weapon systems. SC-IV to SC-V. The apex Tier 3 naval combat vessel. Direct successor of the BWV-2 War Galleon but orders of magnitude more destructive. Part 5 combat: fleet-scale naval combat at Tier 3 enters E5.

*DWV-3 Deeprunner Submarine:* Submarine vessel capable of extended submerged operations. SC-III to SC-IV. Speed: 30–60 km/h submerged. Depth: up to 500 m. The first stealth military naval platform — undetectable to surface vessels without acoustic detection infrastructure.

### 2.5 — Tier 4 Advanced Atmospheric Vehicles (ST-4/SC-I to SC-V)

**Category: Highwind Craft (HWC) — Advanced Atmosphere**

*HWC-1 Stratosleeker:* Advanced high-speed jet aircraft for personal, courier, or luxury transit. SC-I to SC-II. Speed: 800–1,500 km/h. Range: 5,000–15,000 km. The global-reach personal transit vehicle of Tier 4 — used by high-status individuals, government officials, and wealthy private parties.

*HWC-2 Mass Skyvessel:* Large jet aircraft for mass passenger transit. SC-III to SC-IV. Speed: 850–1,200 km/h. Capacity: 150–500 passengers. The backbone of Tier 4 global passenger transit. The HWC-2 is the vehicle that makes global transit accessible to non-elite populations for the first time.

*HWC-3 Cargo Skyvessel:* Jet-powered heavy cargo aircraft. SC-III to SC-IV. Cargo capacity: 50–300 tons. Speed: 700–1,000 km/h. Range: 8,000–20,000 km per flight.

*HWC-4 Precision Strike Warwing:* Advanced military jet aircraft with precision-guided weapon systems, electronic warfare capabilities, and stealth coating equivalents. SC-I to SC-II. Speed: Mach 2–4. The apex Tier 4 atmospheric combat vehicle. Part 5 combat: a single HWC-4 provides E3–E4 combat output; coordinated formations reach E5. Classified MIL.

**Category: Subterranean Rail (STR)**

*STR-1 Undercity Express:* Underground electromagnetic rail transit system for urban and metropolitan mass transit. SC-III to SC-IV (as a system). Speed: 80–200 km/h. Capacity per train: 200–2,000 passengers. The STR-1 is the backbone of Tier 4 urban mobility — its existence or absence defines urban livability at this tier.

*STR-2 Continental Velocity Rail:* High-speed magnetic levitation rail (maglev) for intercontinental passenger transit. SC-IV. Speed: 350–800 km/h. The fastest ground transit of Tier 4, competing with HWC-2 Skyvessel routes economically. Range: continental with transfer hubs.

**Category: Early Void Craft (EVC)**

*EVC-1 Orbital Climber:* Early rocket or electromagnetic launch vehicle capable of reaching low orbit. SC-II to SC-III. Not a true spacecraft — cannot maneuver in orbit; launch only. Speed at apogee: 7.9 km/s (orbital velocity). The first vehicle to leave the planetary atmosphere intentionally. Cultural significance: enormous — the EVC-1 Orbital Climber is typically the most-celebrated technological milestone of Tier 4, equivalent to the first ocean-crossing vessel for Tier 2 civilizations.

*EVC-2 Orbital Station:* Not a transit vehicle but an orbital infrastructure platform — the fixed base that early space-capable vehicles dock at. SC-IV to SC-V in scale. Governs the first permanent human presence off the surface.

**Category: Deepwater Apex Vessels (DAV)**

*DAV-1 Nuclear Ironhull:* Nuclear-powered naval warship of enormous scale and range. SC-V. Speed: 60–100 km/h. Operational duration: months without refueling. The apex Tier 4 naval combat platform, carrying aircraft, missile batteries, and electronic warfare suites. Part 5 combat: a single DAV-1 fleet operates at E5–E6.

*DAV-2 Deep Pressure Sub:* Nuclear or advanced-propulsion submarine capable of operating at extreme depths (>5,000 m). SC-III to SC-IV. The apex undersea stealth combat vehicle at Tier 4. Armed with long-range strike weapons, capable of operating undetected for months.

---

### 2.6 — Tier 5 High Atmosphere and Early Space Vehicles (ST-5/SC-I to SC-VI)

**Category: Ascendancy Craft (ASC) — Orbital Transition**

*ASC-1 Orbital Shuttle:* Reusable surface-to-orbit vehicle capable of routine transition between planetary surface and low orbital space. SC-III. Speed: 7.9–11 km/s during ascent. Transit time surface to orbit: 8–30 minutes. The first *routine* space access vehicle — not a research mission, but a transit commuter to orbital infrastructure.

*ASC-2 Skyhopper:* Hypersonic atmospheric transit vehicle capable of surface-to-surface planetary transit via ballistic arc or suborbital skip trajectory. SC-II to SC-III. Speed: Mach 10–25 (12,000–30,000 km/h). Any point on a planet: 30 minutes to 2 hours. The ASC-2 makes the planet feel small — a Tier 5 civilization with broad ASC-2 deployment has effectively connected all its surface populations into a single transit system.

*ASC-3 Interplanetary Scout:* Small, fast spacecraft capable of chemical or early ion drive transit between inner system planetary bodies. SC-II. Speed: 20–100 km/s. Range: inner stellar system. The first dedicated spacefaring vehicle with destination-specific mission profiles.

**Category: Planetary Defender Craft (PDC)**

*PDC-1 Atmospheric Defense Wing:* Advanced atmospheric military aircraft capable of operating at the edge of atmosphere and contributing to orbital defense. SC-II to SC-III. The Tier 5 military vehicle that bridges ground air defense and orbital defense. Part 5: E4–E5.

*PDC-2 Early Orbital Defense Platform:* Fixed or semi-mobile orbital weapons platform. SC-IV to SC-V. The first dedicated military infrastructure in space. Not a transit vehicle — a weapon system in orbit. Part 5 combat: orbital defense platforms enter E5–E6 range.

**Category: Surface Mastery Vehicles (SMV)**

*SMV-1 Groundmaster Maglev:* Magnetic levitation surface transport with intercontinental routing and speeds exceeding conventional aircraft for surface-bound routes. SC-IV. Speed: 1,000–5,000 km/h on dedicated tubes. Any surface point on the planet: hours. The SMV-1 makes surface transit competitive with atmospheric routes by eliminating all terrain constraints via tube/tunnel networks.

*SMV-2 Deep Arcology Vessel:* Mobile surface habitat capable of sustained cross-terrain travel — a city that moves. SC-V to SC-VI. Used in hostile surface environments, military deployment zones, or frontier planetary surfaces where fixed settlement is premature. Speed: 5–50 km/h depending on terrain system.
