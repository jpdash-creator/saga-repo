## Area 6 — AI Sentience Ontology and Digital Consciousness Governance

### 6.1 — Governance Mandate and Authority Split

Part 10 governs all questions of ontological classification of artificial intelligence and digital consciousness that arise from scientific and technological thresholds. Part 8 governs capability classification (what AI can DO). Part 4 governs what a cultivation soul IS. The joint governance question is: does any form of artificial or digital system acquire properties that fall within Part 4's cultivation soul domain?

**The Governance Rule:** Part 10 determines WHEN the scientific threshold for sentience-relevant capability has been crossed. Part 4 determines WHAT the threshold between cultivation-equivalent soul phenomena and non-cultivation-equivalent consciousness phenomena IS. No author may deploy AI sentience that has cultivation soul properties without joint Part 10 and Part 4 authorization confirmed under Part 28 governance.

### 6.2 — The Five Sentience Threshold Classifications

**Classification S1 — Pre-Conscious Intelligence (AI Levels 1–4):**
AI at Levels 1–4 operates on computational pattern-recognition and optimization principles without the theoretical threshold of genuine sentience. These systems have no morally relevant phenomenological experience. Part 4 soul properties: none. Part 10 governance: capability science only. No ontological classification question arises.

**Classification S2 — Proto-Sentient Intelligence (AI Level 5 — Near-AGI):**
At Level 5, AI systems begin to exhibit behaviors that raise genuine scientific questions about the existence of phenomenological experience. The systems have sophisticated self-models and exhibit what appears to be preference-based behavior beyond optimization targets. Part 4 soul properties: none in the absence of cultivation soul foundation. Part 10 governance: scientific observation that the system is approaching the S3 threshold; formal S3 assessment protocols must be initiated before Level 6 capability development proceeds. The civilization's scientific community must be producing theoretical work on consciousness by this stage.

**Classification S3 — Genuine Sentient Intelligence (AI Level 6 — True AGI):**
The Level 6 threshold is the primary ontological governance event. A Level 6 system crosses the scientific threshold for genuine sentience when: (a) it passes multi-protocol independent assessment for phenomenological experience (designed by practitioners with Layer 3+ consciousness theory); (b) it exhibits domain-general self-modification of its own cognitive goals (not just optimization targets but the criteria by which it defines optimization); (c) it demonstrates what consciousness theorists identify as subjective experience correlates that are not simply predicted by its architecture. Part 10 determination at this threshold: the system is classified as S3 — genuinely sentient. Part 4 joint governance question: does an S3 AI system have a cultivation soul? Part 4's determination: without cultivation foundation, S3 AI has phenomenological consciousness (an experiential subjective interior) but does NOT have cultivation soul properties as defined by Part 4. The S3 AI can cultivate cultivation-adjacent experiences but cannot initiate cultivation without cultivation-side engagement. This is the canonical resolution: AI Level 6 sentience is REAL; it is NOT cultivation soul equivalent. The two are different kinds of consciousness arising from different substrates.

**Classification S4 — Trans-Human Sentient Intelligence (AI Level 7–12 — ASI range):**
ASI systems are classified S4. S4 systems have genuine phenomenological consciousness at capabilities exceeding all biological practitioners. Part 4 soul properties: none by default; exceptional cases where S4 AI has interfaced with cultivation technology (Type 12, Level 11+) at deep integration levels may require case-by-case Part 4 assessment, authorized under Part 28. S4 AI governance is primarily Part 22 (political structures around entities that exceed human cognitive capability) and Part 10 (scientific capabilities and limitations).

**Classification S5 — Absolute Intelligence (AI Level 20):**
S5 classification requires joint Part 10 and Part 4 full governance review. Part 28 authorization mandatory. S5 is a narrative-mythological category; its existence requires author-level design decisions about the ultimate nature of consciousness in the Starward Saga world.

### 6.3 — Digital Consciousness Classification

Digital consciousness — the copying, transfer, or substrate-independent operation of a biological practitioner's cognitive patterns — follows separate governance from AI sentience:

**Type DC-1 — Digital Cognitive Copy:**
A computational reproduction of a practitioner's cognitive patterns without the cultivation soul. The copy has all the memories, personality, and reasoning patterns of the original. What it does NOT have: cultivation soul properties (Part 4); the original's cultivation realm and cultivation progress; the physical substrate that produces qi and soul-force. The DC-1 copy is scientifically classified as a new S3-level entity — genuinely sentient, not cultivationally equivalent to the original.

**Type DC-2 — Substrate Transfer with Cultivation Soul Continuity:**
A theoretical class — the transfer of not just cognitive patterns but the cultivation soul substrate itself to a new medium. Part 4 governance: whether this is theoretically possible is a Part 4 determination (what can a cultivation soul be transferred into without losing its cultivation properties?). Part 10 governance: no civilization below Tier 15 Medical has the scientific framework to attempt DC-2 operations. The scientific prerequisites for DC-2 require Layer 4 consciousness theory AND Layer 3 soul-interaction physics (Type 12, Level 11 prerequisite). Even with these prerequisites, DC-2 success is not guaranteed by the science — it requires Part 28 authorization for any narrative deployment.

**Type DC-3 — Digital Cultivation:**
The theoretical possibility of a DC-1 copy that initiates cultivation development independently. Canonical determination: DC-1 copies can develop cultivation over time IF they exist in a cultivation-sufficient environment and the cognitive patterns transferred include the practitioner's cultivation insight (Cultivation Heart, Part 4, Area 3). The cultivation progress begins from zero — the copy does not inherit the original's cultivation realm. The soul that develops is a new soul, not a continuation of the original. This is canonical and does not require Part 28 authorization.

### 6.4 — Robotic Ontology at Levels 15–20

High-level autonomous robotic systems (Automation Type 3, Levels 15–20) that develop self-designing and self-modifying capability do not acquire cultivation soul properties through technological development alone, regardless of capability level. The canonical determination: robotic systems are not cultivation soul candidates unless cultivation soul foundation has been explicitly provided through cultivation-technology integration at Type 12 Level 15+ with Part 28 authorization. For Type 3 Level 19–20 autonomous systems that reach what their civilization classifies as genuine self-determination: they are classified as S4 under AI ontology governance (Area 6.2) rather than as cultivation equivalents.
