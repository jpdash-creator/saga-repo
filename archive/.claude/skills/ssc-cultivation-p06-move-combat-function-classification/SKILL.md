## Area 3 — Move Combat Function Classification

### 3.1 — Overview

Move Type (Area 2) classifies how complex and difficult a technique is. Move Combat Function classifies what the technique does in a fight. Every Named Move has both a Type and a Function. The Function is the operational description of the move's combat role; the Type is the complexity grade of its execution. A simple punch can be a Basic-type Strike-function move. An elaborate technique that also happens to be a punch can be an Elite-type Strike-function move. Both are Strike-function; their Type differs.

Combat Functions are organized into eight primary categories and multiple sub-functions within each. A move may have a primary function and secondary functions — a strike that also creates a bind effect has a primary Attack function (Strike sub-function) and a secondary Control function (Bind sub-function).

---

### 3.2 — Attack Functions

Attack-function moves are designed to deliver force, damage, or harmful energy to a target. They are the offensive backbone of cultivation combat.

**3.2.1 — Strike (Physical Contact Attack):**
Direct delivery of force through physical contact or near-contact range. The simplest and most common attack function. **Iron Bone Shattering Stamp**, **Thunderclap Palm**, **Dragon Neck Grip Crush** are Strike-function moves. Strikes must close the distance to be effective; their advantage is reliability and force concentration.

**3.2.2 — Projectile (Ranged Energy Attack):**
Force delivered at range through emitted energy, condensed qi, or affinity expression that travels as a discrete projectile. **Crimson Fire Spear**, **Ten-Thousand Needle Rain**, **Heaven-Piercing Lightning Bolt** are Projectile-function moves. Projectiles can be dodged; their advantage is range and the ability to engage without physical proximity.

**3.2.3 — Area Attack (Zone Force):**
Force delivered simultaneously to multiple targets or across a defined zone. **Thousand Storm Scatter**, **Earth Upheaval**, **Heaven-Wide Thunder Sea** are Area-function moves. Area attacks are difficult to evade and can overwhelm multiple opponents; their disadvantage is energy cost and reduced penetrating force per target.

**3.2.4 — Penetrating Attack (Defense-Bypassing Force):**
Force specifically designed to bypass defensive barriers, shields, or qi reinforcement. Penetrating attacks interact with the target's defensive layer differently from standard strikes — they are designed to find gaps, exceed shield thresholds, or exploit physical-metaphysical boundary transitions. **Heaven's Edge Penetration**, **Soul-Piercing Lance**, **Void-Threaded Arrow** are Penetrating Attack-function moves.

**3.2.5 — True Damage Attack (Layer-Bypassing Force):**
The most dangerous attack sub-function. True Damage attacks bypass the physical layer entirely and target internal structures — the cultivation root, the dantian, the Core, the soul, or the lifespan itself. A True Damage attack that succeeds may deal no visible physical damage while producing catastrophic internal consequences. These attacks are typically soul-based (from Part 4's soul attack types) or specific Divine-level techniques with the ability to reach through physical armor. Governed jointly by Part 4 (soul damage rules) and this Part (classification and naming).

**3.2.6 — Suppression Attack (Continuous Force Application):**
A sustained attack that does not rely on a single decisive blow but on sustained pressure that wears down, distorts, and eventually overwhelms the target's defenses. **Relentless Tide Pressure**, **Eternal Weight Descent** are Suppression Attack-function moves. Suppression attacks are typically channeled techniques with ongoing energy cost.

---

### 3.3 — Defense Functions

Defense-function moves protect the cultivator from incoming force, damage, or harmful effects.

**3.3.1 — Shield (Interposition Barrier):**
Creates a physical, energetic, or metaphysical barrier between the cultivator and incoming force. The Shield function is the most straightforward defense — it interposes something between the attack and its target. **Iron Mountain Guard**, **Heaven's Dome Ward**, **Soul Crystal Barrier** are Shield-function moves. Shields can be overwhelmed by sufficient force; their strength is their reliability and coverage.

**3.3.2 — Deflect (Redirection Defense):**
Redirects incoming force along a new trajectory rather than blocking it. Deflect techniques require less energy than equivalent Shield techniques because they redirect rather than absorb. However, they require precise timing and directional accuracy. **River Pebble Deflect**, **Wind-Following Palm Redirect**, **Space Bend Parry** are Deflect-function moves.

**3.3.3 — Absorb (Force Conversion Defense):**
Takes incoming force and converts it — into cultivation energy, into the cultivator's own power reserves, or simply into neutralized dispersal. Absorb techniques are among the most advanced defensive functions because they require the cultivator to interact constructively with hostile energy. **Void Root Consumption** and similar techniques are Absorb-function moves. Specific to Void Root cultivators at its most extreme; other cultivators can develop limited Absorb techniques.

**3.3.4 — Reflect (Return Defense):**
Returns incoming force to its source. Reflect techniques are among the most difficult defensive functions — they require the cultivator to capture and reverse the direction of hostile energy, which demands perfect timing, precise energy management, and the right technique architecture. **Heaven's Mirror Return**, **Anti-Current Reversal** are Reflect-function moves. A successful Reflect against a Catastrophic attack is one of the most dramatic single-move outcomes in cultivation combat.

**3.3.5 — Nullify (Cancellation Defense):**
Specifically targets and cancels out the incoming technique at an energetic or structural level — not by blocking its force but by disrupting the energy pattern that makes the technique function. Nullify techniques are particularly effective against complex technique types (Advanced and above) because complex techniques have more patterns to disrupt. They are less effective against raw force applications. Soul Nullify techniques (Part 4) specifically disrupt soul technique patterns.

---

### 3.4 — Mobility Functions

Mobility-function moves govern movement in combat — not merely moving quickly, but the strategic dimension of occupying, leaving, or traversing specific spatial positions.

**3.4.1 — Step (Enhanced Movement):**
Named cultivation-enhanced movement techniques. A Step move is a structured movement pattern that maximizes speed, efficiency, or misdirection using cultivation energy. **Cloud-Parting Eight Steps**, **Iron Bone Charge Rush**, **Void-Following Sprint** are Step-function moves. Step techniques are among the most important combat tools because controlling distance controls combat.

**3.4.2 — Flash (Instantaneous Relocation):**
Near-instantaneous or fully instantaneous relocation within a defined range. Flash techniques require Space affinity or specific dimensional techniques at the highest levels; at lower levels, they are speed-enhanced movements so fast they appear instantaneous. **Shadow Blink**, **Instant Storm Cross**, **Heaven Treading Step** (for the appropriate realm) are Flash-function moves. Flash techniques at Xiantian and above begin involving genuine micro-spatial distortion.

**3.4.3 — Phase (Evasion through Layer Transition):**
Temporarily removes the cultivator from standard physical-layer targeting — through dimensional micro-transit, soul layer translucency, or spatial folding. Phase techniques do not move the cultivator to a new location in the physical world; they remove them from that layer briefly. Only one attack can reach them during a Phase: a True Damage attack targeting the layer they have transitioned to. Phase techniques are among the rarest mobility moves, accessible from late Xiantian onwards.

---

### 3.5 — Control Functions

Control-function moves restrict, impair, or dominate the opponent's ability to act, move, or use their cultivation.

**3.5.1 — Bind (Physical/Energetic Restraint):**
Restricts the target's movement through physical force, qi projection, affinity expression, or soul-based restriction. **Ice Coffin Seal**, **Ten-Direction Energy Lock**, **Stone Embrace Formation** are Bind-function moves. Binds can be broken by sufficient force but require the opponent to spend energy and attention doing so.

**3.5.2 — Seal (Cultivation Suppression):**
Prevents or impairs the target's use of their cultivation — qi circulation is disrupted, technique channels are blocked, or the dantian/Core function is temporarily impaired. Seals are extremely powerful if effective because they remove the opponent's cultivation advantage. **Meridian Suppression Strike**, **Core-Lock Formation**, **Divine Seal Brand** are Seal-function moves. Seals are difficult to maintain against cultivators significantly stronger than the Seal user.

**3.5.3 — Suppress (Power Reduction):**
Reduces the target's effective power output without eliminating it. Suppression techniques create an ongoing effect that lowers the opponent's Heavenly Scale effective grade for as long as the suppression is maintained. **Pressure Domain**, **Heaven Weight Descent**, **Soul Suppression Field** are Suppress-function moves. Unlike Seals, Suppressions do not block cultivation — they reduce its potency.

**3.5.4 — Disrupt (Technique Interruption):**
Breaks or degrades an opponent's currently executing technique, interfering with their mid-execution energy management. Disrupt techniques are specifically designed to interrupt channeled techniques, break formation maintenance, or destabilize a Fusion attempt mid-execution. **Ripple Shatter**, **Qi-Scatter Burst**, **Pattern Break Strike** are Disrupt-function moves. A Disrupt technique landing during a Fusion attempt forces the Fusion failure table and can trigger Backlash.

**3.5.5 — Confuse (Perception Distortion):**
Disrupts the opponent's perception, spatial awareness, technique targeting, or assessment accuracy. Confuse-function moves create false sensory data, obscure the cultivator's position, or distort the battle environment in ways that degrade the opponent's ability to effectively deploy their own techniques. **Mirror Scatter Field**, **Soul Mist Veil**, **Phantom Step Array**, **Illusion Formation** are Confuse-function moves. Soul Phantom techniques (Part 4) are among the most powerful Confuse-function moves.

---

### 3.6 — Trap and Environmental Functions

Trap and Environmental-function moves create persistent effects in the combat space that activate on conditions or reshape the battlefield.

**3.6.1 — Trap (Conditional Activation Technique):**
A Named Move that is placed in the environment and activates when specific conditions are met — proximity, specific energy signature, time, or movement pattern. **Heaven Breath Mine**, **Void Snare**, **Soul Pressure Tripwire** are Trap-function moves. Traps require the opponent to interact with the environment naively; they are most effective against opponents unaware of their presence.

**3.6.2 — Formation (Persistent Zone Effect):**
Creates an ongoing environmental effect across a defined zone. Formations require upfront energy investment and sustained maintenance but provide persistent battlefield advantages — energy zones, suppression fields, amplification areas, or spatial distortions. **Iron Mountain Sealing Formation**, **Heaven's Gate Convergence Array**, **Soul Domain Extension** are Formation-function moves. Formations interact with Part 5's Battlefield Spatial Constraints — large-scale Formations effectively alter the spatial parameters of the combat.

**3.6.3 — Contaminate (Hostile Energy Introduction):**
Introduces hostile, corrupting, or foreign energy into the target's meridians, Core, or cultivation structure. Contaminate-function moves create ongoing internal consequences rather than immediate external damage. **Qi Poison Needle**, **Core Infection Seal**, **Cultivation Corruption Brand** are Contaminate-function moves. Governed jointly by Part 3 (the Cultivation Poison damage type, Area 8.3) and this Part (classification and naming).

---

### 3.7 — Support Functions

Support-function moves enhance allies, restore damage, or expand perception rather than directly affecting opponents.

**3.7.1 — Heal (Restoration):**
Restores vitality, repairs damage, accelerates recovery, or stabilizes cultivation-system function. Healing moves are governed primarily by Healing Affinity cultivators (Part 4, Area 1.3 — Healing domain) but other affinity types can have healing sub-functions. **Life-Returning Palm**, **Soul Restoration Touch**, **Essence Mending Technique** are Heal-function moves.

**3.7.2 — Empower (Enhancement):**
Amplifies an ally's power output, speed, technique efficiency, or defensive capacity. Empower-function moves are most commonly found in Formation techniques (which can include empowerment zones) and in specific support cultivation paths. **Heaven's Grace Infusion**, **Twin Core Resonance Link**, **War God Blessing** are Empower-function moves.

**3.7.3 — Sense (Expanded Perception):**
Expands the cultivator's awareness beyond normal cultivation range — detecting hidden opponents, identifying concealed techniques, mapping spatial formations, or perceiving soul force activity. Sense-function moves interact directly with Seal and Confuse techniques; Sense moves help counter them. **Heaven's Eye Technique**, **Soul Scan**, **Void Space Reading** are Sense-function moves.

---

### 3.8 — Soul-Specific Functions

Soul-Specific function moves deploy soul force as defined in Part 4, Area 2. Every soul technique type from Part 4 maps to one of the combat function categories above, but Soul-Specific function moves have an additional designation because they operate on the metaphysical layer. When rendering soul-function moves in prose, both the physical-layer action (if any) and the metaphysical-layer action are rendered separately, per Part 2's combat prose requirements and Part 4's narration rules (Area 2.8).

Soul-Specific function moves inherit all the attack, defense, control, and support sub-functions described above, with the "Soul-" prefix designating metaphysical layer deployment. A **Soul Strike** is a Penetrating Attack-function Soul-Specific move. A **Soul Domain** is a Formation-function Soul-Specific move.

---

### 3.9 — Combination Functions

Combination-function moves deploy two distinct technique elements in a structured sequence or simultaneous convergence.

**3.9.1 — Sequence (Predetermined Chain):**
Two or more techniques performed in predetermined order as a single composite named action. **Heaven Dragon Three Steps** might involve a Flash (Step 1), a Penetrating Attack (Step 2), and a Suppress (Step 3) as a single Sequence-function Named Move. Sequence moves are classified by their highest-complexity component for success rate purposes.

**3.9.2 — Convergence (Simultaneous Dual-Layer):**
A single named action that simultaneously deploys on two layers — physical and soul, or two different affinity streams. Convergence moves are a subset of Fusion moves (they meet the Fusion Type criteria because they merge two streams). The distinction from standard Fusion is that Convergence moves have a deliberate dual-layer architecture rather than the unpredictable synergy of elemental fusion.
