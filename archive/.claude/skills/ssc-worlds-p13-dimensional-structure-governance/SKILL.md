## Area 12 — Dimensional Structure Governance

Beyond physical space, the Starward Saga universe contains dimensional layers that interact with physical space at specific locations.

### 12.1 — Dimensional Layers

**Physical Dimension:** The standard three spatial dimensions plus time. Where all normal existence occurs. The domain of Part 13's standard location classification system.

**Qi Dimension / Cultivation Space:** The dimensional substrate through which cultivation energy flows. Not physically traversable by ordinary beings but perceptible to cultivators through qi sense. At very high cultivation development, physical-and-qi-dimensional overlap becomes achievable.

**Soul Dimension:** The dimensional substrate where soul constructs, spiritual entities, and soul techniques operate. Soul sense operates directly in the soul dimension. Soul attacks traverse the soul dimension. Part 4 (Affinity, Heart and Soul Law) governs soul dimension mechanics from the cultivation side.

**Extradimensional Pockets:** Small stable dimensional spaces detached from standard physical coordinates. Created by artifact construction (Part 12, Area 9), dimensional technique users, or natural spatial anomalies.

**Stable Dimensional Rifts:** Persistent connections between two non-adjacent physical locations or between dimensions. Of great strategic value. Formation, governance, and closure rules follow Rule 12.2 below.

**Unstable Dimensional Tears:** Dangerous openings in spacetime caused by extreme energy events, cultivation technique misuse, or dimensional pressure. Unlike stable rifts, tears are uncontrolled and may expand, collapse, or shift.

**Void Zones:** See Area 11.

**Resonance Planes:** Cultivation-adjacent dimensional zones associated with specific affinities. Fire Plane, Ice Plane, Soul Plane — dimensional spaces where the corresponding cultivation energy is the dominant medium. Access to Resonance Planes is governed jointly by Part 4 (affinity mechanics) and Part 13 (spatial properties).

### 12.2 — Dimensional Rift Rules

**Rule 12.2.1 — Stable Rift Formation:** A stable dimensional rift requires a specific formation event — either extreme energy convergence at a cosmologically significant location, deliberate dimensional formation by an appropriately capable practitioner, or an artifact of Part 12 Tier 4+ classification.

**Rule 12.2.2 — Rift Permanence:** Once formed, a stable rift persists until deliberately closed or until a disrupting event of sufficient scale. It cannot be accidentally opened or closed.

**Rule 12.2.3 — Rift Strategic Value:** Stable rifts connect two fixed points in space. They cannot be moved. Controlling both endpoints grants strategic access that no other mechanism replicates. Rifts are among the most contested spatial features in the Starward Saga.

**Rule 12.2.4 — Unstable Tear Hazard:** An unstable tear is a spatial emergency. It does not connect two known points — it opens into uncertain dimensional territory. Entry is potentially fatal. Any faction with capability will attempt to seal an unstable tear, as its expansion can damage surrounding physical space.
