## Area 15 — Cross-Part Interaction Governance

### 15.1 — Part 3 (Power and Lifespan Law)

**Governed jointly:**
- Cultivation consequence for harmful actions (Part 27 establishes the consequence; Part 3 governs the physical cultivation mechanics)
- Realm regression as consequence (Part 3 governs the physical reality; Part 27 governs the causal chain)
- Physical injury consequences (Part 3 governs injury mechanics; Part 27 governs consequence propagation from injury events)
- Extended lifespan consequences — the specific consequences that arise from outliving everyone you know (joint Part 3 lifespan mechanics and Part 27 consequence framework)

### 15.2 — Part 4 (Affinity, Heart, Soul Law)

**Governed jointly:**
- Heart ceiling as consequence of moral transgression (Part 4 governs the Heart power mechanics; Part 27 establishes this as a consequence of action)
- Soul technique consequence for violation use (Part 4 governs the soul; Part 27 governs the consequence of misuse)
- Heart awakening as consequence of genuine experience (Part 4 governs the awakening; Part 27 establishes it as a consequence of genuine action)

### 15.3 — Part 5 (Combat and Fight Law)

**Governed jointly:**
- Post-combat consequences (Part 5 governs the combat; Part 27 governs what follows)
- Escalation violation consequences (Part 5 classifies the violation; Part 27 governs the systemic consequences)
- Death in combat versus murder — different consequence profiles (joint governance)
- Collateral damage consequences (both Parts involved — Part 5 governs battlefield containment rules; Part 27 governs consequence for their violation)

### 15.4 — Part 26 (Psychological Law)

**Governing Boundary:**
- Part 26 governs the internal psychological experience of consequence events
- Part 27 governs the external consequence structure — what happened, what it caused, what is now different in the world
- These are the internal and external faces of the same event; both must be simultaneously satisfied for any significant action event in the narrative
- Joint governance: psychological consequences are both consequence events (Part 27) and psychological states (Part 26)

### 15.5 — Parts 21, 22, 23 (Civilization, Faction, Affiliation Laws)

**Governed jointly:**
- Civilizational consequences for civilizational-scale actions (Part 21 governs the civilization; Part 27 governs the consequence framework)
- Faction formation, dissolution, and betrayal consequences (Part 22 governs the faction mechanics; Part 27 governs the consequence structure)
- Affiliation breach and alliance dissolution consequences (Part 23 governs the affiliation; Part 27 governs the consequences)

### 15.6 — Part 25 (Culture and Social Law)

**Governed jointly:**
- Cultural destruction events — how they propagate through affected communities (Part 25 governs the cultural dimension; Part 27 governs the consequence architecture)
- How cultural context modifies consequence responses — honor cultures respond differently to betrayal than exchange-value cultures (Part 25 governs cultural context; Part 27 governs the consequence framework)
- Reputation consequences in cultural context (joint)

### 15.7 — Parts 33 and 32 (Weapon and Vehicle Law)

**Governed jointly:**
- Weapon deployment consequences by WS class (Part 33 governs weapons; Part 27 governs consequences)
- Vehicle destruction consequence cascades (Part 32 governs vehicles; Part 27 governs consequences)
- Autonomous weapon deployment consequences (Rule 33.12.1 — deploying faction bears full consequence under Part 27's consequence framework)
