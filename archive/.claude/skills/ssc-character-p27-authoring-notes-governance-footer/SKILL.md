## Area 19 — Authoring Notes and Governance Footer

**What Part 27 Adds to the Starward Saga:**
Part 27 closes the loop on everything else the Canon governs. Part 3 describes what a cultivator can do. Part 5 describes how they fight. Part 25 describes the world they live in. Part 27 describes what it all means: every action, every choice, every moment of heroism or failure — it echoes. It changes things. It has effects. The world is real because actions matter. Part 27 is the law that makes that real.

**The Partnership with Part 26:**
Part 26 (Psychological Law) and Part 27 (Consequence Law) are the two halves of the same governing principle: that living in the Starward Saga world has weight. Part 26 governs the inner weight; Part 27 governs the outer weight. Every significant event is simultaneously a psychological event (Part 26) and a consequence event (Part 27). Both must be satisfied.

**Governing Mandate Summary:**
- Every significant action produces tracked consequences across all five consequence domains
- Consequences scale with power, intent, role, and context
- No character is narratively immune from consequence
- Positive consequences are as canonical as adverse ones
- Consequences propagate through time and social systems — delay is not absence
