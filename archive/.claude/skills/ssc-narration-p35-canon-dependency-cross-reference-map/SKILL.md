**Part 36 Sovereignty Delineation (Canon v4.31):** Part 35 governs **content balance** — what proportion of each prose unit's word count is devoted to each content category, and whether Sections meet the Phase 1 target ranges. Part 36 governs **thematic quality of execution** — not how much combat content appears (Part 35), but how the combat that appears feels (Part 36). High CDL-2 combat scores are compatible with Part 36 violations. Low CDL-2 combat scores are compatible with Part 36 compliance. Neither substitutes for the other. Both must be satisfied simultaneously. The PPCI Block A appends a Part 36 NTL carry-forward line (Rule 10.5.12) alongside the CDL-2 CI block. Cross-reference: Part 36 Area 15.2 (Sovereignty Delineation — Part 36 vs. Part 35).
