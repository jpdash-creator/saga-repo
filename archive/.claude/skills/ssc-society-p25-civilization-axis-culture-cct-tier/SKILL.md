## Area 3 — Civilization Axis (CA-C): Culture by CCT Tier

### 3.1 — Overview

CCT tier is the macro-structural determinant of cultural complexity and cultural reach. The following table maps CCT tiers to their canonical Cultural Paradigm Class:

| CCT Tier | Civilization Name | Cultural Paradigm Class | Dominant Cultural Orientation | Characteristic Cultural Form |
|----------|-----------------|------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|
| CCT-1 | Subsistence | CPC-1 Survival-Collective | Group survival, elder wisdom | Oral tradition, survival rite |
| CCT-2 | Formative | CPC-2 Tribal-Sacred | Sacred lineage, spirit covenant | Totem, ancestor veneration |
| CCT-3 | Industrial | CPC-3 Labor-Stratified | Artisan guild, labor hierarchy | Guild culture, craft pride |
| CCT-4 | Planetary/Type I | CPC-4 Nation-Ideological | National identity, political ideology | National myth, civic ritual |
| CCT-5 | Multi-World | CPC-5 Imperial-Civilizational | Imperial identity, civilizational manifest | Imperial court culture, colonial mission |
| CCT-6 | Solarian/Type II− | CPC-6 Solar-Mythic | Stellar mythology, solar deity culture | Stellar calendar, solar rite |
| CCT-7 | Stellar/Type II | CPC-7 Interstellar-Explorer | Frontier culture, exploration ethos | Pilot culture, void-sailor myth |
| CCT-8 | Interstellar/Type II+ | CPC-8 Network-Cosmopolitan | Gate network culture, cosmopolitan identity | Spaceport culture, polyglot norm |
| CCT-9 | Expansionary | CPC-9 Domain-Administrative | Administrative culture, bureaucratic ritual | Court protocol, jurisdictional honor |
| CCT-10 | Clusteral/Type IIb | CPC-10 Clusteral-Philosophical | Philosophical tradition across star clusters | Grand school culture, debate tradition |
| CCT-11 | Galactic/Type III | CPC-11 Galactic-Institutional | Institutional culture, legacy preservation | Archive culture, lineage school |
| CCT-12 | Intergalactic/Type III+ | CPC-12 Cross-Galactic Pluralism | Multicultural synthesis | Cultural embassy, hybrid art |
| CCT-13 | Cosmological/Type IIIb | CPC-13 Cosmological-Contemplative | Cosmic philosophy, time-scale perspective | Contemplative order, deep-time meditation |
| CCT-14 | Superclusteral | CPC-14 Superclusteral-Synthetic | Meta-civilizational synthesis | Cultural observatory, meta-tradition |
| CCT-15 | Liminal-Universal | CPC-15 Liminal-Transcendent | Transition identity, threshold philosophy | Initiation order, liminal rite |
| CCT-16+ | Universal and beyond | CPC-16+ Absolute Cultural Forms | Transcendence of prior cultural categories | Part 28 authorization required |

### 3.2 — CPC Cultural Complexity Index

Each Cultural Paradigm Class (CPC) generates cultural complexity at a different scale. CPC-1 through CPC-5 produce locally coherent cultures that are internally consistent but limited in scope and vocabulary. CPC-6 through CPC-10 produce civilizations with rich multicultural internal variety. CPC-11 through CPC-15 produce civilizations that are themselves meta-cultural — they govern, compare, and synthesize cultural diversity at a civilizational level.

### 3.3 — Civilization Type Cultural Profiles

Each Part 21 civilization type generates a characteristic cultural temperament regardless of CCT:

| Civilization Type | Cultural Temperament | Primary Value Axis | Characteristic Social Convention |
|-------------------|---------------------|-------------------|----------------------------------|
| Cultivation Specialist (CS) | Hierarchical-Sacral | Spiritual achievement | Realm-based deference protocol |
| Technology Specialist (TS) | Meritocratic-Functional | Technical competence | Role-based title system |
| Balanced Practitioner (BP) | Syncretic-Adaptive | Dual-track achievement | Hybrid honorific structure |
| Extreme Specialization (ES) | Absolutist-Focused | Singular mastery | Purity culture, exclusion ritual |
| Latent Synthesis (LS) | Aspiration-Tension | Dormant integration potential | Cultural nostalgia, revival tradition |
| Cultivation-Primary (CP) | Cultivation-Dominant | Cultivation above all | Mandatory cultivation evaluation ceremony |
