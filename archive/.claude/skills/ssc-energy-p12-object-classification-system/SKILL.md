## Area 1 — Object Classification System

### 1.1 — The Four Object Classes

All objects governed by this Part fall into one of four classes. Classification is not about power level — it is about the nature of how the object stores and expresses energy.

---

**Class I — Relics**

A Relic is an object whose power was imprinted through extended immersion in extremely high-density energy fields rather than through deliberate crafting inscription. Relics form naturally (Naturally Formed Relics) or acquire power through proximity to a specific event or being over long periods (Historically Imprinted Relics). The key characteristic of a Relic is that its power architecture is holistic and emergent — it was not designed by a crafter but shaped by the energy conditions of its formation.

*Naturally Formed Relics:* Physical matter — stone, metal, crystal, bone, wood — that has spent centuries in a high-class Formation Zone (Part 11, Area 3; Part 13) and gradually absorbed CPM energy until it achieved a structural crystallization. These are not CPM substances — they are objects that have absorbed so much CPM energy over time that their physical structure has been permanently altered. The result is an object that continues to emit, focus, or channel energy without requiring an external power source. Natural Relics are rare, unpredictable in function, and cannot be fully specified in advance.

*Historically Imprinted Relics:* Objects that have acquired power through prolonged contact with an extremely powerful cultivator, presence during cosmological events (major battles, Divine Sea Realm manifestations, mass death events), or extended residence in Sacred Grounds. A sword that a Divine Realm cultivator used daily for ten thousand years may become Historically Imprinted — it carries the cultivator's technique patterns, their qi signature, their intent. When a cultivator of compatible realm and affinity holds it, the sword resonates and produces output that the current holder's cultivation alone could not generate.

---

**Class II — Artifacts**

An Artifact is a deliberately crafted object whose power is the result of intentional construction — inscription of energy patterns, embedding of CPM materials, binding of formation arrays, or infusion of cultivator energy during manufacture. Artifacts are designed with specific functions and have predictable performance within their design parameters.

All four construction methods detailed in Area 3 (Inscription, Tempering, Formation Binding, and Soul Architecture) produce Artifacts. The crafter's cultivation level determines the maximum tier achievable.

---

**Class III — Formation Arrays**

A Formation Array is a constructed spatial architecture — a network of energy-conducting elements (formation stones, CPM nodes, inscription lines, spatial anchors) that defines a zone and produces an ongoing energy effect within it. Formation Arrays are not single objects — they are systems. A cultivation chamber's five-wall inscription matrix is a Formation Array. A sect's defensive perimeter is a Formation Array. A Grand Array that covers a continent is a Formation Array.

Formation Arrays are classified separately from Artifacts because their governance involves spatial law (Part 13), faction territorial law (Part 22), and combat law (Part 5) in ways that single-object relics do not. Their cultivation environment effects are governed by Part 3, Area 14.6. Their destruction in combat is governed by Part 5.

---

**Class IV — Enchanted Objects**

An Enchanted Object is an ordinary-material object that has been given a single, simple energy imprint — a minor permanent effect at low energy cost. A lantern that burns with qi-fire indefinitely without fuel. A garment that repels rain. A lock that opens only for a specific qi signature. Enchanted Objects are the widest category and the lowest tier. They are not cultivation tools in a serious sense — they are the background material culture of a cultivation civilization.

---

### 1.2 — Terminology Standards

| Term | Formal Meaning | Common Usage | Note |
|------|---------------|--------------|------|
| Relic | Naturally formed or historically imprinted power object | Any high-tier cultivation object | The common "relic" usage (for any powerful cultivation object) is colloquially acceptable in prose |
| Artifact | Deliberately crafted cultivation object | Spirit Tool, Spirit Weapon, Magic Weapon | In-world names vary by faction; all map to Artifact class |
| Formation Array | Spatial energy architecture | Array, Formation, Matrix | "Formation" alone often refers to a Formation Array in-world usage |
| Enchanted Object | Low-tier single-imprint ordinary object | Enchanted gear, magic item | Lowest class; used as background culture detail |
| Treasure | Colloquial catch-all for any high-value cultivation object | Used loosely | Not a formal class; maps to Relic or Artifact by formal classification |
