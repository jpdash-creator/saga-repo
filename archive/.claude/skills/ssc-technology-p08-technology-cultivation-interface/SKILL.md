## Area 6 — Technology-Cultivation Interface (Technology Side — Threshold Tables)

*This Area governs the technology-side threshold requirements for integration models and hybrid classes. The synergy outputs produced when thresholds are met — what each model enables, how cultivation and technology enhance each other, and what emerges from integration — are governed by Part 31 (Cultivation-Technology Synergy Law), Areas 5, 7, 8, and 9.*

### 6.1 — Joint Governance Principle

Part 7 (Fusion Law, Area 6) establishes the cultivation-side thresholds. This Area establishes the technology-side thresholds. Both must be simultaneously satisfied. Part 31 governs the synergy outputs when both thresholds are met.

**Rule 6.1.1 — The Lower Threshold Is the Effective Ceiling:** The achievable integration model is the lower of: (a) the cultivation-side maximum from Part 7, Area 6.2, and (b) the technology-side maximum from this Area's threshold table.

**Rule 6.1.2 — Integration Is Not Required:** Model A (Parallel) is always valid at any tier combination. Integration is a capability unlock requiring investment by both sides; it is not automatic or required.

**Rule 6.1.3 — Integration Is Not a Technology Tier Requirement:** Technology-predominant civilizations with no cultivators advance through all 20 tiers without integration. Neither pure-technology nor pure-cultivation trajectories are deficient.

### 6.2 — Technology-Side Integration Threshold Table

| Technology Tier | Maximum Integration Model Accessible | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1–2 | None — No Integration Capability | Technology at these tiers has no interface capability with cultivation energy; cultivation and technology are strictly Parallel even if deployed together |
| Tier 3 | Model A (Parallel) | Technology and cultivation deployed together without interface; no energetic or architectural connection |
| Tier 4 | Model A–B | Basic CPM-powered devices; cultivation biometric monitoring; early relic authentication; no deep interface architecture |
| Tier 5 | Model B (Augmentative) | Stable augmentative integration; CPM-powered technology at industrial scale; cultivation-state detection and response; Relic Class 1 and 2 achievable |
| Tier 6 | Model B | Expanded Model B capability; multi-world cultivation integration systems; inner system scale deployment; improved CPM conversion efficiency |
| Tier 7 | Model B–C | Emerging symbiotic capability; outer system integration networks; early Relic Class 3 prototypes; CPM conversion approaching 70% |
| Tier 8 | Model C (Symbiotic) | Stable symbiotic integration architecture; Relic Class 3 fully achievable; high-fidelity CPM conversion with affinity preservation; cross-realm cultivation interface begins |
| Tier 9 | Model C | Expanded Model C capability; multi-stellar integration networks; CPM conversion 72–80% |
| Tier 10 | Model C–D | Advanced symbiotic standard; early unified integration achievable at cluster scale; CPM conversion 80–88% |
| Tier 11 | Model D (Unified) | Full unified cultivation-technology systems; galactic-scale integration infrastructure; CPM efficiency 85–95% |
| Tier 12–20 | Model D | Full Model D standard maintained and extended to civilization scale; CPM at near-theoretical maximum efficiency; integration architecture expands to match civilization scale |

### 6.3 — Technology-Side Requirements for Technology-Relic Hybrid Classes

The Technology-Relic Hybrid Classes defined in Part 7, Area 5.2 require minimum technology levels in specific domains. These are minimums across Materials Science (Type 14), Energy Technology (Type 7), and Cultivation Integration Technology (Type 12) specifically — not the civilization's majority-domain tier.

| Hybrid Class | Materials Science Minimum | Energy Tech Minimum | CI Technology Minimum | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Class 1 — Technology-Powered Relic | Level 4 | Level 4 | Level 4 | Interface layer design requires precise material engineering and energy conversion systems |
| Class 2 — Relic-Enhanced Technology | Level 5 | Level 5 | Level 5 | Stable relic energy architecture integration requires molecular-precision materials |
| Class 3 — Integrated Hybrid System | Level 8 | Level 8 | Level 8 | True architecture merger requires atomic-scale fabrication and high-fidelity qi conversion at stellar-tier precision |

**Rule 6.3.1 — Domain-Specific Requirements Apply:** All three domain minimums must be met simultaneously. If the civilization's Materials Science domain is at Level 8 but its Cultivation Integration Technology is at Level 4, Class 3 hybrid construction is not achievable regardless of the Materials Science tier.

**Rule 6.3.2 — Relic Grade vs. Technology Tier:** The Technology-Relic Hybrid Class requirements define the technology side of the hybrid. The relic component must independently meet Part 12's relic-grade requirements for the hybrid class. A Class 3 hybrid requires both Level 8 technology domains AND a relic of appropriate grade as defined by Part 12.

### 6.4 — CPM Interface Technology Requirements by Substance State

The technology-side interface for Condensed Power Material (Part 11) requires minimum Cultivation Integration Technology (Type 12) levels by CPM state:

| CPM State (Part 11) | Minimum CI Technology Level | Conversion Efficiency at Minimum | Conversion Efficiency Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Power Crystal (Grades 1–5) | Level 3 | 20% | 20–40% | Basic extraction possible; significant qi quality loss at minimum; approaching 40% with refinement |
| Gem (Grades 6–10) | Level 4 | 40% | 40–55% | Energy density requires advanced shielding to avoid system damage |
| Shard (Grades 11–14) | Level 5 | 55% | 55–70% | Molecular-level precision required for safe handling |
| Core (Grades 15–19) | Level 8 | 70% | 70–85% | Atomic-scale precision required; Core energy density damages Level 5–7 systems |
| Divine Liquid (Grade 20) | Level 11 | 85% | 85–95% | Divine Liquid energy properties interact with spacetime at micro-scale; Level 10 and below containment fails |

**Rule 6.4.1 — Attempting Higher-Grade CPM with Insufficient Technology:** Technology cannot safely convert CPM at grades above what its CI Technology level allows. Attempting higher-grade CPM in insufficient systems causes conversion failure with energy release proportional to the CPM grade — potentially catastrophic for the surrounding environment and all nearby cultivators.

**Rule 6.4.2 — Affinity Preservation and CI Level:** Maintaining the affinity character of CPM through conversion requires CI Technology at least one level above the minimum for the grade. At the minimum level, conversion produces generic qi-power output; the affinity character is lost. Affinity-preserving conversion enables the affinity-specific outputs described in Area 7.
