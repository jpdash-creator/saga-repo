## Area 14 — Interface with Part 15 (Travel Law)

### 14.1 — Distance as Travel Law Input

Part 14 defines the distances. Part 15 defines how they are traversed:

| Question | Authority |
|---|---|
| What distance must be traversed to reach destination X from origin Y? | Part 14, Areas 3–7 |
| What mechanism of travel is available at each cultivation realm or technology tier? | Part 15 |
| How long does traversal take? | Part 15 (using Part 14 distance as input) |
| Can a cultivator fold space to bypass the physical distance? | Part 15 governs the fold mechanics; Part 14 governs the canonical distance being bridged (P14-DIM-1) |
| Does FTL change the canonical distance? | No — P14-FTL-1 (FTL changes transit time, not distance) |
