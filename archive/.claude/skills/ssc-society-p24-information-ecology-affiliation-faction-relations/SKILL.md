## Area 19 — The Information Ecology of Affiliation and Faction Relations

### 19.1 — Information as Affiliation Currency

Faction affiliations (Part 23) are maintained, established, and destroyed through information flow. The information economy between affiliated factions is a critical governance layer:

- **Alliance Maintenance Information Flow:** Allied factions share operational intelligence (Tier III–IV) as the ongoing currency of active alliance. A faction that stops sharing relevant intelligence has signaled affiliation cooling before any formal declaration.
- **Secret Alliance Communication:** Factions maintaining a covert AFF-2 or AFF-3 affiliation (Part 23 Area 2) must communicate through concealed channels (ITC-1, ITC-2 sealed, ITC-5 SEDT) to prevent the alliance's existence from becoming known. The communication channel architecture for secret alliances is a Part 24 governance event.
- **Hostile Faction Information Denial:** At AFF-9 through AFF-14, factions actively deny each other intelligence through counter-intelligence, disinformation, and communication disruption. The escalation from AFF-8 to AFF-9 (competitive rivalry to cold war) is often marked by the transition from passive intelligence collection to active information denial.

### 19.2 — The Double Agent Architecture

The double agent — a person who appears to be working for one faction but is actually loyal to another — is one of the most potent and most narratively rich information governance scenarios in the series. Its governance:

**Double Agent Phase 1 — Establishment:** A faction recruits or creates a person with genuine-appearing credentials and access to an adversary faction. The establishment phase may take years of genuine service to the adversary faction before operational use begins.

**Double Agent Phase 2 — Operation:** The double agent feeds genuine intelligence to the employing faction to build credibility while selectively distorting intelligence delivered to the adversary. Maintaining this selective distortion without detection is the permanent operational challenge.

**Double Agent Phase 3 — Revelation:** Eventually, most double agents are detected — either by sophisticated soul scanning (P24-SS-2 Moderate depth detects the emotional stress of sustained deception over time), by behavioral pattern analysis, or by counter-intelligence correlation. The revelation event is always a Part 27 consequence event.
