## Area 11 — Interface with Part 5 (Combat and Fight Law)

### 11.1 — Battlefield Distance and Escalation Tier

Part 5 (Area 9 — Battlefield Spatial Constraints) uses Part 14's canonical distance values as its input for minimum engagement distance calculations and combat footprint governance:

| Combat Distance Question | Primary Authority |
|---|---|
| What is the spatial footprint of a given Grade's passive aura? | Part 3, Area 20 |
| What is the maximum soul sense range at which a cultivator can detect the opponent? | Part 14, Area 4 |
| What is the maximum technique projection range? | Part 14, Area 4.3 |
| What is the minimum safe engagement distance for civilian protection? | Part 5, Area 9.2 (using Part 3 Area 20 radius values) |
| Do protected zones and neutral zones have defined distance boundaries? | Part 13, Area 17 (spatial zone definition); Part 14 (distance values for those boundaries) |
