## Area 2 — Cultivation Travel: Foundational Framework

### 2.1 — Overview

Cultivation travel is the intrinsic movement capability that arises from advancement through the cultivation ladder (Part 3, Area 1). It requires no vehicle, no fuel supply, and no external infrastructure. It scales from basic physical enhancement at Mortal Tier 1 to spatial-folding reality traversal at the Divine Sea Realm. Cultivation travel is divided into five primary modes:

1. **Body Movement Enhancement** — the raw physical speed and capability improvement that every cultivation realm provides
2. **Qi-Assisted Movement** — using qi externally to enhance running, jumping, and brief aerial movement at lower realms
3. **Cultivation Flight** — sustained aerial travel under qi or divine energy power, beginning at Mortal Tier 2 upper realms
4. **Spatial Stepping** — exploiting spatial folds or dimensional shortcuts to cover extraordinary distances instantaneously
5. **Existence Transit** — at the highest Divine Realm stages, travel that ceases to resemble movement and becomes manifestation

### 2.2 — Cultivation Travel: Environmental Conditions

**Atmospheric Travel:** All cultivation travel within a standard planetary atmosphere is baseline. The capability tables in Areas 3–6 assume atmospheric conditions with standard gravity. Atmospheric flight is fully available to all realms that have flight capability.

**Vacuum Travel:** Cultivators in vacuum (space) must sustain their own life-support through cultivation energy. This is accessible at different thresholds:
- **Xiantian: True God and above** — qi field can maintain personal atmospheric bubble for minutes to hours in vacuum
- **Core Formation and above** — Core's energy field provides indefinite vacuum survival; no qi expenditure for life support
- **Divine Realm** — vacuum is irrelevant; divine energy sustains all biological functions at zero cost

**High-Gravity / Extreme Environment Travel:** Extreme gravity, radiation, and exotic stellar environments impose penalties on Mortal and Xiantian cultivators but become irrelevant at Core Formation and above.

### 2.3 — Cultivation Travel and Space Flight

**The fundamental question for cultivation space travel: At what realm can a cultivator travel meaningfully between celestial bodies?**

| Threshold | Capability |
|---|---|
| Mortal Tier 2: Monarch | Brief low-orbit capable (qi burst ascent); cannot sustain orbit; atmospheric flight only |
| Xiantian: Demigod | Atmospheric flight plus orbital escape velocity with sustained effort; reach low orbit as a strain event |
| Xiantian: Void God | Low orbit flight sustainable; cannot cross interplanetary distances in reasonable time |
| Xiantian: True God | Orbital flight without strain; vacuum survival minutes to hours; interplanetary distances require months even at peak speed |
| Xiantian: Absolute God | Comfortable vacuum and orbital flight; interplanetary travel begins to be feasible over months |
| Core Formation | Vacuum permanent; interplanetary travel feasible in weeks to months (Core propulsion) |
| Core Assimilation | Interplanetary travel in days to weeks; fast enough to be operationally significant |
| Core Solidification | Interplanetary travel in hours to days; beginning of stellar-system-scale travel capability |
| Divine Transformation | Stellar system navigation feasible; inner system traversal in hours |
| Sea Formation | Interstellar distances become approachable; pre-spatial-fold speeds approaching light fraction |
| Heaven Treading | **Spatial folding unlocked** — instantaneous or near-instantaneous traversal of astronomical distances; practical interstellar travel |
| Divine Ascension | Spatial folding at multi-star-system scale; galactic region traversal feasible |
| Divine Sea Realm | Galactic-scale spatial traversal; in-universe travel without effective limits below multiverse scale |
