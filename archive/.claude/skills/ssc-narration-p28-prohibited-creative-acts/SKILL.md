## Area 12 — Prohibited Creative Acts

### 12.1 — Absolute Creative Prohibitions

The following creative acts are prohibited under Part 28 regardless of narrative convenience, dramatic appeal, or creative preference:

**Causally Unsupported Cultivation Jumps:**
A character may not advance in cultivation in a way that bypasses Part 3's breakthrough requirements without an established, Canon-compliant mechanism for the bypass. Surprise cultivation breakthroughs mid-combat — a character manifesting power they have not established, as a consequence of extreme circumstances — are prohibited. The only permitted exception is a breakthrough that has been narratively prepared: the character has been shown approaching the breakthrough threshold through established cultivation accumulation, and the breakthrough is triggered by an extreme circumstance that catalyzes readiness that was already present.

**Causally Unsupported Technology Jumps:**
A character or faction may not suddenly possess technology that exceeds their established TA axis tier without a Canon-compliant acquisition mechanism (cross-civilizational transfer, relic inheritance, faction merger, leading-edge breakthrough within the Part 9 one-tier individual leading edge rule). Technology that appears when the narrative needs it, without prior establishment, is prohibited.

**Causally Unsupported Location Changes:**
Characters may not appear in a location without a travel-time-consistent explanation for how they got there. Part 15's travel mechanics apply to all character movement. A character who is established in Location A cannot appear in Location B without the prose accounting for the travel time required at that character's established travel capability.

**Causally Unsupported Faction or Affiliation Changes:**
A faction may not change its Part 23 affiliation position with another faction (from rivalry to alliance, from neutrality to open conflict) without the establishing events having been rendered in the prose. The affiliation change is the consequence of events — the events must be shown. An affiliation change that occurs between chapters, without being rendered, is prohibited.

**Causally Unsupported Character Behavioral Reversals:**
A character may not reverse a deeply established behavioral pattern — an ideology, a loyalty, a tactical philosophy, a relational stance — without a causally grounded arc of change having been rendered in the prose. The change must be earned. A villain who is suddenly compassionate after twenty scenes of brutality without an established arc of psychological shift is a Canon violation.

**Information Miracles:**
Characters may not possess information they cannot plausibly have acquired through mechanisms consistent with Part 24's information architecture. A character who "just knows" something they have no established means of knowing — without a soul-sense revelation, a contact network use, an intelligence operation, or a lucky discovery rendered in the prose — is an information miracle and is prohibited.

**Consequence Evasion:**
A character may not escape consequences that Part 27 requires without a Canon-compliant resolution pathway being shown. Injuries do not heal instantly. Social consequences of betrayal do not dissolve in a single conversation. Ideological rupture events do not resolve before their Part 20 reconstruction pathway has been traversed. The protagonist is not exempt from consequence — P27-1 is absolute.

**Civilization Characteristic Violations:**
A civilization may not behave in ways that are inconsistent with its CCT tier and civilization type. A Subsistence-tier civilization cannot deploy Galactic-scale military force. A CS-Type civilization cannot de-prioritize cultivation in favor of technology investment without a complete ideological restructuring being rendered in the prose.

### 12.2 — Forbidden Realm Vocabulary (Non-Canonical Cultivation Terms)

The following cultivation-realm terms are **non-canonical** to the Starward Saga and may not appear in prose, dialogue, thought, narration, ledger entries, or any other authored text. They are generic xianxia / cultivation-novel vocabulary that predates the SSC Canon; they are not part of the Part 3 realm ladder (Area 1.1, Area 1.6). References to any of them constitute a Part 3 Rule 1.7 violation audited under ALCA dimension AL-6.

**Forbidden Realm Names (Hard Block — All Contexts):**
- **Nascent Soul** — not a realm in this canon. Part 3 terminal Tier 1 stage is **Soul**; Part 3 Tier 2 summit is **Monarch**; Part 3 Core realms are **Core Formation / Core Assimilation / Core Solidification**. "Nascent Soul" is not in the ladder at any position.
- **Foundation Establishment** — not a realm. Part 3's equivalent conceptual band is **Mortal Tier 2 Senior–Warrior**, named differently.
- **Qi Condensation** (as a realm label) — not a realm. "Qi condensation" as a generic cultivation activity may appear in descriptive prose (e.g., "he condensed his qi"), but never as a capitalized realm-name.
- **Golden Core** — not a realm. Part 3 Core realms are named above; "Golden Core" is forbidden even as a synonym.
- **Spirit Severing** — not a realm.
- **Dao Seeking** — not a realm.
- **Mahayana** — not a realm.
- **Tribulation Transcendence** — not a realm. (Tribulation events may be depicted per Part 3 breakthrough law, but "Tribulation Transcendence" as a realm name is forbidden.)
- **Eternal Life** — not a realm. Lifespan mechanics are governed by Part 3 Area 2 (Entropy Resistance Principle); "Eternal Life" as a realm label is forbidden.
- **Immortal Ascension** — not a realm. The SSC Canon does not use "Immortal Ascension" as a cultivation milestone. The Xiantian and Divine realms (Part 3 Area 1.4–1.5) are the canonical equivalents.

**Note on Valid "Nascent" Usage:**
"Nascent" as a general adjective is permitted and canonical in **non-realm** contexts — specifically:
- **Part 13 V-2 Nascent Village** (location tier)
- **Part 13 E-1 Nascent Empire** (location tier)
- **Part 21 CCT Nascent Phase** (civilizational phase)
- General descriptive usage (e.g., "a nascent rivalry," "nascent fear")

The prohibition applies specifically to the compound realm name **Nascent Soul** and to use of "Nascent" as a modifier of any Part 3 realm stage.

**Enforcement:**
A prose unit containing any forbidden realm name without it being an in-prose act of explicit correction or a canonical character correcting another character's false terminology constitutes an ALCA Mode A failure under dimension AL-6 Cultivation Alignment (Part 1 Area 8.13). The unit must be corrected before being committed to `.docx` / `.md` output files. A governance document (any SSC Canon Part or ledger file) containing a forbidden realm name is a canon bug requiring immediate corrective edit per Part 1 Rule 4.8 / Rule 5.4.

**Relationship to Part 3 Rule 1.7:**
This prohibition is the corollary to Part 3 Rule 1.7 (Phase-Appropriate Realm Vocabulary Mandate). Rule 1.7 governs which **canonical** realms may be referenced in a given scene based on power ceiling. Area 12.2 governs which realm names are **never** permissible because they are not in the canon at all.
