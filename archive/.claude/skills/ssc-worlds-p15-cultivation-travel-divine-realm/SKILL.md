## Area 6 — Cultivation Travel: Divine Realm

### 6.1 — Divine Travel Overview

The Divine Realm breaks the physics of movement entirely. Travel at Divine level is not faster movement through space — it is a progressive disconnection from the requirement to cross space at all. The five Divine realms represent a spectrum from enhanced physical-spatial operation to pure existence-manifestation. At the summit, a Divine Sea Realm cultivator does not "travel" in any conventional sense — their presence manifests where their will directs it.

The defining transition in the Divine Realm is **Heaven Treading** (Third Divine Realm), where true spatial folding — not shortcutting, but actual space-folding — becomes available. This is the cultivation travel equivalent of FTL: it makes interstellar and even multi-star travel practically viable for a single cultivator.

### 6.2 — Divine Travel Capability Table

| Realm | Intrinsic Speed | Spatial Fold Capability | Scale of Operation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Divine Transformation | ~0.5c sustained; ~0.8c burst | Spatial fold up to ~1 AU per step | Inner star system at ease | Spatial barriers become suggestions; FTL-approach speeds in raw propulsion |
| Sea Formation | ~0.8c sustained; approaching c burst | Spatial fold up to ~10 AU per step; multi-fold bridging | Full star system in single session | Divine sea enables multi-simultaneous location awareness during travel |
| Heaven Treading | **Light-speed+ (FTL via spatial fold)** | **True interstellar spatial fold — light-years per step** | **Interstellar travel unlocked** | Steps between star systems; geography becomes courtesy, not constraint |
| Divine Ascension | Multi-light-year per step; galaxy-region traversal | Galactic-cluster-scale spatial fold capability | Multi-star-system region traversal in hours | Movement IS an environmental event; spatial wake persists after transit |
| Divine Sea Realm | Existence-manifestation; no conventional speed metric | Galactic-scale and beyond | In-universe navigation without practical speed limit | Location is a choice, not a journey |

### 6.3 — Heaven Treading: The Spatial Fold Threshold

Heaven Treading is the governance threshold at which a cultivator transcends the speed of light constraint through cultivation alone. This is the single most significant travel upgrade in the cultivation ladder. Its mechanics:

**Heaven Treading Spatial Fold Mechanics:**
- **Mechanism:** The cultivator perceives the spatial fabric of reality as foldable — they bring two spatially distant points into contact and step through the interface. The points separate again after transit.
- **Range:** A single Heaven Treading fold can cover distances up to **10 light-years** at Heaven Treading Level 9 Peak. Average operational range is 1–5 light-years per fold.
- **Fold preparation time:** ~1–10 seconds for a prepared fold; ~1 minute for an improvised fold in unfamiliar space. Can be reduced through spatial cultivation techniques (Part 6).
- **Multi-fold chains:** Heaven Treading cultivators can chain folds in sequence — each fold takes seconds, enabling effective multi-light-year traversal in minutes.
- **Navigation requirement:** Spatial fold requires the cultivator to have spatial knowledge of the destination — either through direct prior perception (soul sense visit, prior travel) or through a spatial beacon at the destination. Folding to an unknown location is possible but imprecise (landing accuracy degrades with unfamiliarity).
- **Fold detection:** A spatial fold creates a brief spatial ripple detectable by same-tier or higher cultivators within ~100 km. It is NOT detectable by technology at Tier 10 and below.
- **Atmospheric/vacuum:** Spatial folds work equally in vacuum, atmosphere, or any physical environment. The fold is spatial — not dependent on medium.

**Rule 6.3.1 — Heaven Treading Spatial Fold Is Not Teleportation.**
The fold is a traversal — the cultivator moves through the folded connection. It takes seconds to minutes depending on fold size. It is not instantaneous in the way a gate network is instantaneous. The difference matters for combat and pursuit governance (Part 5).

**Rule 6.3.2 — Heaven Treading Cannot Fold Beyond the Current Universe Scope.**
Heaven Treading's spatial fold operates within a single universe. Multi-universe traversal requires Divine Sea Realm capability — and even then is deferred scope (Area 18).

### 6.4 — Divine Ascension and Divine Sea Realm Travel

**Divine Ascension Travel:**
- Movement is no longer measured in speed — it is measured in reach. Divine Ascension cultivators can fold space at multi-star-system scales, covering galactic regions in a sequence of folds.
- A complete galactic crossing (100 kly) takes Divine Ascension cultivators hours via fold-chains.
- The spatial wake from a Divine Ascension fold is detectable by any cultivator at Core Formation or above within 1,000 km.

**Divine Sea Realm Travel:**
- The Divine Sea Realm does not travel in a spatial sense — the cultivator manifests where their divine sea extends. Existence precedes location.
- In practical terms: a Divine Sea Realm cultivator can "appear" anywhere within their divine sea's extension radius. They can extend this radius at will up to planetary scales (as governed by Part 3's soul sense table: ~0.001–0.01 AU for divine sea spatial extension).
- For traversal beyond divine sea radius: the cultivator performs fold-chains of unlimited range within the same universe.
- **Effective galactic crossing time:** minutes to hours.
- **Cross-universe scope:** Divine Sea Realm is the cultivation threshold at which multi-verse transit becomes theoretically possible — but this is deferred scope per Area 18.
