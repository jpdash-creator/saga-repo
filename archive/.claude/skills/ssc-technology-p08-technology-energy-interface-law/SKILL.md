## Area 7 — Technology-Energy Interface Law

*The synergy-side content of this Area — how CPM serves as technology fuel (synergy outputs), how technology interacts with relics beyond threshold requirements, and how energy technology creates cultivation-enriched environments — is governed by Part 31 (Cultivation-Technology Synergy Law), Areas 3.1, 3.2, 9, and 10. The technology-side CPM interface threshold requirements are governed by Part 8, Area 6.4. This Area retains Area 7.4 (pure technology cross-domain energy dependency), which is technology-only governance.*

*For reference: CPM-technology synergy → Part 31 Area 9; technology-relic synergy → Part 31 Area 7.3; energy technology and ambient cultivation energy → Part 31 Area 9.2 and Area 10.*

### 7.4 — Energy Technology Cross-Domain Interdependencies

The Energy domain (Type 7) is the primary enabling domain for all other technology types. Advancing any other domain to its next tier may require Energy Technology to have advanced first, because high-tier capabilities in other domains are only possible with the energy budget to power them.

**Rule 7.4.1 — Energy Domain Lag Limits Other Domains:** If a civilization's Energy Technology domain is significantly below its majority-domain tier, the energy-intensive domains (Industrial, Military, Space, Transport) cannot reach their theoretical ceiling. The energy budget required to operate Tier 11 Industrial systems requires Tier 11 Energy technology at minimum. Energy is the foundational enabler.

**Rule 7.4.2 — Energy Surplus Accelerates Adjacent Domains:** Civilizations with Energy Technology significantly above their majority-domain tier can advance adjacent domains more rapidly. Surplus energy availability reduces the material, time, and resource barriers for development in other domains — it cannot skip the scientific prerequisites (Part 10), but it can compress timelines once those prerequisites are met.
