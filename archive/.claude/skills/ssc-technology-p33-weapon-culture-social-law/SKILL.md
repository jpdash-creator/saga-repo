## Area 15 — Weapon Culture and Social Law

### 15.1 — Cross-Reference to Part 25

Weapon culture, social prestige hierarchies around weapon ownership, dueling weapon traditions, gifting weapons as political acts, weapon art and aesthetic traditions, and social stigma around weapon types are governed by Part 25 (Culture and Social Law). Part 33 provides the weapon object governance; Part 25 provides the social meaning governance.

**Key Part 25 Interface Points:**
- A-Class practitioner dueling weapons are a Part 25 culture governance event with specific weapon type preferences by culture.
- Weapon gifting between faction leaders is a Part 25 social ceremony with political meaning distinct from the weapon's Part 33 classification.
- Prohibited weapon ownership as social stigma (T-PSI weapons in civilian contexts) is a Part 25 social norm, not merely a Part 33 legal restriction.

### 15.2 — Weapon Prestige Scale

Weapons carry social prestige independently of their combat capability, governed by the following Part 25 / Part 33 joint prestige framework:

| Prestige Tier | Weapon Character | Social Signal |
|---|---|---|
| P-I | Standard Issue / Utilitarian | Minimal social signal; functional only |
| P-II | Quality Military Arm | Professional military status marker |
| P-III | Named Weapon (individual designation) | Elite practitioner or significant warrior status |
| P-IV | Artifact-Grade Technology Weapon | Wealth and access to rare manufacturing capability |
| P-V | Soul-Bonded Synergy Weapon | Integrated Practitioner; rare and powerful |
| P-VI | Legacy Weapons from Prior Civilizations | Historical significance; signals connection to lost civilizations |
| P-VII | Civilization-Class Ordnance (owned by individual) | Extraordinary; narrative-mythological status signal |
