## Area 21 — Technology Domain Level Notes: Key Capability Benchmarks

Area 21 provides a per-domain capability benchmark table that gives authors precise, concrete capability expectations at key levels within each domain. These benchmarks complement the level tables in Area 2 and are the primary reference for capability rendering in prose.

---

### 21.1 — Domain 1 (Industrial): Key Benchmarks at Critical Levels

| Level | Single-Day Production Benchmark | Largest Constructable Object | Material Complexity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Handful of tools; one pot; one hide garment | Small dwelling | Natural materials only |
| Level 3 | Thousands of standardized parts; a locomotive | A factory building | Advanced metals, early synthetics |
| Level 5 | Millions of molecular-precision components | Arcology block | Any material with known atomic structure |
| Level 8 | Planetary mass equivalent in processed material per year | Dyson sphere panel (km-scale) | Exotic matter including neutron-density |
| Level 11 | Galactic-mass-equivalent material processing in civilizational timeframes | Ringworld-class megastructure | Negative-mass, wormhole-stable |
| Level 16 | Not bounded by conventional quantity | Spacetime structure modifications | Spacetime-fabric material |
| Level 20 | Existence-level output | Anything that can exist | Fundamental-principle matter |

### 21.2 — Domain 2 (Information): Key Benchmarks at Critical Levels

| Level | Peak Computation Equivalent | Maximum Communication Range | Maximum Storage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Human brain (unassisted) | Line of sight / courier | Human memory |
| Level 4 | 10^18 floating point operations per second (exa-scale) | Global instant (digital) | Zettabyte-class |

| Level 16 | Universal computation | Universal instant | Universal information fabric |
| Level 20 | Absolute computation | All states of existence | Absolute information |

### 21.4 — Domain 8 (Military): Threat Assessment Reference

This table provides the per-tier threat capability summary for use by authors when writing combat, strategic conflict, and political tension involving technology-based military forces.

| Level | Individual Threat Capability | Civilization Threat Capability | Key Weapon Class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1–2 | Can threaten individual non-cultivators; no threat to Mortal Tier 1 cultivator in direct combat | Cannot threaten organized groups | Melee and simple ranged weapons |
| Level 3 | Can kill Mortal Tier 1 cultivators through ambush or sustained engagement | Can destroy settlements | Firearms, artillery, explosives |
| Level 4 | Can reliably threaten Mortal Tier 2 low-stage cultivators; ambush effective against Xiantian | Can devastate national infrastructure | Nuclear weapons, precision strikes |
| Level 5 | Directed energy poses real threat to low Xiantian; exosuit operators challenge mid-Mortal | Orbital bombardment threatens planetary infrastructure | Directed energy, autonomous weapons |
| Level 6 | Multi-world coordinated strike; no direct individual threat above Xiantian without mass deployment | Planetary destruction becomes achievable | Relativistic impactors |
| Level 8 | Stellar detonation capability; single-star system annihilation | Single civilization annihilation | Stellar weapons, self-replicating platforms |
| Level 11 | Galaxy-scale topology alteration | Galaxy-civilization annihilation possible | Galactic-scale weapons |
| Level 16 | Physical law manipulation as weapon | Universe-scale threat | Spacetime weapons |

### 21.5 — Domain 12 (Cultivation Integration): CPM State Summary Reference

| CPM State | Technology Level Required | Safe Conversion Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power Crystal Gr. 1–5 | Level 3 minimum | 20–40% | First viable CPM-technology interface |
| Gem Gr. 6–10 | Level 4 minimum | 40–55% | Requires advanced shielding |
| Shard Gr. 11–14 | Level 5 minimum | 55–70% | Molecular precision required |
| Core Gr. 15–19 | Level 8 minimum | 70–85% | Atomic-scale precision; Level 5–7 systems fail |
| Divine Liquid Gr. 20 | Level 11 minimum | 85–95% | Spacetime interaction; lower levels catastrophically fail |

**Energy Density Scaling:** Each CPM state transition represents approximately a 100–1,000× increase in energy density per unit mass. This is why higher-grade CPM requires dramatically higher CI Technology levels — the energy density increase overwhelms lower-level systems physically, not merely in capacity.
