## Area 5 — Side Character Consequence Architecture

### 5.1 — Side Characters and Part 27

All named side characters carry a consequence arc under Part 27. Their actions produce CSS events. The consequences of their choices — to support the hero, to betray them, to sacrifice themselves, to refuse to act — propagate through the world.

**Rule 19.5.1.1 — Side Character Sacrifices Are Not Free:** When a Tier A side character sacrifices something significant — their cultivation path, their faction standing, their life, their relationship — this sacrifice is a real consequence event for every character whose world it changes. The hero who benefits from an ally's sacrifice carries Part 27 debt (CSS consequence weight of the sacrifice). This debt must be narratively acknowledged.

**Rule 19.5.1.2 — Side Character Betrayal Is a CSS Event:** When a Tier A side character betrays the hero or villain they were affiliated with, this is simultaneously a CSS event for the betraying character (the relational consequence of betrayal), a CSS event for the betrayed character (the harm received), and an IE event (Part 20) for both — the betrayer's ideology has produced a rupture; the betrayed character's ideology about trust and loyalty has been challenged.

**Rule 19.5.1.3 — Side Character Consequence Accumulation Is Visible:** Like villain consequences, the accumulating consequences of a side character's choices must be visible in their present state. A Tier A ally who has sacrificed their sect standing, broken a covenant, and used forbidden techniques to support the hero carries those choices — their social position reflects them, their psychology reflects them, their faction relationships reflect them.
