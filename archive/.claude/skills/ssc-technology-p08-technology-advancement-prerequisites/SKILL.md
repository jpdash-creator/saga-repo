## Area 10 — Technology Advancement Prerequisites

### 10.1 — The Three-Category Prerequisite System

Every tier advance requires satisfaction of all three prerequisite categories before the next tier becomes achievable:

**Category 1 — Scientific Prerequisites (Part 10):** The theoretical and empirical scientific framework enabling the technology must have been developed. Part 10 (Technology Science Law) governs what discoveries are required. Authors must not advance civilization technology without acknowledging that the corresponding scientific breakthroughs have occurred.

**Category 2 — Civilizational Prerequisites (Area 5):** The civilization must have reached the corresponding civilization tier (Area 5.1). A Civ-5 civilization cannot sustain Tier 10 technology regardless of available resources or cultivation power contributions — the organizational and population infrastructure is insufficient. Advancement through civilization tiers requires meeting the population, organizational, and governance thresholds for the next Civ tier.

**Category 3 — Resource Prerequisites:** The material, energy, and CPM resources required for the technology must be accessible. This includes raw materials for construction, energy sources for operation, and CPM for cultivation integration technology. The energy budget requirement is the most commonly limiting resource prerequisite — a civilization cannot build Tier 8 energy infrastructure without the means to capture stellar-scale energy.

**Rule 10.1.1 — All Three Required:** All three categories must be satisfied simultaneously. A civilization with the scientific knowledge and resources but insufficient civilizational organization cannot build the technology. A civilization with the organization and resources but not the science cannot engineer it. A civilization with the science and organization but not the resources cannot construct it. No single factor substitutes for the others.

**Rule 10.1.2 — Cultivation Advancement Is Not a Prerequisite:** Cultivation realm advancement is not a Category 2 or Category 3 prerequisite for technology advancement. Cultivation may provide resources (CPM), scientific contributions (Part 10 soul-sense research), and energy supplements — but it is not a required input. A civilization that lacks cultivators advances through technology tiers on the strength of its other prerequisites alone.

### 10.2 — Intermediate Tier Advancement (Tiers 6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15, 17)

The eight intermediate tiers (6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15, 17) each require their own set of prerequisites, distinct from the anchor tiers they bridge. They are not shortcuts through the anchor tiers — a civilization must genuinely develop the capabilities of each tier before advancing to the next.

**Tier 6 Prerequisites (Inner Solar System):** Multi-world governance established; fusion power operational (Tier 5 energy); orbital construction as routine industry; interplanetary transportation infrastructure; inner-belt mining operations at commercial scale.

**Tier 7 Prerequisites (Outer Solar System):** Full inner system exploitation operational; deep-space manufacturing infrastructure; outer system exploration missions returning resource data; gas giant atmospheric operations demonstrated; complete stellar system map and resource characterization.

**Tier 9 Prerequisites (Multi-Star System):** FTL travel demonstrated and operational (or equivalent: near-FTL with practical interstellar transit); multiple star systems with habitation established; multi-stellar governance framework functional; stellar manufacturing coordination networks operational.

**Tier 10 Prerequisites (Stellar Cluster):** FTL standard across multiple star systems; cluster-scale energy integration infrastructure; governance spanning 5+ star systems; manufacturing networks resilient to individual stellar node loss.

**Tier 12 Prerequisites (Local Group):** Full galactic governance operational; FTL standard across galactic distances; cross-galactic transit infrastructure established; inter-galactic manufacturing networks functional.

**Tier 14 Prerequisites (Galaxy Supercluster):** Multiple galaxy clusters under coordinated governance; wormhole or equivalent inter-cluster transit standard; galaxy-cluster-scale energy infrastructure fully operational.

**Tier 15 Prerequisites (Observable Universe Fringe):** Multiple superclusters under governance; cosmological-scale logistics accounting for universal expansion; information infrastructure with near-universal reach.

**Tier 17 Prerequisites (Trans-Universal):** Universal-scale governance; trans-universal pathways demonstrated; energy infrastructure drawing from outside the home universe early-stage; multi-universal communication established.

### 10.3 — Advancement Blockers

The following conditions prevent tier advancement regardless of other prerequisites being met:

**Civilizational Collapse:** A civilization that suffers collapse below its existing civilization tier loses the organizational infrastructure that maintains its current technology tier. Effective operational technology tier drops to what the surviving organizational capacity can sustain. Recovery dynamics are governed by Part 9.

**Scientific Gap:** If the Part 10 scientific threshold has not been achieved, advancement cannot occur regardless of resource availability or civilizational organization.

**Resource Depletion:** A civilization that loses access to essential resources cannot maintain technology that depends on those resources. Technology dependent on unavailable resources degrades to its lowest-resource-requirement operational state.

**Energy Infrastructure Failure:** Because Energy Technology is the foundational enabling domain, catastrophic failure of energy infrastructure affects all other domains simultaneously. A civilization that loses its Tier 8 stellar energy capture capability experiences immediate degradation of all Tier 8+ capabilities in energy-intensive domains.
