## Area 5 — Cultivation Travel: Core Realm

### 5.1 — Core Travel Overview

The Core Realm is the first cultivation stage where genuine space travel — not merely orbital capability — becomes intrinsic. The Core generates a continuous propulsive field that powers both movement and life support simultaneously. Core cultivators operate in space as naturally as Mortal cultivators operate on land. Their speeds begin approaching meaningful interplanetary travel times, and at Core Solidification, even interstellar distances are theoretically approachable through raw Core propulsion (though impractical in time terms — this is where spatial shortcuts become significant).

### 5.2 — Core Travel Capability Table

| Realm | Sustained Propulsion Speed | Burst Speed | Environment | Spatial Capability | Travel Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core Formation | ~1,000 km/s sustained | ~5,000 km/s burst | All environments; indefinite vacuum | Spatial shortcuts up to ~500 km; fold-navigation begins | Inner system transit: weeks. Outer system: months |
| Core Assimilation | ~5,000 km/s sustained | ~20,000 km/s burst (~0.07c) | All environments; full atmospheric/vacuum transition | Spatial shortcuts up to ~5,000 km; multi-fold chains | Inner system transit: days. Outer system: weeks |
| Core Solidification | ~30,000 km/s sustained (~0.1c) | ~100,000 km/s burst (~0.33c) | All environments; stellar proximity tolerable | Spatial shortcuts up to ~50,000 km; reality-fold detection in wide area | Inner system transit: hours. Outer system: days. Nearest star: 10–40 years (raw propulsion) |

### 5.3 — Core Realm Space Travel Economics

**Travel Time Reference (Core Realm, Raw Propulsion):**
| Journey | Core Formation | Core Assimilation | Core Solidification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planet surface to low orbit (500 km) | ~0.5 seconds | ~0.1 seconds | instantaneous effectively |
| Planet surface to moon (~380,000 km) | ~6 minutes | ~1 minute | ~13 seconds |
| Planet surface to 1 AU (150M km) | ~1.75 days | ~8 hours | ~83 minutes |
| Across full star system (50 AU) | ~87 days | ~17 days | ~69 hours |
| Nearest star system (4 light-years) | ~120,000 years | ~55,000 years | ~12,000 years |

**Practical Implication:** Core Realm cultivators have transformed their relationship with a single star system — they can cross it in days to weeks. But raw propulsion alone cannot bridge interstellar distances in meaningful time. This is why the Core Solidification-level cultivators who need to travel between star systems use spatial fold technology or rely on technology transport. The boundary where intrinsic cultivation travel makes interstellar travel practical is the Heaven Treading realm.

### 5.4 — Core Spatial Shortcut Development

At Core Realm, spatial shortcuts become genuinely travel-relevant (not just combat-relevant). Core Solidification cultivators can chain multiple spatial shortcuts in rapid succession — effectively "skipping" across space in jumps rather than crossing continuously. This is still not Heaven Treading's full spatial fold, but it prefigures it:

| Core Sub-Realm | Single Shortcut Range | Chain Capability | Practical Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core Formation | ~500 km per step | 3–5 steps before recovery pause | Evasion, close-system shortcuts |
| Core Assimilation | ~5,000 km per step | 8–12 steps in rapid succession | Inner system tactical shortcuts |
| Core Solidification | ~50,000 km per step | 20+ steps; near-continuous with rest | Outer system shortcutting; multi-hop interplanetary |
