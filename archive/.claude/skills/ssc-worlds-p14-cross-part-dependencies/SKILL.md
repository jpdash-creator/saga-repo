Part 15 informs Part 14: Part 15 journey time tables and cultivation travel speed tables provide the canonical time-axis content that Part 14's joint time-and-distance narration standards (N14-5 and Area 8) reference — authors use both Parts together to render time-and-distance descriptions correctly |

| Canon-Part 23 | Part 14 → Part 23 — Active | CMS distance definitions govern faction operational range in affiliation obligations — a military alliance treaty (AFF-1 through AFF-3) requires the allied faction to be capable of projecting force to the threatened faction's location; Part 14 distance thresholds define what "within response range" means for mutual defense treaty activation (Part 23 Area 4.3). Pilgrimage culture (Part 25 Area 4 — Sacred Ground Pilgrimage-type cultural formation) is directly shaped by Part 14 distances: a pilgrimage to an S-Grade 6 Sacred Ground that is 500 light-years away is qualitatively different from one 500 km away; the cultural institution of the long-distance pilgrimage (multi-decade journey, one-way sacrifice) requires FTL travel to become practical, aligning it with Part 15 Tier 8+ civilizations and reshaping the cultural significance of the act. All CMS distance values in Part 25 cultural isolation threshold tables must use Part 14 CMS units per N14-1 |
