## Area 2 — The Five Civilization Axes

> **AXES NAMING DISAMBIGUATION — Part 21 vs. Part 25:**
> Part 21 and Part 25 both define "Five Axes" systems, but they are completely different in purpose and partially overlap in abbreviation. Writers must be careful not to conflate them.
>
> | Abbreviation | Part 21 Meaning | Part 25 Meaning |
> |---|---|---|
> | **CA** | Cultivation Axis — civilization's cultivation depth/breadth (levels 1–20) | *(does not exist in Part 25 — "CA-C" means Civilization Axis, not Cultivation Axis)* |
> | **CA-C** | *(does not exist in Part 21)* | Civilization Axis — how CCT tier shapes cultural values |
> | **TA** | Technology Axis — civilization's technology tier (levels 1–20, from Part 8) | *(does not exist in Part 21 — "TA-C" = Technology Axis in Part 25)* |
> | **TA-C** | *(does not exist in Part 21)* | Technology Axis — how technology paradigm shapes culture |
> | **LA** | Location Axis — civilization's geographic/spatial resources | *(does not exist in Part 25 — "LA-C" = Location Axis in Part 25)* |
> | **LA-C** | *(does not exist in Part 21)* | Location Axis — how place/world-type shapes culture |
> | **EA** | Energy Axis — civilization's CPM substance access tier | *(has no equivalent in Part 25)* |
> | **SA** | Synergy Axis — cultivation-technology integration depth | *(has no equivalent in Part 25)* |
> | **CuA-C** | *(does not exist in Part 21 — Part 21 uses "CA" for cultivation)* | Cultivation Axis — how cultivation realm and affinity shape culture |
> | **FA-C** | *(does not exist in Part 21)* | Faction Axis — how faction type and FPT shape culture |
>
> **Critical collision:** Part 21 uses **CA** for Cultivation Axis. Part 25 uses **CA-C** for Civilization Axis. These are completely different concepts. When "CA" appears in context without a -C suffix, it always means Part 21's Cultivation Axis. When "CA-C" appears, it always means Part 25's Civilization Axis. Always include the -C suffix when referring to Part 25 axes to avoid ambiguity.

### 2.1 — Cultivation Axis (CA) — Levels 1–20

The Cultivation Axis measures the depth and population-level distribution of cultivation power within a civilization. It is not a measure of the single most powerful individual — it is a measure of what the civilization as a structure can field, sustain, and reproduce in cultivation capability.

**CA Assessment Criteria:**
- **Peak Realm Present:** The highest cultivation realm held by any known individual within the civilization at the time of assessment.
- **Population Distribution:** What fraction of the civilization's population actively cultivates at any level; what fraction reaches Core Grade or above.
- **Institutional Infrastructure:** Whether the civilization has schools, academies, technique libraries, and cultivation support systems that reproduce cultivation capability across generations.
- **CPM Access:** Whether the civilization has reliable access to CPM substances (Part 11) at grades supporting cultivation advancement.

| CA Level | Peak Realm Present | Population Distribution | Institutional Depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA-1 | Mortal Tier 1–2 scattered individuals | Less than 1% cultivating at any level; no formal cultivation institutions | None — cultivation is informal tradition or accident |
| CA-2 | Mortal Tier 3–4 occasional practitioners | 1–5% cultivating; early cultivation families or clans | Oral tradition; no formal cultivation schools |
| CA-3 | Early Xiantian boundary individuals | 5–10% with some cultivation awareness; first formal sects emerging | Small sects; basic technique inheritance |
| CA-4 | Xiantian Grade reached by a known few | 10–20% attempting cultivation; organized sects functioning | Regional sects; Core-building techniques accessible |
| CA-5 | Xiantian Grade reached across a fraction | 20–35% cultivation-capable population; multiple sect generations of inheritance | National sect networks; Xiantian-grade curriculum |
| CA-6 | Core Formation reached by known masters | 30–40% active cultivation; inter-sect exchange networks | Multi-generational cultivation academies; Core Formation accessible |
| CA-7 | Core Formation reached by a recognized class | 40–55% active cultivation; major sect confederations | Major cultivation universities; Core Formation widely achievable |
| CA-8 | Early Divine Realm individuals present | 50–65% actively cultivating; high social prestige for cultivation | Extensive cultivation infrastructure; Divine Realm pathway documented |
| CA-9 | Divine Realm (early stages) accessible | 60–70% cultivation-capable; cultivation as societal organizing principle | Multi-world cultivation academies; Divine Realm reached by regular practitioners |
| CA-10 | Divine Realm broadly reached across elite | 70–80% cultivating at some level; civilization built around cultivation | Cluster-scale cultivation institutions; advanced Divine Realm techniques taught |
| CA-11 | High Divine Realm (Formation and above) | 75–85%; cultivation infrastructure embedded in all societal layers | Galaxy-scale cultivation academies; formation-level practitioners common |
| CA-12 | Sea Formation / Divine Sea Realm present | 80–88%; cultivation as fundamental civilizational resource | Divine Sea cultivation infrastructure; celestial-grade techniques |
| CA-13 | Divine Sea Realm represented in leadership | 82–90%; multi-world cultivation networks spanning cluster to quadrant | Sacred Ground formation capability; cultivation inheritance across star systems |
| CA-14 | Primordial Realm individuals known | 85–92%; civilization with functional Primordial-level presence | Quadrant-scale cultivation networks; Primordial-accessible institutions |
| CA-15 | Primordial Realm accessible to elites | 88–94%; Primordial cultivation infrastructure present | Galactic-scale cultivation governance; Primordial paths documented and maintained |
| CA-16 | Phase-level cultivation known | 90–96%; cultivation integration in all aspects including technology and governance | Universal-scale cultivation traditions; Phase Realm understood theoretically |
| CA-17 | Phase Realm individuals present | 92–97%; cultivation as the underlying physics-layer of civilization function | Phase Realm institutions; trans-universal cultivation traditions |
| CA-18 | Phase Realm accessible | 94–98%; cultivation as existential civilizational substrate | Multi-universal cultivation networks |
| CA-19 | Above-Phase cultivation known | 96–99%; near-total population integration with cultivation substrate | Dimensional cultivation traditions governing physical law at scale |
| CA-20 | Absolute cultivation presence | 99%+; cultivation and existence are indistinguishable | Absolute cultivation infrastructure; cultivation governs physical reality |

### 2.2 — Technology Axis (TA) — Levels 1–20

The Technology Axis is the civilization's technology tier as defined by Part 8 (Technology Scale Law) — the 20-Tier technology classification system. Part 8 governs all technology tier definitions. Part 21 receives the TA value from Part 8 and uses it as one of five inputs to the CCT synthesis.

**TA Assessment:** Per Part 8, Area 5 — the 7-of-14 majority domain rule. A civilization's TA equals the technology level achieved in at least 7 of its 14 technology domains (Part 8 Area 5.3).

**TA-to-Part 8 Tier Equivalence:**

| TA Level | Equivalent Part 8 Technology Tier | Infrastructure Scale |
|---|---|---|
| TA-1 | Tier 1 | Pre-industrial settlement tools |
| TA-2 | Tier 2 | Early industrial capacity |
| TA-3 | Tier 3 | Industrial civilization |
| TA-4 | Tier 4 | Digital/nuclear civilization |
| TA-5 | Tier 5 | Automation/consciousness-mapping civilization |
| TA-6 | Tier 6 | Inner stellar-system civilization |
| TA-7 | Tier 7 | Outer stellar-system civilization |
| TA-8 | Tier 8 | Stellar-capture civilization (Dyson-scale) |
| TA-9 | Tier 9 | Multi-star reach |
| TA-10 | Tier 10 | Stellar cluster technology |
| TA-11 | Tier 11 | Galactic-scale technology |
| TA-12 | Tier 12 | Local Group technology |
| TA-13 | Tier 13 | Galaxy cluster technology |
| TA-14 | Tier 14 | Supercluster technology |
| TA-15 | Tier 15 | Near-universal technology |
| TA-16 | Tier 16 | Universal-scale technology |
| TA-17 | Tier 17 | Trans-universal technology |
| TA-18 | Tier 18 | Multi-universal technology |
| TA-19 | Tier 19 | Dimensional technology |
| TA-20 | Tier 20 | Absolute technology |

*Full technology tier governance is Part 8's exclusive domain. Part 21 uses TA as input only.*

### 2.3 — Energy Axis (EA) — Levels 1–20

The Energy Axis measures a civilization's command over energy resources across both technological and cultivation-derived energy forms. It is distinct from TA (which measures technology sophistication) and CA (which measures cultivation power). A civilization with exceptional CPM resources but primitive technology has a high EA and low TA; a civilization with advanced energy technology but no cultivation access has a high TA in Energy domain and a low CA — their EA is scored separately.

**EA Assessment Criteria:**
- **CPM Substance Access:** What grade of CPM (Part 11) the civilization has reliable, civilization-scale access to (not just individual practitioner access).
- **Technological Energy Budget:** The civilization's total energy budget per capita and total, in Part 8 technology-tier terms.
- **Energy Storage and Distribution:** Whether the civilization can store, transport, and distribute energy at civilization scale.
- **Energy Conversion Efficiency:** For civilizations with both CPM and technological energy, the efficiency of converting between them (Part 31 Area 9).

| EA Level | CPM Access | Technological Energy | Total Energy Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| EA-1 | None — no CPM in civilization | Pre-industrial energy (biomass, wind, water) | Subsistence-scale energy |
| EA-2 | Trace CPM (Power Crystal Grade 1–2, individual only) | Early industrial energy (steam, early combustion) | Settlement-scale energy budget |
| EA-3 | Power Crystal access (Grades 1–3, small scale) | Industrial energy (combustion, early electrical) | Regional energy distribution |
| EA-4 | Power Crystal civilization-scale (Grades 3–5) | Electrical grid, nuclear fission equivalents | National energy infrastructure |
| EA-5 | Gem-grade CPM accessible (Grades 6–7) | Fusion equivalents; early space energy collection | Planetary energy budget achievable |
| EA-6 | Gem-grade reliable at faction scale (Grades 7–9) | Multi-planetary energy systems; anti-matter traces | Multi-world energy distribution |
| EA-7 | Gem-grade civilization-wide (Grades 8–10) | Inner stellar energy capture (partial Dyson) | Stellar-budget energy beginning |
| EA-8 | Shard-grade CPM accessible (Grades 11–12) | Full stellar energy capture (Dyson-complete) | Stellar energy at civilization scale |
| EA-9 | Shard-grade reliable faction-wide (Grades 12–13) | Multi-star energy collection networks | Multi-stellar energy budget |
| EA-10 | Shard-grade civilization-wide (Grades 13–14) | Cluster-scale energy collection | Cluster energy dominance |
| EA-11 | Core-grade CPM accessible (Grades 15–16) | Galactic-scale energy infrastructure | Galactic energy command |
| EA-12 | Core-grade faction-wide (Grades 16–17) | Multi-galactic energy systems | Local Group energy scale |
| EA-13 | Core-grade civilization-wide (Grades 17–18) | Galaxy cluster energy systems | Galaxy cluster energy command |
| EA-14 | Divine Liquid access (Grade 20, limited) | Supercluster energy systems | Supercluster energy scale |
| EA-15 | Divine Liquid faction-accessible | Near-universal energy systems | Near-universal energy budget |
| EA-16 | Divine Liquid civilization-accessible | Universal energy infrastructure | Universal energy command |
| EA-17 | Divine Liquid abundantly available | Trans-universal energy channels | Trans-universal energy budget |
| EA-18 | Divine Liquid as standard substance | Multi-universal energy systems | Multi-universal energy scale |
| EA-19 | CPM above Grade 20 theoretical | Dimensional energy manipulation | Dimensional energy governance |
| EA-20 | Absolute energy access | Absolute energy command | Energy and existence unified |

### 2.4 — Location Axis (LA) — Levels 1–20

The Location Axis measures the physical scale of territory a civilization structurally administers — defined by Part 13 (Location Law) cosmological classification and Part 14 (Distance Law) CMS scale notation. LA is not population count; it is the structural extent of governed space.

**LA Assessment:** Based on the highest cosmological scale of territory the civilization can sustain administrative, defensive, and infrastructure presence across.

| LA Level | Territorial Scale | Part 13 Cosmological Reference | Part 14 CMS Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-1 | Single settlement to small cluster of Villages | Village (V-class) level | Meter to kilometer scale |
| LA-2 | Region / Town-City territory | Town and City (T/C-class) | Kilometer to Megameter (Mm) scale |
| LA-3 | Kingdom or early nation-state territory | Kingdom (Km-1 to Km-5) | Megameter to Gigameter (Gm) scale |
| LA-4 | Empire or continent-spanning territory | Kingdom (Km-6 to Km-10) / Empire (E-class) early | Gigameter to sub-World surface scale |
| LA-5 | Single World fully administered | World (W-class) level | Gm to AU scale |
| LA-6 | Inner Star System administered | Star System (SS-1 to SS-5) | AU to light-minute scale |
| LA-7 | Full Star System administered | Star System (SS-6 to SS-10) | Light-minute to light-hour scale |
| LA-8 | 2–5 Star Systems administered | Star System (SS-11 to SS-15) + neighbors | Light-hour to light-day scale |
| LA-9 | 6–50 Star Systems administered | Multi-system near-cluster | Light-day to light-year scale |
| LA-10 | Star Cluster administered | Star Cluster (SC-class) | Light-year to kilolight-year (kly) scale |
| LA-11 | Star Quadrant administered | Star Quadrant (SQ-class) | 10–100 kly scale |
| LA-12 | Multiple Quadrants / early Galaxy | Galaxy (G-1 to G-5) | 100 kly to early Mly scale |
| LA-13 | Galaxy substantially administered | Galaxy (G-6 to G-10) | Megalight-year (Mly) inner scale |
| LA-14 | Galaxy fully administered | Galaxy (G-11 to G-15) | Mly full galactic scale |
| LA-15 | Local Group (2–10 Galaxies) administered | Galaxy Cluster (GC-class) early | 1–10 Mly scale |
| LA-16 | Galaxy Cluster fully administered | Galaxy Cluster full | 10–100 Mly scale |
| LA-17 | Galaxy Supercluster administered | Galaxy Supercluster (GSC-class) | 100 Mly to 1 Gly scale |
| LA-18 | Observable Universe early administration | Near-universal territory | 1–10 Gly scale |
| LA-19 | Universe fully administered | Universe territory | 10–100 Gly scale |
| LA-20 | Multiverse or dimensional territory | Multiverse / The Primordium / Absolute Chaos territory | Trans-universal / Primordial scale |

### 2.5 — Synergy Axis (SA) — Levels 0–10

The Synergy Axis measures the degree to which a civilization has deliberately integrated cultivation and technology into unified systems. It is a distinct axis from CA and TA — two civilizations with identical CA and TA profiles may have very different SA values depending on whether they have invested in integration. SA is governed by Part 31 (Cultivation-Technology Synergy Law) in terms of what integration produces; Part 21 governs SA as a civilization classification input.

**SA Assessment Criteria:**
- Integration Model achievable at civilization infrastructure level (Part 31, Area 5).
- Fraction of population operating in synergistic modes (Model A or higher).
- Existence and scale of integration institutions (academies, laboratories, guilds, government bodies).
- Cross-domain synergy outputs at civilization scale.

**Note:** SA-0 is valid — civilizations with zero cultivation or zero technology are SA-0 by definition. Pure-cultivation and pure-technology civilizations are not disadvantaged in CCT by SA-0; SA-0 is offset by higher CA or TA values in the synthesis formula.

| SA Level | Integration Depth | Part 31 Model Equivalent | Civilizational Expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| SA-0 | No integration — single-track civilization | None | Cultivation-only or technology-only civilization; no meaningful interface |
| SA-1 | Superficial coexistence | Pre-Model A | Cultivators use basic technology tools; early CPM as fuel experiments; no institutional framework |
| SA-2 | Coordination synergy | Early Model A | Cultivators applying physical/affinity capabilities to technology construction; CPM used as supplementary energy |
| SA-3 | Basic augmentation | Model A threshold | Power Crystal as CPM fuel; soul-sense research applications; early CI Technology |
| SA-4 | Operational Model A | Model A fully operational | CPM-powered devices at individual scale; biometric cultivation monitoring in technology; Relic Class 1 achievable |
| SA-5 | Model A–B transition | Model A–B | Industrial-scale CPM integration; multi-world cultivation support through technology; Relic Class 2 achievable |
| SA-6 | Operational Model B | Model B fully operational | Shard-grade CPM interface; soul-sense research at planetary scale; formation array + technology hybrid infrastructure |
| SA-7 | Model B–C transition | Model B–C | Emerging symbiotic standard; Relic Class 3 prototypes; stellar-scale integration networks |
| SA-8 | Operational Model C | Model C fully operational | High-fidelity Core-grade CPM interface; cross-realm cultivation architecture; stellar-scale technology-enhanced Sacred Ground approximation |
| SA-9 | Model C–D transition | Model C–D | Advanced symbiotic standard; CPM conversion 80–88%; early unified galaxy-scale systems |
| SA-10 | Operational Model D | Model D fully operational | Full unified cultivation-technology civilization; galactic-scale ambient qi enrichment achievable; near-theoretical CPM efficiency |
