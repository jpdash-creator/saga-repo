| Part 17 (Character — Main Character / Hero Law) | Active | Hero weapons are tracked in Hero Ledger Section 8 per Part 33 WU-class and weapon type taxonomy; weapon tier advancement and acquisition of significant weapons are mandatory Hero Ledger update triggers; Rule 33.11.1 (weapon discharge = CR-3 hostile act) applies to heroes and feeds Hero Ledger Section 10 Canon Events Log; a hero's primary weapon is part of their combat identity (Part 17 Rule 17.10.3.1) and its WS-class and WU-class profile are governance-critical Hero Ledger data |
