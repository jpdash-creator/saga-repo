## Area 13 — The Ideology Ledger — Master Tables

### 13.1 — Ideology Category Quick Reference

| Code | Category | Core Question | Primary Virtue Potential | Primary Corruption Potential |
|------|----------|---------------|--------------------------|------------------------------|
| SDI | Strength-Dominant | Who has power? | Uncompromising capability; strategic realism | Exploitation; cruelty rationalized as efficiency |
| JCI | Justice-Centered | What is right? | Accountability; equity; moral courage | Rigidity; vengeance in justice's clothing |
| LBI | Loyalty-Bound | Who do I owe? | Faithfulness; sacrifice; relational depth | Complicity; blind loyalty to wrong causes |
| FSI | Freedom-Sovereign | Who governs me? | Self-determination; authenticity | Irresponsibility; isolation; contempt for others' need |
| TSI | Transcendence-Seeking | What is beyond? | Vision; sacrifice; spiritual depth | Detachment; devaluation of the present |
| OMI | Order-Maintaining | What holds things together? | Stability; reliability; structural wisdom | Oppression; resistance to necessary change |
| ADI | Ambition-Driven | What can I achieve? | Drive; excellence; legacy-building | Instrumentalization of others; meaningless accumulation |
| VAI | Vengeance-Anchored | What must be paid? | Accountability enforcement; grief channeling | Expansion to innocents; void on completion |
| TRI | Truth-Seeking | What is real? | Accuracy; intellectual courage; clarity | Callousness; truth at wrong moment |
| PCI | Protection-Centered | Who depends on me? | Sacrifice; guardianship; relational depth | Control; harm justified by protection |
| HLI | Hedonistic-Libertine | What is worth experiencing? | Presence; aesthetics; joy in existence | Avoidance of necessary suffering; impact blindness |
| SCI | Sacred-Covenant | What have I promised? | Reliability; depth; covenant integrity | Fanaticism; harm in covenant's name |

The [IDEOLOGY AUDIT] command (C-17) is the direct invocation of Part 20 IS-state audit against Character Ledger Section 4 for all active characters.
