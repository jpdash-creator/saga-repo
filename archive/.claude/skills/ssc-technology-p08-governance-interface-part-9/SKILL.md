## Area 24 — Governance Interface with Part 9 (Technology Growth Law)

### 24.1 — Part 8's Role in Part 9 Governance

Part 9 (Technology Growth Law) governs the mechanics of civilization technology advancement. Part 8 establishes the framework within which Part 9 operates:

**What Part 8 Provides to Part 9:**
- The 20-Tier Scale (the advancement ladder Part 9 governs movement through)
- The three-category prerequisites (Area 10 — the conditions Part 9 must verify before authorizing advancement)
- The Technology Ceiling concept (Part 8, P8-2 — the structural ceiling Part 9's advancement mechanics must not violate)
- The per-tier prerequisites for intermediate tiers (Area 10.2 — the specific prerequisite conditions for each of the 8 intermediate tiers)
- The civilization tier classification (Area 5 — the Civ tier progression that must accompany technology tier advancement)

**What Part 9 Provides (fully authored — see Part_9_Technology_Growth_Law.md):**
- Advancement timeline mechanics: standard timelines for all 20 tier transitions (Part 9, Area 5)
- Accelerated advancement conditions: five acceleration mechanisms including Research Density Investment, Accidental Discovery, Crisis-Driven Innovation, Cross-Civilizational Transfer, and Cultivation-Assisted Research (Part 9, Area 6)
- Decelerated advancement conditions: five deceleration event types including Resource Scarcity, War and Conflict, Institutional Collapse, Cultivation Suppression, and Environmental Catastrophe (Part 9, Area 7)
- Regression mechanics: Active, Completed, and Fragmentary regression with Degradation Timeline Table and Re-Advancement multipliers (Part 9, Area 8)
- Individual and factional leading edge: Leading Edge Principle corresponding to Part 8 Rule 5.1.1 (Part 9, Area 9)
- CPM Growth Cycle: the positive feedback loop in integrated civilizations (Part 9, Area 10.3)
- Narration standards for technology advancement events (Part 9, Area 11)

### 24.2 — Technology Ceiling and Narrative Phase (Part 30 Non-Relationship)

For clarity and finality: Part 30 (Novel Macro Story Law) provides story rhythm guidance on narrative phase escalation. It governs when and how narrative escalation milestones are appropriate in the story arc structure. It does NOT:
- Set a maximum technology tier for any narrative phase
- Restrict authors from depicting technology at any tier that the civilization has validly earned
- Create a "Technology Ceiling" separate from the in-world Technology Ceiling governed by Part 8, P8-2
- Function as a cap on what civilizations can do or deploy within any story phase

The in-world Technology Ceiling (Part 8, P8-2) is the governance of civilizational maximum capability. It is a structural world-law, not a narrative pacing restriction. Part 30's escalation guidance is separate and operates at the story-structure level, not the world-law level. When in doubt: if the civilization has legitimately earned the technology tier through the prerequisites in Area 10, the technology is available for narration regardless of Part 30's story phase. Part 30 governs whether the author should deploy that technology in a given narrative moment for story quality reasons — that is a different question from whether the technology exists.
