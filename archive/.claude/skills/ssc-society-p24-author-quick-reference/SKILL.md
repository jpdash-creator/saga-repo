## Area 25 — Author Quick Reference

### 25.1 — Five Questions Before Writing an Information Scene

1. **What information is moving?** Identify its Tier classification (Area 2.1).
2. **Through which channel?** Select from ITC-1 through ITC-7 (Area 3.1). What speed, range, and interception risk does this create?
3. **What is each character's information state?** What does each character know, believe, suspect, and conceal? (P24-10)
4. **What lag applies?** What is the distance involved, and does it create L1–L4 conditions? (Area 10.1)
5. **What are the consequences if this information is exposed?** What Tier I–II information might be at risk, and what Part 27 cascade does its exposure trigger?

### 25.2 — Quick Reference: Soul Scanning Summary

| Scan Depth | Cultivation Requirement | Target Aware? | Information Accessed |
|-----------|------------------------|---------------|---------------------|
| Impression | Mortal Tier 2+ | No | Emotion, health, realm |
| Identity | Core Formation+ | Dimly | Exact realm, affinity, active technique |
| Profile | Divine Realm | Clearly | Personality, recent memories |
| Memory Trawl | Heaven Soul | Acutely (painful) | Specific memories |
| Consciousness | Heaven Soul High+ | Traumatic | Near-complete knowledge access |

### 25.3 — Quick Reference: Transmission Channel Summary

| Channel | Speed | Range | Intercept Risk | Access Requirement |
|---------|-------|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| ITC-1 Soul | Instant | Soul sense range | High | Soul cultivation |
| ITC-2 Formation | Near-instant | Network reach | Medium | Formation expertise |
| ITC-3 Courier | Travel speed | Unlimited | Medium-High | None (universal) |
| ITC-4 Technology | Light-speed to FTL | Tier-dependent | Variable | Tech infrastructure |
| ITC-5 Synergy | Near-instant to FTL | Relay-dependent | Low-Medium | Dual-track expertise |
| ITC-6 Environmental | Instant | Broadcast radius | Very High | None (public) |
| ITC-7 Ambient | Passive | Variable | N/A | None (uncontrolled) |
