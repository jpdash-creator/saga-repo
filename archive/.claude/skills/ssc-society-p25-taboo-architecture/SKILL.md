## Area 11 — Taboo Architecture

### 11.1 — Universal Cultivation Taboos

Certain taboos are so widespread across cultivation civilizations that they function as near-universal social laws. Violations cause social consequences regardless of cultural context:

| Taboo | Violation | Social Consequence | Rationale |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| Soul-Sense Invasion | Using soul sense to probe another cultivator without permission | Immediate combat permission granted; social exclusion | Equivalent to physical assault; violation of internal sovereignty |
| Realm Deception | Claiming a higher cultivation realm than one actually holds | Formal exposure and social destruction | Cultivation realm is the bedrock of social contract |
| Master Betrayal | Actively harming or exposing one's master or sect | Faction exile; potential death sentence in strict sects | Master-disciple bond is the foundational social institution |
| Technique Theft Public Display | Using a stolen technique in public confrontation with its originator | Immediate challenge right; social condemnation | Flaunting theft undermines the cultural value of technique lineage |
| Breakthrough Interference | Deliberately interrupting another cultivator's breakthrough | Near-universal combat justification | Breakthrough is the most vulnerable and sacred personal moment |

### 11.2 — Faction-Specific Taboos

Beyond universal cultivation taboos, each faction type maintains specific taboos that are absolute within its cultural context (see Area 5.2 Taboo Architecture column). Narrative note: a character who violates a faction-specific taboo in ignorance receives partial social forgiveness; a character who violates it knowingly receives none.

### 11.3 — Location-Specific Taboos

Sacred Ground communities (Area 4.3) and specialized world-type communities (Area 4.4) maintain location-specific taboos. Authors must check Part 13 location class before writing scenes in specialized locations.
