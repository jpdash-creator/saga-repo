## Rule Text (Verbatim Source — Authoritative)
## Area 21 — Cultivation Synergy Percentage and Technology Synergy Percentage System

### 21.1 — Foundational Definition and Purpose

Every entity in the Starward Saga canon carries two independent synergy percentage values:

- **Cultivation Synergy Percentage (CS%)** — a value from 0% to 100% expressing the degree to which an entity is integrated with, adapted to, optimized for, and operating within the cultivation energy paradigm as a synergistic framework.
- **Technology Synergy Percentage (TS%)** — a value from 0% to 100% expressing the degree to which an entity is integrated with, adapted to, optimized for, and operating within the technology paradigm as a synergistic framework.

**What CS% and TS% Measure:**
These values do not measure raw power, tier ceiling, or developmental history alone. They measure the degree to which the entity is actively functioning as a participant in each respective paradigm — drawing energy from it, contributing to it, being shaped by it, and shaping it in return. An entity with high CS% is not simply a powerful cultivator; it is an entity whose existence is deeply interwoven with the cultivation energy field around it. An entity with high TS% is not simply technologically equipped; it is an entity whose structures, operations, and outputs are built on and dependent on technological paradigms in a synergistic relationship.

**The Two Values Are Fully Independent:**
CS% and TS% are not complementary variables that must sum to 100. An entity can have high values in both, low values in both, or any asymmetric combination. A world-tier Sacred Ground at the center of a galactic-scale civilization may have CS% = 95% and TS% = 80%. A wilderness relic site with no technology presence may have CS% = 90% and TS% = 5%. A purely mechanical technology station with no cultivators may have CS% = 0% and TS% = 100%. A primitive, uncultivated, low-technology asteroid may have CS% = 3% and TS% = 2%.

**Rule 21.1.1 — CS% and TS% Are Living Information (L):** Both values are Living Information — they change as the narrative progresses, as entities develop, as civilizations advance, and as cultivators and technology systems interact. They are not fixed at entity creation. They are tracked in the appropriate ledger section for each entity type (defined in Areas 21.4–21.14) and updated per Part 1 Area 6 Post-Write Ledger Update Protocol when a CS%/TS%-triggering event occurs.

**Rule 21.1.2 — CS% and TS% Are Entity-Level, Not Individual-Achievement-Level:** CS% and TS% reflect the entity's synergistic state — its active participation in each paradigm as a whole entity — not merely the highest individual achievement within it. A civilization with one god-realm cultivator and a billion untrained mortals does not have CS% = 100%. Its CS% reflects the degree to which the civilization as a whole entity operates synergistically with cultivation energy. An individual character's CS% reflects their own integrated participation in cultivation as a synergistic system — including whether they draw on cultivation environments, contribute CPM to synergy systems, and benefit from formation arrays and Sacred Ground amplification — not merely their cultivation realm.

### 21.2 — What Each Percentage Value Means

#### 21.2.1 — Cultivation Synergy Percentage (CS%) Band Definitions

| CS% Range | Designation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0% | Null Cultivation | No cultivation presence, no cultivation energy interaction, no CPM production or use. Pure non-cultivation entity. |
| 1%–10% | Residual Cultivation | Trace cultivation presence. Occasional ambient qi exposure. No systematic cultivation practice or CPM production at meaningful scale. Minimal or no cultivation benefit to the entity's function. |
| 11%–25% | Emergent Cultivation | Early or sparse cultivation presence. Some cultivators active within the entity or associated with it. Ambient qi is slightly above baseline in some areas. CPM production is artisanal or incidental. The entity's core operations are not cultivation-synergistic. |
| 26%–40% | Partial Cultivation | Moderate cultivation presence. A meaningful fraction of the entity's population, structure, or operation is cultivation-active. Formation arrays appear in important contexts. CPM is traded or stored at small scale. The entity benefits from cultivation but is not organized around it. |
| 41%–55% | Functional Cultivation | The entity has a genuine, functioning cultivation ecology. Cultivators at multiple realm tiers exist. Formation arrays are integrated into key infrastructure. CPM supply chains exist. Sacred Grounds (if present) are recognized and utilized. Cultivation and the entity's other operations co-exist productively. |
| 56%–70% | Advanced Cultivation | Cultivation is a defining paradigm for the entity. High-realm cultivators are present and culturally central. Formation arrays are broadly deployed across the entity's infrastructure. CPM is a major energy and economic resource. Sacred Grounds are politically significant assets. The entity's identity is substantially shaped by its cultivation ecology. |
| 71%–85% | Deep Cultivation | The entity is deeply structured around cultivation energy. High-realm and apex cultivators are present. Formation arrays saturate critical infrastructure. Sacred Ground management is a primary political priority. CPM supply chains operate at industrial scale. The entity's power, culture, economy, and spatial character are all cultivation-synergistic. |
| 86%–95% | Apex Cultivation | The entity is a cultivation-paradigm entity at near-maximum integration. Apex-realm cultivators are actively present. Sacred Ground networks operate at S-Grade 6–8. Ambient qi is Class 6–7 across significant portions of the entity's space. Formation arrays operate at civilization-scale. Cultivation is the dominant energy paradigm and co-equal with all other entity functions. |
| 96%–99% | Transcendent Cultivation | The entity's existence is substantially constituted by cultivation energy. Cultivation energy has reshaped the entity's physical structure, ambient field, and operational logic. The boundary between the entity and cultivation energy is blurred — the entity is cultivation-energy-active in its own right. Reserved for entities like living Sacred Grounds, cultivation-awakened planets, and formation-saturated dimensional pocket spaces. |
| 100% | Absolute Cultivation | The entity exists entirely as a cultivation-energy expression. No part of the entity is outside the cultivation paradigm. Reserved for entities at Absolute Chaos / Primordium proximity or cultivation-energy-constituted constructs. Part 28 Tier 2 Authorization required to assign this value in prose. |

#### 21.2.2 — Technology Synergy Percentage (TS%) Band Definitions

| TS% Range | Designation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0% | Null Technology | No technology presence. No engineered systems, manufactured tools above primitive hand-craft, or scientific infrastructure. Pre-industrial entity. |
| 1%–10% | Residual Technology | Trace technology presence. Simple manufactured goods and basic tools. No systematic technology infrastructure, no energy systems beyond biological, no digital or computational capability. |
| 11%–25% | Emergent Technology | Early technology presence. Basic industrial capability. Some mechanical or simple electronic systems. No interplanetary technology. No CI Technology presence. Technology does not constitute the entity's operational foundation. |
| 26%–40% | Partial Technology | Meaningful technology presence. Industrial-to-early-interplanetary capability. Some energy production beyond biological. Basic computational systems present. Technology is a practical tool for the entity but not a defining paradigm. |
| 41%–55% | Functional Technology | Genuine technology ecology. Multi-domain technology infrastructure. Interplanetary or early stellar capability. Energy, transport, medical, and communications systems are technology-based at meaningful scale. CI Technology may be at low levels if cultivation is also present. The entity's operations depend substantially on technology. |
| 56%–70% | Advanced Technology | Technology is a defining paradigm. Multi-stellar or early galactic capability. High-level energy and weapons systems. CI Technology at significant level if cultivators are present. The entity's power, culture, economy, and spatial reach are substantially shaped by its technology level. |
| 71%–85% | Deep Technology | The entity is deeply structured around technology. Galactic-scale operations. High-tier technology across most domains. CI Technology at advanced levels if cultivation present. The entity's infrastructure, military, economic, and cultural systems are deeply technology-synergistic. |
| 86%–95% | Apex Technology | Near-maximum technology integration. Technology is the dominant operational paradigm. Nearly all the entity's systems are technology-mediated. High-tier weapons, FTL travel, galactic to universal scale reach. CI Technology at apex if cultivation present. Technology has reshaped the entity's operational identity. |
| 96%–99% | Transcendent Technology | The entity's existence is substantially constituted by technology architecture. Physical structure, ambient operational environment, and functional logic are technology-designed. The boundary between natural and engineered is dissolved. Reserved for entities like fully constructed artificial worlds, deep-space megastructure civilizations, and technology-infrastructure-saturated dimensional spaces. |
| 100% | Absolute Technology | The entity exists entirely as a technology expression. No aspect of its function is outside technology governance. Reserved for constructs like Tier 19–20 civilization megastructures or artificially engineered cosmic entities. Part 28 Tier 2 Authorization required to assign this value in prose. |

### 21.3 — Interaction Between CS%, TS%, and Integration Models

CS% and TS% interact with the Integration Model framework (Area 5) to produce the entity's effective synergy profile. Neither percentage alone determines what integration is accessible — the Integration Model requirements (Part 7, Part 8) govern access to Models A–D. CS% and TS% describe the texture and depth of each track's participation.

**The Four Interaction Zones:**

| CS% Level | TS% Level | Synergy Profile Description |
|---|---|---|
| High (56%+) | High (56%+) | **Balanced Deep Synergy** — the entity draws deeply on both paradigms simultaneously. Integration at Model C or D is the expected operating mode for individual integrated practitioners in this entity. The entity's narrative identity is defined by the co-equal presence of both tracks. Sacred Ground + CI Technology combined environments likely present. The Equalizer Effect (Area 15.1) is continuously active. This is the Starward Saga's primary narrative model for advanced civilizations. |
| High (56%+) | Low (0%–40%) | **Cultivation-Dominant** — the entity is deeply cultivation-synergistic but technology-weak. Cultivators are the primary power expression. Technology serves as a tool where present but does not constitute a paradigm. Integration models are effectively Model A or below. The entity's narrative tension may arise from its vulnerability to technology-dominant entities or its internal resistance to technology adoption. |
| Low (0%–40%) | High (56%+) | **Technology-Dominant** — the entity is deeply technology-synergistic but cultivation-weak. Technology is the operational foundation. Cultivators may exist but are rare or peripheral. CI Technology is absent or minimal. The entity's narrative tension may arise from its inability to access cultivation-sourced capabilities (Sacred Ground synergy, CPM-powered systems, formation arrays) or from its confrontation with cultivation-paradigm entities. |
| Low (0%–40%) | Low (0%–40%) | **Dual-Nascent / Primitive** — the entity has minimal presence in either paradigm. This characterizes very early civilizations, isolated communities, uninhabited or wild locations, primitive weapons and vehicles, and entities in pre-development narrative states. Neither paradigm has meaningfully shaped the entity. |

**Rule 21.3.1 — CS%/TS% Do Not Override Integration Model Requirements:** A character or civilization with CS% = 90% and TS% = 90% still requires Part 7 and Part 8 threshold satisfaction for each Integration Model. CS%/TS% describe the breadth and depth of participation — they are not a substitute for the specific threshold requirements that govern model access.

**Rule 21.3.2 — CS%/TS% Scale With Integration Model Achievement:** As a character advances through Integration Models A → B → C → D, their CS% and TS% typically rise in parallel — because deeper integration means broader and more systemic participation in both paradigms. However, this is a tendency, not a law. A character can have high CS% (deep cultivation ecology participation) while remaining at Model A if their technology access is simply not architecturally integrated at deeper levels. CS%/TS% reflect the total synergistic state of the entity, not merely the integration model floor.

**Rule 21.3.3 — CS%/TS% Inform the Narrative Framing of Integration:** When writing prose, CS% and TS% calibrate the author's understanding of how deeply embedded both tracks are in the narrative environment. A scene set in an entity with CS% = 80% and TS% = 70% is set in a world where cultivation energy and technology are both atmospherically present — ambient qi enrichment, formation arrays visible in the architecture, CI Technology humming alongside cultivation chambers, cultivators and technologists in mixed professional environments. A scene in an entity with CS% = 85% and TS% = 15% is set in a world where cultivation energy dominates and technology is a distant peripheral presence.

### 21.4 — CS%/TS% for Individual Characters (All Character Types)

All individual characters — heroes (Part 17), villains (Part 18), Tier A/B/C side characters (Part 19) — carry personal CS% and TS% values. These values reflect the character's own integrated participation in each paradigm as a functional actor, not just their raw power level.

**What Determines a Character's CS%:**

- Cultivation realm (Part 3) — higher realm = broader cultivation energy interaction, but realm alone does not determine CS%
- Participation in cultivation ecology systems (formation arrays, Sacred Ground cultivation, sect or institutional cultivation practice)
- CPM production and contribution to synergy systems
- Affinity typing depth and breadth (Part 4) — single-affinity specialists may have lower CS% than multi-affinity practitioners at the same realm, because the former's cultivation energy interaction is narrower
- Degree to which cultivation is the character's primary energy framework for all life decisions (not merely combat)
- Cultivation Heart state (Part 4 Area 3) — Sovereign Heart practitioners typically have higher CS% because their cultivation ecology participation is more coherent and stable
- Duration of cultivation practice within cultivation-enriched environments

**What Determines a Character's TS%:**

- Access to and operational use of technology systems (Part 8 tier)
- CI Technology operational level (for integrated practitioners)
- Degree to which technology mediates the character's daily operations, mobility, communication, and combat
- Named Weapon and Named Vehicle technology tier (Part 33, Part 32) in active use
- Technology education, professional identity, and institutional affiliation with technology-producing factions
- Modification, augmentation, or architectural integration of technology into the character's physical person (for Integrated Practitioners at Model C/D)

**Character CS%/TS% Reference Ranges:**

| Character Profile | Typical CS% | Typical TS% |
|---|---|---|
| Unawakened mortal, no technology access | 1%–5% | 1%–10% |
| Mortal Tier 1 cultivator (Skin–Marrow stages), low-tech civilization | 10%–25% | 5%–20% |
| Mortal Tier 2 cultivator (Senior–Monarch), mid-tech civilization | 20%–40% | 15%–35% |
| Xiantian realm cultivator, balanced civilization | 35%–55% | 20%–45% |
| Core realm cultivator, no technology use | 55%–70% | 5%–20% |
| Core realm Integrated Practitioner, Model B | 55%–70% | 40%–60% |
| Divine realm cultivator, technology-peripheral | 65%–85% | 5%–25% |
| Divine realm Integrated Practitioner, Model C | 70%–85% | 60%–75% |
| Model D Unified Practitioner | 80%–95% | 75%–92% |
| Pure technologist, no cultivation practice | 2%–10% | 50%–85% |
| High-tier pure technologist, Tier 12+ civilization, no cultivation | 2%–8% | 75%–92% |
| Wild cultivator, isolation-trained, no technology access | 50%–80% | 1%–8% |

**Rule 21.4.1 — Character CS%/TS% Is Tracked in Character Ledger Section 2:** Both values are Living Information (L) stored in Section 2 (Classification) of the character's ledger file (ledger-character-hero, ledger-character-villain, or ledger-character-side as appropriate per Part 16). They are included in the Pre-Write Ledger Check (Part 1 Area 6.2) before any prose where the character's integrated capabilities are narratively relevant.

**Rule 21.4.2 — Character CS%/TS% Update Triggers:** The following events trigger mandatory Post-Write Ledger Update (Part 1 Area 6.3) to the character's CS% and/or TS%:
- Major realm breakthrough (Part 3) → CS% increase
- Integration Model advancement (A→B, B→C, C→D) → both CS% and TS% increase
- Extended cultivation in Sacred Ground of S-Grade 3+ → CS% increase
- Permanent architectural integration of CI Technology system (Model C/D) → TS% increase
- Loss of access to cultivation ecology (destruction of affiliated formation arrays, loss of sect, exile from Sacred Ground) → CS% decrease
- Destruction or permanent loss of integrated technology systems → TS% decrease
- Major cultivation injury affecting paradigm participation → CS% decrease

### 21.5 — CS%/TS% for Worlds and Planets

Worlds (planets, inhabited locations of world-scale, per Part 13 World Classification W-1 through W-10) carry CS% and TS% values that reflect the aggregate cultivation-technology synergistic state of the world as an entity.

**What Determines a World's CS%:**

- World classification (Part 13 Area 15) — W-5 (Affinity-Rich) worlds have inherently higher baseline CS% than W-1 (Barren) worlds
- Ambient Energy Class (Part 13 Area 2.3) — Class 1 ambient = very low baseline CS%; Class 6–7 ambient = very high baseline CS%
- Special Zone Status (Part 13 Area 2.5) — Sacred Ground presence dramatically elevates CS%; Void Zones may suppress it
- Population of active cultivators and their realm distribution
- Formation array infrastructure density across the world
- CPM extraction and production infrastructure
- Sacred Ground management and cultivation ecosystem maintenance

**What Determines a World's TS%:**

- Technology tier of the civilization currently inhabiting or controlling the world (Part 8, Part 21)
- CI Technology infrastructure deployed on the world
- Technology domain coverage across the world's urban and rural infrastructure
- Transportation network technology tier (Part 15, Part 32)
- Weapons system technology tier (Part 33) present and controlled on the world
- Manufacturing and production infrastructure technology level

**World CS%/TS% Reference Ranges:**

| World Classification (Part 13) | Typical CS% Range | Typical TS% Range |
|---|---|---|
| W-1 (Barren World) — uninhabited | 2%–8% | 0%–3% |
| W-2 (Sparse World) — minimal life | 5%–15% | 0%–5% |
| W-3 (Common World) — inhabited, basic civilization | 15%–35% | 15%–40% |
| W-4 (Affinity-Touched) — mild cultivation-active | 25%–45% | 15%–45% |
| W-5 (Affinity-Rich) — cultivation-significant | 40%–65% | 20%–60% |
| W-6 (Vortex World) — natural formation zone active | 55%–75% | 25%–65% |
| W-7 (Sacred Ground Minor, S-Grade 1–3) | 65%–82% | 25%–70% |
| W-8 (Sacred Ground Major, S-Grade 4–6) | 75%–90% | 30%–75% |
| W-9 (Sacred Ground Apex, S-Grade 7–8) | 85%–95% | 30%–80% |
| W-10 (Dimensional World) | 70%–92% | 35%–85% |

**Rule 21.5.1 — World CS%/TS% Is Tracked in the Location Ledger:** World-tier entities tracked in ledger-worlds-location carry CS% and TS% in their Section 2 (Spatial Data) as Living Information (L). Updates are triggered by Sacred Ground formation or loss, civilization tier advancement of the controlling civilization, major infrastructure events (construction or destruction of formation arrays at world-scale, CI Technology network deployment), and world-level cultivation ecology events.

### 21.6 — CS%/TS% for Star Systems

Star systems (Part 13 Area 16, SS-1 through SS-10) are multi-world entities and their CS%/TS% values reflect the aggregate across the system.

**Star System CS%/TS% Principles:**

- A star system's CS% is the aggregate of its inhabited worlds' cultivation-ecology states, weighted by narrative significance and cultivator population density
- A star system's TS% is the aggregate of its inhabited worlds' technology-infrastructure states, weighted by the controlling civilization's technology tier
- Uninhabited worlds in a system contribute minimally to CS% (ambient energy only) and negligibly to TS%
- Sacred Grounds within a star system contribute significantly to CS% even if they are not on inhabited worlds — Sacred Ground cultivation energy radiates at system scale in some configurations (Part 13, Area 9)
- CI Technology infrastructure deployed system-wide (interplanetary transport networks, system-level defense arrays, star-system formation arrays) contributes to TS%

**Star System CS%/TS% Reference Ranges:**

| Star System Classification | Typical CS% Range | Typical TS% Range |
|---|---|---|
| SS-1 (Barren System) | 1%–5% | 0%–2% |
| SS-2 (Sparse System) | 3%–12% | 2%–10% |
| SS-3 (Common System) | 12%–28% | 12%–35% |
| SS-4 (Affinity System) | 25%–45% | 18%–45% |
| SS-5 (Rich System) | 40%–62% | 22%–58% |
| SS-6 (Vortex System) | 55%–72% | 25%–62% |
| SS-7 (Sacred System Minor) | 65%–80% | 28%–68% |

| SS-9 (Sacred System Apex) | 82%–93% | 32%–78% |
| SS-10 (Dimensional System) | 68%–90% | 35%–82% |

**Rule 21.6.1 — Star System CS%/TS% Is Tracked in the Location Ledger:** Star system entries in ledger-worlds-location carry CS% and TS% in their Spatial Data section.

### 21.7 — CS%/TS% for Star Clusters, Quadrants, Galaxies, and Larger Cosmic Entities

All cosmic-scale entities — Star Clusters (Part 13 Area 17), Star Quadrants (Area 18), Galaxies (Area 19), and larger entities (Area 20) — carry CS%/TS% values representing the aggregate synergistic state at their scale.

**Cosmic-Scale CS%/TS% Principles:**

- At galactic and larger scales, CS% and TS% are statistical expressions of the combined cultivation-technology profile of all entities within the cosmic region
- A galaxy with CS% = 75% contains regions of extreme cultivation richness and regions of minimal cultivation presence, but the aggregate across all star systems, nebulae, Sacred Ground networks, and cultivator populations within it produces the 75% value
- A galaxy with TS% = 60% has broad but uneven technology infrastructure across its civilizations, with some advanced and some primitive
- Cosmic entities do not have CS% or TS% values updated at the pace of individual character events — they shift on civilizational timescales (decades or longer for quadrant-scale, centuries or longer for galactic-scale)
- The Primordium / Absolute Chaos (Part 13 Area 21) is not assigned CS%/TS% values — it is beyond classification per P13-1

**Cosmic Entity CS%/TS% Reference Ranges:**

| Cosmic Entity Classification | Typical CS% Range | Typical TS% Range |
|---|---|---|
| SC-1 to SC-3 (Sparse to Common Cluster) | 5%–30% | 5%–35% |
| SC-4 to SC-6 (Affinity to Vortex Cluster) | 28%–58% | 20%–55% |
| SC-7 to SC-9 (Sacred Cluster Minor to Apex) | 55%–85% | 25%–72% |
| SC-10 (Dimensional Cluster) | 60%–88% | 30%–78% |
| SQ-1 to SQ-4 (Barren to Affinity Quadrant) | 5%–45% | 5%–45% |
| SQ-5 to SQ-7 (Rich to Sacred Quadrant Minor) | 35%–70% | 25%–65% |
| SQ-8 to SQ-10 (Sacred to Dimensional Quadrant) | 60%–88% | 30%–78% |
| Galaxy-scale entities | 10%–90% | 10%–88% |
| Galaxy Cluster and above | Author-assessed per world-state | Author-assessed per world-state |

**Rule 21.7.1 — Cosmic-Scale CS%/TS% Requires Author Assessment, Not Formula:** At galaxy-cluster and larger scales, CS% and TS% values are assigned by author assessment based on world-building choices about the dominant character of the cosmic region. These values should be internally consistent with the cultivation-technology profiles of the civilizations and locations established within that region.

### 21.8 — CS%/TS% for Factions

Factions (Part 22) carry CS% and TS% values reflecting the faction's collective paradigm integration — the degree to which the faction, as a social and operational entity, is structured around and benefits from each paradigm.

**What Determines a Faction's CS%:**

- Cultivation realm distribution of faction members
- Formation array assets controlled by the faction
- CPM supply chain and holdings (Part 11)
- Sacred Ground access (directly controlled or alliance-access)
- Faction doctrine orientation (CPT-1 cultivation-pure vs. CPT-5 syncretic) — Part 25
- Institutional cultivation training programs and cultivation advancement support
- Cultivation-derived power in faction hierarchy (whether realm-rank is the primary leadership axis)

**What Determines a Faction's TS%:**

- Technology tier of technology systems controlled or fielded by the faction
- CI Technology assets (SII affiliation, Model-level capability of faction practitioners)
- Weapon system technology tier (Part 33 — WS class distribution across faction arsenal)
- Vehicle/fleet technology tier (Part 32 — Scale Class distribution across faction fleet)
- Technology-based intelligence and communications infrastructure (Part 24)
- Technology-producing capacity (manufacturing, research labs, Part 10 science infrastructure)
- Whether technology-tier positions constitute the faction's primary hierarchy axis

### 21.9 — CS%/TS% for Civilizations

Civilizations (Part 21) carry CS% and TS% values reflecting the civilization's aggregate paradigm integration across all members, territories, and systems. These are among the most significant CS%/TS% values in the canon because civilizations are the primary entities through which both paradigms are developed and deployed at scale.

**What Determines a Civilization's CS%:**

- Cultivation Axis (CuA-C) rating from Part 21 — the primary determinant; CuA-C score maps directly to cultivation-side paradigm depth
- EA Axis (Part 21 Area 2) — energy paradigm axis reflects cultivation energy access and integration
- Cultivator population distribution across realm tiers within the civilization
- Formation array infrastructure density across the civilization's controlled territories
- CPM production infrastructure scale (Part 11)
- Sacred Ground network — number, grade, and access distribution
- Cultural Paradigm Type (CPT, Part 25) — CPT-1 through CPT-6 determines ideological orientation toward cultivation paradigm
- Cultivation advancement institutions (sects, academies, training infrastructure)

**What Determines a Civilization's TS%:**

- Technology Axis (TA) rating from Part 21 — the primary determinant for technology-side depth
- CTD (Civilization Tier Designation) and CCT — higher CTD = broader institutional access to technology paradigm depth
- Technology domain coverage (Part 8) — how many of the 14 technology domains are developed and at what level
- CI Technology level within the civilization's institutional infrastructure
- Research and development infrastructure (Part 10)
- Technology manufacturing capacity
- Military technology tier across the civilization's defense systems (Part 33, Part 32)

A cultivation-dominant civilization (below TS% tendency range) will have its TS% shifted downward; a technology-dominant civilization (below CS% tendency range) will have its CS% shifted downward. The CTD sets the ceiling; the CuA-C and TA axes within that CTD determine where within the range the civilization sits. For an apex-phase civilization within its CTD (per Part 21 Area 3.3 — all axes 2+ levels above the CTD minimum and within 2 levels of the next CTD minimum), CS%/TS% values may approach the upper bounds of the CTD band; nascent-phase civilizations occupy the lower bounds. The 20-tier CTD ladder supersedes the prior 15-tier Hold Designation ladder that appeared in Part 31 prior to v3.1 — all ledger entries and cross-Part references now use CTD exclusively.

**Rule 21.9.1 — Civilization CS%/TS% Is Tracked in the Civilization Ledger:** Civilization entries in ledger-people-society-civilization carry CS% and TS% as Living Information (L) in Section 2 (Power Axes). Updates are triggered by CTD transitions, TA axis breakthrough events, CuA-C axis shifts, Sacred Ground network changes, and major infrastructure events.

### 21.10 — CS%/TS% for Relics and Artifacts

Relics and artifacts (Part 12) carry CS% and TS% values reflecting the degree to which the object is integrated with and operating through each paradigm.

**What Determines a Relic's CS%:**

- Relic tier (Part 12 — Tier 1 through Tier 9) — higher tier = deeper cultivation energy integration
- Affinity character of the relic — single-affinity specialized relics may have higher CS% in that affinity domain; null-affinity relics have lower baseline CS%
- Formation array density and complexity within the relic
- Soul Bond status (Part 12 Area 11) — Soul-Bonded relics have higher CS% because the soul-bond constitutes a deep cultivation-energy architecture integration
- Sacred Ground formation or infusion history of the relic
- Whether the relic actively produces, stores, or amplifies cultivation energy as its primary function

**What Determines a Relic's TS%:**

- Technology-Relic Hybrid class (Part 12, Part 8 Area 6.3) — Hybrid Class 1 through 3; higher class = higher TS%
- Manufactured component density within the relic
- CI Technology integration level of the relic's technology components
- Whether the relic is primarily a technology system with cultivation augmentation (high TS%, moderate CS%) vs. a cultivation artifact with technology components (high CS%, moderate TS%)
- Power source architecture — does the relic use technology energy, cultivation energy, or both

**Relic/Artifact CS%/TS% Reference Ranges:**

| Relic Category | Typical CS% | Typical TS% |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 Relic (natural formation, no tech) | 25%–45% | 0%–5% |
| Tier 3 Relic (cultivation-active) | 45%–65% | 0%–10% |
| Tier 5 Relic (Sacred Ground-formation) | 65%–80% | 0%–15% |
| Tier 7+ Relic (apex cultivation artifact) | 75%–90% | 5%–20% |
| Technology-Relic Hybrid Class 1 | 40%–60% | 35%–55% |
| Technology-Relic Hybrid Class 2 | 45%–65% | 50%–70% |
| Technology-Relic Hybrid Class 3 | 55%–75% | 60%–80% |
| Pure Technology Artifact (no cultivation) | 3%–10% | 55%–85% |

**Rule 21.10.1 — Relic CS%/TS% Is Tracked in the Relic Ledger:** Relic entries in ledger-energy-relics-artifacts carry CS% and TS% as Base Information (B) established at entry creation, with Living Information (L) updates triggered by Soul Bond formation, Hybrid class upgrade, relic damage or enhancement, and Sacred Ground infusion events.

- T-SYN class (Part 33 — Synergy Strike weapons) — T-SYN weapons with higher cultivation-side requirements have elevated CS%
- Soul Bond status — Soul-Bonded weapons have higher CS% than non-bonded equivalents
- Formation array integration within the weapon
- CPM channel architecture — weapons with CPM channels have higher CS%
- Affinity typing of the weapon — deeply affinity-typed weapons have higher CS% in their affinity domain

**What Determines a Weapon's TS%:**

- Weapon technology tier — the Part 8 technology tier of the weapon's manufactured components

### 21.13 — CS%/TS% for Locations (Non-World Tier)

Named locations below world-tier (settlements: villages, towns, cities, capitals per Part 13 Areas 3–6; special zones: Sacred Grounds, Formation Zones, Void Zones, Dimensional Rifts) carry CS% and TS% values.

**Settlement CS%/TS%:**

- Settlement CS% reflects the cultivation-ecology of the settlement — cultivators present, formation arrays deployed, ambient qi level (Part 13 Area 2.3), Sacred Ground proximity
- Settlement TS% reflects the technology infrastructure of the settlement — technology tier of buildings, transportation, communications, energy systems

**Special Zone CS%/TS%:**

- Sacred Grounds have inherently high CS% — S-Grade 1 begins at approximately CS% = 55%; S-Grade 8 approaches CS% = 95–98%
- Sacred Ground TS% depends entirely on whether technology infrastructure has been deployed within the Sacred Ground's influence zone
- Void Zones have suppressed CS% (cultivation energy is hostile within them) — a Void Zone may have CS% = 5–15% despite being a supernatural location
- Dimensional Rifts and Resonance Planes have variable CS%/TS% depending on their dimensional properties
- Formation Zones have elevated CS% from their natural formation energy

**Rule 21.13.1 — Location CS%/TS% Below World-Tier Is Tracked in Location Ledger:** Named location files in ledger-worlds-location that represent sub-world-tier entities (Sacred Grounds, major settlements, special zones) include CS% and TS% in their Section 2 (Spatial Data).

### 21.14 — CS%/TS% Governance: Update Protocol, Commands, and Audit

**Update Protocol:**
CS% and TS% are Living Information (L) for all entity types (except where designated as Base Information (B) at relic/weapon entry creation for the initial establishment value). They are governed by the Part 1 Area 6 Post-Write Ledger Update Protocol. The following prose events trigger CS%/TS% update assessment:

1. Any cultivation realm breakthrough or regression for a character
2. Any Integration Model change for a character or faction
3. CTD (Civilization Tier Designation) change for a civilization
4. Sacred Ground formation, activation, destruction, or grade change for a location
5. Significant formation array deployment or destruction at scale
6. Major CI Technology infrastructure investment or loss for a faction or civilization
7. Named Weapon or Vehicle CHV class change or soul-bond formation
8. Civilization-scale CPM supply change (Part 11) that affects cultivation ecology depth
9. Cultural paradigm shift (Part 25) that reorients a civilization's cultivation vs. technology ideological orientation

**Pre-Write Ledger Check for CS%/TS%:**
Before writing any prose where an entity's synergistic capability is directly relevant (Integrated Practitioner combat, civilization-scale synergy events, Sacred Ground interactions, combined cultivation-technology operations), the Pre-Write Ledger Check (Part 1 Area 6.2) reads the relevant entity's CS% and TS% values to calibrate the prose-level description of the entity's synergistic presence.

**Command Interface:**
- **[STATUS] (C-13):** Reports current CS%/TS% values for major characters, active locations, and currently relevant civilizations and factions alongside other status information.
- **[CHARACTER SHEET] (C-19):** Renders CS% and TS% as part of Section 2 (Classification) of the character's ledger, alongside realm, integration model, and other classification fields.
- **[RUN CANON AUDIT] (C-10):** Includes consistency check of CS%/TS% values against established narrative events — verifies that claimed CS%/TS% values are consistent with the entity's established cultivation realm, technology tier, and synergistic history.
- **[CANON CHECK] (C-20):** Passages depicting entities operating at synergistic depth inconsistent with their CS%/TS% values are flagged as potential Part 31 violations.

**Rule 21.14.1 — CS%/TS% Cannot Be Self-Assigned Without Basis:** An entity's CS% and TS% are not cosmetic labels. They must be grounded in the entity's established canonical history: cultivation ecology, technology infrastructure, integration model, Sacred Ground access, CPM production, and relevant domain participation. Authors may not assign CS% = 90% to a character whose ledger shows no cultivation realm, no formation array interaction, and no CPM production. The value must reflect the entity's actual canonical state.

**Rule 21.14.2 — Asymmetric CS%/TS% Profiles Are Narratively Significant:** An entity with strongly asymmetric CS%/TS% (CS% = 85%, TS% = 15%; or CS% = 10%, TS% = 88%) is a narratively exceptional entity — a paradigm specialist. This asymmetry should be reflected in the prose: the entity operates confidently within its dominant paradigm and is either disadvantaged, cautious, or dependent on allies within the paradigm it lacks. Symmetric high values (CS% = 80%, TS% = 78%) indicate a balanced integration entity — one of the rarest and most powerful narrative configurations in the Starward Saga.

**Rule 21.14.3 — CS%/TS% = 96–100% Requires Part 28 Authorization:** Values in the 96–100% range for either axis represent entities that are paradigm-constituted in a fundamental sense — entities whose very existence is an expression of that paradigm. Assigning such values to any entity requires Part 28 (Novel Creative Flexibility Law) Tier 2 Authorization, as these values represent the upper extreme of the world's synergistic reality.

### 21.15 — CS%/TS% in Prose: Narration Standards

**Authors should:**

- Treat CS%/TS% as atmosphere calibrators, not power labels. High CS% means the cultivation energy paradigm is atmospherically present in how the entity moves, speaks, and exists — not merely that it can fight at a high level.
- Use CS%/TS% to calibrate how naturally an entity navigates each paradigm's environment. A character with CS% = 78% and TS% = 22% feels fully at home in a Sacred Ground and disoriented in a pure technology installation. The reverse for TS% = 80%, CS% = 18%.
- Reflect asymmetric CS%/TS% in the entity's blind spots and gaps. Low TS% entities may underestimate technology threats; low CS% entities may fail to perceive cultivation energy phenomena that are obvious to high-CS% observers.
- Use the gap between a character's CS% and the CS%/TS% of their environment as a source of dramatic tension. A CS% = 35% character in a CS% = 88% Sacred Ground environment is out of paradigm depth — overwhelmed by cultivation energy they cannot fully process.
- In cosmic-scale scenes (involving star systems, galaxies, quadrants), use CS%/TS% to establish the dominant synergistic character of the region before individual entity interactions — the reader should understand whether they are in a cultivation-rich or technology-rich or balanced cosmic region.

**The CS%/TS% Atmosphere Test:**
Before writing any scene in a new environment or introducing an entity for the first time, authors should check: "What are the CS% and TS% values for this entity/location? What does that mean for how I describe it?" A CS% = 90%, TS% = 15% Sacred Ground city feels different from a CS% = 45%, TS% = 82% technology megacity even if both are high-power environments. The cultivation-energy atmosphere of the former saturates the description; the engineered precision and scale of the latter dominates.
