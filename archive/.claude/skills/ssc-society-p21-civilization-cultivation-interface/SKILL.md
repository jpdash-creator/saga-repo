## Area 11 — Civilization and Cultivation Interface

### 11.1 — Cultivation as Civilizational Infrastructure

Cultivation is not merely individual power in the Starward Saga — it is civilizational infrastructure. A civilization's CA value represents cultivators functioning as:

- **Administrative infrastructure:** Soul-sense-based communication, record-keeping, and surveillance exceeding early technology equivalents.
- **Economic infrastructure:** CPM production, refinement, and trade as economic substrate.
- **Military infrastructure:** Cultivators as force multipliers that scale non-linearly with technology military force.
- **Research infrastructure:** Soul-sense observation contributing to scientific prerequisites (Part 10 Area 7).
- **Spiritual infrastructure:** Cultivation as the cultural value system that motivates population behavior (Part 25 adjacent governance).

### 11.2 — The Sacred Ground Civilization Milestone

Sacred Ground formation (Part 13 Area 14) within a civilization's territory is one of the most significant CA and EA advancement events possible. It:

- Drives CA upward by providing ambient energy Class 7 cultivation environments that accelerate practitioner advancement.
- Drives EA upward by potentially providing Divine Liquid (Grade 20 CPM) formation zones at S-Grade 5+.
- Drives SA upward by creating the ideal environment for technology-cultivation synergy experiments (Part 31 Area 10).
- Increases the civilization's status and attractiveness as a destination, driving population and practitioner immigration.

**Rule 11.2.1 — Sacred Ground Ownership:** The faction or civilization that administers a Sacred Ground gains the full CA, EA, and SA benefits. Contest over Sacred Ground administration is among the most common causes of inter-civilization conflict.

### 11.3 — Cultivation Realm Distribution and CA Assessment

CA is not assessed by the single peak cultivator — a civilization where one Divine Sea Realm cultivator exists among a billion non-cultivators is not CA-12. Distribution matters:

**Distribution Requirements for CA Assessment:**
- CA-8 and below: Peak realm is sufficient indicator if the civilization has functional institutional structures.
- CA-9 and above: Both peak realm AND institutional distribution (percentage of population at Core Grade or above) must satisfy the criteria in Area 2.1.
- CA-13 and above: The civilization must have governance infrastructure specifically designed to identify, develop, and utilize high-realm cultivators at civilizational scale.
