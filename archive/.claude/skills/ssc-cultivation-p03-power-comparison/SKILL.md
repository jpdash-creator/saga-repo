## Area 7 — Power Comparison

### 7.1 — The Hierarchy of Comparison

When two cultivators are compared for combat or survival assessment, the following priority hierarchy governs:

**Tier 1 — Major Realm (Highest Priority):**
Major Realm difference (Mortal vs Xiantian vs Core vs Divine) is always determinative. A Core Realm cultivator cannot be overcome by any Mortal or Xiantian Realm cultivator through any normal means. Major Realm differences cannot be overcome by skill, technique, environment, or any factor short of divine artifact use, specific narrative plot mechanics covered by Part 28 (Creative Flexibility), or the limits of the realm ceiling.

**Tier 2 — Cultivation Realm:**
Within the same Major Realm, the specific Cultivation Realm (e.g., Warrior vs Monarch within Mortal Tier 2) determines the base power relationship. A Monarch at Level 1 Low will defeat a Senior at Level 9 Peak in a straightforward contest. Realm difference within a Major Realm is significant but not absolutely decisive — extreme skill differentials can narrow the gap.

**Tier 3 — Level:**
Within the same Cultivation Realm, Level difference (e.g., Level 3 vs Level 7 of Warrior) is the next determining factor. A 3-level gap within a single Realm is the threshold at which the higher-level cultivator becomes effectively invincible to the lower in equal conditions. Below a 3-level gap, skill and other factors can legitimately decide outcomes.

**Tier 4 — Sub-Level:**
Within the same Level, the sub-level (Low/Mid/High/Peak) difference determines fine-grained power advantage. A sub-level difference of 1–2 steps can be overcome by skill advantage, environmental advantage, or technique mastery. A sub-level difference of 3–4 steps (full Level gap equivalent) returns to Level-difference rules.

### 7.2 — Modifying Factors

**Affinity Advantage (Canon-Part 4):**
A cultivator whose affinity alignment reaches Harmony level (Level 6) or above gains an amplification effect on affinity-aligned technique output equivalent to up to one Cultivation Realm in effective power. This is not a raw power density change — it is an amplification effect in aligned technique execution. A Warrior-level cultivator with Harmony-level Fire Affinity produces fire technique output comparable to low Champion. This can tip Realm-boundary confrontations where aligned techniques are relevant. This amplification does not override the 3-Level Rule or Major Realm barriers. Full rules: Canon-Part 4, Area 1.5.

**Skill and Move Advantage (Part 6):**
Mastery of techniques governed by Part 6 provides a combat effectiveness multiplier. A cultivator with exceptional move mastery fighting a stronger opponent can function as if their Level is half a Level higher than actual in practical combat. This is not a power density change — the stronger cultivator is still stronger — but skill can manufacture outcomes that raw power comparison would seem to forbid. Skill advantage cannot overcome a 3-Level gap.

**Environmental Advantage:**
A cultivator in a high-energy environment, fighting in terrain that suits their specific cultivation type, or actively drawing on environmental energy sources during combat gains up to a half-Level equivalent boost. This is temporary and location-dependent. Environmental advantage cannot overcome a 3-Level gap.

**The 3-Level Rule:**
A 3-Level gap (within the same Cultivation Realm) is the absolute threshold. Above this gap, no combination of skill, environment, technique, or external resource can produce an outcome where the lower cultivator defeats the higher through a fair contest. The 3-Level Rule is inviolable in canon unless a specific creative variance is declared under Part 28 and Part 1's variance process.

### 7.3 — Comparing Across Realm Boundaries

When comparing cultivators across Realm boundaries (e.g., Mortal Tier 2 Monarch Level 9 Peak vs Xiantian Demigod Level 1 Low), the **5× entry multiplier** at the Major Realm boundary always applies. A Demigod entry-level cultivator is categorically stronger than the Mortal Tier 2 summit — not merely by raw power but by the nature of what their power can do. Mortal-tier physical refinement cannot process or respond to Xiantian energy expressions.

This means: a Mortal Tier 2 Monarch does not "almost match" a Demigod. They are in different operational categories. The Demigod does not simply hit harder — they operate under different physical rules.
