## Area 8 — Technology Travel: Foundational Framework

### 8.1 — Technology Travel Overview

Technology travel is the systematic application of scientific principles and industrial capability to move people, goods, and information across distances that exceed individual biological or cultivation capability. Technology travel is governed by Part 8 (Technology Scale Law) for the capability framework and Part 9 (Technology Growth Law) for how those capabilities are reached. Part 15 governs the specific travel mechanics, transit times, and infrastructure rules that those capabilities imply.

Technology travel operates across five transport paradigms that emerge in sequence as civilization advances:
1. **Surface Transport** — ground, water, and atmospheric travel within a planetary environment (Tiers 1–5)
2. **Orbital and Inner-System Transport** — movement between planetary bodies within a star system (Tiers 5–7)
3. **Interstellar Transport** — movement between star systems via sub-light or FTL drives (Tiers 8–10)
4. **Galactic Transport** — movement across galactic scales via gate networks and advanced FTL (Tiers 11–14)
5. **Cosmological Transport** — movement at universe and beyond scales (Tiers 15–20; partially deferred)

### 8.2 — Technology Travel Type Classification

All technology travel vehicles and systems are classified under **Part 8 Type 9 (Transportation Technology)**. The twenty levels of Type 9 correspond directly to the five transport paradigms. Part 15 expands the Type 9 definitions with full travel mechanics for each level.
