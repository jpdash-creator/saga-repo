## Area 30 — Stub Development Guidance for Unstubbed Areas

### 30.1 — Part 9 Summary (Technology Growth Law — Fully Authored)

Part 9 (Technology Growth Law) is fully authored. The integration requirements originally described here have been resolved as follows:

- Advancement timeline mechanics for all 20 tier transitions: Part 9, Area 5 (Standard Advancement Timelines) and Area 4 (Tier Transition Prerequisite Reference Table)
- Intermediate tier transition timelines (30–50% faster than anchor tier equivalents): Part 9, Area 5.3
- Acceleration and deceleration events: Part 9, Areas 6 and 7
- Regression and civilizational collapse dynamics: Part 9, Area 8
- Inter-civilizational technology competition (factional competition as 10–20% acceleration): Part 9, Area 9.3
- CPM abundance effect on advancement (CI Technology and Medical domain acceleration; CPM Growth Cycle): Part 9, Areas 6.6 and 10.3

Part 8's independence principle (P8-2.1: cultivation realm advancement is not a prerequisite for technology advancement) is explicitly honored in Part 9, P9-11 (Technology Growth Is Foundation of All Other Advancement) and the domain-independence principle in Part 9, Area 10.

### 30.2 — Part 10 Integration Note (Technology Science Law — Fully Authored)

Part 10 (Technology Science Law) is fully authored (Areas 1–16, v1.0, 2026-04-12). All prior stub guidance in this section has been resolved:

- **Tier-by-Tier Scientific Prerequisites:** Part 10 Area 3 provides the complete prerequisite table for all 20 tier transitions, specifying required Knowledge Layers (L1–L4) and critical discovery requirements at each transition. These supersede all [P10 stub] entries previously in Part 9 Area 4.2.
- **FTL Prerequisites:** Part 10 Area 5 governs the FTL Physics Framework in full: three prerequisite categories (Spacetime Geometry Theory L3+, Exotic Matter Physics L3, Energy Architecture Engineering L3); Minimum Physical Duration of 2–5 generations from first FTL-relevant anomaly to working prototype; maximum cultivation contribution reduces timeline by up to 40% but cannot compress below minimum.
- **AI Sentience Classification:** Part 10 Area 6 governs the full AI Sentience Ontology — S1 Pre-Conscious (Levels 1–4), S2 Proto-Sentient (Level 5), S3 Genuine Sentient (Level 6 — True AGI, joint Part 4 governance), S4 Trans-Human Sentient (Levels 7–12), S5 Absolute Intelligence (Level 20); Digital Consciousness DC-1/DC-2/DC-3 classification; Robotic Ontology R1–R3.
- **Trans-Universal Science (Tier 17+):** Part 10 Areas 3 and 11 govern Tier 17–20 scientific prerequisites; at Tier 17+, technology science and cultivation knowledge are treated as two vocabularies for the same underlying reality; joint Part 4 governance required.
- **Dark Matter/Dark Energy Science:** Part 10 Area 4 (Type 7 Energy Physics, Level 13+) and Area 3 (Tier 13→14 transition) govern the theoretical framework prerequisites for dark matter/dark energy harnessing.
- **Cultivation-Science Interaction:** Part 10 Area 7 (Science-Cultivation Interface) governs soul sense observational resolution by realm, breakthrough-state scientific observation, and energy architecture intuition by affinity domain; Part 10 Area 13 (Interface with Part 31) governs the scientific advantage multipliers for integrated civilizations.

### 30.3 — Part 13 Integration Guidance (Location Law)

When Part 13 (Location Law) is authored, it will need to:

- Define what Part 8 technology-enhanced environments (Area 7.3) can and cannot do to Sacred Ground formation
- Establish whether Tier 8+ ambient qi enrichment technology can create new Sacred Grounds or merely mimic their energy properties
- Govern the interaction of megastructure construction (Level 11+ Engineering) with existing Sacred Ground spatial properties
- Address the "cultivator-enhancing formation arrays as architecture" question (Area 15.6.2) — what Part 8 provides architecturally and what Part 13 governs spatially

### 30.4 — Part 21 Integration Guidance (Civilization Law — Stub)

When Part 21 (Civilization Law) is authored, it will need to:

- Use Part 8, Area 5 (Civilization Classification System) as the technology-axis input
- Integrate cultivation realm axis (from Part 3), energy axis (from Part 11), and location axis (from Part 13) alongside the technology axis
- Define how the four axes interact in the overall civilization tier determination — does the lowest axis determine overall tier? Average? Weighted combination?
- Address civilizations that are extremely asymmetric across axes (high technology, low cultivation; or vice versa)
- Maintain the independence of the technology and cultivation axes established by Part 8 (P8-2.1, P8-6): the four-axis model should not reintroduce hidden cultivation-technology dependencies

> **[Repo layout note]** Both 30.3 and 30.4 above frame Part 13 (Location Law) and Part 21 (Civilization Law) as not-yet-authored stubs. In the current repo, both are fully authored: see `.claude/skills/ssc-worlds-p13-*` (26 skills) and `.claude/skills/ssc-society-p21-*` (22 skills). The integration guidance above should be treated as historical authoring notes to reconcile against those skills' actual content, not as a statement of current canon completeness.

Part 8 explicitly yields to Part 21 for overall civilization tier classification (Rule 5.1.3). When Part 21 is authored, it becomes the final governance authority for civilization tier; Part 8 provides the technology-axis input only.
