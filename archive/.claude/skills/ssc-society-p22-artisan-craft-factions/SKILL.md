## Area 12 — Artisan and Craft Factions

### 12.1 — Overview: Artisan and Craft Factions (ACF)

Artisan and Craft Factions are organized institutions whose primary purpose is the creation of objects — relics, artifacts, technology products, weapons, armor, formation arrays, buildings, ships, and all other manufactured goods. They sit at the intersection of cultivation craftsmanship (Part 12 governance for relic objects) and technology manufacturing (Part 8 governance for technology products).

**Natural Scale Range:** Village (SCL-1) through Galaxy (SCL-11)

### 12.2 — Artisan Faction Classifications

| Artisan Type | Products | Scale | Governing Part |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blacksmith Guild | Mundane weapons, tools, basic armor | SCL-1 to SCL-3 | Part 8 (basic manufacturing) |
| Armorsmith Association | Combat armor; protective equipment | SCL-2 to SCL-5 | Part 8 + Part 12 (enchanted armor) |
| Inscriptors' Guild | Formation array inscription; relic empowerment | SCL-3 to SCL-9 | Part 12 (Formation Arrays) |
| Relic Crafters' Association | Relic construction; artifact empowerment | SCL-4 to SCL-10 | Part 12 (Relics and Artifacts) |
| Talisman & Seal Crafters | Combat consumables; single-use Formation seals | SCL-2 to SCL-7 | Part 12 (Artifacts) |
| Technology-Relic Hybrid Workshops | Class 1–3 Hybrid construction (Part 12, Area 13) | SCL-7 to SCL-12 | Parts 7, 8, 12 joint governance |
| Ship Construction Guilds | Spacecraft; void vessels; atmospheric craft | SCL-7 to SCL-12 | Part 8 (Type 9 Transportation) |
| Architectural Engineering Firms | Megastructure construction; habitation engineering | SCL-9 to SCL-13 | Part 8 (Type 6 Engineering) |
| Formation Array Architecture Firms | Planetary and system-scale formation grid design | SCL-7 to SCL-11 | Part 12 (Formation Arrays) |

### 12.3 — Artisan Hierarchy Model

Artisan factions use a **Master-Apprentice Craft Ladder** — the universal skilled trade hierarchy:

| Rank | Title | Capability |
|---|---|---|
| Grand Master Craftsman | Sage Craftsman / Ancestor Inscriptor | Legendary output; unique creations |
| Master Craftsman | Relic Master / Master Inscriptor | Tier 4 relics; Tier 3 arrays |
| Journeyman Craftsman | Senior Craftsman | Tier 2–3 relics; Tier 2 arrays |
| Apprentice Craftsman | Craftsman Apprentice | Tier 1 relics; Tier 1 arrays; mundane products |
| Workshop Staff | Furnace Tender / Material Handler | Support roles; no independent creation |
