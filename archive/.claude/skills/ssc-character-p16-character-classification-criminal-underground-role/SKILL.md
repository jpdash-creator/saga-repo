## Area 8 — Character Classification by Criminal and Underground Role

### 8.1 — Criminal Character Architecture

Criminal characters occupy a distinct structural position in the Starward Saga — they operate outside or against institutional authority, and their character classification reflects that structural opposition.

**Canonical Criminal Character Types:**

| Criminal Role | Description | Governing Parts |
|--------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Street Criminal / Thug** | Low-level force-for-hire; no institutional affiliation; opportunistic | Parts 22, 25 |
| **Enforcer / Hired Muscle** | Employed violence; serves criminal organization or faction | Parts 22, 5 |
| **Thief / Infiltrator** | Skill-based criminal; stealth, deception, lock-circumvention | Parts 22, 24, 6 |
| **Assassin (Independent)** | Skilled individual killer; operates on contract; may have cultivation or technology specialization | Parts 22, 5, 24 |
| **Smuggler** | Black-market goods movement; operates in information asymmetry | Parts 22, 24, 15 |
| **Spy / Counter-Intelligence Agent** | Information operative for a criminal or rival faction | Parts 22, 24 |
| **Crime Boss / Syndicate Leader** | Organizational head of criminal faction; W-Class and A-Class elevated despite institutional exclusion | Parts 22, 25 |
| **Underground Cultivator** | Cultivator who operates outside legitimate sect structure; may have stolen techniques or forbidden cultivation paths | Parts 3, 22, 24 |
