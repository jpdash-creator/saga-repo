## Area 3 — Character Classification by Technology

### 3.1 — Technology Character Classification Framework

In technology-dominant or hybrid civilizations, characters are classified by their relationship to technology: the technology tier they operate, the integration model they use, and their professional role within the technology system.

**Technology-Character Tier Classification (by Part 8 Technology Scale):**

| Technology Tier Range | Character Scale | Narrative Context |
|----------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1–2** | Settlement/Local practitioner | Basic tool users, early mechanical operators, pre-industrial technicians |
| **Tier 3–4** | Industrial/Planetary specialist | Factory operators, engineers, early weapons technicians, planetary infrastructure builders |
| **Tier 5–6** | Orbital/Early-Space operator | Starship crew, aerospace engineers, orbital platform technicians, orbital defense operators |
| **Tier 7** | Solar-System-scale specialist | Tier 7 military operators; solar-system infrastructure designers; first-generation FTL navigators |
| **Tier 8–10** | Interstellar operator | FTL pilots, stargate engineers, interstellar military commanders, interstellar intelligence operatives |
| **Tier 11–14** | Galactic-scale specialist | Galactic fleet admirals, galactic gate network architects, galactic intelligence directors |
| **Tier 15–20** | Cosmological/Absolute-tier operator | Characters whose technology relationship exceeds ordinary narrative scale; Part 28 consultation required |

### 3.2 — Technology Integration Character Types

Based on their relationship to cultivation-technology integration (Part 7 and Part 31):

| Integration Character Type | Integration Model | Description |
|--------------------------|------------------|-------------|
| **Pure Technology Operator** | None (WU-I-A, WU-I-B) | No cultivation; operates technology by skill and training alone; maximizes technology tier capability |
| **Technology-Enhanced Cultivator** | Model A–B (Light) | Cultivator who uses technology as tactical supplement; cultivation remains primary; technology enhances mobility, communication, logistics |
| **Integrated Practitioner** | Model B–C | Genuine cultivation-technology synthesis; neither is purely primary; synergy outputs arise from integration; Part 31 governs outputs |
| **Deep Integration Practitioner** | Model C–D | Technology is inseparable from self; identity considerations arise (Part 20 ADI/FSI tensions); Part 26 Area 12 (integration psychology) applies |
| **Model D Fusion Individual** | Model D | The deepest integration; cognitive architecture is partially technological; identity boundary questions (Part 20 IS-4/5 risk); Part 26 governs |

### 3.3 — Technology Professional Character Roles

Technology civilizations produce distinct professional character categories that are the equivalent of cultivation profession types:

**Technology Professional Character Types:**

| Professional Role | Description | Primary Technology Domains (Part 8) |
|-----------------|-------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Combat Soldier (Tech)** | Individual weapons operator; WU-I-A or WU-I-B; the technology equivalent of a combat cultivator | Military (8), Automation (3) |
| **Combat Specialist (Tech Elite)** | Advanced weapons certification; WU-I-B; equivalent to a combat specialist cultivator | Military (8), Materials Science (14) |
| **Engineer / Constructor** | Technology creation, maintenance, and infrastructure; builds what soldiers operate | Engineering (6), Industrial (1), Energy (7) |
| **Scientist / Researcher** | Knowledge frontier operators; discovery agents; their work enables the next technology tier | All domains; Information (2), AI (4) |
| **Pilot / Navigator** | Vehicle operators; transportation specialists; WU-II through WU-III at high vehicle tiers | Transportation (9), Space (10) |
| **Medical Technician** | Technology-based healing; augmentation surgery; integrated practitioner medical support | Medical (5) |
| **Intelligence Operative (Tech)** | Information collection, signal interception, AI-assisted analysis; Part 24 operators | Information (2), Sensory/Detection (13), AI (4) |
| **AI Governance Specialist** | Experts in AI management at Sentience Level S3–S5; the rarest and most critical technology character type | AI (4) |
| **Weapon Systems Commander** | Fleet or installation commanders; WU-IV through WU-VI; large-scale weapon deployment authority | Military (8), Space (10) |
