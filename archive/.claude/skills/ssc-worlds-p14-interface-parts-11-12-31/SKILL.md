## Area 15 — Interface with Parts 11, 12, 31

### 15.1 — Energy Substance Formation Distance

Part 11 (Energy Resource Law) governs what forms in Energy Formation Zones. Part 13 governs the spatial structure of those zones. Part 14 governs the distance over which those zones' effects extend:

- An Energy Formation Zone's substance-formation field extends to its spatial boundary (governed by Part 13 Area 14)
- The detection of an Energy Formation Zone's presence is governed by Part 14 Area 4 (soul sense at the zone boundary) and Part 14 Area 5 (technology sensor at the zone boundary)
- The distance between Energy Formation Zones (how spread out they are on a planet) is world-building governed by Part 13's classification, not by fixed Part 14 rules

### 15.2 — Formation Array Range

The formation array radius table in Part 14 Area 6 is the canonical distance reference for all Part 12 formation array effect governance. Part 12 governs the object; Part 14 governs the distance to which its effects extend.

### 15.3 — Sacred Ground Synergy Distance

Part 31 (Cultivation-Technology Synergy Law, Area 10) governs Sacred Ground synergy amplification. The spatial extent of that amplification zone is:
- Core zone (full Class 7 ambient effect) = Part 14 Area 7.3 core effect zone for the relevant S-Grade
- Fringe zone (partial ambient enhancement) = Part 14 Area 7.3 fringe influence zone
- Beyond fringe zone: no Sacred Ground synergy amplification applies per P14-SG-1
