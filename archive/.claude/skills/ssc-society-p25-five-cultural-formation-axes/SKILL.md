## Area 2 — The Five Cultural Formation Axes

> **AXES NAMING DISAMBIGUATION — Part 25 vs. Part 21:**
> Part 25 and Part 21 both define "Five Axes" systems but they serve entirely different governance purposes. The five Part 25 Cultural Formation Axes (CA-C, FA-C, LA-C, CuA-C, TA-C) measure how cultural values are shaped by different structural forces. The five Part 21 Civilization Axes (CA, TA, LA, EA, SA) measure actual civilization capability levels for CCT synthesis. These are orthogonal systems — a civilization's CA level (Part 21, how much cultivation power it has) is an INPUT to the CA-C axis (Part 25, how much the civilization's culture is shaped by its civilizational CCT tier) and also an input to the CuA-C axis (Part 25, how much the cultivation realm environment shapes cultural values).
>
> **Abbreviation collision warning:**
> - **CA** (no -C suffix) = Part 21 Cultivation Axis — a civilization's cultivation power depth (numeric levels 1–20)
> - **CA-C** (with -C suffix) = Part 25 Civilization Axis — how CCT tier shapes cultural values (ratings C-1 through C-5)
> - **TA** (no -C suffix) = Part 21 Technology Axis — civilization's technology tier from Part 8 (levels 1–20)
> - **TA-C** (with -C suffix) = Part 25 Technology Axis — how technology paradigm shapes culture (C-1 through C-5)
> - **CuA-C** = Part 25 Cultivation Axis (cultural formation side); the equivalent Part 21 axis uses the abbreviation **CA**, not **CuA**
>
> Rule: When writing, always use the -C suffix for Part 25 axes (CA-C, FA-C, LA-C, CuA-C, TA-C). Never use bare "CA", "TA", or "LA" to refer to Part 25 cultural axes — those bare forms belong to Part 21.

### 2.1 — Axis Overview

Every culture in the Starward Saga forms along five primary axes. Any given cultural entity (civilization, faction, sub-culture, individual) receives cultural input from each axis at different intensities. The dominant axis for a given culture is the one that provides the most foundational value structure.

| Axis | Designation | Primary Driver | Governance Area |
|------|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Civilization Axis | CA-C | CCT tier and civilization type | Area 3 |
| Faction Axis | FA-C | Faction type and FPT | Area 5 |
| Location Axis | LA-C | Location class and world type | Area 4 |
| Cultivation Axis | CuA-C | Cultivation realm and affinity | Area 7 |
| Technology Axis | TA-C | Technology tier and paradigm | Area 8 |

### 2.2 — Multi-Axis Cultural Profile

A complete cultural profile for any civilization or faction is written as a Multi-Axis Cultural Profile (MACP):

**MACP Format:** `[CA-C rating] / [FA-C rating] / [LA-C rating] / [CuA-C rating] / [TA-C rating]`

Each axis rating runs from C-1 (Baseline) to C-5 (Apex) indicating how strongly that axis dominates the cultural formation. A cultivation sect on a Sacred Ground with minimal technology would have profile: `CA-C: 2 / FA-C: 5 / LA-C: 4 / CuA-C: 5 / TA-C: 1`. A space-faring mercantile civilization with integrated practitioners: `CA-C: 4 / FA-C: 3 / LA-C: 2 / CuA-C: 3 / TA-C: 5`.

### 2.3 — Axis Dominance and Cultural Conflict

When two axes are both rated C-4 or C-5 in a given cultural entity, internal cultural tension is structurally guaranteed. A cultivation sect (CuA-C: 5) that is also a major trading house (FA-C: 5) carries a permanent internal culture war between cultivation-purity values and exchange-value values. These tensions are narrative assets. Authors should identify dominant-axis conflicts in every major faction.
