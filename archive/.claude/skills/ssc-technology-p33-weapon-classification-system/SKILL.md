## Area 1 — Weapon Classification System

### 1.1 — The Five Weapon Deployment Paradigm Bands

All weapons in the Starward Saga are classified within one of five weapon deployment paradigm bands that correspond to the five technology transport paradigm bands defined in Part 32, Area 1.1, and the five technology travel paradigms in Part 15, Area 8.1. The weapon paradigm band is the primary organizational category — it establishes the scale of operation, theater of deployment, and physics context for the weapon.

| Paradigm Band | Designation | Technology Tier Range | Operational Theater |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface Warfare Band | TW-Band | Tiers 1–5 | Planetary surface, atmosphere, ocean floor; no sustained orbital or space operation |
| Orbital and Inner-System Warfare Band | OW-Band | Tiers 5–7 | Orbit to stellar inner system; subluminal inter-planetary conflict; no FTL deployment |
| Interstellar Warfare Band | IW-Band | Tiers 8–10 | Between star systems; FTL-delivered or FTL-range weapons; gate-launched ordnance |
| Galactic Warfare Band | GW-Band | Tiers 11–14 | Galaxy-scale and intergalactic theaters; high-FTL and gate-network weapon delivery |
| Cosmological Warfare Band | CW-Band | Tiers 15–20 | Universe-scale and beyond; partially deferred scope; existential-class instruments |

**Cross-Band Note:** Some weapons are designed to be deployed *from* one band against targets in another band. A Tier 8 interstellar-range weapon deployed from an orbital platform is still classified in the IW-Band by construction tier; its deployment context falls under OW-Band operational rules. The construction tier determines band classification; operational employment determines which band's deployment rules apply.

---

### 1.2 — Weapon Scale Class

Within each paradigm band, weapons are further classified by their **Weapon Scale Class** — an independent dimension from tier that defines the spatial footprint of the weapon's destructive effect and the target magnitude it is designed to affect. Weapon Scale Class uses a universal seven-level designation system (WS-I through WS-VII) applicable across all bands.

| Scale Class | Designation | Effect Footprint | Primary Target Magnitude | Escalation Tier Implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WS-I | Precision Strike | Centimeter to meter radius | Individual combatant or single object | E1–E2 deployment context |
| WS-II | Squad Suppression | Meter to 10-meter radius | Small group (2–20 individuals) or light structure | E1–E3 deployment context |
| WS-III | Area Denial | 10 m to 1 km radius | Military unit (20–500 combatants), fortifications, vehicles | E2–E4 deployment context |
| WS-IV | Siege Class | 1 km to 100 km radius | City-level infrastructure, fleet action zones, mountain-scale terrain | E3–E5 deployment context |
| WS-V | Continental Class | 100 km to planetary radius | Continent-level destruction, ecological disruption, planetary atmosphere | E5–E6 deployment context |
| WS-VI | Planetary Class | Planet to stellar system radius | Full planet sterilization, moon destruction, planetary orbit disruption | E6–E7 — requires Strategic Ordnance authorization |
| WS-VII | Existential Class | Stellar scale and beyond | Star system annihilation, galactic topological disruption, multi-galactic events | E7 only — Absolute Authorization Protocol required |

**Important:** WS class is independent of weapon tier. A Tier 3 hand-grenade equivalent is WS-II; a Tier 3 strategic aerial carpet-bomb payload is WS-IV. A Tier 11 precision soul-targeting weapon may be WS-I if designed as a single-target instrument. WS classification follows the weapon's designed effect footprint, not the civilization tier that built it.

### 1.3 — Weapon Configuration Code

Every weapon carries a configuration code that describes its primary operational purpose and deployment mode. Configuration code governs legal treatment under Part 5's combat law and Part 22's faction weapon governance.

| Configuration Code | Full Name | Deployment Mode | Primary Governance |
|---|---|---|---|
| SDW | Standard Direct Weapon | Held, mounted, or fired directly at target in line-of-sight | Part 5 E1–E4; personal and tactical combat |
| DEP | Deployed Emplacement | Fixed or semi-fixed installation; area denial or base defense | Part 5 E3–E5; faction asset governance |
| VHM | Vehicle-Hardpoint Mounted | Integrated into vehicle weapon hardpoints; fires from vehicle platform | Part 32 MIL/MIL-CAP + Part 33 joint governance |
| ORD | Strategic Ordnance | Deployed at WS-V+ scale; requires Strategic Authorization | Part 5 E5–E7; Part 21 civilization governance |
| CRY | Covert / Restricted | Concealed deployment, targeted assassination, or prohibited weapon class | Part 24 Information and Secrecy Law governance |
| AUT | Autonomous Weapons Platform | Self-directed targeting and firing without direct operator control | Part 33 Area 12 Autonomous Weapons Governance |
| SYN | Synergy-Integrated Weapon | Cultivation-technology hybrid weapon; requires Integrated Practitioner | Part 31 + Part 33 joint governance |
| BIO | Biological / Vector Weapon | Delivers biological or genetic disruption agent as primary mechanism | Restricted under Part 33 Area 13; Special authorization |

### 1.4 — Weapon Tier Bands

Weapon tier is sourced directly from Part 8's Type 8 Military & Defense Technology 20-level domain. For clarity of cross-reference, Part 33 organizes weapon tiers into six named Tier Bands that correspond to the five paradigm bands (TW/OW/IW/GW/CW) with TW-Band split into two sub-bands for narrative granularity.

| Tier Band | Designation | Tiers | Paradigm Band Correspondence |
|---|---|---|---|
| TB-Alpha | Primitive and Pre-Industrial | Tiers 1–2 | TW-Band (early) |
| TB-Beta | Industrial to Advanced Conventional | Tiers 3–4 | TW-Band (mature) |
| TB-Gamma | Post-Industrial to Space-Threshold | Tier 5 | TW/OW transition |
| TB-Delta | Orbital to Stellar System | Tiers 6–7 | OW-Band |
| TB-Epsilon | Interstellar | Tiers 8–10 | IW-Band |
| TB-Zeta | Galactic to Cosmological | Tiers 11–20 | GW-Band and CW-Band |
