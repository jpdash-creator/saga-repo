## Area 13 — Resonance Plane Governance

Resonance Planes are dimensional zones adjacent to physical space where a specific affinity type dominates. They are accessible at specific physical locations where the boundary between the physical dimension and the Resonance Plane is thin.

### 13.1 — Resonance Plane Properties

- Each major affinity type (Fire, Ice, Lightning, Earth, Wind, Life, Death, Soul, Space, Time) has a corresponding Resonance Plane.
- Cultivators with strong alignment to the plane's affinity experience enhanced cultivation states within it.
- Opposing affinities are suppressed within the plane.
- Non-aligned cultivators may enter if capable of navigating the extreme environmental conditions.
- Physical laws within a Resonance Plane reflect the dominant affinity — a Fire Plane has no cold; a Space Plane has non-standard spatial geometry.

### 13.2 — Access Points

Physical locations where Resonance Plane access is possible:
- Resonance Plane Access Points are typically also high-class Formation Zones or Sacred Grounds of the corresponding affinity.
- Access Point locations are immutable (P13-3) — they do not move unless a catastrophic spatial event occurs at that location.
- Controlling an Access Point is a major political and military asset.
