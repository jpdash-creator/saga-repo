## Area 17 — Vehicle Law Domain Ownership and Disambiguation

### 17.1 — This Part vs. Part 15 (Travel Law)

Part 15 owns travel mechanics; Part 32 owns vehicle classification. An author asking "How fast can a Tier 9 ship travel?" consults Part 15. An author asking "What type of ship is this, what does it look like, what is its scale class, and what is it named?" consults Part 32. An author asking "What happens when this ship enters warp?" consults both Parts simultaneously.

### 17.2 — This Part vs. Part 8 (Technology Scale Law)

Part 8 owns technology tier classification at the civilization level; Part 32 owns vehicle classification at the vehicle level. Part 8 Type 9 establishes the 20-level framework that Part 32 expands. Part 8's Technology Force Category table is authoritative for combat output; Part 32's Area 11 escalation floor references derive from that table and cannot override it.

### 17.3 — This Part vs. Part 12 (Energy Relics and Artifacts Law)

Formation arrays aboard CHV vehicles are Part 12 objects. Their tier, output ceiling, and collapse behavior are all governed by Part 12. Part 32 governs the vehicle that houses them and the interaction between the formation and the vehicle's overall operational envelope.

### 17.4 — This Part vs. Part 5 (Combat and Fight Law)

All vehicle combat is governed by Part 5's engagement framework. Part 32 provides the force output and combat capability data that Part 5's framework operates on. Part 32 cannot authorize combat escalation or determine combat legitimacy — only Part 5 can.
