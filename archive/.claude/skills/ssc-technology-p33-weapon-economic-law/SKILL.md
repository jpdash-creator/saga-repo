## Area 14 — Weapon Economic Law

### 14.1 — Weapon Market Classification

Technology weapons are classified into three weapon market tiers that define their economic governance:

| Market Tier | Designation | Available To | Governance |
|---|---|---|---|
| WM-I | Open Market | Any individual, faction, or civilization meeting technology tier prerequisites | Commerce law; standard Part 22 trade rules |
| WM-II | Faction Market | Military-authorized buyers only; requires faction-level purchase authorization | Part 22 military procurement law; classified transaction records |
| WM-III | Civilization Strategic Market | Civilization-level buyers only; multi-civilization treaties governing stockpile limits | Part 21 arms control governance; treaty-bound |

**WM-I Open Market Weapons:** Tier 1–4 weapons across all non-restricted types are open market items in most civilizations. Personal defense weapons, hunting arms, and light commercial security systems operate in open markets.

**WM-II Faction Market Weapons:** Tier 5+ weapons and all vehicle-hardpoint systems (VHM configuration) require faction-level authorization and enter classified procurement channels. Unauthorized purchase of WM-II weapons is a Part 22 faction violation.

**WM-III Civilization Strategic Market:** WS-V+ strategic ordnance, Absolute Prohibition weapons (when illegally traded), and all WS-VII systems operate (legally) only within civilization-to-civilization treaty frameworks. Weapons in this tier are not traded — they are manufactured, stored, and deployed under civilization-sovereign authority.

### 14.2 — Weapon Valuation Framework

**Rule 33.14.1 — Weapon Value Scaling:** Technology weapon value scales geometrically with tier, following the same geometric law as civilization tier advancement. A Tier 7 weapon is not simply twice the value of a Tier 6 weapon — it represents the entire industrial and scientific civilization infrastructure that makes it possible.

**Rule 33.14.2 — Arms Trade and Faction Economics:** Arms trade between factions of different technology tiers is a major economic event under Part 22. A lower-tier faction purchasing Tier 7 weapons from a higher-tier faction acquires a strategic asset it cannot reproduce or maintain — this creates permanent dependency on the supplier faction and is a recognized form of political leverage.

### 14.3 — Weapon Stockpile as Advancement Indicator

A faction or civilization's weapon stockpile composition is a Part 9 Technology Growth Law advancement indicator. The presence of:

- Tier 5+ weapons in a faction arsenal signals TB-Gamma civilization minimum.
- Tier 8 IW-Band weapons signal IW-Band capability milestone (Part 9 reference cross-point with Part 32's IS-Band fleet indicator).
- WS-VI civilization-class weapons signal Tier 11+ civilization floor.
- Operational WS-VII weapons signal Tier 15+ floor or extraordinary artifact possession.
