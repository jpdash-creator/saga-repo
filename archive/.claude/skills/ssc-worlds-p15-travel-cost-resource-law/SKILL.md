## Area 17 — Travel Cost and Resource Law

### 17.1 — Cultivation Travel Costs

**Individual Cultivator Qi Cost:**

| Travel Mode | Qi Cost per Hour | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Normal walking/running | None | Biological movement; no qi required |
| Qi-assisted sprint (Mortal T2) | Low (5–15% reserve/hour at max speed) | Sustainable for hours |
| Mortal Tier 2 flight (Ruler/Monarch) | High (20–40% reserve/hour) | Combat-speed flight; requires rest or recovery |
| Xiantian sustained flight | Very Low (1–5% reserve/hour) | Qi field is efficient at this level |
| Xiantian spatial shortcut | Moderate per use (~10–30% reserve per shortcut) | Larger shortcuts cost more |
| Core Realm propulsion | Negligible per hour | Core regenerates continuously; travel is essentially free |
| Core Realm spatial shortcuts | Low per step | Core energy is renewable; sustained stepping possible |
| Divine Realm spatial fold | Context-dependent | Folding large objects / large distances costs more; single-person folds are trivial for Heaven Treading+ |

**Rule 17.1.1 — Travel Exhaustion Affects Combat Readiness.**
A cultivator who has expended significant qi in high-speed travel cannot immediately deploy their full combat capability. Authors respect the travel cost — a character who just crossed a star system at Core propulsion arrives with some qi fatigue, not at 100% reserve.

**Rule 17.1.2 — Formation Gate Activation Cost.**
Activating a Formation Teleportation Array requires CPM (Part 11) of grade proportional to the array's range. Personal arrays at local scale require lower grade CPM; interstellar-range arrays require Divine Liquid grade (Grade 20) CPM.

### 17.2 — Technology Travel Costs

Technology travel costs are expressed in fuel, energy units, and time:

| Travel Scale | Tier Range | Primary Cost | Secondary Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planetary surface | Tier 1–5 | Fuel (animal feed, coal, petroleum, fusion) | Road/rail infrastructure maintenance |
| Orbital and inner system | Tier 5–7 | Propellant mass + energy for fusion drive | Station maintenance, traffic control |
| Interstellar (subluminal) | Tier 8 | Enormous propellant mass; lifetime cost of crew | Ship maintenance over decades |
| Interstellar (FTL) | Tier 9+ | FTL drive energy (scales with mass and distance) | Navigation beacon fees; FTL permits |
| Gate transit | Tier 10+ | Gate activation energy (massive per transit) | Gate maintenance; political transit fees |
| Wormhole transit | Tier 13+ | Stabilization energy (ongoing maintenance cost) | Effectively transit fee based on infrastructure |
