## Area 12 — Growth Law Interface with Part 8 (Scale Law)

### 12.1 — The Growth Law Serves the Scale Law

Part 8 defines what each technology tier is. Part 9 defines how civilizations get there. This relationship is strictly hierarchical: if Part 8 defines a capability as characteristic of Tier 7, then Part 9's advancement mechanics govern how a civilization achieves the prerequisites for that capability. Part 9 does not redefine what the tiers look like — it only governs movement through them.

**Rule 12.1.1 — Tier Descriptions Are Part 8 Governed:**
Authors determining what a civilization at a specific tier can do consult Part 8. Authors determining how long it took to reach that tier, what growth mechanisms produced the advancement, and what the growth history looks like consult Part 9. Both must be simultaneously consulted for any narrative claim about technology advancement events.

### 12.2 — Part 9 Governs P8-2.2 and P8-2.3 Mechanics

Part 8, P8-2.2 states that technology above the Technology Ceiling degrades without maintenance. Part 8, P8-2.3 states that technology from a prior era at a higher tier operates in a suppressed state. Part 9, Area 8.2 (Degradation Timeline Table) provides the specific mechanics governing both.

### 12.3 — The Fourteen-Domain Interaction in Growth

Part 8 defines fourteen domains that advance independently. Part 9 governs how they interact in advancement:
- Domain-to-domain prerequisite dependencies (Area 4.3)
- Growth Ceiling Asymmetry: faster-advancing domains create advancement opportunities for dependent domains (Area 4.3 prerequisites) and also create pressure asymmetries within civilizations (the civilization with Tier 8 Energy but Tier 5 Industrial is under pressure to advance Industrial, because the Energy infrastructure generates resources faster than Industrial can deploy them)
