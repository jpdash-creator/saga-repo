## Area 16 — Part 21 and Technology Growth (Part 9 Interface)

### 16.1 — Technology Tier as TA Axis Input

Part 9 governs how civilizations advance through technology tiers. The output of Part 9's advancement mechanics is the TA axis value for Part 21. Part 21 does not govern TA advancement — it receives TA as input and synthesizes it into CCT.

**Rule 16.1.1 — TA Input Timing:** TA axis updates to Part 21's CCT assessment are triggered by Part 9 advancement events that move the civilization's majority-domain tier (Part 8 7-of-14 rule).

### 16.2 — CCT as Growth Context

Part 9 advancement timelines are influenced by civilizational context. Part 21's CCT governs the available institutional and resource base for Part 9 growth:
- Higher-tier civilizations have larger research institutional investment bases, faster advancement timelines per the Part 9 Systematic Research mechanism (Area 2.1).
- Lower-tier civilizations have limited cross-civilizational transfer opportunities, slower acceleration.
