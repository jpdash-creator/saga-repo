## Area 5 — Governing Laws of the Content Density Framework

**Law CDL2-L1 — Section Aggregate Supremacy.**
The Section-aggregate CDL-2 score is the sole enforcement metric. No individual Prose unit, Chapter aggregate, or Area aggregate can pass or fail CDL-2 independently. All enforcement consequences derive from the Section-close audit alone. A Section in which early Prose units deviate heavily from targets but later Prose units compensate is not in violation provided the Section-close aggregate is within range. The converse is equally true: a Section in which early Prose units are all within target but later Prose units pull the aggregate out of range fails at Section-close regardless of the early compliance.

**Law CDL2-L2 — The Four Anchor Floors Are Non-Negotiable Without Override.**
Any Section in which any of the four Universal Anchor Floor categories (★ COMBAT, ★ CULTIVATION GROWTH, ★ DIALOGUE & SOCIAL INTERACTION, ★ LORE & WORLD-BUILDING) falls below its Anchor value at Section-close is in Hard FAIL. The only mechanism to resolve a Hard FAIL is: (a) additional Prose content that brings the aggregate within range, or (b) a documented CDL-2 Section Override with explicit user approval per Area 2.4. Hard FAILs block Phase progression until resolved.

**Law CDL2-L3 — Independent Lenses Cannot Be Traded Against Each Other.**
Exceeding one category's target does not compensate for falling below another. A Section with 95% COMBAT aggregate does not receive credit against LORE & WORLD-BUILDING falling below its Anchor floor. Each category is an independent measure. The enforcement tests are category-by-category, not holistic.

**Law CDL2-L4 — CDL-2 Does Not Supersede Part 28 Depth or Part 30 Story Law.**
CDL-2 governs content distribution at the category level. It does not override Part 28's Pacing-Appropriate Depth Mandate (3500%–5000%), Part 2 Area 6.2.1's pacing-determined word count range, or any Part 30 arc mandate. A Section that passes CDL-2 but violates Part 28 depth requirements or Part 30 story laws is still non-compliant. CDL-2 compliance is necessary but not sufficient for Section-level canon compliance.

**Law CDL2-L5 — CDL-2 Governs Phase 1; Future Phases Require Parallel Tables.**
The Phase 1 Section × Category targets in Area 3 are specific to Phase 1's story arc as defined in Part 30. When Part 30 is expanded for Phase 2, Part 35 must be expanded with a corresponding Phase 2 Section × Category table derived from Phase 2's Section arc definitions. The framework architecture, ten categories, Anchor floors, and enforcement pyramid apply universally across all Phases; only the per-Section target values change.

**Law CDL2-L6 — Named-Move Density Cannot Fall to Zero for More Than Three Consecutive Prose Units.**
Even outside of Named Move-heavy Sections, the Starward Saga's move architecture requires that named cultivation techniques remain present in the narrative fabric. If three consecutive Prose units within a Section score 0% NAMED MOVES & TECHNIQUES, the AI issues a CDL-2 WARN and the next Prose unit must include at least one Named Move deployment, development, or naming event, even if brief. This law ensures the move taxonomy does not disappear from large non-combat arcs.

**Law CDL2-L7 — Section 8 Consolidation Exemption for Named Moves.**
Section 8's NAMED MOVES & TECHNIQUES target of 8–15–25% (the Phase minimum) reflects the canonical consolidation arc of Phase 1's close. Section 8 is the sole Section in which CDL2-L6's consecutive-zero rule is modified: up to five consecutive Prose units may score 0% NAMED MOVES without triggering a WARN, provided the Section aggregate remains within the 8–25% advisory range. This exemption exists because Section 8's relational and discovery focus is canonical by Part 30 law and does not represent a narrative failure.
