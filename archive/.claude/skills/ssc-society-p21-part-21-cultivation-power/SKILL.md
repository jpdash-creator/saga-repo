## Area 17 — Part 21 and Cultivation Power (Part 3 Interface)

### 17.1 — CA Axis and Part 3

The CA axis value is determined by the cultivation realm distribution described in Part 3. Part 3 governs the physical cultivation laws (realm ladder, breakthrough mechanics, 3-Level Rule, Categorical Impossibility Law). Part 21 uses the civilization's Part 3-governed cultivation state to assign CA.

**Rule 17.1.1 — CA Does Not Override Part 3:** A civilization's CA value does not grant individuals access to cultivation realms they have not personally achieved. CA describes civilizational distribution, not individual capability. The Categorical Impossibility Law (Part 3 Area 17.3) applies regardless of CA; CA does not override Part 3 for individuals.

### 17.2 — Civilization Cultivation Tier vs. Individual Realm

A CA-12 civilization (Divine Sea Realm present in leadership) does not mean all citizens are Divine Sea Realm. CA describes the peak and distribution; individual realm is governed by Part 3.
