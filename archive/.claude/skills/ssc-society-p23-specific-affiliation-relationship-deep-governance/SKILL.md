## Area 15 — Specific Affiliation Relationship Deep Governance

### 15.1 — Cultivation Sect Affiliation Specific Rules

**Cultivation sects have unique affiliation features not shared by other faction types:**

**Lineage Affiliation:** All cultivation sects that share a common founder ancestor are automatically in **Lineage Affiliation** — a background bond of shared cultivation heritage that functions as a minimal cooperative baseline. Even rival sects of the same lineage will typically maintain some minimal cooperation (mutual recognition of lineage members in danger, avoidance of extinction campaigns) that other faction types do not share.

**Technique Transmission Compacts:** Cultivator sects may form specific technique-sharing affiliations — formal agreements to share specific cultivation techniques between sects. These are high-value affiliations; the sharing of a powerful technique is one of the strongest affiliation formation triggers in the cultivation world.

**Disciple Exchange Programs:** Sects may formally exchange disciples — sending their own disciples to train in the other sect and accepting the other sect's disciples in return. This is a cultivation-specific affiliation tool that creates deep personal bonds across sect lines, making institutional hostility harder to maintain when individuals have genuine cross-sect relationships.

**Sect War Governance (Specific):**
When two cultivator sects enter open conflict, cultivation-specific rules apply beyond the general affiliation conflict law:
- **Peak Cultivation Engagement:** Conflicts between sects typically follow an implicit rule that the highest-realm members do not directly engage in the initial stages; escalation should be proportionate
- **Disciple Protection Principle:** Many cultivation conflicts exempt disciples below a defined realm from direct targeting, directing conflict toward senior members
- **Sacred Ground Neutrality Claims:** If one sect controls a Sacred Ground that the other sect's members need for breakthrough resources, explicit neutrality agreements for Sacred Ground access during conflict are common

### 15.2 — Government Body Affiliation Specific Rules

**Government body affiliations have formal diplomatic governance:**

**Diplomatic Recognition:** Government bodies require formal diplomatic recognition from each other before any formal affiliation relationship is established. A government body that does not recognize another does not engage in formal affiliation with it — they exist in a pre-diplomatic state.

**Sovereignty Principle:** All government body affiliations explicitly acknowledge each party's sovereign authority within its own domain. Alliance treaties between governments do not create authority of one government over the other's internal affairs.

**Diplomatic Immunity in Affiliation:** Diplomats and formal representatives operating under a government body affiliation relationship hold protected status. Harming a formal diplomatic representative is a violation event that activates conflict-level response.

**Ambassador Corps Affiliation (Practical):**
Government bodies maintain permanently stationed Ambassador Corps in allied or partner factions. The ambassador relationship is itself an affiliation structure — the ambassador holds the relationship in trust between high-level diplomatic interactions.

### 15.3 — Criminal Faction Affiliation Specific Rules

**Criminal faction affiliations operate outside the formal governance frameworks:**

**Blood Oath Affiliations:** Criminal factions use blood oaths — cultivation-infused sworn commitments with spiritual consequence for violation — as binding mechanisms in place of formal legal charters. Blood oath affiliations are typically more binding than formal charters because the violation consequences are spiritual, not merely political.

**Territorial Non-Interference Compacts:** The most common criminal faction affiliation is simple territorial non-interference — we do not operate in your territory; you do not operate in ours. Violations are immediately responded to with violence.

**Joint Venture Crime:** Criminal factions may form single-operation alliances — sharing resources, personnel, and risk for a specific criminal enterprise — without creating ongoing affiliation. These operational alliances dissolve on operation completion.

**Black Market Network Affiliations:** Multiple criminal factions may share black market distribution infrastructure — overlapping their distribution networks to cover territories too large for any single faction to maintain alone.

### 15.4 — Religious and Ideological Faction Affiliation Specific Rules

**Religious factions operate through doctrine-governed affiliation frameworks:**

**Doctrinal Compatibility Requirement:** A religious faction can only form full affiliations with other factions whose doctrines are assessed as compatible or at minimum not contradictory. Doctrinal incompatibility creates affiliation ceiling — the religious faction cannot advance beyond a certain level of cooperation with a doctrinally incompatible faction.

**Mission Territory Agreements:** When multiple religious factions operate in overlapping territories, they may negotiate Mission Territory Agreements — defining which faction has exclusive right to proselytize in which areas. These are among the most pragmatic religious faction affiliations.

**Holy Patron Relationships:** Religious factions may have patron relationships with powerful individual cultivators or political entities who provide material support in exchange for religious legitimation — the powerful patron receives spiritual endorsement; the religious faction receives resources and protection.

**Heresy Designation:** A religious faction may formally declare another faction heretical — placing it in a mandatory hostile affiliation. Heresy designations are among the most relationship-crystallizing events in the religious faction ecosystem; they are extremely difficult to reverse.

### 15.5 — Noble House Affiliation Specific Rules

**Noble house affiliations are governed by bloodline and honor culture:**

**Marriage Compacts:** The primary affiliation formation tool for noble houses is marriage — a formal union between members of two noble houses that creates permanent bloodline connection, creating affiliation obligations that persist beyond the lives of the married individuals and may extend for generations.

**Feudal Hierarchy Affiliations:** Noble houses exist within feudal hierarchy structures — a major noble house holds the affiliation of multiple minor houses as vassals; all are held within the affiliation structure of the royal house. This multi-level hierarchical affiliation is the defining structural feature of noble house networks.

**Honor Debt Governance:** Noble houses operate under an honor culture where significant favors, life-saving actions, and meaningful gifts create binding honor debts. An unrepaid honor debt creates an affiliation obligation; failure to repay honor debts destroys a noble house's reputation across the entire noble network.

**Inheritance Claims:** When a noble house line dies out, its affiliated houses may have inheritance claims on its territory, relics, or techniques. These inheritance affiliation claims are a frequent source of noble house conflict.
