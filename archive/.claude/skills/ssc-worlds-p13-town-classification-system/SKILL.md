## Area 4 — Town Classification System

A Town is a larger inhabited location with organized governance, established commerce, a permanent economic function, and multi-domain social activity. Towns have formal administrative structures and sustained populations larger than a Village.

### 4.1 — Town Class Table

| Class | Designation | Population Scale | Ambient Energy | Affinity Profile | Special Zone | Historical Depth | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-1 | Frontier Town | 1,000–5,000 | Class 1–2 | Neutral | None | New | A newly organized settlement that has crossed the threshold from Village to Town. Basic governance structures forming. |
| T-2 | Common Town | 3,000–15,000 | Class 2 | Neutral | None | Young to established | The standard inhabited Town. Commercial center for a local region of Villages. Basic governance, local market, organized community. |
| T-3 | Trade Town | 5,000–25,000 | Class 2–3 | Neutral | None | Established | Built at the intersection of trade routes or near resource extraction zones. Merchant activity defines the economy. Higher commercial throughput than population alone suggests. |
| T-4 | Garrisoned Town | 5,000–30,000 | Class 2–3 | Neutral | None | Established | Military presence defines the Town's identity. Barracks, training grounds, defensive formation arrays, armories. Regional security hub. |
| T-5 | Rooted Town | 10,000–40,000 | Class 2–3 | Neutral or mild affinity | None | Long-established — centuries | A Town of significant age and deep cultural tradition. Community identity is layered and complex. Historical significance within its local region. |
| T-6 | Affinity Town | 5,000–30,000 | Class 3–4 | Active affinity | Possible: Passive Formation Zone | Variable | A Town where the environmental affinity is a defining feature of daily life and commerce. The affinity shapes local culture, economy, and what kinds of practitioners settle here. |
| T-7 | Energy Town | 8,000–40,000 | Class 3–4 | Neutral or affinity | Passive Formation Zone | Variable | A Town built on or around a productive Passive Formation Zone. Energy resource extraction drives the economy. |
| T-8 | Vortex Town | 5,000–35,000 | Class 4–5 | Affinity aligned | Vortex Formation Zone | Variable | A Town built on a Vortex Formation Zone. The Vortex's energy environment and resource output are the Town's reason for existing. |
| T-9 | Ancient Town | 3,000–40,000 | Class 3–5 | Variable | Variable — historical ruins, dormant formations | Very old — millennia | A Town of ancient lineage. Deep historical accumulation, legendary past events, possible dormant formation arrays in old districts. Known across its region for its age and tradition. |
| T-10 | Sacred Town | 500–20,000 | Class 6–7 | Aligned with Sacred Ground | Sacred Ground (any grade) | Variable | A Town within or adjacent to a confirmed Sacred Ground. The most exceptionally situated Town possible. Access may be restricted. Known across its World or Star System for its cultivation significance. |
