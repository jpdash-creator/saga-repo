## Area 7 — Cultivation Axis (CuA-C): Culture by Realm and Affinity

### 7.1 — Realm as Cultural Caste

Cultivation realm (Part 3) is the primary social caste marker in cultivation civilizations. Each realm boundary crossing is not merely a physical transformation — it is a social and cultural rite of passage. The following breakthrough-to-cultural-rite table governs the social meaning of realm advancement:

| Breakthrough Event | Cultural Rite | Social Status Change | Cultural Obligation Acquired |
|-------------------|--------------|---------------------|------------------------------|
| First Qi Sense (Mortal T2) | Awakening Ceremony | Enters cultivator social class | Must seek formal training; family obligation shifts |
| Core Formation | Core Ascension Ceremony | Exits Mortal social sphere | Takes formal disciples; social peers narrow to Core Formation+ |
| Core Solidification | Solidification Proclamation | FTL-capable; joins interstellar social sphere | May leave home system; void-oath obligation |
| Divine Realm | Divine Recognition Ceremony | Joins faction governance social layer | May found a sub-sect; territory governance obligation |
| Heaven Soul | Heaven Ascension Rite | Near-mythological social figure within living memory | Becomes a cultural anchor; cannot easily withdraw from public role |
| Divine Sea | Absolute Recognition | Civilizational cultural figure; transcends factional identity | Cultural monument status; cross-civilizational social claim |

### 7.2 — Affinity as Cultural Tribe

Affinity type (Part 4) generates cultural tribe identities that cross-cut faction and civilization boundaries. Cultivators who share an affinity type share cultural resonances regardless of sect origin:

| Affinity | Cultural Tribe | Core Cultural Value | Characteristic Expression | Shared Taboo |
|----------|--------------|--------------------|--------------------------|-----------| 
| Fire | The Burning | Passion; decisive action; sacrifice | Public breakthrough display; flame memorial | Hesitation; slow death of flame |
| Water | The Flowing | Adaptation; persistence; patience | River ceremony; depth meditation | Forced rigidity; dammed flow |
| Wood | The Rooted | Growth; connection; memory | Planting ceremony; lineage tree | Root cutting; deforestation without rite |
| Earth | The Foundation | Stability; endurance; protection | Foundation stone laying; mountain oath | Abandoning shelter; betraying ward |
| Metal | The Edge | Precision; discipline; refinement | Blade dedication; forge ceremony | Blunted purpose; wasted material |
| Lightning | The Struck | Sudden change; illumination; crisis | Storm vigil; transformation marking | Stagnation; fear of change |
| Ice | The Still | Preservation; clarity; judgment | Stillness ceremony; clear-mind vow | Emotional clouding; hasty judgment |
| Wind | The Passing | Freedom; messenger role; change delivery | Departure ceremony; wind dedication | Cage-building; gossip (polluted wind) |
| Void | The Empty | Absence; potential; dissolution | Void vigil; self-emptying rite | Attachment; claiming space not earned |
| Light | The Revealed | Truth; healing; guidance | Illumination ceremony; healing dedication | Willful blindness; hidden falsehood |
| Shadow | The Concealed | Protection; mystery; hidden truth | Night-vow; concealment dedication | Exposing the protected; false shadow |
| Dual/Triple | The Bridge | Integration; synthesis | Integration ceremony | Rejecting either path of nature |

### 7.3 — Sect Cultural Heritage

A cultivation sect is simultaneously a spiritual institution, a combat organization, and a cultural community. Its internal culture is governed by its founding principles, its dominant affinity type, its historical encounters, and its transmission chain health. Key sect cultural institutions:

**The Sect Chronicle:** Every major sect maintains a Chronicle — the living record of its cultural heritage. Chronicles contain breakthrough records, named move libraries, master-disciple lineage trees, and the defining stories (victories, defeats, betrayals, transcendences) that constitute the sect's cultural identity. Chronicle maintenance is a sacred duty; Chronicle falsification is the deepest cultural crime in sect culture.

**The Annual Gathering:** Most sects hold an annual internal assembly that serves simultaneously as a cultivation assessment, a cultural renewal ceremony, and a social cohesion ritual. The specific form of the Annual Gathering is one of the most distinctive cultural expressions of a sect's identity.

**The Inner Disciple Selection:** The ceremony by which disciples advance from Outer to Inner disciple status is the sect's primary social class transition ritual. Its fairness and rigor are the primary indicators of sect cultural health.

**The Funeral Tradition:** How a sect buries its dead (or transforms them) says more about its cultural values than almost any other practice. Cultivation sects that have members spanning Mortal through Divine Realm must accommodate radically different cultural needs within a single funeral tradition.
