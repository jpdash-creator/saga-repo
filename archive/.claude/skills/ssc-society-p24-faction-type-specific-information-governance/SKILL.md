## Area 21 — Faction-Type Specific Information Governance

### 21.1 — Intelligence and Espionage Faction (IEF) Governance

The IEF (Part 22 Area 13) is the institutional actor around which most Part 24 mechanics center. Specific governance rules for IEF operations:

**IEF Rule IE-1 — Cell Integrity:** No operative in an IEF faction can be required to know more than their cell and their immediate handler. Violations of cell integrity (a cell knowing another cell's identity) are treated as security failures requiring restructuring.

**IEF Rule IE-2 — Source Protection:** An informant's identity is classified Tier I for the duration of their service. Exposing a source is treated as organizational treason regardless of the information value of doing so.

**IEF Rule IE-3 — Plausible Deniability Architecture:** IEF factions at SCL-6+ maintain formal institutional separation from the factions that employ them such that attribution of specific operations cannot be confirmed without internal testimony. This institutional separation is itself an information governance structure.

**IEF Rule IE-4 — Information Commercialization:** IEF factions at SCL-5+ may sell intelligence to multiple parties simultaneously, with exclusivity only for specific information objects at specific price points. This creates the canonical IEF faction as a neutral-appearing information broker whose actual loyalties are defined by their primary employment relationship.

### 21.2 — Assassination and Hunting Faction (AHF) Governance

The AHF (Part 22 Area 14) operates with specific information protocols that differ from standard factions:

**AHF Information Protocol:**
- The contractor's identity is classified Tier I and never shared with the operative executing the contract
- The target's information profile is classified Tier I during operation and Tier III after execution
- Operatives operate in a strict information compartment: they know target + method + logistics; they know nothing about why, who commissioned, or what political context the elimination serves
- This compartmentalization protects the operative from compelled revelation (they cannot disclose what they do not know) and protects the contractor from attribution
