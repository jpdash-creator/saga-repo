## Area 12 — Interface with Part 8 (Technology Scale Law)

### 12.1 — Governing Split

| Technology Distance Question | Primary Authority |
|---|---|
| What sensor range is achievable at each technology tier? | Part 14, Area 5 |
| What is the mechanism of sensor technology at each tier? | Part 8, Area 15.3 (Sensory/Scanning domain) |
| What communication range is achievable? | Part 14, Area 5.2 |
| What is the FTL physics framework? | Part 10, Area 5 |
| What travel distances are possible at each tier? | Part 15 (Travel Law) using Part 14 distance definitions |
| What weapon effective range is achievable? | Part 14, Area 5.3 |
| What technology-tier megastructure is possible at what scale? | Part 8, Areas 15.1–15.2; Part 14 provides the spatial scale context |
