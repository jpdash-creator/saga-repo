## Area 5 — Galactic Transport Vehicles (GT-Band, Tiers 11–14)

### 5.1 — Vehicle Type Philosophy: GT-Band

GT-Band vehicles operate at galactic scales — across thousands to millions of light-years. They require drives that make even Tier 10 FTL look slow, and gate networks that span a full galaxy. At this tier, vehicles are not merely machines — they are extensions of civilizational will, each one representing centuries of industrial and scientific investment.

---

### 5.2 — Tier 11 Galactic Vessels

**Category: Galactic Traverse Vessels (GTV)**

*GTV-1 Galactic Scout:* SC-III rapid FTL survey vessel for galactic mapping and first-contact operations. FTL speed: 50,000–200,000× light. Transit time to galactic core (50,000 ly): 3–10 months. Crew: 20–200. The tip of the spear of Tier 11 civilizational expansion.

*GTV-2 Galactic Merchant:* SC-V to SC-VI cargo vessel for inter-civilizational trade across galactic distances. Cargo capacity: 1,000,000–10,000,000 tons. FTL speed (cargo-optimized): 10,000–50,000× light. The vehicle of galaxy-spanning economic systems.

*GTV-3 Galactic Ark:* SC-VI to SC-VII population carrier for civilizational-scale migration across galactic distances. Population capacity: 100,000–10,000,000. Transit time (50,000 ly): weeks via gate network, months via raw FTL. The vehicle of galactic civilizational diaspora.

*GTV-4 Galactic Gate Tender:* SC-V specialized construction and maintenance vessel for Tier 11 gate network infrastructure. Carries gate construction components, maintenance crews, and emergency repair capability for the most strategically critical infrastructure of galactic civilization.

**Category: Galactic Warcraft (GWF)**

*GWF-1 Galactic Warship:* SC-V to SC-VI capital warship for galactic-scale military operations. Part 5 combat: a single GWF-1 operates at E7 by definition — its weapons systems have civilization-scale destructive potential. Classified MIL-CAP. A GWF-1 requires Part 28 authorization before deployment as a narrative element in active storylines.

*GWF-2 Galactic Fortress Ship:* SC-VII mobile fortress — a vessel that is simultaneously a warship, a military headquarters, and a self-sustaining civilization node. Crew: 100,000–1,000,000. Classified MIL-CAP. Requires Part 28 authorization.

---

### 5.3 — Tiers 12–14 Intergalactic Vessels

**Category: Expanse Travelers (ET)**

*ET-1 Intergalactic Runner:* SC-IV rapid FTL vessel for transit between galaxies within a galaxy cluster. FTL speed sufficient to cross 1,000,000 ly in weeks via established intergalactic lanes.

*ET-2 Supercluster Transit Ship:* SC-V to SC-VI long-haul vessel for transit across galaxy superclusters. Wormhole-network-dependent for practical transit at Tier 13.

**Category: Wormhole-Transit Vessels (WTV)**

At Tier 13, wormhole technology (permanently open spatial tunnels, unlike gates which require both ends active) enables a new vehicle category: vessels optimized specifically for wormhole transit.

*WTV-1 Wormhole Clipper:* SC-III to SC-IV high-speed vessel sized for wormhole aperture transit. Optimized for rapid back-and-forth transit through established wormhole networks.

*WTV-2 Wormhole Freighter:* SC-V cargo vessel for bulk inter-galactic-cluster freight via wormhole network. The equivalent of the CRV-2 Cluster Merchant at wormhole scale.
