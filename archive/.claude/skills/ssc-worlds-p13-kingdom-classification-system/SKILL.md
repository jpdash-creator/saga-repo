## Area 7 — Kingdom Classification System

A Kingdom is an organized political territory with defined borders, a recognized governing structure, military capability to defend those borders, and a cultivation/technology character profile that defines the political unit. "Kingdom" is used as the governance term for any organized polity at this territorial scale — kingdoms, republics, principalities, theocracies, and other political forms are all classified here based on territorial scale and capability.

### 7.1 — Kingdom Class Table

| Class | Designation | Territorial Scale | Dominant Character | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Km-1 | Minor Kingdom | County to Province | Any | A minor lordship or principality controlling limited territory. Often a vassal of a larger power. Recognized sovereignty but limited reach. |
| Km-2 | Independent Kingdom | Large Province | Any | An independent Kingdom with genuine sovereignty. The first tier of recognized diplomatic standing. Full court, standing army, taxation system. |
| Km-3 | Standard Kingdom | Country-sized territory | Any | The baseline independent political unit. Full court, army, and cultivation or technology establishment. Recognized across its continent as a sovereign power. |
| Km-4 | Powerful Kingdom | Large country to sub-continental | Any | A powerful Kingdom with regional influence. Other Kingdoms may be tributaries, allies, or rivals. Commands major resources. |
| Km-5 | Dominant Kingdom | Sub-continental to continental fraction | Any | Controls a major portion of a continent. Major military projection. Often the predecessor to Empire. The defining Kingdom of its region. |
| Km-6 | Continental Kingdom | Full continent or approaching continent-spanning | Cultivation-dominant / Tech-dominant / Hybrid / Military-dominant | A Kingdom spanning an entire continent. The dominant political structure of its continental region. The governing character — whether cultivation or technology defines power — is a key descriptor. |
| Km-7 | Multi-Continental Kingdom | Multiple continents of a World | Any dominant character | Spans multiple continents. World politics organize around it. Has achieved World-continental authority short of full World-spanning status. |
| Km-8 | World Kingdom | World-spanning | Any dominant character | Has achieved authority across its entire World. The cultivator or ruler-class leadership's power literally shapes the World's political reality. |
| Km-9 | Orbital Kingdom | World + orbital bodies and moons | Technology-augmented / Hybrid | Extends beyond a single World to orbital installations, moons, and near-space. Technology is necessarily a component at this scale. |
| Km-10 | Multi-World Kingdom | Multiple Worlds within a Star System or across Star Systems | Any dominant character | Controls multiple Worlds. Governance infrastructure matches the scale. The Kingdom's dominant character at this tier defines its entire political identity across systems. |
