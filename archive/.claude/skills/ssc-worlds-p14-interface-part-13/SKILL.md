## Area 13 — Interface with Part 13 (Location Law)

### 13.1 — The Location–Distance Boundary

Part 13 and Part 14 jointly govern the spatial layer of the Starward Saga:

| Spatial Governance Question | Primary Authority |
|---|---|
| What kind of location exists at a given position? | Part 13 |
| What are the environmental properties of that location? | Part 13 |
| What is the distance between two locations? | Part 14 |
| How does the location's properties affect perception range at that distance? | Part 14 (range modifiers) + Part 13 (ambient energy class) |
| What is the influence zone distance of a Sacred Ground? | Part 14, Area 7.3 |
| What are the cosmological hierarchy's canonical distances? | Part 14, Area 3 |
