## Area 5 — Technology Distance Perception and Effect Range

### 5.1 — Technology Sensor Range by Tier

Technology sensor capability scales with civilization tier (Part 8). The following table establishes canonical active sensor ranges for standard sensor systems. These are operational ranges for standard sensors — specialized sensor arrays may extend beyond these values at the same tier but require dedicated construction and power investment.

| Technology Tier | Standard Active Sensor Range | Long-Range Passive Detection | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 (Pre-Industrial) | Touch / visual only | ~50 m horizon (flat terrain) | No technological sensors |
| Tier 2 (Industrial) | ~1–5 km (optical, radio) | ~10 km | Telescopes, early radio detection |
| Tier 3 (Early Tech) | ~50–100 km (radar equivalent) | ~500 km | Surface radar, satellite optical |
| Tier 4 (Advanced Tech) | Full planetary surface | ~500,000 km (~0.003 AU) | Planetary sensor grids, orbit tracking |
| Tier 5 (System Expansion) | ~1 AU | ~5 AU | Inner star system sensor coverage |
| Tier 6 (Inner System Unified) | ~5 AU | ~30 AU | Full inner system sensor grid |
| Tier 7 (System Unified) | ~50 AU | ~200 AU | System-boundary sensor awareness |
| Tier 8 (Multi-System Early) | ~500 AU – 1 light-day | ~1 light-year | Early interstellar detection; FTL begins at this tier |
| Tier 9 (Multi-System Active) | ~1–5 light-years | ~10 light-years | Multi-star system sensor network coordination |
| Tier 10 (Cluster Entry) | ~50 light-years | ~200 light-years | Star Cluster approach detection |
| Tier 11 (Cluster Control) | ~500 light-years | ~2,000 light-years | Cluster-wide sensor dominance |
| Tier 12 (Quadrant Entry) | ~1,000–2,000 light-years | ~5,000 light-years | Multi-cluster awareness |
| Tier 13 (Quadrant Expansion) | ~5,000 light-years | ~10,000 light-years | Star Quadrant strategic sensor coverage |
| Tier 14 (Galaxy Approach) | ~10,000 light-years | ~25,000 light-years | Galactic arm strategic awareness |
| Tier 15 (Galaxy Expansion) | ~25,000 light-years | ~50,000 light-years | Half-galaxy sensor dominance |
| Tier 16 (Galaxy Unified) | Full galaxy (~100 kly) | Several hundred kly | Full galactic sensor network |
| Tier 17 (Trans-Galaxy Entry) | Several hundred kly | ~1 Mly | Galaxy Cluster approach awareness |
| Tier 18–20 (Trans-Galaxy Active) | 1–20 Mly | Galaxy Cluster scale | Cluster-wide awareness at Tier 20 |

### 5.2 — Technology Communication Range and Lag

Communication range follows the same tiers as sensor range. Pre-FTL communication is limited by the speed of light, creating information-lag governance:

| Technology Tier | Communication Range | Lag Governance |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1–4 | Planetary scale | Instant at planetary scale (light-lag under 1 second) |
| Tier 5 | Inner system (~1 AU) | ~8 minutes lag at 1 AU (light-speed) |
| Tier 6 | Star system (~5 AU) | ~40 minutes lag at outer system scale |
| Tier 7 | System boundary (~50 AU) | ~6–7 hours lag at system edge |
| Tier 8 (pre-FTL comm) | Light-years | Years of lag; communication impractical for real-time governance |
| Tier 8 (FTL comm unlocked) | ~1–5 light-years | Near-instant to days; FTL communication requires Part 10 Area 5 framework |
| Tier 9+ | Scales with tier | FTL communication; effective range follows Part 10 FTL framework |

**Communication Lag Governance Rule (P14-Comm-1):**
In any pre-FTL communication scenario, information delivered across a distance of D light-years arrives with a lag equal to D years. Authors must account for this lag when writing political decisions, military coordination, and cultural exchange at interstellar scale. A civilization sending a message 10 light-years away must wait 10 years for a response if operating below Tier 8 FTL communication threshold.

### 5.3 — Technology Weapon / Effect Range

Technology weapon range follows a similar scaling pattern to sensor range:

| Technology Tier | Standard Weapon Effective Range | Maximum Strategic Range |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1–3 | Meters to kilometers (line-of-sight) | Continental (ballistic) at Tier 3 |
| Tier 4 | Hundreds of km – orbital strike | Full planetary scale |
| Tier 5 | ~0.5 AU | ~1 AU |
| Tier 6 | ~5 AU | ~15 AU |
| Tier 7–8 | ~50 AU – 1 light-day | ~1 light-year |
| Tier 9 | ~1–3 light-years | ~5 light-years |
| Tier 10–11 | Star Cluster scale | ~500 light-years |
| Tier 12–14 | Star Quadrant scale | ~10,000 light-years |
| Tier 15–16 | Galactic arm scale | Full galaxy (~100 kly) |
| Tier 17–20 | Trans-galactic | Galaxy Cluster scale at Tier 20 |
