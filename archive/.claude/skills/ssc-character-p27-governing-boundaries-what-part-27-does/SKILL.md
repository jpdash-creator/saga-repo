## Area 17 — Governing Boundaries — What Part 27 Does NOT Govern

The following are explicitly NOT within this Part's governance domain:

1. **Internal psychological states** → Part 26 governs the internal psychological experience of consequences
2. **Physical cultivation mechanics** → Part 3 governs cultivation physics; Part 27 governs the consequence of actions that affect cultivation
3. **Combat engagement structure** → Part 5 governs combat mechanics; Part 27 governs what follows combat
4. **Soul energetic mechanics** → Part 4 governs soul architecture; Part 27 governs consequence of actions that affect soul relationships
5. **Cultural norms** → Part 25 governs culture; Part 27 governs how cultural context modifies consequence expression
6. **Faction political mechanics** → Part 22 governs faction structure; Part 27 governs consequence cascades through faction systems
7. **Information mechanics** → Part 24 governs information; Part 27 governs the consequences of information events
8. **Move mechanics** → Part 6 governs technique mechanics; Part 27 governs what techniques cause in consequence terms
