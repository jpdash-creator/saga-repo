## Area 13 — Civilization Regression and Recovery

### 13.1 — Regression Classification

Civilizations in regression are classified by their regression type:

| Regression Type | Definition | Narrative Character |
|---|---|---|
| Axis Regression | One axis declining while others remain stable | The civilization is losing capability in a specific domain; not yet CCT-declining |
| CCT Regression | The synthesis formula now produces a lower CCT | The civilization has crossed a tier threshold downward; visible structural change |
| Cascade Regression | Multiple axes declining simultaneously | Crisis state; CCT declining rapidly; civilization in existential risk |
| Terminal Regression | All axes declining with no recovery mechanisms | Civilization ending; may produce Remnant entities or complete dissolution |

### 13.2 — Recovery Trajectories

Recovery from regression follows Part 9 re-advancement mechanics for TA. CA recovery follows cultivation generational timelines:

- CA recovery from 1 level below: 2–4 generations of institutional investment.
- CA recovery from 3+ levels below: Potentially irreversible without external cultivation knowledge transfer or Sacred Ground access.

LA recovery: Requires either political re-annexation (governance re-establishment) or military reconquest with viable administrative infrastructure.

EA recovery: Follows CPM resource rediscovery or new formation zone access. If energy infrastructure technology is intact, EA can recover faster than CA.

SA recovery: Requires rebuilding integration institutions after collapse; typically slower than TA or EA recovery due to the institutional depth required.

### 13.3 — Irreversible Regression Conditions

**Rule 13.3.1 — Cultivation Knowledge Loss:** If cultivation academies and technique libraries are destroyed and no practitioners above a certain realm threshold survive, CA may face irreversible regression below the level where re-establishment of institutional cultivation infrastructure is possible. This is one of the most catastrophic forms of civilizational regression.

**Rule 13.3.2 — Sacred Ground Destruction:** Part 13 governs Sacred Ground collapse; the effect on Part 21 is permanent EA and CA loss at the level of the destroyed Sacred Ground's contribution.

**Rule 13.3.3 — Technology Regression:** Per Part 9 Area 8, complete loss of institutional knowledge in 7+ technology domains drives TA below recovery threshold — "civilizational dark age" conditions.
