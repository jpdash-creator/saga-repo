Whenever a line of prose says how old a character will be after N years — or how many years
until some age — do the arithmetic explicitly against the ledger's Locked Canon start age
before handing the draft to the Continuity Checker.

**Why:** Prose 1 shipped the line "And in six years she will be fifteen" about Varya, whose
Part 30 §1.3.1a Locked Canon start age is 8 (8 + 6 = 14). Gate 1 FAIL. Character ages are
Locked Canon and cannot be bent to fit a nicer-sounding sentence — the *number of years* is
the negotiable side of the equation, so fix the interval, not the age.

**How to apply:** Phase 1 anchor numbers for the child protagonists, from the hero ledgers:
Varya starts at 8, ends Phase 1 at 17 (9 in-story years). First Awakening at the
Section 1 -> Section 2 boundary, age 9 -> 10. Apprentice's Choice in Section 6 at ~16.
If a sentence contains the same interval twice ("in six years... spent the six years"),
correct every instance — internal inconsistency inside one sentence is its own FAIL.

See also [[dialogue-italics-gate1-fail]], [[revision-tasks-need-full-draft-text]].
