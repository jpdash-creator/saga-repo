Render ALL direct speech as `*"the exact spoken words"*` — double quotation marks wrapped
in italics, simultaneously, at every instance. Never plain `"words"`.

**Why:** Prose 1 "Eleven Years of Mornings" was drafted with every one of its ~30+ dialogue
lines in plain double quotes and took a Gate 1 Post-Draft Audit FAIL on
Rule 29.2.1.1 / Part 2 Rule 2.2.2, forcing a full mechanical re-pass. The rule is easy to
overlook because plain quotes are the ordinary novel convention — this canon deliberately
departs from it.

**How to apply:** Apply the italic wrapper while drafting, not as a cleanup pass. Related
formatting rules that ride along in the same skills:
- **Highest-risk spot (recurred in Prose 2 "The Two Hands of the Morning"):** a speech that
  is *interrupted mid-turn by an action beat* — `*"...first half."*` + beat + `*"...second
  half."*`. The closing `*` on the first half is the one that gets dropped, because the eye
  reads the beat as still inside the quote. Before hand-off, scan specifically for `."` or
  `,"` followed by a space and a capital letter with no `*` between them.
- Nested reported speech inside a spoken line uses single quotes inside the italic frame:
  `*"she said, 'you will not pass this gate,' as if it were settled."*` (Rule 29.2.1.1a)
- Named Moves / Named Techniques / qualifying cultivation level designations take
  `***bold-italic***` everywhere, inside and outside dialogue (Rule 29.2.1.1d). Standalone
  realm names (Champion, Warrior, Ruler, Monarch) stay plain text.

Rule text lives in `ssc-narration-p29-dialogue-formatting-structural-standards` and
`ssc-narration-p02-conversation-rules`. See also [[age-arithmetic-must-be-computed]].
