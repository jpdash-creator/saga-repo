Azure Sky Kingdom's territory is bounded on the **west** by the Ember Reach (volcanic,
unrelated fire-affinity region) and on the **east** by the Lyraen River, whose far bank is
Gale Kingdom's sphere (`ledger-worlds-location/azure-sky-kingdom.md` §5.1). Gale Kingdom's
own ledger confirms the same relationship from its side: "immediately east of Azure Sky
Kingdom across the Lyraen River" (`ledger-worlds-location/gale-kingdom.md` §5.1), with its
own Aerie-Plateau-to-Long-Coast axis running further east from there. Both
`ledger-worlds-kingdom-state/azure-sky-kingdom.md` and `.../gale-kingdom.md` restate this
independently. The named route between the two capitals, **the Sapphire Road**, explicitly
runs "via the Lyraen crossings" (`ledger-worlds-travel/_index.md`).

**Why this matters:** caught a genuine Gate 1 FAIL in Prose 2 ("The Two Hands of the
Morning") where a Gale courier's letter was staged as arriving at Azure Sky's capital via
"the west gate" — contradicted by all four ledger files above, and additionally
self-contradicted within the same draft (its own closing line has a reply courier "taking
two signatures east" toward Gale). Not a subtle inference — a direct, checkable geography
fact.

**How to apply:** Any time a courier, army, delegation, refugee, or traveler moves between
Azure Sky Kingdom and Gale Kingdom (or is staged at a gate/border implied to be on that
route), verify the direction is east/west-consistent with this fact before passing Check #1
(Parts, specifically 13/15) or Check #2 (ledgers). Ember Reach is the real western neighbor
(Cindervast and other fire-affinity polities) — a "west gate" reference only makes sense if
the traffic is to/from Ember Reach or the Southern March, not Gale. Related: the
`ledger-reset-contamination` pattern (outline-planner memory) is a separate, already-known
issue in the same ledger files (Windward Pass CPM crisis residue) — do not conflate the two;
the residue is stale prior-run plot data, this geography fact is live current-canon Base
Information.
